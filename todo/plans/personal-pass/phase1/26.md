# Card 26, The Taming Power of the Great: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Read once, judged as read. No card edited.

## Suspect sentences

1. "None of that is the flaw." CODE, first paragraph. A pronouncement with the flaw unnamed until the next paragraph; it would need the fear of shortage named here.
2. "You came to this by getting what you have without scheming, and what was got honestly is what can be stored." ICHING, Reading. Assigns the reader a life, then closes on a pronouncement; it would need the Judgement's counsel said as counsel.
3. "Know the road is hard, stay upright on it, and it is right to have somewhere to go." ICHING, Line 3. The classical tail kept as a riddle; it would need the line's plain point, that a goal is right once the skill matches the force.
4. "and the moment you stop pushing in order to hear it, everything changes." KEYS, Gift. "Everything changes" carries nothing; it would need what changes.
5. "here the pride is not destroyed but polished, all the way, until the same energy that grasped is a jewel put to the service of everything." KEYS, Siddhi. A picture with no plain meaning beside it; no fact here to carry.
6. "The laughter that comes off them rings like a string of bells and asks nothing back." KEYS, Siddhi. Decoration; no fact here to carry.
7. "The thymus is the place in you where your body learned what is its own, and this energy lives there because the fear under pride is of never having enough, of being no one." BODY, Physiology. The join is an equivalence to decode (knowing what is yours set against fear of being no one); it would need the reason the three sources agree on, or none.
8. "A will that cannot rest wears your guard down; a will that has softened is felt in the same place, as warmth through your chest." BODY, Physiology. Invented relation; no fact here to carry.
9. "Four kin in you are built of it, sharing one work, which is what your body does with light, and yours is the part done by the will: what you let in is turned to your use." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule, said as a riddle.
10. "The one thing you can check on any bright day is this: sunlight on your skin makes something in you that your body needs and cannot make in the dark." BODY, Amino acid. A true check about a different substance, with no join to threonine.
11. "What comes to you from outside, your will turns into what you have." BODY, Amino acid. A moral pinned to the fact above.

## Unsure

1. "Under it runs a quieter thing, an intention that was there before you chose anything, and everything you have done well was done in its direction." CODE, first paragraph. The last clause tells the reader what their past contained.
2. "Carried well, the will is not spent; it is penned." CODE, second paragraph. The pen is the I Ching's picture, given here before it is explained.
3. "it does not move to be looked at; standing there is enough." ICHING, Upper trigram. Voice, though a reader could test it against a stubborn person.
4. "Empty the mind of the fight and fill the belly." ICHING, Line 5. Classical and near a riddle.
5. "does not see that what is taken from one place is taken from the whole." KEYS, Shadow. A source claim that a reader may not be able to check.
6. "What opens is artfulness, which is pride used instead of being used by." KEYS, Gift. The turn is real but the phrasing is a puzzle.
7. "who lure the mind with a puzzle to lead it past its need for answers." KEYS, Siddhi. The trickster source line, but the reader must decode it.
8. "An orchid is not worth more than a daffodil, and they were never asked to be." DESIGN, Where it lives. Picture to decode, though the meaning arrives in the sentence before.
