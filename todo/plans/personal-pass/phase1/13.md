## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "It lives wherever two people are more than the words passing between them." CODE. Unsure: "more than the words" has to be decoded, and now that the fire clause is cut it is the paragraph's last sentence. Source: gk-64ways-13, "It's about the human emotional connection. It's what has brought us together around the council fires forever."

2. "People feel it and set things down in front of you, the secret, the old hurt, because something in you can hold what is said without needing to answer it." CODE. Sourced (center-identity, "the magnetic pull that draws people to tell you their secrets"; gate-13, "create safe spaces for others to open up"), but DESIGN's first paragraph says the same sentence again: "They sit down beside you and out comes the secret, the story they have carried for years, because something in you holds what is said without needing to fix it." One of the two is the other's copy; which is Adrian's call.

3. "Carried well, it begins at home." CODE. "Begins at home" is a saying standing in for the fact. Source: gk-64ways-13, "This is the gift of hearing another's heart. We can only do that when we can hear our own heart."

4. "While someone is still talking you catch your own reply forming underneath, set it down, and go on listening. What you hear then is the note under the note, the thing a person means and cannot say. They feel it as trust before they know why." CODE. Sourced (gk-64ways-13, "Someone is talking to us and our mind is busy formulating an answer"; "listening for the hidden notes and chords"; "the more others will feel that trust emanating from us"), but the Gift says the same three sentences in nearly the same words: "You catch your own reply forming under someone's words and let it wait. You hear the note under the note, what a person means and cannot say. Because you heard it, they trust you before they know why." The mechanism allots the reply and the note to CODE; the Gift's copy is the one that should not be there. Left, because the fix is not one of the five things.

5. "Listening asks nothing of anyone, which is why it is the one power that changes people without pushing them." CODE. Unsure of "the one power". Source: gk-64ways-13, "because it's so giving, trusting, without agenda, it alone has the power to bring permanent change to our world."

6. "There are seasons when all you can hear is the bad news, and it feels like seeing clearly. It is only the oldest part of you, listening for danger alone, and it can be heard too." CODE. Sourced (gk-64ways-13, "only reporting the bad news"; "the old reptilian brain still dominates, and that means we only hear the frequency band that it hears"), but the Shadow opens on the same two facts: "Something older than thought in you is tuned to danger. On the days it has the ear, the whole world comes through as bad news ... It feels like being realistic." The mechanism gives CODE "the bad-news season named once" and KEYS "the one band"; on the page they are the same page.

7. "It joins nothing." ICHING, Upper trigram. Three words a reader cannot test; it means the sky does not cling as fire does, which the next sentence of the Lower trigram says properly. No source found beyond the Qian field, "heaven, sovereign, father; the highest realm, situated above the human world."

8. "It is what everything under it faces, the same from every side." ICHING, Upper trigram. Recorded under lore as an extension of the Qian fields; nothing in the packet says it. Nearest: oracle-13, "unceasing forward movement, dynamic, enduring, untiring."

9. "Its risk is parching: an aim held so high and so dry that nobody can warm themselves at it, a cause with no fire under it." ICHING, Upper trigram. The picture must be decoded (a dry aim, warming at it) before it works. Source: oracle-13, "With the pronunciation GAN: parch, dry up, burn, exhaust."

10. "Its risk is that a fire takes the shape of whatever it clings to, so a small circle makes a small light." ICHING, Lower trigram. Unsure: "a small circle makes a small light" is line 2's fault said as the trigram's. Source: line-13-2, Cleary Taoist, "This is sameness which is weak and not far-reaching."

11. "Not blood, because the open country is far from the family house, and not habit, because none of you has been here before." ICHING, Reading. A matched pair set up to be knocked down by the next sentence, and a fragment. Left, because it carries two source facts: Legge, "its being in the remote districts of the country, where people are unsophisticated"; line-13-2, "If one can only be the same as relatives or friends and not strangers, this is the path of shame and regret."

12. "There is always one soft place in the middle of such a company, the person everyone wants beside them, and the circle holds as long as that person is not claimed by any one side." ICHING, Reading. The second half is my reading of the line structure, not a line in the packet: in the sources the soft line is claimed, by the fifth, after the third and fourth fail. Source: Legge, "The one divided line is naturally sought after by all the strong lines."

13. "Look at each face as it is, unhurried, and keep your hands open." ICHING, Reading. "Keep your hands open" is a stage direction; the nearest source is an ideogram. Source: oracle-13, Associate, "a pair of hands reaching downward meets a pair of hands reaching upward, helpful association."

14. "It is warm, it is small, and it ends in regret, because sameness with your own kind is the one sameness that costs nothing." ICHING, Line 2. Unsure of "costs nothing". Source: Huang, "It is selfish and stingy."; Cleary Taoist, "This is sameness which is weak and not far-reaching."

15. "The other side is too strong, so the wanting turns into watching, then the watching into a life." ICHING, Line 3. "The watching into a life" is decoration on the three years. Source: Huang, "The opponent is too firm. Three years, unable to go into action."; Cleary Buddhist, "he hides fighters to lie in wait and climbs the high hill to spy."

16. "Your way is true and your heart is straight, so the meeting comes, with a laugh you did not expect." ICHING, Line 5. "A laugh you did not expect" is recorded under lore as a rendering of the Taoist note, and it is a stretch from it. Source: Cleary Taoist, "before there was no one who was the same, but afterward there will surely be those who will emulate"; Huang, "Straight in hearts."

21. "You did not ask to be told. You were told anyway." DESIGN, The drive. Two fragments that say what the paragraph already meant. Source: gate-13, "They have an innate ability to attract confidence from others."

22. "Other people's lives come in and are made louder, their wounds with them, so that another's story can be felt so completely it is mistaken for one's own." DESIGN, Where it lives.
