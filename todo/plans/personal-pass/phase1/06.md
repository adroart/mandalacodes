## Suspect sentences, for the margin

Each: the sentence, its lens, and the source line it should have carried.

1. "Every quarrel you have ever had began there, before a word was said." CODE. Source: 64ways, "Every Shadow is rooted in a frequency of conflict… It's all about the fear that another person can hurt us." Sourced, but the card says it twice more: the Shadow has "That is where every quarrel starts", and Physiology has "the knot you feel in an argument before a word is said." One idea, three sentences.

2. "Carried well, this energy is honesty at the right moment." CODE. Source: gene-key-06, "Honesty carries an extraordinary power and is one of the keys to Diplomacy. The other key is timing." Sourced; flagged because "Carried well, this energy is" also opens card 50's second paragraph, and it is the CODE cousin of the banned "Held well" opener in KEYS. Two cards is within the line, so it was left.

3. "What looks like calm from outside is a person who can stand in the middle of their own alarm without leaving." CODE. Source: 64ways, "This ability to be with oneself, to take care of oneself internally and to be safe even in the midst of fear, is a hallmark of the 6th Gift." Sourced; listed because the CODE reading spends this and the Gift spends "It has nothing to do with the careful phrase", so the reading and the Gift share the "looks like X from outside, is Y" shape.

4. "In a family it is the father whose word is final, who does not argue, only leaves the room." ICHING, Upper trigram. The father is Shuogua lore (meta.lore); no source found for "does not argue, only leaves the room". A picture the writer made from "heaven moves upward".

5. "Its risk is winning every point alone, since a strength that rises away from what is beneath it has nothing left to hold." ICHING, Upper trigram. Nearest source: Legge line 6, "is there to be no end of striving? Persistence in it is sure to end in defeat and disgrace." Unsure whether a reader can say what "nothing left to hold" means.

6. "In a family it is the middle son, caught between, who is right and cannot be heard." ICHING, Lower trigram. The middle son is lore; "who is right and cannot be heard" is the writer's from Legge's "the undivided line in the centre of Khân is emblematic of sincerity". Unsure.

7. "In the body this energy is the ear, hollow on the outside so that it can take a thing in." ICHING, Reading. Shuogua lore (meta.lore). No packet line gives the ear; the counsel "listen before you answer" is Deng's "compromise… as a strategy for peace" at most. Unsure whether it earns its sentence.

8. "Trouble passes over a house that does not rise to meet it." ICHING, Line 2. Source: Cleary Taoist, "If there are three hundred families in your domain, you can thereby escape the barbs of opponents; though there is trouble, it cannot reach you." Close, but the card's picture (a house that does not rise) is not the source's (a small domain to hide in).

9. "In the centre and fair, this is the one place in the whole matter where a case may be pressed, because the one pressing it wants nothing for themselves." ICHING, Line 5. Source: Deng, "Out of six lines, only one, line 5, can press the dispute to a successful outcome"; Cleary Taoist, "one can subdue others by virtue… correct yourself and others". No source found for "wants nothing for themselves".

10. "Truth lands clean here." ICHING, Line 5. Nearest source: Huang, "One is in the central and correct position." Suspect: a four-word line that sounds like an oracle; a reader cannot say what it means in other words.

11. "So you guard. The chest hardens and a small distance is kept going." KEYS, Shadow. Source: 64ways, "Thus we harden our heart, and make internal or external judgments about them, or we just plain avoid them." Sourced; "a small distance is kept going" is unsure as English.

12. "The relationships that will not leave you alone are the ones holding the thing you have not yet felt, which is why they stay." KEYS, Shadow. Source: 64ways, "these relationships, these conflicts, are always here. They're where the transformation occurs. Once we begin to heal ourselves inwardly then these relationships no longer have such a hold over us." Sourced; the paragraph before it already said "they are the only one who can bring it up", so this may end the paragraph by saying what it meant.

13. "What was swept under lies there until it blows." KEYS, Repressive nature. Source: gene-key-06, "The repressive nature will sweep conflict under the carpet… unless conflict is dealt with transparently it tends to eventually explode." Sourced; "lies there until it blows" is the one line in the natures I was unsure reads as English.

14. "Being this vulnerable is the strength." KEYS, Gift. Source: 64ways, "They understand defences because they themselves are willing to be vulnerable. They're the most vulnerable; they're right there with us, with their hearts on their sleeves." Sourced; a short line that may read as pronouncement.

15. "Where there had been two sides to keep even there are no sides now, and the evening-out that took such effort stops on its own." KEYS, Siddhi. Source: gene-key-06, "The 6th Gift is marked by a constant balancing of opposites… at a certain point, this process spontaneously gives way to the 6th Siddhi and duality itself is transcended." Sourced; unsure whether "two sides to keep even" is readable without the source.

16. "The cost is being called difficult by people who wanted the warmth without the rub." DESIGN, The drive. Nearest source: channel-6-59, "Misunderstandings can arise when others interpret this energy as invasive or overwhelming." No source found for "called difficult"; an inference from the channel text.

17. "Those who carry this through life learn that the rub was the way in, and that a closeness with no friction in it never got close." DESIGN, The drive. ## Least sure

The way-is-to repair. "The fear lives in you, older than you, carried in the blood from people you never met, and whoever is standing closest only wakes it" drops the word "find", so the paragraph's counsel (look inward, not at them) now rests on the next sentence's "Felt there, in you, it loosens." It reads as one thought, but the reader is no longer told to look.
