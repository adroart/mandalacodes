# Card 57, Flight of the Tao: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Report only, no edits. RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "It lives in the moment before you decide." CODE, reading. A location for a knowing; nothing a reader can check. Would need to carry: the pull arrives before the reasons, which the first sentence already carries.

2. "Then walk that way, softly." CODE, reading. Voice with nothing inside; "softly" is the card name's mood, not a fact. No fact here to carry.

3. "You came to this after a stretch with no place to stay, so the way in is the only way there is." ICHING, Reading. The wanderer must be decoded, and the conclusion does not follow from it. Would need to carry: after having no place, small steady entry is how a place is made.

4. "Go under the surface, where the thing is decided, and weigh each move by where you stand, since a light thing pressed from the right place moves a heavy one." ICHING, Reading. The counterpoise as a picture the reader must decode. Would need to carry: a small move placed right outweighs a large one placed wrong.

5. "Notice where you are holding yourself now." ICHING, Reading. Formula line on 23 cards. No fact here to carry.

6. "Let the walk be soft, and let it be long." ICHING, Reading. Voice; restates the sentence before. No fact here to carry.

7. "The aim is frustrated, and the frustration is the shame." ICHING, Line 3. A pronouncement; a reader cannot say what the shame is in other words. Would need to carry: having to reconsider the same thing over and over is what humiliates.

8. "Trust is a field, and you rise into it by listening." KEYS, Gift. Pronouncement; no life in it. Would need to carry: the more often you act on the hunch, the more of your life you can trust it with.

9. "It is not always wrong; a life needs its turmoil." KEYS, Reactive nature. Pronouncement with nothing to check. No fact here to carry.

10. "The knowing is like water, finding the easiest way to the centre of the hardest thing." KEYS, Gift. A picture with nothing plain beside it. Would need to carry: intuition gets to the answer without pushing.

11. "There is no path after that and no more knowing the way, because you are there." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle. No fact here to carry.

12. "There is no fear, because there is no tomorrow, so there is no death." KEYS, Siddhi. A chain of pronouncements; each link is a claim a reader cannot test. No fact here to carry.

13. "Everything is joined by it, and now you see it." KEYS, Siddhi. Voice closing the section. No fact here to carry.

14. "This is the oldest thing written in you, the one that makes the world feel solid, and it was built to be unlocked." BODY, Amino acid. Pronouncement; "makes the world feel solid" cannot be checked against a life. Would need to carry: the fear of tomorrow is the oldest fear and it can be outgrown.

15. "In the ones who share this energy with you the substance is the same." BODY, Amino acid. A kin fact, not a life fact; the formula closer. No fact here to carry.

## Unsure

- "Its risk is going under and staying there, so far beneath notice that even you no longer know what you know." ICHING, Lower trigram. The last clause is a riddle, but a reader who has talked over a hunch for years could say yes to it.
- "Softness carried past its moment stops being gentle and becomes a way of losing: your means, then your say." ICHING, Line 6. "Your means, then your say" decodes the axe and the property; the reader may not get there.
- "It was there before you were born; it drives what you want and what you fear more than you would like." KEYS, Shadow. The second half is an assertion about the reader's life; the first half carries the conception fact.
- "You are a tuning fork, pitched to whatever is set beside you, and the world is pitched to fear." KEYS, Shadow. The picture carries a testable thing (you take the pitch of the room), but "the world is pitched to fear" is a pronouncement.
- "What they learn, when they are clear, is which of it is theirs." DESIGN, Where it lives. Carries the fact that an open centre takes in others' fears, but "when they are clear" is loose.
- "Whichever of the three sits in you or in someone near you, the hunch becomes a life only where one of them meets it." DESIGN, What completes it. "Becomes a life" is a phrase standing in for the channel fact.
- "In you this energy is the spleen, and the reason is quiet." BODY, Physiology. "The reason is quiet" is a riddle until the paragraph pays it off; it does pay it off.
- "so you are resting your hand on the part of you that knows first and says nothing." BODY, Physiology. A pleasant closer that restates the paragraph above.
