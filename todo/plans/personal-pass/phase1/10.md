## Side by side

Cut from the CODE reading: "It lives in every hour you have to walk close to something bigger than you and come through as yourself." The ICHING Reading opens "You are walking close to something that could hurt you ... You will come through" and closes "Walk as yourself, walk lightly, and the way that is dangerous becomes the way through", which is the same proposition. The light step stays in both: in CODE it is what being yourself looks like ("a lightness in the step that a performed walk never has"), in ICHING it is the counsel ("Keep your steps light").

## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "You do the thing the way you do it, and nothing about it is heavy: a person walking as themselves has a lightness in the step that a performed walk never has." CODE. The opening clause is said again in the Gift, "You do the thing the way you do it, and the doing has play in it, the way a child draws." One line, two sections. Source: gk-64ways-10, "If it's playful, if it doesn't take itself too seriously, if it's fresh, original, then it's something emerging from nothing."

2. "Carried well, this energy has nothing to defend, because you have stopped holding a position on yourself." CODE. The Gift has the same clause word for word: "There is nothing to defend, because you have stopped holding a position on yourself." Source: gk-64ways-10, "We have nothing to defend with this Gift. There's no need to defend any position or opinion, because we don't really have one."

3. "What you do with ease has play in it, and people relax near it, because someone at home in themselves gives everyone else leave to be at home too." CODE. DESIGN carries the same proposition: "When you are at home in yourself the people near you relax and are freed to be at home in theirs." Source: center-identity, "When you love yourself, you electromagnetically empower everyone around you to love themselves."

4. "The easy road is there, seats along it, friends along it, and most people turn back from it because it asks so little." CODE. "Because it asks so little" is the card's reason; the source gives a different one. Source: gk-64ways-10, "most of us won't take this path in life ... our pattern of self-obsession, our addiction to the drama and the struggle is too great. So we'll likely turn away."

5. "The tiger does not bite because there is no fear in the step, only a plain gladness at walking, and gladness provokes nothing." ICHING, Reading. "Gladness provokes nothing" goes a step past the source. Source: Legge footnote, "The attribute of Tui is pleased satisfaction. Of course such an attribute could not be predicated of one who was in the fangs of a tiger."; practical-10, "Remain inoffensive, and the risks you take will pay off."

6. "Do not provoke what is bigger than you, and do not shrink from it either, because both are ways of watching it instead of walking." ICHING, Reading. The second half is the card's own reasoning; no line says shrinking is watching. Source: practical-10, "Do not provoke. May the weaker tread softly"; Deng on lines 3 and 4, "misfortune results from overestimation and fearfulness."

7. "This is the ground a life is built on, the way you behave when nobody is making you, and it either holds or it does not." ICHING, Reading. "It either holds or it does not" says nothing. Source: oracle-10, Additional Texts, "Treading: actualizing-dao's foundation indeed."

8. "Notice where you are holding yourself now: the head up and clear, the mouth easy, the weight not yet on the foot that is down." ICHING, Reading. A template sentence (26 cards open on "Notice where you are holding yourself"); the foot is the only part of it that is this card's. Source: meta.lore, "the Shuogua body parts in the Reading (Heaven the head, Lake the mouth)"; practical-10, "Keep your footsteps light."

9. "The road is the company." ICHING, Line 2. A pronouncement the reader has to unpack. Source: practical-10, "Devote time to being with yourself so you learn how to be yourself."; Legge, "a quiet and solitary man."

10. "Only a soldier fighting for his lord gets to be that reckless." ICHING, Line 3. Unsure: the sources split on this clause. Wilhelm and Huang read the warrior as serving his prince by firm will; Legge reads him as a bravo playing at ruler. The card follows Wilhelm and adds "gets to be", which neither has. Source: Wilhelm, "Thus does a warrior act on behalf of his great prince."; Huang, "Like a warrior works for the great king, Because of his firm and strong will."; Legge, "We have a (mere) bravo acting the part of a great ruler."

11. "Not the fright that freezes, which gets bitten, but a watchfulness that goes on walking, firm where it must be and soft everywhere else." ICHING, Line 4. A "not X but Y" pair with no main verb, left because no cut serves it and both halves are sourced; noted so it is counted with the hinge. Source: Deng, "the person who steps on a tiger's tail and then gives way to fright and panic only gets bitten."; Cleary Taoist, "Being firm but not impetuous ... flexible in action, forestalling danger."

12. "A step checked before it lands is not a step lost." ICHING, Line 4. Sounds like wisdom; the source is plainer. Source: Legge footnote on line 4, "he bethinks himself, and goes softly."

13. "What is left when it stops is you, unwatched." KEYS, Shadow. A closing line I was unsure of; "unwatched" is the card's word. Source: gk-64ways-10, "This is how transformation occurs - through doing nothing and simply seeing how manic we've become."

14. "Whatever authority they need they find in someone else, because looking in has been made unbearable." KEYS, Repressive. "Unbearable" is borrowed from the Human Design gate line, not this nature's source. Source: gene-key-10 p5, "Their self-denial does not allow them to recognise the Divine within themselves, finding it far easier to project such an authority outside them."

15. "The moment being natural becomes a method it is the watching again in a new coat." KEYS, Gift. "In a new coat" must be decoded. Source: gk-64ways-10, "You can't decide to be natural, and then go and make a career of it."; "we may one day see that we've quietly taken on another set of views and opinions - new layers of complexity and thought."

16. "There is laughter in this, because from here the whole search looks like a joke everyone is in on." KEYS, Siddhi. "Everyone is in on" reverses the source, where almost nobody gets it. Source: gk-64ways-10, "pure Being alone sees the great human joke."; "If we do get the big joke in this Gene Key, then we may come to the end of our spiritual path quicker than we thought!"

17. "This is what ease becomes when even the one being easy lets go." KEYS, Siddhi. A riddle as a paragraph opener. Source: gene-key-10 p10, "at this siddhic level, everything dissolves ... self, not-self, mind, form, purpose and meaning."

18. "It is the plainest state there is." KEYS, Siddhi. The paragraph's last sentence, saying what "so ordinary you would not spot it in a room" already said. Source: gk-64ways-10, "This 10th Siddhi is so normal that it seems radical."; "Being is too simple to describe."

19. "People who carry this all their lives learn to tell the attention that keeps behaviour honest from the attention that only watches. They tell them apart by how the body feels while it does each." DESIGN, The drive. A template opener (38 cards), and no source found for telling the two apart by the body. Nearest: gate-10, "it's crucial for individuals with this gate in their chart to continually work on their self-awareness."

20. "For someone like that there is a self to be true to, which does not move with the room, and what starves it is a borrowed manner kept up to pass. Keep that up for years and the heart goes out of you." DESIGN, Where it lives. No source found for the starving or the heart going out; the teaching says only that the defined centre has a stable self. Source: center-identity, "With a Defined G Center, you have a stable sense of identity ... you have an innate solidity of self."

21. "Whichever half sits in you, or in someone near you, behaviour becomes a life only where the two meet." DESIGN, What completes it. A template close (50 cards on "whether the other half sits in"), and "behaviour becomes a life" must be decoded. Source: _reference-principles, "With them the whole path lights and something is possible that neither of you has alone."

22. "Everything this energy is kin to shares the one substance, a single chemistry for the whole family, most of which is about the heart, about what happens when it is trusted." BODY, Amino acid. "Most of which" overstates the source's "many". Source: body/amino-acids/arginine.md quoting key 17, "many of the keys concern love and the human capacity to trust in the authority of the heart (325-327)."

23. "So ease is not added to you from outside. It is there inside you already, the moment you stop arguing with your life." BODY, Amino acid. The card's one hinge, kept because a reader does assume ease is acquired; noted so it is counted. Source: gene-key-10 p9, "naturalness is the simplest thing of all. It is simply there inside you the moment you decide to stop arguing with life!"

24. The entrance, meta.centre: "You would sooner be disliked for the person you are than liked for a part". DESIGN carries the same sentence: "you would rather be disliked as yourself than liked as someone else". The brief says the entrance borrows no phrase from any section. Not touched (the entrance is not one of the five things). Source: gate-10, "live their truth without fear of judgment or rejection."
