# Card 61, The Holy of Holies: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Report only, no edits. RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "Let the chest open, the middle of you empty, and speak only what the whole of you is standing on." ICHING, Reading. Voice; the last clause is a picture standing in for "say only what you are sure of". No fact here to carry.

2. "You have a good cup and someone to share it with, none of it arranged." ICHING, Line 2. The goblet kept as a picture the reader must decode. Would need to carry: what you have is worth sharing and the one to share it with is already there.

3. "The only release is to walk into the middle of the pressure instead of making it go away, and that takes apart what the mind built, which is why it has to be done slowly, in a body, on the ground." KEYS, Shadow. "In a body, on the ground" is a riddle at the end of a usable sentence. Would need to carry: the source's reason it must be slow, or cut the tail.

4. "Being born in a body is being born with a wound, and this is how the wound is tended." KEYS, Gift. A life assigned to everyone, in a pronouncement. No fact here to carry.

5. "Holiness is what is left when the inspiration has used itself up." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement. No fact here to carry.

6. "The pressure that drove the question leaves the head to go out through the whole of you, everywhere at once, so that you are entirely empty and endlessly full in one moment, with no one inside to have the experience." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle. No fact here to carry.

7. "Until you rest in this you are asleep, whatever else you know." KEYS, Siddhi. A verdict on the reader's life. No fact here to carry.

8. "It is a lonely way, a lovely one: a flash of it, then a fall and the work begun again, until the night you sit on open rock while the full moon drifts up the mountain, with nothing left to empty." KEYS, Siddhi. A picture with nothing plain beside it, and a life assigned. No fact here to carry.

9. "What both are given, when the pressure is not obeyed, is the same: a mind that can go still, the way a mountain stays where it is while everything on it moves." DESIGN, Where it lives. The formula's consolation line, closed with a picture. Would need to carry: a question left open stops hurting.

10. "The mystery beats in every cell of your body, and whatever is of true value in you is hidden inside your body, never on the outside." BODY, Amino acid. Pronouncement; nothing to check. No fact here to carry.

11. "That is chemistry as much as feeling, one substance shared with your kin." BODY, Amino acid. Riddle plus the kin fact. No fact here to carry.

## Unsure

- "Not knowing is the whole of what is asked; the mind is not your enemy, only it was never there to keep you safe." CODE, reading. The second clause is a riddle; the first is the card's counsel.
- "Stay firm in what you know without being able to prove it, because that is where this strength is made." ICHING, Reading. "Where this strength is made" is voice on a real instruction.
- "In the body this energy is the thighs above the mouth: what carries you, over what you say." ICHING, Reading. The two body parts are glossed, but the join to a life is thin.
- "This is as far as sincerity goes, and it is enough." ICHING, Line 5. Voice; it does carry the commentary's "this is the highest".
- "Losing touch with what is real is the ordinary state of a mind, and in this energy it shows first as a question, the question why." KEYS, Shadow. The first half is a pronouncement about every mind; the second half is the card.
- "The mind is sick for one reason: it is trying to find itself with itself, and it cannot, any more than an eye can see itself." KEYS, Shadow. The eye makes it checkable; "sick" is a verdict.
- "Each flash takes apart a piece of what the mind had built in place of reality, and the energy that was tied up in the building is let loose, wanting to be made into something." KEYS, Gift. The source's mechanism; a reader may not be able to check the energy released.
- "what the two make is a knowing you can hold, though not always one you can say; not everything needs to be understood." DESIGN, What completes it. The last clause is a slogan; the rest is the channel.
- "two things that weigh the same, spelled from the same pieces, are not the same thing, and the difference is inside, out of your sight." BODY, Amino acid. The isomer fact reaching for the card; the reach is visible.
