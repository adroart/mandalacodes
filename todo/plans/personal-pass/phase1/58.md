- "Whatever gladness shows at the surface is held up from here, by something solid that stays put when the surface is stirred." ICHING, Lower trigram. The firm line under the open line, rendered as a picture; a reader may not get to "a good mood that survives a bad day".
- "Some people go against the rhythm on purpose, doing anything to get a reaction from life, and take a dark kind of relief in that." KEYS, Shadow. "The rhythm" is undefined here; the rest is checkable.
- "It is the life in a body, and a body cannot refuse its own life without dying." KEYS, Repressive nature. Carries the source's reason there is no repressive side, but reads as a pronouncement.
- "People with this alive in them walk into a room and the room moves better." KEYS, Gift. A reader could check it against someone they know; "moves better" is loose.
- "Joy gives the why; correction gives the what." DESIGN, What completes it. A slogan for the channel; it does carry the two gates.
- "and what was going out of you toward tomorrow turns back into you as warmth." BODY, Physiology. The exercise before it is checkable; this closing clause is a picture.
- "Bliss itself, when it comes, is made in you, a release of chemicals in the brain, felt as gladness with no cause." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the state a bodily cause I could not trace to the source.
