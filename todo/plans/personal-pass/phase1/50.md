# Card 50, The Cauldron: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "The ears are hollow, the handle solid: soft and firm carry each other." ICHING, Line 5. The line structure read as a picture the reader must decode; no fact about a life inside it.

2. "Nothing you do now fails to help." ICHING, Line 6. A pronouncement; would need to say what the finished cooking lets a person do.

3. "The note joins a music that was already playing." KEYS, Siddhi. Picture with nothing plain beside it.

4. "Balance was the flowering. This is the fruit, and it falls when it is ripe, into a harmony that was never made, only entered." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle on top of a picture; "never made, only entered" is left for the reader to unpack.

5. "Before you can feel it every sense of being separate has to have gone, and then you melt into it." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement; nothing a reader could check.

6. "The body becomes a chamber that rings, then the chamber itself dissolves, and the vessel turns out to be everything there is." KEYS, Siddhi. Picture to decode; no fact here to carry.

7. "Every life is a note on one score, and the score grows sweeter." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement.

8. "Under the surface of your days it was playing the whole time, whether or not you could hear it, and everything you ever did was moving you toward it." KEYS, Siddhi. A life assigned to the reader.

9. "You are not the player. You are the instrument, and what plays through you is the music." KEYS, Siddhi. Voice with nothing inside.

10. "The message that carries the instructions for life in you can be misread, and fear in a room misreads whatever is said." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the molecule a reason the section never sources; the two halves are joined by the word misread only.

11. "The balance this energy is about is felt in your cells first, and belonging somewhere brings it there." BODY, Amino acid. Pronouncement; no body fact under it.

12. "The liquid in your every cell is tuning itself to whatever you have lined up with, so if that is chaos, chaos is what your body feels like." BODY, Amino acid. No fact here to carry.

13. "At the height everything out of tune empties out of you, and your body becomes a chamber the harmony sounds in." BODY, Amino acid. Picture; the Siddhi already spent it.

## Unsure

- "Take a stand instead, and a force wakes in you that wavers at first, then stays." CODE. The waver-then-stay is checkable; "a force wakes" is voice.
- "Then take your place, stand in it rightly, and a life whose place is right sets firm the way cast metal does." ICHING, Reading. The Image text carried whole; "stand in it rightly" is the part that says nothing.
- "Cook gently, and your ears grow clear, your eyes sharp." ICHING, Reading. Judgement text as advice; the reader has to decode gentleness into a life.
- "Stand as a pot stands over the fire, on three feet, still, holding what is not yet finished." ICHING, Reading. The posture is a picture; it may be the section's job.
- "Regret comes like rain, and the rain clears it." ICHING, Line 3. Compressed near a riddle; the rain is the line's own.
- "Strength with no strain in it is what lifts the pot at last, so lift it, and give what is in it away." ICHING, Line 6. Picture as advice.
- "Tuned by ear, the same string holds its note, and the note is balance." KEYS, Gift. Picture, but it opens the section and the next sentence lands it.
- "Balance can be lost. This cannot." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement, but it is the one plain distinction in the paragraph.
- "One person who arrives here turns the whole thing, since what was hidden comes to light around them." KEYS, Siddhi. Large claim; the Gift's honesty line is under it.
- "The way through is to see it for what it is, thank it, then go on." DESIGN, Where it lives. "Thank it" is the wellness register.
- "When this centre is at ease there is an aliveness in you, the lightness of a body that feels safe, and it meets each moment fresh, which is why it keeps the time." DESIGN, Where it lives. "Keeps the time" must be decoded.
- "Nothing you eat is yours until it has been cooked there, and everything your body has made since the day you were born was cooked there first." BODY, Physiology. Chinese medicine spoken as anatomy; the next sentence half takes it back.
