# Card 29, The Abysmal: Phase 1 report

## Suspect sentences

1. "It is a dishonesty practised on yourself, and a life that is being lied to withdraws." KEYS, Shadow. "A life withdraws" must be decoded; it would need what withdraws (the days go dull, which the next sentence says).
2. "Then the love poured out begins pouring back, from everything, until the rocks and the trees seem to bleed it toward you." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
3. "There is no worshipper left, only what was loved." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
4. "Whoever comes near this catches it." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked.
5. "To be devoted is to lie forever in the lap of what you love." KEYS, Siddhi. A pronouncement; no fact here to carry.
6. "The part of you kept back is made of the same substance." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule.
7. "What you keep from someone you love is kept below words, in your chemistry, out of fear." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason.
8. "Down there a yes that keeps back one part in a hundred is the same as one that keeps back all but one." BODY, Amino acid. Same, and a repeat of the CODE's own claim dressed as chemistry.
9. "Three other things in you share this substance; it is the same in all four." BODY, Amino acid. "Things" carries nothing; it would need the kinship said as the one fact the source gives.
10. "In you it is the yes said to life itself, with no promise about where it goes, since the only promise your body can keep is to what it is being led to now." BODY, Amino acid. Made reason; the source that speaks of valine is another card's chapter.

## Unsure

1. "A thing begun whole makes its own luck; a thing begun with one hand on the door makes the other kind." CODE, second paragraph. A matched pair; "makes its own luck" is a claim, though the source makes it.
2. "Its risk is the heart that aches, because a thing that only ever goes down can go past where anyone can reach it." ICHING, Upper trigram. Picture first, meaning half given.
3. "Its picture is one firm line held between two soft ones: a strength nobody sees, inside a giving way that everybody does." ICHING, Lower trigram. The line structure read as a person; near a riddle.
4. "Danger is not only what you fall into; it is what keeps a city safe, the mountain no army crosses, the river at the wall, and there is a time when it is the thing to use." ICHING, Reading. Three examples, and the classical turn carried nearly whole.
5. "Held whole, it rides out any storm, because it was never about the other person. It was about leaping with your eyes open." KEYS, Gift. "Leaping with your eyes open" is a phrase the reader must unpack.
6. "The one marriage this place asks of you is to its own yes and no." DESIGN, Where it lives. Picture to decode, though the next sentence unpacks it.
