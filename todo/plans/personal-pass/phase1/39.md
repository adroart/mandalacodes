1. "Every block is a breakthrough not yet made." CODE. A pronouncement; the sentence before it already carries the fact (the block is where your own force gathered and stopped).
2. "Stand where you are, feet on the ground, and let the stop be the stop." ICHING, Reading. A riddle on the feet template; would need to say what a person does in a stopped week.
3. "It feels like a physical death, every plan and piece of work surrendered into a single vanishing point, the tunnel of agony that turns, without fail, into ecstasy." KEYS, Siddhi. "Without fail" is a promise, and the tunnel is a picture to decode; would need what is given up, said plain.
4. "What comes back is larger than freedom." KEYS, Siddhi. Voice with nothing inside; no fact here to carry.
5. "It makes an enormous pressure in you and in the room around you; that pressure is what makes things: it is what drives a person past what they were." BODY, Amino acid. The molecule is given the card's job; would need a serine fact, or nothing.
6. "A block is where the pressure in you has gathered, which makes it a place to look rather than a wall." BODY, Amino acid. No body in it; it restates CODE inside the amino section.
7. "That breath, not the rush, is what lasts in you." BODY, Physiology. A pronouncement closing on a phrase; the rush clearing in two minutes is the fact, and the breath is decoration on it.
