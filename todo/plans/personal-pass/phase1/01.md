## Sentences that say nothing, or say what was already said

Each fails "could a reader say it in other words and check it against a life", or repeats a proposition the card already carries.

1. "In all the years there have been it has not missed once." (ICHING, Upper trigram.) Restates the sentence before it (the sun sets and is back the next morning). Nothing new for a reader to check. Source it could have carried: oracle-01-energy.md, "constant as the motion of heavenly bodies in their orbits" (Persist, JIAN), which is already the previous sentence.

2. "Hold yourself the way the sky moves, steady and unhurried, with nothing in it strained." (ICHING, Reading, last sentence.) The paragraph ends by saying what the second paragraph already said ("The sky moves through the day and the night without straining"). A posture sentence is wanted here; this one repeats the earlier picture rather than adding the posture. Source: wilhelm-01, The Image, "The movement of heaven is full of power. Thus the superior man makes himself strong and untiring." No second line in the packet for a bodily posture; the head is Shuogua lore (meta.lore).

3. "That is the work of the creative force too." and "The liver is where the body does the same." (BODY, Physiology, first paragraph.) Three closing sentences restate the join as an equivalence after the join has already been given in the first sentence and unfolded. Adrian read the liver section on the page and passed it; listed only because these are the shape the brief names as the failure ("the join said as an equivalence to decode"). Source: body/organs/liver.md, "The join, in plain words": the liver is the body's beginning, where the year starts, where the plan is made, where the vital energy is set moving.

4. "The energy that begins things was given to the one organ that can begin itself again." (BODY, Physiology, second paragraph.) This is the proposition Adrian rejected on card 1 in an earlier version ("what begins things in you is built to begin again", read as abstract and a waste of his time), back in slightly different words. The fact beside it (the liver grows back) stands on its own. Source: body/organs/liver.md, checkable anatomy, "The liver is the one large organ in you that grows back."

5. "It is the same with the fire in you." (BODY, Amino acid.) The hinge sentence that turns lysine into a reason, the shape the brief records failing three times ("Creation is the same"). What follows it is sourced; the hinge is not. Source the paragraph should carry: gene-key-01.md page 9, "creativity can never be controlled. It simply comes when it comes, and when it is absent there is nothing for you to do but wait and relax."

6. "Fed, it comes back to you on its own." (BODY, Amino acid, last sentence.) Repeats the first paragraph's "What brings it back in you is being fed, from outside and in its own time", and with the sentence before it ("Push it up by will and you pay") makes the pair the brief forbids as a moral on a fact ("it cannot be forced, only fed"). Source: gene-key-01.md page 9, the same "wait and relax" line; no source gives "fed".

7. "Push it up by will and you pay, in what you give up to keep it lit." (BODY, Amino acid, second paragraph.) Not empty, but the same source line is already spent in the first paragraph ("nothing you do by force brings it back") and a third time in RELATIONS, Codon ring ("forcing it back up is how you end up making bargains with yourself"). One card, three tellings. Source: gene-key-01.md page 10, "If you then try to rekindle your creativity through force of will, you will only end up making huge compromises, both with yourself and with others."

8. "That is the same fire a season on, gathering a harvest round it, and in your body the two are made of one thing." (BODY, Amino acid.) "The same fire a season on" with the harvest is RELATIONS, Codon ring's sentence on this same card ("the same fire a season on, with a harvest standing round it"), which Body is told not to repeat. Source: _hexagram-01.md, Codon Ring, "Shares this ring with: 14 Great Possession"; gene-key-01.md page 9, "chemically bonded to the 14th Gene Key".

9. "They tend to think everyone should be going their way, and if they give up their own way to follow someone else's, something in the chest goes out." (DESIGN, Where it lives.) The second half is the sentence before it again ("the thing that starves them is being made to work in another person's manner"). Source for both: center-identity.md, the Defined G grapples, "You can think people should go the direction you're going. You can lose heart if you follow someone else's direction."

10. "With it comes something that has to be said or made, in your voice or with your hands." (KEYS, Gift.) Not empty, but the same source line is already spent in the CODE reading ("something in you has to come out, and bringing it out is a joy"). The brief's rule is each line once, where it belongs; the Gift is where it belongs. Source: gene-key-01.md page 8, "with the joy also comes the need to express it, and especially to express it through your voice or your art."

## Sentences I was unsure of

11. "When it is moving you are more alive than at any other time: the work has light in it, something in you has to come out, and bringing it out is a joy." (CODE, reading.) Thirty-three words, a list of three after the colon; "the work has light in it" is decoration rather than a thing to check. Adrian read the reading on the page. Source: gene-key-01.md page 8, "Freshness conveys aliveness, as though it refers to something burning with an inner fire."

12. "Its risk is the same drought, doubled, since nothing under this strength can tell it whether it is warming a life or scorching one." (ICHING, Lower trigram.) The doubled trigram makes the two paragraphs end on the same risk; the second adds "doubled" and little else. Card 2 has the same shape. Source: oracle-01-energy.md, "With the pronunciation GAN: parch, dry up, burn, exhaust."

13. "The sky moves through the day and the night without straining, which is why it never wears out." (ICHING, Reading.) "Without straining" is the writer's gloss; the sources say strong, persistent, untiring, and never say effortless. Source: wilhelm-01, "The movement of heaven is full of power"; oracle-01-energy.md, "Heaven moves persistently."

14. "Brought up now, before it is ripe, it would be spent on whatever met it first." (ICHING, Line 1.) "Spent on whatever met it first" is not in any translation of the line; it leans on the target hexagram's name (Coming to Meet). Source: line-01-1.md, Cleary (Taoist), "This is the time to nurture the sound energy; one should not yet rely or presume on this strength."

15. "Feed it where nobody is watching, so that when it does come up it has somewhere to go." (ICHING, Line 1.) The first clause is sourced; "so that when it does come up it has somewhere to go" is added and cannot be checked. Source: line-01-1.md, Cleary (Buddhist), "working quietly and unassumingly to develop it inwardly."

16. "From outside, doing it your way every time can look like stubbornness." (DESIGN, The drive.) The source's word is self-centred, not stubborn; "stubbornness" reads as the writer's softening. Source: gate-01.md, "their relentless drive for self-expression might sometimes come across as self-centeredness"; center-identity.md, "You are not easily swayed by people's expectations."

17. "In the act of making they are somebody, for as long as the making lasts, and even where they sit in a room can change what they make." (DESIGN, Where it lives.) The last clause stretches "where you sit in a restaurant matters" (about the Open G's sense of self) into a claim about the work. Source: center-identity.md, "if you have an Open G with Gate 1 Self-Expression, you will feel like you have an identity when you are creatively expressing yourself"; "Where you sit in a restaurant matters."

18. "This is the fire when there is no longer anyone it is happening to." (KEYS, Siddhi.) A rendering of the source's "there is no one to comprehend it"; a reader may not be able to say it in other words. Source: gene-key-01.md page 12, "True beauty is emptiness. There is no one to comprehend it and nothing to feel it."

19. "What brings it back in you is being fed, from outside and in its own time." (BODY, Amino acid.) "In its own time" is sourced; "being fed, from outside" is the lysine analogy carried over to the fire, which the brief allows as the source's own join but which reads close to a made reason. Source: gene-key-01.md page 9, "when it is absent there is nothing for you to do but wait and relax."

20. "The moment it arrives they fill every hour and call everyone they know, anything rather than feel what is there." (KEYS, Reactive nature.) "Call everyone they know" is the Shadow's example ("reach for the phone or call someone") used a second time in the same section. Source: gene-key-01.md page 7, "these people immediately increase their activity and contact with others."
