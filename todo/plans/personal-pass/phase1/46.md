3. "Let the lift come from low down, as a tree's does, and the top of you can stay light." ICHING, Reading. A picture standing in for a plain act.
4. "It comes from owning what is yours, the acceptance of a thing seen; once a thing has been seen and let go, more of you is free for being glad." KEYS, Gift. "Owning what is yours" is undecoded.
5. "Ecstasy is what the body is for, and it comes through the heart, not from anywhere far away." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement; the second half is the source's.
