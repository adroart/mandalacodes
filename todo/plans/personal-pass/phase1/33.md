## Suspect sentences

2. "Let the hand rest, keep the head where it is, and go." ICHING, Reading. A riddle built on the head-and-hand template; would need to say what a person does with a stopped hand and a head still looking.
3. "Go, and go gladly, because everything serves." ICHING, Line 6. "Everything serves" is a pronouncement; would need the line's own fact, that nothing in you pulls the other way, which the sentence before already gave.
4. "Revelation is a flood, and it comes as silence." KEYS, Siddhi. Two pictures with nothing plain between them; no fact here to carry.

## Unsure

1. "This energy stops and turns." CODE. Short and true to the card, but it says only what the first sentence said.
2. "Done with a friend, it is the oldest thing people do: sit by a fire and talk about the good times." CODE. "The oldest thing people do" cannot be checked; the fire can.
3. "Something is being read in there that has not finished." CODE. A picture of the quiet; a reader may or may not get what is being read.
4. "Knowing when to withdraw is the hardest wisdom there is, and the whole of it is timing: a step back at the right hour turns out later to have been the way forward." ICHING, Reading. "Hardest wisdom there is" is a superlative; the second half is checkable.
5. "Yellow is the colour of the soil at the centre, and hide cured that way does not give." ICHING, Line 2. A classical gloss a stranger cannot follow, though it explains the image.
6. "That is what forgetting is like from inside: not a gap, but the present." KEYS, Shadow. Compressed to the edge of a riddle; the sentence before makes it readable.
7. "It has the colour of autumn: the leaves come down, brighter just before they fall, and the light turns inward." KEYS, Siddhi. A picture; "the light turns inward" is the part a reader has to decode.
8. "Here the join is to the centre of the chest, where who you are lives, so what you say comes from who you are, and you are what you talk about." DESIGN, Where it lives. The last clause is a flourish on a real fact.
9. "That is looking back in your flesh: a life heard from the inside by the one it happened to." BODY, Physiology. The join sentence; it names the energy, but the equivalence is a stretch from bone conduction.
