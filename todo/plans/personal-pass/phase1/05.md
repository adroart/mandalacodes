# Card 05, The Space Between Time: Phase 1 report

Tells before: sentences 148, twoAnds 0, notXItIsY 0, heightOpeners 0, wayIsTo 0, thisIsTheEnergy 0, systemWord 1, threeCommas 20, long30 12.
Tells after: sentences 149, twoAnds 0, notXItIsY 0, heightOpeners 0, wayIsTo 0, thisIsTheEnergy 0, systemWord 0, threeCommas 17, long30 8. lint:prose: no banned words on this card, no stem pair involving it (the BODY entry shared "the body gives this energy to" with card 23; card 05 has a new entry).

## What was changed

- CODE reading: cut "the strength in you is whole, and" (the I Ching Reading carries "You are strong, the strength is ready"); cut the closing sentence "Rest where you are, stay with what is near you, and let what is coming find you" (the I Ching Reading carries the laid table, the ones who wait beside you, and the rain finding a rested person ready). Recorded under meta.side_by_side.
- Upper trigram: cut "you stay with it," from a list of three.
- Lower trigram: cut "you get up," and "you do not tire easily," from a list of four.
- Shadow: cut "the light will not change," from a list of three.
- Reactive nature: cut "plans get forced," from a list of three.
- The drive: split "…at the same times, and in you it runs…" into two sentences.
- Where it lives: "Its gift is the pause" became "What it brings is the pause" (gift is a system word; the reference entry's own line was "you have the capacity to bring ease").
- Physiology: new entry, "Holding on is the body's way of waiting, and the sacral plexus is where it holds" (the old entry's first five words were shared with card 23; the reason is the reference entry's "the reason is holding on, said as the body's own waiting"). Split "a weave of nerves gathers, and out of it run…" into two sentences.
- Amino acid: "Anyone who has been in love knows what it does: the hours pass differently then, as if you had stepped out of time" became "When you are in love the hours pass differently, as if you had stepped out of time" (a life assigned to the reader; the source's own words are "When you are in love… Time will pass differently"). This is the one place a sentence was replaced rather than cut.

Cut: 7 (two sentences, five list items). Split: 2. Replaced: 3 (the pause, the Physiology entry, the in-love sentence).

## Suspect sentences, for the margin

Each: the sentence, its lens, and the source line it should have carried.

1. "Certainty is what it becomes when it is carried well." CODE. Nearest source: practical-05, "Heaven has set the course for an outcome in your favor, but patience is required"; Wilhelm Judgement, "If you are sincere, you have light and success." Unsure whether a reader can test "certainty" against a life; the source's claim is that the outcome is set, not that the person becomes certain.

2. "Act from resolve and the timing holds. Act from hurry and everything lands a beat off, however good the plan was." CODE. Source: gene-key-05, "There is a vast difference between acting out of impatience and acting out of resolve"; 64ways, "The timing will be out." Not empty, but the Shadow says the same thing in the same words: "everything begun from it lands a beat off, because action out of agitation is never the same as action out of resolve." One of the two is a repeat. Not cut, because the side-by-side test is CODE against I Ching only; the meta.mechanism line shows the writer allotted it to both.

3. "Water is the deep, and what the deep does is go on without seeing." ICHING, Upper trigram. Source: oracle-05-attending, KAN "stream, flowing water… Actions: venture and fall." "Go on without seeing" is the lore's gloss (meta.lore names it), not the packet's; the next sentence explains it, so it may be fine.

4. "In the body this energy is the head and the ear. The head stays up and clear while the rest of you is made to sit. The ear hears the change before the eye sees it, so stop watching the sky and listen." ICHING, Reading. The head and ear are Shuogua lore (meta.lore). Nearest packet line for listening: practical-05 line 4, "This is not the time for speaking; this is the time for listening"; oracle-05-attending line 4, "Yielding uses hearkening." No source found for "the ear hears the change before the eye sees it"; that mechanism is the writer's.

5. "Stop pushing, be careful, be courteous, and give the trouble nothing to hold." ICHING, Line 3. Source: oracle-05-attending, "Respectful consideration, not destroying"; Cleary Taoist, "It should be resolved." Four imperatives in a row read as a list; left because it is the counsel, not examples, and no cut was clearly right.

6. "It feels like drive, it gets used as drive, and everything begun from it lands a beat off, because action out of agitation is never the same as action out of resolve." KEYS, Shadow. Source: gene-key-05, "impatience can be viewed… as a positive trait, since it can goad you into action… This is a mistaken impression since impatience is rooted in agitation and all action arising from agitation is out of harmony with the whole." Sourced; listed as the other half of item 2.

7. "Feel the restlessness instead of obeying it and it sinks on its own." KEYS, Shadow. Source: gene-key-05, "The more you become aware of your own impatience and restlessness, the deeper you begin to sink into your true centre." The source has the person sinking into their centre; the card has the restlessness sinking. Small drift, unsure whether it matters.

8. "Under it is the plain sense that life keeps its own time, and that you are inside that time, not outside it waiting to be let in." KEYS, Shadow. Source: gene-key-05, "Everything is always exactly as it should be. What has happened is that you have fallen out of your natural state, the state of trust." The "inside, not outside waiting to be let in" is the writer's picture; a reader may or may not be able to say it in other words.

9. "Nothing has changed and everything is easier." KEYS, Gift. Nearest source: gene-key-05, "Patience smoothes rough edges, keeps your heart and mind open and makes life seem simple and easy"; 64ways, "Then our life will start to become simple once again, all on its own." Suspect: it sounds like a wisdom line and a reader may not be able to check it.

10. "Patience is trust when it is not being practised." KEYS, Gift. Source: gene-key-05, "Patience is about Trust. These two words have very similar meanings"; "Patience is the natural ground of your being, whereas impatience arises out of fear and conditioning." Unsure: it compresses two source lines into something close to a riddle.

11. "Everything alive is waiting, and a life can be seen as one long wait for its end; from here that is not sad, only true." KEYS, Siddhi. Source: gene-key-05, "All beings in life are waiting. Life itself can be seen as a waiting for death." "Not sad, only true" is the writer's addition; no source found for it.

12. "The strength that stood under the sky is still there, and so is the sky. The rain is falling on the field." KEYS, Siddhi. No source found; this is the I Ching scene resolved as the section's last picture. Unsure whether a reader who has only the KEYS panel can say what it means.

13. "An open engine needs a rhythm more than a fixed one does; it will not make one on its own." DESIGN, Where it lives. Nearest source: center-sacral, Open Sacral grapples, "Timing can be a challenge… If you have an Open Sacral, you do not have this gift" and "You need more rest." No source found for the stronger claim that an open engine needs a rhythm more than a fixed one; it reads as an inference.

14. "Whether the other half sits in you or in someone you live beside, where the two meet the day stops being a timetable and becomes weather with a tide under it." DESIGN, What completes it. Source: channel-5-15, "an unusual rhythm that's both consistent and unpredictable"; _reference-principles, "you carry half of a working circuit and are drawn… to people who carry the other half." Sourced; unsure whether "weather with a tide under it" is a picture the reader decodes.

15. "Patience felt deep in your body does the same thing more slowly." BODY, Amino acid. Source: gene-key-05 250-256, "Patience, when really experienced deep within the body, leads to the opening of your heart and the consequent turning on of a higher code within your DNA." "More slowly" is not in the source; "the same thing" leans on the in-love sentence before it.

16. "It opens your heart, and the opened heart lets the light in." BODY, Amino acid. Source: gene-key-05 236-243 and 250-256. The first paragraph already says "an open heart draws it in", so the section ends by saying what it said; left because the last sentence is the one that carries the patience line.

17. The entrance (meta.centre): "Every hour spent checking is taken from the hour you are ready for, so keep your meals, keep your sleep, keep company with people who can sit through a silence beside you." A list of three, 32 words, three commas. Left untouched because the entrances were rewritten as a deck-wide pass in PR #211 and the entrance rule says it is written from the finished card; flagging it here for Adrian.

## Least sure

The new Physiology entry. "Holding on is the body's way of waiting, and the sacral plexus is where it holds" carries the reference entry's join in its own words, but it is a new sentence on a section Adrian read closely on card 1, and "where it holds" may read as a hand gripping until the next sentences place it.
