# Card 56, The Wanderer: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "That is not a fault in you; it is the door." CODE. Riddle; "the door" to what is left for the reader.

2. "Fire is light that clings to what it burns, and what fire does is spread." ICHING, Upper trigram. Voice with nothing inside; the sentences after it carry the trigram.

3. "You have let it pierce you over and over until there is nothing left to defend." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement; nothing a reader could check.

4. "You are distracted still, by nothing but what is luminous, drunk on a love that pours from your own heart." KEYS, Siddhi. Voice; a life assigned.

5. "The mouth takes life in deeper than any other sense; a kiss gives and takes at once." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement with no source in view.

6. "What the stop does in you is know exactly when enough is enough." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the stop a reason; the codon fact before it is the checkable thing, and this turns it into the energy.

7. "It is a seal on the five senses, the places where the life drains out of you, and as they are sealed a flame grows inside you that needs nothing from outside to burn." BODY, Amino acid. Picture with no body fact under it.

8. "It is the middle of three such stops in you, the one that shows how the forgetting happens, out through the senses." BODY, Amino acid. Riddle; the three stops are never explained and "the forgetting" is never named.

9. "What your body does as the leaks close is turn its energy inward, toward the flame." BODY, Amino acid. No fact here to carry; the Gift already said it in plain words.
