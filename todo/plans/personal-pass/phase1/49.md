# Card 49, Revolution: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "This force was once what let a human being kill; that is how strong it is, and at its best it is turned only on what has already died." CODE, third paragraph of the second block. A pronouncement about the force's history; no fact here a reader could check against a life.

2. "Lake is the open face of still water, and what it does is open." ICHING, Upper trigram. Voice with nothing inside; the next two sentences carry what this one gestures at.

3. "It has nothing of its own to stand on, so it holds to what it burns until that is gone." ICHING, Lower trigram. A picture the reader must decode (fire clings). Would need to say what the clinging is in a life.

4. "Change like the tiger, all the way through, and let the stripes speak." ICHING, Line 5. The picture said again as advice; the sentence before it already carries the fact.

5. "The revolution is done; stay upright where you stand, and let what you are do the rest of the work." ICHING, Line 6. "Let what you are do the rest" is voice; it would need to say what the deep one does now that the face-changers do not.

6. "The pattern was built into the bond from the first day, or there would have been no fire to light it." KEYS, Shadow. A pronouncement about every bond; no fact here to carry.

7. "That leap is this: it comes because it must, and still someone has to make it." KEYS, Siddhi. A riddle. Would need the source's fact about what makes the leap out of the circle.

8. "It was love that had not finished arriving." KEYS, Siddhi. A life assigned to the reader as a phrase; no fact here to carry.

9. "Two people who love each other were never two." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle.

10. "Between the one and the other moves the awareness, a third there from the start." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle; the "third" is never named in plain words.

11. "No one is cut off from anyone." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement.

12. "You are more yourself than ever, and you can no longer hide." KEYS, Siddhi. Voice with nothing inside.

13. "A principle on its own is a cut with no heart." DESIGN, What completes it. Riddle; the plain version is in the sentence after it.

14. "It is where you lose your centre, because it is your centre." BODY, Physiology. A riddle built on the word centre; no fact here to carry.

15. "The change this energy makes begins in your body, and it needs heat: the hotter it runs, the faster it goes." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the molecule a reason; no source line in view.

16. "What it rebuilds in you it builds from what was already there, out of the ashes of the old." BODY, Amino acid. Picture with no body fact under it.

- "Its risk is rising too early, under water not yet warm, and going out." ICHING, Lower trigram. Picture to decode; the fact (acting before the moment) is there if the reader works.
