# Card 31, Influence: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Read once, judged as read. No card edited.

## Suspect sentences

1. "What you say after that carries more than you." CODE, third paragraph. A riddle; it would need what the words carry after the fall (the room's feeling rather than your need to be right).
2. "Let it, because a back that nothing moves moves nobody." ICHING, Line 5. Wordplay standing in for the line's counsel; it would need what the back is (the part of you moved without being caught).
3. "From here nobody is doing anything, so there is no one to be proud of and no one to influence." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
4. "You leave the world exactly as it is." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked; no fact here to carry.
5. "What speaks then speaks through you. Whatever it says is right, because the words come from everyone and go back to everyone." KEYS, Siddhi. A pronouncement; "whatever it says is right" is a verdict no life can test.
6. "Your name has gone with the rest." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
7. "It is quiet down here, and the ones still standing pity you, not knowing the fall was the whole point." KEYS, Siddhi. A scene the reader must decode; "the whole point" is a pronouncement.
8. "The sentence that leaves you before you have chosen it was made where the colour of your eyes was made." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule, said as a riddle; the fact under it (tyrosine goes into the pigment of the eye and into what wakes you) is fine on its own.
9. "The words you follow and the words you lead with are chemistry before they are meaning, one substance, the same one the part of you that names things runs on." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason.
10. "Cut an apple and leave it: the browning is the kin of what made your colour, still at work in the open air, as a word is once it has left you." BODY, Amino acid. A real check with a moral pinned to its tail.

## Said twice

- Saying what the room felt is said four times. CODE, second paragraph: "what you say is what the room already felt, put into words it could not find, so the people who follow you are following themselves." KEYS, Gift: "You have an ear for what a crowd is feeling before it has found the words, and you say it plainly." DESIGN, The drive: "to say what the group has felt but not yet said" and "the ones who follow are following what you heard in them". DESIGN, Where it lives: "saying what the whole of it was feeling."
- CODE, second paragraph: "wrong sometimes, down sometimes, back on their feet with the heart still open." CODE, third paragraph: "Get up with the heart still open." KEYS, Gift: "The one who is followed falls, gets up, and keeps the heart open."

- CODE, third paragraph: "Wait until you are asked." DESIGN, The drive: "it works only when the call comes from outside you". DESIGN, Where it lives: "Left silent until asked".

## Unsure

1. "Carried in balance, this power does not believe its own speech." CODE, second paragraph. Decodable, but the reader has to do it.
2. "The ones who carry this well are the most human in the room" CODE, second paragraph. "Most human" is voice; the rest of the sentence carries it.
3. "Reach out to draw someone in and what comes back is exactly the size of the reaching." ICHING, Reading. Near a riddle, though a reader could test it.
4. "Give more than you are given, which is how you come to have more." ICHING, Reading. A pronouncement from the classical source.
5. "There is strain inside every attraction, two grounds leaning toward each other; it is what carries the feeling across, so do not smooth it away." ICHING, Reading. The picture is the hexagram's; the reader has to hold it to get the counsel.
6. "You will be caught out, and something that was shut will open." KEYS, Shadow. "Something that was shut" is unnamed.
7. "The words are only the means; what moves people is what stands behind them, and a thing said at the right hour reaches far past what it says." KEYS, Gift. "What stands behind them" is unnamed.
8. "When the heart speaks, the words come out in order before you have thought what they mean." BODY, Amino acid. Opens on a claim about the reader with the substance not yet named.
