# Card 25, Innocence: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Read once, judged as read. No card edited.

## Suspect sentences

1. "Everything alive hears it and is for a moment what it was before it learned to pretend." ICHING, Combination. A picture to decode; it would need to say plainly that the shock strips the act off.
2. "Let the step be as plain as it rose, and the sky will not stand in its way." ICHING, Reading. The sky stands for the head and the reader must work that out; no fact here to carry.
3. "What is true at the root stays true the whole way up." ICHING, Line 1. A pronouncement; it would need the line's own fact, that a start with nothing false in it succeeds.
4. "there is an air of another world about you while your feet stay on the ground." CODE, second paragraph. Voice with nothing inside; it would need what people actually notice about you.
5. "You have to slow down to do it, and the day tastes different." CODE, second paragraph. "Tastes different" cannot be checked; no fact here to carry.
6. "Fixing it is not the answer." CODE, third paragraph. A pronouncement with no "it" named until the next sentence; it would need the tightness named.
7. "Life keeps testing it, and each time it goes deeper." KEYS, Gift. "It" and "deeper" carry nothing; it would need the source's layers, each acceptance uncovering the next place you do not.
8. "It is the thing that holds everything together, and from here you cannot say you love anyone more than anything else, because the same warmth goes to all of it." KEYS, Siddhi. First clause is a pronouncement no life can test; the rest is fine.
9. "what remains is a knowing in the cells that everything is one." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
10. "Every time someone comes through to this, a knot loosens a little in everyone, in people not yet born." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked; it would need the source's claim about the lineage said as what it is.
11. "What waits on the far side of that fear is the strangest thing this pull gives: to stand not on an identity at all, but on being love itself." DESIGN, Where it lives. Riddle; no fact here to carry.
12. "The tightness you were born into comes in layers, wound close, the way the thread that carries what you are is wound in every cell, and the layers are relaxed one at a time, deep inside you, until the flow of love is moving in you again." BODY, Amino acid. A picture (arginine in the wound thread of the cell) the reader must decode, and a reason the source did not give the molecule.
13. "What you are accepting is in your body, wound into it, and it loosens as your body does." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason, restated.

## Unsure

1. "It moves the way weather moves, with force and without a plan, the first thunder of the year rolling under an open sky." CODE, first paragraph. The thunder is the I Ching's furniture spent in the reading; the weather half is checkable.
2. "Love opens the way a season does, in its own time, once nothing in you is holding it shut." CODE, third paragraph. Ends by saying what the paragraph already meant.
3. "In you it is the part that keeps going without a lie in it, straight because straight is easier." ICHING, Upper trigram. "Straight because straight is easier" can be tested, but it reads as a phrase.
4. "Its risk is drought, a force so sure of itself that it burns what it was warming." ICHING, Upper trigram. Picture first, meaning never quite given.
5. "What comes up like that is right in a way no plan can be, because it was not made to get anything." ICHING, Reading. Large claim; the reason clause may carry it.
6. "Hold to what you are, which was never something you had to make, and the moment passes clean." ICHING, Line 4. "Passes clean" is voice.
7. "Fear is what the grip is made of, and fear is clever." KEYS, Shadow. The next sentence earns "clever"; on its own it is a phrase.
8. "The tightness is not the enemy. It is the start of the way, and turning toward it instead of away is the whole of the beginning." KEYS, Shadow. A matched pair set up to be knocked down; "the start of the way" needs decoding.
9. "Letting what is there be there is this energy at its full, and it is a seeing rather than a method." KEYS, Gift. "Seeing rather than a method" is a distinction the reader may not be able to test.
10. "taking up no more room in the world than its place, no less either." DESIGN, The drive. Near a riddle.
11. "That is what this love is like from inside, a warmth that goes to everything because that is the only way it goes." BODY, Physiology. The join said as equivalence.
12. "It does not have to be told. It only has to stop being held." BODY, Physiology. Two fragments that sound like wisdom; the sentence before already said it.
