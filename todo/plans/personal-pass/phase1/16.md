## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "Where it is missing nothing is wrong, and nothing moves." CODE. Compressed to the edge of a riddle; a reader has to supply that the plans stay on the table. Source: gene-key-16, "This 16th Shadow can be absolutely full of plans and good intentions about the world, but they rarely get off the ground"; "indifference cannot overcome inertia because it doesn't feel strongly enough to do anything about it."

2. "Carried well, this energy begins before it feels ready." CODE. Unsure, because the I Ching Reading two panels down says "prepare, then go" and "Think beforehand, lay in what you need, study the mark before you let the arrow go", and a reader will feel the two pull against each other; DESIGN reconciles them ("pack the bag, then jump") but the reading and the I Ching do not. Source: gene-key-16, "The truth is that you are always ready. You are ready right now."

3. "Nobody is coming to help you begin; that part is done alone, and it is the part that costs." CODE. The first half is sourced; "it is the part that costs" is not, and the sentence sits against the I Ching's "setting helpers in place and setting out with them". Source: gk-64ways-16, "No one's ever going to come to help us. This is something we have to do ourself. Alone."

4. "The caring has to be kept alive, because the flat days come." CODE. Mine, the new opener. Source: gk-64ways-16, "Humanity is all about caring"; gene-key-16, "What is lacking in the lives of those in the grip of this Shadow is one vital factor, enthusiasm."

5. "Far from draining you, waking up floods you with purpose." CODE. Mine. Source: gk-64ways-16, "Waking up isn't draining though; it's the opposite. It gives us vast energy"; "We'll be flooded with purpose."

6. "Then do not hesitate, because a strength like this spoils if it is held." ICHING, Reading. "Spoils if it is held" is the card's reason, not the source's; the sources say only not to delay. Source: practical-16, "Renounce hesitation"; line-16-2, "Do not let even a day pass in hesitation."

7. "Your foot is where the body feels this." ICHING, Reading. Mine. The foot is Shuogua lore (meta.lore); no packet line.

8. "Turn back to your own joy quickly, and there is less of it." ICHING, Line 3. "Less of it" reads as less joy; it means less regret, and the reader has to go back a sentence to find that. Source: cleary-taoist (line-16-3), "The way to repent is to do so quickly once it is realized that there is no benefit in looking to others; then one can be without regret."

9. "There is safety in numbers and nothing else in them." KEYS, Shadow. The second half is a flourish on the source's phrase, a pair set up to be knocked down. Source: gk-64ways-16, "There is safety in numbers. If no one else is really bothering, why should I?"

10. "Then the strange thing happens." KEYS, Gift. Says nothing on its own; a stage direction before the sentence that does the work. Source it introduces: gene-key-16, "it is at this key stage of transcendence that the Gift of Versatility engages."

11. "The greatest player can still improve; here the improving is over, because the one who played has gone and left the playing." KEYS, Siddhi. "The one who played has gone and left the playing" has to be decoded. Source: gene-key-16, "Even the greatest pianist can still improve and expand. However, with the 16th Siddhi of Mastery the whole game of expanding or evolving ... is over"; "your identity is erased and you become an empty vessel for life."

12. "Every piece ever written for the piano is already in the body of everyone alive, and from here it could be played by someone who has never touched the keys." KEYS, Siddhi. The source's claim, carried flat; a reader cannot test it against a life, and it is the largest claim on the card. Source: gene-key-16, "the kinaesthetic knowledge of how to play this piece is actually already contained in every single human being alive ... you could play this piece of music as well as any piano maestro who ever lived without ever having touched a piano in your life."

13. "It enters at Gate 16: the zest that says yes before the details are in, the love of learning that dives to the bottom of a subject, the wish to share what you can do." DESIGN, The drive. Three things in a row after the colon; left because each is a sourced facet of the gate rather than an example, but it reads as a list. Source: qw-gate-16, "enthusiasm, zest, and the spark that wants to share your passions, talents, and ideas with the world ... that electric joy of learning, mastering something you love."

14. "That fear sends you deeper." DESIGN, What completes it. Mine, from a cut. Source: channel-16-48, "this fear can also be a driving force for them to delve deeper, perfect their skills, and strive for excellence."

15. "The move that comes easily in you is coming out of years, and the day nothing moves is the day nothing is drawn." BODY, Physiology. The join said as an equivalence (the frozen day as the day the gland draws nothing), which the brief names as the shape that failed on card 1; the organ entry proposes the line, so I left it, unsure. Source: body/organs/parathyroid.md, "the move that comes easily is coming out of years, and the frozen day is the day nothing is drawn."
