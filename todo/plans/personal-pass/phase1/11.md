## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "That picture is what lights you." CODE. "Lights" stands in for the source's three verbs and a reader cannot say what it means beyond "it matters to you". Source: gk-64ways-11, "Our ideal energises us. It illuminates us. It enlightens us. It gets us out of bed in the morning with enthusiasm."

2. "What you were looking for was already in you, waiting to be let out." CODE. Sounds like wisdom; a reader cannot test "already in you". It now carries the paragraph's "not in the next thing" after the cut above. Source: gene-key-11 p13, "The light has never been outside us but resides deep within matter itself"; p5, "the dream that has been hidden inside you for so long shakes you awake."

3. "It is not a plan to be built as drawn; it is what draws you upward." CODE. The second hinge by meaning (the script counts only the full-stop form). Left because the pair is the source's own. Source: gk-64ways-11, "the ideal itself is not something literal and personal ... our ideal is simply the carrot we use that draws our frequency upwards."

4. "In you it is the part that can receive a thing, hold it, and let it become what it is going to be." ICHING, Upper trigram. "Let it become what it is going to be" is loose; a reader would restate it as "let it be" and could check nothing. Source: oracle-11, Kun's action "yield, SHUN: give way, comply, agree, follow; bear produce, nourish, provide."

5. "It is the first month of spring, with half a year of growing ahead, and it came because you walked the last stretch carefully." ICHING, Reading. The second half tells the reader what they did, which is near the assigned-life line. Source: oracle-11, Preceding Situation, "Treading and-also compenetrating. Therefore afterwards peace"; Legge, "the first month of the natural spring, when for six months ... the processes of growth will be going on."

6. "It lasts longer for the one who braces it: who spends what they have to keep the meeting open, who bears with what is rough and unfinished, who does not forget the ones far off or close the circle round a few." Source: oracle-11, Patterns of Wisdom, "The crown prince uses property to accomplish heaven and earth's dao. And braces heaven and earth's proper reciprocity"; Legge line 2.

7. "Notice where you are holding yourself now." ICHING, Reading. An instruction with nothing in it; a reader cannot say what to notice. No source found. 8. "Here is the border where sky touches ground, and the tide is about to turn." ICHING, Line 3. The tide is a second picture the sources do not give; the border is theirs. 9. "What was solid has thinned, and force will not thicken it." ICHING, Line 4. Source: oracle-11, comment on Six at fourth, "Altogether letting-go the substance indeed"; Cleary Buddhist, "Although one has no real true ability or power to effect order, still if one relies on those with the same aspiration, it is thereby possible to prevent disastrous confusion."
