# Card 30, The Clinging Fire: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Read once, judged as read. No card edited.

## Suspect sentences

1. "That one you can trust." CODE, third paragraph. A pronouncement about a wanting the reader has not been shown; it would need what the one wanting is (the Siddhi says it: the longing to be done with suffering).
2. "the hunger is not there to keep you safe, it is there to drive you into every experience there is, so that something larger than you can learn from your mistakes." KEYS, Shadow. The last clause cannot be checked against a life; it would need the source's claim said as what it is or cut.
3. "Life is playing its game through you." KEYS, Gift. No fact here to carry.
4. "Real lightness is never afraid of being overwhelmed. Only the pretended kind is." KEYS, Gift. A matched pair set up to be knocked down; no fact here to carry.
5. "You become your longing. That is what belonging is: being your longing entirely, and nowhere else." KEYS, Siddhi. Wordplay in place of a thing; no fact here to carry.
6. "From here the fire is bliss, burning you over and over." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
7. "The smallest thing sets you alight, and whoever comes near catches it, because the heat is carried outside the body now." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked; and "whoever comes near catches it" is also card 29's Siddhi.
8. "There is nobody left in there that wanting could cling to." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
9. "What is left in you after a wanting is followed is partly this chemistry, a heaviness the next day." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule.
10. "Your fire burns to purify, and the same substance is in the part of you that listens as in the part that burns: one chemistry that lets go of wanting by burning through it." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason, as a riddle.
11. "As the longing grows the body begins to fill with light, not by anything you do, only as the fire burns." BODY, Amino acid. No fact here to carry; Body is the floor of the card and this is its ceiling.

## Said twice

- CODE, first paragraph: "the wanting that lives in a body before it has found anything to want. It was in you at birth, with no instruction for it." KEYS, Shadow: "You were made as a creature that yearns, and the yearning was in you before any object was, so the object is never the point."
- CODE, third paragraph: "then it is back, because it was never for the thing it said it was for." KEYS, Gift: "When you do follow one, you find that getting the thing was not what you were after."
- CODE, third paragraph: "until one wanting is left under all the others." KEYS, Siddhi: "Rapture is what wanting becomes when only one want is left."
- CODE, third paragraph: "It cannot be escaped, only accepted, the way being mortal is." KEYS, Gift: "The wanting cannot be beaten, nor can it be dropped".
- ICHING, Combination: "A flame has no shape of its own; it clings to the thing it burns". ICHING, Reading: "it lives by clinging, and it takes the shape of whatever it clings to."
- ICHING, Lower trigram: "the fire that comes all at once, burns what it touches and dies, leaving nothing it could stay on." ICHING, Line 4: "It burns what it lands on, then there is no place for it."

## Unsure

1. "It is where a life gets its heat." CODE, first paragraph. A picture standing for a claim; a reader may be able to test it.
2. "Let it be felt all the way, lightly, and it burns off what it was feeding on, until one wanting is left under all the others." CODE, third paragraph. "Burns off what it was feeding on" must be decoded.
3. "One light after another, that is how it lasts: the brightness handed on, never held." ICHING, Reading. Classical turn, near a riddle.
4. "It is not for anything." KEYS, Shadow. A fragment pronouncement the next sentence explains.
5. "The wanting to be one with God and the wanting to destroy an enemy are the same fire wearing two faces." KEYS, Shadow. The source's claim; a reader may not be able to check it.
6. "A life spent on every desire belongs to desire." KEYS, Reactive nature. Aphorism; the sentence before carries the fact.
7. "From then on there is a glint in your eye." KEYS, Gift. Voice, though people do see it.
8. "Feeling here rises wanting to become a vision, and a vision wanting to become a fire you can live by." DESIGN, The drive. A chain to decode.
9. "Passion fed by devotion lasts, and passion fed by depletion burns out." DESIGN, The drive. "Fed by depletion" does not parse.
10. "You take it in from meat, fish or eggs" BODY, Amino acid. Three examples, and the body makes glutamine for itself; the brief asks whether the body makes it or must be given it, and this sentence implies the wrong answer.
11. "The wanting in you is a fire in the body, and when you give in to it, it takes all of you, the body first." BODY, Amino acid. Opens the amino paragraph on a claim, with the substance not yet named.
