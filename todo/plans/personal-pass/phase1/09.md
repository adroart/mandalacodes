## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "In a life this is the long stretch made of small acts, the errands, the same tasks every day, which either add up or do not." CODE. "Which either add up or do not" says nothing; the source says which. Source: gk-64ways-09, "They focus on small goals, small successes, and lots of small successes add up to victory."

2. "Do a thing with your heart in it, then do it again, and a groove forms that the rest of your life begins to run down on its own." CODE. Said again, nearly word for word, in the Gift: "a thing done with heart, then done again, until the energy of your life follows the track on its own." One line, two sections. Source: gene-key-09 p7, "This gift creates a groove and once that groove is dug, all energy in your life will tend to follow the same pattern."

3. "Look up at the whole mountain and the knees go weak. Look down at your feet, and one step is small enough to take, with the next one easier." CODE. The mountain and the weak knees are on the card three times (here, the Shadow's "the mountain is far too high, the knees go weak", the Gift's "The mind shows you the whole mountain at once and the knees go weak just looking"). Source: gk-64ways-09, "The very looking at it makes our knees feel weak."; "if we look down at our feet, we can take that first step."

4. "Its risk is thinness, a touch so light that nothing is held at all, only weather." ICHING, Upper trigram. "Only weather" has to be decoded. No source found for thinness in the packet; the lore block credits the Eranos fields for SUN, whose fields read "base on which things rest, ground, foundation; mild, subtly penetrating; nourishing" and say nothing of a risk.

5. "Everything needed is present, the charge is high, and still it does not fall, because the moment has not come; forced, it would scatter." ICHING, Reading. "Forced, it would scatter" has no line behind it. Nearest: practical-09, "Passivity has taming power ... It is a period to wait and see."; Legge, "The dense clouds do not empty their stores."

6. "In the body this energy is the thighs under the head, the strength that walks, checked by what is thought above it." ICHING, Reading. Unsure, and I think reversed. The lore gives Wind the thighs and Heaven the head; in this figure Wind is above and Heaven below, so the thighs sit over the head, and the strength being checked is Heaven (the head), checked by Wind (the thighs). The sentence puts the strength in the thighs and the check in the head. Source: meta.lore, "the Shuogua body parts in the Reading (Wind the thighs, Heaven the head)"; oracle-09, "Wind moves above heaven."

7. "The obstacle is small; the pushing is what has stopped everything." ICHING, Line 3. "The pushing" is inferred. Source: practical-09, "Yet only minor impediments are frustrating your progress."; Cleary Taoist, "When strength acts on its own and one is self-centered ... sure to end in ruination of strength."

8. "Looked at from inside the circle, the mountain is far too high, the knees go weak, and the question of what the point is arrives on time." KEYS, Shadow. "Arrives on time" is a flourish on the source's plain line. Source: gk-64ways-09, "What's the point even trying, we think."

9. "The mountain is not the trouble; the looking up is." KEYS, Shadow. The card's one hinge, kept because it carries the source's own turn; noted so it is counted. Source: gk-64ways-09, "The Dilemma of this Gene Key is perspective. If we're looking up at the whole mountain, we'll feel overwhelmed."

10. "Nothing in them chose this; the will has frozen along the old grooves, and from outside it looks like stubbornness." KEYS, Repressive. "Looks like stubbornness" is inferred from the onlookers' frustration. Source: gene-key-09 p5, "The reluctance to move out of one's patterns is not a conscious choice but an inner dynamic where all one's life force remains frozen ... It can be frustrating to onlookers that these people are unable to break their patterns."

11. "Determination is one step, taken." KEYS, Gift. Compressed to the edge; a reader may not know what "taken" is doing. Source: gk-64ways-09, "We take a first step. Actually, the first step is the hardest."

12. "The word sounds like force, gritted teeth, a long war of will, and it is closer to the opposite." KEYS, Gift, and "The word sounds like an angel with a sword, and the thing is a child, soaked to the knees, running into a field of golden mist because she asked if she could." KEYS, Siddhi. Two heights open their turn on "The word sounds like". Both are the source's own move, so left; unsure whether it reads as a formula on the page. Source: gk-64ways-09, "Determination may be a misunderstood term. It sounds like it involves a great deal of power, or force, or discipline, or willpower."; "Invincibility. It sounds like a great Archangel wielding a great power."

13. "Small acts done with heart gather a momentum that stops being stoppable, and one morning you are halfway up without having noticed the climb." KEYS, Gift. "Stops being stoppable" is the phrase I was least sure of. Source: gene-key-09 p7, "All those little acts done with heart begin to build up an inner momentum that eventually becomes unstoppable."; gk-64ways-09, "one day we'll find ourself halfway up the mountain."

14. "Nothing that delicate can be defeated, because there is nothing in it to defeat; what has let go of its edges is defenceless and unbeatable at once." KEYS, Siddhi. A riddle a reader cannot test. Source: gene-key-09 p10, "the moment something loses all boundaries, it becomes two contrary things ... simultaneously defenceless and invincible."; gk-64ways-09, "it isn't really weak, it's just so delicate."

15. "The smaller a thing becomes, the more is packed inside it, until the very small opens into the boundless." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked against a life. Source: gene-key-09 p9, "the smaller something becomes, the more condensed is the universal energy within it ... Thus the infinitesimal becomes the boundless."

16. "All that ever was can sit in a palm, the size of a hazelnut, and the step you are taking is that small." KEYS, Siddhi. The hazelnut must be decoded before it works. Source: gk-64ways-09, "a tiny object before her, the size of a hazelnut ... 'it is all that ever was, and all that ever shall be'."

17. "Light brought to one point burns through anything, which is why a person living here can be hard to stand near: it lights up whatever you hoped not to see." KEYS, Siddhi. Sourced, but the same picture is spent again in BODY ("Here it is light brought to one point. A fine enough beam goes through anything"). Source: gene-key-09 p10, "like a laser that can pinpoint a very specific aspect of life ... An ordinary person may find the aura of such a person almost unbearable, since it spotlights those aspects of your shadow nature that you most fear."

18. "In you it is the pull to narrow down, to find the few details that serve the larger thing, letting the rest fall away." DESIGN, The drive. Close to ICHING's "Tend the details that serve the larger thing" by meaning. Source: gene-key-09 p2, "you tame the small by applying your energy only to that which serves your higher purpose."

19. "People who carry this all their lives learn to hold the single step and the whole direction at once, the tree with the wood, letting neither erase the other." DESIGN, The drive. "The wood" for a forest is one country's word; "the trees and the forest" is everyone's. Source: qw-gate-09, "This energy teaches you to see both the trees and the forest."

20. "Their gift is the pause, the reading of when a thing is finished, which is the very thing a fixed engine cannot do for itself." DESIGN, Where it lives. Unsure, and possibly against the reference: the Sacral entry says the fixed engine can answer whether it is finished or only paused, if asked as a yes or no. Source: _reference-sacral, "Its gift is the pause. An open engine reads other people's energy better than they read it themselves, knows when to stop"; and "Whether you are finished or only paused is a question this place can answer, if it is asked as a yes or a no."

21. "In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time." DESIGN, Where it lives. Not suspect in itself; noted because it opens the second paragraph of Where it lives on cards 03, 05, 09, 27, 29, 34 and 42 with the same words. Below lens-entry level, so the lint does not see it.

22. "When your will is moving, the day has somewhere to go; when it stands, everything in you stands with it." BODY, Physiology. A matched pair by shape, modelled on the liver line in the brief; no source line found for either half. Nearest: body/organs/sacral-plexus.md, "the will that keeps going", and the join, "the sacral plexus is where the body keeps going without being watched."

23. "Beyond the building, it sets how your cells take light in and turn it into something you can use." BODY, Amino acid. This is card 05's line, and card 05 says it ("it sets in you how your cells take light in and turn it into something you can use"); card 11 says it a third way. The threonine entry gives card 09 the four unlocking time and light and the laser, and says card 5 alone carries the cells taking in light. Left for Adrian because it is a cut of a sibling's line, not one of the five things. Source: body/amino-acids/threonine.md, "Card 5 ... what the source says it does: it is the code in you for how your cells take light in and turn it into something you can use (key 5, 232-236) ... Card 9 ... Its line is the family unlocking time and light together, and the 9th as the laser-like focusing of light (key 9, 358-364; key 11, 45-47)."

24. "A fine enough beam goes through anything, and that is what your focus is doing in you when it lands." BODY, Amino acid. The beam going through anything is physics put in as the reason; the source gives only the laser as focus. Source: gene-key-09 p10, "The Taming Power of the Small is like a laser that can pinpoint a very specific aspect of life, bringing enormous power to bear on that area."; gene-key-11 45-47 via the entry, "the 9th focuses light like a laser."
