## Suspect sentences

2. "Work within what is fixed, and inside it you are without limit." ICHING, Line 4. Riddle. Would need to carry: a limit accepted stops being felt as one.

3. "Let the regret turn you, and it disappears." ICHING, Line 6. "It" could be the regret or the rule; the sentence is a riddle either way. Would need to carry: the fault is small and mends the moment the rule is loosened.

4. "That is realism." KEYS, Gift. Voice; names the section. No fact here to carry.

6. "From here nobody is punished, because no separate person is left to have done the thing; the balance rights itself with no one keeping the books." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement. No fact here to carry.

7. "What loosens at this height are the laws themselves, time first, then the pull that holds matter together." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement no reader can check. No fact here to carry.

8. "Drop the past and you are light, in both senses of the word." KEYS, Siddhi. A pun in place of a fact. No fact here to carry.

9. "Every wall you ever built turns out to have been holding you up only until you could stand." KEYS, Siddhi. A life assigned, in a picture. Would need to carry: the structures were scaffolding, not the building.

10. "Its cost is intense." DESIGN, The drive. Voice; "intense" says nothing. No fact here to carry; the next two sentences carry the cost.

11. "Whether the other half is in you or in someone near, the new thing takes form only where the two meet." DESIGN, What completes it. The formula closer. Would need to carry: without Gate 3 the pressure holds and nothing changes.

12. "Three energies in you share this one substance; here it is at its heaviest, the plain material density of the earth, held as you are between an upward drive and the pull back down." 13. "The same substance is in the moment a thought breaks out of its habit, so what holds you and what frees you are one chemistry in you." BODY, Amino acid. The codon sibling decoded as a riddle; gives the molecule a reason. No fact here to carry.
