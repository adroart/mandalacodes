## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "Carried well, this energy knows what the wrong turns are for." CODE. The energy is made to know something; a reader cannot check what an energy knows. Source it should carry: practical-03, "Failures at the beginning, even repeated missteps, are blessings of strength."

2. "That is what a beginning is from the inside, fullness and not knowing in one breath." CODE. Says what the two sentences before it already said. It does have a source: oracle-03 Image Tradition, "Thunder and rain's stirring-up: plenitude overfilled. Heaven creates grasses' duskiness." Unsure whether it earns its place or is the restatement the brief cuts.

3. "It has no shape yet, and it is already the most real thing you carry." CODE. Unsure. "The most real thing you carry" can be tested against a life, but it is a large claim. Nearest source: practical-03, "You nurture within you a rising power."

4. "Settle it now and you settle it small." CODE. Compressed to the edge of a riddle; a reader has to work out that settling means deciding the shape. Source: line 5, "The small: Trial, significant. The great: Trial, pitfall."; qw-gate-03, "force innovation into a space that isn't yet ready."

5. "The store you began with is for one morning, the morning the ground gives, and a seed does not spend it early on the way there." CODE. Unsure: the seed's store is a picture the reader has to hold across three clauses before it is used. Source: practical-03, "for now, you must store your energy, build momentum slowly and steadily, and prepare for the sudden breakthrough."

6. "That is the picture of a newborn thing, firm where it grips, firm where it reaches, with nothing fixed in between." ICHING, The symbol. Unsure; it is the line structure read as a body, which is the section's job, but "firm where it reaches" is decoration. Source: Image Tradition, "Solid and supple begin to mingle and-also in heaviness generate."; Legge's note on lines 1 and 5.

7. "You can sit with what has no answer yet." ICHING, Upper trigram. "Sit with" is the wellness register, and the sentence adds nothing to the one before it. No source found beyond Kan's field, "critical time, test."

8. "In you it is the first move, the surge that says go before anything is ready, a courage that comes up from the gut rather than down from the head." ICHING, Lower trigram. Unsure of the gut and head; nothing in the Zhen field says either. Source: oracle-03, "thunder rising from the depth of the earth; fertilizing intrusion ... come out of the egg or the bud."

9. "Keep your strength in, and let it thicken until the rain comes." ICHING, Reading. "Thicken" is the one word I could not trace; the sap and the oil are line 5's. Source: practical-03, "store your energy, build momentum slowly and steadily"; line 5 field, "Juice, GAO: active essence; oil, grease."

10. "What is joined in its time has a house to keep it." ICHING, Line 2. "A house to keep it" must be decoded (the marriage, the children). Source: Legge, "After ten years she will be united, and have children."; Huang, "Hardship ends; a normal cycle returns."

11. "Seeking and going are one motion here, with light in it." ICHING, Line 4. Carries the classical comment nearly word for word and reads as a riddle for that reason. Source: oracle-03 comment on Six at fourth, "Seeking and-also going. Brightness indeed."; Huang, "Seeking what you want. Go ahead. There is light."

12. "What is rich in you is spreading and cannot shine." ICHING, Line 5. Same kind: the classical line kept, with "cannot shine" left for the reader to unpack. Source: comment on Nine at fifth, "Spreading, not-yet shining indeed."

13. "Grieve it honestly, without pretending the effort is alive." ICHING, Line 6. "Pretending the effort is alive" is not in any of the five readings of the line. Nearest: Cleary Taoist, "Weak and helpless, one is astride a horse but does not go forward, and has no whip in one's hand ... of what use is it in fact?"; Deng, "if we are too late, riding a team of horses is useless."

14. "Then begin again, smaller." ICHING, Line 6. No source found on line 6 itself; the small is line 5's ("Rectitude in small matters is good"), and the target hexagram 42 is Increase, not a smaller beginning. Cleary Taoist has "being small and incapable of greatness" as the fault, not the counsel.

15. "It is quieter than that, an unease under ordinary days, a low hum you stopped hearing years ago." KEYS, Shadow. "Years ago" is not in the source. Source: gk-64ways-03, "it's often very subtle. It's an undercurrent. It's an anxiety, a nervousness, a deep and hidden fear inside."

16. "What you are afraid of is called chaos, and it is not the storm you picture." KEYS, Shadow. The card's one "not X, it is Y", kept because it carries the source's own turn; noted so it is counted. Source: gk-64ways-03, "We often think of chaos as a hurricane or a tempest or something awful, but it's often very subtle."

17. "What you took for the ground going was something starting." KEYS, Shadow. Unsure: a reader has to decode "the ground going". Source: gene-key-03, "there never was any chaos. It was all simply the difficulty at the beginning."; "chaos is about beginnings."

18. "You can work while the ground moves." KEYS, Gift. A picture with nothing plain beside it. Source: gk-64ways-03, "The first thing about this 3rd Gift is that we have to be okay with chaos."

19. "Other people's hands end up in the work, and it is better for them." KEYS, Gift. "Them" could be the people or the work. Source: gene-key-03, "Innovation implies cooperation by its very nature. In order to mutate life to a higher order, you must integrate and synthesise."

20. "Home was never in the body or anywhere in the sky. It is the feeling of love itself, and you are in it." KEYS, Siddhi. A second matched pair by meaning (the tell script does not count it because of the lowercase). Left, because the pair is the source's own and the second half is the section's last fact. Source: gene-key-03, "Your true home is not located within your body, nor is there somewhere in the universe a single centre ... The centre you are looking for is the feeling of love itself."

21. "Everything you have begun since was begun the same way, on a store you were given, before you could do it for yourself." BODY, Physiology. Unsure: it is the join the reference entry prescribes, and it is a claim about every beginning in the reader's life, which is near the "assigns a life" line. Source: body/organs/navel.md join, "everything you have ever begun since was begun the same way, on a store you were given, before you could do it for yourself."

22. "There is more of it in you than of any other piece you are built from, about one in every ten, so it is in everything you are made of, the hands as much as the heart." BODY, Amino acid. "The hands as much as the heart" is not in the entry and reads as a flourish on a fact. Source: body/amino-acids/leucine.md, "There is more of it in your proteins than of any other piece, about one in ten."

23. "While this energy is in you, the change is what you feel." BODY, Amino acid. Says nothing a reader could check; it closes the section on a phrase. No source found. The nearest line is the one the previous sentence already spends: gene-key-03, "if you are not continually transcending, then you are dying."
