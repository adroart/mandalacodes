## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "Nobody can." CODE. A two-word fragment. It is the answer to the fear in the sentence before it and carries the source, so it was left; noted because Adrian read fragments as chopping. Source: gk-64ways-12, "we realise that other people can't touch us."

2. "The aloneness you were looking for was never aloneness. It was this, your own heart clean, depending on nothing outside you." CODE. A hinge by meaning (never / It was) the script does not count. Left because the turn is the source's own. Source: gk-64ways-12, "the aloneness we are seeking is really not aloneness but purity. It's the purity of our own heart and it doesn't depend on any external conditions."

3. "The shut is not coldness." CODE. "The shut" is a coined noun a reader has to accept before the sentence works; a paragraph earlier it was "the guard". Source: gk-64ways-12, "people with the 12th Gene Key can sometimes appear very collected and calm and yet there are deep seated boundaries within them."

4. "It comes with a guard, built early, which is what most people meet before they meet the heart." CODE. Unsure of "which is what most people meet": it says something about other people's experience of the reader, which the reader cannot check. Source: gk-64ways-12, "deep seated boundaries within them that probably evolved in early childhood. And these boundaries are designed to keep others away."

5. "On the outside of the situation that is how things look: firm, strong, in order, nothing showing." ICHING, Upper trigram. Four descriptors in a row; firm and strong are one word in the source. Left because it is the trigram's account, not a list of examples. Source: oracle-12, Image Tradition, "Inside supple and-also outside solid. Inside the small in the person and-also outside jun zi."

6. "None of it is your doing." ICHING, Reading. Unsure: the Judgement lays the block at the door of "people who are no people", and the practical guide at "the malice of others", so it is sourced, but it hands the reader an absolution some readers will not have earned. Source: oracle-12, "Obstruction's in-no-way people"; practical-12, "The malice of others creates an impasse."

7. "It is your turn to move and there is nowhere to move to; that is the whole of it." ICHING, Reading. "That is the whole of it" adds nothing a reader could restate. Source for the first half: practical-12, "You know it's your turn to move, but you don't know where to go. Feeling like a draw."

8. "Feel for it now: the face in order, the belly gone quiet under it." ICHING, Reading. My rework of the copied "Notice where you are holding yourself now:", kept because the face and the belly after the colon are the picture the Reading is built on; the instruction itself still has no source. The head and the belly are lore (the Shuogua body parts), recorded in meta.lore.

9. "The world is not at peace, you are uneasy at heart, and the unease is the very thing that will get you through, because it keeps you from being carried off with the crowd." ICHING, Line 2. Thirty-four words carrying the Buddhist note almost whole; left as one thought. Source: Cleary Buddhist, "one sees the world not yet at peace and is still choked and uneasy at heart; yet it is precisely because of this unease that one can bring about a successful way through impasses and be invulnerable to mass derangement."

10. "No one will warn you, since the shame is the warning." ICHING, Line 3. Unsure: "the shame is the warning" is my reading of Legge, compressed toward a riddle. Source: Legge's note, "He is left therefore to the shame which he ought to feel without a word of warning."

11. "First the block, then the joy, in that order and no other." ICHING, Line 6. "In that order and no other" says nothing the first half did not. Source: oracle-12, "Beforehand obstruction, afterwards joy"; Legge, "Before this there was that condition. Hereafter there will be joy."

12. "It is not pride, which needs an audience." KEYS, Shadow. Fine by the script (no "It is Y" follows), noted because it is a matched pair by shape. Source: gene-key-12 p2, "Unlike pride, which thrives before an audience, vanity is a far more internal Shadow."

13. "The mind is sharp, always able to find the reason the other person falls short." KEYS, Shadow. Unsure of "falls short": the source says "make them wrong". Source: gk-64ways-12, "It has a laser-like mind, which can make us believe that we alone can see the real reasons for everything. One can so easily use this mind to push others away, or make them wrong."

14. "The sense is done by feeling, and it cannot be argued away." KEYS, Gift. "Done by feeling" is not English a person who loves English would keep; the fact under it is sourced. Source: gene-key-12 p8, "one with the Gift of Discrimination will immediately feel it as a deep discomfort."

15. "The people who carry it are moved by feeling all their lives, and they are here to pass it on." KEYS, Gift. Unsure: "they are here to" is the register of the Human Design sources, not the card's; and it tells the reader their purpose. Source: gene-key-12 p7, "you will be strongly motivated and moved by feelings and emotions throughout your life. Your Gift is to communicate these feelings to others."

16. "The self you were once fell in love with itself; here, what is highest in you falls in love with you, and the two turn out to be one love with the separation gone." KEYS, Siddhi. "The self you were once" reads as a stumble ("the self you once were"); the thought is the source's mirror. Not a tell, so not touched. Source: gene-key-12 p10, "With vanity your lower self falls in love with itself, and with purity the Higher Self falls in love with itself. You might say that purity is when the Divine falls in love with You."

17. "Its colour is white beyond white." KEYS, Siddhi. A reader cannot check it; kept because it is the source's own line, verbatim. Source: gene-key-12 p12, "Its colour is the white beyond white for it is the eternal child within you."

18. "When they will not form, that is not failure; it is the signal to wait." DESIGN, The drive. A semicolon hinge by meaning, not counted; the appendix's new note says to say Y plainly and drop the "not X" where the reader was not assuming X. A reader who cannot find words probably is assuming failure, so it may be the kind that earns the pair; left for Adrian. Source: qw-gate-12, "Shyness is not a weakness, it is often a signal that the timing is not yet aligned to share your insights."

19. "A word said at its hour weighs more than a hundred said before it." DESIGN, The drive. A pronouncement; the source says only that their words carry weight. Source: gate-12, "when they do speak, their words often carry weight and depth."

20. "This pressure joins it to the feelings, deep in the belly, so what comes out of you is how you feel." DESIGN, Where it lives. My rework of "Here the join is to the feelings"; unsure whether "this pressure joins it" is exact, since it is the channel (12-22) that joins the throat to the feelings and the pressure is one end of it. Source: channel-12-22, "a direct connection between the Throat Center ... and the Solar Plexus Center, connected to emotions."

21. "The pauses in a voice can carry as much as the sound." DESIGN, Where it lives. Sourced, but it closes the paragraph on a saying rather than a fact. Source: center-throat (Winn), "The pauses in the melody can be as powerful as the music."

22. "That is this energy in your flesh." BODY, Physiology. The join said as an equivalence with nothing new in it; the sentence before it already gave the reason (everything in you stands still so that one thing can pass), and the entry's join is that sentence, not this one. A cut would be safe. Source: body/organs/throat.md, "this energy is given to the throat because the throat is where everything in you stands still for something to pass."

23. "A baby can breathe while it feeds; you lost that when your voice box came down to where words are made, which is the price of speech." BODY, Physiology. "Which is the price of speech" is a moral pinned to a fact, though it is the standard account (the descended larynx allows speech and forbids breathing while swallowing). Source: body/organs/throat.md, the newborn that can breathe while it feeds and the larynx that dropped; the entry's "Not this" has no line against it.

24. "It ends your separate existence too, and the sense of being separate goes with it: crossing your own throat, you are swallowed by life." BODY, Amino acid. The entry says this line is carried "as what happens in a body and never as a claim about the reader's attainment"; "It ends your separate existence" reads as the claim, and "crossing your own throat" is a picture to decode. Source: gene-key-12 p13, "it brings an end to your own individual existence, revealing vanity for what it truly is, the illusion that you are separate ... In crossing the great cosmic throat centre, you are swallowed by life itself."

25. "Two others in you carry the same stop, and of the three this is the last, the innermost." BODY, Amino acid. "In you" places the kin cards inside the reader (as card 11's "Three kin in you share it" does). Source: gene-key-12 p2, "The 12th Shadow of Vanity marks the inner core of the Ring of Trials ... the third and final aspect in this trilogy."
