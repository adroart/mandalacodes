## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "You do the work in your own manner, you learn what you do not know, and you are not made smaller by help." CODE. Sourced (gene-key-14, "it has flair because it is doing something in a manner that no one else can"; "If you do not know how to do something, you learn"; "these people do not reject the advice of others"), but the Gift says the first two again: "It has a manner that is only yours, and it is loose enough to take on a job it has never done, then learn it." Which section keeps them is Adrian's call.

2. "It cannot be done in hiding, because the heat of it spreads." CODE. The Gift opens its second paragraph on the same sentence: "You cannot do it in hiding. The heat of it spreads the way the leak used to, only outward." Source: gk-64ways-14, "The key is not to isolate ourselves"; gene-key-14, "Genius itself is highly contagious, just as compromise is."

3. "What is given makes room, and the room fills." CODE. A picture the reader must decode (room for what, filled by what). Source: Deng on line 3 (via lore), "an offering is an investment that will bring a great return later"; practical-14, "By giving more with such a sincere heart, you will receive more."

4. "Work is the shape this energy takes in a life, and the work you love is the only kind that keeps it." CODE. Closes the reading by restating its first paragraph. Source: gene-key-14, "If you do not enjoy it you can never excel in it. Enjoyment and enthusiasm are the fuel in the turbine that drives you to excel."

5. "Its risk is glare, showing everything until the warmth goes out of it." ICHING, Upper trigram. Must be decoded, and nothing in the Li field says it. Nearest: oracle-14, "glowing light, spreading in all directions ... warming and consuming aspect of burning."

6. "Its risk is not knowing how to stop, because a strength that goes on past what was needed burns the one carrying it before it burns anything else." ICHING, Lower trigram. Recorded under lore as an extension of Qian; the packet has only the parch reading. Source: oracle-14, "With the pronunciation GAN: parch, dry up, burn, exhaust."

7. "None of it was luck; it is the harvest of what you have been, and now you stand beside a full load wondering what a full load is for." ICHING, Reading. "None of it was luck" is my sentence, not a line; the sources say the opposite as often (practical-14, "Heaven bestows great blessings"). Nearest: Huang's title Great Harvest (via lore); gene-key-14, "themes linked to good fortune as well as hard work."

8. "What holds all this strength in place is one soft opening at the top, a place in you that is modest and can be reached, which the strong parts answer to without being made to." ICHING, Reading. Sourced (Legge, "the place of honour occupied by a weak line, so that its subject will be humble; and all the other lines, strong as they are, will act in obedient sympathy"), but Line 5 says it again in nearly the same words: "In the high seat sits the one open place, and every strong part answers to it, since you can be reached."

9. "give the best of the harvest upward, to whatever is larger than your own house." ICHING, Reading. Line 3 says the same: "give it upward, into the service of something bigger than your own house." Source: line-14-3, Cleary Taoist, "The work of barons serves the son of heaven"; practical-14, "You place your assets at the disposal of the greater good."

10. "Sort what you are holding." ICHING, Reading. Four words that tell the reader to do a thing the paragraph has not made concrete. Source: Image, "makes distinctions between things" (Wilhelm); "Brightness differentiates clearly indeed" (line 4 comment).

11. "Keep the load moving; it was loaded to be carried out, not kept." ICHING, Reading. Unsure: it is Line 2's counsel said in the Reading, and a second matched pair by meaning. Source: line-14-2, Cleary Taoist, "when virtue is mastered within, it should be evidenced outwardly. Only if there is a place to go ... is it really great possession."

12. "because a thought you feed is company too." ICHING, Line 1. Compressed to the edge of a riddle. Source: line-14-1, Cleary Taoist, "if there still are wrong thoughts arising within, these too are 'things,' and are harmful and blameworthy."

13. "Remember that the ground is hard, that the work repeats, and nothing will go wrong." ICHING, Line 1. "The ground is hard" must be decoded as the difficulty. Source: Legge, "Let there be a realisation of the difficulty"; oracle-14, "Drudgery, JIAN: difficult, hard, repetitive work ... soil difficult to cultivate."

14. "A full wagon standing is the one thing this line does not allow." ICHING, Line 2. "This line" is the card talking about itself by another route (the script counts only "this card" and "this reading"). Source: Huang, "There is somewhere to go"; Cleary Taoist, "Only if there is a place to go."

15. "Notice which of the two is speaking in you when the offering comes due, and give." ICHING, Line 3. Unsure of "when the offering comes due"; the two selves are practical-14's, the due date is mine. Source: practical-14, "You are both the duke and the commoner ... If you can let the duke win out, then great gains are to be had."

16. "Let others go their own way without your principles pressed on them." ICHING, Line 4. Carried from one reading only, and it sits oddly beside the line's counsel of restraint. Source: practical-14, "do not enforce your principles onto one whose principles seem to be different from and opposite of yours."

17. "Let the dignity be the plain kind, since preparing to be impressive shows you doubt you are." ICHING, Line 5. Unsure: the source's "preparation is a manifestation of insufficiency" is turned into a claim about doubt. Source: line-14-5, Cleary Buddhist, "preparation arises from insufficiency, and thus is a manifestation of insufficiency, which causes awesomeness to be diminished."

18. "The tell is the same every time: you do not feel good, something low down contracts, and the wagon, still standing in the yard, begins to leak from underneath." KEYS, Shadow. "Something low down contracts" is the belly tightening, which the small-intestine entry reserves for card 6 (the gut's nerves); here it is in KEYS, not BODY, and the source is the talk's own. Source: gk-64ways-14, "When we make an inner compromise, we don't feel good. A part of us shrinks. A part of us contracts."; gene-key-14, "The moment you make a compromise, your wagon begins to leak energy."

19. "It only happens because the load is still there to lose, and one act a day that does not sell it is enough to feel the leak stop." KEYS, Shadow. Unsure of "enough to feel the leak stop"; the source promises a difference, not a stop. Source: gk-64ways-14, "even one uncompromising act a day will make a difference."

20. "Power that has to prove itself belongs to its audience." KEYS, Reactive nature. A pronouncement that reads as wisdom; a reader can restate it but hardly check it. Source: gene-key-14, "These people are enslaved to their own need for recognition ... True power never needs to prove itself to others or the world."

21. "The work goes low, into the belly, and from there it finds the simplest way through anything, the way a saw drawn toward you cuts finer for less effort than one shoved away." KEYS, Gift. "The work goes low, into the belly" must be decoded before the saw arrives. Source: gene-key-14, "The secret of the belly is about drawing rather than pushing ... the saw is pulled towards the belly ... The resulting cut is finer"; "it finds the quickest and simplest solutions to any obstacles."

22. "Whoever was driving the wagon is gone." KEYS, Siddhi. Unsure whether a reader can say what it means without the chapter (the ego driven out of the vehicle). Source: gene-key-14, "this transformation is so physiologically powerful that it literally drives the occupant, commonly known as the ego, right out of the vehicle."

23. "Think of a person like that and something in your own week loosens." KEYS, Siddhi. Unsure: the source's claim is about prosperity flooding in, the card's is a loosening; either way it is a claim the reader cannot test against a life. Source: gene-key-14, "merely thinking about them will trigger a flood of prosperity on many levels in your life."

24. "When they leave work that was wrong for them, the energy comes back within weeks." DESIGN, Where it lives. "Within weeks" is a number the source does not give; the packet has no source for it at all, and the chapter the lore cites (32-evolution-sacral-center, not in the packet) says only "your energy will bounce back quite quickly."

25. "Its strength is the pause. When you are producing nothing, look at what you last said yes to." DESIGN, Where it lives. The second sentence is advice, and it is the defined centre's counsel (Winn, "if you are not creating them ... look at whether you are following your yes") placed at the end of the open centre's paragraph. Source: center-sacral, "the Open Sacrals are like the pause between the notes."

26. "Together they make the Channel of the Beat: a road for the engine, legs for the knowing, and a tempo that is nobody else's." DESIGN, What completes it. "A road for the engine, legs for the knowing" is two pictures to decode; "a tempo that is nobody else's" is sourced. Source: channel-2-14, "Individuals with this Channel defined often are driven by their internal rhythms"; gate-14, "it needs direction and focus, or it could end up causing restlessness."

27. "What you are good at was in you as a spark at birth, and a spark has to be fanned into action, by a childhood if you were lucky, by you if not." BODY, Amino acid. Sourced, and it is the ring's line the entry allots to Body; unsure only because it sits between two chemistry sentences with nothing joining it to lysine except position. Source: gene-key-14, "genius is a spark that needs to be fanned into action ... The spark of genius is there at birth, and if recognised at an early age, the child can lead a childhood individually tailored to fan the flames."

28. "From lysine your body also makes the carrier that brings stored fat out to be burned, so on a long walk with the meal hours behind you, what you are running on was carried there by it. That is the fire with something to feed: what you took in weeks ago, spent now, on the road." BODY, Amino acid. The whole second paragraph. The lysine entry says the carrier may be one clause at most and never the reason, and that card 14's page "carries the carrier as its whole paragraph ... biology given a reason the source did not give, the same fault as card 1's first three"; "That is the fire with something to feed" pins the card's energy to the carrier as a moral. Source for what it should carry: gene-key-14, "genius is a spark that needs to be fanned into action"; the entry's "the fire needs something to feed." No source found for the carrier as this energy.
