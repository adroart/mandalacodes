- "the allowing works like a valve: the whole force behind it comes through." KEYS, Gift. A picture that is at least mechanical.
- "It is not gentle." KEYS, Gift. Voice, but it does carry the source's "raw power".
- "Two people met like this make a third thing between them, a field neither had alone, and the more unlike the two are, the further it can take them." KEYS, Gift. "A third thing" is the source's; a reader who has been in a good pairing could say yes.
- "The one purpose this energy ever had was to break down every barrier to union, and when the last one is down it is no longer needed." KEYS, Siddhi. A pronouncement, but it is the whole card's claim said plainly.
- "and the old physicians stored in the same low ground what makes children." BODY, Physiology. "The old physicians" is unexplained; the Chinese-medicine kidney is under it.
