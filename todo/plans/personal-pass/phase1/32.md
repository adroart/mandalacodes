# Card 32, Duration: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Read once, judged as read. No card edited.

## Suspect sentences

1. "Letting life in, even where it hurts, is what makes you stronger." CODE, second paragraph. Voice with nothing inside; it would need the source's fact about what letting the wider world in does to a group that would otherwise die out.
2. "When you let go into the pattern life moves in, the support was there all along, and what you truly need comes when it is needed." KEYS, Shadow. No fact here to carry; "the pattern life moves in" cannot be tested.
3. "Nature is the old root, you the young shoot, which is why the old ways draw you." KEYS, Gift. A picture to decode with a "which is why" it has not earned.
4. "Then you see that what you were fighting to preserve was never the real thing, and the shell of it cracks." KEYS, Gift. "The real thing" is never named; no fact here to carry.
5. "It gives off something, a scent caught on someone in whom wisdom has taken up residence." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
6. "Wisdom lives in a host, passes through, and moves on to the next one." KEYS, Siddhi. A pronouncement no life can test.
7. "In the old medicine the spleen keeps from the other side too: it holds the blood inside its channels, moves the water through you, and turns what you eat into what you live on." BODY, Physiology. Names the source instead of carrying the fact; the three things it lists are fine without "in the old medicine".
8. "What is yours in it is the fear of dying with your life unfinished, the fear that ties death to money in a body that keeps and counts; that fear is chemistry too, older than any account." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule.
9. "The same substance is the old root and the young shoot: what has held for generations, with the new set onto it so it grows." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason; a molecule is not a graft.
10. "In your body the keeping with the grafting is one chemistry, shared with one other energy, the one that fears the ending itself." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason, and it is card 28's amino closing sentence with the nouns swapped ("It is one chemistry either way, the postponing with the giving, and in your body it is shared with one other energy, which carries the other half of the fear.").

## Unsure

1. "What lasts does not hold still." CODE, first paragraph. A riddle here; the I Ching Reading unpacks it later.
2. "Carried well, it comes with one knowledge: the only failure is the failure to live well." CODE, second paragraph. The source's line, but as a pronouncement.
3. "An ending is not a failure when it ends with grace." CODE, second paragraph. A verdict; a reader may be able to test it.
4. "Keep near the ones least like you, because a thing that lasts is made of parts that are not the same." ICHING, Reading. Checkable, but the reason clause is asserted.
5. "Go back to your own ground and be still there, since only stillness catches anything." ICHING, Line 4. "Only stillness catches anything" is a pronouncement.
6. "The one who buried what he was given, for fear of losing it, lost it." KEYS, Gift. A parable the reader may not know, given as a sentence.
7. "Even the smallest insect is holding you up." KEYS, Siddhi. True in one sense, but the reader must supply it.
8. "It is built into the letters your body is written in, part of what each of them is made from." BODY, Amino acid. A fact wearing a picture; the reader cannot find the letters.
