## Suspect sentences (say nothing a reader could restate and check) and sentences I was unsure of

Quoted whole, with the lens, and the packet line the sentence should be carrying.

1. "Nobody has to be told." CODE. On its own it says almost nothing; a reader has to supply what nobody is told. Source it leans on: practical-15, "When you strive to be undistinguishable, you become the most distinguishable."

2. "It lives in ordinary days, the same street, the same people, and makes them the place where everything that matters happens." CODE, first paragraph. The same source line is spent again in the second paragraph ("you never need to walk out of your own door to meet them"). Source: gene-key-15, "in order to find heaven you never need even walk out of your own door."

3. "A grey morning is part of this." CODE. A four-word thought with nothing in it a reader could test; and the grey morning is the Shadow's whole scene. Source: gene-key-15, "On a cloudy, grey day all human beings experience subtle changes in their physiology."

4. "What you make of a day runs underneath the mind and the mood, deciding which world you live in." CODE. Mine; unsure whether "runs underneath the mind and the mood" still carries the source's point that attitude is not the mind even though it works through it. Source: gene-key-15, "You may think it is the way you think, but it is not your mind even though it operates through your thinking ... it operates underneath even the deepest levels of your psychology"; "What you do with this truth determines what kind of universe you inhabit."

5. "Slowing down is how this is carried." CODE. Mine, the new opener. Source: gene-key-15, "One of the great challenges for modern humanity is to learn how to slow down."

6. "Some days go grey with nothing wrong in them, and the dread underneath is of every day being this one." CODE. Word for word the Shadow's opening ("A grey day, with nothing wrong in it, is the whole of this low ... the dread of the same day tomorrow, then the one after, without end"). One of the two should go; the mechanism allotted the grey day to KEYS. Source: gene-key-15, "It is the fear of the ordinariness of life ... to be locked into a repeating rhythm that never changes."

7. "Let the grey in rather than outrun it. What looked like the life going out of you turns out, at the bottom, to be full." CODE. The same two source lines are carried in the Shadow's second paragraph ("What works is letting the grey all the way in ... until at some depth it is not empty at all") and again in the Gift ("what you feared was draining the life out of you turns out to be its source, an empty vessel found full"). Three tellings of one thing. Source: gene-key-15, "The more deeply you embrace the dullness, the more mystical it becomes"; "The very state that you feared was taking away your life force actually becomes an awesome font of vital magnetic energy ... an empty vessel and suddenly realise that it isn't empty but full."

8. "The old character sets words beside uniting, what is said kept close to what is so." ICHING, Combination. Unsure: "what is said kept close to what is so" has to be decoded. Source: oracle-15, "Ideogram: words and unite, keeping words close to underlying facts."

9. "Of all the shapes a life can take, this is the one in which every step turns out well, from the bottom to the top, and the old readers found that nowhere else." ICHING, Reading. "The old readers found that nowhere else" is a source in sight, the tradition named in all but name. Source: Huang (line-15-6), "Among the sixty-four gua, only in Humbleness are all lines auspicious."

10. "A mountain inside the earth does its work the way a hand does, without needing to be looked at." ICHING, Reading. A picture inside a picture; the reader has to work out what work a buried mountain does and how that is like a hand. No source found for the hand doing its work unlooked at; the hand itself is Shuogua lore (meta.lore).

11. "Lowering yourself like that is how you are made ready." ICHING, Line 1. Mine. Source: cleary-buddhist (line-15-1), "Managing oneself means developing oneself until one can act."; Huang, "not acting negatively by holding oneself back but acting positively by moving forward."

12. "It rings true because it comes from the heart of you, not from a manner put on for the room, and it stays true only while it does." ICHING, Line 2. "Only while it does" makes the reader go back to find what "does" refers to. Source: cleary-buddhist (line-15-2), "it is correct insofar as it comes from one's true nature."; Huang, "Virtue comes from the core of the heart."

13. "Rage is not dull, grief is not dull; dullness is the dead middle, where there is no charge either way, and every low state has this at its core." KEYS, Shadow. "Grief" is the card's word; the source's second example is violence. Unsure whether grief is a charge in the source's sense. Source: gene-key-15, "Rage is not dull. Violence is not dull. Only the lack of any charge, positive or negative, is dull ... all the Shadow states have at their core this same dullness."

14. "A new town or a new intensity, always moving, so the flatness never catches them, which from outside looks like passion." KEYS, Reactive nature. The source says the opposite of the last clause: the movement has no directed passion in it. Source: gene-key-15, "Being extremists they can find themselves in all kinds of diverse situations and places, but again, with no real directed sense of passion."

15. "This is not charisma, which is life force spilling over the surface of a person. This works from below the surface, may never show in the personality at all, and is felt in a room as something around a person that nobody can name." KEYS, Gift. Left as the source's own shape, but it is a second not-this-but-that pair on a card allowed one. Source: gk-64ways-15, "That's charisma, an overflowing of the life force beneath the surface. True magnetism comes from way below the surface ... It may not so much as flicker through one's personality."

16. "The grey of the low is the colour here." KEYS, Gift. A riddle unless the reader already knows the source; "the colour here" is not a thing a person can check. Source: gk-64ways-15, "the grey of the Shadow becomes the colour of the Gift."

17. "It comes to a person the way spring comes, on no schedule, and everyone recognises it once it has." KEYS, Siddhi. The picture argues with itself: spring keeps a schedule. Source: gene-key-15, "It is a state that occurs to humans sporadically, meaning that no one can predict if or when it will occur to a particular person. One thing alone is certain, we humans recognise it when it occurs in another."

18. "From outside it reads as inconsistency, nothing followed through, and the misreading is exactly backwards." DESIGN, The drive. "Exactly backwards" is a verdict with nothing to check it against; the sentence never says what the right reading is. Source: gate-15, "these fluctuations can sometimes seem inconsistent or unpredictable to others, causing confusion or misunderstanding."

19. "Here a life's tempo and the self that lives it are one thing." DESIGN, Where it lives. No source found for tempo and self being one thing; the centre holds identity and direction, and the gate's rhythm sits in it, and the sentence fuses the two. Nearest: center-identity, "It sets the direction of your life."; gate-15, "It's the gate of rhythm and flow."

20. "When you trust your own timing you trust yourself, and the two turn out to be the same act." DESIGN, Where it lives. Says what item 19 already said, two sentences later. Source: center-identity, "when we don't trust ourselves ... we resist what we magnetize and go to war with ourselves"; qw-gate-15, "trust your own flow and rhythm."

21. "Through it the pace of living things enters a life." DESIGN, What completes it. Unsure: "the pace of living things" is large and vague for a sentence that stands alone. Source: center-identity (5-15), "With your strong connection to nature's rhythms, you have a deep love of life, of nature, of animals, and of humanity."

22. "The liver is where your body keeps this energy, and the reason is the level it holds." BODY, Physiology. The reason given is the steady sugar level, which the liver entry lists under checkable anatomy, not under the join; the entry's join for this card is direction and free movement, and neither is in the paragraph. That is the brief's "true biology chosen because it rhymed" (here with the Image's weighing and evening). Source: body/organs/liver.md, "On card 15 the join runs through the Identity centre's direction and the free flow"; "when the liver is well, everything moves, and when it is bound, everything stalls."

23. "It sits under your right ribs, out of sight, holding your level without a word." BODY, Physiology. The card's own lore note says the right ribs are card 1's and are not repeated here, and then the prose repeats them. Source: body/organs/liver.md, "It sits under the right ribs, just beneath the muscle that draws each breath."

24. "You will never feel serine being made; nothing on your plate is needed for it." BODY, Amino acid. The brief records that "you will never feel lysine at work" on card 1 read to Adrian as "something is missing"; this is the same sentence with a different molecule, and its second half repeats "so it never has to be fed" from the paragraph before. Source: body/amino-acids/serine.md, "No check exists: the making cannot be felt; none is invented."
