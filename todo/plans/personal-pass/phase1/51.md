## Suspect sentences

1. "Thunder is the shock, and what a shock does is wake." ICHING, Upper trigram. Voice with nothing inside; the next sentence carries the fact.

2. "Your deepest fear meets you on this road, and it does not stop you." KEYS, Gift. A life assigned to the reader; no fact here to carry.

3. "It is not a leap you take. The leap takes you." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle; the sentence before it already said waking comes without cause.

4. "After it there is nothing left in you for a shock to land on, because there is no separate one there to be struck." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement; nothing a reader could check.

5. "What is left is a vessel that knows only how to give, with nothing in the way, and the giving is love, though not always the warm kind." KEYS, Siddhi. Picture with a hedge on the end; no fact here to carry.

6. "Even now your presence stirs the room." KEYS, Siddhi. A life assigned to the reader.

7. "The sound that frightened you at the bottom is, at the top, the sound of you giving." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle.

8. "Together they make the Channel of Initiation, the one path off the will whose force is not the wish to do." DESIGN, What completes it. The second half is a riddle; would need the plain fact about what drives this channel if not will.

9. "Leaving the crowd for the wild country of your own heart is a road every human spirit walks at some point, and the road has a chemistry, this one, the same in you as in everyone who ever set out." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the molecule a reason; a life assigned to every reader.

10. "In you it is the substance of the step off the known path." BODY, Amino acid. The molecule made an equivalence for the energy; no body fact.

11. "What wakes one person in the end wakes everyone, family by family, and the chemistry of that waking is already in you, shared with your kin." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the molecule a reason; no source in view.
