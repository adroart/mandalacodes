# Card 55, Abundance: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "Let yourself be as you are, and if that changes, let it." CODE. Voice with nothing inside.

2. "The awareness that was shut in one body slips the net and runs between people, so that a feeling belonging to someone becomes a strange idea." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement; nothing a reader could check.

3. "There is one wave; some of you make it while others receive it." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle.

4. "Behind the longing for the perfect other was this all along: a net across the whole world with a jewel at every knot, each one holding all the rest." KEYS, Siddhi. A picture the reader must decode; the net is never said in plain words.

5. "The cup one side pulled at while the other pushed away is full now and running over, because only one is drinking from it." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle.

6. "You no longer fall in love. You rise in it, and there is a stillness in your eyes." KEYS, Siddhi. Wordplay standing in for a fact; the stillness in the eyes is decoration.

7. "A change is coming in the cells of your body, and this energy sets it going." BODY, Amino acid. Pronouncement; gives the chemistry a reason before the substance is named.

8. "Your body is getting ready for it as you read, in its own chemistry, and so is every other body." BODY, Amino acid. A life assigned to every reader; no body fact.

9. "What runs through all of humanity as a change runs through you as one person: a rising and a letting go, a force that spirals up through you, as the body's own does at the height of love." BODY, Amino acid. No fact here to carry.

10. "The same substance runs in the ones who share this energy, so what rises in you is one chemistry with theirs." BODY, Amino acid. Gives the molecule a reason; the kin fact is already stated by naming it.
