# Card 52, Keeping Still: Phase 1 report

Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). RELATIONS not read.

## Suspect sentences

1. "Mountain is the ground stood up, and what a mountain does is stay." ICHING, Upper trigram. Voice with nothing inside; the next sentence carries the stop.

2. "What you began with is the seed, and it already holds everything the thing will need, even what draws the right people at the right time." KEYS, Gift. A pronouncement about every beginning; nothing a reader could check.

3. "Push any movement to either end and you arrive at the same place." KEYS, Siddhi. Riddle.

4. "Stillness waits at both ends, and it is not empty but full, more alive than what it replaced." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement; no fact here to carry.

5. "Your body goes on being rebuilt every hour, and underneath the building nothing moves at all." KEYS, Siddhi. The second half is a riddle the first half does not earn.

6. "Whatever moves from here moves only to touch the same stillness in someone else." KEYS, Siddhi. Pronouncement.

7. "There is more life in you than you can spend, pressing to get out." BODY, Amino acid. Voice; gives the molecule a reason before the molecule is named.

8. "Of the pressures in you this is the one that makes the others last." BODY, Amino acid. Riddle; the marking fact before it is the checkable thing, and this sentence turns it into a claim about the energy.

9. "At the far end your body makes something else, a chemistry that quiets thought, so the stillness is in your body before it is in your mind." BODY, Amino acid. "Something else" is never named; no fact here to carry.

- "The way is down." CODE. Riddle until the next sentence lands it.
- "Steady as the peak and lasting as the ground, you complete what was begun." ICHING, Line 6. Decoration on the line's fact.
- "Then it spreads through you like a coin dropped in a pond, and dissolves back into the quiet it came from." KEYS, Siddhi. Picture; it does describe a feeling a reader could watch for.
- "Meanwhile the whole field opens at once, the shape of what needs to happen and the order it needs to happen in." DESIGN, The drive. "The whole field" must be decoded.
- "Each breath you take arises from it and returns to it; nothing in you is lower." BODY, Physiology. The breath does not arise from the perineum; the pelvic floor moves with it. Half anatomy, half picture.
