## Suspect sentences (fail "could a reader say it in other words and check it against a life")

CODE, paragraph 2: "If you are holding it right, being thanked stops mattering." Reads as a verdict on the reader's holding. Source: gene-key-07.md page 10, "Virtue is its own reward... it lies beyond the need for recognition and beyond the need to be of service." The sentence has the fact but frames it as a test the reader can fail.

ICHING, Reading, paragraph 3: "In the body these forces are the belly, which carries the weight, and the ear, which hears what is still under the ground." "Hears what is still under the ground" must be decoded before it does anything. Source: lore only (Shuogua body parts, recorded under meta.lore); no packet line found. The Reading's brief allows "the body once", so the sentence has a place; what it says about the ear is not checkable.

ICHING, Line 1: "Set out in good order, then even a mistake later can be borne." No source found in line-07-1.md. The nearest is line 2's "No harm comes despite errors made" (practical-07-the-army.md, Second Line), which belongs to line 2, not line 1. Line 1's sources say only that a bad order brings misfortune (Wilhelm, Legge, Huang, Deng).

ICHING, Line 2: "The trust comes to him from above, whole, given once and meant every time." "Given once and meant every time" is a riddle. Source: legge-07-the-army.md footnote, "It does not mean that the appointment came to him three times; but that it was to him exclusively, and with the entire confidence of the king." The plain fact (the charge was his alone, with the king's whole trust) is clearer than the sentence that carries it.

ICHING, Line 4: "Knowing you cannot advance and withdrawing to safe ground is the discipline of position, which keeps the constant." "Keeps the constant" cannot be said in other words by a reader. Source: line-07-4.md, Cleary (Buddhist) image, "Retreating and camping, without blame, means that one has not lost the constant", and Deng, "there must be consideration of position on the battlefield." The two sources are joined into one phrase that names neither.

KEYS, Reactive nature: "It looks like strong leadership." No source found for the appearance. gene-key-07.md page 6 says this leadership "demands complete reliance, either through pure power of presence or through subtler means"; nothing says it looks strong. The sentence before it ("mistake reliance for respect") already carries the confusion.

KEYS, Siddhi, second half: "From here the whole long history of ranks reads as the world waiting to be put back together." Sounds like wisdom; a reader cannot test it. Source: gene-key-07.md page 12, "the 7th Shadow of Division represents the force that shatters the vessels and pulls the world apart, whereas the Gift of Guidance begins the process of reuniting the shards." The source's fact is that division breaks and guidance repairs; the sentence turns that into a view of history.

DESIGN, The drive, paragraph 2: "People who carry it all their lives learn that influence done from beside is worth more than a title, and that the room follows the one who knows where they stand." The second clause, "the room follows the one who knows where they stand", has no clear meaning (knows their rank? knows their place beside the front?). Nearest source: gate-07.md, "Individuals with this gate often have a strong sense of self and purpose... This inner certainty can serve as an inspiration to others." The first clause is sourced (qw-gate-07-collaboration.md, "leadership is only as strong as the support it receives"; "influencing leadership from behind the scenes").

DESIGN, Where it lives, paragraph 1: "The same force keeps every other part of you joined into one whole, which is where the pull to gather other people comes from." The first half is sourced (center-identity.md, "The G Center, through the Magnetic Monopole, keeps all the Centers connected as a whole"). The second half, that this is where the pull to gather people comes from, is no source found; it is the writer's join between the centre and the gate.

DESIGN, Where it lives, paragraph 2: "Someone like that can merge with the people they love, falling for what a person could become rather than what they do." Merging is sourced (center-identity.md, "you may latch onto or merge with people with Defined Gs and follow their direction"). "Falling for what a person could become rather than what they do" is no source found.

DESIGN, Where it lives, last sentence: "Either way the guidance you give is only as steady as the ground you give it from." A pronouncement joining the centre to the gate; no source found. It is plausible and unverifiable, which is the brief's definition of the sentence to cut or say plainly.

BODY, Physiology, paragraph 1, last two sentences: "Breathing low, everything in you is gathered round one centre. When the breath climbs high in the chest, the middle of you is straining after something, and the rest comes apart around it." No source found in body/organs/diaphragm.md, whose "Not this" list excludes "the breath as a practice or a cure, calm as advice". The sentences are not advice, but the low-breath / high-breath contrast is not in the entry's three threads or its checkable anatomy, and "the rest comes apart around it" is an image doing the work of a fact.

BODY, Amino acid, paragraph 2: "While you sit like that, your body is already in it, below any plan to help." Unsure. Source: gk-64ways-07-the-army.md, "Guidance is really about being with someone. It's about presence. It's about resonating with them, taking them in, drawing their energies into our cells, our blood, our heart." The sentence renders "drawing their energies into our cells" as "your body is already in it"; a reader may not be able to say what "in it" means.
