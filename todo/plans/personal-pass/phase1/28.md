## Suspect sentences

2. "The demons, looked at, were angels in disguise." KEYS, Gift. A picture the reader must decode; it would need what an accepted fear turns into.
3. "Time can stop, and when it does there is nothing left for death to happen to." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
4. "That is the thing in you that was there before the play and is there after it. It watches, listens, breathes." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
5. "The self that could be lost has dissolved into it, and what is left cannot die because there is nobody in it to die." KEYS, Siddhi. A pronouncement no life can test.
6. "Near someone who has arrived here your own fears show themselves, which is the only way they ever leave." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked; "the only way" is a verdict.
7. "Two fears live there as one, the fear that life has no point with the fear of running out, and the pair is quiet exactly as long as the store is being spent on something." BODY, Physiology. The join made as an equivalence; "exactly as long as" has no source behind it.
8. "What is yours in it is the oldest fear, of death itself, and the life put off because of it, saved for a year when it will mean something." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule.
9. "The same substance is the moment given everything, whatever part you are playing, and the calm that runs under it when nothing is held back." BODY, Amino acid. Same made reason; a molecule cannot be a moment.
10. "It is one chemistry either way, the postponing with the giving, and in your body it is shared with one other energy, which carries the other half of the fear." BODY, Amino acid. Same; the kinship fact is buried under a made one.
