## Suspect sentences (fail "could a reader say it in other words and check it against a life")

CODE, paragraph 1, last sentence: "It is the whole of what you have to give, and it is enough." Reassurance rather than a fact. Source: qw-gate-08-fulfillment.md, "your very being, your presence and truth, is your purpose"; "I am the contribution that I am here to give the world." The source's fact is that the being is the contribution (DESIGN carries it); "and it is enough" has no line behind it.

CODE, paragraph 3, last sentence: "What you fear is being seen, and being seen is the way in." "The way in" to what is not said; a reader cannot test it. Source: gk-64ways-08, "a pattern rooted in the deep-seated fear of what others may or may not think of us"; qw-gate-08, "express yourself authentically and vulnerably, trusting that the right people will recognize your value." The second half of the source (the right people recognise you when you are seen) is the fact the sentence gestures at.

ICHING, Reading, paragraph 3: "In the body this energy is the belly, with the ear above it: what you hear goes down into you and is kept, as the ground keeps rain." Lore only (the Shuogua body parts, recorded under meta.lore); no packet line. "What you hear goes down into you and is kept" cannot be said in other words by a reader.

ICHING, Line 2: "What you hold to from within, you cannot lose." Source: line-08-2.md, Cleary (Buddhist) image, "Accord coming from within means not losing oneself", and Eranos, "Not the origin letting-go". The source says you do not lose yourself; the sentence says you cannot lose the thing you hold. Close, not the same.

ICHING, Line 3: "You are standing among people who are not yours, and you know it by the effort it takes." "You know it by the effort it takes" is no source found. line-08-3.md gives only the wrongness of the company (Legge, "such as ought not to be associated with"; Huang, "How can it not be hurtful?"); practical-08-alliance.md, "those who do not share your focus... will inevitably dissent against you later."

KEYS, Shadow, paragraph 2: "So you dream of the lives you might have had, watching other people live them on a screen, moved and going nowhere." Tells the reader what their evenings contain. Source: gene-key-08.md page 4, "most of us are simply content to dream about what we could have achieved in our lives. We may watch the films and movies of others and may even be deeply moved by them, but the 8th Shadow prevents us from believing in ourselves enough to embody such a life." The source is about the whole culture ("most of us"); the sentence assigns it to "you".

KEYS, Shadow, last sentence: "Every child gives off a light, and yours went dim a little at a time, so slowly that you never noticed the day it did." "Yours went dim" is a verdict on the reader; "you never noticed the day it did" is no source found. Source: gk-64ways-08, "We have forgotten the light that every child emanates, and which gradually over time, through education, upbringing, local and global conditioning, becomes throttled and grows dim."

KEYS, Repressive nature: "They are of some use, decent, reliable, without a spark of fire or grit." "Decent, reliable" is no source found; a list of three qualities. Source: gene-key-08.md page 5, "people whose words and actions may be of some service to the world but who, at the same time, lack fire and grit."

DESIGN, The drive, paragraph 2: "What is going on is quieter: they cannot be anyone but themselves without their throat closing." "Without their throat closing" is no source found; it is the writer's image joining the gate to its centre. Source: gate-08.md, "They are unapologetically themselves"; qw-gate-08, conforming to an image "can lead to inner conflict, burnout."

DESIGN, What completes it: "Through it a self is put into words unusual enough to catch the heart of a thing, and the words carry because a person is standing behind them." The first half is sourced (center-throat.md, Winn on the 1-8: "words that capture the heart of a situation in an unusual way"). "The words carry because a person is standing behind them" is no source found; channel-1-8.md says the channel is "projected" and "requires recognition or an invitation from others before it can effectively be shared", which is a different fact.

BODY, Physiology, last sentence: "It is in the sound of your voice too, in the timbre nobody else has." "It" is the gland's setting, and the sentence puts the pace in the timbre of the voice. body/organs/thyroid.md allows card 8 "the timbre of the voice as its talk's own line" (gk-64ways-08: "Our style is in our radiance, our aura, the timbre of our voice"), which is a line about style, not about the gland; nothing in the entry says the thyroid is in the timbre. A reader cannot check it in a minute.

BODY, Amino acid, last sentence: "The part of you that receives whatever is given runs on it too, so the receiving and the self that comes of it are built from one thing." "The self that comes of it" (of receiving?) has to be decoded. Source: gene-key-08.md page 10, "the Siddhi of Exquisiteness is chemically bonded to the 2nd Siddhi of Unity. These are the two great feminine Gene Keys"; body/amino-acids/phenylalanine.md says card 8 may say the kinship as chemistry but not "in card 2's words", and "runs on" is card 2's own verb ("The trust this energy runs on has a substance in you").
