Read 2026-09-16 (Fable). Read once, judged as read. No card edited.

## Suspect sentences

1. "Giving and receiving are one law seen from two sides." CODE, first paragraph. A pronouncement; the sentence before it already carries the fact (what feeds is fed in turn).
2. "The shock is the point." KEYS, Reactive nature. Says nothing the next sentence explains; it would need what the shock does to the one who gave.
3. "Purpose rises out of this the way water rises from a spring, warm, and spreads to whoever stands close." KEYS, Gift. A picture with no plain meaning; it would need what purpose does in a day.
4. "What is left is a hunger the body always carried, for a harmony so complete that food was only its outer sign." KEYS, Siddhi. Cannot be checked; no fact here to carry.
5. "Where an ounce of life is left in a thing, this reaches it." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
6. "From here the world feeding on itself sounds like music, and love is all they hear." KEYS, Siddhi. No fact here to carry.
7. "You arrive with no self, you leave with no self, and the one in the middle was the dream." KEYS, Siddhi. A pronouncement no life can test.
8. "Beside it, low in the back, is kept the store you were fed from before you could feed yourself, the given part of you, which every meal since has added to." BODY, Physiology. A picture the reader cannot place in the body (which store, which organ); it would need the plain thing named or a cut.
9. "What closes in you and what gives in you run on this one chemistry; the two pull opposite ways, the closing toward death, the giving toward life, in you as in everyone alive." BODY, Amino acid. A reason the source did not give the molecule.
10. "Give with nothing wanted back and something moves low in your body, a current that is real, one you can feel." BODY, Amino acid. Assigns the reader a feeling; no source for the current.
11. "Five other things in you share this substance, and it is the same substance in all six." BODY, Amino acid. "Things" carries nothing; it would need the source's one fact, that the energy shares its chemistry with kin.

## Unsure

1. "It lives there, and it lives in how you keep yourself." CODE, first paragraph. "It" and "there" lean on the sentence before.
2. "Given freely, time stretches." CODE, second paragraph. A pronouncement, but the next sentence gives the picture that makes it testable.
3. "A day spent with an open heart runs like a river through open country, and a day spent holding on is over before you have drawn a breath." CODE, second paragraph. Matched pair; the second half is checkable, the river is decoration.
4. "The ones who reach upward for what they should be giving down will fall away from you for a while, which is part of it." ICHING, Reading. Line 2 compressed; the reader must decode "upward" and "down".
5. "What fills the mouth is sought from its source, and the source is yourself." ICHING, Judgement. Classical, rendered as a riddle.
6. "because the part of you that is starving is the part that lives." ICHING, Line 3. Near a riddle; the line's point (the spirit neglected for the belly) is not quite said.
7. "Sit in the small room long enough to ask what the larger reason would be, and the room begins to widen." KEYS, Shadow. The room is a picture carried across two paragraphs before it is used.
8. "There is nobody at the head of the table now, and the feeding goes on with no one doing it." KEYS, Siddhi. The source's claim, but a reader must decode "nobody".
