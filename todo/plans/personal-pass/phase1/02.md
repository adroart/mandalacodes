## Sentences that say nothing, or say what was already said

1. "Its creature is the mare, who runs as far as any horse and runs behind." (ICHING, Combination) and "What it asks is the strength of the mare: she can run as far as any horse and still she runs behind, along the ground, going where it goes." (ICHING, Reading.) The same sentence twice in one section; the Combination is the picture and the Reading is where the mare does her work, so one of them is spending the other's line. Source: legge-02, footnote, "the correctness and firmness is defined to be that of 'a mare,' 'docile and strong'"; wilhelm-02, "Furthering through the perseverance of a mare."

2. "What you are carrying has a whole life ahead of it, from its first showing to its last test, and your part belongs to all of that, not to this stretch alone." (ICHING, Reading.) This is card 01's I Ching proposition (the four seasons; your question belongs to the whole cycle, not to this part), which the brief says is not this card's even where the source repeats it. Source: oracle-02-space.md, "Spring, Growing, Harvesting, female horse's Trial ... They indicate that your question is connected to the cycle as a whole rather than a part of it." What is this card's own in that line is the mare's Trial, which the sentence does not carry.

3. "Rest in what is steady and it goes well." (ICHING, Reading, end of the second paragraph.) Word for word the third Judgement bullet ("Rest in what is steady, and it goes well."), which the page shows a few lines below. Source: wilhelm-02, "Quiet perseverance brings good fortune"; legge-02, "If he rest in correctness and firmness, there will be good fortune."

4. "Stay soft, stay where you are, and receive." (CODE, reading; corrected by Adrian.) By meaning the same closer as the ICHING Reading's "Let the belly go, sit as the ground sits, and hold to what was handed to you until it is done": soften, stay put, receive. Recorded on the sheet as the second side-by-side overlap. Source: gk-64ways-02, "Become soft, become like water"; practical-02, "Be soft, be tender, be accommodating."

5. "In a life this is the stretch when your work is to hold what has come to you and let it become what it is going to be." (CODE, reading; corrected by Adrian.) The first side-by-side overlap: the ICHING Reading opens on the same thought ("You have been given something to carry through, and the carrying is the whole of the work now"). Source: wilhelm-02, "Seek not works, but bring to completion"; oracle-02-space.md, "Carry, ZAI: bear, carry with you; contain, sustain, support."

6. "That is the work of the ground too: it starts nothing, everything is set into it, and it carries what it is given." and "The breastbone is where the body does the same." (BODY, Physiology.) The join has already been given ("It holds the whole front of you together by being where things arrive, which is this energy's whole work"); these two restate it as an equivalence, the shape the brief names as the Body failure. Card 01 closes its Physiology with the same two sentences ("That is the work of the creative force too ... The liver is where the body does the same"), so it is a template, not a choice. Source: body/organs/sternum.md, "The join, in plain words": everything in the front of you comes to it, it never moves itself, every breath carries it.

7. "As you are carried into a wider life, your own face begins to show." (BODY, Amino acid.) The same source line is already spent in KEYS, Gift ("and your own face begins to show"). The reference entry assigns the face to Body's second paragraph, so the Gift is the one carrying a line that is not its own; either way it is said twice. Source: gene-key-02.md pages 7 to 8, "As you become oriented into a wider context, your real face begins to show itself in the world."

8. "Those turn out to be one thing." (DESIGN, Where it lives.) Said of who you are and which way your life faces. Nothing a reader can restate or check; the source names both as what the centre holds and never says they are one. Source: center-identity.md, "In the Identity Center, we recognize who we are ... you have a definite direction in life." No source found for the identity claim.

9. "This person has let go of the thread." (KEYS, Repressive nature.) The thread is a picture the reader has to decode before the paragraph starts, and the paragraph then says the thing plainly. Source: gene-key-02.md page 5, "being out of alignment with one's true universal destiny ... Without a cosmic connection within one's being, one will always flounder in the world."

10. "Nothing leads now and nothing follows." (KEYS, Siddhi; corrected by Adrian.) A matched pair that reads as a riddle; the reader cannot say what it means in other words. Source it may be reaching for: gene-key-02.md page 10, "One is one, it is consciousness resting in its own nature"; gk-64ways-02, "There aren't two things anymore. There is no separation."

## Sentences I was unsure of

11. "A seed, a stone, rain or a dead leaf, all of it is received, and what can grow from it grows." (CODE, reading; corrected by Adrian.) A list of four examples, where the brief's limit is two. Source: oracle-02-space.md, "support of all existence ... nourishes all things"; practical-02, "the sage accepts the character of all, for better or for worse."

12. "There will be days when it feels as if you have been left behind and ought to be making something happen, and the ground has an answer to that: everything that has ever grown has grown out of it, and none of it was hurried." (CODE, reading; corrected by Adrian.) Forty-five words, two ands, three thoughts. Source: gene-key-02.md page 6, "there is no question of any doing on your part"; gk-64ways-02, "It's to trust in the timing of everything."

13. "In you it is deep patience, the part that can wait without proof that the waiting is working, hold what it was given, keep it warm and say nothing." (ICHING, Lower trigram.) A list of four verbs; and the seed through the winter and the field at the start of winter, in the two sentences before it, are one picture given twice. Source: line-02-1.md, Deng, "Earth as the yin force works gradually"; Huang's tenth month is lore (meta.lore).

14. "When you act from what you are, the act comes out straight, keeps its own shape, leaves nobody out, and is right everywhere at once." (ICHING, Line 2.) Four commas, a list of four, rendering the source's three. Source: line-02-2.md, Cleary (Buddhist), "One is upright because of living according to orderly and harmonious patterns of conduct. One is straight because of embodying good ways. One is great because of impartial concern for all beings."

15. "Do the plain thing once, as yourself, and let others fall in behind." (ICHING, Line 2.) "Let others fall in behind" is not in any reading of the line; it leans on the target hexagram's name (The Army). Source: line-02-2.md, Legge, "without repeated efforts, will be in every respect advantageous." No source found for the last clause.

16. "See it through without a word about your part, and keep the quiet of having done it." (ICHING, Line 3.) "The quiet of having done it" is decoration; the reader cannot restate it. Source: line-02-3.md, Huang, "Claim no credit, Carrying things through to the end"; Cleary (Taoist), "completion without fabrication."

17. "The sack tied at the neck, with one emptiness on both sides of the knot." (ICHING, Line 4.) A picture the reader has to decode before the line does its work; the next sentence says it plainly. Source: line-02-4.md, Cleary (Taoist), "tying up the opening of a bag, inside and out one emptiness."

18. "Keep your plans where they are, and the knot will come loose in its own time." (ICHING, Line 4.) No reading of the line says the sack is untied later; the promise is the writer's. Source: practical-02, "conceal your own intentions ... Success will hinge on discretion"; line-02-4.md, Huang, "Through caution, there will be no harm." No source found for the loosening.

19. "Wear the good thing low, and let the ones who belong near you gather on their own." (ICHING, Line 5.) The gathering is the target hexagram (Holding Together) rather than the line. Source: line-02-5.md, Cleary (Taoist), "one's virtue is sufficient to make people obey; one is trusted without speaking."

20. "The feeling is real as a feeling and false as a fact." (KEYS, Shadow; corrected by Adrian.) A neat matched pair; it can be restated (the lostness is felt but you are not lost), so it may pass, but it is built to the shape the eye test names. Source: gene-key-02.md page 2, "you have never actually lost your way, it just feels that way."

21. "Your timing sharpens until being out of step registers in your body as plainly as hunger does, and your own face begins to show." (KEYS, Gift; corrected by Adrian.) Two thoughts on one and; the second does not follow from the first. Source for the first: gene-key-02.md page 9, "Your timing becomes more and more fine tuned, so much so that if you find yourself out of harmony the magnetic mineral transducers in your body immediately convey this to your brain." The second is item 7's line.

22. "What you do with it for other people is the strange part." (KEYS, Gift; corrected by Adrian.) Reads as speech. Source it introduces: gk-64ways-02, "To orient another person correctly ... is to not give them advice."

23. "This is what being carried becomes when the one being carried lets go." (KEYS, Siddhi; corrected by Adrian.) Abstract; the reader may not be able to say what it means. Source: gk-64ways-02, "The only way to find unity is to dissolve."

24. "The grid keeps the lost feeling at a distance without touching it, and the harder life pushes, the tighter it is drawn." (KEYS, Reactive nature.) The second half has no source; it is a reasonable inference. Source for the first: gene-key-02.md page 6, "the enforcement of an external rhythm or structure over the top of life. This stems from deep anger that has never been resolved."

25. "It works like a magnet with a single pole, only ever drawing: what a life needs is pulled toward it, the people as much as the work, and the direction of the life is the sum of what it drew." (DESIGN, Where it lives.) Forty words; the last clause is a second thought. Source: center-identity.md, "It is the seat of the Magnetic Monopole ... it only attracts ... It draws what's needed for your life, from people and work, to places and experiences. It sets the direction of your life."

26. "Your body takes it in, from meat, eggs or beans, or goes without." (BODY, Amino acid.) Three foods where the brief's limit is two and its own model has two. Source: body/amino-acids/phenylalanine.md, "it has to arrive in food (meat, fish, eggs, cheese, beans, nuts)."

27. "What you let all the way in goes into the body as chemistry, not as a thought, which is why the body can feel carried before you have understood a thing." (BODY, Amino acid.) The "which is why" builds a reason on the fact; the entry blesses the join but the causal clause is the writer's. Source: gk-64ways-02, "our acceptance of the present moment, of the way we think and feel, sends a signal into our DNA, which then releases chemicals and hormones which make us feel better."

28. "The more you give way, the more comes through you." (BODY, Amino acid, last sentence.) Sourced, but as a closer it reads as advice, which Body may not give. Source: gene-key-02.md page 8, "The more you let go into the feminine yielding quality of this Gift, the more universal power floods through you."
