The brief should gain five enforcement rules: first, test every essence sentence alone with its pronouns unresolved and its source nouns removed. Second, compare CODE and Reading sentence by sentence against later explanations, Judgement, Image and moving lines, recording the duplicated proposition rather than merely duplicated wording. Third, apply the Siddhi absence-and-paradox check to the whole closing paragraph, so a decorative final sentence cannot conceal it. Fourth, flag every physiological “all,” “only,” “never,” fixed duration and universal causal sequence for evidence at exactly that scope. Fifth, read each section’s last sentences consecutively across the batch: repeated grammatical templates and interchangeable wisdom fail even when their first five words differ.
