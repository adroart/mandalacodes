# The Fable pass: six cards read as a reader, the model of one energy in six sections, and the table of contents

Written 2026-09-12 against cards 01, 02, 23, 33, 47 and 64 as they sit in `oracle/cards/`, the concept document, and the two parts of the reading rewrite that carry Adrian's diagnosis and the four-questions method. No card was edited. Part 1 was written from the cards before the survey's section 3 was allowed to lead; where I agree with a measurement I say so, and where my read differs I say that too.

A note on how to read the verdicts. "Carries" means the section could not be moved to another card without becoming false. "Lifts" means it could be moved to a neighbour (a ring sibling, a channel partner, any card with the same organ) with only the names changed and nothing would jar. Most sections sit between, and I say which sentences hold them to the card and which let go.

## Part 1. Six cards, thirty-six sections

### Card 01, Earth's Breath

CODE. Carries. The fire is the card's own and it is handled as a thing that rises and falls rather than a talent: "It was never yours to command. Only to carry while it is here, and to let go the moment it goes." One paragraph lit, one low, one on the art; of the six readings this is the closest to balance, though the third paragraph spends most of its instruction on the low season.

ICHING. Carries, and is the best section on the card. The dragon arc is taught as a motion, not a possession: "Originating power is not a thing you hold. It is a motion you are briefly inside of." Its last line, "The fire was never yours," is the CODE's truth said again from the tradition's side, and it lands because the sentence has been earned by the dragon's six positions above it. The moving lines each stand alone.

KEYS. Carries, by the fire. Entropy is written as a banked fire rather than a diagnosis: "A low fire is not a dead fire. Something is happening down in the coals that the bright flame could never do." The Gift's "nerve to start something before they are ready" answers the CODE's "move before you feel ready." What lets go is the Gift's closing flourish, "The maker gets made," which could close any Gift on any card, and the Repressive and Reactive openers, which are the deck's stem.

DESIGN. Carries. The drive is Gate 1's own itch and the channel paragraph brings the fire back: "Alone, this code is a fire in a closed room. Joined, it becomes inspiration, a thing that lights other rooms." The Identity Centre paragraph is the weak one; "That sounds simple. It is the work of a life" is true of every centre on every card.

BODY. Carries, and the amino acid is the card's second-best paragraph: "The fire is fed before it is lit." Lysine has to be received, and on the first code of all that is a genuine shock. One sentence should go: "This is why the card keeps returning to the same instruction in different voices." That is the maker explaining the card's own mechanism to the reader.

RELATIONS. Carries, thinly. The Tarot paragraph is bespoke and honest about a thin source: "Almost nothing is dealt to this hexagram, and the near silence is the reading." The Pair and Programming partner paragraphs say the same thing twice in a row, and the Pair paragraph is the identical sentence that opens card 02's Pair, so a reader holding both cards meets it twice.

The whole. One argument in six voices, and the mechanism is a single image at different heats. The CODE's fire, the ICHING's untiring sky, the Shadow's coals, the channel's closed room, the liver's hidden forge, the Ring of Fire. The thesis travels with it: never yours to command, the fire was never yours, carry the fire do not claim to have made it, the fire is fed before it is lit. This is the card the survey should have named as its evidence; the dragon and the itch are not competing metaphors, the dragon is the tradition's picture of the fire and the itch is the fire wanting out. Best sentence: "The fire is fed before it is lit." It is the card's whole teaching in six words and it is found in the chemistry, which is where it is least expected. Worst sentence: "The maker gets made." It summarises a paragraph that did not need summarising, and it is a shape that will fit any Gift. Adrian's diagnosis: present only faintly. The reading arrives as an energy, then spends its ask on managing the drop.

### Card 02, Beyond the Shell

CODE. Lifts, and it lifts from its own card. The opening sentence is the Shadow's opening sentence rearranged: CODE has "a quiet sense of being slightly out of place, not in any one room but in all of them," KEYS has "a feeling of being slightly out of place that follows some people everywhere. Not in any one room, in all of them." The second paragraph is the Shadow's fourth paragraph and the mare; the third is the Gift compressed. The one sentence that belongs to the reading alone is "There is a compass low in the body that comes back online when the gripping stops," and it is the best thing in it.

ICHING. Carries, and is the best section on the card. "Here you are asked to be the thing most people are taught to mistrust. Not the move, but the ground the move lands on." The mare, the southwest, the frost, the two dragons, all taught with their weight. Line 5 is the best moving line in the six cards: "If you have to tell people what you are, you are not yet that."

KEYS. Carries at the Shadow and the Siddhi, lets go in the middle. The Shadow is the strongest Shadow of the six ("accurate as a feeling and false as a fact"). The Gift's third paragraph teaches the pineal magnetite, and BODY teaches it again two panels later, so one of them is re-reading. The Siddhi closes on "It was love that had not finished arriving. It was always arriving. It is here," which is a fine sentence that also closes card 64's Siddhi and appears on seven other cards.

DESIGN. Carries. The channel paragraph has the card's one image that is the card's own rather than the tradition's: "Alone, this code points and cannot walk." The Identity Centre paragraph restates the CODE ("The knowing comes when the gripping stops"), which is the right direction of travel if the CODE had not already been assembled from KEYS.

BODY. Carries at the sternum, lifts at the amino acid. "This is the bone you touch when you mean yourself" is exact and bodily. Phenylalanine yields "The field that takes everything in is built, at the bottom, from something taken in," which is the same move card 01 makes with Lysine, and on the receptive it is not a shock, it is a tautology.

RELATIONS. Carries. The ring paragraph earns water: "there is an old sadness in water, the longing for home that drives the whole search." The Tarot sequence is the card's best piece of ordering: "The grip loosens first, then comes the dark climb behind the lamp, and last the field where the seeking rests."

The whole. Two arguments, not one. The tradition's image is earth, ground, the field that receives, and it lives in ICHING, RELATIONS and the Siddhi. The writer's image is the compass, the gripping that stops, and it lives in CODE, the Gift, DESIGN and BODY. They are both true of hexagram 2 and they are never joined; nothing says the compass works because the ground is trusted. Where the card does hold, it holds by the mare, one sentence carried from ICHING into CODE. Best sentence: "Not the move, but the ground the move lands on." Worst sentence: the CODE's opener, because it is the Shadow's opener and the reader who swipes to Gene Keys next meets it again. Adrian's diagnosis: fully present. Two paragraphs on the lost feeling, one on the way back; what the reading is mainly about is an ache, and the energy of receiving is never given a lit paragraph.

### Card 23, Beneath the Surface

CODE. Half lifts. The first paragraph is the DESIGN drive said again ("Something in you knows things whole, all at once, before you have any words for them" against DESIGN's "The knowing arrives whole and wordless"). The second paragraph is the Shadow, down to the exact phrase "shut out, misread, left on the wrong side of the door." The I Ching is not in it; a reader who knows only hexagram 23 would not find the erosion, the bed, or the fruit with its pit anywhere in this reading. The closer is the card's refrain and the reason the reading is still worth keeping: "What gets stripped was never lost. It was only freed to be said again, simpler."

ICHING. Carries, and is the best section on the card and one of the two best in the six. "No blow is coming. What is coming is an erosion, the slow stripping of a thing from underneath." "A bed loses its legs before it loses its frame." The last paragraph gives the card its truth: "What is essential cannot be stripped. It can only be uncovered." Line 1 ends "Do not pretend the bed is whole," which is counsel a caster can use alone.

KEYS. The survey says this section fails the lift test, and I half agree. What holds it to the card is the throat: "the speech that sounds clever and leaves the room colder than it found it," "So the speaking speeds up, trying to be understood, and the speed itself is what gets it misunderstood," and the two natures, one choked quiet and one circling. What lets go is the general virtue of simplicity: the Shadow's first paragraph ("The mind does not trust simple things. A simple thing gives it nothing to do") and the Gift's kitchen reduction and its praise of people who "waste almost nothing, in their rooms, their money, their speech." Those could sit under any card about tidiness. The Siddhi is held by one clause, "Here the splitting apart finishes its work," and let go by the stock line "There is no technique for arriving."

DESIGN. Carries, fully. "The teaching is to let the throat be a gate, not an engine" is the best Human Design sentence in the six cards, and the channel paragraph closes on the card's own phrase, "a knowing that can be heard."

BODY. Carries, and the amino acid is the best of the six. "Speech has to squeeze past a gland on its way out." "Speed lives here. So does the choke." Leucine is the signal to begin building, and the paragraph joins the hexagram's stripping to the chemistry's building to the Gift's simplicity in one motion: "What gets stripped is never lost. It is only freed to be made again, simpler." This is the only amino acid paragraph of the six that uses the I Ching.

RELATIONS. Carries. "Splitting apart and returning are the same midnight, read from either side of it." "Knowing and saying must keep the same clock." The Tarot lead gives the card its one picture: "Under all five sits one picture, a hand letting go, and what stays behind in the open palm." The Immortals paragraph earns its two figures: "A stripping takes only what is still gripped."

The whole. Two images, one bridge. The knife and the erosion and the pit belong to ICHING, RELATIONS and the amino acid; the throat and the tangle belong to CODE, KEYS, DESIGN and the physiology. The bridge is one sentence, stripped then freed to be said again simpler, which fuses the hexagram's stripping with the Gene Key's simplicity, and it is used twice, in CODE and at the very end of BODY, non-adjacent, and it works exactly as a refrain should. The throat holds across three sections, as the survey says. What is missing is the I Ching in the reading. Best sentence: "What is essential cannot be stripped. It can only be uncovered." Worst sentence: "The art is timing, and it is small, and it takes most of a life," because it is card 01's closer ("So the art of it is small and takes most of a life") with the noun changed, and the same stem is on four other cards. Adrian's diagnosis: present. The first paragraph is lit, the second is the tangle at length, the third is instruction; the card is mainly about the fear in the speaking.

### Card 33, Echos of Time

CODE. Lifts from its own card more completely than any other reading in the six. The first paragraph is the Shadow's second paragraph ("You read your life as a forward rush" against "Most people read their lives as a forward rush"), the argument-and-cold-week is the Shadow's first paragraph, the two ways of going wrong are the Repressive and Reactive natures compressed, "Both are the same gate jammed shut, one clamped, one leaking" is BODY's sentence, "hold it in the throat a beat longer than feels comfortable" is DESIGN's sentence, "Leave the thing while leaving is still your own choice" is the ICHING reading's first line. It opens on the survey's stem, "What this asks is small." As a reading it is a trailer for the five sections behind it.

ICHING. Carries, and is the best section on the card. "Leave while leaving is still yours to choose, before the choice gets made for you." "It is the harvest taken in before the frost." Its last paragraph does something no other ICHING in the six does: it reaches across to the Gene Key on purpose, "A deeper layer hears another word inside the withdrawal: forgetting," and so the reader arrives at KEYS already carrying the bridge. Line 5: "Leave at the top of the arc, not the far slope."

KEYS. Carries. The Shadow has the best two sentences in the section: "It does not feel like a gap. It feels like the present." The Gift's clearing in the forest, hui gan, and "Drink the tea and actually taste it" are this card's and no other's. The Siddhi's flood, "a memory of your own future," is specific. Nothing here mentions retreat; the section is about memory, and it is the ICHING's last paragraph and the Gift's "turn around and look out the back window at the road" that make memory and withdrawal one thing.

DESIGN. Carries, with one theft. The drive is Gate 33's own, and "Hold it in the throat a beat longer than feels comfortable" is the right sentence in the right place. But the Throat Centre paragraph teaches the thyroid, "the gland that governs timing and pace, how fast or slow the whole body runs," and that is BODY's line; it is also, word for word, card 23's BODY line ("quietly governs how fast or slow the whole body runs"). One sentence, on two cards, in two different sections.

BODY. Carries, and the stop codon is the best amino paragraph in the six alongside card 23. "Put a hand to your throat and swallow." Then: "A protein with no stop never finishes, never folds, never becomes itself." The card of retreat runs on the one letter whose job is to halt, and the paragraph says so without stating a moral. The tea held in the mouth ties back to the Gift's tea.

RELATIONS. Carries. "They are the in-breath and the out-breath of the same power." The ring paragraph joins the three trials to the stop codon: "the three places in the code where the sequence stops." The Tarot lead earns its phrase, "a stepping back that is also a stepping up."

The whole. This is the card the survey calls the most unified, and it is unified the way a trailer is unified: the CODE reuses six sentences from the other five sections. Behind the CODE, though, the braid is real and it is the strongest of the six. The throat holds DESIGN and BODY; stop holds ICHING, the codon and the ring; forgetting and retreat are joined by the ICHING's last paragraph and the Gift's back window. The mechanism where it works is a single verb, stop, carried through withdrawal, halt, the clearing, the stop codon, the three trials. Best sentence: "A protein with no stop never finishes, never folds, never becomes itself." Worst sentence: "What this asks is small and cuts against the rush," because it is the stem the survey counted and because "small" is this deck's most repeated adjective for an ask. Adrian's diagnosis: fully present, and this card is the clearest case of it. Three paragraphs and none of them lit; the reading opens on the rush, moves to the two shadow faces, and ends on instruction. The energy of the one who turns to look back and hands the story on, the harvest taken in before the frost, the tea tasted, never gets a paragraph of its own.

### Card 47, Garden of Alchemy

CODE. Lifts from KEYS. "Some mornings the weight is on you before anything has happened" is the Shadow's "You wake up already carrying something, a heaviness with no event behind it." The leftover fear looped through the blood, the new city and new partner, the two natures, "turn toward the buried fear instead of away" and "something starts to cook," "stops needing to be anyone fixed," "The thing that crushes you was always the raw gold": every beat is the Gene Keys section said shorter. The I Ching's dry lake and penned tree are absent; the Human Design's confusion before clarity is absent. One image is the reading's own and it is wrong for the card: "You are a wave that keeps hitting its own edge and dissolving into something wider," water on a card whose whole situation is that the water has drained.

ICHING. Carries, and is the best section on the card. "Sooner or later a life gets pressed dry, and this is that hour." "Not killed. Penned." "There are words here, and the words are not to be trusted." "The way through is not spoken, it is borne." Its last line reaches to the alchemy on purpose: "You change frequency inside it, and the walls become the vessel." Line 3 ends "Stop creating the wall you keep hitting."

KEYS. Carries. "The thing crushing you is also the only door." The Gift is specific about the mechanism and the pace ("a long run of small mutations, most of them invisible"). It has one problem that is structural rather than a sentence: "It happens in the belly, low and slow," on a card whose DESIGN puts the code in the Ajna and whose BODY puts it in the neocortex. The Gene Key's alchemy is done in the belly; the gate is in the head; the card never says which the reader should feel first.

DESIGN. Carries, and holds the card's second image: "The room has to stay warm and the lid has to stay on." The drive is faithful to Gate 47 ("A mind sits inside an unfinished thought and cannot yet see the way out"), and it is a different subject from the inherited grief the rest of the card is about; pressure is the only word that joins them.

BODY. Carries at the skull, lifts at the amino acid. "The grip in the skull is the muscular version of hopelessness" and "The way through confinement was never more strength against the walls" both belong to this card and answer the ICHING. But the Glycine paragraph's first half is card 64's Glycine paragraph: "it slips into the tight turns" and "the molecule that tells a firing nerve to settle" are on both cards. The ring shares the amino acid, so the fact is shared; the sentences should not be.

RELATIONS. Carries. "the bottom of any oppression is the lip of a source." The Tarot lead earns the card's face: "the depletion turns out to be the Magician's table, already set." The Immortals earn their pair: "the breaking is not the end of a life, it is the material."

The whole. One argument, held by a word rather than an image. Pressed dry, pressed down, the pressure of confusion, the pressure of the unresolved, opens under pressure. The survey says five sections converge on pressure becoming fuel without copying a sentence, and that is true of ICHING, KEYS, DESIGN, BODY and RELATIONS. The CODE is the sixth and it copies. The vessel image (walls become the vessel, lid stays on, the house with four walls in the Hebrew letter) is the card's second thread and it is the one the reading should have taken, because it is the one that joins the I Ching's enclosure to the alchemist's flask. Best sentence: "Not killed. Penned." Worst sentence: "You are a wave that keeps hitting its own edge and dissolving into something wider." Adrian's diagnosis: fully present. Two paragraphs of weight, one of turn; the reading is mainly about a burden.

### Card 64, Communion

CODE. The right shape, the wrong provenance. This is the one reading of the six that has the four beats in the order the rewrite asks for: the force lit ("When you trust it, you receive things out of the mist that were nowhere an hour before, and you have the nerve to build them"), the low face as a lived image (the fox that wets its tail), the turn and the ask together. But nearly every sentence is borrowed from the card's own sections: "too big to lay flat" and "takes years to unpack" and "Stay with the image" from DESIGN, "Action out of confusion only makes more confusion" and "Confusion needs time. Not an answer, time, and the nerve to stay uncertain inside it" from KEYS, "drag the wheel rather than forcing the ford" from ICHING. The last sentence, "Let the dark below the ground do its slow work," is card 01's ("let the thing in the dark do its slow work").

ICHING. Carries, and is the best section on the card. "Nothing is correct, and yet nothing is out of relation. That is the whole teaching of the difficult last step." "This is where the whole book chooses not to end in order." Line 2's "The wise one looks slow, the expert looks clumsy" and line 6's "Drink until the head goes under" are lines a caster can hold alone.

KEYS. Carries. The Shadow opens on the word's root, "to be fused, poured together," which is this card's and nobody's else. The Gift's iron tower and wild horse are specific. The Siddhi holds at its last line, "And still, even now, they remain unfinished, before completion, which is the joke and the mercy of it," and lets go at "Both are true at once, which is how you know you have arrived" and at the borrowed "It was love that had not finished arriving." The Siddhi also puts the alchemy in the belly, on a head-centred card, the same unreconciled seat as card 47.

DESIGN. Carries. "A mind here is meant to broadcast questions, not to manufacture the answers under duress." The drive and the centre are Gate 64's own and they set up the refrain that runs through the card.

BODY. Carries at the pineal, lifts at the amino acid. "The pineal reads a light it never sees" and "It is the crown going quiet enough for something to shine in it" are exact. Glycine is card 47's paragraph in its first half; the second half is this card's, "The smallest letter is the one that says be still."

RELATIONS. Carries, and solves a hard case well. UL 63 is pair, inverse and programming partner at once, and the writing owns it instead of hiding it: "UL 63, Adornments of Time, returns here a third time, as pair, inverse and programming partner." The Tarot lead gives the card its gesture: "the maker's hand still hovering over the work."

The whole. One argument, and the mechanism is a refrain. "Confusion needs time" and "it will not clear one hour sooner for being pushed" and "light arises in an empty room" appear in ICHING, in the Shadow and in the CODE, and "the nerve" recurs from Shadow to Gift to centre. The head holds DESIGN and BODY. The fox is the picture. This is the card the survey names as the one where a refrain works, and it does, with a caveat: the three sections that carry it are the first three panels in swipe order, so a reader who reads in order hears it three times in a row. Best sentence: "Nothing is correct, and yet nothing is out of relation." Worst sentence: "Both are true at once, which is how you know you have arrived," the stock Siddhi paradox, on a card whose Siddhi already had a better last line. Adrian's diagnosis: largely absent in shape, since the first paragraph is lit and the low face is a picture rather than a list. The remaining problem is that the reading was assembled from the sections rather than written from the energy.

### What the six cards say together, before the numbers

Five things that the close read found and the measurement in section 3 did not, or found differently.

First, the readings are digests. On four of six cards (02, 33, 47, 64) the CODE is built from sentences that already sit in KEYS, DESIGN, BODY or ICHING. The concept's nesting law says a deeper layer never restates the shallower one; when the shallower one is a digest of the deeper ones, every panel after the first is re-reading. This is a bigger fault than the register fault, and it is the mechanism behind the register fault: a reading assembled from the Shadow will be about a problem, because the Shadow is.

Second, the formula the survey measured in KEYS and Immortals is in every section except ICHING, as shape rather than stem. DESIGN's three subsections each carry a fixed beat: the drive ends on "The misread is to call this X" (40 cards), the centre paragraph contains "the whole teaching" (27 cards), the channel closes on the Alone-then-Joined turn (52 cards). BODY's physiology runs organ, then "The shadow has its bodily floor here" (32 cards, re-measured), then the climb out; the amino acid runs fact, then "There is a teaching in that" (39 cards), then "The code runs all the way down to this" (45 cards). The Siddhi ends by looking back down the road and declaring the Shadow was the raw material (01, 02, 47, 64 all do it, two of them with the same sentence). ICHING alone is written fresh each time, and the reason is plain: it is the one section with six translations and a natural image behind it. Formula grows where the source is one line.

Third, cross-card lifts exist, not only within-card ones. "It was love that had not finished arriving" is on nine cards. "takes most of a life" closes six. "how fast or slow the whole body runs" is on 23 and 33. The Glycine paragraph is on 47 and 64 and will be on 6 and 40 if the pattern holds, because the ring shares the amino acid. A test for lift-ability has to be run against the ring siblings and the channel partner, not only against the deck at random.

Fourth, the Ring of Alchemy cards carry two seats of the body and never reconcile them. The Gene Keys section does its alchemy in the belly; Human Design puts gates 47 and 64 in the head; BODY follows Human Design. Adrian's model gives the body question to Human Design, so the head is the felt seat, and the belly can only be where the climb out goes, which is how card 64's BODY already handles it and card 47's Gift does not.

Fifth, where the six cards hold together they hold by four different mechanisms, and the guideline should name all four rather than pick one: an image at different heats (01, fire), a verb carried through every system (33, stop), a word that names the situation in every tradition (47, pressure), and a refrain of the counsel (64, confusion needs time; 23, stripped then freed). Card 02 has no mechanism and is the one that splits.

## Part 2. One energy across six sections

Written against the card as it exists: six panels swiped in order Universal, I Ching, Gene Keys, Human Design, Body, Relations; the name, keywords, hexagram name, gate and the three Gene Keys names already shown as chips before any prose; no markdown surviving to the page; the CODE's first paragraph shown alone as the card's essence in search, in the MCP summary and after a cast; and a cast showing one moving line by itself plus that essence.

### Who owns the governing image

The I Ching's natural image governs, and CODE owns its first showing. Those are two different things and both are needed.

The image governs because it is the only picture all four traditions already share. Rudd named his keys from the hexagram lines; Human Design's gates keep the hexagram numbers; the tarot mapping hangs off the trigrams. The dragon, the mare, the bed stripped from below, the mountain under the sky, the tree penned in its yard, the fox at the ford: these are older than the deck and every voice on the card can be shown to be talking about them. A writer who invents a governing image (the wave on card 47) is building a fifth tradition.

CODE owns the first showing because it is read first and because its first paragraph is the essence shown everywhere. The image appears there in plain clothes: no hexagram named, no dragon called a dragon if the word would name the system, but the fire, the ground, the erosion, the leaving, the dry pool, the last step, in the reader's own week. ICHING then teaches the image whole, with its trigrams, its judgement, its lines. The reader who swipes from CODE to ICHING is meant to feel they have found where the picture came from. That is the nesting law working: ICHING answers the question CODE's image raised.

### How the image travels, and what is forbidden

Four ways, and each section gets one of them.

Restate is CODE's and ICHING's. CODE states the image in plain clothes; ICHING restates it in the tradition's clothes and at full length. This is the only permitted restatement on the card.

Vary is ICHING's moving lines and KEYS. The lines vary the image six times (the dragon hidden, in the field, in the sky, too high). KEYS varies it by altitude: the same fire as coals, as flame, as the beauty of a thing being entirely itself. KEYS never re-teaches the situation; it says what the image is at its lowest and highest heat.

Answer is DESIGN's and BODY's. Each answers a question the image raised. DESIGN answers where the situation is felt and what it presses to do. BODY answers what is literally happening in the tissue while it is felt. Card 23 is the model: the erosion raised "what survives a stripping," and BODY answers with Leucine, the letter that says begin building.

Callback is RELATIONS' alone, once, in the Tarot lead sentence, which is already the place the cards put their single picture ("a hand letting go," "the maker's hand still hovering over the work"). The Pair paragraph may callback too when the pair is the image's other half (splitting and returning as one midnight).

Forbidden: a section opening its own image from nothing when the card already has one; a section teaching a fact another section teaches (magnetite on card 02, the thyroid on card 33); any sentence in CODE that also stands in another section of the same card; the same sentence on two cards.

### The one-breath truth

There is one per card, and it is the sentence on the distillation sheet that Adrian ratifies. The master guide's truth and the reading rewrite's centre sentence are the same sentence; the guideline says so and the contradiction closes.

It lives in CODE, in the third paragraph, as the turn, in words a stranger could repeat. It does not live in the first paragraph, because the first paragraph is the essence and the essence is the energy lit, not the counsel; the counsel is what the essence leaves the reader wanting.

It may recur verbatim once, and the place is the far end of the card: the last sentence of BODY's amino acid paragraph or the last sentence of ICHING's reading, so the card closes on what it opened with, from the floor or from the root. Card 23 does this (CODE then BODY, non-adjacent) and it works. Card 64 does it as a refrain in three sections, and that is the ceiling, allowed when the sentence is the counsel itself, is under twelve words, and does not sit in three adjacent panels. Everywhere else the truth is varied, not repeated: "never yours to command" becomes "the fire was never yours" becomes "the fire is fed before it is lit." A card that says its truth the same way four times has one good sentence and five panels of wallpaper.

### Register, and whether it should move

It should move, in one direction, and each section should hold one register throughout. The reader is walking from intimacy to structure as they go deeper, and the pronouns should walk with them.

CODE speaks to you, and only to you; it never says code, card or deck. ICHING speaks to you from the tradition: the reading in "you," the combination and trigrams in "the." KEYS speaks about a person, because the arc is everyone's, and drops to "you" only for the counsel. DESIGN returns to you, because it is your body. BODY speaks about the body, in "it" and "the," because it is describing tissue. RELATIONS speaks about this code, because it is the deck describing its own field.

What is forbidden is the swing inside a section (card 01's Gift moves from "a person" to "you" mid-paragraph) and the maker's voice anywhere ("This is why the card keeps returning to the same instruction in different voices"). The reader should feel the register change when they swipe, and never inside a panel.

### What each section owes its neighbours

CODE owes ICHING the image, unnamed, and owes KEYS the two faces, felt rather than listed. It owes every section the rule that none of its sentences may be theirs.

ICHING owes CODE the image's origin and owes KEYS a hand-off in its last paragraph, the way card 33 does it ("A deeper layer hears another word inside the withdrawal: forgetting"). It owes the caster six lines that each stand alone with the essence.

KEYS owes ICHING silence about the situation and owes DESIGN the body unlocated: the arc is felt but KEYS does not say where. When Rudd locates it (the belly on the alchemy ring), KEYS says so once as where the work goes, not as a seat.

DESIGN owes KEYS the misnaming beat and owes BODY the organ: DESIGN names the region and the pressure, never the gland. It owes RELATIONS the partner gate, kept inside as internal relating.

BODY owes KEYS a first reading (the deep-pass rule stands) and owes DESIGN the boundary in the other direction: BODY names the organ and what it does, never the centre. It owes the ring siblings a different amino paragraph, because they share the fact.

RELATIONS owes every section the doorway out and nothing inward: it may callback the image once, it never re-teaches the arc, the situation, the drive or the organ.

### The misread beat

Two beats were written as one and they are different. DESIGN's is the misnaming by others: what a person carrying this drive gets called (ego, passive, arrogant, dwelling, scattered). KEYS' is the mishandling by self: what the reader does with their own low season (treats it as a fault, thinks harder, rearranges the outside). DESIGN owns the first; KEYS owns the second; the stem "The misread is to call this X, or Y, or Z. It is none of those" is retired from both, and CODE may carry one of them only as the card's own low face in the second paragraph, never as a pair listed.

### The two body claims

Human Design answers where the energy is felt: a region, a pressure, a function. The throat as a gate. The crown as a broadcaster. The Body section answers what tissue is doing the work: the thyroid setting the pace, the pineal reading a light it never sees. So DESIGN's centre paragraph names no organ, gland or chemical, and BODY's physiology names no centre. When they coincide at the same place (throat and thyroid on 23 and 33, head and pineal on 64), DESIGN keeps the sensation and BODY keeps the mechanism, and the same sentence never appears in both. The test is the reader who swipes from Human Design to Body: they should feel they went from feeling to fact, not from one description of the neck to another.

### The one-minute test that fails a moveable section

Cover the chips. Take the section's first sentence and its last sentence and strike from them the card's name, the hexagram's name, the gate's name, the three Gene Keys names, the organ and the amino acid. Now put what is left under the ring sibling's name, then under the channel partner's. If either sentence is still true there, the section is not this card's yet. Run it on the sibling and the partner specifically, because that is where the lifts are (Glycine on 47 and 64, the thyroid on 23 and 33). A section passes when its first and last sentences would be false on the two cards nearest it.

### The register of the reading itself

In Adrian's terms: the card arrives as a live energy that has a difficult face and a gifted face, and the reading is mainly about the energy. Balance, not positivity; the shadow stays and sits inside the energy instead of standing in for it. The test he gave is the test: read it and ask what it is mainly about, and if the answer is a problem the reader has, it fails.

Concretely, in three paragraphs. The first is the force lit, in the body and in a lived moment, and it is the essence, so it must stand alone as the whole energy: what this is like awake in a person, on a good day, this week. The second is the other face, as a picture rather than a diagnosis: the fox that wets its tail, the fire banked low, the mare that sets herself first and loses her companions. Not both natures listed, not the Shadow text shortened. The third is the turn and the ask in one breath, ending up. The whole thing is written from the distillation sheet and the four answers, and not one sentence of it comes from the five sections behind it. Card 64's shape with card 01's provenance is the target.

## Part 3. The guideline, one document, in order

Fifteen headings. Each line says what the heading carries forward from the sixteen and what is new. Nothing is history; a writer at the desk should be able to open the heading they need and write.

1. What a card is. Carries forward the concept's spine (one code, six voices each faithful to it, the nesting law, no voice a compression of another). New: the render facts a writer must respect, in one paragraph: chips before prose, no markdown, the first paragraph as essence, the cast showing one line alone.

2. The energy sheet. Carries forward the reading rewrite's distillation sheet (one-breath truth, the four answers each traced to a vault file, the tone note) and Adrian's ratification gate. New: the sheet governs all six sections, not only the reading; it names the governing image, the mechanism the card will hold by (image, verb, word or refrain), and the one place the truth recurs.

3. Voice. Carries forward the standing rules: no em dash, no italics, no source naming in the transmission layer, no borrowed prose, plain words, no anthropomorphised card. New: the register map by section from Part 2, the one-per-card limit on "Not X. It is Y," the retired closers ("The maker gets made," "takes most of a life," "What this asks is small"), and the maker's voice banned.

4. How one energy crosses six sections. New in full: who owns the image, the four ways it travels and which section gets which, what is forbidden, what each section owes its neighbours, the misread beat split, the two body claims kept apart, the swap test.

5. The reading. Carries forward the four-questions method whole (Steps A to E: sheet, ratify, write from the centre, lens validation, sourcing log) and the four-beat shape. New: the register in Adrian's terms with his test; the ban on any sentence shared with the card's own sections; the first paragraph written to stand alone as essence.

6. Keywords. Carries forward the stranger test. New: the chips are shown before the prose, so no section re-announces a keyword, name or altitude in its first sentence.

7. I Ching. Carries forward the deep-pass standard, the layered structure (combination, trigrams, reading, judgement, image, lines) and the classical-text method for rendering judgement, image and lines fresh from the six translations. New: ICHING owns the image's full showing; the last paragraph may hand to the Gene Key by name of the shadow, not of the system; each moving line is written to be read alone after a cast, with counsel in its last sentence; Wilhelm is in the vault and should be cited.

8. Gene Keys. Carries forward the three altitudes and the rule to describe the energy, never diagnose the reader; the longer length wins. New: the stem ban applied here (the natures open without "the inward face"); the Siddhi does not end by looking back at the raw material; KEYS owns the self-mishandling beat; when the source locates the work in the body, it is said once as where the work goes, never as a seat.

9. Human Design. Mostly new. Carries forward only the settled placement: the channel lives here as internal relating, the partner card stays in Relations. New: the drive without the misread stem, the centre as felt region and function with no organ named, the channel paragraph without the Alone-then-Joined turn, and the rule that the centre paragraph answers the image's question rather than describing the centre in general.

10. Body. Carries forward the deep-pass standard and its one instruction, read KEYS first. New: the organ boundary (tissue and mechanism, never centre), the three-beat shape made optional rather than required, and the ring rule: siblings share the amino acid, so the amino paragraph is written per card from the card's own image, and the shared fact is stated in one clause.

11. Relations. Carries forward hide the machinery, the two-tier source naming (lineages named here, nowhere in the transmission layer), the heading logic for Pair, Inverse and self-inverse, and the placement of trigram tarot under Tarot with sky and letter under Deeper correlation. New: the Tarot lead sentence is the card's one callback slot; what to write when one card is pair, inverse and partner at once (card 64's answer); the Immortals opener freed from "stands above"; and what to say when the source is empty, which for trigram meaning, immortals and cross-mapping is most of the time.

12. The anti-formula method. Carries forward the stem-ban method and its test, would this sentence work on another card. New: the test is extended from stems to shapes (a fixed order of beats is a formula even with fresh words), the list of shapes found in this pass, and the rule that the test is run against the ring siblings and channel partner before the deck at large.

13. Sources and what they allow. Carries forward the sourcing log, per section including CODE, in one convention, and the paraphrase rule for the long copyrighted families. New: the thin-sources table from the survey's section 5 turned into instructions (what a writer does when the trigram file is empty, when hexagrams 50 to 64 have stubs, when the organ is one line), and the rule that a fact table may be stated and a chapter may not be quoted.

14. Settled questions. New: the ten contradictions from the survey's section 6, each answered in one line, so the older documents can carry a two-line supersession notice pointing here.

15. Status and passes. Carries forward per-section status as the files already do it. New: the order of passes on a card (sheet, reading, ICHING, KEYS, DESIGN, BODY, RELATIONS, swap test, sourcing log), what final means, and the instruction to re-run the measurement script after any pass.
