# Oracle Continuous Reading Design

## Direction

The Universal Language card becomes one continuous editorial reading modeled on Teajia Read. The Oracle keeps its artwork, systems, diagrams, overlays, acquisition, sharing, and card content, but removes the horizontally paginated application-shell feeling.

## Structure

- Pin the reading to the espresso, cream, taupe, and restrained bronze palette regardless of the global site theme.
- Present the hero followed by all six system chapters in normal vertical document flow.
- Replace the sticky system-tab rail with quiet in-flow movement markers and links that scroll to each chapter.
- Provide one restrained sticky reading header with card identity and a 2px document-progress rule.
- End with previous and next cards in document flow. The previous card aligns right toward the center; the next aligns left toward the center.
- Do not use a drop cap.

## Typography and Effects

- Cormorant Garamond for display, Lora for reading, Plus Jakarta Sans for interface labels, and IBM Plex Mono for technical metadata.
- Body copy uses a 16–18px fluid size, 1.8 line-height, and a 632–680px reading measure.
- Section headings use restrained italic Cormorant and generous vertical whitespace.
- Retain fixed 5% fractal grain, faint localized bronze light, thin translucent rules, and selective dark plates for diagrams and interactive material.
- Reveal meaningful editorial blocks once with opacity plus a 26px rise over 900ms using Teajia's easing. Reduced-motion and observer failure remain immediately visible.

## Responsive and Accessibility

- No horizontal page scroller or document overflow at 320px and above.
- Sticky navigation remains legible without obscuring headings.
- Interactive controls keep at least 44px targets.
- The progress bar is decorative; navigation retains explicit accessible names.
