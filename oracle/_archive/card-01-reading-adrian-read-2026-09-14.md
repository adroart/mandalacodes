## CODE

_Keywords:_ Creative Power · Making Something New · In Your Own Way · The Dry Spell Between · Waiting for the Next Wave · Nothing Forced · Beauty in What You Make

This is the creative force, the energy that begins everything and never stops renewing itself. In you it is the drive to make something that could only have come from you, in your own style, and it runs on its own timing: it comes when it comes, and it cannot be forced. When it is moving you are fully engaged, and when it is not you are completely at rest. There is no half state with this energy.

Held well, it is fresh, and fresh means unafraid of making mistakes. Everything original arrives through hundreds of wrong turns, so you make the thing, let it go wrong, and make it again. You cannot control what it becomes; you can only be there when it happens, with a light hand, the way a midwife is there for a birth. And it changes you as you make it. The person who finishes is not the person who started, and that is where the beauty is.

The quiet stretches between are part of it, and they are the part most people get wrong. When the fire is low, something is gathering in you that you cannot see yet. Do not look for the reason and do not try to fix it; that is how a low turns into a long one. Be alone with it, rest, and trust your own timing. It returns as suddenly as it went, and what comes with it is new.

### Reading
The situation is a beginning with the whole of its life still ahead of it. Everything that starts goes through four seasons: it springs up, it grows, it is harvested, and it is tested. You are at the first, and the counsel is to see all four from here, because what you begin now will have to be grown and tested before it is yours.

Keep going, and do not push. The picture for this energy is the sky: it moves all day and all night without forcing anything, and it never wears out because it never strains. Make yourself strong the same way, by using your strength a little every day rather than spending it in one blaze. The one danger in a force this strong is going too far. It can burn what it has made before the thing has taken shape, and a person who never stops will have cause to regret it. Part of the strength is knowing when far enough is far enough.

This energy has a place in the body, and it is the head. Feel for it now: the lift at the crown, the readiness behind your eyes. A beginning of this size is felt there first, before your hands have anything to do.
