## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Entropy · _Gift:_ Freshness · _Siddhi:_ Beauty

### Shadow — Entropy
The days go flat. You get to the end of one tired and cannot say what it was for. The colour has gone out of the work and out of the people, and nothing you do puts it back. This is numbness, and numbness is a feeling, though almost nobody knows they are in it. The first thing most of us do is get away from it, to the phone or the fridge, anything rather than sit in the flat. None of it works for long. What you are getting away from is a gap, and the gap came on by itself and will go by itself if it is left alone. Try to fix it, or find the reason for it, and it sets hard. The more you make, the deeper these days go.

The way through is to feel the numbness as numbness. Put a hand on your chest and notice how little is there, and let that be allowed. The energy has not gone. It has gone down to gather, and something you cannot see yet is being made in the dark. It wants you alone and quiet for as long as it takes. Trust the flat and it turns on its own.

### Repressive nature — Depressive
This is the person who lets the flat set hard. Fear has taken over, and it keeps pulling the energy down and away from the surface of their life, until the eyes lose their shine and some days the bed is as far as they get. Only they can walk out of it, and only by turning to face the fear that froze it.

### Reactive nature — Frenetic
This is the person who cannot bear the flat for a minute. When it arrives they fill every hour and ring everyone they know, and the plans get wilder, all to get away from what is going on inside. They are running against their own chemistry, and the body pays for it.

### Gift — Freshness
The light comes back all at once. One morning the colour is in things again, and with it comes something you have to say or make, and what comes out is new. Nobody has made it before and nobody else could have, because it came up out of your own dark. You take the step where there is no path, and the path is made by the walking. You are not afraid of the mistake. The mistake is how the new thing gets found, and most of what you try will fail, and you go on trying with a kind of joy. A small room with you in it wakes up. You never wanted to be followed, and people follow anyway.

The flat days are what this is made from. What gathered under the surface while nothing showed is what comes up now, and there is no having the one without the other. You cannot make it come, and when it goes there is no calling it back. What you can do is be there when it happens and stay soft while it takes its shape. An idea moves one way and then another. Let it. Then, when it has been given, go quiet again. Fresh things do not keep.

### Siddhi — Beauty
At the height of this energy, everything you look at is beautiful. A face, a field at first light, a flat grey week: each one shines with being exactly what it is, and it is all one light. This is what happens when you give yourself to what you make so completely that it changes you. There is nothing left between you and what you see. It needs quiet, the same quiet you learned in the flat days, because a beauty this fine can be cracked by one word said about it. So you say less, and you see more.

This is what freshness becomes when you stop being the one it happens to. There is no maker now. Whatever is made comes through you and goes out, and it is the more beautiful for nobody claiming it. Even the flat days are beautiful from here. It cannot be taught, only lived. You are lit the way a morning is, and anyone who wants to can come and stand in it.
