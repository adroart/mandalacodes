## CODE

_Keywords:_ Simplicity · Speaking Clearly · Right Timing · Cutting Away · The Tangle · Inner Knowing · Getting To The Point

Something in you knows things whole, all at once, before you have any words for them. The knowing arrives quiet and complete, deep in the gut, and then the whole work of your life is the crossing, getting it from that silent place into a clean line another person can actually take in. When it lands, a hard thing comes out simple in one stroke and the room goes still, because you finally said the thing everyone was circling. You can cut to the center of a person with a word and not even be trying.

Then fear gets into the speaking and it thickens. You feel it first as a tightness in the throat, the breath going high and fast, the words running out ahead of the meaning, and the more you try to be understood the further you push the room away. This is the tangle, and it is the same gift wearing the wrong face. The mind does not trust the simple thing, because the simple thing gives it nothing to hold, so it reaches for the complicated version and calls the long way round thoroughness. Underneath is the old fear of being shut out, misread, left on the wrong side of the door, and the rush to fix it is exactly what garbles what comes out.

The art is timing, and it is small, and it takes most of a life. Wait for the knowing to fully arrive before you let the throat carry it, and wait for the person who is ready to hear. Cut away everything that is not the thing. What gets stripped was never lost. It was only freed to be said again, simpler.
