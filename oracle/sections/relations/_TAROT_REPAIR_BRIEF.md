The first diversification pass fixed the I-Ching Reading, both Body fields, and

## The shape of the paragraph

Every Tarot field is one lead paragraph followed by a bulleted list of arcana.
You touch the lead paragraph only. The bullets are never edited.

The lead paragraph does three jobs: it announces which arcana this card draws,
it says by what route each arrives (the codon ring, the upper trigram, the lower
trigram), and it closes by saying what the set means taken together.

## Your job

For each assigned card, rewrite only the sentences named in your work list.

**opening** means the first sentence of the lead paragraph.
**closing** means the last sentence of the lead paragraph.

Everything between them stays exactly as it is.

## Banned, absolutely

- Any opening beginning "The Tarot meets this hexagram", in any rewording.
  "This hexagram meets the Tarot", "The Tarot finds this hexagram", and "Two
  axes carry the Tarot" are all the same sentence and all banned.
- Any closing beginning "Read together" or "Read as one" or "Held together" or
  "Taken together". The whole family is banned, not just the one phrase.
- The phrase "the whole motion of the card" anywhere.

## Required, and this is the point

The closing must still do its job: say what the arcana add up to for this card.
Find a way to say that which belongs to this card's specific images.

Do not reach for the first alternative that comes to mind. The reason this field
failed twice is that every agent reaches for the same alternative. Your wave has
been given a primary move below. Use it as your centre of gravity, and let the
individual card pull you off it where the paragraph asks.

## Holds

- Every arcana name stays, spelled as it is, with its Roman numeral where the
  text carries one.
- The routes stay accurate. If the paragraph says the Magician arrives by the
  codon ring and the Fool by the upper trigram, that stays true.
- No em dash, no en dash, no underscore emphasis.
- Paragraph length within ten percent of what you found.
- Never touch a card outside your list. Other agents hold those files.

## Report
