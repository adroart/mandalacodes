The sibling brief in `keys/_DIVERSIFY_BRIEF.md` fixed this problem for the Gene
Keys section, and `design/_DIVERSIFY_BRIEF.md` fixed it for Human Design. It was
never run on the other three. This brief is that pass.

The content of these sections is right. The way each one opens is not. Every
batch agent copied the opening sentence of the one worked sample in its brief,
so the same sentence now starts the same field on nearly every card. Read four
cards in a row and the machine shows.

## Your job

For each card you are assigned, rewrite the **first sentence** of each field
named in your work list, and for the Tarot closing, the **last sentence**.
Nothing else in the field changes. No other field changes.

If the second sentence leaned on the first, adjust the second sentence as little
as possible so the paragraph still flows.

## How to rewrite an opening

1. **Do the same work the old opening did.** Name the energy, set up the
   paragraph, point the reader at the right thing. The paragraph beneath it is
   good writing. Serve it.
2. **Do not match any banned stem above**, in any tense or rephrasing. Rewording
   "The situation this hexagram names" into "What this hexagram names" is not a
   fix, it is the same sentence wearing a hat.
3. **Stay in voice.** Intimate teacher, plain, concrete. No em dashes, no en
   dashes, no italics, no underscores, no triads, no summary sentences.
4. **Fit this paragraph, not a generic one.** Read the whole field first, then
   write the opener that belongs to that specific card.
5. **Vary within your own batch.** If two of your eight cards would open the
   same way, change one. You are one of eight agents working in parallel, so
   reach for the opening this card asks for rather than the one that came to
   mind first.

Genuinely varied ways in. The move is the shape, the words are yours every time:

- Start in concrete physical detail. "The coffee cools. The room you cleaned
  gathers dust again."
- Start with a small observation about people. "People sort themselves into
  ranks the moment they walk into a room."
- Start with the felt experience as a statement. "Numbness is quieter than
  sadness."
- Start with an imperative or invitation. "Watch a street of houses at dusk."
- Start with the misread named. "Most people read the unsettled feeling as proof
  something outside needs fixing. They are wrong."
- Start with a fragment that names the thing. "Pure beginning. The force before
  the form."
- Start with the mechanism. "Two proteins meet and one changes shape."
- Start with a question. Sparingly, at most one across your whole batch.

This is not a template list. It is proof there are many ways in. Each card finds
its own.

## Section-specific holds

**ICHING > Reading.** The reading is the working heart of the lens. Keep every
claim it makes about the hexagram. Only the way in changes.

**BODY > Physiology and Amino acid.** These carry real biology. Do not invent,
soften, or drop a mechanism, an organ, a system, or a compound name to make a
sentence prettier. If the opener carried the amino acid's name, the new one
still carries it or the second sentence does.

**RELATIONS > Programming partner.** The partner card number and name are load
bearing. They stay, in the opening or immediately after it.

**RELATIONS > Tarot.** The named arcana stay. The two axes idea may stay if the
paragraph needs it, but it cannot be the opening formula. For the closing, write
an ending that belongs to this card instead of the shared one.

## Do not change

- Any heading, at any level.
- Any card number, card name, hexagram name, arcana name, amino acid, gate,
  channel, center, codon ring, or trigram.
- Any field not on your work list.
- Field length by more than ten percent either way.

## Hard rules

- No em dash. No en dash. No underscore emphasis markers.
- The file stays valid Markdown with its heading structure intact.
- One file per card. You own only your assigned cards. Never edit a card outside
  your list, and never edit a file two agents could both hold.

## When done

Report per card: which fields you rewrote, the old first sentence and the new
first sentence for each, and confirm no em dashes and no length breach.
