Passes one to three fixed the OPENINGS of the I-Ching Reading, both Body fields,

A full scan of every field then found the same disease in closings nobody had

- `ICHING > Image`, last sentence, 51 cards begin "So the one who would ..."
- `RELATIONS > Immortals`, last sentence, 35 cards begin "Together they teach that ..."
- The banned closing family, still present in 28 places across several fields:

## What this pass must NOT touch, and why it matters

The scan also flagged repetitions that are CORRECT and must be left exactly alone.
These are reference entries, not prose. They repeat because the thing they describe
is the same thing:

- Trigram definitions. "Water, Kan, a single firm line between two yielding" opens
  seven cards because seven cards carry Water as a trigram. It must read the same
  on all of them. Changing it would make a reference inconsistent and would be a
  real defect introduced in the name of variety.
- The eight Immortals' name introductions. "Li Tie Guai stands above" opens the
  cards Li Tie Guai actually stands above. The NAME sentence stays. Only the
  closing "Together they teach that" sentence is in scope.
- Codon ring labels, and the inward/outward face labels in the Keys section.

If you find yourself about to edit a sentence that states a fact about a trigram,
an Immortal, or a ring, stop. That is not this pass.

## Judgement required on "held together"

"Held together" is ordinary English. A sentence like "a fellowship held together by
one fire" is good writing and stays. What is banned is the FORMULAIC SUMMARY use,
the sentence that steps back at the end of a field and says "Held together, they
are ..." as a stock way of totalling up what came before.

Read the sentence. If the phrase is doing real work inside a live image, keep it.
If it is a stock summary opener, replace it. Say in your report which call you made
and why, for every instance.

The same judgement applies to "read as one". "What they know together is" is always
a stock summary and always goes.

## How to write the replacements

**`ICHING > Image` closing.** The Image section carries the classical image and
then its application to a person. The application stays. What changes is that all
51 apply it with the identical construction. Vary the way the person enters the
sentence, and let the specific image of THIS hexagram shape it.

**`RELATIONS > Immortals` closing.** Says what the two Immortals teach together.
Keep the teaching. Say it in terms of these two specific figures and their actual
attributes, so the sentence could not be moved to another pair.

**The test, unchanged from pass three.** Take your new first five words and ask
whether that sentence would work unchanged on another card. If yes, it is a
formula whatever its grammar.

# Decided keeps. Do not "fix" these.

Seven instances of "held together" survive the pass ON PURPOSE. Each was read by an
editor who judged the phrase load bearing, not formulaic. A future scan will flag
them again. It is wrong. The reasoning, recorded so nobody has to re-derive it:

- **Card 7, Relations, Inverse.** "the gathering held together by trust." Hexagram 8's
  own English name is Holding Together. The sentence is built on that, and removing
  the phrase detaches the field from the hexagram it names.
- **Card 8, I-Ching, Moving lines, line 3.** "Held together with those who are not
  yours." This is the classical line text for hexagram 8 line 3. Changing it would
  misquote the source and make one line read unlike its five siblings.
- **Card 17, Keys, Gift.** "the part and the whole held together." The far-sighted eye
  genuinely holds two things at once. The phrase states the simultaneity the whole
  paragraph is about.
- **Card 27, Keys, Gift.** "A group held together by a bond nobody can see." Closes the
  dolphin pod image. A literal claim inside a live picture, the opposite of a summary.
- **Card 37, Design, The drive.** "the instrument the group is held together by." The
  group cohering is the literal subject of the field.
- **Card 44, Keys, Gift.** "the one held together by control and rank." Names how the
  rejected order coheres, inside a live contrast between two kinds of order.
- **Card 64, Relations, Codon ring.** "still being held together at once." The massa
  confusa: here "together" means UNDIFFERENTIATED, which is the exact tension against
  this hexagram's own counsel to tell fire from water. Replacing it costs real meaning.

The rule these illustrate: **a phrase is banned as a construction, never as a string.**
"Held together, they are ..." totalling up a field is the formula. "A group held
together by a bond nobody can see" is writing.

# Correctly repeating, never diversify

Trigram definitions, the eight Immortals' name sentences, codon ring labels, and the
Keys inward/outward face labels repeat because the thing they describe is the same
thing. A reference entry that varies is a broken reference. Leave them.
