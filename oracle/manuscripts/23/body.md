## BODY

### Physiology
The body seats this code at the throat, in the thyroid, the small gland at the base of the neck that sets the pace of everything. It is the right place for a code about transmission. The throat is the one narrow passage where the inside of you crosses over into sound, and the gland that rules it quietly governs how fast or slow the whole body runs while it does. Speed lives here. So does the choke.

The shadow has a bodily floor at the throat too. When fear drives the speaking, the neck tightens, the breath goes shallow and high, and the words come too fast or not at all. You can feel a held throat from across a room. It is the body bracing against being misheard, and the bracing is the very thing that garbles what comes out. Most of the tangle the mind makes is felt first as a tightness right here.

The way down begins lower than the throat. The old teaching puts the real seat of this code deeper, in the belly, in a still point below the navel where things are known before they are ever said. When the knowing rises from there, slowly, in its own time, the throat does not have to force anything. It just opens and lets the clear thing pass. A loosened neck. A breath that drops. Speech that finally matches what was true underneath it.

### Amino acid
The chemistry of this code answers to Leucine, one of the small set of letters the body cannot make for itself and has to take in from what it eats. It does one job no other amino acid does as well. It is the signal that tells a cell to begin building, the molecule that flips the switch on new tissue. Nothing starts until enough of it has arrived.

There is a teaching folded into that, and it sits at the heart of this whole ring of life and death. The code of stripping away, of cutting back to the essential, runs on the very chemistry that says begin again. Because the two are one motion. You pull the old structure apart so the same building blocks, the same letters, can be put back together at a higher pitch. Solve and coagula. Dissolve, then recombine.

The code runs all the way down to this. What gets stripped is never lost. It is only freed to be made again, simpler.
