# The entrance: every rule, in one list

**The control comes first.** Six entrances are Adrian's own or approved by him (1, 38, 46, 48, 53, 58). Any rule that fails one of those is not a rule, it is my misreading, and it gets corrected here before it is used on anything else. Four of the first draft's rules failed his lines and were demoted on 2026-09-22.

Consolidated 2026-09-22 from `WRITERS-BRIEF.md`, section "The entrance", which stays the
prose that teaches the shape. This file is the checklist that shape is tested against, and
`scripts/lint-entrance.mjs` runs every rule marked AUTO. Adrian asked for it after five
lines reached him three times in a row carrying faults the brief already forbade.

A rule is AUTO when a script can decide it, EYE when only a reader can. EYE rules are
listed all the same, so the reader has one place to look instead of four paragraphs.

## The three directives (EYE, a card fails on any one)

1. EMBODIED. The energy lived well and what it gives. Never the lack, the fix, the struggle, the wound, the guard.
2. RELATABLE. Grounded in something the reader has felt, so they are inside the first sentence.
3. PROPER ENGLISH. Complete sentences, tenses agreeing, the words Adrian would say aloud.

## Shape

4. AUTO. Three sentences, four at most.
5. EYE. Each sentence carries one idea, and grows out of the one before.
6. EYE. Sentence one: the energy is the subject.
7. AUTO. Sentence two says how it behaves, one fact, not a second definition.
8. EYE. The last sentence is the choice and what grows from it, said as what will happen. Card 38 ends "staying till it matures" and is Adrian's, so no script decides this.
9. AUTO. Sentence one is twenty words at most. Card 38 is exactly twenty.
10. AUTO. No sentence in the entrance runs past 25 words.

## What the subject may be

11. EYE, with a script warning. The energy is named in a word a person already says they feel: joy, luck, a spark, an urge, a pull, hunger, warmth, a sense, a charge, a wish, a yes, a quiet.
12. AUTO. Not a virtue: patience, honesty, modesty, care, integrity, humility, courage, kindness, discipline, generosity, loyalty, wisdom. Those are names given afterwards to what a person did.
13. AUTO. Not an action wearing a noun: the wait, the listening, the mending, the reaching, a knowing, the wanting. Anything built from a verb and standing behind "the" or "a".
14. EYE. What it does, as something you could watch happen in a life.
15. AUTO. The person may be in it, as where the energy acts or as the one carrying it.

## Banned outright

16. AUTO. The painting's title in the sentence.
17. EYE. Any verb for the painting. The painting does nothing.
18. AUTO. A lesson in the tail, phrased as advice.
19. AUTO. A list joined by "and" carrying three systems.
20. AUTO. "some people".
21. EYE. Any claim about the reader's situation or character.
22. EYE. A scene.
23. AUTO. The em dash character.
24. AUTO, once only. Cards 1 and 38 use "This energy" as the second sentence's subject and are Adrian's, so one use passes and two fail.

## The words

25. AUTO. No "not X but Y".
26. EYE. No negation as a reveal.
27. AUTO. No fragment.
28. EYE. No line that could be quoted on its own.
29. AUTO. No adverb.
30. EYE. No adjective that carries no fact: clean, calm, deep, true, profound.
31. AUTO. No simile: like, as if, as though.
32. EYE. No picture standing in for the thing.
33. EYE. No card image where the reader's own word exists.
34. EYE. No verb doing a trick: land, hold space, unlock.
35. AUTO. One word for one thing. The energy is named once and never renamed.
36. EYE. Every actor is a person or the energy doing a plain verb.
37. AUTO. The energy "gives" and "makes" nothing as a turn of phrase.
38. EYE. Name the thing that changes, so the reader does not read it as themselves.
39. AUTO. At most two uses of "it" in a sentence, and EYE that neither could point two ways. Adrian's own card 58 carries two that point one way.
40. AUTO. "this", never "the", to point back.
41. EYE, with a script warning. No content word twice, unless the repeat is the point: card 1 repeats wave, card 38 repeats stay.
42. AUTO. Commas only where English demands them: at most one per sentence, and none that a reader would not pause on.
43. AUTO. A feeling is "of", felt from inside, never "at".
44. EYE. Cut every word whose removal keeps the meaning.

## Said aloud (added 2026-09-22, after "readiness keeps its hours in you")

45. EYE. No script can hear this one. Say the subject and its verb aloud on their own. Joy grows: yes. Readiness keeps its hours: no. A pairing you would only write is a fault however good the rest is.
46. EYE. Say the whole sentence to a friend. No phrase survives that you would only write.
47. EYE. Name the energy in one word and what it does, one second each. If a listener would ask "the what?", it fails.

## Across the deck

48. AUTO. Sameness: no two cards opening on the same three words, no energy word on more than four cards.
49. EYE. Every fact is on the card's page: the I Ching, the Human Design drive, the Gene Keys gift and the body together. Nothing invented.
