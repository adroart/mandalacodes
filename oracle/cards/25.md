# UL 25 — The Mysteries Play

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Love Without Favourites · Letting a Day Be · Belonging Anywhere · Nothing to Gain · A Heart Opening Slowly · A Tight Chest · Worry With No Cause

Love runs through you most freely on a day that does not go to plan. The car ahead is slow and the rain comes at the wrong moment, and you let each be as it is. You slow down, and the day tastes different. You feel you belong wherever you are, and a grudge is hard for you to keep. What you do comes with nothing to gain from it, and people can feel that.

Some days a worry hums in you with no cause you can name. Thinking will not get you out of it. Feel it where it sits in your body, and it starts to loosen.

Nobody can force a heart open, and yours opens in its own time. The more you accept yourself and the people around you, the more love grows in your life. You are at your strongest when you are most natural. In the end the same warmth goes out from you to everyone alike.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Thunder is the hexagram of acting with nothing false in you. Thunder is the sudden move in you that comes before you have thought it through. Heaven is the strength in you that keeps going and asks nothing back. The move sits under the strength because it goes well only when it serves more than what you want for yourself. This hexagram has long been read as thunder rolling under the sky. Thunder is loud and means no harm. When nothing is hidden in what you do, people trust even your strongest words. You do best when you want nothing back.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the steadiness in you that people can count on, there every day. It sits on the outside because it is the part of you the world sees. Heaven has long been read as the sky and its sun, which makes a sprout grow and can also dry it out. Your steadiness does the same for the people near you, helping them grow as long as it does not press too hard. On its own it keeps going until it has burned itself out.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the excitement that shakes you awake to something new. It lies underneath, on the inside, because it starts in you before anyone else can see it. Thunder has long been read as a rumble rising from deep in the ground, the shake that brings a chick out of its egg. Good news does the same when it sets your hands trembling before you know what to do. On its own it startles, and it shakes you as much as anyone near you.

### Reading
You are acting from the heart, with nothing to gain. That is when things go best, and it is also when the unexpected finds you. Something may go wrong that you did not cause. Being blameless does not keep trouble away, but it does not have to throw you off balance either.

What this time asks is to keep your aim plain. A wish to be seen or a hidden bargain turns the same move against you. Then no direction helps. This is not the time for a long journey or a big plan. Wisdom here is knowing when fixing a thing would bring more trouble than leaving it be. Do your part, and let the result take its own course.

Stop turning it over in your head, and let your breath settle low in your belly. Let what you do be as plain as what you mean.

### Judgement
- Innocence. Great success, and holding to what is true brings it through.
- If there is a crookedness in you, there is trouble, and setting out helps nothing.

### Image
- Under heaven the thunder rolls, and everything alive returns to what it is.
- So the kings of old, rich in their nature and in step with the season, fed and raised every living thing.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ setting out with nothing false in it. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
You start out with nothing false in you, and the going is fortunate. Everything turns on the start. If the thought is right, the act that follows is right too. Do not reach for anything heroic. Go as you are, and what you wish for comes.

**Line 2** · _image:_ ploughing without counting on the harvest. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
You plough without counting on the harvest and clear the ground without thinking of what it will yield. Work done for its own sake goes well. Do not sow and reap in the same breath, or plant again in the same tired field. When the wanting drops out of the work, any direction is a good one.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a tethered ox led away by a passer-by. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
The ox was tied where it should be, a traveller led it off, and the village is blamed for the loss. Trouble can find someone who has done nothing wrong, and being right does not stop it. What harms you now is clinging to the principle after the ox has gone. Let the loss be a loss.

**Line 4** · _image:_ holding fast to what you are. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
You are strong, and able to act, and that is the danger. Nothing here is wrong yet, and nothing needs doing to keep it so. Do not act just because you can. Hold fast to what you are, and you stay free of blame.

**Line 5** · _image:_ an illness that needs no medicine. → becomes Hexagram 21, Biting Through.
You have done nothing wrong and you have fallen ill anyway. What came through no fault of yours will leave the same way, in its own time. Reach for the medicine and you will cling to it until the clinging is the illness. Leave it alone, and there will be cause for joy.

**Line 6** · _image:_ acting after the time has closed. → becomes Hexagram 17, Following.
The move has run its whole course, and even a true one goes wrong from here, because the time for acting is over. You know how to go forward and not how to stop, so you go at full strength into a dead end. Be still, and let what has rolled be finished.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Constriction · _Gift:_ Acceptance · _Siddhi:_ Universal Love

### Shadow — Constriction
Constriction starts in the breath. It sits as a tightness across the chest and a pressure pushed down into the belly. Most people learn it very young, from the way their parents breathed. Fear is what holds it in place, and fear is clever. It is afraid of itself, so it never looks at itself, and that is how it survives. The grip spreads into how you hold on to things, an opinion, an object or a person. Love grows where there is room and dies in a grip. Turn away from the pain and life shrinks to distraction, a half-life lived around the tightness. The harder you choke it down, the tighter it holds.

Something wound this tight is there to be unwound. Turn toward it, and the unwinding has already begun.

### Repressive nature — Ignorant
Pain that is never looked at still runs the whole person. All their strength goes into not seeing it, and they take the numbness for peace. The deeper it is kept, the more the rest of them shuts down. Now and then something breaks it open, and relief floods the chest.

### Reactive nature — Cold
Some cannot bear how their pain feels, so they hate it, and the hatred comes out as coldness. They take it out on the people closest to them. Warmth reminds them of their own pain, so they push it away the moment it comes near. Nobody gets close for long.

### Gift — Acceptance
Acceptance is a way of seeing, and it comes in three steps. The first step is to allow. Whatever is happening, you let your body feel what it feels, and after a while your breath slows. Then you accept. With the breath settled, you can feel your feelings without drowning in them. You may weep, go numb or be afraid, and your heart reminds you that underneath it all things are still all right. Last you embrace, and the mind lets go like a factory manager who has spent all day keeping machines from overheating and gives up.

Resignation is the dropped head of someone who has decided nothing can change. Acceptance is done with your head held high, almost in defiance. It says to the pain: I see you, and you are only the beginning. Each time you try to accept yourself, you find a further place where you do not. Life keeps testing it, and each time it goes deeper.

### Siddhi — Universal Love
There is nothing personal in universal love. It is what holds everything together, and here you can no longer say you love anyone more than anything else. It cannot be willed. You go on opening and softening, and it arrives on its own once the personal flavour has burned off. What remains is a knowing in your body that everything is one.

Blood has long stood for this love. What your family handed down to you in it carries both their old hurts and their healing. From here the tightness is nothing to be ashamed of. It was the grit inside the oyster, and the pearl formed around it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 25, Spirit
Gate 25 is the love of spirit, a love for life itself. It drives you to live as an offering instead of a performance put on to prove your worth. It carries a purity so bright it can overwhelm. You pick up what the people around you feel, sometimes before they have said it. Setbacks do not easily disillusion you, because you see them as part of a larger pattern. The same openness can make you idealise a partner and miss what is plainly wrong. When this pull is mistrusted, you try to make your life by willpower alone, and you end up feeling cut off and unworthy. Lived well, you take up no more room than is yours, and no less.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
This love is felt in the centre of your chest, in the Identity Center. It holds who you are and where your life is heading, and it is love itself. It works like a magnet with one pole: it only draws. It pulls toward you the people, the work and the places your life needs. When you trust yourself, what you need arrives. When you do not, you push away what you are drawing in and go to war with your own life.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They know who they are and where they are going, and somewhere underneath they know they are lovable. The other four in ten have it open. They can arrive not knowing who they are or which way to go, and whether they are lovable stays a question. Where the defined choose their life from a menu, the open go to the buffet and piece one together.

### What completes it — the Channel of Initiation (25–51)
Gate 51 is the gate of shock. It sits in the Will Center, which is also called the Heart Center. With Gate 25 it makes the Channel of Initiation, the move from the mind's control to the wisdom of the heart. A shock often opens this love all the way. Gate 51 may be in you or in someone close, and the love wakes when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The heart is given this energy because of the blood. It sends the same blood to every part of you and picks no favourites. Your brain and your little toe get it alike. The whole of it goes round your body in about a minute. It began beating about three weeks after you were conceived, before there was a brain to know it. That is what this love is like from inside, a warmth that goes to everything because that is the only way it goes.

Put your hand flat on the left of your chest and you will find the beat. When you are afraid, your chest tightens around it and your breath rises high. Let the fear be felt instead of braced against, and the chest gives, a little at a time. It does not have to be told. It only has to stop being held.

### Amino acid
This love has a chemistry in you, and one substance in it is arginine. It is part of every protein in your body. Your body makes it. A body healing from a wound cannot make enough, so it takes the rest from meat, eggs or beans.

What you carry is wound in layers deep in your body, one around another. As each layer relaxes, you can feel love moving in you again.

## RELATIONS

_Each of these takes the same open love somewhere else: into waiting, into delight in the body, or into the story every human life tells._

### Pair — UL 26, Lighter Than a Feather (Great Taming)
This love acts on the spot, with no plan, and what nobody planned can catch it out. Lighter Than a Feather is where it goes next: the strength that holds back and stores up until the time is right. Open it when acting on the spot has cost you and you need to know when to wait. Holding back is how an open heart lasts.

### Inverse — UL 26, Lighter Than a Feather
Upside down this is Lighter Than a Feather again, so the bond is one and it is written above.

### Programming partner — UL 46, Fountain of Light
This love opens as you stop gripping, and a grip is where it runs out. Fountain of Light holds what it lacks: delight in being in a body, the soft approach to life. The tighter you hold anything, an opinion or a person, the more it holds you. Open it when accepting life has started to feel like hard work.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Humanity
This love is the core of being human, and it starts from a wound. Its family of six tells the one story every life tells. The Mysteries Play is the wound you start with. Inner Light Symphony is the fight with your own shadows. Pearl of Christos is the mind's limits outgrown, and Beyond Binary is the need to control given up. Internal Treasure is finding who you are. Unshakable Arrival is the waking after. Open any of them to see where you stand.
