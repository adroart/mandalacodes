# UL 61 — Celestial Remembrance

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The Question Why · Knowing Without Proof · Living Without Answers · The Flash That Changes You · Making to Clear the Mind · Moving Others Without Force · Sincere All the Way Through

Inner truth is the energy here, the knowing in you that has no proof, and it comes with a question. Why is the first question anyone asks, and in you it never stops: it wells up from somewhere deeper than thought, refusing every made-up answer. It is what you know without knowing how you know, the thing you would still hold to if everyone at the table disagreed. In a life it is the wondering that keeps you awake, with a quiet certainty underneath it.

This knowing stops needing the question answered once you trust it. The why cannot be answered by thinking, because thought goes round in a circle to the question again, and a mind under that pressure will accept anything that promises to end it. What comes instead is inspiration, in flashes, when it comes and for as long as it stays; after one you are never quite the same. It cannot be forced, so it has to be waited for, and between the flashes there are flat days. The way through them is making something with your hands, a garden or a song. The moment the making begins the mind empties, and what was troubling it settles like silt in still water.

Stand inside the question instead of running from it. Not knowing is the whole of what is asked; the mind is not your enemy, only it was never there to keep you safe. Something in you is sure without a reason, the way you know a friend is telling the truth before the sentence is finished, and that sureness moves people more than any argument could. Live without the answer long enough and the pressure of the question turns into wonder.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Lake: the wind cannot be seen, and the water shows it, every gust written on the surface. What is inside you is like that, unseen, known only by what it moves. The traditional character is an arrow through the centre of a target, beside a bird's foot laid over its young: a bird sitting on the nest, truth kept warm in the middle until its day comes.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is air moving, and what wind does is get in. It goes under the door, through the gap in the wall, into the one place that was thought closed, without pushing, by keeping on. In the family it is the eldest daughter, the one who gets her way by never insisting. In you it is influence of the quiet kind: what you are sure of reaches people slowly, through what you do, long before you argue for it. Its risk is scattering, the wind that goes everywhere and settles nowhere, a mind that turns with every opinion it meets.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Underneath is the lake, open water, the surface that shows what moves it. Inside you it is the gladness that comes out in the face and the mouth, the part of you people can read. Here the lake is what the wind writes on: whatever is true in you, unseen, shows in what you say and in how your face sits when you say it. The two open lines in the middle of the six are the lake's part: an inside kept clear of any second purpose, with something solid above it and below. Its risk is the surface again, a warmth that agrees with everyone and holds to nothing.

### Reading
Sincerity is asked of you here, and it is a thing you are, not a thing you say. Truth held in the middle of you reaches people the way wind reaches water, unseen, plainly shown in what it moves. It reaches even those nobody else can reach, the ones who trust no one and have heard every argument. What moves them is not a better case. It is that there is no gap in you between what you show and what you hold.

What it asks is emptiness in the middle. Go in with no fixed idea of how it should come out, the way an empty boat crosses water that a loaded one would sink in, and you can cross what looks too wide. Where you have to judge someone, judge slowly: take the time to understand why they did what they did before you decide what it costs them, and put the sentence off as long as you can. Answer, rather than react. Stay firm in what you know without being able to prove it, because that is where this strength is made.

In the body this energy is the thighs above the mouth: what carries you, over what you say. Notice whether the two agree now, whether your walk and your word are one thing. Let the chest open, the middle of you empty, and speak only what the whole of you is standing on.

### Judgement
- Inner truth. It reaches even the pig and the fish, and it goes well.
- It is worth crossing the great water. Holding to what is true brings it through.

### Image

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ at rest in yourself, and no rest if you look elsewhere. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
At the start of trust, you are at rest in yourself, and that is the good fortune. Look to someone else for the certainty, or keep a private plan behind the trust, and the rest goes. The moment a second thought arises there is something else in the room, and the mind is no longer still. Decide what you can believe before you believe it, once, at the beginning.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a crane calling from the shade, and its young answering. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
A crane calls from the shade where nobody can see it, and its young answer, knowing the voice. Something true in you, said quietly and out of sight, is heard by the ones who share it; you do not need to be seen to be heard. You have a good cup and someone to share it with, none of it arranged. Speak from the shade, and let the answer come.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a comrade found, and the drum beaten then dropped, the sob then the song. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You have found someone to match yourself against, and now your mood is theirs to set: you beat the drum, then stop; you weep, then sing. A truth that depends on another person's answer is not yet held in the middle of you, and it swings as they swing. None of that is wrong, only out of place. Bring the certainty back inside, where nobody else can turn it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the moon nearly full, and a horse leaving the one it was harnessed with. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
The moon is nearly full, and a horse has left its team-mate, with no blame on either. Something in you has quietly grown near its full, and to go further you must leave the company you kept, the friend who matched you at the last stage. It is not a betrayal; a horse that stays in the team never rises. Turn toward what is higher, and let the pair go.

**Line 5** · _image:_ truth that binds everyone to it, with no fault found. → becomes Hexagram 41, Decrease.
In the place of authority, you are sincere all the way through, and it binds people to you the way one hand holds another. Nothing has to be added and nothing argued. What is inside matches what shows, so the whole room trusts it and stays. This is as far as sincerity goes, and it is enough. Keep it firm, and let the trust do the joining.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a rooster crowing up at the sky, as if the crowing could take it there. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
At the top, the voice has climbed past what is behind it, a rooster crowing at the sky as if the crowing could take it there. Words that fly higher than the truth under them fall, however sure they sound, and a bird built for the yard is hurt in the trying. Even holding firm will not save this. Bring the voice back down to what you actually know.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Psychosis · _Gift:_ Inspiration · _Siddhi:_ Sanctity

### Shadow — Psychosis
Losing touch with what is real is the ordinary state of a mind, and in this energy it shows first as a question, the question why. It is the first word anyone learns to ask, and in you it rises from somewhere under thought, as a pressure the mind cannot bear to leave open. So the mind answers it, with anything, a religion or a science, whatever promises to make the pressure stop. The mind is sick for one reason: it is trying to find itself with itself, and it cannot, any more than an eye can see itself. The worst of it is worship. The moment you put someone above you as the one who knows, you have set yourself below the truth in you, and there you stay.

Everyone is in this to some degree, and being in it is no fault. What is asked is not that you think harder. The only release is to walk into the middle of the pressure instead of making it go away, and that takes apart what the mind built, which is why it has to be done slowly, in a body, on the ground.

### Repressive nature — Disenchanted
This person turned away from the question when it frightened them, usually young. They went the ordinary road, school or career, did what was expected, did it well, and something in them gave up. Whatever they gain, they stay restless underneath, because the question is still there, unasked.

### Reactive nature — Fanatical
Here is someone who found the answer. One answer, one teacher or one creed, with the whole of life built round it so nothing is asked again. They carry it to everyone as a mission. Underneath it is a well of not knowing, and it comes up as anger whenever the question gets too near.

### Gift — Inspiration
Inspiration is breath coming in, and this energy at its fullest is exactly that: something enters you that you did not think, changing the way you see. It comes in flashes, sudden and short, sometimes a longer spell; after even one you are not the person you were. Each flash takes apart a piece of what the mind had built in place of reality, and the energy that was tied up in the building is let loose, wanting to be made into something. So this is a maker's energy. When the mind is troubled, take up a trowel or a pencil; the moment the making starts the mind lets go, empties, and leaves only what matters, along with the thing you made.

It cannot be forced, so patience is most of the work, and the days in between can be flat. A man who lost his wife rose every dawn for a year to pour the grief into a garden; at the end there was a garden, a clear mind, something made for her. Being born in a body is being born with a wound, and this is how the wound is tended.

### Siddhi — Sanctity
Holiness is what is left when the inspiration has used itself up. One day a great silence comes down, full and heavy; the mind stops, all of it, in the middle of a question. The pressure that drove the question leaves the head to go out through the whole of you, everywhere at once, so that you are entirely empty and endlessly full in one moment, with no one inside to have the experience. The question why does not get answered here; it ends. What remains is living without answers, being the mystery instead of solving it, knowing nothing, in its own time. Until you rest in this you are asleep, whatever else you know. It is a lonely way, a lovely one: a flash of it, then a fall and the work begun again, until the night you sit on open rock while the full moon drifts up the mountain, with nothing left to empty.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 61, Wonder
Wonder is the drive here, the pressure to know why. Gate 61 is where it enters: the part of you that reaches past what can be explained toward the mystery of the thing itself, and comes back with a knowing it cannot account for. You know things. You do not know how you know, you could not show your working, and you know them all the same. It is the drive that sits up at night with a question nobody else in the house is asking.

From outside it looks like being lost in thought, or out of step; people carrying this are often misunderstood, and the cost is that they begin to doubt what they know because nobody around them can confirm it. The other cost is the pressure itself. Left to run, the need to figure it out turns bitter, and the story of why things happened to you replaces the wonder that they happened at all. People who carry it all their lives learn the one trade this drive offers: give up needing the answer and keep the wonder, since being present turns out to hold more than knowing ever did.

### Where it lives — the Head Center
The top of the head is where this pressure gathers, the Head Centre, the triangle at the very top of the chart. It makes questions and inspiration, nothing else: it has no engine, it cannot act, it decides nothing, its one road out is to the mind just beneath it, which has to turn its questions into something usable. It is a place of pressure, and the pressure is to answer. The moment a question rises, the need to close it rises with it, and the whole art of this centre is to leave the question open long enough for something to arrive.

Three people in ten have this pressure switched on, and their mind broadcasts all day like a station that never goes off air. For someone like that, quiet is not on offer, and the danger is the loop: a question with no answer coming, turned inward until it is despair. In the other seven it is not, so the questions in a room are taken in from whoever is there and made louder. For someone like that, the mind is tossed by every conversation, and by evening they cannot say what they think. What both are given, when the pressure is not obeyed, is the same: a mind that can go still, the way a mountain stays where it is while everything on it moves.

### What completes it — the Channel of Awareness (61–24)
What rises as a question has nowhere to go until it is thought. Its other half is Gate 24, in the mind beneath the head, the part of you that turns a thing over until it makes sense. Together they are the Channel of Awareness: an inner process that keeps returning to the same question and refining the answer as more comes in. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone you are drawn to, what the two make is a knowing you can hold, though not always one you can say; not everything needs to be understood.

## BODY

### Physiology
In the middle of your head, where the two halves of the brain meet, is a gland the size of a grain of rice called the pineal gland, and this energy is given to it because it works in the dark. Nearly everything in your brain comes in pairs, left and right; this one is single, in the centre. It never sees light; it learns from your eyes whether the day has gone, and while the light is on it does nothing at all. When the light has gone it begins, and what it makes is your body's dark. The question in you rises the same way, from a part of the mind no light reaches, and the knowing comes without your seeing how it was made.

Feel it over an evening. Sit in a dim room; over an hour or two your body slows and grows heavy, which is the gland at its work. Turn a bright light on and within minutes it stops. It cannot be felt happening, only arriving, as everything from this part of you arrives.

### Amino acid
The pressure of the mystery in you has a substance, and the substance is isoleucine. It is one of the twenty small pieces every protein in you is built from, one of the nine your body cannot make; it comes in with meat or beans, or not at all. It is the twin of another of the twenty, the same atoms in a slightly different order: two things that weigh the same, spelled from the same pieces, are not the same thing, and the difference is inside, out of your sight.

The mystery beats in every cell of your body, and whatever is of true value in you is hidden inside your body, never on the outside. That is chemistry as much as feeling, one substance shared with your kin. Taken apart in you, it goes two ways, part burned and part turned to sugar, which its twin cannot do.
