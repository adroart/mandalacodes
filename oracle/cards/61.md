# UL 61 — Celestial Remembrance

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Inspiration · A Sense of Wonder · Knowing Without Proof · Making Something by Hand · Sincere Through and Through · Living Without Answers · A Restless Mind

Inspiration is alive in you, and it begins with a question you cannot work out by thinking. You carry the question through your day, and it keeps you wondering. Then something comes in that you did not think, and afterwards you see your life a little differently. It feels less like learning something new than like remembering it.

Some days the question presses, and your thoughts go round and round. Your mind will grab any answer that promises to end the pressure, and none of them lasts. Leave the question open, because the next flash comes in its own time. Between flashes the days can go flat.

When your mind is troubled, pick up a pencil or a trowel and make something. As the work takes shape, the worry empties out and only what matters is left. Other people feel it in what you make, and they are drawn to it. Each flash goes into your hands and comes out as something real.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Lake is the hexagram of the truth you hold inside. Lake is the openness in you that shows on your face and in what you say. Wind is the gentleness in you that reaches people without pushing. The openness sits inside and the gentleness outside, because what is true in you reaches others only when nobody is pushed. This hexagram has long been read as wind moving over open water. Nobody sees what you hold inside, but everyone sees what it moves in them. The less you push, the further you reach.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind reaches people through what you do, long before you argue for anything. It sits on the outside because it is the part of you others meet first. Wind has long been read as the weather, which changes the mood of a whole place without touching anyone. Your influence moves the same way, gently, until it is everywhere. On its own, it goes wherever the fashion goes.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the part of you that opens up and says what you feel. It lies underneath because it is what you are inside, before anyone hears it. Lake has long been read as open water, which shows every breath of wind on its surface. Your face and your voice show what moves you in the same way, and people read it there. On its own, it says whatever pleases the person in front of it.

### Reading
Here your word is believed, because there is nothing hidden behind it. It reaches even the people who trust nobody, the ones no argument can move. A small offering made honestly counts for more than a grand one made for show. With this behind you, you can take on something large and hard and see it through.

What it asks is an open heart with no hidden plan in it. Go in empty, the way an empty boat crosses a wide river. When someone has done wrong, take your time before you judge them. Find out why they acted as they did, and answer them instead of reacting.

Sincerity is felt in the chest before anyone sees it. Keep your chest open and your plans in plain sight, and speak from there.

### Judgement
- Inner truth. It reaches even the pigs and the fishes, and it goes well.
- It is worth crossing the great water. Holding to what is true brings it through.

### Image
- Wind over the lake: the image of inner truth.
- So the wise weigh each case with care, and put off the sentence of death.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ at rest in yourself, and no rest if you look elsewhere. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
At the start of trust, you are at rest in yourself, and that brings good fortune. Look to someone else for your certainty, and the rest is gone. Once a second thought slips in beside the trust, the mind cannot settle. Weigh what you can believe before you believe it, and then keep to it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a crane calling from the shade, and its young answering. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
A crane calls from the shade, and its young answer, knowing the voice. What is true in you, said quietly and out of sight, is heard by the people who share it. You do not need to be seen to be heard. Speak from where you are, and share what you have with whoever answers.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a match found, and the drum beaten then dropped, the sob then the song. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You have found your match, and now your mood rises and falls with theirs. Now you beat the drum and now you stop. Now you weep and now you sing. What you trust has moved outside you, so it swings as they swing. Bring your certainty back inside, where nobody else can turn it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the moon nearly full, and a horse leaving the one it was harnessed with. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
The moon is nearly full, and a horse leaves the one it was harnessed with. Something in you has grown close to its fullest. Going on means parting from a companion who matched you at the last stage, and there is no fault in it. Turn toward what is higher, and let the pair go.

**Line 5** · _image:_ truth that links everyone to it, hand in hand. → becomes Hexagram 41, Decrease.
You are sincere through and through, and it links people to you hand in hand. Nothing has to be added and nothing argued. The whole room trusts it and stays. Keep it firm, and let the trust do the joining.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a rooster's crow climbing toward the sky. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
A rooster's crow climbs toward the sky, but a rooster cannot fly. Words that go higher than the truth under them do not last, however sure they sound. Even holding firm will not save this. Bring your voice back down to what you actually know.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Psychosis · _Gift:_ Inspiration · _Siddhi:_ Sanctity

### Shadow — Psychosis
Losing touch with what is real is the ordinary state of most minds, and almost nobody knows they are in it. Here it starts as the question why, and a pressure to answer it that the mind cannot bear. One side of the mind reaches for a faith to end it, and the other side reaches for a science. Neither of them can end the pressure. The mind is sick for one reason: it is looking for itself. You cannot think your way out from inside the thinking.

The worst of it is worship. The moment you put someone above you as the one who knows, you set yourself below them and stay there. The only way through is to walk into the pressure instead of trying to make it stop. Do it slowly, in your body, with your feet on the ground.

### Repressive nature — Disenchanted
This person turns away from the question young, because following it would mean leaving everything comfortable. They take the expected road through school and work, and do it well. Whatever they gain, they stay restless underneath, because the question is still there.

### Reactive nature — Fanatical
Here is someone who fixed on one answer and built a whole life around one teacher or one creed. They carry it to everyone as a mission. Underneath is a well of not knowing, and it comes up as anger whenever the question gets close.

### Gift — Inspiration
Inspiration means breath coming in, and this energy at its best is exactly that. Something enters you that you did not think, and it takes apart a piece of what the mind had built. The energy locked up in that building is set loose, and it wants to become something. What comes out of you is your own, unlike anyone else's.

A man loses his wife, and for a year he rises at dawn and pours his grief into a garden. At the end there is a garden, a clear mind and something made for her. Being born in a body means being born wounded, and this is how the wound is tended. As you tend your own, you begin to tend it in others.

### Siddhi — Sanctity
Holiness is what is left when inspiration has used itself up. A great silence comes down, and all the noise of the mind stops at once. The pressure leaves the head and goes out through the whole of you, everywhere at once. You feel empty and full at the same time, and the sense of someone watching it is gone. The question why is never answered here. It ends, and what remains is living without answers. It arrives as a glimpse, and then you fall and begin the work again. One night you sit alone on open rock and watch the full moon rise, and there is nothing more to ask.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 61, Wonder
Gate 61 is the drive to know why, and its name is Wonder. It reaches past what can be explained toward the mystery itself, and comes back with a knowing it cannot account for. You know things without being able to say how, and you know them all the same.

From outside it can look like being lost in thought, and it is often misunderstood. Nobody around can confirm what it knows, so doubt creeps in. Left to run, the need to figure things out turns bitter. The story of why things happened takes the place of wonder that they happened at all. Let the answer go and keep the wonder. Being present turns out to hold more than knowing.

### Where it lives — the Head Center
The Head Center sits at the crown, and it makes questions and inspiration, nothing else. It has no motor and decides nothing. Its one way out is the mind just beneath it, which has to turn each question into something usable. It is a pressure centre: the moment a question rises, the pressure to close it rises too. The art here is to leave the question open long enough for something to arrive.

About three people in ten have this centre defined. Their mind broadcasts all day, a radio station that never goes off the air, and it will not simply go quiet. Under pressure with no answer in sight, the question can turn inward as worry or despair. The other seven in ten have it open. They take in the questions of the people around them and make them louder, so every conversation tosses the mind about. Defined or open, a mind that stops obeying the pressure can rest, the way a mountain stays still while everything on it moves.

### What completes it — the Channel of Awareness (61–24)
A question alone has nowhere to go until it is thought through. Gate 24, in the mind just below, turns a thing over until it makes sense. Together they make the Channel of Awareness, which keeps coming back to the same question and refines the answer as more comes in. Gate 24 may be in you or in someone close. What the two make is a knowing you can hold, even before you can say it. Not everything needs to be understood.

## BODY

### Physiology
The dark is the reason this energy is given to the pineal gland. The gland sits in the very middle of your head, the size of a grain of rice. Nearly everything in the brain comes in pairs, and this one is single. It never sees light, and it learns from your eyes when the day has gone. While the light is on it does nothing, and when the light goes it begins its work. The question in you rises the same way, from a part of the mind no light reaches.

Sit in a dim room in the evening, and over an hour or two your body slows and grows heavy. That is this gland at work. Turn on a bright light and within minutes it stops. You cannot feel it working, only what it brings.

### Amino acid
The pressure of the mystery in you has a substance, isoleucine, one of the twenty pieces that build every protein in you. Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with food such as meat, eggs or beans. It has a twin of exactly the same weight, built from the same pieces in a slightly different order. The two are not the same thing, and the difference is inside, where nobody can see it.

Deep in your body the pressure of the mystery beats in every part of you. Whatever is of real value in you is hidden inside, never on the surface. You share this one substance with two other energies.

## RELATIONS

_UL 62 is this card's pair and its partner at once: the words that let what you know be heard._

### Pair — UL 62, Voice of Nature (Small Preponderance)
This energy knows more than it can say, and it runs out at the edge of words. Voice of Nature is where it goes next: the small, exact words that let a little of what you know cross to someone else. Open it when you know something and cannot get it across. What is lived without words still wants to be told.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Turn this energy end for end and it comes back the same. What is true at your centre looks the same from every side, so there is no hidden face behind it. What it lacks sits beside it, in its partner, never underneath.

### Programming partner — UL 62, Voice of Nature
This energy is a pressure in the head that has not yet become a question. Voice of Nature holds what it lacks: the mind that takes the pressure and puts it into words. Together they turn what you cannot name into something you can say. Open it when your thinking has crowded out your wonder.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Gaia
This energy looks inward, where every living thing keeps its truth. It belongs to a family of three, the Ring of Gaia, whose theme is the life of the whole planet. UL 19, Solection, carries the closeness that lets you feel what another creature feels. UL 60, Woven Light, carries the limit that gives life its form. This one carries the question hidden inside everything.

### Tarot
Three of the tarot's major cards arrive here, one through the ring and two through the Wind.

- **Ring Arcana — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress feeds everything that grows, and your inspiration feeds what you make.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel turns with nobody pushing it, and your answers come when the turn comes.
- **Wind (upper) — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death is a change of form, and each flash ends one way of seeing so another can begin.

### Immortals
Wind above carries He Xian Gu, the one woman among the eight, who holds a lotus that grows clean out of the mud. Lake below carries Zhong Li Quan, whose fan is said to bring the dead back to life. Keep them near for what they stand for in you: a clear mind out of a troubled one, and breath coming back.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Venus:** the planet of love and of what draws people close, the way your sincerity draws them without force.
- **Hebrew letter — Daleth, the Door:** the door between one room and the next, and each flash of inspiration opens one for you.
