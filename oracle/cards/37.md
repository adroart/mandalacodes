# UL 37 — Journey Home

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Belonging Somewhere · Feeding Your People · Leaning on Each Other · Giving From the Heart · Hoping for Something Back · Lifting Whoever Struggles · Coming Home

At the end of the day you sit down to eat with the people who are yours. You feel affection for them in your body, as the warmth that spreads through you after a meal eaten together. You need no shared blood for this, since any few people drawn together can become a family. In you it is the wish to belong, and to see that everyone near you is fed.

Some days your giving carries a quiet hope of something back. Giving that comes from your head always does. Look after yourself first, and ask your own heart what it needs. The giving that follows asks for nothing.

Let people lean on you, and lean on them without shame. What you give from the heart comes back to you, often from somewhere you were not looking. Then your affection reaches past your own table, to people you have never met.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Fire is the hexagram of the people you live with. Fire is the warmth in you that draws your people close. Wind is the gentle way that warmth reaches every one of them. The warmth sits underneath because a home is made from the inside out. This hexagram has long been read as wind coming out of a fire. Your warmth at home spreads the same way, like the heat of a stove moving into every room of a cold house. So what you say at home needs something behind it, and the way you live needs to stay the same from day to day. When your own home is right, the good in it goes out into the world.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the mood you bring into a room before you say a word. It sits on top because it is the side of your home that other people meet, the air of the place. Wind has long been read as a breeze, which gets in under a door and fills a house without force. Your good humour moves the same way, and it reaches everyone without being pushed on them. On its own, wind goes wherever the weather goes, and your mood goes with it.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the light by which you see each person at home as they are, and know where each one belongs. It lies underneath because it is the inside of the home, the part nobody outside sees. Fire has long been read as a flame, whose light spreads in every direction at once. That is why people pull their chairs in close around you. On its own, fire uses up whatever it burns, and you can wear yourself out keeping everyone warm.

### Reading
Something out in the world has hurt you or worn you down, and you have turned back toward home. That is the right way to turn. When a birth, an illness or a death changes a life, people go home to their own. The strength here is inside the house, and what is set right there sets right what lies beyond it. So the one who keeps the house matters most. That person sees to the food, leaves no chore undone and does not follow every whim out the door.

This time asks two things of you. Let your words have something behind them, so that what you say at home can be trusted. Let the way you live stay the same from day to day, long enough to be relied on. Give each person their own place, the older as the older and the younger as the younger.

This time lives in your legs and your eyes. Your legs bring you home, and your eyes see each person there as they are. Come in and sit down among your own before you do anything else.

### Judgement
- The family. The steadiness of the woman, the one who keeps the house, furthers it.
- Each in their place, the father a father and the child a child, and the house comes right. A house set right sets the world right.

### Image
- Wind comes out of fire: the family.
- So the wise put substance in their words, and keep to one way of living long enough to be trusted.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a fence put up around the house while there is still nothing to keep in. → becomes Hexagram 53, Development.
A fence goes up round the house before anyone has moved in. Set the house rules now, before habits form and feelings argue with them. Anything young in your care needs the line drawn before it tests one. Guard your home early, and there is nothing to regret later.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the one at the centre of the kitchen, seeing to the food, reaching for nothing past it. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You are at the centre of the house, seeing to the food. Someone comes home beaten by the day and finds a meal waiting, and that is a real fortune. Look for your blessings inside the house, not out in the world. Follow no whim out the door, keep everyone fed, and it goes well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the household scolding, scolding, and the wife and children giggling, giggling. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
Tempers rise in the house, or else everyone laughs and nobody is in charge. Too strict, and there is regret, yet it ends well. Too loose, and it ends in shame, because the order of the house is lost. If you have to lean one way, lean toward firm.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a rich household. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
A household grows rich, and not only in money. Everyone who depends on each other rises together. If you were born into a family like this, you were given a head start. Know that you were, and use it well, and it renews itself day by day.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the king who comes home to his family. → becomes Hexagram 22, Grace.
Even a king comes home to a family. Whoever runs everything else still needs a place where they are only themselves. When real trouble comes, your strength is there, among people who love you and are loved back. There is nothing to worry about. Come home, and it goes well.

**Line 6** · _image:_ sincerity carried with dignity, at the top of the house. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
At the top of the house, your word is trusted and you carry yourself with dignity. You earned that by being the same inside as out. The good end may be slow to come. Look to yourself before anyone else, and stay sure that it is coming.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Weakness · _Gift:_ Equality · _Siddhi:_ Tenderness

### Shadow — Weakness
Weakness here is a belief that you are powerless, and it feels like plain fact. You look at the part of your life that struggles most, money or health or one bond, and decide it is a dead end. So you bow your head to it and call that being realistic. Most people would rather stay beaten than find out otherwise. Giving in wears you down with a tiredness that has nothing good behind it. It is not the tiredness you feel after work you were glad to do.

You have been leaning on crutches you never needed. You need them only as long as you believe you are weak without them. Put them down, and you find your legs were strong all along. The part of your life that struggles most is where the whole of it can be lifted from, once you stop hiding it.

### Repressive nature — Over-Sentimental
This person drowns the fear in feeling. They speak warmly about people and believe their moods are their heart. They go over every feeling until everyone near them is drained. The feeling is real, but it has no spine, and the fear underneath is never touched.

### Reactive nature — Cruel
This person answers the fear by going hard. They see only who is up and who is down, and close round their heart until they forget what love feels like. Soft people look like fools to be used. They need a willing victim, and crumble first when someone stands up from the heart.

### Gift — Equality
Equality begins inside you, between the two sides of you. You have a sharp mind and a soft heart, a will that pushes and a willingness to give way. When one side rules and the other gives in, you are split and weak. Let the two meet as equals, and the balance you find goes with you into every room.

The one a group worries about is often the one who holds it together. In a family or a team you go to that weak place and help it mend, without taking it over. You hold a young tree up until it can stand, then let go. The ones you set free are the ones who stay loyal. You are never too proud to ask for help. People know you by your friendliness.

### Siddhi — Tenderness
Tenderness is the way a mother holds a child, and no single person owns it. You can feel it only with other people. It passes between people and grows among a few who have been drawn together. On a bright morning in a busy market you can see it pass from one pair of eyes to the next. It has no tension in it, so nothing can push against it. It is nearer the surface than anyone thinks, and in the right conditions it comes up by itself. What frightened you most is what changes you, once you trust it. Soft as it is, nothing can beat it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 37, Friendship
Gate 37 sits in the centre of feeling, in your belly, and presses you to belong. It is the wish to have a few people who are yours and to see them looked after. Its fear is that your own needs will go unmet while you meet everyone else's. When it runs well, your loyalty holds a group together and people know they can rely on you. When it runs badly, you give until nothing is left and keep the peace by leaving the hard thing unsaid. The peace worth keeping is the one where it gets said. Its nervousness is about tradition, the way things have always been done, kept long after it has stopped serving anyone.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The Emotional Solar Plexus Center sits under your ribs, and there feeling is both an engine and a way of knowing. It moves in a wave, and there is no truth in the middle of it. What a feeling meant becomes clear once the wave has passed.

A little more than half of all people have this centre defined. Where this gate is part of a defined channel, the wave builds. You put one upset away, then the next, sure you are fine. Then someone breaks a promise, and the whole store comes out at once. A hand on your shoulder calms you faster than any words. The rest have the centre open. They take in the feelings of the people around them and feel them louder than that person does. To avoid the blast they may stay nice and leave the truth unsaid. Alone, they settle into a steady peace. For everyone, a feeling that is felt passes in a few minutes, and one that is held is stored in the body as tension.

### What completes it — the Channel of Community (37–40)
Loyalty on its own is a promise with no means to keep it. Gate 40, in the Heart Center, is the drive to step away alone and provide. Together they make the Channel of Community, the bargain a group runs on: support going one way and provision coming back the other. It keeps asking whether the bargain is fair. Gate 40 may be in you or in someone close, and the channel runs only when both are there.

## BODY

### Physiology
A shared meal is the reason this energy is given to your solar plexus. This knot of nerves sits behind your stomach and reaches everything a meal touches, from the stomach down to the gut. After you eat, blood goes to your belly along a nerve that runs through it, and your whole body slows and warms. It runs the part of you that is fed. It knows whether you have been fed long before you have a word for it.

Sit down to eat with people you belong to. Half an hour later the hollow under your breastbone is warm and your whole body has settled. Rest a hand there and you are resting it on the part of you that knows it has been fed.

### Amino acid
The warmth that passes between your people has a substance in you, and it is proline. Your body makes its own from another of the twenty pieces your proteins are built from, so it never has to come from food. Milk, cheese and bread are full of it all the same. Boil bones for a stock and let it go cold: it sets to a jelly, and the setting is proline's doing.

This energy is never carried by one person alone. It is the heart of a family, whatever the blood. You will not feel it being made. You feel the warmth when you are among your people.

## RELATIONS

_UL 38 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the world outside, where people differ, and this one is the home they share._

### Pair — UL 38, Inner Light Symphony (Opposition)
This energy draws your own people close, and it runs out at the door, where the world does not think the way your family does. Inner Light Symphony is where it goes next: standing by what matters to you among people who see it differently. Open it when you step out of the house and meet people who are not yours. Every family meets that world sooner or later.

### Inverse — UL 38, Inner Light Symphony (Opposition)
Turn this energy end for end and the inside becomes the outside. Inner Light Symphony is the same bond seen from out in the world, where people pull apart. Open it when the closeness at home starts to feel like a wall against everyone else.

### Programming partner — UL 40, Eternal Wellspring
This energy holds people together, and it runs out when you give until nothing is left. Eternal Wellspring holds what it lacks: the will to step away alone and fill up again. Giving in to weakness here ends in exhaustion, which is where that card sits at its lowest. Open it when you have looked after everyone and are empty.

### Codon ring — Ring of Divinity
This energy gathers a few people into a family. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Divinity, whose theme is suffering that opens you. UL 22, Treasure of the Way, carries grace. UL 36, Crystal Creation, carries the dark stretch that tests you. UL 63, Adornments of Time, carries the questions that come once a thing is finished. This one carries the tenderness that can only be felt among people.
