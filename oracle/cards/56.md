# UL 56 — Infinite Journey

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Delight in Life · Tasting Everything · One Sip Is Enough · Letting It Settle · The Story You Bring Back · Wonder in Ordinary Days · Always on the Move

Somewhere new, away from everything you know, your senses come wide awake. This is where delight lives in you. The food tastes of the place, and the light falls differently. You can tell what feeds you from what only passes the time.

Some days you jump from one new thing to the next and cannot settle. Let yourself be bored for a while instead. When nothing is going on, you notice what is really there. That feeds you more than any rush.

Taste one thing at a time with your whole attention, and a little goes a long way. Leave room between one job or one home and the next. In that gap, what you gathered settles and becomes yours. Later it comes out of you as a story that other people love to hear. Lived like this, life is a journey that never runs out of things to taste.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Mountain is the hexagram of being a stranger far from home. Mountain is the stillness in you that stays in one place, wherever your feet go. Fire is your attention, bright and open to everything new. The stillness lies underneath because away from home there is nothing outside you to lean on. This hexagram has long been read as fire passing over a mountain. The mountain stays where it is like an inn, and the fire moves through it like a traveller. You can stay a night anywhere and leave the place as you found it. You travel well when you are still inside and wide awake outside.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the part of you that notices. It picks one thing out from another, the colour of a door or the tone of a voice, and puts what it sees in order. It sits on top because in a strange place everything you meet is on the outside. Fire has long been read as a flame, which holds on to whatever it burns. Your attention holds on in the same way to whatever is new and beautiful. On its own, it goes from one bright thing to the next and never settles.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that knows where to stop. It stays quiet while everything around you changes, and it knows your limits. It lies underneath because a stranger needs one thing in them that does not move. Mountain has long been read as the place where the land stops rising. You reach your own stopping place at the end of a long walk, when you sit down and let what you saw settle. On its own, it hardens and refuses to go anywhere at all.

### Reading
You are away from home, among people who do not know you. What counted where you came from counts for little here. Few people are close to you, and you are watched the way any stranger is watched. This is a time for small gains, and small gains are enough. Every place on the road is a place to pass through.

Travel light and carry only what you need. Be humble, and keep what you have out of sight. Keep your loyalties to yourself until you know the ground, and stay out of the local gossip. When you have to judge someone, judge clearly and let it end there. A guest who treats the house well is welcome to come back.

Your eyes do the work now, and they take in more than you can hold. Let them rest on one thing at a time, and keep your hands still. Sit the way a traveller sits at an inn, with the pack set down.

### Judgement
- The traveller. Success in small things.
- Holding steady on the road brings the traveller good fortune.

### Image
- Fire on the mountain: it moves on and does not stay.
- So the wise judge with a clear mind and with care, and let no case drag on.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the traveller fussing over small things. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
You fuss over small things at the start of the road, coins clinking loudly in your pocket. Showing off in a strange place draws trouble, and the trouble is your own doing. You have wandered off your own path. Lift your aim above the small things and go quietly.

**Line 2** · _image:_ an inn, money kept close, a young helper. → becomes Hexagram 50, The Cauldron.
You reach an inn with your money kept close, and a young helper stays loyal to you. You mix with whoever is there without being pulled down by them. Being modest is how a stranger earns trust. Keep what you have out of sight and treat the people who help you well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the inn burned, the helper gone. → becomes Hexagram 35, Progress.
The inn burns, and the helper is gone. You were too hard and too proud with the people who served you, and they left. Even if you are in the right, you are in danger now. Be the first to forgive, and show the people around you that you care.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a shelter, money and an axe, and a heart that is not glad. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still.
You have found shelter, money and an axe to keep you safe, and still your heart is not glad. No one here knows what you can do, and there is no place yet where you can do it. What you have is real, but it does not fill the heart. Stay alert, and keep looking for the place where you are needed.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a pheasant shot, one arrow lost. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
You shoot a pheasant and lose one arrow, and in the end you are praised and given a place. You win it without forcing your sight or your strength. Stay open to the good people here, and who you are will move them.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a bird burning its own nest. → becomes Hexagram 62, Small Preponderance.
A bird burns its own nest. You laughed at first from the high branch, and now you weep with nowhere to land. You blame others and never look at yourself. Through carelessness you lose your mild nature. Nothing is heard of you again. Look at yourself before you look at anyone else.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Distraction · _Gift:_ Enrichment · _Siddhi:_ Intoxication

### Shadow — Distraction
Under all the busyness sits an ache, an old longing with no name. You go looking for pleasure to be rid of it. The pleasure fades and leaves the ache deeper, so the two keep turning each other like a wheel. Out through the senses you go, to whatever is bright or loud, and while they are full you feel nothing underneath. Too much or too little, either one keeps you numb. Meanwhile the world tells you what to want and whose fault it is. Blame what happens to you, and the same pattern comes round again. Take it as it comes, and your life starts to move. Some turn the other way, into a private dream with no ground under it.

Switch off the screen and there it is, the ache you were running from. It was the one thing you needed to feel.

### Repressive nature — Sullen
Too little comes in, and the spirit sinks into a numbness that can last for years. It is a teenager's long sulk, kept into adult life. The days turn to tedium, and the eyes lose their shine.

### Reactive nature — Overstimulated
Everything has to keep moving. The eyes above all must be fed, with something to watch or somewhere new to go. Inside there may be a whole private life of daydreams that no one sees. Feeling what is really there is the one thing never tried.

### Gift — Enrichment
Enrichment is something you stop doing more than something you do. Once you stop feeding the chase, whatever you do starts to feed you. You learn, in the moment, what nourishes you and what drains you. You would enjoy the cake, but today it would not do you good, so you let it pass. Another day you have it. This is choosing, and nothing is given up. There is a simple test: is there anything you cannot say no to? While there is, it has a hold on you.

The same energy that went out through your senses turns inward, toward where it came from. Real enjoyment starts inside you, and your senses grow finer. You find wonder where others find sameness, and beauty where others see something ugly.

### Siddhi — Intoxication
Love is the arrow here, and you have let it pierce you again and again until nothing is left to defend. Keeping this takes no discipline at all. You are still distracted, but only by what lifts you. Nothing of the senses is given up. Everything is tasted slowly, the bitter as sweetly as the pleasant.

Of all the senses, the mouth takes life in deepest, and a kiss gives and takes at once. You laugh easily from here, having found there is no point to any of it, only wonder. The people near you catch it, the way a good wine is shared.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 56, Expansion
Gate 56 sits in the throat and turns what you have lived into a story. It gathers first, place after place and person after person, then shapes what it found into something to say. It can make an ordinary day sound like an adventure. From outside it can look restless, a life that never settles on one thing. Its cost is talking more than listening. A story told from lack or struggle makes the people hearing it smaller, and one told from what is possible makes them bigger. The feeling behind a story counts as much as what happens in it. The right story, told to the right person at the right time, works like medicine. Silence can tell one too.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center is where what is inside you comes out as a word or a deed. Eleven gates lead into it, more than into any other centre, so the whole of you is trying to reach it. It drives nothing by itself. It turns whatever drives you into speech or action. Through gate 56 what comes out is a story, and the timing decides whether anyone hears it.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their voice has one steady tone that is always there for them, like a bird with its own song. With gate 56 they turn every explanation into a story without noticing. Told when no one has asked, the story fills the air and nobody listens. The other three in ten have it open. Their voice shifts to meet whoever they are with, and a story gives it solid ground. Told at the right moment, what they say has real power.

### What completes it — the Channel of Curiosity (11–56)
A story needs an idea to hold it together, or it is only a list of things that happened. Gate 11 sits in the mind and is where ideas form, more of them than a life can use. Together the two gates make the Channel of Curiosity, which turns what you think into stories. The idea gives the experience its shape, and the experience gives the idea a body. Gate 11 may be in you or in a person you spend time with, and the story comes out whole only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The journey is the reason this energy is given to the thyroid. Before you were born, the gland began at the back of your tongue. It travelled down the front of your neck to where it sits now, across the windpipe below the Adam's apple. It has not stopped moving since. It is tied to your voice box, so it rises every time you swallow. Put two fingers either side of your windpipe and swallow. What lifts under them is the voice box, with the gland going along. In a healthy neck you feel the lift and not the gland.

On the back of the thyroid sit four smaller glands, each the size of a grain of rice. Whatever you ate and wherever you are, they hold the calcium in your blood at one steady level. When it drops, they borrow from your bones and pay them back later. It is the one thing in your blood that does not wander.

### Amino acid
No substance in you belongs to this energy. What belongs to it is a stop. Your body builds each protein by reading a message one piece at a time. At a stop the reading ends, and the finished protein is let go whole. Every protein in you ends at a stop, and there is no other way one can end. Anywhere you looked in your body's own instructions, you would meet a stop every now and again.

In you, this stop is knowing exactly when to stop. It closes the places where your life leaks out, and your five senses are those places. As they close, your energy turns inward and a flame grows inside you that needs nothing from outside. It is the middle one of three such stops in you, the place where your senses lead you to forget yourself.

## RELATIONS

_UL 55 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the full house at the height of plenty, where this one is the road away from it._

### Pair — UL 55, Untouched Perfection (Abundance)
This energy tastes the world on the move, and it runs out when you need somewhere to stay. Untouched Perfection comes next: a fullness at its height, the home that plenty builds. Open it when you are ready to stop travelling and fill one place. A full house cannot last, and in time it sends someone out on the road again.

### Inverse — UL 55, Untouched Perfection (Abundance)
Turn this energy end for end and the open road becomes the harvest at home. Untouched Perfection is the same life seen from the full table instead of the path. Open it when you have travelled so long that you forget what you set out with. Neither the table nor the road lasts for good.

### Programming partner — UL 60, Woven Light
This energy takes in everything and can lose itself in the taking. Woven Light holds what it lacks: limits, a shape and a structure to live inside. It asks you to accept the moment exactly as it is, with no daydream laid over it. Open it when you are drifting from one thing to the next and need a form to hold you.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Trials
This energy is how you forget yourself, out through the senses. It belongs to a family of three, the Ring of Trials, three tests every life meets again and again. The first belongs to UL 33, Echos of Time: forgetting who you are. The last belongs to UL 12, Petals of Freedom: giving up everything you have gained. This one carries the middle, the pull of the world through your five senses.

- **Ring Arcana — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man stops and sees the world upside down, the pause in which what you gathered starts to make sense.
- **Fire (upper) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): The Emperor puts things in order, the way your attention sorts a strange place into what matters.
- **Fire (upper) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): The Sun shows a thing as it is, so what you see on the road is plain.
- **Mountain (lower) — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teach): The Hierophant keeps what was handed down, the teaching you carry with you wherever you go.
- **Mountain (lower) — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): The Hermit walks alone with his own lamp, as you do when you are far from home.

### Immortals

Mountain below carries Zhang Guo Lao, the old hermit who rode his donkey facing backwards and folded it away like paper when he stopped. One keeps you clear on the road, and the other lets you rest anywhere.

### Deeper correlation

- **Hebrew letter — Mem, the Water:** the letter of the deep, where you sink below the busy surface and see what is there.
