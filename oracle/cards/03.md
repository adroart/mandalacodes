# UL 3 — Messengers of the Infinite

## CODE

_Keywords:_ New Beginnings · Not Yet Settled · Root Before Shoot · Small Rough Moves · The Pull to Hurry · Order Out of Chaos · Starting Again

At the start of something new, nothing about it has settled yet. That is where your playfulness comes alive. You try the thing one way, then another, to see what it wants to become. Whatever is around you gets into it and changes it as you go. Making something new like this is one of the best feelings you know.

Some days the mess feels like the ground going out from under you. You want to name the thing or finish it early, just so the not knowing stops. Feel that unease low in your belly and let it be there. Stay with it, and after a while it turns into a feeling of space, often with laughter in it.

Keep playing, and give the new thing the time it needs. It will reach other people when they are ready for it. What comes of it will go further than anything you have made before.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Thunder is the hexagram of a hard beginning. Thunder is the jolt in you that gets something moving. Water is the part of you that feels how much could still go wrong. The risk sits above the jolt because the moment you start, you run into everything you do not know yet. This hexagram has long been read as clouds and thunder, a storm not yet broken. You know the air before a storm, heavy and still until the rain comes. It also asks you to sort loose threads and twist them into one cord. You cannot hurry the rain, but you can be ready when it falls.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the risk you feel once something has begun. Everything hangs on a few early choices, and any one of them could go wrong. It sits on top because the danger lies outside you, in a world that has not made room for the new thing yet. Water has long been read as a stream in a steep gorge. You get through a narrow stretch the way a stream does, by finding the low way round. On its own, this caution sinks into worry and keeps you from moving at all.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the jolt that gets you going before you feel ready. It stirs you from somewhere deep, and you want to move. It lies underneath because a start comes from inside you, below anything other people can see. Thunder has long been read as a power rising from deep in the ground. In you it is the push that breaks a shell open from the inside, the moment you finally begin. On its own, it jumps in and has nothing to hold on to.

### Reading
You have begun something, and it is harder than you expected. There is more force in it than shape, and the world has not made room for it yet. None of this means you are failing. Every new thing is hardest at its beginning, and this one is full of life.

This time asks you not to set out for the goal yet. A big move now has nothing under it. Find the people who can carry part of this, and give them real charge. Sort what you have, one strand at a time, so you are ready when things start to move. Do not force anything.

Your feet do the work now, the part of you that steps first. Keep them under you and ready, like a rider already in the saddle, waiting for the road to open.

### Judgement
- Beginning. Great success comes to the one who keeps going.
- Set out for no goal yet. It helps to appoint helpers.

### Image
- Clouds and thunder: the storm gathered, and the rain not yet fallen.
- So the wise sort the loose threads and twist them into a cord.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a stone standing in the road, and you stopped beside it. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
A stone stands in the road, and you stop beside it, unsure. Stopping here is right. Stay where you are and hold to what you know is right. Treat the people nobody notices as if they matter, and they will come to help you. Build from where you stand.

**Line 2** · _image:_ riders taken for raiders, who have come to offer a bond. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
Riders come toward you, and you take them for raiders, though they want to offer a bond. Someone wants to join what you are starting, and it is too soon. Say no kindly and wait out a whole cycle, however long that is for you. What is joined in its own time holds.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a deer chased into the forest with no one who knows the woods. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
You chase a deer into the forest with nobody who knows the woods. Eagerness at a beginning sends you after the wrong thing. Going on will only get you lost. Stop and turn back while you can still see the way, then find someone who knows this ground.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the horse saddled, and this time ridden toward help. → becomes Hexagram 17, Following.
The horse is saddled, and this time you ride toward someone. You cannot make this start alone, and the help you need is close. Bring together what you have kept apart. Go and ask the ones who really know, and everything goes well.

**Line 5** · _image:_ plenty stored up, and a narrow way out for it. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
There is plenty in you to give, and the way out for it is still narrow. Small kindnesses go well now. A grand gesture goes badly, because it is still early. What you have is spreading and has not begun to shine. Give in small ways and let that be enough.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the horse that will not go, and tears that run like blood. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
You sit on the horse and it will not go, and you weep until the tears run like blood. The strength for this start has run out. Grief like this cannot last long. Let yourself feel it fully, then let the old attempt go.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Chaos · _Gift:_ Innovation · _Siddhi:_ Innocence

### Shadow — Chaos
Holding on is where this begins. You hold on to the things that make life feel safe, like the people you love and your work. Each one is an anchor, and none of them can stop things changing. The fear under all that holding is called chaos, and it is rarely a storm. More often it is a low unease you stopped noticing years ago. When one of your anchors is threatened, the threat does not make the chaos. It shows you it was there all along.

The usual move is to change something outside, finding a new anchor before the old one gives way. It works for a while, until you notice you are holding on again. Stay with the fear instead, and it opens. Holding on turns out to be love that has not gone all the way yet.

### Repressive nature — Anal
Fear tightens the muscles low in the body, and the breath goes shallow. This person keeps a grip on everything, with the day planned and the plan checked. From outside it looks like calm. Only feeling the fear underneath lets the grip go.

### Reactive nature — Disordered
Turned outward, the fear becomes anger, and the anger makes the very mess it was afraid of. It has no direction and no rhythm. Sometimes it looks like aggression, sometimes like breaking every rule. Every flash of anger this person does not own comes back to them.

### Gift — Innovation
Innovation begins when you let change in. Cut a bush down in a garden and within days something else is growing in the space. Life fills whatever opens, and so do you. A child playing alone repeats one game a little differently each time, until a new idea turns up. You work the same way. Most of the tries fail. A mistake is only a change you have judged.

What is around you changes you too, and you let it. You drop an old belief the moment it stops helping. Give all this making one ideal to serve, or it runs wild. You feel how things fit long before you can see it, and you have enormous fun on the way.

### Siddhi — Innocence
Innocence is what the fear was hiding. A newborn has it without knowing. You can find it again late in life, after a long search with a good deal of pain in it. The two are the same, and the only difference is that the second time you know it. Nobody is in control of life, and the child in you has always wanted to find out what happens then.

Life becomes a play. You know how it ends and not how it gets there, and that is the fun of it. You can grieve on the outside while inside you watch the play go on. Home is not a place. It is the feeling of love, and you are in it.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 3, Ordering
Gate 3 is the pressure to bring order to something new as it arises. It pushes a raw idea toward a form it can live in. Where other people see only a mess, you can see the pattern trying to come through it. The way there is never straight, and to anyone watching it can look like disorder. A new thing needs time to take root, the way a child needs years to find a place in the world. The waiting is where doubt gets in. The panic to show the thing before its time is what burns you out.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The Sacral Center sits low in the body, under the navel and above the legs. It is your strongest engine, the energy for making things and for keeping at them for years. Instead of deciding, it answers. Put something new in front of it and a sound rises before you think, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time, and acting on its yes renews it. A start made with no yes behind it burns the same engine out. In the other three it is open. It takes in the energy of the people nearby and makes it louder, so you have to find which yes is yours. Every start here goes through the same cycle. First comes the excitement, then the jump in, then a stretch where nothing moves. That stretch is where people quit, and it is often the pause before the next step up.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mutation (3–60)
On its own, the pressure to order something new has nothing to push against. Gate 60, in the Root Center at the base of the spine, is the pressure to work inside a limit. Together they make the Channel of Mutation, which brings something new into the world in bursts you cannot predict. Between the bursts the seed is growing under the ground, out of sight, and nothing you do will push it. Gate 60 may be in you or in someone close, and the change comes when both are there.


## BODY

### Physiology
Birth is the reason this energy is given to the navel. The navel is the mark of the first beginning you ever made. For nine months you were fed there, through a cord, by a supply you did not make. Within minutes of your birth the cord's vessels closed on their own, and from then on you fed yourself. One of those vessels is still there, a thin cord inside your belly wall running up to your liver. Every start you have made since has gone the same way, on what you were given, until you could stand on your own.

Lie down and press a finger straight into your navel. You meet something firm at once, the scar that closed it. Press a finger's width to either side and the wall gives. You are touching the place where you first had to begin.

### Amino acid
This energy has a chemistry in you, one substance it shares with its kin, and that is leucine. Your body cannot make it, so you take it in from meat or beans or go without. There is more of it in you than of any other piece you are built from, about one in every ten.

Everything alive in you keeps changing, and what you become carries along what you are leaving behind. In the body this is plain fact. The you of next year will be built from the same substance as the you of today, all of it taken in from outside. The change goes on for as long as you are alive.


## RELATIONS

_UL 4 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the young mind that meets what this one has just begun._

### Pair — UL 4, Veils of Knowledge (Youthful Folly)
This energy gets something new started. It runs out once the thing is in the world and you cannot say what it means. Veils of Knowledge is where it goes next: the wish to understand what you have begun. Open it when the making is done and the questions start. Whatever is born still has to learn.

### Programming partner — UL 50, Melt Into Perfection
This energy brings in what is new and untried. Melt Into Perfection holds what it lacks: the harmony that comes when a thing fits the whole. Its low is corruption, which the fear of change keeps alive in you. Your chaos is the road back into its harmony. Open it when your new ideas keep coming apart and nothing holds together.

### Codon ring — Ring of Life and Death
This energy starts things in your life. Its family of six, the Ring of Life and Death, holds a life from beginning to end. Open UL 24, Frequency Flutter, when you keep going back to the same drama. Open UL 23, Beneath the Surface, when your life needs to become simple again. Open UL 27, Inner Majesty, when caring for yourself turns into caring for others. Open UL 42, Moving to Perfection, when something of yours is ending. Open UL 20, Emerging as the Code, when you want to watch it all from outside.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, come to this card through its family and its two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death clears away an old form so a new one can grow, the ending that every beginning in your life comes out of.
- **Water (upper) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess keeps what she knows until it is ready to be said, as you hold a new idea until its time.
- **Water (upper) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs still and sees things the other way up, which is what the stuck middle of a start asks of you.
- **Thunder (lower) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Tower is struck and falls, the jolt that breaks your old shape open so you can start something new.
- **Thunder (lower) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): Judgement calls the dead up out of the ground, a fresh start for what you thought was over.

### Immortals
Water above carries Li Tie Guai, the healer who came back from death to find his body burned. He went on in a beggar's body, leaning on an iron crutch. Thunder below carries Cao Guojiu, who walked out of a palace to live simply. Both began again with less than they had. They show you that a new start can be made from what is left.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Scorpio:** the sign of what dies and is remade, the change that goes all the way down in you before something new can grow.
- **Hebrew letter — Nun, the Fish:** the fish lives in deep water without drowning, the way you can move through a beginning you cannot yet see.
