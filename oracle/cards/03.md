# UL 3 — Messengers of the Infinite

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Something Just Begun · Full With Nothing to Show · Root Before Shoot · The Pull to Hurry · Trying Until It Holds · Order Out of the Mess · Starting Again

This is the energy of a beginning, the moment something new is alive in you and does not yet have a shape. A seed lies in the ground the whole winter, alive, with nothing above the ground to say so, and that is where you are now. You can feel how real it is without being able to say what it will become.

In balance, this energy takes its time. Whatever you begin, you begin on strength stored up beforehand, against ground that does not give. The first tries fail, and the failing is not the end of anything. Each one puts strength into what is coming, so what finally comes through lasts longer than anything that came easily. You make small moves and let them be rough. The ones that hold become ground for the next. There is more power in a new thing than looks possible. A shoot, when the rain finally wakes it, will split a rock.

Nothing about this is orderly, and it is not meant to be. What looks like chaos is only what a beginning looks like from inside it. There was never any chaos, only the difficulty of starting. The hard part is the not-knowing. It asks to be settled early, and settling it early is the one way to lose it. A thing pushed to be finished before its root has gone down goes flat. Build slowly and stay steady, because when the breakthrough comes it comes all at once.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Thunder: clouds built up overhead, and under them a shock in the ground, the first movement that wakes what was asleep there. The storm is full and the rain has not fallen. The traditional character is a picture: a blade of new grass above the line of the ground, and under it a root drawn longer than the shoot.

### The symbol
Six lines, read from the bottom up, the bottom where this begins and the top where it ends. A whole line pushes and a broken line gives way. Here one whole line sits at the very bottom under two that give way, and one sits high in the fifth place with a broken line on either side. Something strong has started under a weight, and something strong holds above it, hemmed in. The middle has not settled yet.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the stream, and a stream goes down. It runs into the gorge, fills the low place to the brim, then goes on, so wherever the ground drops it is the depth, the pit that has to be crossed. In the year it is the rain that has gathered and not yet come down. In a family it is the one who goes into the deep places the others walk around. In you it is depth: you feel what is happening all the way down, and you keep going. Its risk is falling in, when the low place you went through becomes the place you stay.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the shock, and it comes up out of the ground before it is heard in the sky. One crack of it wakes what slept through the winter: the seed splits, the bud breaks. It is the force that moves heavy things by startling them. In a family it is the eldest son, the first to try anything. In you it is the first impulse, the surge that says go before anything is ready, felt in the gut long before the head has finished. Its risk is starting what it cannot yet carry, so that the leap is taken with nowhere to land.

### Reading
A beginning is under way in you, and this is the hardest part of it. Two things have met in you for the first time, the push to move and the weight above it. The difficulty is nothing more than that meeting. The storm has built and the rain has not come down. What you have begun belongs to the whole cycle ahead of it, the growing, the gathering, the trial, not only to this rough first part. Disorder is how everything starts. Order comes after, a little at a time.

What it asks of you is to stand firm where you are. Do not set out toward the goal yet, because from here it cannot be seen. Take the loose strands of what you have and twist them into one cord you can hold. Give people real charge of the parts you cannot carry, and keep the ground you have found. Your mind leads the body the way a ruler leads a country, so set it right before anything else. Hurrying is the one thing that turns difficulty into danger.

In the body this energy is the foot and the ear: the foot that steps first, the ear that hears what is coming before the eye can see it. Notice which of the two is louder in you now. Hold yourself the way a seed holds under the ground, with the root going down while nothing shows, because the root is what the whole plant will stand on.

### Judgement
- The sprout, in the difficulty of its beginning, comes through whole: it springs, it grows, it is gathered, it is tried.
- It goes well for the one who stays true.
- Do not set out toward a goal yet. Put the helpers in their places.

### Image
- Clouds and thunder, the storm built and the rain not yet down.
- So the wise sort the loose strands, and twist them into a cord that holds.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a post set in the ground, the marker that will not be moved. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
Stopped at the first step, with the ground saying no, you hesitate, and the hesitation is right. What is in you is strong and it is at the bottom, where a foundation belongs. Make one fixed point here that people can find, and keep it. Value the ones nobody counts, because they are the ones who gather. Stay on the ground you have found and let them come to you.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a horse saddled and standing still, and riders coming who are not raiders but suitors. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
Mounted, not moving, wanting to act without acting, you see riders coming and brace for raiders. They have come to ask for you. The offer is real, and the time is not yours yet. What is joined too soon does not hold. Let a whole cycle pass if it takes one, and do not reach for the quick answer while you are in the hard part.

**Line 3** · _image:_ chasing a deer into a forest with no guide. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
Chasing a deer into the forest with no one who knows the ground, you are eager and lost. What you are after is not what you took it for, and the eagerness is what mistook it. The wise read the signs and give up the chase. Going on from here brings regret. When you are lost, do not wander: stay where you are and trace your steps back.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the same horse, and this time it moves toward the meeting. → becomes Hexagram 17, Following.
Mounted and standing still again, you are sought by someone who can help, so this time the answer is yes. What has changed is not the horse but the time. Bring together what you have been keeping apart, the parts of the work and the parts of yourself. Going to find the ones who really know is the intelligent thing. Go, because there is light in it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the sprout full of sap, the oil in the press not yet poured. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
Full of sap, not yet able to pour it, you have the riches gathered with no way yet to give them out. It is spreading in you and cannot shine yet. Small acts done right will prosper. Great ones will fail, because the time is early and the way out is narrow. Let the striving end here, and keep to the small things until it is time.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the horse saddled and standing, and tears that run like blood. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
Mounted, unable to go, weeping until the tears run like blood, you have reached the end of the difficulty with nothing left to drive the horse. The way out is in sight, you cannot take it by yourself, and that is the whole of the grief. Nothing lasts, not even this. When the weeping is done, get down from the horse and go on with what you have.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Chaos · _Gift:_ Innovation · _Siddhi:_ Innocence

### Shadow — Chaos
At its lowest this energy is the fear of change, and the fear closes the hands. Whatever you rely on, the people, the house, you hold as if holding could make it stay. It works for a while, since a thing held still looks like safety. The chaos you dread is rarely a storm. It is an undercurrent, a low unease that lives in the belly and never quite settles. When one of the things you hold is threatened, the unease comes up, and what it shows is not a new disorder but how hard you have been holding all along. The usual move is to find something else to hold before the old one gives way, so the fear never has to be felt. Changing everything around you does not touch it. You were made to change, and every part of you is afraid to.

The word for it once meant open space, and fear turned it into disorder. Stay with the unease where it lives and it shows what it is: a love that has not gone the whole way yet. Love a little further, and the hands open on their own.

### Repressive nature — Anal
One person keeps everything in its place. Fear has tightened the muscles low in the body, the breath has gone shallow, and the days are run so nothing can move. From outside it looks like steadiness. Only feeling the fear loosens it, and the more they allow, the more they find they were held.

### Reactive nature — Disordered
Another throws the fear out as anger. A fight is picked, a plan is blown up, and the mess the fear was about is now outside where everyone can see it. It has no direction, no rhythm and no purpose. Every anger that is not owned adds to it, and it comes back.

### Gift — Innovation
At its best this energy makes something new out of what is changing, and your hands are busy with it. Clear a space and something grows into it. That is how life works, and it is how you work. You try a thing, then the same thing a little differently, the way a child sorting buttons into boxes stumbles on a new game. Most of the tries fail. The failing is not a mistake but the trying, and it goes on until one holds. What you are making is not made alone. What is around you gets in and changes the work, then changes you, so you keep updating where you stand. You can feel that it all fits before you can see how. It is the best feeling you know.

The order you are looking for is already inside the mess, the way the tree is already inside the acorn, and the work is letting it out. Keep the best version of the thing in view and give the energy to that, because this much energy with nothing to serve will drive you mad.

### Siddhi — Innocence
At its height this energy is the innocence of someone who has been through everything and opened again. A newborn has it without knowing. So does the old woman whose heart came open after a long life, and the only difference between them is that she knows. No one is in control, and the child in you has always wanted to find out what happens. The hands are open because there is nothing left to guard.

Turned loose into a fresh field, even old cattle jump like calves, and that is what this is: the joy of filling an empty space with your own dance. You know how it ends, nobody knows the road, and the road is the play. You can grieve on the outside and watch it go on underneath. What you were looking for at the heart of everything turns out to be love.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 3, Ordering
In the body this energy is the pressure to bring something new into a shape it can live in. Gate 3 is where it comes in: the drive that takes what has never existed and tries form after form until one holds. It can see the pattern in a mess where other people see only the mess. It runs in you as an urge to get the raw thing into some order the moment it arrives, and the order never comes in a straight line. A new thing takes time to take root, the way a child takes years before it can do a day's work, and the waiting is where you doubt yourself. The pressure is to force it early, to push too hard or speak too soon, into a world that is not ready for it.

From outside the trying looks like disorder, and you are misunderstood more than most. People who carry this all their lives learn that what they bring lands when the time is right, not before. The timing is as much the genius as the idea.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
Low in the belly, between the hips, is where this pressure rises: the Sacral Centre, the strongest engine you have. It is the energy that gets up in the morning, works all day and falls asleep used up. It does not decide, it answers. Put the new thing in front of it and a sound rises before you have thought, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat. It answers only what is actually there, never a question you asked yourself in your head.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time. For someone like that, a yes to the new thing means the energy will not run out, because it renews by being used, while a start with no yes behind it burns them out. In the other three the engine is not fixed, and it runs on what is around it. For someone like that, the pull to begin may be everyone else's, taken in and made louder, so their strength is knowing when to stop. Every drive here goes through one cycle: the excitement, the jump in, then the plateau where nothing seems to move. The plateau is where people quit, and it is usually the pause before the next stretch. Whether you are finished or only paused is a question this place can answer, if you ask it as a yes or a no.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mutation (3–60)
The pressure to give a new thing a shape needs something to hold against, or it changes for changing's sake. Its other half is Gate 60, at the base of the body where the pressure to keep moving lives, and that gate holds on to what works. The two make the Channel of Mutation, through which change comes in bursts, never on a schedule: a seed under the ground, growing, unseen, that nothing can push. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the new thing only takes when the push and the holding meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
In the body this energy is given to the navel, and the reason is birth. For nine months everything that fed you came in at that one point, from a supply you did not make. In your first minutes the cord was cut, and the opening closed itself. From then on you did for yourself what had been done for you, on nothing but what you had been given. Just under it, low in the belly, is where the energy you started with is kept, the root that everything after grows from. Everything you have begun since has begun the same way: fed before it could feed itself, then cut loose, with only what it had been given to live on.

Press a finger straight into your navel and it meets a firm floor at once, the scar that closed the opening. Press a finger's width to either side and the wall gives, because there is only belly behind it. Rest a finger on the firm point and you are resting it on the first beginning you ever made.

### Amino acid
The energy that begins things has a chemistry in you, shared with its kin, and the substance is leucine. You cannot make it; your body takes it in, from meat, beans or milk, or goes without. There is more of it in you than of any other piece you are built from, about one in every ten. What begins in you is built of what you were fed, taken in from outside, none of it made by you.

Life goes on changing, and as it changes it takes with it what it has just left. In your body that is chemistry before it is anything else. What you are becoming is built of the same substance as what you were, and the change runs on what you take in. What stops changing in a body is dying.


## RELATIONS

_Read alongside UL 4, this is the whole arc of first arrival, the birth that comes in a storm and the long apprenticeship after it._

### Pair — UL 4, Veils of Knowledge (Youthful Folly)
Veils of Knowledge is this beginning a season on. The shoot is up and does not yet know what it is. The not-knowing has moved from your belly to your head. This card asks you to wait. That one asks you to ask once and then listen. Open it when the thing has started and you have begun hunting for the reason. The two are one arrival: the push out and the schooling of what pushed out.

### Programming partner — UL 50, Melt Into Perfection
The Gene Keys set this card opposite Melt Into Perfection, the slow refining of what is already there. You bring the new thing raw. It holds the standard and cooks out what is wrong one habit at a time. Each is the other's missing half: a beginning with nothing to test it against and a refining with nothing new to refine. Open it when you keep starting and nothing keeps. What you are bringing has to pass through what is already cooking.

### Codon ring — Ring of Life and Death
This card belongs to a family of six that hold one whole life between them. This one begins it. The others are the watching and the cutting away, then the return and the feeding, then the finishing. What the six share is a trust in what looks like a mess. The mess is where life finds its next shape. Every one of them knows it from a different point in the life. Open any of them to see that trust further along. Here it is the first breath.

### Tarot
Clouds and thunder, the rain not yet down: five faces wait in that weather.

- **Ring Arcana — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Through its family this card carries Death: the passage every new thing crosses to get into the world. The seed stops being a seed so that it can become a shoot. Meet this face when the old form has to go before the new one can stand.
- **Water (upper) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The storm overhead is also a depth and the High Priestess reads it. She sits between two pillars and does not move. Her counsel is this card's own: gather and listen and do not set out yet.
- **Water (upper) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs by one foot and is calm about it. From upside down the situation finally makes sense. He is the pause this card asks for, held until the view turns over.
- **Thunder (lower) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): Lightning into the top of a building that was going to fall anyway. The Tower is the crack that lets the buried thing out. Meet this face when a structure has to break for the beginning to happen.
- **Thunder (lower) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): A trumpet sounds and what was buried gets up. Judgement is the call the push at the root answers, whether or not the ground agrees. Meet this face when the new thing rises before you feel ready.

### Immortals
Two who began again in poor circumstances go with this card. Li Tie Guai was the healer who came home to find his body burned and started over inside a dead beggar's body on an iron crutch with a gourd of medicine. Cao Guojiu walked out of a palace in disgust at his family and in the stories became the patron of the stage. One began again in a borrowed body and the other in a chosen poverty.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Scorpio:** the sign of what has to die in order to change, a depth that keeps what it holds until it is transformed. A seed rots a little in this ground before it splits.
- **Hebrew letter — Nun, the Fish:** the letter of life carried whole through moving water. What passes through this beginning comes out the other side still alive.
