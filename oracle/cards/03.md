# UL 3 — Messengers of the Infinite

## CODE

_Keywords:_ New Beginnings · Not Yet Settled · Root Before Shoot · Small Rough Moves · The Pull to Hurry · Order Out of Chaos · Starting Again

Something new is alive in you, and this is the energy of its beginning. It has no shape yet, and it is already the most real thing you carry. You can feel how much is there without being able to say what it will become. That is what a beginning is from the inside, fullness and not knowing in one breath. Underneath, where nothing shows, a root is going down, and a root goes down for a long time before anything comes up.

Carried well, this energy knows what the wrong turns are for. Everything that has ever grown began by pushing against something harder than itself, and the pushing is where its strength comes from. So the missteps at the start are not the cost of the thing but the making of it. What you have to push with is the store you began with, and it is enough, because everything this will become is already folded inside it. Keep the moves small and let them be rough. A small move made now holds.

The hard part is the pull to hurry. Not knowing what this will become is uncomfortable, and the discomfort asks you to settle it early, to name it or plan it to the end so the not knowing can stop. Settle it now and you settle it small. The store you began with is for one morning, the morning the ground gives, and a seed does not spend it early on the way there. Stay with the small moves, and when the ground gives it will not be little by little.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Thunder: the storm gathered above, and under it the shock that starts things. Clouds and thunder fill the sky, with the rain not yet fallen. The traditional character draws a sprout pressing up through hard ground, with the root drawn far longer than the shoot: what shows is the least of it, and what holds it is below.

### The symbol
One whole line lies at the bottom with two open lines on top of it, and one whole line stands in the fifth place between two open ones. Read from the bottom, where every figure begins: the strength here is at the root, under a weight, and the other strength is high up with give on either side. Between them everything is open, so the middle is not settled yet. That is the picture of a newborn thing, firm where it grips, firm where it reaches, with nothing fixed in between.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the deep place and the way through it. A river in a gorge cannot see out, and it does not stop. It finds the low ground, fills it, goes on. Overhead here it is the cloud, full and not yet rain. In a family it is the middle son, the one who goes down into what the others walk around. In you it is depth: you feel a thing all the way down and keep moving anyway. You can sit with what has no answer yet. Its risk is going under, when the depth stops being a way through and becomes the place you live.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the shock that starts the year. One crack of it in early spring and what slept in the ground is awake. Its whole work is to come out, out of the egg, out of the bud. In a family it is the eldest son, the one who moves first. In you it is the first move, the surge that says go before anything is ready, a courage that comes up from the gut rather than down from the head. It is what gets a thing started at all. Its risk is starting what it cannot yet hold, the leap taken with nowhere to land.

### Reading
You have started something in a hard place, and the difficulty you feel is the difficulty of every start. The push is from below, the shock that woke the thing; the weight is above, a storm that has built and not broken. Between the two, what you have begun is trying to get its head above the ground, and the ground is hard.

What it asks of you is not to set out for the goal yet. Nothing large should be undertaken from here. Gather the loose strands of what you have, sort them, and twist them into one cord you can hold, because the disorder is only strands that have not been laid together. Then find the people who can carry part of it and give them real charge, since a beginning that is one person's alone stays small. Keep near you someone who knows this ground, and do nothing by force.

In the body the shock is the foot, the part of you that steps first. The deep water is the ear, which hears the rain coming before the eye sees it. Notice which of the two you are living in now. The posture for this hour is a horse saddled and standing still: ready in every muscle, going nowhere yet. Keep your strength in, and let it thicken until the rain comes.

### Judgement
- The sprout. Through the difficulty, it grows all the way.
- It goes well for the one who stays true.
- Do not set out toward a goal yet. Put the helpers in their places.

### Image
- Clouds and thunder, the storm built and the rain not yet down.
- So the wise sort the loose strands, and twist them into a cord that holds.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a post set in the ground, the marker that will not be moved. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
A post set in the ground, and you standing at it, wanting to go on. The ground says not yet, and your hesitation is the right answer. Instead of pushing forward, make one point that does not move, where people can find you. Value the ones nobody counts; a beginning is won by the many, not the few. Stay, and let them gather.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a horse saddled and standing still, and riders coming who are not raiders but suitors. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
Hoofbeats out of the dust, and you brace for raiders who turn out to be suitors. Someone wants to join what you are starting, and the offer is real; the timing is not. Decline it kindly and let a whole cycle pass, however long that is in your life. What is joined too soon does not hold. What is joined in its time has a house to keep it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ chasing a deer into a forest with no guide. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
A deer glimpsed ahead, and you after it into the forest with nobody who knows the woods. Eagerness at a beginning mistakes what it is chasing, and there is no shame in that until you go on. Going deeper ends in a place you cannot name, with a good beginning spent on the way. The way back is still visible. Turn while it is.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the same horse, and this time it moves toward the meeting. → becomes Hexagram 17, Following.
The same horse, still saddled, and this time it moves toward the meeting. You have stopped trying to make the beginning alone. Someone has been reaching toward you, and it is time to go to them. Bring together what you have kept apart, in the work as in yourself, then find the ones who really know. Seeking and going are one motion here, with light in it. Go.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the sprout full of sap, the oil in the press not yet poured. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
The sprout full of sap, and the sap not able to get out. What you have been growing has gathered inside you, and the way out is still narrow. Give what you can in small things and it prospers. Reach for the great gesture and it fails; the giving is not ripe yet. What is rich in you is spreading and cannot shine. Let the small good acts be enough.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the horse saddled and standing, and tears that run like blood. → becomes Hexagram 42, Increase.
The horse saddled, standing, and tears that run like blood. At the top of the difficulty the strength for it has run out; you sit in the saddle with no whip in your hand, while the thing you wanted stands in plain view. Nobody can say how long this lasts, only that it does not last. Grieve it honestly, without pretending the effort is alive. Then begin again, smaller.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Chaos · _Gift:_ Innovation · _Siddhi:_ Innocence

### Shadow — Chaos
The fear in this energy is that what you lean on will not stay. The people you love, the work you do: each is a hand holding something still, and somewhere you know that none of it can be made to stay. So the hands close. What you are afraid of is called chaos, and it is not the storm you picture. It is quieter than that, an unease under ordinary days, a low hum you stopped hearing years ago. A threat to one of the things you hold does not make the chaos. It shows you it was there. The usual move is to change something outside, to find a new thing to hold before the old one gives. That works for about as long as it takes to notice you are holding again.

The fear lives low in the belly as a tremor, and it asks only to be felt. Stay with it and it does not get worse; it opens. What you took for the ground going was something starting. Under the fear, the whole time, was love, and the clinging was only love that had not gone all the way.

### Repressive nature — Anal
One person holds everything in place. The day is planned, the plan is checked, and the breath is kept small so nothing inside can move. From outside it looks like steadiness. Nothing in there has been let change for years, and only touching the fear itself would loosen the grip.

### Reactive nature — Disordered
The other throws the fear out as anger. A fight is picked, a plan is blown up, and the mess they were afraid of is now outside where everyone can see it. It has no direction and no rhythm. Every anger that goes unowned adds to it, and comes back.

### Gift — Innovation
Something new comes out of what is changing, and making it is the best feeling you know. Cut a bush down in a garden and within days something else is growing in the space; that is this energy in you. You can work while the ground moves. You try a thing. Most of the tries fail, and the failing is how the shape is found. A child at play repeats one thing, a little differently each time, until something nobody planned appears, and you are doing what the child does. Mistakes are only changes with a judgement on them.

The hands that were holding are open now, so what is around you gets in and changes you. Other people's hands end up in the work, and it is better for them. What the trying needs is one fixed thing to turn around: keep the best version of what you are making in view and give the energy to that, because this much making with nothing to serve runs wild. You do not need to know the way there. You can love the work now without needing it to stay.

### Siddhi — Innocence
Innocence is what is left when you have lost what you held, more than once, and are still delighted by what arrives. The hands are open because there is nothing left to protect. A newborn has it without knowing; a very old person has it again, after a long search with a good deal of pain in it. The two are the same thing, and the only difference is that the old one knows. Nobody is in control. The child in you has always wanted to find out what happens then.

From here life is a play. You know how it ends, nobody knows the road, and the road is the play. You can grieve on the outside while underneath you watch the play go on, and the grief is part of it. Home was never in the body or anywhere in the sky. It is the feeling of love itself, and you are in it.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 3, Ordering
Giving a new thing a shape that will hold is the pressure this lens sees in the energy of beginning. Gate 3 is where it enters: the drive that takes what has never existed and finds the form it can live in, without which nothing new would ever settle into the world. In you it runs as an urge to get the raw thing into some order the moment it arrives. You try a shape, then another, and keep going until one holds. Where others see a mess you can see the structure that is trying to come through, and that is the strength. It is also why the process cannot be made to run straight.

From outside, the trying looks like disorder, and you may be called someone who starts things without finishing them. What is happening is that a new thing takes time to take root, the way a child takes years before it can be productive, and the waiting is where the doubt gets in. People who carry this all their lives learn to let the tries be tries, and that the panic to bring it out before its time is what burns them.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The place this pressure rises from is low in the body, under the navel, above the legs: the Sacral Centre. This is the engine that gets up in the morning, works all day, and falls asleep used up. It is the strongest engine you have, the energy for making things, and a new thing that is trying to begin begins from here. It does not decide. It answers. Put the new thing in front of it and a sound rises before your mind has had a say, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time, and the lift is the most trustworthy thing they have. Acted on, it does not run out, because it renews by being used; a start made with no yes behind it burns the same engine out. In the other three it is open; it takes in the energy of whoever is near, makes it louder, and leaves you pulled by everyone else's yes, so the question is which one is yours. Every beginning here goes through a cycle: the first excitement, the jump in, then the stretch where nothing seems to move. That stretch is where a person quits, and it is often the pause before the next level. Finished or only paused is a question this place can answer, if it is asked as a yes or a no.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mutation (3–60)
The pressure to give a new thing a shape needs a limit to push against. Its other half is Gate 60, at the base of the trunk, the pressure that accepts what cannot be changed and works inside it. Together they make the Channel of Mutation, through which the new gets into the world and stays. It moves in bursts, with a still stretch between them in which the seed is growing and nothing can push it. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the change only happens where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
The place in the body for this energy is the navel, and the reason is birth. The navel is the mark of the first beginning you ever made. For nine months you were fed there by a supply you did not make. In your first minutes outside, the cord was cut and the vessels closed on their own. From then on you did for yourself what had been done for you. What the vessels became still ties your wall at that point, one cord running up to your liver, two running down. Everything you have begun since was begun the same way, on a store you were given, before you could do it for yourself.

Press a finger straight into the navel and you meet something firm at once, the scar that closed it. Press a finger's width to either side and the wall gives. There is no muscle under your finger there, only the sheets from the two sides meeting, with the scar in their one gap. You are pressing on the place where you first had to begin.

### Amino acid
The beginning in you has a chemistry, one substance it shares with its kin, and the substance is leucine. You cannot make it; your body takes it in, from meat or beans, or goes without. There is more of it in you than of any other piece you are built from, about one in every ten, so it is in everything you are made of, the hands as much as the heart.

Everything alive in you is changing, and what you are becoming takes with it what you have just left. In the body that is a fact about substance: what you will be is built of the same stuff as what you were, taken in from outside. The change never stops while you are alive, and the day it stops is the day you are not. While this energy is in you, the change is what you feel.


## RELATIONS

_Read alongside UL 4, this is the whole arc of first arrival, the birth that comes in a storm and the long apprenticeship after it._

### Pair — UL 4, Veils of Knowledge (Youthful Folly)
Veils of Knowledge is this beginning a season on. The shoot is up and does not yet know what it is. The not-knowing has moved from your belly to your head. This card asks you to wait. That one asks you to ask once and then listen. Open it when the thing has started and you have begun hunting for the reason. The two are one arrival: the push out and the schooling of what pushed out.

### Programming partner — UL 50, Melt Into Perfection
The Gene Keys set this card opposite Melt Into Perfection, the slow refining of what is already there. You bring the new thing raw. It holds the standard and cooks out what is wrong one habit at a time. Each is the other's missing half: a beginning with nothing to test it against and a refining with nothing new to refine. Open it when you keep starting and nothing keeps. What you are bringing has to pass through what is already cooking.

### Codon ring — Ring of Life and Death
This card belongs to a family of six that hold one whole life between them. This one begins it. The others are the watching and the cutting away, then the return and the feeding, then the finishing. What the six share is a trust in what looks like a mess. The mess is where life finds its next shape. Every one of them knows it from a different point in the life. Open any of them to see that trust further along. Here it is the first breath.

### Tarot
Clouds and thunder, the rain not yet down: five faces wait in that weather.

- **Ring Arcana — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Through its family this card carries Death: the passage every new thing crosses to get into the world. The seed stops being a seed so that it can become a shoot. Meet this face when the old form has to go before the new one can stand.
- **Water (upper) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The storm overhead is also a depth and the High Priestess reads it. She sits between two pillars and does not move. Her counsel is this card's own: gather and listen and do not set out yet.
- **Water (upper) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs by one foot and is calm about it. From upside down the situation finally makes sense. He is the pause this card asks for, held until the view turns over.
- **Thunder (lower) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): Lightning into the top of a building that was going to fall anyway. The Tower is the crack that lets the buried thing out. Meet this face when a structure has to break for the beginning to happen.
- **Thunder (lower) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): A trumpet sounds and what was buried gets up. Judgement is the call the push at the root answers, whether or not the ground agrees. Meet this face when the new thing rises before you feel ready.

### Immortals
Two who began again in poor circumstances go with this card. Li Tie Guai was the healer who came home to find his body burned and started over inside a dead beggar's body on an iron crutch with a gourd of medicine. Cao Guojiu walked out of a palace in disgust at his family and in the stories became the patron of the stage. One began again in a borrowed body and the other in a chosen poverty.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Scorpio:** the sign of what has to die in order to change, a depth that keeps what it holds until it is transformed. A seed rots a little in this ground before it splits.
- **Hebrew letter — Nun, the Fish:** the letter of life carried whole through moving water. What passes through this beginning comes out the other side still alive.
