# UL 41 — Beginning and the End

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Hungry for a Better Life · Living on Hope · Dreaming Instead of Doing · Plans Held Lightly · Sensing What Is Coming · Less on Purpose · Every Wish a Wish for Home

The pressure toward what has not happened yet is this energy, the hunger in you for a better life than the one you are standing in. It pictures more than is here and wants to feel it. It is the first move of every new stretch in a life, the wanting that comes before there is anything to want, and you feel it low in the body, the way hunger is felt. It is older than you: everything that came before you presses in it, dragging you down and urging you to break free in the same moment.

Carried well, this hunger is a way of seeing rather than a way of leaving. You feel what is on its way before it arrives, and you set to work bringing it through. Whatever you have pictured is held lightly, the way you hold a plan for a journey, since the road will change it and you would rather have the road. Small plans are good, what to eat tomorrow, where to meet a friend; it is the large plan, the whole life arranged in the head, that this hunger is forever tempted into. The ones who carry this best live simply, less attached to how their own story turns out, and are glad to be in it.

This energy comes in cycles. A dream rises, gives you years of hope and falls away, then another rises in its place. The hope is so good that you can live on it without ever setting out, and whole industries are built to keep you there. Every wish carries an if, if this came I would be happy, and life takes the ifs away one at a time. It never feels like a kindness, though it is one. What goes each time is the picture; the wanting under it stays, and it was always the same wanting. Each turn is another chance to read it right. Begin this time, with the nearest thing, and let the story find its own ending.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Lake: the still height, and at its foot the open water. The lake gives up its water as mist, the mist feeds the heights, and so the low is lessened to fill the high. The old character shows a hand and a ceremonial vessel, an offering carried to the altar: to decrease is to give up what is yours on purpose.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the ground that has risen and stopped. It is the limit at the edge of the country, the line past which nothing can be seen, the sun going down in early winter. In a family it is the youngest son, the one who keeps his own counsel. In you it is the part that can stop: the foot that stops walking, the hand that stays closed, the want not acted on. Above, it is the height that receives what the low gives up. Its risk is obstinacy: the stop meant to steady you sets like stone, and what should have moved on stays put.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Underneath is the lake, water lying open to the sky with mist coming off it. It is the mouth, speech, the pleasure of exchange, the marsh that makes the ground rich. In a family it is the youngest daughter, who says what she feels and makes the room glad. In you it is the part that gives freely and enjoys it: what you hand over lightly, the word that costs nothing to say. Below, it is the thing willingly lessened, the water that rises to feed the mountain. Its risk is draining, giving until there is nothing under the surface and the joy is spent on whoever asks.

### Reading
You are in a time of having less, and the whole question is whether you choose it. Loss is coming either way. Taken from you, it is only loss; given up on purpose, from below to feed what stands above you, it becomes an offering, and an offering is how a hard time turns into a good one. What you give does not have to be much. Two small bowls carried to the altar are enough when what is in them is sincere, because a small thing given honestly reaches further than a large thing given for show.

What it asks of you is to decrease yourself first. Curb your anger, hold back the appetites that run ahead of you, bring whatever is in excess back to its right size. It is heavy at the start and light afterward, which is the order this time keeps, so do not read the heaviness as a sign that you are wrong. Keep harm at a distance while you are lighter than usual. Do not dwell on what has concluded, and set out on what is next, because this is a time when moving on goes well. The turn is already in it: where decrease ends, increase begins, and what you gave from the bottom comes back from the top.

In the body this is the hand. Notice what yours is holding now, and how tightly. An offering begins as a closed hand opening. Hold yourself the way the mountain holds the lake, still above, giving below, with nothing in it grasped.

### Judgement
- Decrease, with sincerity in it, brings the highest good fortune. There is no blame in it, and it can be held to.
- It furthers you to set out. How is it to be done? Two small bowls are enough for the offering.

### Image
- At the foot of the mountain lies the lake: the image of decrease.
- So the wise curb their anger and hold back their appetites.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ your own work set down, and a quick step toward someone who needs you. → becomes Hexagram 4, Youthful Folly.
Your own work is set down so you can hurry to someone weaker, and there is no fault in it. The fault would be in the measure. A strong hand helping has a way of giving too much, until the one helped is swamped and the one helping is empty. Before you reduce anything of yours, weigh how much the other can take.

**Line 2** · _image:_ standing firm in the middle, where to set out would be wrong. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.
Strong and steady in the middle of things, you want to ride out to fix what is weak. Do not. Here the help is to stay whole where you stand, because someone who keeps their own ground gives more than someone who spends it. Know when enough is enough. Hold to what is right in you, and the other is increased without your losing a thing.

**Line 3** · _image:_ three walking together and one falling away; one walking alone and finding a friend. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
Three set out together and the road takes one of them, because three breed doubt, which travels badly. One walking alone is not alone for long. The friend the three could never have found comes to the one. There is loss inside every gain and gain inside every loss on this road, so do not grieve the number coming down. Walk single-minded, and the right company finds you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ an old ailment lessened, and help coming quickly, glad to come. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
Something has been weakening you for a long time, a fault you have carried until it felt like part of you. Lessen it, even a little, and the help that was waiting comes at once, glad to be asked. The joy in this line belongs to both of you. Nobody cures themselves alone, and there is no blame in needing the hand that is already reaching for you.

**Line 5** · _image:_ ten pairs of tortoise shells given, and no one able to refuse them. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
Someone is increasing you, and the increase cannot be turned away. Ten pairs of tortoise shells, which in the old days were money and oracle both, arrive without your having sought them. This is the gain that comes to the one who emptied out honestly and asked for nothing, blessed from above. Do not argue with it, and do not shrink from it. Take what is given, all of it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ increased without anyone being decreased; servants gained, and no separate house any more. → becomes Hexagram 19, Approach.
At the top the giving up is over, and you are increased without anyone being lessened. What you have now is for everyone, and people come to serve what you serve, more than can be counted. You gain them and lose your separate house, because a life given to something larger is not lived from behind one door. Find the ones with no direction, and give them yours.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Fantasy · _Gift:_ Anticipation · _Siddhi:_ Emanation

### Shadow — Fantasy
A dream of a better life sits in you, and most days you live in the dream instead of the life. The pressure that made it was the pressure to grow; through a tired mind it comes out as the pressure to feel happy, and the mind paints a picture, the right person or the money. Then it falls in love with the picture. You hold the key to the dream and never turn it in the lock, because what you live on is not the dream coming true but the hope it gives. Hope is cheaper than a first step. The gauge in you swings. Empty, you dream of being full. Full, you feel the weight of everything behind you and want to be rid of it.

The picture was a light thrown down the road to show the way, and you have stood still, looking at the light. Every dream you have will be taken from you, one at a time, which is good news that never arrives dressed as one. When one goes, look at what is left. The emptiness can be filled, though never by the next picture, and never by anything you achieve.

### Repressive nature — Dreamy
This person has gone into the dream and shut the door; whatever you say passes through it. They do not want the dream to come true, only to go on dreaming it, and the body slows to match until the days have no pull. The way out is to make one piece of it real.

### Reactive nature — Hyperactive
Here the dream is fuel, and the person is always a step ahead of themselves. Nothing they do is fast enough for the picture, so they run harder, until the body gives out. What saves them is letting someone else into the dream, and letting go of its exact shape.

### Gift — Anticipation
The same dream, once you have seen to the bottom of it, becomes a way of sensing what is coming. Under every fantasy you have ever had, the lover or the fortune, there is one ache, to connect, to be part of something whole. Once you know what the picture was really asking for, the picture can be used. You stop mistaking a wish for a sign. You begin to feel the shape of a future that is actually forming, the way a musician hears a whole piece before it is written and simply takes it down. The dreamer and the one who brings it through have the same dream; one is working while the other is still looking.

The light thrown down the road is walked toward now, and the road corrects the picture as you go. Because you traced your own dreams to the root, you can see where another person's dream is really heading, and sense the moment a stuck pattern in them is ready to break, a help advice never is. Two things last, children and stories. This energy knows how a story goes; it knows the ending and can wait for it.

### Siddhi — Emanation
You thought you were travelling forward, and you were going back. Every dream you had was an impulse from the source you came from, looking for the way home the only way it knew, throwing little lights ahead; followed all the way, the lights lead back to where they were thrown from. Here the picture is gone, the road with it, and no one is left standing. It is not brought on by anything you do. It arrives from nowhere, and the one it has happened to cannot say how, since no one remains for it to have happened to.

There is no more hunger, so no more pressure to become anything. Speak, and people hear an agenda in it; keep still, they hear one in that. You are caught, and it does not matter. All you are is the beginning of the story and its end. It was a dream, and you had to be asleep to hear it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 41, Imagination
The pressure to picture more than is here, then go and feel it, is this energy in the body. Gate 41 is the door it comes through: the drive to imagine a fuller life, a better world, and then to want it with the whole body. It is the first pressure in every new stretch of experience, the one that opens the year, and what it starts it does not always finish, since starting is its work. From outside it can look like restlessness, someone never satisfied with the room they are in. What is going on is simpler: the imagination is doing what it was built to do, refusing to call the present the whole story.

The cost is being taken for a dreamer. People carrying this hear that their ideas are unrealistic, or too much, and learn to keep the picture to themselves, until the picture curdles into the worst that could happen instead of the best. People who carry this all their lives learn to share the picture when the time is right, not before, and to rest in the pleasure of imagining, which was the point all along.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The base of the body is where this pressure rises, the Root Centre, the square at the bottom of the chart, the place that gets a body moving. It is a motor and a pressure at once. What it holds is the drive to act, the raw push that begins things. Its one law is timing: the push comes in pulses, on, then off, like the spark that fires an engine, so when it is off no amount of wanting will start you. The urge to begin is not yours to summon.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on, and the drive comes back reliably in its own time. For someone like that, pressure is familiar, almost comfortable; they can hold it and point it at one thing. The trouble is the off beat, when the thing they know they must do will not begin, and they turn on themselves for it. In the other four it is not, so the pressure they feel is borrowed from whoever is near, taken in and made louder. For someone like that, every task in the room becomes theirs to do, and doing it never brings the quiet, because the next one is already pressing. They have trouble starting, and once they are moving they can finish more than anyone. What is their own is the wisdom, over a life, about which pressures were ever theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of Recognition (41–30)
A picture of more has no feeling of its own to run on. Its other half is Gate 30, in the belly above the navel, the wanting to feel. The two make the Channel of Recognition, the picture of what is possible meeting the desire to go through it. Follow the urge at once and the passion scatters. Let the dream sit until you know it is yours, and it becomes a vision with the heat to carry it. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the dream only moves where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
In you this energy is given to the adrenals, and the reason is the morning. Two small glands sit one on top of each of your kidneys, high at the back under the last ribs, and they run the day's tide. The tide is lowest around midnight, rises through the last hours of your sleep, and is highest in the hour after you wake, so the day starts in you before you do. Before your eyes open the glands have readied you, whether the day will be good or not. That is this energy's work too, beginning ahead of you, and the adrenals are where the body does the same.
