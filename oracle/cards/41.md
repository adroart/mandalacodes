# UL 41 — Beginning and the End

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Longing for More · Sensing What Is Coming · Plans Held Lightly · Taking the First Step · Travelling Light · Living on Hope · Every End a New Start

When one part of your life ends and the next has not begun, a longing for more rises in you. You feel it low in your body, as a pull toward a life you can already picture. You sense what is on its way before it arrives, and you want to feel it for yourself. The picture shows you which way to walk.

Some days the picture feels so good that its hope is enough, and you stay where you are. Then life takes that picture away, and it can feel like a loss. What you wanted stays with you, and a new picture rises in its place.

Hold each picture the way you hold a plan for a trip, ready for the road to change it. Keep your plans small, and take the first real step toward what you see. Each ending then clears the way for a new start. Step by step, the picture becomes a life you are living.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Lake is the hexagram of giving up what you do not need. Lake is the joy in you that gives freely. Mountain is the part of you that knows when to stop. The stopping sits above the joy because a pleasure you can stop is one that does not run your life. This hexagram has long been read as a lake at the foot of a mountain. The lake gives its water up as mist, and the mist falls as rain on the heights. What you let go of below comes back to lift you. What you give up on purpose makes room for what raises you.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that can stop. You put the fork down when you have had enough, and you leave a thing alone once it is said. It sits on top because in a time of having less, the first thing asked of you is to stop reaching. Mountain has long been read as a foot that stops walking, and as the sun going down at the start of winter. Both are an end you can see coming and do not fight. On its own, your stopping hardens, and what should have moved on stays where it is.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the pleasure you take in sharing what you have. You hand something over and are glad to, and a kind word costs you nothing. It lies underneath because in this time the giving starts inside you, from a part of you that is glad to give. Lake has long been read as open water with mist rising off it, and the mist makes the ground around it rich. What you share gladly makes the people around you richer too. On its own, your gladness gives until there is nothing left under the surface.

### Reading
Something in your life is ending, and you have less to hold on to than before. The loss comes either way. What you can choose is whether you give it up freely. Given up on purpose, what you lose becomes an offering. It need not be much. Two small bowls are enough when what is in them is sincere.

Start with what you have too much of. Hold back your anger, and let a craving pass without feeding it. This is heavy at the start and light afterward, so do not take the weight as a sign that you are wrong. Keep away from what could harm you while you have less. Do not dwell on what has already ended. Setting out goes well now, and where having less ends, having more begins.

In the body this time asks for less. Eat until you have had enough, then stop, and notice how much lighter you feel. A few days by a lake or in the mountains can give back what the everyday wore away.

### Judgement
- Decrease, with sincerity in it, brings the highest good fortune and no blame. It can be held to.
- It furthers you to set out. How is it to be done? Two small bowls are enough for the offering.

### Image
- At the foot of the mountain, the lake: the image of decrease.
- So the wise hold back their anger and restrain their appetites.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ your own work set down, and a quick step to help someone. → becomes Hexagram 4, Youthful Folly.
You set your own work down and hurry to help someone who needs it, and there is no fault in that. A strong hand can give too much, though. The one you help can be swamped, and you can be left empty. Weigh how much the other can take before you give away what is yours.

**Line 2** · _image:_ standing firm in the middle, where setting out would go badly. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.
You are steady where you are, and you want to go out and fix what is weak. Stay where you are instead. Here you help most by keeping your own ground and knowing when enough is enough. Hold to what is right in you, and you add to someone else without losing anything yourself.

**Line 3** · _image:_ three walking together and one leaving; one walking alone and finding a friend. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
Three set out together and one leaves them, because three begin to doubt each other. The one who walks alone is not alone for long. On this road every loss carries a gain inside it. Keep your mind on one thing, and the right companion finds you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ an old ailment lessened, and help coming quickly and gladly. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
Something has worn you down for a long time, a fault you have carried until it felt like yours. Lessen it even a little, and help comes quickly. The one who helps is glad to be asked. Let yourself be helped, and there is no blame in it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ ten pairs of tortoise shells given, and no one able to refuse them. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
Someone adds to what you have, and it cannot be turned away. Tortoise shells were once used as money and to read what was coming. Ten pairs of them arrive without your asking. This is the gain that comes when you give up honestly and ask for nothing. Take what is given, all of it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ increase that takes from no one, and helpers gained with no house apart. → becomes Hexagram 19, Approach.
The time of having less is over, and you gain without anyone else losing. People come to help with what you are doing, more than you can count. You gain them and no longer keep a house apart from them. Find the people with no direction, and give them yours.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Fantasy · _Gift:_ Anticipation · _Siddhi:_ Emanation

### Shadow — Fantasy
A picture of a better life sits in you, and most days you live in the picture instead of the life. The pressure behind it is the pressure to grow. Through a tired mind it comes out as the pressure to feel happy, and the mind paints the right person or the money. You hold the key to your dream and never turn it in the lock. The hope it brings feels so good that you never take a step. When you feel empty, you picture being full. When you feel full, the past weighs on you and you want to be rid of it.

The picture was a light thrown down the road to show you the way, and you stand still looking at the light. Life will take each of these pictures from you, one at a time. That is good news, though it never feels like it. The emptiness a picture leaves can be filled, though never by the next picture, and never by what you achieve.

### Repressive nature — Dreamy
Held down, this person has gone into the dream and shut the door. Whatever you say, they hear through it. They do not want it to come true, only to keep dreaming it. Their body slows until the days drag. The way out is to make one small piece of it real.

### Reactive nature — Hyperactive
Let out, the picture becomes fuel, and this person is always a step ahead of themselves. Nothing they do is fast enough for what they see, so they push harder until the body gives out. What saves them is letting other people into the picture and letting go of its exact shape.

### Gift — Anticipation
Anticipation is the same picture once you have seen what it was asking for. Under every fantasy there is one ache: to be loved and to belong to something whole. Once you know that, the picture can be used. You stop mistaking a wish for a sign, and you begin to feel what is really taking shape. A musician can hear a whole piece before one note is written, then write it down. Someone who only pictures a thing and someone who brings it into the world may see the same thing, and only one of them starts.

The light down the road is something you walk toward now. Having found the root of your own pictures, you can see where someone else's is heading. You can sense when a habit that has held them is ready to break. Of all you make, the children you raise and the stories you tell are what last. You know how a story goes, and you can wait for its ending.

### Siddhi — Emanation
Emanation is the end of the road turning out to be where it began. You thought you were travelling forward, and you were travelling back to where you came from. Each picture that rises in you is a small light thrown ahead from that source, looking for the way home. Followed all the way, the lights lead back to where they were thrown from. Nothing you do brings this on. It simply arrives, and then there is no one there for it to happen to.

There is no more hunger here, and no more pressure to become anything. People hear an agenda in whatever you say. If you keep quiet, they hear one in that too. None of it matters to you now. It was all a dream, and you had to be asleep to hear it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 41, Imagination
Gate 41 sits at the base of the body and presses you to picture more than is here. It imagines a fuller life or a better world, then makes you want to feel it for yourself. It gives the first push whenever something new begins in your life. What it starts it does not always finish, because starting is its work. From outside this can look like restlessness, someone never content with what they have. People may call your ideas unrealistic, or say you ask too much. Then you may keep your pictures to yourself until they turn into the worst that could happen. Share a picture when the time is right, and enjoy imagining it for its own sake.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center sits at the base of the body, where the push to act begins. It is a motor and a pressure at once. The push does not run all the time. It comes in pulses, on and then off, like the spark that fires an engine. When it is off, no amount of wanting will get you started.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. For them pressure is familiar, and they can hold it and aim it at one thing. The hard part is the off beat, when the thing they must do will not begin and they turn on themselves for it. The other four in ten have it open. They take in the pressure of everyone around them and feel it louder. Every job in the room feels like theirs, and finishing one never brings rest, because the next is already waiting. Their wisdom, over a life, is knowing which pressures were ever theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of Recognition (41–30)
A picture of more has no feeling of its own to run on. Gate 30, in the Solar Plexus, is the wish to feel it. Together they make the Channel of Recognition, a picture of what is possible joined to the passion to live it. Follow every urge at once and the passion is spent. Let a picture sit until you know it is yours, and it has the heat to carry you. Gate 30 may be in you or in a person close by.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy belongs to the adrenals because of the morning. These two small glands sit one on top of each kidney, high at the back under the last ribs. Each night they begin your day before you wake. Their tide is lowest around midnight and rises through the last hours of sleep. It is highest in the hour after you wake, so your day has already begun when you open your eyes. They ready you whether the day will be good or not.

You cannot feel them, even with a hand flat on your back below the ribs. What you can notice is waking just before the alarm. When you know the hour you must be up, their tide rises earlier to meet it. Most people have done this without knowing why, and this tide is part of the reason.

### Amino acid
The longing for more has a chemistry in you, and one substance in it is methionine. Every protein your body makes begins with this one piece, because the code that spells it also says begin. Your body cannot make it. It comes in from eggs, fish or meat, and beans are short of it. Once the building is under way, that first piece is most often trimmed off.

Every story your body tells opens on the same first word. Beans are short of this piece, and rice is short of another. Eaten together they are short of nothing, which is why kitchens all over the world pair them.
