# UL 31 — Theater of Truth

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Drawn Before You Decide · Speaking for the Room · Needing to Be Right · Waiting to Be Asked · A Word You Cannot Take Back · Falling Where Everyone Can See

Influence is this energy, the pull that passes between people. In you it is the feeling that crosses a room ahead of any thought, and the way others fall in behind what you say once you have named what they could not. It lives wherever one person moves another: the first look between two who will be together, or the one sentence at a table that everyone was waiting for. Most who carry it never asked to lead, and that is the reason they can be trusted with it.

Carried in balance, this power does not believe its own speech. You know that words go on working long after they are said, that a single one can turn a whole life, so you choose each one. You lead by listening first: what you say is what the room already felt, put into words it could not find, so the people who follow you are following themselves. The ones who carry this well are the most human in the room, wrong sometimes, down sometimes, back on their feet with the heart still open. Being misread is part of the price, and you pay it without hardening.

Wait until you are asked. What is said before then, to be heard, has nothing behind it, and people feel the lack before they know what the words meant. There will be a time when you were sure you were right, then wrong where everyone could see, and that fall is not the end of this energy but the first clear view of it, since the part of you that needed to be right could never see itself. Get up with the heart still open. What you say after that carries more than you.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Mountain: still water lying in the hollow at the top of a mountain, held by the rock, wetting the rock. Each gives the other what it does not have. The traditional character shows all together, every part joined at once, and with a heart drawn under it the same word means to feel, to be moved.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is water lying open to the sky, and what it does is give back. It lies still, takes the light, and sends vapour up off it all day; a marsh feeds the ground round it without moving from where it lies. In a family it is the youngest daughter, the one everyone softens toward, glad in company and quick to speak. In you it is the part that delights and is easily delighted, the open face, the mouth that answers. Its risk is talk: a surface that gives back everything it is shown, until nothing under it is being said.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain lies underneath, the ground that has stopped. Its picture is a foot that stops walking: the sun going down behind the range, the beginning of winter. In a family it is the youngest son, the quiet one who stays where he is put. In you it is stillness, the part that can be moved without being swept, that keeps its place when something in it stirs. Here it is the rock the water rests on, the strong thing set below the soft one, which is how a mountain comes to hold a lake. Its risk is going hard, a stop that keeps out what should have come in.

### Reading
You are at the first pull between two things that belong together, before either has decided anything. Under the name is a picture: a bowl broken in two, each half kept by one of two people, so the join would show years on that they had found each other. It is the beginning of a union, of two people or of you with a piece of work, and the whole of it is still ahead.

What it asks of you is to receive rather than reach. The mountain holds its lake because there is a hollow at the top of it, and the ones who move people keep that hollow in themselves. Reach out to draw someone in and what comes back is exactly the size of the reaching. Be still inside, glad outside. Give more than you are given, which is how you come to have more. There is strain inside every attraction, two grounds leaning toward each other; it is what carries the feeling across, so do not smooth it away. Once the feeling is real, commit to it: taking the maiden as wife is the old text's picture of that, to take what has stirred you and stand by it.

In the body this energy is the mouth, set over a hand that stays still: the open face that answers, above a stillness that does not reach. Notice where you are holding yourself now. Let the face be open, keep the hands where they are, and let what is coming come to you.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a stirring in the big toe. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
A stirring in the big toe is where a feeling first shows, and a toe can move without walking anywhere. Something in you has turned toward something outside, and it is too faint yet to tell whether it will come to anything. Nothing here is right or wrong. Feel it, keep still, and let it show what it is before you go anywhere on it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a stirring in the calves, and staying put brings good fortune. → becomes Hexagram 28, Great Preponderance.
The calves are moved next, and calves cannot move on their own; they only follow the feet. What you feel now is the wish to go before anything has carried you, and going on it goes badly. Stay where you are. Kept still, you will be moved from above when it is time, and nothing is lost by the waiting.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a stirring in the thighs, holding on to those it follows. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
The thighs are moved, and they cling to whoever is walking ahead. You have set your aim on following someone into being what you are not, and to go on that way brings shame. What you cling to sets your standard, and this one is low. Stay who you are, and let the ones behind you follow instead.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the heart unsettled, going and coming, and only friends follow its thoughts. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
This line is the heart, where influence is decided. Held steady it brings good fortune, with nothing left to regret. Let it waver, going out, coming back, and only the friends you already have will follow your thought. The reach is not yet great because the centre is not yet still. Settle the heart, and what you feel carries further than the people you know.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a stirring in the flesh of the back, above the heart, with nothing to regret. → becomes Hexagram 62, Small Preponderance.
The flesh of the back lies above the heart and is not the heart. What moves you here is near the centre without being caught by it, so nothing selfish is in it and there is nothing to regret, though it moves little. Something is behind you, moving you as you move others. Let it, because a back that nothing moves moves nobody.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a stirring in the jaws, the cheeks and the tongue. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
At the top the feeling reaches only the jaws and the tongue. What comes out here can be teaching that never tires, since the cheek is also the brace of a cart and the tongue the clapper of a bell, or it can be flattery, sweet words that are not the true ones. Do what you have been talking about, because nobody was ever moved by talk alone.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Arrogance · _Gift:_ Leadership · _Siddhi:_ Humility

### Shadow — Arrogance
Wanting to be right is where this energy goes when it is afraid. Under the raised chin is a person who does not feel safe and has decided that being agreed with is the same as being safe, so the days bend toward being seen. The loud kind is easy to spot, the one who knows best; the bully is always the least sure. The quiet kind is harder, the soft certainty of having found the way while others have not. It can wear robes and speak gently. Both are a hunger for words: the argument won, the name remembered, and the intention behind the words forgotten.

The strange thing about this is that it cannot see itself. Look for it in yourself and the part doing the looking is the part hiding it. What shows it is a mistake, made where people can see, and life keeps that appointment with everyone. You will not think your way out of this. You will be caught out, and something that was shut will open.

### Repressive nature — Deferring
This person hands their power to whoever is nearest and stands themselves below. They care more than anything what others think, fear most of all being called proud, and so wear the humility where it can be seen. It draws the very attention it claims not to want, and under it is fear.

### Reactive nature — Scornful
Here the arrogance is open and runs on anger. Such a person sees how easily people are led and holds them in contempt for it, without seeing how led they are themselves, since they need those people's recognition too. Praise from people you look down on never feels like enough, so the scorn grows.

### Gift — Leadership
Being followed is what this energy becomes, and it happens to people who did not want it. There is little in you that wishes to lead, and that reluctance is exactly what makes others trust you with it. You have an ear for what a crowd is feeling before it has found the words, and you say it plainly. The words are only the means; what moves people is what stands behind them, and a thing said at the right hour reaches far past what it says.

You use what you know of how people are led to walk them out of the traps you once sat in yourself. It takes patience with your own wrong turns, and the quieter courage of standing in what you know is true while people read you wrong. The one who is followed falls, gets up, and keeps the heart open. What is new in you is grafted onto old roots, since nobody has ever led the way forward who could not speak the language of what came before.

### Siddhi — Humility
Not minding what anyone thinks is the whole of this, and it is not gentle. Humility is not a soft face or a lowered voice, which are pride in a costume; it is what is left when life has stripped you to the bone and you stopped minding. From here nobody is doing anything, so there is no one to be proud of and no one to influence. You would not mind being thought arrogant. You leave the world exactly as it is.

What speaks then speaks through you. Whatever it says is right, because the words come from everyone and go back to everyone. Where they land is no concern of yours. Your name has gone with the rest. You fell where everyone could see and stayed down. It is quiet down here, and the ones still standing pity you, not knowing the fall was the whole point.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 31, Leading
Speaking for more than yourself is the pressure this energy puts on the body. Gate 31 is where it enters: the drive to be the voice of the people around you, to say what the group has felt but not yet said, and to be followed for it. Being called is what it takes, and it works only when the call comes from outside you; taken, it goes dead in the mouth. When the call comes the words are already there, and they are the room's words more than yours.

From outside this can look like ambition, or like hiding. It fails in two ways, by pushing forward to be approved of or by shrinking back from the fear of being unheard, and both lose the voice. People who carry this all their lives learn that the ones who follow are following what you heard in them, and the moment your own wanting steps in front of that, the power leaves the voice.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
This pressure is felt at the throat, the Throat Centre, the one place in a person where what is inside becomes something outside, a word or a deed. Everything else in you is pushing to get here. It has no engine of its own and cannot start anything; whichever part of you it is joined to is the one that talks through it. Here the join is to the centre of the chest, so what you say comes from who you are, and a hard word about it lands on you, which is why people built this way can go quiet for years after one bad hearing. The mind writes the script; the throat says something else.

In about seven people in ten this centre is switched on, and the voice is the same, one song. For someone like that speaking comes easily, sometimes bluntly, since the voice goes out rather than in. The work is to speak at the right time, so that it is heard. In the other three it is not, so the voice takes its shape from whoever is in the room and gives it back. For someone like that, the words come before the thought does, sometimes as a blurt, and there is a pressure to talk without knowing what to say. Left silent until asked, that same person becomes the voice of the room, saying what the whole of it was feeling.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Alpha (7–31)
A voice with nobody behind it is noise. Its other half is Gate 7, in the centre of the chest, the sense of where a group should go, and the two make the Channel of the Alpha: direction that has found its mouth. The direction comes from the chest, the words from the throat, looking ahead at what could be made better, and the leading is given rather than taken, by the people who choose to follow. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the voice only leads where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
Your throat is the organ this energy is given to, and the reason is the air. A voice is air borrowed from the room, then sent back into the room through two closed folds with your shape on it. Nothing in it is yours but the shape. It is the farthest-reaching thing your body makes, the one part of you that arrives inside another person across a room, which is what influence is in the flesh: something taken in, given a shape, and returned to the people it came from. When the throat is easy, what you say goes out on the breath and lands. When it is tight, you are pushing air, and everyone can hear it.

Say a long sentence out loud and notice where it stops: the breath ran out. Every sentence you have ever said ended where a breath did, and nothing at all can be said on the breath in. What you say is made of what you took in first.

### Amino acid
When the heart speaks, the words come out in order before you have thought what they mean. That has a substance in you, and the substance is tyrosine, one of the twenty small pieces you are built from, made in you from what cheese or meat brings in. From it your body makes what wakes you in the morning, then the colour of your skin and your eyes. The sentence that leaves you before you have chosen it was made where the colour of your eyes was made.

The words you follow and the words you lead with are chemistry before they are meaning, one substance, the same one the part of you that names things runs on. Cut an apple and leave it: the browning is the kin of what made your colour, still at work in the open air, as a word is once it has left you.

## RELATIONS

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of No Return
This is one of the smallest families in the deck, just two cards bound by a single amino acid. Its only sibling is UL 62, the precision of language, the skill of naming a thing exactly. Influence and exactness chemically bonded. The ring is called No Return because once a word is spoken it stands for eternity, and once the leap past the self is made, the name that made it cannot be taken back.

### Tarot
Five Majors gather around this hexagram. The codon ring gives one Arcana, Judgement, the trumpet that calls the dead up out of the ground. The two trigrams each give their own faces. Lake above, the open responsive mouth, wears the Lovers and the Star, the choice that unites and the hope that heals. Mountain below, the still ground, wears the Hierophant and the Hermit, the keeper of tradition and the seeker of the right path. Together they sound one thing, a waking call answered by union above and rooted in old wisdom below.

- **Ring Arcana — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): Through the Ring of No Return, this code carries the Arcana of Judgement. A voice sounds and the buried answer it, rising changed. This is influence at its truest, a word that wakes what was sleeping.
- **Lake (upper) — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lake above wears the Lovers, the two halves of the broken vessel finding their match. The open mouth that delights and persuades is also the one that chooses union over isolation.
- **Lake (upper) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): Lake also carries the Star, the quiet pouring-out that asks nothing back. The marsh that gives its vapour to the air is this same generous emptying, influence that heals because it does not grasp.
- **Mountain (lower) — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teaching): The Mountain at the root wears the Hierophant, the keeper of the old ways. Real leading grafts the new onto the ancient rootstock, and the still ground below is where that respect for tradition lives.
- **Mountain (lower) — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, the Right Path): Mountain also carries the Hermit, who stops, stands still, and finds the way by going inward. The bound stillness of the lower trigram is this same patient holding, the wisdom that does not chase.

Ridden backward, the mule keeps its rider's face on what he already knows, and the fan revives in hands that gave up command; influence here comes of both.
