# UL 31 — Theater of Truth

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Saying What Everyone Feels · Leading Without Wanting To · Listening Before You Speak · Words That Keep Working · Needing to Be Seen · Falling in Front of Everyone · Not Minding What People Think

Sometimes everyone in a room feels the same thing, and nobody says it. You sense it before anyone speaks. When you say it, they recognise what they felt and could not put into words. You have no wish to lead, and that is why they trust you.

Some days you speak to be seen, and the words go hollow. When you notice it, stop talking and listen again. Then ask what would help the people in front of you.

Listen before you speak. Let people see you make mistakes, because they follow the most human person in the room. Choose each word with care, because words go on working long after they are said. Said at the right moment, one plain sentence of yours can turn a whole room.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Mountain is the hexagram of two people drawn to each other. Lake is the part of you that opens up, smiles and answers. Mountain is the part of you that stays still and does not reach. The openness rests on the stillness because only someone who is not grabbing can be moved. This hexagram has long been read as a lake on top of a mountain. A proud peak holds no water, but a hollow at the top holds a whole lake. It works the same way between you and a crowd. Let people move you, and you will move them.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is how you meet people, with a warm face and a ready word. It sits on top because this is what others see and hear first. Lake has long been read as a marsh open to the sky. Its water rises as mist and feeds the ground around it. Your warmth reaches people the same way, even ones who never come near you. On its own, it turns into talk that pleases and means little.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the stillness in you that keeps its place when something stirs. It sits underneath because a feeling needs something steady to rest on. Mountain has long been read as sunset and the start of winter, the time when things come to rest. You come to rest in the same way, long enough to learn whether a feeling is real. On its own, it turns stubborn and lets nothing in.

### Reading
You and someone else are drawn to each other, and neither of you has moved yet. The other side may be a person, or a piece of work that has caught hold of you. What this time asks is that you receive rather than reach. Chase someone and they back away. Stay open and still, and they come to you in their own time. Give more than you are given, and you end up with more.

Every attraction has a strain in it, and that strain carries the feeling across. Once you know the feeling is true, commit to it. What you put in is what you get back.

Your body feels it first, as a stir that climbs from your toes to your mouth. Stand still with your face open, and let what is coming come to you.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a stirring in the big toe. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
The feeling stirs in your big toe, and a toe can move but cannot walk. Something outside you has caught your attention. It is too early to tell whether it will come to anything. Notice it, and take no step on it yet.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a stirring in the calves, and staying put brings good fortune. → becomes Hexagram 28, Great Preponderance.
The feeling moves into your calves, which cannot walk on their own and only follow the feet. You want to go before anything has carried you. Going now would go badly. Stay where you are until the time to move comes to you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a stirring in the thighs, holding on to those it follows. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
The feeling reaches your thighs, and you hold on to the person walking ahead of you. Following someone into being who you are not will shame you. What you cling to here is beneath you. Stay who you are, and let the people behind you follow you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the heart unsettled, going and coming, and only friends follow its thoughts. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
This line is the heart, where a feeling is decided. Hold it steady and good fortune comes, with nothing to regret. If your mind goes back and forth, only the friends you already have will follow your thoughts. Settle your heart, and what you feel will reach further than the people you know.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a stirring in the flesh of the back, above the heart, with nothing to regret. → becomes Hexagram 62, Small Preponderance.
The feeling reaches the flesh of your back, close to the heart but not in it. Nothing selfish moves you here. Something you cannot see is moving you, as you move others. Leave room in yourself for a change of heart, and you will have nothing to regret.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a stirring in the jaws, the cheeks and the tongue. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
At the top the feeling reaches only your jaws and tongue. What comes out can be tireless teaching or sweet talk that is not true. Talk alone does not move anyone for long. Do what you have been saying.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Arrogance · _Gift:_ Leadership · _Siddhi:_ Humility

### Shadow — Arrogance
The need to be recognised grows out of feeling unsure of yourself. The less sure you are, the more you look to others to tell you who you are. One kind is loud: the one who knows best and makes sure you know it. The bully is always the least sure person in the room. Another kind is quiet and harder to spot, the calm certainty of having found the right way while others have not. Both hold on to the words and lose the feeling behind them.

The strange thing is that you cannot see it in yourself. Look for it and the part of you doing the looking is the part that hides it. It shows only when you fall in front of people. Sooner or later life arranges that fall for everyone.

### Repressive nature — Deferring
This person hands their power to anyone close by and places themselves below. They care deeply what others think and fear above all being called proud. Their modesty is worn where it can be seen, so it draws the notice it claims not to want. Under it is fear.

### Reactive nature — Scornful
Here the pride is open, and it runs on anger. Such a person sees how easily others are led and looks down on them for it. They miss how much they need those same people to notice them. Praise from people you look down on never feels like enough, so the scorn keeps growing.

### Gift — Leadership
Being followed happens to people who are not looking for it. You have an ear for what people are about to feel, and you can say it in words they understand. What moves them is the feeling behind the words. A thing said at the right time reaches much further than what it says.

You use what you know about how people are led to lead them out of traps you were once caught in. To speak for what is new, you first learn the language of what came before. It asks for patience with your own mistakes. It asks for the quieter courage to stay with what you know is true while people misread you. You will fall where everyone can see, and you get up with your heart still open. That is what people follow.

### Siddhi — Humility
What anyone thinks of you stops mattering, and nothing about it is gentle. A soft voice and a lowered head are often pride in disguise. This comes when life has stripped you to the bone and you stopped minding. You would not even mind being thought proud. You leave the world as it is.

You say what has to be said, knowing the words came from everyone and go back to everyone. Where they land is not your concern. You fell where everyone could see, and this time you stayed down. It is quiet down here. From the ground you can look up and laugh.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 31, Leading
Gate 31 sits in the throat and presses you to speak for a group. It says what the group has felt and not yet said. It works only when you are called into it. Take it for yourself and the voice loses its power. The moment your own wanting steps in front of the group, people stop hearing you.

From outside this can look like ambition or like hiding. It fails in two ways: pushing forward to be approved of, or shrinking back for fear of not being heard. Called, it listens first, and people follow what you heard in them.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center is where what is inside you comes out as words or as action. Every other part of you is pressing to reach it. It has no power of its own. It works like a gearbox, and whatever part of you is joined to it does the talking. Through this gate it reaches the centre of the chest, the part of you that knows who you are. So what comes out is you, and a harsh word about it lands on you and not on your idea. The mind writes a careful script, then the voice says something else.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their voice is steady, the same tune every time, and it carries. It goes out more easily than it takes in, and it is heard best at the right time. The other three have it open. They take in every voice in the room and feel the pressure to talk without knowing what to say. Words come before the thought, sometimes as a blurt. Left quiet until they are invited, they can speak for the whole room.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Alpha (7–31)
On its own, this voice needs something to steer it. Gate 7, in the centre of the chest, knows which way a group should go. Together the two make the Channel of the Alpha, the leader the people choose. The direction comes from the chest and the words from the throat, looking ahead to what could work better. Gate 7 may be in you or in a person close by, and the group is led only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to your throat, and the reason is the air. Your voice is air taken from the room and sent back into it through two closed folds, with your shape on it. The shape is the only part that is yours. It is the one thing your body makes that reaches inside another person across a room. That is what influence is in the body: something taken in and given back. When your throat is easy, the words go out on the breath. When it is tight, you are pushing air, and people hear the push.

Say a long sentence out loud and notice where it stops. It stops where the breath ran out. You cannot say a word on the breath in. What you say is made from what you took in first.

### Amino acid
When you speak from the heart, the words come out in order before you have thought about what they mean. That has a substance in you, and the substance is tyrosine. It is one of the twenty your body is built from. Your body makes it from another one you must eat, and meat, eggs and beans carry it too. From it your body makes what wakes you up and gets you moving, and the colour of your skin, hair and eyes. The sentence that leaves you before you choose it is made from the same substance as the colour of your eyes.

The word that forms in your head when you look at a tree runs on this substance too. So do the words you follow and the words you lead with. Cut an apple and leave it on the table, and it turns brown. A close relative of what made your colour does the browning. It goes on working in the open air, as a word does once it has left you.

## RELATIONS

_UL 32 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the long stay, where this one is the first pull._

### Pair — UL 32, Art of Living (Duration)
This energy is the first attraction between people, and it runs out when the newness fades. Art of Living is where it goes next: the sense of what will last, which keeps a bond alive after the first thrill. Open it when the early feeling has gone and you want to stay. What starts by drawing people in lasts only if it is kept.

### Inverse — UL 32, Art of Living (Duration)
Turn this energy end for end and the first pull becomes the long stay. Art of Living is the same bond seen years later. Open it when you are asking whether something will last.

### Programming partner — UL 41, Beginning and the End
This energy gives words to what is new, and it has nothing to say until the new thing arrives. Beginning and the End holds what it lacks: the picture of a better life before it has a shape. One sees the future and the other says it out loud. Whatever is new meets resistance at first, and the two face it together. Open it when you are asked to speak and nothing new has come to you.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of No Return
This energy moves people with words, and it cannot take back what it says. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of No Return, whose theme is words that cannot be unsaid. Its one kin is UL 62, Voice of Nature, which finds the exact word for you. This one gives you the word that turns a room.
