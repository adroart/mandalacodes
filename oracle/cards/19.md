# UL 19 — Solection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Wanting Closeness · Feeling What Others Need · Needing to Be Needed · Standing on Your Own · Waiting for Trust · Fed by Life · Part of Everything Alive

The energy here is wanting, the pull toward closeness that feels what another person needs before a word is said. In you it is a fine sense, tuned to the room. You know when someone has gone cold, where the warmth is missing, and you want to go to it. It lives in every bond you have, with a person or an animal, and it is the part of you that cannot be near a need without answering it.

Carried well, it begins with your own needs. You see to your own needs first, sleep among them. Only then does what you feel in others come through clean, because you no longer want anything from them. Then it is touch, and touch does not grab: what you know about a person this way is more than they could have told you, at no cost to them. The strength of it is that you can lean into life instead of hiding from it, the hard conversation or the sick friend, and stay soft while you do.

The pull has a lower face, and it is the older one. It rests its whole weight on someone and calls the resting love. It needs to be needed, keeping a quiet account of everything it gives. Under both is the fear of standing alone and finding out whether you can. No better person to hold will settle it, because the weight was the trouble and not the one who carried it. Let it come back into your own legs. Standing alone is not where this ends, only the door. Past it, nothing that lives is separate, and you were never held up by one person but by all of it.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Lake: the field, and under it the marsh that warms it from below. Something greater is drawing near to something smaller, bending down to look with care. Two firm lines have come in at the bottom under four that give way, the first strength of the year. The old calendar puts it in the twelfth month, when the year has turned but the cold still holds the ground.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth here is the field, the wide open ground that everything is set into and that asks nothing to be different. Above, it is the outer face of the situation: the country the new warmth rises into, giving way under it, making room the way a mother makes room. In you it is the part that receives what comes near and lets it in without a struggle, the part of you that can be approached. Its risk is going slack, when giving way turns into lying down under whatever arrives and calling that patience.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the marsh, the open face of still water with the vapour lifting off it. In the family of forces it is the youngest daughter, the mouth. It pleases, it stirs, it trades: words go across an open surface the way vapour does. Below, it is the inner side of the situation, the gladness at the root that draws the greater thing down to meet it, since nothing comes near what gives it no welcome. In you it is the warmth people feel before you have spoken. Its risk is sweetness, the pleasure of being approached enjoyed for its own sake until nothing further happens.

### Reading
Something greater than you is drawing near, and it cannot be hurried. You are in the twelfth month, the first light after the longest night: the year has turned, the strength has entered at the bottom, and the cold still owns the ground. What is coming is welcome, and it is early. It is not to be seized, only let come the distance it is ready to come, so that what is growing runs its whole course before you call it yours.

What it asks is the posture of the greater toward the smaller, bending to look with care and never from a height. Reaching this light after a long dark, do not look down on the ones still in it, or think less of work you take to be smaller than yours. Teach without wearing anyone out, and hold the people near you the way a full stream bed holds water, keeping them without fencing them in. There is a warning folded into the welcome. The eighth month is the far side of the year, the autumn that answers this spring, and what rises now will also fall. Do the work while the light is climbing, and do not try to keep alive what is on its way down.

In the body this is the mouth, the open place that speaks and receives, the youngest of the family. Notice whether yours is soft now or set. Let it be soft, say less than you could, and let what is coming near arrive.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Co-Dependence · _Gift:_ Sensitivity · _Siddhi:_ Sacrifice

### Shadow — Co-Dependence
Relying on something outside you is where this energy begins, and it is older than you. Whatever fed us that we could not control, we made into a god: the rain, the herd. A person who can only be all right while a higher power stays kind is not yet standing, and the same shape repeats in a marriage or with a teacher. It does not feel like weakness from inside; it feels like devotion. It goes wrong at the point where you give, then give more, and without ever deciding to you keep an account. When the account is not paid, the warmth turns.

The old fear of not having enough is dying out of the world, and it is dying hard. Everywhere people are dividing into the ones stepping out on their own and the ones holding tighter to the old way, inside families first. If you are in the middle of that, the pull to hold on is not a fault in you; it is the whole past asking to stay. What answers it is not a better thing to hold. It is finding, once, that you can be fed by life with no one in between.

### Repressive nature — Needy
This person cannot let go of the past, or of the people in it, because being alone is not to be risked. They get clever about it, guilt in a soft voice, a small drama when attention drifts, and any attention will do. It looks like love and works like a hook.

### Reactive nature — Isolated
Loudly alone is the other face: they need nobody, they say, and they make sure to say it where everyone can watch them being alone. Underneath the proud solitude is rage. Left alone, they grow bitter; offered a hand, they explode, and the whole store of anger lands on the one who reached.

### Gift — Sensitivity
Sensing what others need is what this energy becomes, and it opens the moment you stop needing anything from them. The static of your own want goes quiet, and the room comes through. You walk into a house and know what is wrong in it. You feel where the warmth is missing and what a person is carrying, as plainly as most feel the temperature. Much of it comes through the hands, which is why many who have it heal for a living.

It is not the same as rawness. Being easily wounded is the low wearing this as a coat; the real thing is strength, since it knows what it needs and sees to it, so it is never begging through the people it reads. It learns fast, mostly by getting it wrong, because everyone has been careless with someone. Its whole art is the approach: you do not hurry into a sacred place or reach for a wary animal. You wait at the edge, quiet, until the trust is its own, and let it touch you first. What that feels like from inside is coming home, back into the family of living things, soft, open, no longer alone.

### Siddhi — Sacrifice
Nothing held back for yourself: that is where this energy arrives. It is not the small giving-away of your power to be liked, which damages you and honours no one. The sacrifice happens inside you. You give up the lower pull for the higher, the harsh word about someone, the grievance, the need to be understood, until the separate self is laid down on purpose and what is left works for everyone with no thought of itself. It is not sacrifice to the one living it; they know no other way to live.

The greatest of these lives are never seen. Someone gives a whole life for people who never learn their name. Most of the time the sacrifice is not to speak, since what you see cannot be handed to someone who cannot see it yet. Everything alive has lived off something else alive, all the way up to you, and from here that is the chain you belong to.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 19, Wanting
Gate 19 is the pressure of need, the drive to have your needs met and the tribe's with them, which is why its name is Wanting. In the body it is a hunger pointed outward. It reads where a bond has gone cold, in a family, a friendship, a whole society, and wants the change that would make closeness possible again, so that the person carrying it is forever moving toward whatever is missing. The sense behind it is fine to the point of discomfort: some who carry it cannot bear the seam of a sock and wear them inside out, or cannot be touched in certain ways. Nothing about that is fussiness. It is an instrument tuned to what others require. It goes wrong in one direction only, when you turn it away from yourself and forget that you have needs too. From outside it can look like someone who cannot leave a tension alone; what is happening is a compulsion to mend. People who carry this all their lives learn to give from what overflows, never from the bottom of the well, and to want closeness without forcing it.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The pressure rises from the base of the trunk, in the Root centre, the square at the bottom of the figure, where the body keeps its adrenaline. It is the centre of beginnings, the pressure of existence itself, the force that makes a living thing live, grow, adapt, whether or not it wants to. Stress is its fuel; fought against, it turns to low spirits. What it sends up toward the feelings comes as three pressures, and this is the first of them, the pressure of need. It arrives in a pulse, on, then off, so that the want is urgent for a while, then gone; nothing you decide changes the timing.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on. The pressure is their own, familiar as their heartbeat: they can hold it, aim it, let it drive them toward what a bond needs, without being pushed by another person's urgency. Their risk is burning out on it, answering every need as if it had a deadline. In the other four it is not. They take in the pressure of whoever is near and feel it louder than its owner does. Someone says what needs doing, and their body hears an order. For them the need felt in a room is very often not theirs, and what they have is the knack of learning whose it is before they answer it.

### What completes it — the Channel of Synthesis (19–49)
Wanting alone feels a need and reaches for it, no more. Its other half is Gate 49, in the feelings, the drive to keep your principles and break with what has gone false. Together they make the Channel of Synthesis: the sense of what a bond needs joined to the nerve to remake it, driven to connect in good faith, with joy in it. It is also the channel of endings; when the good faith goes, someone is out. Whether the other half is in you or in someone close, the mending happens only where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
In the body this energy is given to the body hair, and the reason is goosebumps. Every hair on your arm has a nerve round its root, so a thing is felt when it bends the hair, before it reaches the skin: you are touched before you are touched. At each root is a muscle the size of a thread, pulled by the same adrenaline that quickens your heart. When it pulls the hair stands and the skin rises with it. Cold raises them, or one bar of a song, and nothing you decide can. In an animal the raised coat holds warmth; in you the reflex still fires for a coat that is gone, so the warmth it once made now comes from clothes or from another body. That is this energy from inside: something approaches, the skin knows first, and your arms have answered before you have named it.

Blow across the back of your hand: the air is felt at once, through the hairs, before it can cool the skin. That is the part of you that feels what is coming before it arrives.

### Amino acid
What you feel in other living things has a chemistry in you, and one of its substances is isoleucine. You cannot make it; your body takes it in, from meat, milk or beans, or goes without. The same piece is in every animal you have ever touched, since every living body is built from the same twenty pieces. It is not this energy's alone: two other energies in you are made of the same substance.

Something is happening in that chemistry now. As the old ways tighten their grip, a reaction is going on inside you, in the body rather than the mind. What it opens is a way of feeling what it is like to live inside another creature. What it brings you back to is union with everything alive on this planet, a thing the body has always known and you are only remembering.
