# UL 19 — Solection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Sensing What Is Missing · A Gift of Touch · Waiting for Trust · Looking After Yourself First · At Home With Animals · Giving Until Empty · Needing to Be Needed

When you come close to someone, you can feel what they are missing before they say a word. That is sensitivity, and it lives in your skin as much as in your mind. You walk into a room and know where the warmth has gone out of it. It is a kind of touch, and it reaches animals as well as people.

Some days you give until nothing is left, and you start leaning on the people you look after. Wanting something back from them blurs the feeling. See to your own needs first, and ask for help when you are low. Then what others are missing is plain to you again.

Lean into the hard conversation, or the friend who is ill, instead of keeping away. Let old resentments go, and look after yourself the way you look after others. Being open and soft is your strength. Living this way, you feel more alive and more human.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Lake is the hexagram of drawing near. Lake is the warmth in you that people feel before you speak. Earth is the part of you that makes room for whatever comes close. The warmth sits underneath because people come near to what is glad to see them. This hexagram has long been read as the turn of the year, when the days begin to lengthen and the cold still holds the ground. You do nothing to bring the spring, and it comes all the same. You cannot hurry what is coming to you, and you do not have to.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the ground in you that carries other people. It sits on the outside because it is the side of you others meet first, open and ready to take them in. Earth has long been read as a field, which feeds whatever is planted in it and asks nothing back. That is how you make room for someone without sizing them up. On its own, it gives way to whatever pushes on it.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake makes you easy to talk to and quick to cheer someone up. It lies underneath because what you feel inside reaches people through your face and your voice. Lake has long been read as a marsh, open water with mist rising off it. Your warmth rises off you the same way, and people nearby feel it. On its own, it enjoys being liked so much that nothing further happens.

### Reading
Something good is on its way to you, and it is still some way off. The worst of the cold is over, though you may not feel it yet. What is coming grows a little every day. Let it travel the whole distance at its own pace. It has to run its full course before you can call it yours.

If you have come through a long dark stretch, do not look down on the people still in it. Do not think less of work that seems smaller than yours. Keep teaching, and do not tire of it. Look after the people near you without fencing them in. There is a warning inside this good time. By autumn, what rises now will be falling. Do the work while things are growing, and let go of what is already on its way down.

Your mouth does the work now, to welcome and to teach. Keep your face open and your words few, and let what is coming arrive.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Co-Dependence · _Gift:_ Sensitivity · _Siddhi:_ Sacrifice

### Shadow — Co-Dependence
Leaning on something outside yourself is where this energy starts, and the habit is very old. People once lived on what nature gave them, and they made gods of it. The same lean runs through a marriage, a family or a faith. You are all right only while someone above you stays kind, which is how it feels to be a victim. From inside, it feels like love. Both people give up part of themselves to keep the other close, and neither feels whole.

The old fear of not having enough is fading, and it is not going quietly. Some people are stepping out on their own while others hold tighter to the old ways, and it shows first at home. What ends the lean is not a better person to lean on. It is finding that life feeds you directly, with no one standing in between.

### Repressive nature — Needy
Afraid of being alone, they cling to the past and to the people in it. Guilt is their quiet tool, and small dramas keep the attention coming. They need to feel needed. Any attention will do, even the angry kind.

### Reactive nature — Isolated
The angry face of this announces loudly that it needs nobody. They make sure everyone sees how alone they are. Underneath the proud talk, they seethe with rage. Offer them friendship, and all that anger lands on you.

### Gift — Sensitivity
Sensitivity opens once you are fed. When your own needs are met, you stop wanting anything from the people around you. Then you start to read everyone nearby the way a barometer reads the weather. Much of it reaches you through the hands, the skin and even the hair, which is why many who have it heal for a living. Being easily hurt is something else. This is a strength, and it looks after itself because it knows what it needs.

Its whole art is going slowly. You do not rush to greet a stranger, and you let them come to you at the speed they like. With an animal you wait until it trusts you, then let it touch you first. You learn this fast, mostly from the times you got it wrong. Once it is yours, you feel strong and soft at the same time, as if you have come home to the family of living things.

### Siddhi — Sacrifice
Sacrifice is where this energy ends up, and it holds nothing back. The small kind gives your power away to please other people, and it harms you. The real kind happens inside you. You give up the harsh word about someone, the grievance, even the wish to be understood. Often the sacrifice is to say nothing, because what you see cannot yet be shown.

The greatest lives of this kind are never seen. Every living thing is fed by the life below it, and you belong to that chain. Past a certain point you work for everyone and no longer call it giving, because you know no other way to live.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 19, Wanting
Gate 19 is a pressure to have your needs met, and the needs of the people around you. You long to be close, and you notice at once when a person close to you is left out. Tension between people presses on you until you want to settle it. The feeling behind it can be fine to the point of discomfort. Some who carry it cannot stand the seams in their socks, or being touched in certain ways. The same pressure makes it easy to give too much. It works best when you give from what is left over, never from what you have to keep for yourself.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center sits at the base of your body and runs on adrenaline. It is where pressure starts, the push to get up and act. It works in pulses, on and then off. When it is off, you cannot make yourself start, however hard you try. From here, Gate 19 sends its pressure up toward your feelings.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. Pressure is familiar to them, and they can hold it and aim it. Their risk is burning out. The other four in ten have it open. They take in the pressure of everyone around them and feel it louder than its owner does. Someone only says what needs doing, and their body hears an order. Their gift is knowing which pressure is theirs to answer.

### What completes it — the Channel of Synthesis (19–49)
Wanting alone longs for closeness and reaches for it. Gate 49, in the feelings, is the part of you that holds to your principles and breaks with what has gone false. Together they make the Channel of Synthesis, which drives you to connect with people in good faith and bring joy. When the good faith goes, someone is out. Gate 49 may be in you or in someone close, and the bond is made whole only when both are there.

## BODY

### Physiology
The body hair carries this energy, and the reason is goosebumps. Every hair has a nerve wrapped round its root, so you feel a thing when it bends the hair, before it reaches your skin. At each root is a muscle the size of a thread, pulled by the same adrenaline that quickens your heart. Cold, fear or a bar of music can raise them, and nothing you decide can. In an animal the raised hair lifts a coat to keep it warm, and in you it still rises for a coat that is gone. Something comes near, your skin knows first, and your arms answer before you have named it.

Blow across the back of your hand. You feel the air at once, through the hairs, before it could cool the skin. That is the part of you that feels what is coming before it arrives.

### Amino acid
What you feel in other creatures has a chemistry in you, and one of its substances is isoleucine. Your body cannot make it and has to take it in from meat, milk or beans. The same piece is in every animal you have ever touched, because every living body is built from the same twenty. You share this substance with two other energies.

As the old ways tighten their grip, a reaction is going on in that chemistry, in your body more than your mind. It opens a way of feeling what it is like to live inside another creature. It brings you back to being one with everything alive on this planet.

_UL 20 is this card's pair and its inverse: the long look from a height, where this one comes close._

### Pair — UL 20, Emerging as the Code (Contemplation)
This energy comes close and feels what someone is missing, and up close it can lose the wider view. Emerging as the Code is where it goes next: stepping back to see the whole of what you came near. Open it when you have been close to something for a long time and cannot see it clearly anymore. Coming near is how you give, and looking from afar is how you understand.

### Inverse — UL 20, Emerging as the Code (Contemplation)
Turn this energy upside down and coming near becomes watching from above. Emerging as the Code is the same care, seen from the height. Open it when this good season starts to turn. By autumn the time for watching comes round again.

### Programming partner — UL 33, Echos of Time
This energy reaches out toward other people, and it can forget the strength it already holds. Echos of Time holds what it lacks: stepping back to remember. Open it when you have looked to others for so long that you forget your own power. You go back into yourself first, and then you have something to bring.

### Codon ring — Ring of Gaia
This energy reaches toward other creatures. It belongs to a family of three, the Ring of Gaia, which ties every living thing on earth into one body. This one carries sacrifice. UL 60, Woven Light, carries the justice that returns to you whatever you give up without thought for yourself. After that comes UL 61, Celestial Remembrance, which carries the sacred in every living thing.

### Tarot
The ring and the two trigrams bring five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana.

- **Ring Arcana — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress feeds everything around her, as you can once you are fed yourself.
- **Earth (upper) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil holds a chain you could slip at any time, the grip you can mistake for closeness.
- **Earth (upper) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World folds every part back into the whole, where you no longer stand apart.
- **Lake (lower) — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lovers choose each other freely, the closeness you can have without holding on.
- **Lake (lower) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star pours water under an open sky, the hope you feel at the first light after winter.

### Immortals
Earth above carries Lan Cai He, who wanders from town to town singing, with a basket of flowers and no care for money. He is the part of you that is at home anywhere. Lake below carries Zhong Li Quan, whose fan brings the dead back to life. Your warmth does the same for someone who has gone cold.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Venus:** the planet of attraction, the pull that draws you close to someone.
- **Hebrew letter — Daleth, the Door:** the door you wait at until you are asked in.
