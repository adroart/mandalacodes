# UL 45 — Tribal Tapestry

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Gathering People · Sharing What You Have · Enough for Everyone · Trusting Others to Lead · Working as One · The Fear of Not Enough · Holding On Too Tight

When people gather around something they all need, goodwill is what lets it go round. You feel it as a wish to see everyone at the table fed, yourself included. You would rather share what you have than sell it, since it goes further that way. People relax near you, since nobody has to fight for a place.

Some days the old fear of not having enough comes back. You feel yourself tighten the moment you step out of the door. Stop and feel the fear where it sits in your body. Given your attention, it melts, and under it is the feeling that everything is fine.

Gather the people you trust and give them the freedom to do things their own way. Let each of them add their own thread. Woven together like this, a crowd becomes one people.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Earth is the hexagram of a crowd coming together. Earth is the part of you that makes room and provides for everyone. Lake is the part of you that makes people glad to be near you. The gladness sits above the making room, because when you go along with people, they are happy to stay. This hexagram has long been read as water gathered on the ground. Water collects where the land lies low, and people collect around whoever makes room for them. Here the gathering is large, a whole people rather than a few neighbours. You bring people together by making room for them, and your welcome keeps them.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is how you meet people today, glad to talk and quick to cheer someone up. It sits on the outside because it is the part of you that others meet first. Lake has long been read as flat water with mist rising off it. Water pools where the surface lies flat, and people gather around whoever is easy to talk with. On its own, it can talk people into anything.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the steady part of you that serves and provides. It sits underneath because every gathering rests on someone willing to carry it. Earth has long been read as the ground the whole human world stands on. The ground takes in the rain and grows food for whoever lives on it. On its own, it goes along with whatever it is asked to do.

### Reading
People are coming together, far more of them than a family or a street, around something they all need. A crowd this size can do what no small group could. It can also come apart in confusion, with people pushing for the better place.

What holds it is something larger than any of you. The king in this hexagram goes first to the temple of his ancestors, to remember where his people came from. Remember your own roots before you lead. Go to someone who sees further than you. Bring your best, never what you can spare. Know where you are going together, because people with nowhere to go drift apart. While all is well, get ready for trouble nobody expects, since friction comes wherever many people meet.

Your mouth does the work here, in the words that bring people together and the food they share. Stand like someone who has made room at the table for one more.

### Judgement

- It helps to see a great person, and holding to what is right brings success.
- Bring great offerings, and it goes well. It helps to have somewhere to go.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dominance · _Gift:_ Synergy · _Siddhi:_ Communion

### Shadow — Dominance
Dominance starts with food. Everyone has to eat, so whoever controls the food holds the power, and money is the same thing in newer clothes. Out of the old fear of not having enough grew rank. To feel safe, you push someone down so you can rise, and the ladder feeds you for staying on it. Nobody is to blame for it, you included. The fear is older than any of them.

In you it began early, the first time the world seemed unsafe and your whole body drew in. Since then you hold on to things, a patch of ground or a person, as if you could take them with you. Most people loosen their grip only near the end. The fear is worth feeling, because it leads you toward your own heart.

### Repressive nature — Timid
Timid people bow their heads to anyone above them. They give up their own spirit to keep the peace. Under it is a fear so old they cannot see it: that nothing good comes of standing up to someone higher. Most people live here without knowing it.

### Reactive nature — Pompous
The pompous climb, pushing others down to get there. Once they are up, they keep the people below them timid, because that is what holds them in place. They and the timid are caught in one game, on opposite sides of it.

### Gift — Synergy
Synergy is what a group feels when nobody needs to be on top. It comes from trust settled in your body, the sense that the whole is holding you up. Then a group finds its own order, the way iron filings on a sheet of paper line up over a hidden magnet. Given time and space, it arrives at something better than anyone could have planned. On the way, one person often carries the whole group's fear, and the group works through it together.

The hard part falls on whoever holds the power. Handing someone complete authority looks like a loss, and it is pure alchemy. The goodwill it sets free spreads outward by word of mouth and comes back many times over.

### Siddhi — Communion
Communion is a meal with no host. You feed each other without keeping count, so money has no work to do. It feels like an early Sunday morning outside, before anyone is up. The world seems newly born, and you have nowhere you need to be. Nobody asks why they are here or what they have to do. Being here is enough.

Dying after a life like that would be no different from living. You drift in and you drift out, with no need even to miss each other. The order you found was there all along, hidden inside the old ladder, waiting.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 45, Distribution
Gate 45 sits in the throat and is the drive to gather people and share out what there is. It has been called the king or queen of the tribe, and when it speaks, people listen. It holds the resources for the whole group, whether money, knowledge or care. Its favourite way to share is to teach, so that the people around it learn to provide for themselves.

Its fear is that there will not be enough. Then it holds back what it should share, or grows loud and showy to prove it deserves its seat. It also needs to be heard, and when it is not, it either goes quiet or gets louder. Its power comes from being present, never from putting on a show. It knows when to step forward and when to step back.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center is where everything in you becomes words or action. Energy from every other centre is on its way here, and nothing happens in the world until it has passed through. It also sets how you take in food, and how you take in life.

About seven people in ten have it defined. Their voice is always there and sounds the same from day to day. Through this gate's channel the will can join it. Then the voice has an engine behind it and speaks like a leader.

The other three in ten have it open. They take in every voice in the room and feel pressure to say what the others cannot. Their words come best when someone has asked for them. Like a mother bird softening food for her young, they take in what people are trying to say and give it back clear. With this gate, theirs is a teacher's voice, speaking to guide the tribe.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Money Line (21–45)
Gate 21, in the will just over the heart, is the drive to take hold of the resources and manage them. With Gate 45 it makes the Channel of the Money Line, the one place in a person where they take charge outright and follow through. Its cost is finding it hard to trust others to be as able, so it does everything itself and wears out. It works best when it tells people what it is about to do. Gate 21 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to the thyroid, and the reason is the sea. The thyroid sits low in the front of your neck, across your windpipe, and it is the one organ in you that needs iodine. Iodine comes from the sea and the soil. You get it from fish and seaweed, from milk and eggs, and from the salt on most kitchen tables. Your thyroid pulls it out of your blood and holds it many times stronger than the blood around it. From it, the gland makes what every part of you runs on. So one thing, gathered out of what everyone eats, is kept in one place and given out to your whole body.

Look at the salt in your kitchen. Somewhere on the packet it says iodised, or iodine added, and that was put there for this gland alone. Rest a hand at the base of your throat, and you are resting it on the part of you that is fed by the sea.

### Amino acid
The gathering in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is cysteine. It is one of the twenty pieces every protein in you is built from, and one of only two that carry sulfur. Where two of them meet across a folded protein, they clasp sulfur to sulfur and hold its shape. The insulin in you holds together at three such clasps. A perm works by breaking those clasps in your hair and letting them join again.

You share this chemistry with one other energy. In your body, separate pieces fuse into a working that is more than any of them.

## RELATIONS

_UL 46 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the one life rising out of a crowd, where this one gathers it._

### Pair — UL 46, Fountain of Light (Pushing Upward)
This energy gathers people and shares out what there is, and it runs out once everyone is fed and settled. Fountain of Light is where it goes next, a slow rise out of that ground into delight. Open it when the gathering is done and something in you wants to climb. A crowd comes in; a rise goes up and does not come back.

### Inverse — UL 46, Fountain of Light (Pushing Upward)
Turn this energy end for end and many people coming in becomes one life going up. Fountain of Light is the same ground seen from whoever grows out of it. Open it when you have held the group long enough and want to grow on your own.

### Programming partner — UL 26, Lighter Than a Feather
This energy shares what there is, and it stalls where sharing would cost it its importance. Lighter Than a Feather holds that pride, the pleasure of feeling important. The two feed each other: pride makes it hard to hand over charge, and keeping charge feeds the pride. Open it when you notice you cannot let anyone else decide.

### Codon ring — Ring of Prosperity
This energy unites what people have, and on its own it can draw everything from a single well. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of Prosperity, whose theme is abundance that is shared. UL 16, Grand Rising, carries the skill of drawing on many sources when one runs dry. This one carries the gathering that brings them together.
