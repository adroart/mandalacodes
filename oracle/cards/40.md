# UL 40 — Eternal Wellspring

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing It Through · Work That Fills You · Saying No · Knowing When to Stop · Time on Your Own · A Body at Ease · Tired All the Way Down

When a long push is over and you can finally set things down, your will starts to fill again. With it you can carry what is yours all the way to its end. It rises from deep inside you the way a spring does, and nothing in it has to be forced. Work that fits you does not drain you, so you can work harder than most and still have strength left.

Some weeks you give to everyone who asks, and the tiredness goes deeper than sleep can reach. That tiredness comes from pushing your life in a direction that is not its own. Slowly put down one thing that is not yours, and your body breathes out.

There is a thing you are here to do, and it is probably in front of you already. Give yourself to it, and say no to everything else without a fight. Stop when it is time to stop, and keep some of every day for yourself. The strength you spend on what is yours comes back as fast as you give it.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Water is the hexagram of the moment a hard time lets go of you. Water is the danger you have been living inside, where each step feels like a risk. Thunder is the part of you that moves, the jolt that gets you up after a long time held still. The movement sits above the danger because you get out of trouble by moving, and here you have moved. This hexagram has long been read as a thunderstorm breaking. The air is heavy for days, then the rain comes, and afterwards you can breathe again. The same rain splits the seeds open, so what was shut tight begins to grow. What held you is coming loose, and your part now is to let it.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is what gets you moving after a long time held still. It starts deep inside you and shows on the outside, in what you do next. It sits on top because the change is what people see first. Thunder has long been read as a sound rising from deep in the earth, strong enough to shift heavy things. That is the jolt that gets you up off the chair when you have sat too long. On its own, it stirs everything up and carries nothing through.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the part of you that has been through the test. Inside, you know what it is to have everything riding on one point. It lies underneath because the hard time is what you are rising out of, and it still shapes you. Water has long been read as a stream running through a gorge. A stream gets through rock by keeping on, and so did you. On its own, it sinks into the low place and stays there.

### Reading
The hard time is over. What held you for so long has let go, the way the air clears after a storm. Now the time can go one of two ways. If there is nothing more to do, go back to your ordinary days and keep the old ways. If something is still to do, do it early at first light, before the trouble settles in again. Do not stay in the relief longer than it lasts. Others have come through this before you, and they were back at work the next morning.

Then forgive. Let the mistakes go, your own with everyone else's, because while you hold on to who was to blame you are still tied. Go gently, and rest the way the ground rests after rain. In the body this starts in the feet. After a long time sitting, your feet want to move before you have decided to walk. Let them take the first step, and keep the rest of you easy.

### Judgement
- Deliverance. The gain is toward the southwest, the level ground.
- When there is nowhere left to go, coming back brings good fortune.
- When there is still somewhere to go, going early brings good fortune.

### Image
- Thunder and rain set in: what was bound comes loose.
- So the wise pardon mistakes, and forgive what was done wrong.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the border where hard meets soft. → becomes Hexagram 54, The Marrying Maiden.
On the border where the hard time meets its end, all that is said is no blame. What happened was not yours to prevent, so do not punish yourself for it. You are still weak from it. Borrow the strength of someone who knows the way out, then cross over and step carefully from here.

**Line 2** · _image:_ three foxes caught in the field, a yellow arrow won. → becomes Hexagram 16, Enthusiasm.
Three foxes are caught in the field, and a yellow arrow is won. The foxes are the small tricks that got you through the hard time. Do not let them run loose now, and do not kill them either. Guide them until they go quiet on their own. The arrow is the straight way through the middle, so keep to it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a porter riding in a carriage, robbers drawn near. → becomes Hexagram 32, Duration.
A porter still carrying his load rides in a carriage, and every robber on the road notices. Relief has you showing more than you have, or riding where you should walk. The trouble this draws is your own doing, and nobody else can be blamed. Get down, carry your own load, and live plainly until you are through.

**Line 4** · _image:_ untangle your big toe from the snare. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
Your big toe is tangled in a poisonous plant, and it has not hurt you yet. A toe can move, but it cannot walk on its own. There is a tie to cut, to someone or something that keeps you near the danger. Cut it, and the friends you can trust will come.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the one in charge lets the difficulty go. → becomes Hexagram 47, Oppression.
The one in charge lets the hard time go, and the petty people around him fall back on their own. Free yourself before you try to free anyone else. A leader who stays strict after the storm keeps everyone tied. Be kind where you could punish, and even those who doubted you will see that you mean it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a prince shoots a hawk on the high wall, and hits it. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
A prince shoots at a hawk on a high wall, and hits it. At the very end of a release one greedy thing remains, sitting high where it can take everything back. Do not wait for it to fly off. The time is right, so aim once and finish what you began, and everyone gains.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Exhaustion · _Gift:_ Resolve · _Siddhi:_ Divine Will

### Shadow — Exhaustion
Tired is where this begins, a tiredness that sleep no longer reaches. Picture a hand so full it cannot open. In it are the job, the people who lean on you and the things you agreed to without wanting them. You were taught that a strong person does more and holds on harder, and both drain you. The cost shows up as thirst. You want something you cannot name, and mostly you do not notice how thirsty you are. Underneath it there is usually a feeling you have refused to feel.

It shows up as too much: too much work and no time for home, or all of you going to other people. You become many things to many people and no one to yourself. Cut off from what feeds you, you run down, the way anything does that is drawn on and never filled. The hand does not need to be forced open. Noticing it is where the tiredness starts to end.

### Repressive nature — Acquiescent
Held down, the tiredness leaves the hand lying open with the palm up for anyone to take from. They never learned that their own needs count, so they give without end, mostly to people who do not care about them. The word no will not come. Their life turns on the day they make a stand for themselves alone.

### Reactive nature — Contemptuous
Let out, the same hand closes into a fist. Underneath is an old rage that was never allowed, turned into scorn for others, the weakest first. They look strong, because rage burns like fuel, and they keep everyone at a distance. Cutting away every support, they wear themselves down from inside, and in the end it shows.

### Gift — Resolve
Resolve starts with an open hand that holds one thing. Take hold of what is truly yours, and the rest falls from your fingers. The favours you did not want to do fall away, and so do the promises made only to please. What stays feeds you. You can work hard and not wear out, because the will is built into the work from the start. You say no without a fight, since whoever you turn away belongs somewhere else. When someone you love calls you away in the middle, you put the work down, and taking that break is part of the strength.

Most of it is knowing how to relax. Rest is everywhere once you stop giving in to too much. It is a walk with nowhere to get to, or the work you love with people you love. None of this is going without. You take what life offers, with measure, and sometimes more than you need. Someone worn out who meets you goes away changed.

### Siddhi — Divine Will
Both hands are empty now, and nothing is missing. You no longer feel like the one making the choices. You are free to do exactly as you please, and none of what you do is in your hands. Every part of your body is at ease, and people near you can see it as a kind of light. Nobody can pretend their way here. Some who are tired of searching take up the idea of surrender to get off the hook, and that only makes them quiet. This comes at the end of the search, when every hope has been dropped, and it comes to you alone.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 40, Restoration
Gate 40 sits in the Will Center and pulls you away to gather yourself. After work or after people, it wants you on your own for a while to restock what you gave. It asks you to value yourself enough to step away, even from the people you love. A partner can read this as rejection, so tell them what it is. When it runs badly, you stay instead: you give to everyone who asks and prove your worth by doing more. Then you blame others for a tiredness you never named. The loneliness that comes with this gate is a signal to turn inward. You come back from the time away with more to give than you left with.

### Where it lives — the Will Center
The Will Center sits just off the heart. It holds the strength to say you will do a thing and then do it. It is one of four motors, the one most concerned with the material world: what you can do and what you are worth. It is built to work and then rest. Pushed on without rest, it burns out, and the body pays.

About four people in ten have this centre defined. They can make a promise and keep it, and people near them borrow that strength while they are close. Their risk is pushing because they can, until the body stops them. The other six have it open. They take in the will in a room and feel it louder than it is, the doubt about worth as much as the strength. Some spend a whole life proving a worth that never needed proving, and wear out doing it. Their gift is a taste for what is truly worth something. Nothing they do adds to their worth or takes from it, any more than an orchid is worth more than a daffodil.

### What completes it — the Channel of Community (40–37)
On its own, stepping away is only someone leaving. Gate 37, in the Emotional Solar Plexus Center, is the pull to belong to a few people and see them fed. Together they make the Channel of Community, which turns the two into a bargain. Its terms are clear and fair to both sides, with your own needs written in. Then comes the strength to work without tiring on what was agreed. Gate 37 may be in you or in a person close by, and the bargain holds only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
Rest is the reason this energy is given to your stomach. The stomach is where what you eat becomes what you run on, and it does that only while you are at rest. It loosens to make room for a meal before the meal arrives, then works on it for hours without being asked. When you are afraid or angry or straining, it stops and the blood goes to your muscles instead. A full stomach leaves the will in you content, and an empty one sends it out to find food. What you force costs you what you had. What you take in at rest is what you have to give.

Your stomach lies high on your left, under the ribs, a little below the tip of the breastbone. After a meal, rest a hand there and you can feel the fullness. On an empty morning, the growl under your hand is its wall working on nothing. Your hand is on the part of you that works only when you leave it alone.

### Amino acid
The will in you has a substance, and it is glycine. It is the smallest of the twenty pieces your proteins are built from. Your body makes most of what it needs, and the rest comes from the skin and bones of meat or fish. A third of the frame that holds you up, your skin and bone, is made of it. In your spinal cord it is the signal that tells a nerve to stop firing.

Three other energies in you run on this same substance, and between them they waste no strength. Together they release everything your body still holds against itself. Carried all the way, that release leaves every part of you at rest.

## RELATIONS

_UL 39 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the hard time building, where this one sees it break._

### Pair — UL 39, Nobel Spark (Obstruction)
This energy lets go when a hard time ends, and it runs out when the ease turns into too much. Nobel Spark is where it goes next: the pressure that builds when the way ahead is blocked. Open it when the relief has lasted a while and something starts to press on you again. Hardship and relief follow each other.

### Inverse — UL 39, Nobel Spark (Obstruction)
Turn this energy end for end and the release becomes the block. Nobel Spark is the same storm seen from before it breaks, when the way will not open. Open it when you are stuck and cannot yet see the rain coming.

### Programming partner — UL 37, Journey Home (The Family)
This energy steps away to fill itself, and it runs out when it keeps everything for itself. Journey Home holds what it lacks: the warmth that feeds a few people around one table. One makes sure you have time and space of your own, and the other gives freely to the people you love. Open it when you have been alone long enough and want to give again.

### Codon ring — Ring of Alchemy
This energy saves your strength by not wasting it. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Alchemy, whose theme is turning something rough into something fine. UL 6, Harmonious Mirage, carries the walls coming down between two people. UL 47, Garden of Alchemy, carries the change of old ways. UL 64, Communion, carries the fresh possibilities that follow. This one carries the new openness into a community.
