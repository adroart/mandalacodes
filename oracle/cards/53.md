# UL 53 — Creation Oscillation

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Starting Something New · Always Chasing the Next Thing · Growing Slowly · Fear at the Start · Letting Others Finish · Softer With Age · In Its Own Time

At the start of something new, the urge to begin rises low in your body. You feel more alive in that first push than at any other time. This urge comes and goes in pulses, and each time it returns a new start is ready in you.

Some days the urge is gone, and you cannot make yourself start. Nothing is wrong with you, since the pulse has only gone quiet. The harm comes from starting early out of fear of being late.

So ask what a new start is really made of, because it goes on the way it began. Start only when every part of you says yes, then slow down and let the thing grow. Someone else may carry it to the end, and it is still yours. Year by year your life outgrows its old shape, and each start reaches wider than the last.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Mountain is the hexagram of growth that takes years. Wind is the part of you that goes on, a little further every day, without force. Mountain is the stillness in you that stays put and keeps what it has. The going on sits above the stillness because only what is rooted can keep growing. This hexagram has long been read as a tree on a mountain. A tree up there grows slowly, and its roots matter more than its height. Its six lines follow a wild goose that lands by stages, the way you arrive in anything new. What you build slowly is what lasts.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the way you work into something new, a little at a time. It sits on top because it is the part people see, steady progress and never a shove. Wind has long been read as both wind and wood, and each gets in by degrees. A breeze finds its way into every room, and a root finds its way into every crack. You settle into a new place the same way, until it is part of you. On its own, it goes wherever the fashion of the day blows.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that stops and stays. It lies underneath because anything that grows needs something steady to stand on. Mountain has long been read as a foot that stops walking. You stop the same way when you reach your limit, and you hold your ground there. That stillness is what lets you grow slowly without being pushed over. On its own, it turns stubborn and will not move at all.

### Reading
Something has begun that will take years, and the years are part of it. What comes quickly leaves the same way, and what grows by degrees stays. Others will seem to move faster. Let them, because your success will come in time and it will last longer than theirs.

Be firm and be slow. Take one small, honest step and then the next, the way a good marriage is made. Play to the nature you have rather than the one you wish you had. When people are coarse with you, answer with dignity, because how you carry yourself is part of what you are building.

The thighs do this work in the body, the long muscles that carry you uphill one step at a time. They keep a pace the head does not. If you are already at the top in your mind, come back to the step you are on.

### Judgement
- Development. The maiden is given in marriage. Good fortune.
- It goes well for the one who holds firm and keeps to what is right.

### Image
- On the mountain, a tree: that is Development.
- So the wise stay in dignity and worth, which betters the ways of the people around them.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the wild goose comes down to the shore; the young one in danger, and there is talk. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
The goose lands on the shore, out of the order it kept in the air. You are young in this, with nobody behind you, and people are talking. It is no fault. Go slowly, put your roots down first, then ask those who could help you for what you need. The talk passes, and the roots stay.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the goose on the great rock, eating and drinking at its ease. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle.
The goose rests on a great rock, eating and drinking at its ease. You have solid ground under you now, and the first good of it is coming in. You earned this rest, and it is not idle. Share it with the people who doubted you, since you will need their trust further up. Rest here, then go on.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the goose strays onto the high plateau; the husband goes out and does not return, the wife carries a child and does not bring it forth. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
The goose strays onto a high, dry plateau, far from water. You climbed too high too fast. What you set out on does not come home, and what you were carrying does not come to term. This is no time to conquer or to start anything new. Stay with your own people and guard what you have.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the goose in the trees, and perhaps a flat branch to rest on. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
The goose is in the trees, where a webbed foot cannot grip, and still it may find a flat branch. You are out of your element, and it does you no harm. Take the branch that will hold you rather than the higher one. Then wait quietly for the next firm step.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the goose on the high summit; for three years the woman has no child, and in the end nothing can stop her. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still.
The goose reaches the summit, alone and in sight of everyone. For three years nothing you hoped for comes, and some people below would be glad to see you fail. Keep to the middle. What was held back was only waiting. In the end what you wanted comes, and it comes to stay.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the goose risen past the summit into the heights, its feathers kept for the sacred dance. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
The goose flies past the summit, out of sight, and its feathers are kept for the dance. There is no further to climb. The order you kept the whole way up is what you leave behind. Others will use it for their own steps. Rest now, and teach the people who come after you.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Immaturity · _Gift:_ Expansion · _Siddhi:_ Superabundance

### Shadow — Immaturity
Looking outside for the answer to a problem that can only be solved inside is where this energy starts. The cause seems to lie with the boss or the town. So you try to fix it out there, where the fix never holds. Under it runs a restlessness that says the next thing will be the one. A life can be spent on the next thing, always one step short. Nothing outside has ever given anyone the fulfilment it promised. Most people keep looking until a book, a teacher or a loss turns them round.

Flowers open at different times of year. Snowdrops come up through the snow, while others wait for autumn and are just as beautiful. People ripen at different times too, and nobody is behind. Under all of it lies a fear of not existing, rooted in you like a weed. The grace here is seeing how much of what you do grows from that fear. Once you see it, your heart begins to trust life as it is.

### Repressive nature — Solemn
Held down, the energy to begin new things turns a person solemn. They stay fixed on one activity their whole life, because anything new feels like a threat. A deep sadness sits under it, as if they might cave in. It takes great pressure to be that serious, and the pressure is fear.

### Reactive nature — Fickle
Let out, the same energy never sits still long enough to grow. This person starts one thing after another and follows none through, because staying would mean facing themselves. Their life looks exciting from outside and has no depth. Their fear comes out as anger, and the anger they meet gives them reason to leave.

### Gift — Expansion
Maturing feels like slowing down. A mature river is wider and slower than the stream it was. When your breath drops deeper, life seems to move in slow motion while your seeing grows sharper. The restless drive that chased the next thing turns round to face what it was running from. It uses your hardest times as fuel.

What grows then is your whole life at once. Your work keeps outgrowing its shape, the way life outgrows every shape it takes. Awareness, love and making grow together, since your life is one thing and not a set of rooms. This growth comes without effort. It brings calm instead of excitement, and perspective instead of ambition. The way in is to give way, to let life carry you and drop your ideas of who you are. Life turns out simpler than you thought.

### Siddhi — Superabundance
Beyond plenty there is a place where nothing is counted. It is neither plenty nor lack, but the source both come from. The seeking of a whole life ends here, when you find that nothing is missing. Life is endless beginnings with no endings, and you watch it with no stake in how it turns out. The excitement is gone, and so is the weight.

From here the rush of the world is a game in time. Run in it and you feel it slow around you. The fruit that was ripening drops from the branch on its own. All that remains to learn is the art of letting go.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 53, Beginnings
Gate 53 is the pressure to start something new, felt low in the body. It gives a new thing its first push, before anyone has agreed it should exist. You feel more alive at that point than at any other. Your part is to strike the match, and someone else may tend the fire. You do not have to carry every fire to its end.

Under pressure, the urge rushes to start out of fear of being late. It then burns out when the thing does not come as hoped. After enough of that, you may hesitate before starting anything at all. It works best when you start only once every part of you agrees, then let go of how it turns out. Value what you started, even when someone else finishes it.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center sits at the base of your trunk and is one of four places in you that make energy. What it makes is the push to move, a raw drive that runs on stress. Its one law is timing. The drive pulses on and off, like the spark that fires an engine. When it is off, you cannot make yourself do what you know needs doing. The danger then is deciding something is wrong with you.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. The drive is their own, and they can bear more pressure than others would believe. The other four in ten have it open. They take in the pressure of the people around them and feel it louder. They clean the kitchen to get rid of it, and the next task is waiting before they finish. Most of that pressure belongs to someone else. Their wisdom is knowing which of it is.

### What completes it — the Channel of Maturation (53–42)
A start is only half a cycle. Gate 42, in the lower belly, is the drive to stay with a thing until it is done. Together they make the Channel of Maturation, a life with clear beginnings, middles and endings. One cycle has to be finished before the next can start. Gate 42 may be in you or in a person close by, and a start matures only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
Growing up is the reason this energy is given to the adrenals. These two small glands grow up in stages, to a clock nobody sets. You were born with them far too big, each a third the size of the kidney it sits on. In your first weeks they lost a third of themselves, as the part built for life in the womb died away. By two they were back to their birth weight, and they reached full size around your puberty.

You can check this by memory rather than by touch. At about seven the glands began a new work, and the first sign was that your sweat changed. Most people remember the year. Growing up had started, before you knew it. The glands sit high at the back under your lowest ribs, each the size of the top joint of your thumb. They cannot be felt.

### Amino acid
The pressure in you to begin has a chemistry, and one substance in it is serine. Every protein in you is built from twenty pieces, and serine is one of them. It sits in the outer skin of every cell you have and in the sheath around each nerve. At its lowest, this pressure comes out as stress and a wish to be rich. What you build then is a stockpile, held out of fear.

The same pressure shows you what your wanting, your greed and your fear are made of. In time it teaches you that what you were seeking is already in you. Slowed down, with your breath deeper, it becomes a flow that widens and narrows with its seasons. You share this chemistry with five other energies.

## RELATIONS

_UL 54 is this card's pair, its inverse and its partner at once: where you start something, it is the drive that carries it higher._

### Pair — UL 54, Everlasting Bounty (The Marrying Maiden)
This energy starts new things, and it runs out once the start is made. Everlasting Bounty is where it goes next: the drive to rise, which carries what was started up through the world. Open it when your beginning has taken root and needs to climb. A start grows only when something carries it higher.

### Inverse — UL 54, Everlasting Bounty (The Marrying Maiden)
Turn this energy end for end and the slow courtship becomes the marriage itself. Everlasting Bounty is what a union needs once it has begun, the conduct that lets it last. Open it when the slow start is over and you have to live inside what you began.

### Programming partner — UL 54, Everlasting Bounty
This energy begins, and on its own it would keep beginning. Everlasting Bounty holds what it lacks: the ambition that pushes one thing all the way up. Together they are two of the most driven energies there are, and they drive a person either mad or to great wisdom. Open it when you keep starting and nothing climbs.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Seeking
This energy pushes outward, and every push is part of a search. It belongs to a family of six, the Ring of Seeking, and each member is a pressure that sends you looking. UL 15, Ordinary Valiance, is the pull you have on people. UL 39, Nobel Spark, is the push that stirs people awake. UL 52, Timeless Blossom, is your power to hold still. UL 54, Everlasting Bounty, is your wish to rise. UL 58, Rhythm of Life, is your joy in being alive. This one is your life outgrowing its shape.

### Tarot
Four of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, show how this energy moves in you.

- **Ring Arcana — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): The Hermit walks alone to find the right path, the slow search inward this energy is made for.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel turns in its own time, the way your beginnings come and go.
- **Mountain (lower) — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teach): The Hierophant teaches what has been handed down, the steady ground slow growth stands on.
- **Mountain (lower) — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): Here the Hermit is the stillness under your growth, the seeker who stops long enough to see.

### Immortals
