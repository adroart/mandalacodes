# UL 53 — Creation Oscillation

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Starting Things · The Next Thing · Growing Slowly · Fear at the Start · Others Finish What You Began · Softer With Age · In Its Own Time

This energy is the beginning of things, the force in you that starts them and starts them again. Everything that is passes away and begins afresh; this is the part of you that does the beginning, the new plan, the first move where nobody has moved yet. It runs the cycles of a life. It wakes in you whenever one of them turns.

A beginning lit with care goes on the way it began. What is at the source of a beginning decides its end: start a thing from fear and you have planted its ending in the same ground, however hard you work after, the way a bent arrow misses, whatever the bow. So before you begin, find what the beginning is really made of. You are built to start rather than to finish, and there is peace in knowing it. Someone else can carry the flame you lit, and what you started is not less yours for that.

The restlessness in it is real. Nothing out there has ever given anyone the fulfilment it promised, and most people spend years on the next thing before a book, or a loss, turns them round to look inside. Maturing cannot be forced. It comes in its own time, and the more of it you have, the softer you are, the easier on yourself.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Mountain: a tree standing on a mountain, wood on rock, growing the only way a tree on a height can grow, slowly and from the root. Its creature is the wild goose, which flies in order, at its season, and comes down by stages. The traditional character sets water beside a cut, water working its way into stone until the stone is wet all through.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is what gets in. It goes under the door, into the grain of wood, without force, and by the time you notice it, it is everywhere. Its other name is wood, the tree, which enters the ground the same way, a root at a time. In a family it is the eldest daughter, the one who gets her way by being gentle. In you it is the part that works by degrees: you do not push, you go on, and what you are getting into gives a little more every day. Its risk is drift, when going on becomes going along.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the stop. It is where a foot stops walking, the edge you cannot see past, the rock that was there before you. In a family it is the youngest son, the quiet one nobody hurries. In you it is the part that can stay still: you hold your ground, you keep to what you have, the next thing that comes along does not move you. Under a tree it is what the roots take hold of, and why the tree above can afford to be slow. Its risk is stubbornness, when the stillness forgets it was meant to be a base and becomes the whole of you.

### Reading
Something has begun in you that will take years, and the years are the point. What you are growing is a tree on a mountain: it goes down before it goes up, and the root has to be deeper than the height it will one day carry. This cannot be hurried, and hurried, it fails. Others will seem to move faster, and some of them will. Let them. What comes quickly leaves the same way, and what has come by degrees stays.

What it asks of you is to be firm and to be slow. Take the small step that is honourable, then the next, the way a good marriage is made, with nothing rushed. Do not try to be a different kind of tree; play to the nature you have. When people are coarse with you, answer with dignity, because how you hold yourself here is part of what you are building. Keep still underneath while moving above, and the movement will not wear you out.

In the body this energy is the thighs, the long muscles that carry you up a slope one step after the last, and they know a pace the head does not. Notice where you are holding yourself now. If you are leaning forward, you are already at the summit in your mind. Come back to the step you are on.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the wild goose comes down to the shore; the young one in danger, and there is talk. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
On the shore the goose has lost the order it kept in the air, and to the ones watching it looks wrong. You are young in this, without backing, and people are saying so. It is no fault. Keep the pace slow, put your roots down before anything else, then ask the ones who could help you for what you need. The talk passes; the roots stay.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the goose on the great rock, eating and drinking at its ease. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle.
On the rock you have solid ground under you, and the first good of it is coming in. Eat, drink: you have earned the rest between one stage and the next. It is not idle. Share it with the ones who doubted you, since their trust will matter further up. The rock is steady, but it is not where a goose lives. Rest here, then go on.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the goose strays onto the high plateau; the husband goes out and does not return, the wife carries a child and does not bring it forth. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
On the plateau you have climbed too high too fast, onto dry ground far from water. What you set out on does not come home, and what you were carrying does not come to term, because the order of the climb was broken. This is not the hour to conquer or to make anything new. Stay with your own kind, and guard what you have.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the goose in the trees, and perhaps a flat branch to rest on. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
In the trees you are out of your element, since a webbed foot cannot grip a branch, and still there is a flat one wide enough to rest on. Nothing is lost by being here. Take the branch that will hold you rather than the higher one, then wait, quiet, for the next firm step. Yielding is not going back; it is how the root is kept.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the goose on the high summit; for three years the woman has no child, and in the end nothing can stop her. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still.
On the summit you are alone, in sight of everyone, and for a long time nothing you hoped for arrives. Three seasons pass with nothing to show, and there are people below who would be glad to see you fail. Keep the middle. What was slandered was not defeated, and what was barren was only waiting. In the end what you wanted comes, and it comes to stay.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the goose risen past the summit into the heights, its feathers kept for the sacred dance. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
Beyond the summit the goose has flown out of sight, past anyone who could bring it down, and what it leaves behind are feathers that others carry in the dance. There is no further to climb. The order you kept the whole way up is the thing that cannot be disturbed, and it is what you leave. Rest now, and teach the ones who come after.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Immaturity · _Gift:_ Expansion · _Siddhi:_ Superabundance

### Shadow — Immaturity
Immaturity is looking outside for the answer to a thing that only resolves inside. Whatever is wrong, the cause is out there, the boss or the town, so the fixing is done out there too, where it never takes. Under it runs a restlessness, the hum that says the next thing will be the one, and a life can be spent on the next thing, always one arrival short. Nobody has ever found it out there. That is the stage before the search turns round.

Flowers open at different points of the season. The snowdrop comes up with snow on the ground; others wait until autumn, and they are as beautiful. Immaturity, at bottom, is a part of the whole that does not yet know it is the whole, pushing at its mother the way a child pushes until she says stop. Everything done from here is done from a fear of not existing, and the fear works in you like a weed. The grace in it is seeing how much of what you do has that fear at its root. Seen, the heart can begin to trust life as it is.

### Repressive nature — Solemn
This person has pressed the energy to begin down so hard that they have gone grave. They fix on one activity for life, since anything new is a threat. Underneath is a sadness so deep they might cave in. It takes enormous pressure to be that serious, and the pressure is fear.

### Reactive nature — Fickle
This one never sits still long enough to grow. They begin things one after another and follow none through, because to stay would mean facing themselves. Their lives look exciting from outside and have no depth. The fear comes out as anger, and the anger they meet everywhere is their excuse to leave again.

### Gift — Expansion
Slowing down is what maturing feels like from inside. A mature river is wider and slower than the stream it was; the old do not hurry; when the breath drops lower, the world seems to move as if in slow motion, while the seeing sharpens. Nothing has to be added to you. The drive that ran you ragged after the next thing turns round, once it tires of the chase, to face the place it was running from.

What you become then is expansion. Life knows only expansion; even when it contracts, it does so to grow again in a new direction. What you are outgrows the shape it is in, then outgrows the next. Awareness, love and making grow together in you, none ahead of the others, because a life is one thing rather than a set of rooms. It comes without effort. It brings calm where there was excitement, perspective where there was ambition. Suffering stops being a thing to run from and becomes the fuel. The way in is to give way, to let life sweep you along and drop your definitions of who you are. Life turns out simpler than you thought it could be.

### Siddhi — Superabundance
Past abundance there is a place where nothing is counted. It is not plenty and it is not lack; it is the source both of them rise from. The seeking that ran a whole life ends here by finding that nothing was ever missing. Life is made of endless beginnings with no endings; you are what watches it, with no edge to stand on and no stake in how it turns out. The excitement is gone, and so is the weight.

From here the world's running is a game in time, one you can run in and feel slow around you. Being born is in the game, dying is in the game, and the one watching is in neither. The fruit that was ripening drops from the branch on its own. There is nothing left to do, and the last thing learned is the art of letting go.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 53, Beginnings
Starting things is a pressure in the body, and Gate 53 is the door it comes through: the drive to give a new thing its first push, before anyone has agreed it should exist, feeling more alive in that push than at any other moment. Beginnings keep arriving in you, one after another, each wanting to be lit. You are the one who strikes the match, not the one who tends the fire. Nothing in you was built to carry every fire to its end, and the peace in this comes from knowing it.

From outside, a life that starts much and finishes less can look scattered. What is going on is that the drive is for the start, so the pull is to rush, to begin out of fear of being late, and to burn out when the thing does not come as expected. After enough of that you hesitate before beginning anything, which is worse. People who carry this all their lives learn to begin only when everything in them agrees, to set the stage and then let go of the outcome, to value what they started even when someone else brings it home.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The base of the trunk is where this pressure rises, the Root Centre, at the very bottom of you, one of four places in you that make energy. What it makes is the push to move: a raw drive that runs on stress and does not care where it goes, only that it goes. Its one law is timing. The motor is not always on. It pulses, on and then off, like the spark that fires an engine; when it is off you cannot make yourself do what you know you need to do. The danger in the gap is deciding something is wrong with you, when the support has only gone quiet.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on, and the drive is theirs, with a tolerance for pressure others find hard to credit. In the other four it is not, so the pressure is taken in from whoever is near, made louder, then let go when they leave. For someone like that the pressure never ends, because it was never theirs: the kitchen needs cleaning, so they clean it, and the next task is there before the cloth is down. They are not built to start things alone, yet they can take in a room's worth of drive and get more done than anyone. The wisdom they come to is which of it is theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of Maturation (53–42)
A start is half a circuit. Its other half is Gate 42, in the low belly, the drive to stay with a thing through its whole maturing until it is done. Together they make the Channel of Maturation, the cycle whole: a life with marked beginnings, middles and endings, with a feel for when to start, when to close. One cycle has to be brought to its end before the next begins, or nothing moves. Whether the other half is in you or in someone near you, what you start only matures where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
The adrenals are where this energy is kept in the body, and the reason is growing up. The adrenals grow up in stages, to a clock nobody sets. At birth they are far too big, a third the size of the kidney they sit on. In the first weeks they lose a third of themselves, as the part built for life in the womb dies away. By two they are back to their birth weight, and they reach their full size around the time you did. That is the work of this energy too: it begins things in stages, and each stage takes the time it takes.

The check is a memory rather than a hand. At about seven the glands began a new work of their own, and the first sign of it was that your sweat changed. Everyone remembers the year. Growing up started there, before you knew. The glands sit high at the back under your last ribs, each the size of the last joint of your thumb, and cannot be felt.

### Amino acid
The pressure in you to begin has a chemistry, shared with its kin, and the substance is serine. Serine is one of the twenty pieces every part of you is built from; it sits in the outer skin of every cell you have and in the sheath around every nerve. At its lowest the pressure in you comes out as stress, and the stress looks like wanting to be rich. What you have then is a stockpile, held out of fear. Slowed, the same pressure in you is a flow that widens and narrows with its seasons.

The pressure was put in you to grow you up, by showing you what your wanting, your greed and your fear are made of, until you find that what you were seeking is in you. Maturing begins in you with slowing down as the breath goes deeper, and what follows is a gradual widening.
