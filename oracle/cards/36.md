# UL 36 — Crystal Creation

## CODE

_Keywords:_ A Heart That Stays Open · Letting It All In · Knowing How People Feel · Hungry for New Experience · The One People Turn To · The Fear of Suffering · Opened by Hard Times

An open heart is alive in you, and it shows when life shakes you. The ground goes out from under you, and your stomach drops before your mind catches up. Your heart takes in the whole of what is happening, because it can hold any amount. Life keeps drawing you toward feelings you have not had yet, and you learn from each one.

Some days your heart pulls back for a moment, and that is human. Look straight at what is happening, and let it in all the way.

Each time you come through, what you feel grows clearer. The pain you let in joins you to other people instead of setting you apart. You understand what people feel, because you have felt it. So they come to you in their worst hour, and near you they open too.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Fire is the hexagram of a light that has to go under for a while. Fire is the clear sight in you, the part that sees what is going on. Earth is the part of you that gives way and carries on. Here the giving way sits on top, because in some seasons what you see is not safe to show. This hexagram has long been read as the sun gone down into the earth. All night the sun is still burning, out of sight. So you can live among others, keep your light low, and still shine. What you know stays lit in you until the morning.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the face you show while a hard time lasts: you go along with the people around you and give them what they need. It sits on the outside because it is the part anyone can see. Earth has long been read as the ground, which carries everything and feeds everything without a word. That is how you can work beside anyone and seem like one of them. On its own, Earth only gives way.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is what you understand and will not unsee. It sits on the inside because this is a time to see clearly and say little. Fire has long been read as a flame, which has to cling to something to keep burning. Your clear sight holds on to what it knows, even when no one around you wants to hear it. On its own, Fire blazes where everyone can see it, and gets put out.

### Reading
The light has gone under. Someone in power is doing harm, and what is clear in you is at risk for being clear. One order is ending and the next has not come. The push for change is in the air, and its time has not arrived.

So keep your head down and your light lit. Do the day's work, even the drudgery. Live among the others and look like one of them. Underneath, stay firm about what is right. Do not force the change. A light shown too early gets put out, and one kept low lasts.

Your eyes do the work now, under a soft belly. Let the belly stay soft, keep your eyes open, and wait for the morning.

### Judgement
- The light is wounded. In hard times it is good to hold firm.
- Know the difficulty for what it is, and keep to what is right.

### Image
- The light has gone down into the earth: the image of the wounded light.
- So the wise live among the many, veil their light, and still shine.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the wounded light in flight, wings let down; three days without food, and somewhere to go. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
Your light is hurt at the very start, before the blow has fully landed. Leave now, wings down, so no one sees you can fly. The going costs you three days without food, and the people you leave will talk. What they say hurts only the outside of you. Go while going is still your choice.

**Line 2** · _image:_ wounded in the left thigh, and saved by the strength of a horse. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
The hurt reaches you this time, in the left thigh, because you left a little late. You are slowed down. A strength that is not yours, a strong horse, can still carry you. Take the help that is offered, and let it carry you out.

**Line 3** · _image:_ hunting in the south, and the great chief of the dark taken. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
At the end of the night you hunt in the south and take the chief of what darkened you. The rest will not change in one stroke. Set everything right at once, with too hard a hand, and you stir up what you just ended. Take the chief, then go slowly.

**Line 4** · _image:_ entering the left side of the belly, finding the heart of the dark, then leaving by the gate and the courtyard. → becomes Hexagram 55, Abundance.
You go deep inside what is doing the harm, far enough to learn what it truly intends. Once you know its heart, you have no reason to stay and argue. Knowing is what lets you go, so walk out through its gate.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the wise man's light hidden at the tyrant's court. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
You are close to the one who does the harm, too close to leave. Keep your light in a basket, so you look empty and stay full inside. Act the fool if that keeps you safe, and stay firm underneath. A light kept like this cannot be put out.

**Line 6** · _image:_ no light, only dark; first up to the sky, then down into the earth. → becomes Hexagram 22, Grace.
At the top, you have climbed to the sky and lit the four corners of the earth. If you never learn to draw your light back in, you fall into the ground and lose it. Draw your light in before it is taken.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Turbulence · _Gift:_ Humanity · _Siddhi:_ Compassion

### Shadow — Turbulence
Disaster could strike at any hour, and everyone knows it. The news says so all day, until a low hum of fear runs under your days like weather. This storm in you is turbulence, and no life is free of it. What overwhelms you is less the suffering than the fear of it. The heart closes, the body braces, and a life gets written as one upset after the next. It shows most in love, where you want the most.

No feeling in you is wrong. You are innocent of your own chemistry and the way it makes you feel. The trouble starts when you push a feeling down or throw it at someone out of fear. Then this energy pulls crisis toward you like a magnet. It keeps bringing you the same lesson until you learn it.

### Repressive nature — Nervousness
Held in, the storm goes into the nerves. This person is terrified of change and keeps the surface calm at any cost. The feeling gets trapped and hums there, so they cannot relax. The hum leaks out and unsettles the very people they are trying to keep steady. They are hard to get close to.

### Reactive nature — Crisis Prone
Let out, the storm comes with no honesty or clarity in it. The same painful scenes play over and over. What was done is often hidden afterwards, out of guilt. Because the feeling is never met cleanly, the next upset comes from a part of life that looks unconnected.

### Gift — Humanity
Humanity is what grows in you once you stop running from pain. You meet the storm with an honest, open heart, and you graduate as a human being. Your suffering stops setting you apart and becomes what you share with everyone. Pleasure comes with pain, and you stop waiting for a life with only one of them. Once you let go of the idea that fate is against you, you speak cleanly with people.

You earn this over time, and you still close sometimes, because that is human too. Then you forgive yourself and the other person. You coax your heart back to life, again and again. Living from the heart, you have an answer to fear. In time nothing can wound you, because nothing wounds a healed heart.

### Siddhi — Compassion
Compassion is the fragrance left when the storm is over. It is earned, on a road through the worst a person can feel, so it speaks in a human voice. The walls between you and everyone else fall away. You feel their pleasure, their pain and their longing as your own.

You go where the light is darkest, into wars or among the poor, without flinching. You have been so low once that nothing can feel that strong again, so fear has nowhere left to live. The look in your eyes or the softness of your voice can open the hardest heart. The seed of this was in the storm all along.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 36, Crisis
Gate 36 sits in the centre of feeling, low in the body, and drives you toward what you have not yet felt. It carries a desire for new experience and a fear of not being equal to it. You learn by going in, through the highs and the lows. Because you have felt so much, you understand what other people feel. The drive runs in cycles, and when boredom comes it tempts you to leap into chaos. Its work is to stay with a thing long enough to become good at it.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The Emotional Solar Plexus Center sits in the belly, and feeling moves through it in a wave. With this gate the wave is the collective kind, with high highs and low lows. The low comes when something you expected does not happen, and the bottom falls out of the world. You are fine one moment and a wreck the next.

In just over half of people this centre is defined. They never know who will let them down next, and when they crash, their feeling fills the room. For them the wave is chemistry. Their work is to notice the dashed expectation, stop and breathe until it passes. The rest have it open. Other people's feelings hit them hard and take them over, then fade once they are alone. Some feel something is wrong with them for feeling so little. Their work is to let what is not theirs pass through, the way air goes through a screen door.

### What completes it — the Channel of Transitoriness (36–35)
On its own, this drive collects experience with no way to tell it. Gate 35, in the throat, is the drive to say what has changed. Together they make the Channel of Transitoriness, which turns what you have lived into stories other people can use. From outside it can look like restlessness. The stories come only when both gates are present, in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
The lurch is the reason this energy is given to the solar plexus. When a lift starts down or a plane drops, your stomach seems to rise before you can think. The loose organs of your belly hang on their folds, so they lag a fraction of a second behind you. The knot of nerves behind your stomach reports that lag. It reaches every organ in your belly, and the pit of the stomach in front of it is where trouble shows first. So when the ground goes out from under your life, you feel it in the same place, before you know it anywhere else. A feeling you fight instead of feel spreads from here through your whole body as a nervous hum.

Rest a hand in the soft hollow just under your breastbone. The knot lies about a hand's depth behind it, against your spine, and it is where a drop is felt first.

### Amino acid
What churns in you when life lurches is chemistry before it is a thought, and one substance in it is proline. Your body makes it for itself, though meat and cheese are full of it too. Collagen is what holds your skin, bones and every healed cut together. About a quarter of it is proline.

This chemistry is shared with a family of energies in you. Together they bring the strongest feelings you will have, and a dark stretch full of doubt that you come out of cleaner. Suffering is built into you to crack you open. Freezing when it comes is something you learned.

## RELATIONS

_UL 35 is this card's pair and its inverse: the sun rising into the day, where this one has gone under the ground._

### Pair — UL 35, Progress
This energy keeps your heart open through a dark stretch, and it runs out once the light returns. Navigational Star is where it goes next: the hunger for life that meets each morning as new. Open it when the hard time has passed and you want to live again. What you came through is what you bring to the day.

### Inverse — UL 35, Progress
Turn this energy end for end, and the light that went under the ground climbs back into the sky. Navigational Star is the same sun seen rising. Open it when you cannot believe the morning will come. Your light will shine in the open day again.

### Programming partner — UL 6, Conflict
This energy stays open to whatever life brings, and it still fails most often between two people. Harmonious Mirage holds what it lacks: the feel for people that settles a quarrel before it starts. The two grow up together, and as your heart opens, you begin to speak cleanly with others. Open it when the hard times keep arriving through the people closest to you.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Divinity
This energy lets suffering in, and suffering is how its family works. It belongs to four energies, the Ring of Divinity, and each of them can wake the highest thing in you. UL 22 is Treasure of the Way, the grace of having your feelings heard. UL 37 is Journey Home, the tenderness that holds your family together. UL 63 is Adornments of Time, the doubt that sets you searching. This one carries the heart that stays open through the dark.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards show faces of this energy, from the weight that holds a light down to the sun that returns.

- **Ring Arcana — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teach): The Hierophant teaches what has been tested, the way your hard times become something you can pass on.
- **Earth (upper) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil is what you cling to, the weight that can keep a light under the ground.
- **Earth (upper) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World is a round completed, so a dark stretch is one part of a whole.
- **Fire (lower) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): The Emperor rules himself, the clear sight that keeps its order while it is hidden.
- **Fire (lower) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): The Sun is the clarity that comes back into the open day.

### Immortals
Earth above carries Lan Cai He, who wanders with a basket of flowers and sings of how brief life is. Fire below carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar whose sword cuts through illusion. One reminds you that everything passes, and the other helps you see clearly while it does.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Taurus:** the steady earth that holds its shape through a long season, the patience to wait out a dark time.
- **Hebrew letter — Vau, the Nail:** the nail that joins two things, so a dark stretch stays fastened to the morning after it.
