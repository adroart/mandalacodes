# UL 36 — Crystal Creation

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The Ground Giving Way · Feeling It in the Stomach · Shaken, Not Shut · Light Kept Low · Becoming Human · The One Others Come To · Tender After the Storm

Crisis is the energy here, the moment the floor you were standing on is not there. It comes as a drop in the stomach before the mind knows what has happened, then as the news itself: the call, or the door closing. Something in you that was lit goes under. A great deal of a life is lived in that light without anyone else knowing.

You become human through this energy when it is left open after the drop. The mind can be overwhelmed, the feelings can be overwhelmed, and the heart cannot; it has no top to it, so it can take in the whole of what is happening. The one who has been shaken like this, more than once, and stayed open through it is the one everybody comes to in their worst hour, because nothing that is brought to them is stranger than what they have already carried. What you have been through is not a wound you hide. It is the reason people trust you with theirs.

The hard hours in this are the ones after the drop, when everything in you wants to shut, or to turn the shaking into a drama so that at least it is outside you. Neither is a fault, and neither is the way. Let it shake you. When you find you have closed, forgive it, then open again, as many times as it takes. What grows in a heart that has been opened this often is a tenderness that nothing can wound.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Fire: the ground, and under it a fire still burning. The sun has gone down into the earth at dusk, and the light that should be in the sky is under the soil. The traditional name is two characters: the rising sun beside the setting moon, the picture of dawn, brightness itself; then a second that means to wound, to level, to make a thing hide.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the ground, and what the ground does here is cover. It lies over everything that has gone into it, the seed as much as the sun, and gives no sign of what it holds. In a family it is the mother, the one who keeps what she is told. In you it is the part that can take a hard thing in and carry it without a word, that keeps a face the same while a great deal is happening under it. Its risk is that the weight smothers what it covers, so that what went under to be kept safe is lost.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Underneath is fire, and here it is the sun after it has gone down: still burning, under the whole weight of the ground. A flame needs something to hold to, or it goes out, and what this one holds to is what it sees. In a family it is the middle daughter, who sees and says little. In you it is clear sight that does not stop when it is no longer safe to look. Its risk is the one the whole figure is about: a light with the ground on top of it can go out, or it can be the one thing down there that is still awake.

### Reading
Something corrupt holds the high ground over you, and what is clear in you is in danger for being clear. This is no time to shine where you can be seen. The light is not put out but put under, where the dark cannot reach it, and it goes on seeing from there. What is ending is ending, what is coming cannot come yet, and nothing you do hurries either.

What it asks is the hardest kind of holding on. Do the day's work, even the drudgery. Live among the others and look like one of them. Keep the light low on purpose, and keep it lit. Do not force the change: the push for it is in the air, the hour has not come, and a light shown early is cut down. The old text stands two men behind this: one shut in a walled cave for seven years, who spent the dark making a book of counsel for times like his own; one at a corrupt court, who played mad so the king would spare him and carried his sense whole into the next age. Neither won by fighting. They outlasted.

In the body this energy is the eye under the belly. The seeing part of you has gone low, under the soft front of you, covered the way the ground covers the sun. Notice where you are holding yourself now. Let the belly be soft over what it hides, keep the eye open under it, and wait for the light to be safe again.

### Judgement
- The light is wounded. In hard times it is good to hold firm.
- Know the difficulty for what it is, and keep to what is true.

### Image
- The light has gone down into the earth: the image of the wounded light.
- So the wise live among the many, veil their light, and still shine.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the wounded light in flight, wings let down; three days without food, and somewhere to go. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
You are hurt at the very start, before the blow has fully landed, and the wise move is to leave now, wings down so nobody sees you can fly. Three days without eating is what the leaving costs. The ones who hosted you will talk. Let them, because what they say reaches the outside of you only. Go while it is still a choice.

**Line 2** · _image:_ wounded in the left thigh, and saved by the strength of a horse. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
The wound has reached you this time, in the leg, on the side that feels; you are slowed, not stopped. What saves you is a strength that is not yours, a horse under you that can still run. The help comes because you stayed soft enough to be helped. Take the strong thing that is offered, and let it carry you out.

**Line 3** · _image:_ hunting in the south, and the great chief of the dark taken. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
At the end of the night, just before dawn, you have the strength to go after the head of what darkened you, and you take him. The night was long, and it does not end in one stroke. Set everything right at once, with too fierce a hand, and you wake the very thing you ended. Take the chief, then go slowly.

**Line 4** · _image:_ entering the left side of the belly, finding the heart of the dark, then leaving by the yard and the door. → becomes Hexagram 55, Abundance.
You are inside the thing now, in the soft left side of it, where its feeling lives. Once you have found what it truly intends, there is nothing left to argue with. You know its heart, so you can leave its house, out through the yard and the door, with nothing to prove. Knowing is what lets you go.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the wise man's light hidden at the tyrant's court. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
You are close to the one who darkens things, too close to leave, and the way is to keep your light in a basket: empty to look at, full inside. Play the fool if that is what keeps you alive, and stay firm underneath. A light kept like this cannot be put out, and it is passed on whole.

**Line 6** · _image:_ no light, only dark; first up to the sky, then down into the earth. → becomes Hexagram 22, Grace.
At the top is the one who did the darkening. He rose to the sky once, lit the four quarters of the world, then went into the ground and lost the way, because he never learned to bring his light in. This is not the wise one hiding; it is the high one falling. Learn to withdraw your light before it is taken from you.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Turbulence · _Gift:_ Humanity · _Siddhi:_ Compassion

### Shadow — Turbulence
Disaster could strike at any hour, and you know it, the way everyone knows it. Most of a day is arranged so as not to feel that you know. Turbulence is what is felt when the arrangement fails: the churn under the breastbone, the body braced for a blow that has not come. It is kept humming by the news, which is one disaster after another until the fear feels like the weather. It shows up most in love, where the wanting runs deepest, and a life gets written as one upset after the next. Some part of you prefers the upsets; a churning life at least feels like a life.

What overwhelms you is the fear of suffering, heavier than anything it fears. Every feeling you have is innocent, and the trouble starts when you fight it or hide it out of fear. This energy pulls crisis toward it the way a magnet pulls, and it will keep bringing you the same hour until you have learned what it came to teach. There is no life without this shaking. The only question is what you do with your heart while it shakes.

### Repressive nature — Nervousness
This person is terrified of change, so the storm is held inside and the surface kept calm at any cost. It does not stay held. It goes into the nerves, hums there so they cannot rest, and the hum leaks out to unsettle the people they are keeping calm for. They are hard to reach.

### Reactive nature — Crisis Prone
Here is someone whose life runs as one wreck after another. The storm is let out, without honesty or clarity, into whoever is nearest, and then hidden with guilt. Because the feeling was never met cleanly, the next crisis arrives from a direction they were not watching, in a part of life that seemed unconnected.

### Gift — Humanity
Being human is what the shaking was for. Meet it openly, once, without the story of being its victim, and something in you graduates: suffering stops setting you apart, becoming the one thing you have in common with every person alive. Pleasure comes with pain here, always, and you stop waiting for a life that has only the one. What you get instead is the antidote to fear, which is living from the heart while the ground moves.

The people who carry this are the ones others turn to in their worst hour, because they have looked into their own dark and are still open. Nothing brought to them is too much. They speak cleanly, having dropped the idea that fate is against them, and they accept without complaint that they live in a body that will die. The skill is small, and it is everything: when something threatens to overwhelm you, the heart draws back for a moment, then opens again, with you still there. When you find you have closed, you forgive the closing, forgive whoever caused it, and open once more. A heart that has healed this many times cannot be wounded.

### Siddhi — Compassion
Compassion is the fragrance that comes off a person after the storm has passed. It cannot be given; it is earned, on a road that runs through the worst of what a human being can feel, so it speaks in a human voice, not from far away. The walls of the self are gone. What another person feels is felt in you, their aches, their wanting, their faults, all at once, and none of it separate from your own.

Those who reach this go toward the places where the light is darkest, the wars, the poor, and they do not flinch, because they have been so low once that nothing can be that intense again. With the fear burned out, what is left is peace. The look in their eyes can open the hardest chest. It was in the shaking all along, a seed inside the turbulence, waiting for you to stop running from it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 36, Crisis
This energy, in the body, is the hunger for what you have not yet felt. Gate 36 is the door it comes through: a pull toward the untried experience, the boundary not yet crossed, so strong that a known life comes to feel like no life at all. Its fear is not being equal to what it finds there. The nervousness it makes is that one, the sense of falling short, felt most in the body and in love. The pressure runs in cycles; the wanting builds until you leap, and the leap is often into chaos, taken because nothing was happening rather than because the moment had come.

From outside this looks like impulsiveness, or a life that lurches from one crisis to the next, and part of that is true. The rest is an explorer's wiring, sent ahead to find out what is dangerous and what only looked it, then to come back knowing. The cost is the leap made too soon, out of boredom, which throws away the reward of staying with a thing long enough to become good at it.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
In the soft middle of you is where this hunger is felt: the Emotional Solar Plexus Centre. Everything felt there comes in a wave, and the wave is a chemistry, not a verdict; it rolls through the body whether or not the mind agrees. With this door the wave is the collective kind, high highs, low lows, with a crash for its low: you expected something, it did not come, and the bottom falls out of the world. You are fine one moment and a wreck the next. Nothing decided at either end holds.

In about half of people this centre is switched on, and the wave is theirs. They never know who will let them down next, and when they crash it jars the people near them, because what they feel fills the room. The work is to see the crash as chemistry, stop, breathe, and wait for the wave to pass. In the other half it is not switched on. They have no wave of their own; what they get instead is hit: another person's feeling arrives in them at twice its size, takes them over, then drains away once they leave the room. Some grew up carrying a whole family's feelings, some wonder what is wrong with them for feeling so little, and the work for both is to let what is not theirs pass through, the way a screen door lets the air.

### What completes it — the Channel of Transitoriness (36–35)
The hunger for experience has no voice of its own; it piles up feeling with no way to say what any of it meant. Its other half is Gate 35, in the throat, the drive to say what has changed and to hand it on. Together they make the Channel of Transitoriness, through which a life of lurches becomes a store of stories other people can use. From outside it reads as restlessness. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the experience only turns to wisdom where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
The solar plexus is this energy's place in you, and the reason is the lurch. Ride an elevator that starts down, or feel a plane drop, and the stomach seems to rise before you can think. The loose organs of the belly hang on their folds, so when the floor goes they lag a beat behind you, and the web of nerves behind the stomach, the solar plexus, reports the lag as the stomach climbing. That web reaches every organ in the belly, and the pit of the stomach in front of it is where trouble shows before it is known anywhere else. So the ground giving way under a life is felt in the same place as the floor going under your feet, and it is felt there first. A feeling fought instead of felt ripples out from here as a nervousness through the whole body.

Check it in any elevator that starts down: the stomach lifts, and for that fraction of a second you are the part of you that has not caught up.

### Amino acid
What churns in you when the world lurches is chemistry before it is thought, and one substance in that chemistry is proline. Your body makes it for itself, and meat has plenty of it besides. It is built above all into the stuff that holds you together, the fibre in skin, tendon, bone and every healed cut, about a quarter of which is proline.

The shaking you feel belongs to a family in you. The same chemistry brings the most intense feelings a person can have, then the darkest stretch, a night riddled with doubt, and what it is for is redemption: you are purged by your own suffering, which is the point. Suffering is built into you to crack you open. Freezing when it comes is a thing you learned, not a thing you are.
