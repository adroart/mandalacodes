# UL 49 — Union in the Ashes

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The Urge for Change · Change That Starts at Home · Knowing What You Truly Need · Keeping What Is Good · Set Off Before You Think · Forgiving Yourself · A Life Made New

The urge for change is alive in you when the way you live stops fitting. It starts in you and in your closest bonds, before it reaches the wider world. You feel it first in your belly, as a warmth that shows you plainly what has to go. At its best you understand how things work, so you know what to keep and how long the change will take.

For a while the people closest to you may seem harder to live with. They are not harder; you are seeing old hurts more clearly, and each one you let go shows a smaller one underneath. Forgive yourself for what you did before you could see.

Whenever something sets you off, look under the feeling for the need behind it. Once you see the need, it loses its hold on you, even if it stays. Act from goodwill toward everyone the change will touch. Some people will step back, and others will ask how you did it. Out of the ashes of the old way comes a closer union with the people you love.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Fire is the hexagram of changing the way you live. Fire is the part of you that sees what is still alive and what has died. Lake is the part of you that says so out loud and brings others with you. They sit this way round because what you see and what you say are at odds until one of them gives way. This hexagram has long been read as a skin that is shed. An animal that has grown sheds its old skin whole, and you leave an old way of living the same way. You do not go back to a life you have outgrown.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the openness in you that says what you feel and listens to the answer. It sits on top because a change takes hold only when people hear it and want to join in. Lake has long been read as the open surface of a marsh, which hides nothing. What you say there reaches everyone, and they say something back. On its own, it talks and cheers people on without having seen anything clearly.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the clear seeing in you that tells one thing from another. It lies underneath because every real change starts with seeing, long before a word is said. Fire has long been read as a flame, which gives light and warmth at the same time. The light lets you see a situation as it is, and the warmth gives you the will to act. On its own, it burns whatever it clings to, the good along with the dead.

### Reading
A habit, an arrangement or a bond you live inside has stopped fitting. Now it has to change as a whole. People trust a change like this only once it is done. Before then they doubt it and dislike it, and you may doubt it too. So you do not make it in a hurry. It has its day, and when that day comes you will know it.

Until then, change the one thing you can: yourself. A change that asks everyone but you to change is the old way in new clothes. Make sure the new way is better, and not only different.

Let the change come the way one season follows another, each one clear when it arrives. It starts in your heart, and your mouth speaks only after your heart has changed.

### Judgement
- Revolution. On its own day it is believed.
- Great success, for the one who holds to what is right. Regret disappears.

### Image
- Fire in the middle of the lake: the image of revolution.
- So the wise set the calendar in order and make the seasons clear.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ wrapped in the hide of a yellow ox. → becomes Hexagram 31, Influence.
You are wrapped tight in the hide of a yellow ox and held still. The change is real, but its time has not come, and no one is there yet to help you. Anything you did now would only copy the changes going on around you. Hold firm, stay where you are, and do nothing yet.

**Line 2** · _image:_ your own day has come, and the change may be made. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
Your day has come, after some time has passed. Now the change is right, and going ahead brings good fortune with no blame. Before you start, set your own wants aside. Then you can see the people around you and find the ones who can teach you. Then go.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the talk of change goes round three times. → becomes Hexagram 17, Following.
Pushing ahead here goes badly, and being right does not make it safe. People will not take a change from someone who has not changed. Talk it through, then talk it through again. By the third time, if you have changed first, they will believe you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ regret disappears, and the order itself is changed. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
Regret falls away here, because people trust what you are aiming for. You are strong, and you do not need to push. You can change the order of your life itself, and not only its surface. Change it with your whole heart, and your life turns for the better.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the great one changes like a tiger. → becomes Hexagram 55, Abundance.
You change the way a tiger does, all at once and plain for everyone to see. People trust it before they ask for any proof, because it shows. No change is stronger than the one you make in yourself. Change the whole of yourself, and let it show.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the leopard changes, while small people change only their faces. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
At the end, some people change the way a leopard shows its spots, all the way down. Others change only their faces and follow along. Chasing their hearts now would go badly, because no one can be pushed into changing. The change is made. Stay upright where you are, and let the way you live change people quietly.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Reaction · _Gift:_ Revolution · _Siddhi:_ Rebirth

### Shadow — Reaction
Someone close to you does a small thing, and the heat is up in you before you have thought. A cup left out or a question not answered, and you are already braced for a fight. You cannot react on your own. It takes another person, and it runs hottest with the one you share a bed with. Under each flare is the fear of being left. The more finely you feel, the closer to the surface that fear lies.

Most of what sets you off is a need you did not know you had, like a coffee at the same hour each morning. Miss it once and the whole day goes wrong. You set these needs yourself, then let other people trip over them. A need makes an expectation, and an expectation makes a disappointment that feels like something they did to you. As long as you are the good one and they are the wrong one, the heat has you.

### Repressive nature — Inert
This person has stopped reacting at all. A shock or a hard childhood shut the feeling down. Their life looks the steadiest of all, with no fights and no spark. With a partner they talk about nothing that matters and never show a weak spot. Under the calm sits a disappointment neither of them admits.

### Reactive nature — Rejecting
Someone with this nature leaves first. The closer someone comes, the stronger the fear gets, so they end the bond before it can hurt them. They end up alone and call it safer. They are made for a shared life, so the only bond that lasts for them is one where the two hardly meet.

### Gift — Revolution
Watched instead of obeyed, the heat becomes a way of sorting. A need rises in your body and you watch it rise. You see whether it is met, and you judge neither. Then you ask whether it was needed at all. Often the body was not hungry, and you ate anyway. Once the loud needs quiet down, you hear the quiet ones, for beauty or for a kind word to yourself. Some needs stay and some go. The sorting is uncomfortable, and it is doing you good.

Your cut grows sharper the longer you use it. You can show someone what they do not need and what they do, and it can turn their life around. Your own life gets simpler, more beautiful and harder to sell things to. You can still have the cake, because it is the right afternoon and the right cake to share. No need drove you to it. This is the revolution: to live simply, and to live well.

### Siddhi — Rebirth
Born a second time, you carry nothing of the pattern you start life with. That pattern is wiped clean, not improved. A butterfly keeps nothing of the caterpillar, though it comes from nowhere else. Change goes round and round, the same fight over and over, until one leap takes you clear of the circle. That leap comes because it must, and still you have to make it.

After it you cannot be a loner. Two people who love each other are never only two, because the love between them is a third. You are more yourself than ever, and you can no longer hide.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 49, The Catalyst
Gate 49 is the pressure in you to live by a principle, and to act the moment it is broken. It does not change things for the sake of change. It reads the agreement underneath a bond, between two people or in a whole country. It feels the day the values that held that agreement together have gone. Then it presses you to end it and make a new one on something truer. From inside it feels like loyalty: you keep your word, and you expect others to keep theirs.

The work is knowing when to hold on and when to let go. This pressure can quit too soon, backing away from real closeness. It can also stay far too long in a dead arrangement, afraid of the upheaval, until giving way has turned into giving yourself up. When you do leave, leave with your integrity whole.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
This pressure rises from the Emotional Solar Plexus Center, low in the front of you. It is the one centre that is both an engine and a way of knowing. It knows in waves, from hope down to pain and back up, so it has no truth to give you in the moment. For this gate the wave builds quietly. You put one upset away after another and tell yourself you are fine. Then someone breaks a promise, and it all comes out at once. A feeling lasts a few minutes when you let yourself feel it. Held back, it is packed into the body as tension. After an outburst, a hand on your shoulder brings you back.

A little more than half of people have this centre defined, and their wave never stops. A decision made at the top or the bottom of it does not hold, so their clarity comes over days. In the rest it is open, with no wave of its own. They take in the feelings of the people around them and feel them even more strongly, then find them gone once they are alone. The pull for them is to be nice instead of truthful, to keep the peace.

### What completes it — the Channel of Synthesis (19–49)
A principle alone can cut without warmth. Gate 19 feels what people need, often before they say it. Together they make the Channel of Synthesis, where knowing what has died meets knowing what people need. A change made there looks after the people living inside it. Gate 19 may be in you or in a person close by, and the change holds only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The solar plexus holds this energy because it is the place in you that gets set off. The solar plexus is a knot of nerves on the front of your spine, behind the stomach. It sits a hand's depth behind the soft hollow under your breastbone. Nerves run out from it to everything in your belly. One reaches the gland that readies you to run or fight. A fright travels this road, and the charge is in your blood within seconds. Each need you have forgotten is a wire across your middle. A touch on it sets off that charge before you know what was said. You lose your centre there because it is your centre. Each need you see through takes one wire out, and your breath sinks a little lower.

Feel it when a door slams behind you: the jolt is under your ribs before you have turned. That is the part of you that fires first.

### Amino acid
The change this energy makes begins in your body, and it needs heat. The hotter it runs, the faster it goes. What it builds in you is built from what was already there, out of the ashes of the old. It has a substance in you, shared with one other energy, and the substance is histidine. It goes into every protein your body makes. You cannot make it, so your body takes it in from meat, eggs or beans. What your body makes from it lies behind the warmth and redness where something has got in, a sting or a bite.

Draw a fingernail firmly once across your inner forearm. Within a minute the line reddens, a flush spreads around it, and the line itself may rise pale as you watch. What reddens along the stroke is heat your body made on purpose, from a substance it could not make at all.

## RELATIONS

_UL 50 is both this card's pair and its inverse: what you do with your life once the change is made._

### Pair — UL 50, Melt Into Perfection (The Cauldron)
This energy clears away a way of living that no longer fits. It runs out once the old is gone and the new has to be kept. Melt Into Perfection is where it goes next: the sense of what will feed the people near you, and the care that keeps it. Open it when the change is done and the new life needs looking after. A change is only as good as what it goes on to feed.

### Inverse — UL 50, Melt Into Perfection (The Cauldron)
Turn this energy end for end and the clearing becomes the cooking. Melt Into Perfection is the same change seen from the other side: the pot that turns what came through into food. Open it when you have cleared enough and want to make something from what is left.

### Programming partner — UL 4, Veils of Knowledge
This energy acts on what it feels, and in the heat of a feeling it can cut too fast. Veils of Knowledge holds what it lacks: the patience to understand why things are as they are. At its height that patience becomes forgiveness. Open it when your anger has run ahead of your understanding. A change goes deeper once you have forgiven what came before.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of the Whirlwind
This energy carries change outward, into a home or a whole society. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of the Whirlwind, which shares one chemistry in the body. UL 55, Untouched Perfection, carries the same change inward. There it is the quiet freedom of a life no longer ruled by its needs. This one carries it out into the world.

- **Hebrew letter — Peh, the Mouth:** your own mouth saying out loud what has to change, so the change can begin.
