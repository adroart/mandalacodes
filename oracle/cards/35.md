# UL 35 — Navigational Star

## CODE

_Keywords:_ A Sense of Adventure · Life Opening Up · Adventure Close to Home · Wanting It All Now · Stopping While Still Hungry · Giving Freely · Anything Is Possible

Life is opening up, and you want more of it. That wanting is your sense of adventure, the pull toward whatever you have not tried yet. You do not have to travel far for it. The streets around your own door hold enough to fill a lifetime, once you meet them as new each time. The same walk is never the same walk twice.

Some days you want everything now, and the wanting gets ahead of you. Impatience and appetite feed each other until nothing you reach for is tasted. Stop just before the edge, the way a glass of wine is best a sip short of too much. When you overshoot, be as kind to yourself as to anyone driven by hunger.

Do the things you already do in a new way. Learn the name of the person who serves you in the shop. When someone starts talking to you in the middle of your work, stop and give them your whole attention. Life comes closer when you pause for it, and every ordinary day holds an adventure.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Earth is the hexagram of rising in the world with ease. Fire is the clarity in you that lets people see what you are good at. Earth is the willingness in you to serve and to follow. The clarity sits above the willingness because you rise when the good you do quietly becomes plain to others. This hexagram has long been read as the sun coming up over the earth. A morning shows each thing more clearly by the minute, and nobody has to push it. You rise most easily when you let the good in you be seen and serve the people around you.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the brightness in you that people notice and gather round. It sits on top because it is what the world sees first. Fire has long been read as light spreading in every direction from a flame that lives by clinging to what it burns. In your day that is the warmth a room gathers round, and it needs something under it to feed on. On its own, it burns through what holds it up and goes out.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the steadiness in you that serves, follows and feeds what grows. It lies underneath because everything you rise on rests on the work done quietly below. Earth has long been read as the ground that holds every living thing and feeds it. In your day that is the patient work nobody praises, which lets the rest of your life come up in its own time. On its own, it lets you give way to anyone who walks over you.

### Reading
Things are going well. What you started is growing on its own, like young plants in full sun, and other people can see it. People want you around, ask you back and trust you with more. The old picture is a lord at ease, given many horses by his king and received three times in one day. None of it was seized.

What it asks is that you rise the way he rose. Serve the people above you with loyalty and the people below you with generosity. Advance quietly, like a mouse, and take your seat when it is offered. Be willing to change before you ask anything around you to change. A good run is the easiest time to overreach, so save some of what comes and stay kind to the people who helped you. Every time of rising passes.

This time shows in your eyes: you look up, and you take in more than you did an hour ago. Hold yourself the way the sun comes up, without hurry, lighting each thing it reaches.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ wishing to advance, and held back. → becomes Hexagram 21, Biting Through.
You try to move forward and something stops you, and nobody trusts you yet. Being blocked says nothing about what you are worth. Stay straight and calm, and keep a generous mind toward the people who doubt you. Be generous before it is earned, and the trust will come.

**Line 2** · _image:_ advancing, and sorrowing; a great blessing from the grandmother. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
You are moving forward and grieving at the same time, the way you can dread a winter before it comes. Both are true, so keep going without hurrying to fix the sadness. A blessing reaches you from the people who went before you, from what they put by long ago. Let yourself receive it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ everyone around you in agreement. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
The people around you come into agreement with you, and the regret you carried lifts. Rising needs people who move with you, as well as people who notice you. Where you cannot see clearly, borrow what they can see, without shame. Go along with them, and let them go along with you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ advancing like a rat. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
You get ahead the way a rat does, quickly and at night, taking what you can. It may look bright from outside while something inside is wrong, and the place you are in cannot hold it. Watch too for anyone feeding on what you have built. Advance only in a way you would be glad to be seen doing.

**Line 5** · _image:_ regret gone; loss and gain no longer worth a worry. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
The regret is gone, and from here it no longer matters whether a single day goes up or down. Gains and losses take their turns, and the long run is good. So stop keeping score. Go forward one step at a time, and everything you meet will help.

**Line 6** · _image:_ advancing the horns. → becomes Hexagram 16, Enthusiasm.
You reach the top of the climb with your horns lowered, and force is all you have left to advance with. Used on other people, it spoils what the good run built. Used on the unruly parts of yourself, it does hard and needed work. Do it quickly and do not draw it out, and accept the small regret of having needed force at all.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Hunger · _Gift:_ Adventure · _Siddhi:_ Boundlessness

### Shadow — Hunger
A hunger runs through every person, and nothing out in the world ends it. You do not know what you want, only that you want it now. While it has hold of you, you cannot think about the cost. So you reach out for food or a new person, and soon after you are full the emptiness comes back. Then comes the mistake. Either you cling to what filled you, or you blame it for failing and move on.

Whatever you reach for outside leaves you hungrier than before. Starving the hunger does not end it, and neither does stuffing it. What loosens its grip is to feel, with all of you, how completely it drives you. Once you see that, the reach stops pouring out into the world and turns back inside.

### Repressive nature — Bored
Afraid of where the hunger would take their life, they push it down out of sight. Holding a force that size takes most of their strength. It keeps rising and tempting them toward something new, and they keep pressing it back. They are bored and may not know it. Their face stops shining.

### Reactive nature — Manic
Afraid of an empty life, they fill every hour. They move from one thing to the next in a frenzy and blame each one for not being enough. They try many things and never join them up, so no wisdom comes of it. Chasing the perfect place or person, they miss the adventure itself.

### Gift — Adventure
Adventure is the hunger with love in it. Love here is not one person filling you for a while. It is the feeling of being joined to everything, and it is the only thing that ends the hunger. The reach turns inward, and what changes now is you. There is still some fear in you, but you stand high enough to outwit it.

Most of us keep our lives in sealed rooms: the job that never touches the marriage, the belief that lets no new idea in. Open one door between two of them and new people walk through. The easiest way to change your life is to give without asking anything back, in as many places as you dare. People stop short when you do it, and something opens in you.

### Siddhi — Boundlessness
Boundlessness is the state in which you no longer feel separate from anything. There is nowhere left to reach, because you are part of everything you once reached for. The fear that gave the adventure its edge falls away too. The mind cannot hold this, but the heart can, and the heart treats love and awareness as the same thing. A child already lives this way, and to a child anything is possible. Near a love like this, what seemed impossible happens.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 35, Experience
Gate 35 sits in the throat and presses you to live things, then pass on what they taught you. It wants the new place, the new feeling and the thing you have never done. You go through them to see what they are like, not to reach an end. You are not here to master one thing; you learn by living many.

Its first danger is the rush: one experience straight into the next, with nothing taken in. Its second comes later. After you have seen a great deal, you can grow bored and sure that nothing is new. Then you settle into a life that asks nothing of you. The drive stays healthy when you choose the experiences worth your time and share what they taught you.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center is where what you have lived becomes words and deeds. More of you runs to it than to any other centre, so it is the town square where every road meets. This drive sits here because an experience is not finished until it has been told.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their voice keeps one steady tone whoever they are with, like a bird with its own song. The other three in ten have it open. Their voice takes in every voice in the room and meets each person in that person's own tone. They can feel pressure to speak with nothing to say, and blurt, or talk past the point. When they stop pushing to be heard, the right people come to them. In an open throat this gate shows as many talents and a voice that speaks from what it has lived. It has little patience for what is not worth doing.

### What completes it — the Channel of Transitoriness (35–36)
Gate 36 sits in the centre of feeling and drives you to feel a new thing to the bottom, crisis included. Joined to Gate 35 it makes the Channel of Transitoriness, the love of adventure. You go through a thing to see what it is like and come back with a story others can use. It runs on a wave of feeling, so wait until you feel clear before you jump in. Gate 36 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to the thyroid, and the reason is pace. The thyroid sits low in the front of your neck, across the windpipe, and what it makes sets how fast your body runs. It is the slowest dial in you. Its hormone lasts about a week in your blood, and a change in it shows only over weeks. Whatever the day's hunger wants, your body's pace was set a while ago. The same hormone turns a tadpole into a frog, and nobody sees the moment it happens.

Put two fingers on the inside of your wrist and find the beat. Fear can raise it in a second. The beat you find at rest was set by this gland, and no amount of wanting hurries it.

### Amino acid
The hunger you were born with runs on a substance, and the substance is tryptophan. Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with meat, eggs or beans. You carry less of it than of any other piece you are built from. From it your body makes serotonin, the chemistry of having had enough, and most of that is made in the wall of your gut. So your hunger and your feeling of enough share one chemistry, made inside you from what you eat.

Serotonin is one link in a chain your body makes for itself. Giving with nothing wanted back sets it running, and what follows is happiness and a trust that your life is in order. A meal of bread or rice lets more tryptophan into your brain than a meal of meat does.

## RELATIONS

_UL 36 is this card's pair and its inverse at once, and UL 5 its partner: the light that hides and the patience that waits, where this one rises and reaches._

### Pair — UL 36, Crystal Creation (Darkening of the Light)
This energy reaches for the next new thing, and it runs out when the new thing turns hard and starts to hurt. Crystal Creation is where it goes next: the dark stretch where you feel things to the bottom and keep your light out of sight. Open it when an adventure has turned into a crisis. What you go through there is what makes the story worth telling.

### Inverse — UL 36, Crystal Creation (Darkening of the Light)
Turn this energy end for end and the sun coming up over the ground becomes the sun gone down under it. Crystal Creation is the same light seen at nightfall. Open it when a good run has suddenly stopped.

### Programming partner — UL 5, The Space Between Time
This energy reaches for the next thing, and it runs out when the reaching turns into a rush. The Space Between Time holds what it lacks: the patience to let what is coming arrive in its own time. Patience and adventure hold each other up, and each keeps the other alive. Open it when you cannot bear to wait for anything.

### Codon ring — Ring of Miracles
This energy reaches out to meet everyone, and one reason is that it stands alone. It is the only member of its family, the Ring of Miracles, whose theme is the impossible made possible. With no kin to lean on, it goes out to connect. This one carries the miracle that love makes possible.

- **Hebrew letter — Teth, the Serpent:** the coiled force at the base, which becomes your strength when it is gentled rather than caged.
