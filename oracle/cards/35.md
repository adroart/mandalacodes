# UL 35 — Navigational Star

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Hunger for Life · Wanting the Next Thing · Full, Then Empty Again · Adventure Close to Home · Stopping While Still Hungry · Giving Freely · Nothing Left to Want

This energy is hunger: the wanting that nothing you have yet has satisfied. In you it is the reach for the next thing: the street you have not walked, the face you have not met, the experience still ahead, wanted now, before the one in your hand is finished. It is the part of you that cannot leave life alone, the appetite under everything you would call progress. Whatever you hold, some of you is already looking past it, and that looking is what moves you.

Carried well, this hunger is sweet. It can be savoured and drawn out. It is what makes you human, and what brings people to one table. It does not need a far country. A circle half a mile wide around your own door holds more than one life can get through, provided you meet the same street as new each time. It is new; even the sky over it has moved since yesterday. What goes stale is the looking, not the world, and looking kept fresh makes an ordinary morning something you taste.

Take the hunger to the edge and stop there, the way a glass of wine is best a sip short of too much, so that a smile from someone you love is what tips you over. Keep a little hunger back. A little of it is your backbone; too much you pay for, in tiredness and in wanting more. In a hard stretch the hunger and your impatience feed each other, wanting it now, then wanting the next thing sooner, until nothing you reach for is tasted at all. You will overshoot more times than you can count. Be as forgiving of that as you would be of anyone else who was hungry.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Earth: the sun, and under it the ground it is climbing away from. This is the sun just up, clear of the field and still rising, the light spreading further every minute until the whole day stands open. Its creature is the horse, spirited strength that keeps to the ground. The traditional character sets the sun beside a sign for reaching: the daylight world, spreading.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is light, and what light does is show things their shape. It has no body of its own; it lives by holding to what it burns, the way a flame holds to a wick, so it is also the part of you that clings. In a family it is the middle daughter. In you it is awareness: the clarity that comes up over a day, the eye that picks one thing out from another, the warmth people gather round. Its risk is that it cannot stand alone. Take away what it holds to and it is gone, so a brightness with nothing under it burns out fast.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Under it lies the ground, wide and level, giving under every step. Whatever is set into it comes up, seed to shoot, in its own time. In a family it is the mother. In you it is the yielding side, the willingness to be carried, to follow a light rather than fight one. Here it is what the sun comes up over, the field the day is about to be laid across, and because it does not push, the rising costs nothing. Its risk is going flat: yielding kept up so long it has stopped being a choice, a field that would let anything at all walk over it.

### Reading
You are in the easy part of something, when the light is coming up and the way ahead is clear. What you set going is growing on its own now, the way young plants grow in full sun, and other people can see it. This is the season when you are wanted. The old picture is a lord at ease, given horses by his king in great number, called in to see him three times in one day, so well liked that his company is wanted morning, noon and night. You did not seize any of it. Being received is the situation itself.

What it asks is that you rise the way he rose. He served the ones above him with loyalty, the ones below him with generosity, and that was the whole of his climb. Advance like a mouse, small and quiet, then take your seat like a lion. Be willing to change before you ask anything else to. The light coming up does not make you good; it shows the good that was already in you, so let it show, then brighten it a little every day. The one thing that can spoil this is grabbing at it, because a good run pushed becomes a chase.

In the body this energy is the eyes. Notice where your looking is now: lifted, ahead, taking in more than it did an hour ago. Hold yourself as the sun holds itself in the morning, rising with no hurry, lighting everything on the way.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ wishing to advance, and held back. → becomes Hexagram 21, Biting Through.
Held back at the first step, with nobody yet ready to trust you. The block is outside you, so is the trust, and neither says anything about what you are. Stay straight, stay calm, and keep a large mind toward the ones who have not believed you yet. Be generous before it is earned; the trust follows the patience, never the other way.

**Line 2** · _image:_ advancing, and sorrowing; a great blessing from the grandmother. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
Going up and grieving at the same time, the way you dread a winter before it comes. Both are true, one does not cancel the other, so hold your course through the sadness without hurrying to fix it. The blessing here comes from behind you, from those who went before and put something by. What arrives now was planted a long time ago; let yourself receive it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ everyone around you in agreement. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
The people round you come into agreement, and the regret you were carrying lifts. Rising is not only being seen from above; it is others moving with you, you with them. Where your own light is dim, borrow theirs without shame, since that is what company is for. Go along with them, and let them go along with you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ advancing like a rat. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
Getting on the way a rat gets on, quick and low, at night, hoarding what it can grab. From outside it looks bright and inside it is dark, so however correct each move is, the place you are in cannot hold what you are reaching for. Watch, too, for who is feeding on what you have built. Advance so that you could be seen doing it, or do not advance.

**Line 5** · _image:_ regret gone; loss and gain no longer worth a worry. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
The regret is gone, and from here it no longer matters whether any one day goes up or down. Loss takes its turn with gain, and what is promised is the long run. Empty your mind of the score, and the belly fills. Go forward now, one step at a time, and everything you meet will help.

**Line 6** · _image:_ advancing the horns. → becomes Hexagram 16, Enthusiasm.
At the top of the climb, horns lowered, the only thing left to advance with is force. Turned outward it is conquest, and it spoils everything the light did. Turned on your own city, the unruly parts of you, it is the hard work of self-mastery, and it succeeds. Do it without drawing it out, and know that having needed force at all is the regret you keep.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Hunger · _Gift:_ Adventure · _Siddhi:_ Boundlessness

### Shadow — Hunger
The low of this energy is a hunger that no meal ends. You eat, and within the hour the emptiness is back, faint at first, then not, until you are reaching again. You do not know what you want; you only know you want it now, and while you want it you cannot think about what it costs. So the reach goes out through the door, for food, for someone, and each fills you for about as long as it takes to notice you are full. Then the emptying starts. At that moment two mistakes wait: you hold on to the thing, calling it love, or you blame it for failing you and move on.

Nothing brought in through that door has ended it, because what would end it is not out there; a taste of fullness fetched from outside leaves you hungrier than before. What changes it is not starving it and not stuffing it. It is to feel, all the way down, how completely the hunger has hold of you. Seen whole, its grip loosens. The reach turns round, comes back in, and that turning is the whole of the way out.

### Repressive nature — Bored
Pushed under, the hunger goes quiet in this person. Afraid of where it would take their life, they hold it down, and holding a force that size takes nearly all they have. It comes up anyway, tempts them, goes under again. They are bored and do not know it. The face has stopped shining.

### Reactive nature — Manic
Too busy to be bored, this person fills every hour. An empty one frightens them: flower to flower in a frenzy, blaming each for not being enough. Nothing lasts, nothing joins up, no wisdom comes of it. They run from their past toward a perfect place or person, straight past the gold, the going itself.

### Gift — Adventure
Adventure is this hunger met: the same wanting with the door open, walked through toward life instead of away from it. The wanting that spilled out into the world turns, comes home, and what changes is you. What breaks the hunger is love, not a person who fills you for a while but the feeling of being joined to everything, felt low behind the chest. From there the frontier is inward. There is still fear in you; you have found a footing high enough to outwit it.

A life is kept in sealed rooms, the job that never speaks to the marriage, the belief that lets no new thought in, and one wall taken down between two of them lets the whole house breathe. Learn the name of the man in the shop; listen to your children's music. When someone begins talking in the middle of your work, stop and give them the whole of you, because that pause is where the strange things happen. Of everything in you this is the one place you have a real choice, and it is taken by giving with no price on it. Give like that and something in you settles.

### Siddhi — Boundlessness
There is no door here, because there is no wall, and no one left inside to be hungry. The one who wanted is gone, and what remains has no edges. The mind cannot hold this; it comes apart on contact, which is the point, since it was never the mind that knew it. The heart knows no bounds; from here the word for love and the word for being aware turn out to be one word. There is no beginning to anything and no end. A child already knows this. To a child anything is possible, and so it is.

Seen from here the whole long hunger was love that had not arrived yet, reaching in the dark for what it already was. The wanting, the bored years, the frantic ones, all of it was this, felt through too small an opening. Nothing is asked of you now. When something impossible happens near you, it is not surprising.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 35, Experience
What presses here is the experience you have not had yet, and then the telling of it. In the body it comes in at Gate 35: a hunger for life itself, for the new place, and for the wisdom that only comes from having lived it. Nothing learned from a page satisfies it. The same road, the same conversation, and something in you is already looking for the next.

From outside it looks like inconsistency. What is happening is the drive doing its work, gathering what it exists to gather, and the wisdom comes afterwards, only from the wandering. The cost is the rush: one experience straight into the next with nothing taken in, until you have done everything and learned nothing. After enough has been seen, the appetite curdles into the certainty that there is nothing new under the sun, and you settle into rooms that ask nothing of you. People who carry this all their lives learn to keep meeting life as if they had not done it before, even when they have done a version of it a hundred times, and to choose the experiences worth their time.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The throat is where this pressure arrives, the Throat Centre, the place everything in you crosses to become a word, an act, or a thing in the world. Of all the places in you it is the one joined to the most others, the town square every road runs into, which is why this drive sits there: an experience is not finished until it has been told, and this is where telling happens. Held back, unspoken, what you lived turns sour in you. Let through, it becomes something another person can use.

In about seven people in ten this centre is switched on, and the voice is steady whoever they are with: one particular song, broadcast rather than received. For someone like that, what they lived comes out as story. In the other three it is not, so the voice takes in every voice in the room and gives it back, matching each person it meets. For someone like that there is pressure to speak with nothing yet to say; they speak without being heard, or blurt, or talk on past the point, and the whole of it works only once they stop pushing to be heard, so the right people can come to them. Carried in an open throat, this drive shows as many talents, a voice that speaks from what it has lived, and no patience at all for what is not worth doing.

### What completes it — the Channel of Transitoriness (35–36)
Experience alone is a string of moments that do not add up. Its other half is Gate 36, deep in the feelings, the drive to feel the new thing to the bottom, crisis included. The two make the Channel of Transitoriness: appetite joined to the feeling that makes an event cut deep enough to mark you, then to become a story others can live by. It runs on a wave of feeling, with a wait in it before you act. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the story comes only when the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to the thyroid, and the reason is that nothing hurries it. The thyroid sits low in the front of your neck, across the windpipe, and what it makes sets the pace your body runs at. It is the slowest dial in you: what it makes lasts about a week in your blood, a change shows over weeks, and nothing done today changes it by tonight. Whatever the hunger of the day wants, the pace of your body was set weeks ago and changes slowly, by the same gland whose hormone turns a tadpole into a frog. The change you wait for arrives on its own, and you never catch the moment it happens.

Put two fingers on the inside of your wrist and find the beat. Fear can put it up in a second; the beat you find at rest is the one this gland set, a while back, and no amount of wanting hurries it. Rest your fingers there and you are resting them on the part of you that cannot be rushed.

### Amino acid
The hunger you were born with runs on a substance, and the substance is tryptophan. You cannot make it; it comes in with meat, eggs, cheese or beans, and of all the pieces you are built from it is the rarest. From it your body makes serotonin, the chemistry of having had enough, most of it in the wall of your gut rather than your head. The hunger and the being satisfied are one chemistry, made inside you from what is fed in, so nothing fetched from outside ends it. What would end it is made in you, where the hunger is.

It is one link in a chain your body makes for itself. What sets it running is giving with nothing wanted back; give like that and what follows is happiness, then a trust that the whole of your life is in order.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Leo:** the fixed fire of the lion, the steady warmth at the heart of the day, courage that does not have to roar to be felt.
- **Hebrew letter — Teth, the Serpent:** the creative fire coiled at the base, the animal force that becomes strength when it is gentled rather than caged. The path runs between severity and mercy on the Tree, from Geburah to Chesed.
