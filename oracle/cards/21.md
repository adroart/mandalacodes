# UL 21 — Beyond Binary

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Taking Charge · Deciding for Others · Trusted to Lead · Seeing Before Acting · Holding On Too Tight · Letting Things Go · Courage With Love

When something is stuck and no one will decide, authority wakes in you. You feel it as a steadiness in your chest before you have said a word. You see what has to happen, you say it plainly, and you stand behind it. People settle when you speak, because they trust you to carry what follows.

Some days you try to control everything around you, and by evening you are worn out. Under that grip is a fear that things will fall apart unless you hold them. Notice the fear and let one thing go its own way. Trust life to do some of the work, and your strength comes back.

Authority is given to you by people who trust you, and it cannot be taken. It grows as you stop needing to be in charge. Live in the balance between letting things go their own way and stepping in. Use it to serve the people who gave it to you. Then no one is above you or below you, and the people you lead work beside you.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Thunder is the hexagram of dealing with what stands in the way. Thunder is the strength in you that moves and acts. Fire is the clarity in you that tells one thing from another. The clarity sits above the strength because a blow struck before you can see lands in the wrong place. This hexagram has long been read as a mouth held open by something caught between the teeth. When something sits unsettled between you and another person, you cannot take anything in or speak freely either. Dealing with it lets the two of you come together again. You act well when you see clearly first.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is how you see a situation for what it is, the true part apart from the part that only looks true. It sits on the outside because a judgement has to be clear to everyone it touches, not only to you. Fire has long been read as light spreading in every direction. When a room is lit, everyone in it sees the same thing, and no one has to take your word for it. On its own, your clarity keeps looking and never acts.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the strength in you that gets up and moves a heavy thing. It sits on the inside because everything you do starts as a stirring nobody else can see. Thunder has long been read as a force rising from deep in the ground. Your strength rises the same way, from somewhere deep in you, and shakes you into moving. On its own, it strikes before it has seen where to strike.

### Reading
Something has come between you and the people you live or work with, and it will not go away by itself. Waiting will not move it, and talking around it will not either. It has to be dealt with, once and fairly. First look at it until you can see exactly what it is and how big it is. Then act, and make what you do fit the size of the wrong. Too little leaves it in place. Too much does harm of its own. Whatever you put right by force costs you something too, so try to settle it by talking first. When talking fails, be firm. Rules that everyone knows in advance save a great deal of trouble later.

Put your attention on your jaw for a moment. A jaw goes tight when something has to be dealt with and has not been. Once the matter is handled, the jaw closes and rests, and people can come together again.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the feet held in the stocks, the toes hidden. → becomes Hexagram 35, Progress.
Your feet are held in the stocks, so you cannot walk any further. The mistake is small and it has been caught early. Being stopped now spares you a worse mistake later. Stay where you are and look at the way ahead before you take a step on it, and no blame comes of it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ biting through soft meat, and the nose sinks in with it. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
You bite into soft meat and your nose sinks in with it. The thing gave way so easily that you went further than you meant to. You are acting on something you only half understand. Go no further until you understand it, and there is no blame.

**Line 3** · _image:_ chewing old dried meat, and finding poison in it. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
You chew old dried meat and find something bad in it. An old matter turns out to be worse than it looked, and you took it on without the standing to finish it. It seemed right, and it was not, which brings a little shame. Stop, make it no worse, and no real harm is done.

**Line 4** · _image:_ meat dried hard onto the bone, and a metal arrowhead in it. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.
You chew meat dried hard onto the bone and find an arrowhead in it. This is a fault that has sat for years, and getting it out is slow, hard work. Know how hard it is before you start. Stay honest and keep at it, and it turns out well.

**Line 5** · _image:_ dried meat, and yellow gold found in it. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
You chew dried meat and find yellow gold. You are the one who judges now, and your judgement is fair without being harsh. The power in your hands is real, and so is its danger. Stay upright and stay wary, and there is no blame.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the heavy board locked around the neck, and the ears gone under it. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing Thunder.
The heavy board is locked around your neck, and it covers your ears. You would not listen, and now you cannot hear any warning. Honest advice grates on the ears, and the trouble began there. If anyone is still telling you something you do not want to hear, listen to it now.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Control · _Gift:_ Authority · _Siddhi:_ Valour

### Shadow — Control
Everything you call yours is ground to be held, and the first ground is your own body. From there the line goes out around your family, your house, your money and your country. Under it is the oldest fear there is, that one day you will simply stop existing. So you take up the sword and hack at life to keep it in line, and life hits back. The grip works for a while. Rule people by force and you get your way for a time, and then it all comes apart. Too much discipline makes people rebel, and too little leaves everything loose. Here the one who controls and the one who is controlled both lose. What drives it all is a distrust of life itself. Hold on tight for long enough and you find you were only ever fighting yourself.

### Repressive nature — Submissive
This person hands the wheel to someone else, or to life, then blames life for where it goes. They call it being easygoing. Underneath, they are afraid to be the one who answers for what happens. They would rather not take part at all.

### Reactive nature — Controlling
Here the anger is coiled so tight that nothing nearby is allowed to move on its own. Change is bearable only if they started it. Cross one of their lines and everything held in goes off at once. The strain lands on the heart, and often only a real crisis brings them down.

### Gift — Authority
Strength of this kind comes from giving up control, never from taking more of it. The sword you were handed is rusty, the horse is old and the odds are against you. That is the test, and it is the size you can meet. Meet each one with honesty and care, and the sword starts to shine where people can see it. Loyalty won through fear moves elsewhere the moment it can. Loyalty won through love stays, because there is more love in you than fear.

At its best the one in charge serves, and the one who serves is trusted with charge. Neither is under the other, and the two of you work with a power neither had alone. In time people come toward you, drawn by the same light they want in themselves.

### Siddhi — Valour
Valour is courage with love in it, and it is what is left once no one in you needs to be in charge. You see something noble in the other person, even the one you cannot stand, and you bow to it. In an old story a young king wins a fight, then hands his sword to the man he beat. When you win and still bow to the one you beat, you gain their loyalty for life. The sword that once hacked is used to heal.

People who live this never feel like an authority, and they meet you as a friend. Threaten what they love, though, and they fight with everything they have.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 21, Self-Regulation
Gate 21 is the drive to manage what you have, from your money and your time to the people who rely on you. It wants a say in how each of these is run. It is known as the hunter and the treasurer, the one who brings in what is needed and then keeps count of it. At its best it starts with you. You give yourself the rest and the limits you need, and act from there.

When fear takes over, you feel you have to earn your place. You take on too much and work for support you could simply accept. The challenge here is to let go of outcomes and of managing other people. What is left is a calm in the middle of your own storm.

### Where it lives — the Will Center
The Will Center sits in the chest and gives you energy for the material world, for work, money and what you own. It carries the feeling of "I can do this." It works in bursts and then rests, and pushed past its rest it burns out.

Nearly four people in ten have it defined. They can say what they will do and then do it. A kept promise feels good to them, and testing themselves is a kind of play. Their sense of their own worth grows with each thing done. Their risk is burnout, or impatience with people who cannot simply decide.

The other six have it open, and their will comes and goes with the people near them. Beside someone with a defined will they feel able to do anything, and the feeling leaves when that person does. They can doubt their own value and soak up other people's sense of being worth little. Some spend years proving a worth that is not in question. Their gift is to know what is truly worth doing, and to have nothing to prove.

### What completes it — the Channel of Money (21–45)
Gate 21 manages what there is, and it has no voice of its own. Gate 45 in the Throat Center gives it one, the voice of whoever speaks for the group and shares out what it has. Together they make the Channel of Money. It gives the people around it the sense that someone is at the helm, who will make the hard calls and can be counted on. Gate 45 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
The breath out is the reason this energy is given to your lungs. You just let one go, and you will let the next one go without deciding to. Your lungs take the air of the room across a wall thinner than paper, and a moment later it is in your blood. Breathing is the one thing in your body your will can take over. You can hold a breath, slow it, or shape it into words. You cannot keep the hold. Within a minute or two your body takes the breath back, whatever you decide. That is where your say over your own body ends.

Your lungs have no muscle of their own, and your ribs move them. Breathe all the way out and wait, and the next breath comes without you. Even after the hardest breath out, about a litre of air stays inside. Your lungs have not been empty since your first breath.

### Amino acid
Leading and being led have a chemistry in you, and one substance in it is arginine. Your body makes it for itself, and more comes in with meat, beans or nuts. It is one of the twenty pieces every protein in you is built from. It is also the last step by which your body turns the nitrogen it cannot use into waste and lets it go.

Rank is one of the oldest wounds between people, one above and one below, and only love heals it. Your body carries the same substance whether you lead or follow. When the one in charge serves and the one who serves is trusted, the difference between them starts to fade.

## RELATIONS

_UL 22 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the grace that follows a hard decision, where this one makes it._

### Pair — UL 22, Treasure of the Way (Grace)
This energy settles what stands between people, and it runs out once the matter is settled and feelings are still raw. Treasure of the Way is where it goes next: the grace that gives everyone their dignity after a hard call. Open it when the decision is made and people still need warmth from you.

### Inverse — UL 22, Treasure of the Way (Grace)
Turn this energy end for end and the firm hand becomes a gracious one. Treasure of the Way is the same meeting seen from the side that softens it. Open it when you have been firm for long enough and the people near you need gentleness.

### Programming partner — UL 48, Doorways of the Unknown
This energy takes charge, and it grips hardest when it feels it is not enough. Doorways of the Unknown holds that feeling of not being enough, and the wisdom that fills it. Every tyrant feels inadequate underneath, and so does everyone who only follows. Open it when you catch yourself gripping and cannot say why.

### Codon ring — Ring of Humanity
This energy decides for others, where the oldest wound between people begins. It belongs to a family of six, the Ring of Humanity, which tells the story every person lives. UL 25, The Mysteries Play, carries the wound you are born with. UL 38, Inner Light Symphony, carries the fight with your shadow. UL 17, Pearl of Christos, carries getting past your own mind. UL 10, Internal Treasure, carries finding yourself. UL 51, Unshakable Arrival, carries waking up. This one carries letting go of the need to run life.

### Tarot
The ring and the two trigrams give this card five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana.

- **Ring Arcana — VII · The Chariot** (Control, Steer): The Chariot steers forces that pull different ways, the way you lead people who do not agree.
- **Fire (upper) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): The Emperor sets the rules and keeps them, the order people rest on when you decide.
- **Fire (upper) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): The Sun shows everything as it is, the clear sight you need before you act.
- **Thunder (lower) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Tower is struck and falls, which is what happens to anything held up by force.
- **Thunder (lower) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): Judgement is the call that wakes the sleeper, the fresh start that comes once a matter is settled fairly.

### Immortals
Fire above carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar whose sword cuts through his own false beliefs, as your clarity can cut through yours. Thunder below carries Cao Guo Jiu, who left the court and the power he was born into. He is the rank you can set down when you no longer need it.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Cancer:** the crab that carries its home on its back, the ground you look after and guard wherever you go.
- **Hebrew letter — Chet, the Fence:** a fence round a field, which can keep people out or keep safe what you look after.
