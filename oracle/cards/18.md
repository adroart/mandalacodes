# UL 18 — Liberation of the Greater

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing the Flaw First · What Reached You Spoiled · Needing to Be Right · Waiting to Be Asked · Strong Without Being Hard · Judging from the Heart · Nothing Was Ever Wrong

Putting things right is the energy you carry: a sense for what has gone wrong, built into you as deep as anything is, and the drive to mend it. You see the flaw before a word is said, in a face across a table or in a piece of work. Judging is how a mind thinks, so there is no stopping it, and nobody ever has. It lives wherever something has been let slide, and it makes you the one who noticed.

Carried well, this sense can come from two hearts, and the flaw looks the same from both. From a closed heart what is wrong is a curse you carry into every room, with nothing to be done about it but suffer it. From an open one the same flaw is the reason to do something, and people call that wisdom. Make a judgement knowing, as you make it, that you are making one, and from then on it does not have you. An opinion held lightly needs no defending. The question that settles most of it is whether you would rather be right or happy.

The work begins in your own thinking. The verdicts you carry were mostly handed to you young, and clearing them out is what gives you back a clear head. Only then is the eye fit to turn outward. There are seasons when everything you look at seems wrong, and the tightness of that is felt by the people near you before they have heard a word. What you cannot stand out there is showing you something of your own that is asking to be settled. Mend that first. You were never asked to make the world perfect, only to work on what was spoiled.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Wind: a hard height standing still, and under it the wind that gets into everything, stopped at the foot of the rock. Where air stops moving, food spoils. The traditional character is a dish with worms in it, what was a meal left standing until it bred them. The situation is not the rot. It is the work the rot now asks for.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the height that has stopped, and what a mountain does is stay. It is the limit of what can be seen, the place a road ends, a foot that has ceased to walk. In a family it is the youngest son, the one who keeps still and watches. In you it is the part that holds firm: you can stand where you are and not be moved, keep a standard, refuse to go along. Its risk is going hard, because a thing that has stopped for long enough stops the air around it, and what has no air begins to rot.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Underneath is the wind, which is also the tree, root and branch. What wind does is get in: under a door, into a crack, through the one gap in a wall, without ever breaking anything. In a family it is the eldest daughter, the one who manages things quietly and has her way in the end. In you it is the part that works into a thing gently, that enters where force would fail. Its risk is that mildness turns to giving way, and a wind stopped under a mountain goes stale where it stands.

### Reading
Something has reached you already spoiled. It was going wrong before it was yours, in a house or a piece of work that somebody before you stopped tending, and now it is in your hands. The rot is not your doing. The mending is. That is the whole of the situation, and the old text calls it a good one: a thing that has gone bad can be worked on, so the working brings it through to growth.

What it asks is that you stir the air. Where nothing moves, everything spoils, so the counsel is to rouse whatever has gone still, in yourself as much as in the people round you, the way wind carries seed up a slope until bare ground greens again. Take the whole task on, the way you would wade a wide river, and take it in order: three days before the turning to look at how the thing went bad, three days after to see what your mending did. What is stopped in the bowl has to be finished with before new food can be made, so do not rush it and do not look away from it. Set right this way, what was spoiled becomes the steadiest ground you have.

In the body this energy is the hand. Notice what your hands are doing now, held still or already reaching for the thing to put right. Hold them the way the mountain holds, use them the way wind enters: firm in what they keep to, gentle in how they go in.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ setting right what the father spoiled, and a son to do it. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
The rot is caught at its start, while the ground under it is still sound, and you take up where your father left off. Take up what he meant, not what he did, and the blame he would have carried is lifted from him. It is dangerous here, because what is let go now is the large thing later. Be careful at the beginning and the end comes out well.

**Line 2** · _image:_ setting right what the mother spoiled. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still.
What has been spoiled here lives inside love, and it cannot be corrected the way a hard thing is. Correct it and you injure the love. Leave it and you injure what is right. Only someone strong who is not hard can do this. Bend where bending serves, keep to the middle, and do not carry your own rightness all the way to its edge.

**Line 3** · _image:_ setting right what the father spoiled, with too strong a hand. → becomes Hexagram 4, Youthful Folly.
The mending is done with more strength than it needed, and there is a little regret in that. The errors are real and they are small, nothing past repair. You were right to act; only the hand was heavy. Keep going, let the regret teach the hand, and no great fault comes of it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ going easy on what the father spoiled. → becomes Hexagram 50, The Cauldron.
The rot is seen and allowed, excused as patience, while it grows. To go on this way is to arrive nowhere and be ashamed of the arriving, because what is indulged does not stay small. The fault belongs to the one who will not look. Turn and face what you have been choosing not to see.

**Line 5** · _image:_ setting right what the father spoiled, with praise. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle Wind.
The mending here is done with an empty heart and the right words. Show yourself as the one who does not know, praise what others have, and let their clarity break through your own ignorance. Honesty makes goodwill, and goodwill undoes what force could not. Done this way the work is recognised, and a good name follows you out of it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ serving neither king nor lord, and looking higher. → becomes Hexagram 46, Pushing Upward.
The work is done, and you stand outside the order of rank. You do not serve for pay or for fame; what you serve is the purpose, not the person. With the ground under you firm there is nothing left to correct, so your concern can rise. Keep to what is highest, and what you did will outlast you.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Judgement · _Gift:_ Integrity · _Siddhi:_ Perfection

### Shadow — Judgement
The flaw is the first thing you see, and the mind that sees it was built that way. The low of it is not that you judge but that you turn into your judgements. Each one feels like you, and it makes you feel safe, so you defend it. The defending makes you tight, and people feel the tightness before they have heard one opinion. What the opinion is hardly matters. What matters is how hard it is held.

Most of a day's thinking is this: complaining inside about the people nearest you, or worrying over money, with the mind fixed on some small detail it has blown out of size. It usually set in during the teenage years, when a young mind escapes the flaws it sees everywhere by freezing into one fixed view, borrowed from friends. The view felt like protection. It also stopped the mind growing. Most do not come all the way out of those years, and go on as a hurt child in an adult's coat. Every complaint does its damage twice, to the one it is about and to the one making it.

### Repressive nature — Inferiority
The judging turns inward in this person and stays there. Held to a hard standard young, they measure themselves against whoever seems above them and always come up short. So they go along with the room, agree with the majority, and undermine themselves quietly, certain they are the lesser one wherever they stand.

### Reactive nature — Superiority
This person aims the judging outward and challenges every authority, certain they stand above it. Underneath is an old anger that nobody held a firm enough line when they were young, so every line is theirs to break. They are volatile, and their whole sense of who they are is built on finding others wanting.

### Gift — Integrity
Integrity is a word builders use for the strength of a structure, the load it bears before it fails, and it is the word for what this energy becomes in you. It is a physical thing, a core that sits low, unbent by the crowd, showing in how you stand and move before you have said anything. Every creature has it without trying, which is why there is no judging in the woods. People have to earn it, and once earned it does not leave, because it was the true shape all along.

The eye that found every flaw turns first on your own thinking, and clearing out the borrowed verdicts gives you back a clear head. From there you hold a high standard in everything you do and ask it of others, from the heart, which is never cruel. You go against the grain calmly, without any show, leading by example rather than by pointing. Part of you is in the work, part of you rests outside it where nothing was ever wrong, and that is where the calm comes from. Knowing how a person gets shaped young, you can help others finish growing up.

### Siddhi — Perfection
Nothing was ever wrong, and now you can see it. The world as it stands is complete, the rot included, and the mind that judged it goes quiet because there is nothing left for it to do. Then comes the strange turn: the clearer it is that nothing needs mending, the more you mend, without rest, until you die. The more good you do, the more you see could be done, and that dissatisfaction is not a fault. It is the work itself, aimed at the whole rather than at you.

Looking at anyone, even one who seems wholly bad, you see only what is highest in them, and holding that sight raises them. The striving has ended, and it has not made you leave. You are the one who could have left it all behind and stayed, because a body is part of the whole body of people, which is not well until everyone in it is.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 18, Correction
The drive here is to put right what has gone wrong, and Gate 18 is where it enters: an eye for the crooked thing, the detail everyone else has stepped over, arriving as an instinct before any thought. Its pleasure is not in the finding. It is in the moment a spoiled thing works again, and it never expects the work to be finished, only carried on.

From outside it can look like a person who cannot leave anything alone, and that misses it. The difficulty is timing. The insight is usually right and usually early; offered before it is asked for it lands as an attack, so people throw back the very thing that would have helped them, leaving you judged for having judged. The whole skill is waiting until the correction is wanted, then giving it for the sake of more joy, more ease between people, never to prove you were right. Being right and keeping it to yourself is a feeling worth getting to know. People who carry this all their lives learn that the seeing is automatic and the patience is the art.

### Where it lives — the Spleen Center
This awareness rises low in the body, on the left side under the ribs, in the Spleen Centre, which is the oldest alarm you have. It reads the moment through the senses, knowing before thought whether a thing is safe or off; it keeps you well, and it keeps time. It speaks once, softly, in the present, and it does not repeat itself, so the loud machinery of the mind can talk straight over it. There is a fear kept here, of being judged and of things not being right; met for what it is, that fear loosens into a relaxed alertness, the cat asleep on the porch with one ear turning.

In a little over half of people this centre is switched on, so the knowing is there every day. For someone like that the body has a glow of health to it, and the risk is taking it for granted, pushing on past feeling unwell because the body has always coped. In the rest it is not, so the body is more sensitive: it knows it is getting ill before anything shows, and it takes in the fears of whoever is in the room, making them louder. For someone like that the work is telling which fears are their own, and the reward is a fine sense of what is healthy for other people.

### What completes it — the Channel of Judgment (18–58)

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to the lymph in the body, and the reason is washing. All day your blood leaves water behind in the ground between your cells, along with whatever the cells are done with, and none of it stays there. A second set of vessels in you gathers it, two or three litres a day, and carries it through several hundred small filters where it is inspected; anything wrong in it is met before it is let back into your blood. Nothing in you pumps it. It moves when you move, lifted by every step you take, and it pools when you sit. That is the work of this energy in you too: what was left behind is not left, it is taken up, looked at and made clean.

The clear fluid that beads on a graze before any blood comes is that water, the same water your lymph gathers and carries back; the word itself means clear water, so you have already seen what it washes.

### Amino acid
The energy that puts things right has a substance in you, and the substance is alanine. It is one of the twenty small pieces every part of you is built from. You make this one yourself, from what the sugar in your blood becomes as it burns, so you were never fed it and never had to be. There is more of it in you than of almost any other piece. The fears this energy carries lie deep in your body, in the part of you that tells your own from what is not, and it is going through them, not round them, that opens what is higher in you.

What corrects in you is made from what already runs in you. The same substance runs in the three energies that stand nearest this one, so what you carry here is a chemistry in you, and one you share.
