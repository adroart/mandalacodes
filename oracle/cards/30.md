# UL 30 — Sparking the Blaze

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Passion You Can Feel · Lit From Within · Warmth Others Catch · Wanting What You Don't Need · The Crash After Hope · Laughing at Yourself · On Fire With Longing

Passion is alive in you as heat, and you feel it first in your stomach. When something catches you, your whole body leans toward it. A spark is enough to set you blazing. Your wants make your days bright. People are drawn to that warmth, and your passion wakes theirs.

Some wants take hold, and your mind fans them the way wind fans a flame. Your body needs food and sleep, but most wants are for things you could live without. In the heat of it, a want feels like a need. Sometimes you follow one to the end and find it cost more than it gave. Forgive yourself, because you needed to learn it.

Feel every want all the way, and follow the few that matter most. A want is neither holy nor wicked, so there is nothing to feel guilty about. You can enjoy the pull of it and still know it will pass. Held like this, passion stops weighing on you, and you move through your days lighter.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Fire is the hexagram of what your light holds on to. Fire below is the warmth in you, the part that feels and wants. Fire above is the clarity in you, the part that sees what is worth wanting. The seeing sits above the wanting because a flame lasts only as long as what it burns. Fire has long been read as clinging, since a flame has no shape of its own. It takes the shape of the wood it is on. What you hold on to is what your light becomes.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Up here the fire is the light people see in you. When you care about something, it shows in your face. It sits on top because this is the side of you that faces the world. Fire has long been read as light that spreads in every direction, the way a lamp fills a room. Your clear sight goes out the same way and helps people tell one thing from another. On its own, this light shines until it has used up what feeds it.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Down here the fire is the warmth you feel before anyone sees it. It is the want that catches in you and stays. It sits underneath because every light you give off starts as something you feel. Fire has long been read as heat that both warms and burns up. Your wanting works the same way: it warms your day, and it uses up whatever it is burning. On its own, it holds to whatever comes near, with no eye for what is worth it.

### Reading
Something has lit you up, and the question is what you are holding on to. Everything that shines or grows holds to something, the sun to the sky and the grain to the ground. Your fire lasts only as long as what it burns. Hold to what is true, and your light goes a long way.

What it asks is gentleness with your own strength. The old counsel is to raise a cow, and like a cow you can be strong without pushing. Tend the light in you before you spend it. No fire burns without fuel, and yours comes from the people and the world around you. So stay close to them.

Your heart is where this fire is kept. Light it first, and let its warmth reach the people nearest you. From there it reaches the four corners of the world.

### Judgement

- Raise a cow, and there is good fortune.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ footprints crossing every way at the start. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
The footprints run every way at the start, because you are eager and do not yet know the road. You want to use your light before you have learned to tend it. You have not lost anything yet. Slow down and take each step with care, and no blame comes of it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ yellow light, the colour of the middle. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
Yellow light is the light of the middle, softer than the glare of noon. You know you cannot see it all, and you let others show you what they see. That openness is what makes you strong. Keep to the middle, and the fortune is as good as it gets.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the sun going down, and a jar to drum on. → becomes Hexagram 21, Biting Through.
The afternoon sun is going down, and no light stays at its height. You can drum on a jar and sing, or you can mourn the years going by. Fear of the ending is what turns this hour bad. Take the evening as an evening, and sing while the light lasts.

**Line 4** · _image:_ it comes all at once, flames up, dies down and is thrown out. → becomes Hexagram 22, Grace.
A want comes over you all at once, flares up, dies down and is thrown aside. You leapt at it with nothing to feed it, so it burns hot and finds no place to stay. Do not wait until you burn out to see that a flare is not a fire. So come slowly.

**Line 5** · _image:_ tears in floods, and a sigh of grief; then good fortune. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
Tears come in floods, with sighing and grief, and good fortune follows. You have seen that you are not as bright as you thought. While you weep you do nothing rash, and your pride goes out with the tears. Feel the pain of others as your own. Keep your place, and let the grief do its work.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the king marches out; he strikes the leader and spares the followers. → becomes Hexagram 55, Abundance.
The king marches out, strikes the leader and lets the followers go. In you, the leader is the one want that drives all the others. Take that one on, and leave the small passing thoughts alone, since they only followed. Aim at the head and spare the rest, and there is no blame.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Desire · _Gift:_ Lightness · _Siddhi:_ Rapture

### Shadow — Desire
Desire is a hunger that has left the stomach and gone out into the world. It is a hunger for experience itself, for every taste there is, the bitter ones too. It is not for anything in particular. You are built to yearn, and what you reach for is never the point. The moment you are full, you are empty again, and the yearning starts over.

The suffering comes from what you do against it. Fight the wanting and it grows. Call it unholy and it grows faster. So most people neither give in nor let go, because both are too frightening, and they live half-heartedly in between. The fire itself is not the trouble. The war with it is.

### Repressive nature — Over-Serious
Pushed down, the wanting takes the life force down with it. The whole person stiffens, in body, feeling and mind. Life turns into a serious business, with rules laid over every appetite. Under the rules is a fear of being burned by what they feel.

### Reactive nature — Flippant
Let loose, the wanting follows every want without care and scorns every rule. When the world disapproves, they throw themselves further in. They mean to end the wanting by wearing it out. It wears them out first, often young.

### Gift — Lightness
Lightness comes when you stop fighting and say to life: all right, do your worst. What gives way then is your distrust, the sense that life is against you. You see the joke, that things had to go the way they went, and you see your own place in the story. From then on there is a glint in your eye.

Wants still come and go. Each one is a guest you ask in for tea, and it either leaves in its own time or stays until it is followed. Either way, you know no want will bring you lasting peace, so it has lost its hold. You laugh at yourself first. Real lightness is never afraid of being swept away by a feeling.

### Siddhi — Rapture
Rapture is the one longing left when all the others have burned down into it. Under the thousands of wants that pass through you each day, one grows stronger. It is the longing to be free of suffering and to go back to where you came from. Step into it willingly, and you give up everything, even the wish to be free. You become your longing, and in that you feel you belong in the world.

Then bliss burns you, over and over. It is so close to the surface that whoever comes near you is likely to catch it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 30, Passion
Gate 30 sits in the belly and carries the wish to feel everything, together with the fear of feeling it. It is the most intense gate there is, and it turns up whatever else you carry. It drives you toward a dream you can feel, strong enough to live by. You can hold that dream a long time and bring it into form.

The risk is burnout, and it is often learned young, from leaping the moment the heat rises. So the drive asks you to know which dream is worth the fuel. Wait for the right time to share it. Fed by devotion, a passion lasts for years. Keep your own flame, and it stays lit for the long road.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The Emotional Solar Plexus Center sits in the belly, and it is where feelings are made. It is the only centre that both drives you and lets you know things. It moves in a wave, from hope down to disappointment and up again. The wave is chemistry, and a single feeling lasts only a few minutes unless you hold on to it. There is no truth at the top or the bottom of the wave, so a real decision waits until it has passed.

A little more than half of people have this centre defined, and their feelings are with them all the time. For them the work is to watch the wave rather than become it: anger is rising, rather than I am angry. The rest have it open. Other people's feelings come in and grow louder, then fade when those people leave the room. Being nice can become a way to keep those feelings off. Lived well, an open centre is empathy, knowing what someone feels as if it were yours.

### What completes it — the Channel of Recognition (30–41)
Gate 41, at the base of the spine, is the pressure to imagine an experience before it happens. With Gate 30 it makes the Channel of Recognition, a dream joined to the feeling that can live it. This channel waits to be recognised and invited before it acts. Its low comes when what you hoped for does not arrive, and the bottom falls out of your world. Gate 41 may be in you or in a person close by, and a dream catches fire only when both are there.

## BODY

### Physiology
Your body gives this energy to the solar plexus, and the reason is hunger. The solar plexus is a knot of nerves behind your stomach, the largest of its kind. Its fibres run out in every direction like the rays of a sun. In front of it is the soft hollow under your breastbone, the pit of the stomach. Miss a meal by an hour, and the pang arrives right there, whether you meant to eat or not. Your fear arrives in the same place. So your body feels a want before you have decided anything about it.

Lay a hand flat in that hollow and take a sharp breath in. It pushes out against your hand, then falls back as you let the breath go. About a hand's depth behind your palm is the part of you that wants first.

### Amino acid
When you give in to a strong want, it runs through you like a fire, and your body feels it first. That fire has a chemistry in you, and one substance in it is glutamine. Your body makes it for itself, in your muscles. Your body builds its proteins from twenty small pieces, and your blood carries more of this one than of any other. It carries what is left of a burnt protein safely out of the muscle to where it can be let go.

Follow every want and you often feel low afterwards, and part of that low is this chemistry. The same substance is in you when you listen as when you burn. Your fire burns to make you clean. As your longing grows stronger, your body begins to fill with light.

### Inverse — its own reflection
This fire looks the same from every side, and turning it around shows you nothing new. Face your wanting from any direction and you meet the same fire. There is no other side of it to escape to.

### Programming partner — UL 29, All In
This fire pulls you toward the next promise, and at its edge it leaves every road half walked. All In holds what it lacks, the yes that commits and stays the course. Open it when you keep starting things and dropping them for something new. You feel light once you know you are committed.

This fire burns off what it feeds on, and it cannot do the burning alone. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of Purification, and the other is Universal Crest.
