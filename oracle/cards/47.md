# UL 47 — Garden of Alchemy

## CODE

_Keywords:_ A Weight That Was Here First · Pressed From Every Side · The Thing You Walk Around · Owning What Was Handed Down · One Fear at a Time · The Long Burn · Never Quite Who You Were

You were born carrying a weight that nothing in your life put there, and this is the energy that turns it into something else. The weight is old fear, the fear of everyone you came from, handed down the line in the blood, and it presses hardest on the one thing you least want to look at. In a life it is the stretch when what you refuse to face keeps arriving anyway, the same trouble in the same place, however far you move.

Carried well, it is the strength to own what was handed to you, since it cannot be fixed and cannot be given to anyone else to hold. What terrifies you most is the way through, because what you have been looking for is under the fear. The weight is a mirror. It points exactly where you would have to go, and it comes back until you go there. Owned, it is no longer something that happens to you. The ones who carry it well are hard to pin down, never quite the person they were last year.

The way is slow, and it is meant to be. It takes a long time to burn what generations laid down, longer than any story wants, and there will be mornings when the whole of it sits on your chest before the day begins, a dread with nothing under it. That is the weight being felt at last instead of walked around, and feeling it is the work. Nothing outside you needs to change. Stay with what you would rather not see. What was pressing you flat turns out to have wanted you larger.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Water: a lake, and under it the water that has drained out of it, so the lake stands empty. The old character for the name is a tree drawn inside a fence, alive, with no room to put out its branches. Joy is the upper face, danger the ground, and between the two the strength of the figure is covered over, not gone.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
The lake is the open face of still water, the place a countryside comes to drink. Rain falls into it, animals come down to it, and it gives back what it is given. In the family it is the youngest daughter, the one everybody tells things to. In you it is the mouth: talk, company, the wish to explain yourself until you are understood. On top here it is a face that keeps its ease while the water under it has gone. Its risk is that the mouth goes on working with nothing behind it, and every word spent from an empty lake costs you more of what is left.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water underneath is the gorge, the deep cut in the ground that the lake's water ran down into. One firm line lies between two soft ones, the way a river keeps its force hidden inside a hollow, so that from the bank you cannot tell how much is moving. In the world it is the pit, the place a foot goes through. In you it is the depth that took what the surface used to hold: your reserves went down here, out of reach, not gone. Its risk is that you go down after what you lost and stay down, until the dark is where you live.

### Reading
The lake you have been drawing on has no water in it. Whatever you had in reserve has gone down below your reach, and from outside nothing shows, because the surface keeps its shape after the water has left. You are hemmed in on every side, a tree alive inside a fence with nowhere to put its branches. The strength is not gone. It is covered over, which is a different thing, and the whole counsel rests on that difference.

What it asks is that you keep your ground and stay where you are. Do not explain yourself. Everything in you wants to make the case, to bargain with whoever will listen, and nothing you say here is believed. The mouth is the one part of you this season exhausts. Act instead, quietly, and let what you do be the only thing that speaks. Keep what you are capable of out of sight until its hour. Rising without a pause is what emptied the lake, so the pause is not a failure of the rising but the next part of it.

In the body this is the mouth. Close it, and notice how much of you was leaving through it. The posture is the strange one this figure asks for: joy while in danger, an ease on the surface that is not pretence but the choice of someone who has decided to live out what fell to them. Stake your life on the purpose you carry. Fate can shut a person in. It cannot touch that.

### Judgement
- Hemmed in, and still it grows.
- Hold firm at the centre, and the great in you comes through it well, without fault.
- What you say here is not believed.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ sitting on a bare tree stump, gone down into a shaded gully. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
Sitting on a bare stump, gone down into a dark valley, three years with nobody seeing you. The trouble at the start is a small one, and help stands close above you. The harm is in sitting down inside it and calling the dark a home, so that a small shortage becomes a long one. Stand up while it is still small, before the dark becomes the place you live.

**Line 2** · _image:_ hemmed in even amid wine and food. The scarlet sashes arriving. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
Hemmed in with wine and food on the table, the red sash of the one who can help already on its way. Plenty is not relief, and you know the difference from inside. Everything in you wants to lunge at the first door, and setting out now brings harm. Stay, keep your sincerity as if you were making an offering, and let the help reach you where you are.

**Line 3** · _image:_ stopped by a rock, hands grasping thorns. The house entered, the wife not there. → becomes Hexagram 28, Great Preponderance.
Stopped by a rock, leaning on thorns, and home at last to a house with nobody in it. This impasse is your own making: the rock would have let you round it, the thorns were your choice of where to rest. You have been oppressed by what was not oppressive. Stop grasping at what cuts you, and turn back to what is inside before you lose that as well.

**Line 4** · _image:_ coming slowly, slowly, hindered by a carriage of metal. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
Coming slowly, slowly, held up by a carriage of metal, on your way to someone lower down who needs you. It is late, there is shame in it, and you are out of your place. Your purpose points the right way, and you are not alone in this, because there are companions. Keep coming at the pace you have, since this one has an end.

**Line 5** · _image:_ nose and feet cut, hemmed in by the crimson sash of office. → becomes Hexagram 40, Deliverance.
Nose cut, feet cut, standing and footing both gone, hemmed in by the very office that should have protected you. You have the whole ability and no opening to use it, which is the hardest confinement there is. Do not force one. Keep the treasure hidden, work alone, and joy comes slowly, to the one who makes an offering rather than a plea.

**Line 6** · _image:_ tangled in trailing creepers, swaying on a dangerous height. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
Tangled in creepers on a high and unsteady place, telling yourself that any move will be regretted. The vines are your own doubts, your old mistakes, grown up around you, and they tighten on whoever freezes. The regret is real. Feel it all the way down, then go, because from here the only door is forward, and it opens.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Oppression · _Gift:_ Transmutation · _Siddhi:_ Transfiguration

### Shadow — Oppression
A weight was on you before anything in your life could have put it there. It comes with no event under it, a grief too big to have been earned in one life, and it was not earned in one. It came down the line you were born into, the old fear of everyone you come from, kept in the blood, handed on, and nearly none of it is personal, though all of it feels personal. Most people never look at it. The ways round it are well worn: work, or a fault found in someone else, and each holds for as long as it lasts, with the weight still there when it stops.

What you refuse to look at does not leave. It comes out in the shape of your life, and the deeper you look the further down it seems to go, which is why nearly everyone stops looking. It will not be fixed. It will not be reasoned with, and nobody can carry it for you. It is the thing in front of your nose that you walk around every day.

### Repressive nature — Hopeless
The weight wins, quietly. The pattern has never once broken, so at some point this person stops expecting it to, bows to it, and calls it normal. Hope was given up years ago without anyone noticing, and what is left is a life of small compromises, sealed shut, the fear safe inside because nobody looks.

### Reactive nature — Dogmatic
Fear turned outward wants a floor, so here the answer is a few hard views frozen into place, from science or scripture, it hardly matters which. The views are the security. Anyone who thinks freely near this person is a threat, so the people around them learn to agree or leave, and nothing changes.

### Gift — Transmutation
The weight, looked at, changes state. It does not lift. It turns into something else, the way a solid thing does in a fire, and the fire is your own attention held on a fear for long enough. One fear is met, felt to the bottom, and it goes. The heat of its going lifts you a little, then the next one surfaces. Most of it is small changes over years. Once in a while enough of them add up to a leap, and your life is standing somewhere it was not standing the week before.

What makes it bearable is that you stop needing to be anyone in particular. Under all the smaller fears is the fear of coming apart, and this asks you to come apart on purpose, a little at a time, letting go of each thing you were sure you were. Nothing outside you is at risk. The only thing in danger is the idea that anything about you is fixed. It takes a long time, and the ones who carry this well come to greet the next fear with something close to gladness, because they have learned what is in it.

### Siddhi — Transfiguration
The change reaches the body. What worked for years through the mind, then through the feeling, comes at last to the matter you are made of, and the cells begin to answer. Every element in you was made inside a star, and here it goes back the way it came, a small star of your own. Nobody arrives here by effort, nobody arrives alone: somewhere below choosing, you agreed to carry more than your own wound, the whole inherited ache of everyone, and grace answered from the other side.

The effort drops away. Life gets softer, quieter, while the change goes on, and the gaps between thoughts widen until the thoughts are the interruption. What remains is a face, your own, the first one, lit from inside, the sun out after the storm. There is nothing solid left for the weight to press on. That is what it was for.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 47, Realization
Something lit you up, a picture of how things could be, and the way to make it real has gone missing. This is Gate 47, the drive to turn raw experience into understanding, and it works by sifting: old impressions, half-remembered rooms, turned over until the pattern shows. The question it keeps asking is how. Nothing about it is straight. There is confusion first, then frustration, then a long stretch of nothing, then the whole picture arrives at once, sideways and late, as a recognition that the scattered pieces were always one thing.

From outside this looks like dwelling. People call you negative, or say you have gone quiet on them, because work done this deep looks like a mood. The one thing that can stop the picture arriving is a mind that has already decided it will not, so the pressure here is not for answers but for openness: keep the thing that lit you alive while the how stays missing, and do not read the not-knowing as proof.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
Behind the forehead, between the eyes, is the place this drive works from, the part of you that takes what arrives from above and makes sense of it. It sees the whole with its parts, holds one thing against another, and turns a flood of input into something usable. What it cannot do is act. There is no line from here to any engine in the body, so an answer formed here has to wait to be asked for, or it turns from medicine into poison in the hearing. Most of the strain of this gate comes from forgetting that.

In about half of people this place is switched on, so the mind works the same way every day: it holds what it is given, it can be relied on, and it can lock, so that a belief laid down at twelve is still running the room at forty. For someone like that the work is to keep the mind able to reverse itself, because a mind used to control tightens the whole body with it. In the other half it is open, with no one way of thinking to count on, and the pull is to squeeze the head for ground that is not there. For them the way is to hold the mind lightly, the way you hold a wheel, and to be wise about what is worth thinking about at all.

### What completes it — the Channel of Abstraction (47–64)
What this place sorts has to come from somewhere, and it comes from above. Its other half is Gate 64, at the crown, where the great unsorted flood arrives: dreams, images, the whole unfinished past out of order. The two make the Channel of Abstraction, a mind that works by soaking rather than by steps, restless for a long while, then clear all at once. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the flash only comes where the two meet, and it comes in its own time.

## BODY

### Physiology
Everything that has happened to you is kept on one sheet, and the sheet is the neocortex, the outside of your brain. That is why this energy lives there: its work is the past, and this is where yours is written. The sheet was nearly blank when you were born, and it was wired, year by year, out of what happened to you, so that what you have lived is now what it is made of. What it has learned it cannot put down. When this energy is felt, this is the sheet turning the old material over, holding what arrives against everything kept there, looking for the pattern.

The sheet in you has no feeling of its own. A surgeon can work on it while you lie awake and talking, because the part of you that reads everything cannot feel itself. So the weight you carry is not read there. It is felt, lower in the body, where you meet it, and the sheet behind your forehead only catches up afterward, with the sense of what happened.

### Amino acid
The weight that came down to you is carried in the blood, and the red of your blood begins with glycine. Every red cell you have starts from it. Your body makes it for itself, then takes in more with food, most of all from the skin and bones of meat. What was handed down to you has a substance in you, then, one that is not yours alone: this energy is a chemical family in your body, and the substance is what the family shares.

The part of the family's work that is yours is letting old ways change. It endangers nothing outside you, only the idea that anything about you is fixed, and the ones who carry it find they are always somewhat new. To be in that change is to be alive, all the way through.

### Pair — UL 48, Doorways of the Unknown (The Well)
Turn these six lines over and you get the well, water that rises from below and has never run dry however many towns were built and abandoned over it. The two are one water. Doorways of the Unknown is the source seen from inside the depth; this code is the same source seen from the empty bed of the lake above it, and the old pairing texts say the two meet each other, that the pond drains so that the well can be found. What confinement empties, the well refills from underneath, on its own time, and nobody reaches the second without being drained by the first.

### Programming partner — UL 22, Treasure of the Way
Set opposite UL 22, Treasure of the Way, this code has a partner at every altitude of the arc. The Gene Keys pair them on purpose. At the bottom, the weight you refuse to look at is a dishonouring of yourself, and if you cannot honour your own history you hear others only through your own agenda. In the middle, the graciousness UL 22 carries is what draws the next shadow inward and makes its burning bearable, the manners of someone who treats their own suffering as a guest. At the top, nobody transfigures on effort alone; grace has to come from the other side, and it comes through this partner.

### Codon ring — Ring of Alchemy
The ring is named for what this code does, the old work of turning base material into something finer, and its three kin do the same work on different material. Harmonious Mirage takes friction and makes peace of it, Eternal Wellspring takes exhaustion and makes surrender, Communion takes confusion and lets it clear, and this code takes the pressure of an inherited weight and makes it burn. The rule of the house is that nothing new is brought in. The refusal is the family's; the heat is this code's.

### Tarot
A lake with no water in it, and five cards laid on the dry bed. The ring puts the Magician down first, the worker who asks for nothing he does not already have. Above, on the open surface, the Lovers and the Star, the choice that binds and the small light that outlasts the storm. Below, in the depth, the High Priestess and the Hanged Man, the one who listens and the one who hangs still until the view comes right. Work with what is here, choose once, hope on purpose, listen down, and do not thrash.

- **Ring Arcana — I · The Magician** (Manifestation): The ring hands this code the Magician, standing at a table with the four tools laid out, one hand raised and one pointing down. He receives what is in front of him and changes it, and he never once reaches for something that is not on the table. When the situation is a shortage, the work begins with what the shortage left behind.
- **Lake (upper) — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The open surface above carries the Lovers, the meeting of two that only holds because a choice was made. Under pressure the mouth wants to keep its options open. The Lovers close them, and the union that comes of choosing is what an easy face cannot fake.
- **Lake (upper) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Lake also carries the Star, a woman kneeling at the water's edge pouring from two jugs, one back into the pool and one onto the ground, with a single large star overhead. That is the water returning to the drained bed, and the one star still up when everything else has emptied.
- **Water (lower) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The depth below carries the High Priestess, seated between two pillars with the scroll half hidden, who acts on nothing and hears everything. What went under when the lake drained is also a knowing, and it is reached by listening down rather than by pumping.
- **Water (lower) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): Water also carries the Hanged Man, suspended by one foot and calm about it, seeing the whole situation reversed because walking forward will not work yet. His stillness is the composure the confinement asks for, the willingness to hang there until the pattern shows.

### Immortals
Above, an old general who lost his army, walked into the mountains, and came out an alchemist, able to turn base metal to silver and give it to the poor, with a fan that brings the dead back. Below, a healer who left his body to travel and came back to find it burned, and took the only body available, a lame beggar's, and has limped on an iron crutch with a gourd of medicine ever since. Zhong Li Quan above, Li Tie Guai below. One made gold out of defeat and the other made medicine out of losing everything he had, including his own shape.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Mercury:** the quickest of the planets and the alchemists' own metal, the liquid one that holds no shape of its own and is the agent by which the others change theirs. A mind that turns a thing over until it shows its other side.
- **Hebrew letter — Bet, the House:** the second letter, and the first with an inside. On the old tree its path runs from the crown down into understanding, the direction this code runs, and the fence around the tree is a house from the inside. A house is where work gets done.
