# UL 47 — Garden of Alchemy

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Hope in Hard Times · Growing Where You Are · Facing What You Avoid · One Fear at a Time · Stronger After Hardship · Worn Out and Hemmed In · Old Fears Handed Down

Hope is alive in you, and it grows strongest when life hems you in. You feel it as a pressure inside to become more than you have been. It keeps you true to what lit you up, even when nothing around you changes.

Some stretches wear you out, and the same fear keeps coming back. Much of it was handed down to you before you were born. You cannot fix it or hand it to anyone, but it is yours to own. When you cannot see the next step, trust that the way will show itself in its own time.

Turn toward the fear you most want to avoid, and stay with it. What you look at steadily begins to change, though it takes years and cannot be hurried. You grow in the very place that hemmed you in, and your hope grows with you.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Water is the hexagram of being hemmed in and worn out. Water is the part of you that goes into danger and keeps moving. Lake is the part of you that stays open and glad and talks with people. The gladness sits above the danger because your ease shows on the outside while the hard part goes on within. This hexagram has long been read as a lake with no water in it, the water drained away beneath. You can look fine to everyone while your reserves run dry. Your strength is covered over for a while, not gone. Being worn out does not mean you have lost what you are here to do.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the ease you show to other people, the part of you that talks and cheers them up. It sits on top because your face is what others meet first, however hard things are underneath. Lake has long been read as a marsh, an open stretch of still water where people meet. That is how your good cheer draws people close even on a bad day. On its own, it keeps talking long after anyone is listening, and it spends what little you have left.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the part of you that faces the test and keeps going. It sits underneath because the real trial in this time happens inside, where no one sees it. Water has long been read as a stream running through a gorge, falling to each low place and finding its way on. That is how you get through a hard stretch, one low point after another. On its own, it sinks into the dark and stays there.

### Reading
Your reserves have run dry, and you are hemmed in on every side. From outside you may look fine, because the surface keeps its shape after what was under it has gone. Your strength is not lost. It is covered over for a while.

What you say in this time is not believed, however true it is. The more you explain yourself, the more it wears you out. So let what you do speak for you, and keep what you can do out of sight until its hour comes. Keep to what matters most to you, and do not let hardship talk you out of it. Climbing without a pause is what drained you, so the pause is part of the climb.

In the body this time lives in the mouth. Close it, and notice how much of you was leaving through your words. Accept what has fallen to you and live it out. Keep an easy face and hold your purpose firm within. A time this hard carries the start of a new growth.

### Judgement
- Hemmed in, and still it grows. Holding firm, the great person meets good fortune, without fault.
- What is said is not believed.

### Image
- There is no water in the lake: the picture of being worn out.
- So the wise stake their life on what they are here to do.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ sitting on a bare tree stump, gone down into a shaded gully. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
You sit on a bare stump and wander into a dark valley, and for three years no one sees you. The trouble is still small here, and help is close by. The harm comes from sitting down in it and giving up. Get up while the trouble is still small.

**Line 2** · _image:_ hemmed in even amid wine and food. The scarlet sashes arriving. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
You are worn out with food and wine in front of you, and help from someone in high office is already on its way. Having plenty does not end the strain. You will want to rush out and change everything at once. Stay where you are, stay sincere, and let the help reach you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ stopped by a rock, hands grasping thorns. The house entered, the wife not there. → becomes Hexagram 28, Great Preponderance.
You are stopped by a rock, you lean on thorns, and you come home to find no one there. This trouble is of your own making. You are pressed by what has no need to press you. Let go of what hurts you, and turn back to what is within.

**Line 4** · _image:_ coming slowly, slowly, hindered by a carriage of metal. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
You come slowly, held up in a golden carriage, on your way to someone who needs you. There is shame in being late. Your heart is in the right place, and you do not go alone. Keep coming at the pace you can, because this has an end.

**Line 5** · _image:_ nose and feet cut, hemmed in by the crimson sash of office. → becomes Hexagram 40, Deliverance.
You have lost your standing and your footing, and the people in office hem you in. You have the ability and no chance to use it. Do not force a way out. Keep your gifts out of sight and work alone, and joy comes slowly.

**Line 6** · _image:_ tangled in trailing creepers, swaying on a dangerous height. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
You are caught in creeping vines on a high and shaky place, telling yourself that any move will be regretted. The vines are your own doubts and old mistakes. Feel the regret, then move. Going forward now brings good fortune.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Oppression · _Gift:_ Transmutation · _Siddhi:_ Transfiguration

### Shadow — Oppression
A weight sits on you that did not start in your own life. It is old fear, carried down the line of everyone you come from. It feels personal, though most of it is not. Few people look at it, because the deeper they look, the deeper it seems to go. So they keep a lid on it and escape into work and distraction, or they blame the people around them.

What is kept down does not stay down. It comes out in the shape of your life, and the pattern goes on repeating. No amount of thinking will solve it. The thing that frightens you most is the road that leads you out.

### Repressive nature — Hopeless
This person bows under the weight and calls it normal. The pattern has never broken, so at some point they stop expecting it to. Hope slips away without a sound. They settle for a life of small compromises, shut in, and the fear stays safe because nobody looks at it.

### Reactive nature — Dogmatic
This person pushes the fear outward and holds on to fixed views to feel safe, whether the views come from science or from religion. The views freeze everything in place. Anyone who thinks freely nearby feels like a threat. People around them agree or leave, and nothing changes.

### Gift — Transmutation
Transmutation begins when you take the lid off and look at a fear. Held in your attention long enough, the fear changes, the way a thing changes in the heat of a fire. It does not simply lift. It becomes something else, and the heat of the change lifts you a little. Then the next fear shows itself, and you meet that one too.

Most of the change is small, one day after another. Now and then enough of it adds up to a leap, and your life stands somewhere new. The only thing in danger is the idea that anything about you is fixed. You let go of each thing you were sure you were. In time you greet the next fear almost gladly, because you know there is more light inside it. This change happens in your belly, the place where you feel things most.

### Siddhi — Transfiguration
Transfiguration is the change reaching all the way into your body. It works first through the mind, then through the feelings, and at last it reaches what you are made of. The elements in your body were made inside stars, and here you shine like one. Nobody reaches this by effort alone, and it comes as grace.

Life grows softer and quieter while the change goes on. The gaps between your thoughts widen until the thoughts are the interruption. What shows at the end is you as you really are, lit from within, like the sun after a storm.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 47, Realization
Gate 47 is the drive to make sense of what has happened to you. It turns raw experience into understanding, and the question it keeps asking is how. How do you make real the idea that lit you up? The way there is never straight. Confusion comes first, then frustration, then a stretch where nothing seems to move. Then the fog lifts and the whole thing comes clear at once.

From outside this can look like brooding, and people may take it for a bad mood. The drive needs a hopeful mind. If you have decided that nothing will come, you miss it when it does.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
The Ajna Center sits behind your forehead, between your eyes. It takes in what comes from above and makes sense of it, seeing the whole and every part. It cannot act on what it knows. So an answer from here waits to be asked for, and given at the wrong time it turns from medicine into poison.

About half of people have this centre defined. Their minds work the same way every day, and they can count on what they think. The risk is an old belief that gets stuck and plays in a loop for years. A healthy mind can change its course. In everyone else the centre is open. They take in everyone's thoughts, have no fixed way of thinking and are not built to be certain. Gripping a thought tenses the whole body. Hold your mind lightly, the way you hold a steering wheel, and you can answer what comes.

### What completes it — the Channel of Abstraction (47–64)
Gate 64 in the Head Center above brings you a flood of images, dreams and memories in no order. Gate 47 sorts them for you. Together they make the Channel of Abstraction, and with it you learn by soaking things up rather than step by step. You are restless for a long time, then clear in a flash. Gate 64 may be in you or in someone close, and the flash comes when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The past is the reason this energy is given to the neocortex, the thin outer layer of your brain. It is wired after birth, year by year, out of what happens to you. So your whole life so far is written into it, and it cannot put down what it has learned. When this energy stirs, this layer is turning your old experience over and looking for the pattern in it.

The layer has no feeling of its own. A surgeon can work on it while the person is awake and talking, because the part of you that takes everything in cannot feel itself. So you feel the weight of the past lower in your body, and the understanding catches up later.

### Amino acid
The change in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is glycine. The red of your blood begins with it, because glycine is the first step in making the part of each red cell that carries iron. Your body makes glycine for itself and takes in more from food, most of all from the skin and bones of meat and fish.

This energy shares that substance with three others in you, one family whose work is turning something rough into something fine. Its own part of the work is letting your old ways change. That change happens in your body before it shows anywhere else, and to be in it is to be fully alive.

## RELATIONS

_UL 48 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the well that fills from below, where this one is the lake run dry._

### Pair — UL 48, Doorways of the Unknown (The Well)
This energy grows under pressure, and it runs out when your own reserves are spent. Doorways of the Unknown is where it goes next: the deep source you reach when you are asked for more than you have. Open it when you are worn out and still have to give. A hardship faced openly shows its hidden meaning, and that meaning is the well.

### Inverse — UL 48, Doorways of the Unknown (The Well)
Turn this energy end for end and the dry lake becomes a well. Doorways of the Unknown is the same water seen from its source, rising from below. Open it when you have been drained and need to find what still flows.

### Programming partner — UL 22, Treasure of the Way
This energy looks straight at what weighs on you and changes it. Treasure of the Way holds what makes that bearable: the graciousness that lets you welcome each new fear. Without it, you cannot honour your own past, and you hear other people only through your own wants. Open it when hardship has made you hard.

### Codon ring — Ring of Alchemy
This energy works on what was handed down to you, and it changes you from the inside first. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Alchemy, whose theme is turning something rough into something fine. UL 6, Harmonious Mirage, carries the walls coming down between you and someone close. UL 40, Eternal Wellspring, carries your new openness out into a community. UL 64, Communion, carries the fresh possibilities that open for you next. This one carries the change in your old ways.

### Tarot
Its family and its two trigrams bring five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, to this one.

- **Ring Arcana — I · The Magician** (Manifestation): The Magician works with the tools already on his table, as you work with the hardship already in front of you.
- **Lake (upper) — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lovers are a choice made with the whole heart, the kind that keeps you true to your purpose when times are hard.
- **Lake (upper) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star is hope and healing, the light you keep hold of through the darkest night.
- **Water (lower) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess listens before she speaks, which is what this time asks when your words are not believed.
- **Water (lower) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs still until he sees things another way, the pause that lets a hard time turn.

### Immortals
Lake above carries Zhong Li Quan, a general who lost his army and came back an alchemist who made silver for the poor. He is your defeat turned into something you can give. Water below carries Li Tie Guai, a healer who came back from his own death carrying medicine in a gourd. He is your loss turned into a way to help others.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Mercury:** the swift planet, and the metal the alchemists used to change one thing into another, as you change what weighs on you.
- **Hebrew letter — Bet, the House:** four walls around you, the room that hems you in and the room where you grow.
