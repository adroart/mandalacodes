# UL 54 — Everlasting Bounty

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Ambition · Starting at the Bottom · Knowing How to Climb · Knowing Your Worth · Sharing What You Gain · Never Enough · No Shortcuts

Ambition lives in you as the wish to rise. You feel it most when you start low, in a place where others got there first. Carried with trust, it settles a room before you speak, and people want to work with you.

Some days you measure yourself against everyone above you. Then the wish to rise starts to grab, and people feel it and pull back. Notice the grab without fighting it, and the trust comes back.

Know what you are worth, and win your own approval first, so you have no one to convince. Take no shortcuts, because rushing ahead before you are ready costs more than the long way. Keep what you gain moving between people, and the bounty keeps coming back. You rise, and you bring others up with you.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Lake is the hexagram of coming into a place below the one you want. Lake is the delight in you that draws you toward what is above you. Thunder is the part of you that jumps to act. The delight sits underneath because a liking moves you before you have thought it through. This hexagram has long been read as a young woman who marries in as a second wife, below the first. A newcomer in a house already arranged finds a place by how she carries herself. The same holds wherever you work under someone else's rule. Your worth, carried quietly, raises you further than any push.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the part of you that is stirred by what happens around you. It sits on the outside because the push comes as events you did not choose. Thunder has long been read as the first rumble rising out of the ground, which wakes everything under it. A piece of news can do that to you, and by evening you are already making plans. On its own, it keeps you jumping at every new shake, with no rest between them.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the ease in you that likes people and enjoys talk and trade. It sits on the inside because what you want in a new place starts as a liking, before there is any plan. Lake has long been read as open water, where streams gather and mist rises in the morning. That is how people drift toward someone who is easy to be with. On its own, it follows whatever pleases it, and others start to take you lightly.

### Reading
You have come into a place below the one you hoped for, and someone else already holds the first seat. A change came that was not in your hands, and you are inside it now. Pushing forward from here goes wrong. Nothing you force will help you.

Know what you are worth, and stand in it without show. When you are sure of your worth you have no need to convince anyone. Do not take the first offer, because waiting can work for you. Rise only by ways you would be glad to be seen using. Whatever happens, keep your composure where others can see it. You begin with no power, and by the end you may be the one holding it. Before you start, decide what you are willing to pay, and pay no more.

Thunder is read as the feet, so this time shows in how you walk into a room. Set each foot down fully, and walk in as someone who came to stay.

### Judgement
- The marrying maiden. To set out brings misfortune.
- Nothing is gained by it.

### Image
- Thunder over the lake: the marrying maiden.
- So the wise see what passes in the light of how it ends.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the maiden married as a junior wife; a lame man who can still walk. → becomes Hexagram 40, Deliverance.
You come in as the second wife, lame and still able to walk. The time is wrong for you and your conduct is right, and your conduct is what carries you. Serve well from below, and let the one above you keep the look of authority. Go forward, and it goes well.

**Line 2** · _image:_ blind in one eye, and still able to see. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing Thunder.
Blind in one eye, you can still see. You do not see the whole of it, and you see enough to act. Keep faith with what you chose, the way someone living alone keeps faith when nobody is watching. Hold to what you know is right, and it holds.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the maiden who waited for the high match, and returns to marry as a junior wife. → becomes Hexagram 34, Great Power.
You wait for a higher match, it never comes, and you go back to take the lower place. What went wrong was reaching for the outside and losing hold of what was inside. Do not try to bend the outcome into what you hoped for. Accept what is here, and it can still end well.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the maiden draws out the time; a late marriage comes in its season. → becomes Hexagram 19, Approach.
You let the usual time for marrying pass, and you do not hurry. The offer that came was not the right one, and you would rather come late than come wrong. Use the wait to become more fully yourself. Hold out, and the right time comes.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the king gives his sister in marriage; her sleeves are plainer than her attendant's; the moon is nearly full. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
The king's sister marries below her rank, in sleeves plainer than her maid's. Your worth is in who you are, and it shows without fine clothes. You are the moon a night short of full, still growing. Take the lower place without pride, and good fortune comes.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the woman holds a basket with no fruit in it; the man stabs the sheep and no blood flows. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
She carries a basket with no fruit in it, and he cuts the sheep and no blood flows. You can go through every step of a thing with nothing inside it. A rise made only for its own sake ends this way. Before you go through with something, look at what is in it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Greed · _Gift:_ Aspiration · _Siddhi:_ Ascension

### Shadow — Greed
Greed is the wish to rise with the trust taken out. It wants to be at the top and to look it, with the new car and the bigger house. It is greediest in love. You taste love and chase more of it, then blame the other person when it slips away. Fear sits at its root, the old fear of not surviving if you do not climb. So it trades its own honesty in a moment for what it wants, and that is its downfall. It cannot trust its allies either, since they want the same things.

None of this is wrong in itself. It is raw fuel, and a child who says I want that is only being honest. Seen for what it is, greed shows you the empty place it comes from, and that seeing is where it starts to turn.

### Repressive nature — Unambitious
Held down, the wanting stops before it starts. They start with fire and then give up. Sure the goal is out of reach, they decide not to begin. The drive goes still, then dark. Some turn from the body too, feeding the spirit while starving the flesh.

### Reactive nature — Greedy
Let loose, the wanting has no brake and feeds itself without ever being full. Owning more becomes their rage made visible. They may grow rich and still lack one thing: the feeling that their life is of use to anyone. With the heart shut, they see people as things to own.

### Gift — Aspiration
Aspiration is the same wanting with its trust kept, pointed at something higher. The word shares a root with breathing: you breathe in the raw fuel of the want and refine it. Ambition aims at one goal. Aspiration is wider, a reach toward something higher that you sense before you can name it. It starts with honesty about the wish to rise in you.

Then one question cuts through it: how can I be of use? What you gather goes down to the people below you, the way fruit falls and feeds the roots of its own tree. Your reach turns into hope you carry into rooms where people feel stuck in lives of compromise. When it runs too hot, life brings you back down to earth, and that is part of the gift.

### Siddhi — Ascension
Ascension is the climb that goes on past every ladder. The pressure that once wanted the top now rises through the body itself. It has long been pictured as a serpent coiled at the base of your spine, waking each part of you as it climbs. No step can be skipped. The one person who always makes trouble for you cannot be walked around; it has to pass through your heart. The wanting is never cut out. It is worn smooth, the way wind wears rocks.

The road up takes a warrior's will, and near the top you find you have become all yielding. At the end your body itself changes, in a heat like liquid fire, and then you go still. Keep reaching, and sooner or later you rise.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 54, Ambition
Gate 54 sits at the base of your body and presses you to move up in life. It gives you a feel for how the ladder is built. The ladder may be work or rank, or how deep your understanding goes. It does not tell you what is fair in the climbing, so that part is yours to bring. Nothing you have now ever feels quite enough, and other people's places get measured against yours. Left to run, it overworks, treats people as steps and burns out. Lived well, it holds an idea until it is ripe instead of forcing it into shape. It wants what answers something in you, and it can be content and ambitious at once.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center sits at the very bottom of your trunk, and the pressure to act comes from here. It works like a motor with a pulse. It switches on and off in its own time, and when it is off, no dream can be pushed into being.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They can carry a steady amount of pressure, and other people's hurry does not move them easily. What wears them down is starting something on the wrong day. The other four in ten have it open. They take in the pressure of everyone near them and feel it louder than it is. They rush to clear it, finish one job and find the next already waiting. Their big dreams are still real, and trying to push them into being does not work. Over a life they learn which pressures are theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of Transformation (32–54)
Gate 32, in the spleen, is the sense of what will last and what will not. Together with Gate 54 it makes the Channel of Transformation, the drive to improve a life in business or on the way to waking up. Survival drives it underneath. Gate 32 tells the drive which dreams to follow. Off course, the fear of failing turns into overwork, and the success slips away. Gate 32 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
The tailbone is where your body keeps this energy, and the reason is the base. It is the last bone of your spine, the bottom of the column, curving forward under you. It is what is left of a tail. Five or six weeks after you were conceived you had one, and your body soon took it back. The floor of your body is anchored to it. The two chains of nerves that run down either side of your spine meet in front of it and become one. Every climb up your back starts here.

Sit upright on a hard chair, and your weight rests on the two bones either side of it, with the tailbone hanging free. Slump and lean back, and it takes the weight. It is the bottom of you, and it carries you the moment you stop holding yourself up.

### Amino acid
The wish to rise in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is serine. Every protein in you is built from twenty pieces, and serine is one. One of its carbons goes into the letters of your DNA. It belongs to a pressure you share with five other energies. Pointed outward, that pressure is the urge to gather more. Turned inward, the same pressure becomes the urge for meaning.

That pressure seeks in six ways, and yours is the way of effort. You yoke yourself to a practice or a teacher and climb by degrees, a little finer at each step.

## RELATIONS

_UL 53 is this card's pair, its inverse and its partner at once: the slow growth that keeps the climb from outrunning itself._

### Pair — UL 53, Creation Oscillation (Development)
This energy wants the top, and it runs out when it tries to skip a step. Creation Oscillation is where it goes next: growth that moves by degrees and lasts. Open it when you are climbing faster than the rest of your life can follow. What rises too fast does not hold.

### Inverse — UL 53, Creation Oscillation (Development)
Turn this energy over and the one who comes in from below becomes the one who grows into a place by degrees. Creation Oscillation is the same arrival, seen from the slow side. Open it when you want the place before the ground under it is ready.

### Programming partner — UL 53, Creation Oscillation
This energy brings the drive, and alone it grows the business without growing the person. Creation Oscillation holds what it lacks: the maturity that finishes one cycle before it starts the next. Open it when you keep getting more and it never feels like enough. The drive and the growing need each other.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Seeking
This energy is one of six ways people look for something higher. It belongs to the Ring of Seeking. Creation Oscillation (UL 53) climbs beside it. When climbing wears you out, the others show you another way to seek. Timeless Blossom (UL 52) and Rhythm of Life (UL 58) sit still and let the energy move. Ordinary Valiance (UL 15) seeks by serving, and Nobel Spark (UL 39) cuts straight through.

### Tarot
Three of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, arrive through the ring and the two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): The Hermit is the seeker on the right path, and he says the way up is yours to find.
- **Thunder (upper) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Tower is the shock that brings down what was built for show, so what you raise has to be real.
- **Lake (lower) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star is the hope that draws you on, the part of your wanting that already reaches higher.

### Immortals
Thunder above carries Cao Guo Jiu, told of as a noble who left the court he was born into. Lake below carries Zhong Li Quan, told of as the eldest of the immortals, whose fan brings the dead back to life. One asks you to rise on your own worth, and the other shows that what seemed finished can live again.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Virgo:** the earth sign of careful, patient work, which suits a climb made one honest step at a time.
- **Hebrew letter — Yod, the Hand:** the hand that does the work, so what you rise by is what you do yourself.
