# UL 62 — Voice of Nature

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Precision · Small Things Done Well · The Right Word · Ready When It Counts · Speaking From the Heart · Lost in the Details · Planning Too Much

Precision comes alive in you on the days when only small things can be done. You see the detail everyone else walked past, and you put it right. Before you speak there is a pause in your throat while the right word arrives. You say that word and stop. This is precision the way nature has it, with the seasons arriving on time and no clock to keep them.

Some days the details become a place to hide. The lists grow so that you never reach the feeling underneath. At other times you plan and plan for fear of not being ready. When you catch this, put the list down and feel what it was keeping away.

Give the small thing your full care, and leave the big step until its time comes. Say less, and let your heart choose what you say while your mind only finds the words. Then people lean in when you speak, because every word you say is one you mean.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Mountain is the hexagram of small things done well. Thunder is the part of you that wants to move and be heard. Mountain is the part of you that knows where to stop. The stillness sits underneath because every move you make now has to come out of it. This hexagram has long been read as a bird leaving its call as it flies. A call sent down to the ground is heard, and a call sent up into the sky is lost. So keep your voice near the people it is for. You go further now by doing small things a little better than by reaching for a great one.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder stirs you into action. It wakes what was still and wants it out in the open. It sits on top because it is the side of this time that others see: the first move and the sound it makes. Thunder has long been read as a sound rising out of the ground, and here the mountain takes most of it in. So what you start now is heard nearby, not across the valley. On its own, it rushes out, and the move is bigger than the moment can hold.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain holds you still inside. It knows the limit, the place where the walking stops. It sits underneath because it is the inside of this time, the part no one sees. The mountain has long been read as a boundary, a range of peaks that ends the view. That is how you know how far is enough, and stop there. On its own, it keeps you still so long that nothing gets done.

### Reading
This is a time when small things can be done and great things cannot. The stir in you wants the big move, and the ground under you holds it back. What gets through is what stays close. A little too little is safer now than a little too much.

Go a little past the usual in the small things, always on the side of modesty. Be a little more respectful than the occasion needs. When you lose someone, mourn with more feeling than show. Spend a little less. Do the near thing a little better than anyone else would, and climb one step at a time. Reaching for the great thing now costs more later than it gains today.

Your feet want to go, and your hands keep things in their place. Let your hands be busy with the near thing, and keep your feet on the ground.

### Judgement
- The small goes a little past the mark, and it goes through. It goes well for the one who holds to what is true.
- Small things may be done; great things may not.
- The bird on the wing leaves its call behind it: not up, but down. Great good fortune.

### Image
- Thunder on the mountain: that is the small going past the mark.
- So the wise go a little far in respect, a little far in grief, a little far in thrift.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a bird flying up, and misfortune. → becomes Hexagram 55, Abundance.
A bird flies up before its wings are ready. You want to rise at the very start, when this is the time to rest and get ready. Hurrying to get ahead is how you fall behind, and once you are up, nothing can be done about it. Stay where you are and improve what you already have.

**Line 2** · _image:_ passing the grandfather, meeting the grandmother; not reaching the ruler, meeting the minister. → becomes Hexagram 32, Duration.
You go to find the grandfather and meet the grandmother; you go to the ruler and meet his minister. You did not get as far as you meant to, and you got far enough. The one you overlooked is the one who can help. Take the meeting you are given, humbly, and there is no fault in it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ no extra care taken, and a blow from behind. → becomes Hexagram 16, Enthusiasm.
You feel strong and take no extra care, and someone comes at you from behind. In a time of small things, the danger is the small enemy you thought not worth guarding against. Your own confidence leaves the door open. Take the precaution you think is beneath you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ no fault; meeting the moment without going past it. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
You meet the moment as it is and go no further, and there is no fault in that. Going on from here is dangerous. Hold one firm stance too long and it fails you, because this will not last. Stay alert, do not act yet, and be ready to change when the time does.

**Line 5** · _image:_ heavy clouds and no rain from the west; the prince takes what was in the cave with an arrow on a string. → becomes Hexagram 31, Influence.
Heavy clouds gather and no rain falls: something is building that has not yet broken. You cannot make it rain alone, however high your seat. The prince shoots an arrow on a string into a cave and draws out what was hiding there. The help you need has stepped out of sight. Look for it low down, among people who keep away from the centre.

**Line 6** · _image:_ passing by without meeting, and the bird flying too far. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
The bird flies past the moment it should have met and goes on too high, away from the flock. You pushed past where you should have stopped, and the harm is your own doing. Claiming more than is true is what brings you down. Know how to come back as well as how to go on.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Intellect · _Gift:_ Precision · _Siddhi:_ Impeccability

### Shadow — Intellect
A name is not the thing it names, and the clever mind lives on names. Look at a tree through it and you see what you were taught about it. You see its name and its kind, and you miss the tree. Every fact needs a name, so a life built on facts is built out of words, and words shift. Today's facts become tomorrow's fables. Your days run on numbers, on the clock and in the bank. Much of what you call the world is only what someone said about it.

You were not born this way. A child's intelligence is in how it moves and how its eyes shine, and schooling fills the head until that brightness goes quiet. Intelligence belongs to the heart and cleverness to the head, and the head will argue to the end rather than give way.

### Repressive nature — Obsessive
Turned inward, this becomes a life lost in small things. They use the details so they do not have to feel what hurts. The days fill up with lists, and there is no room for what they might have made. It is their own mind that holds them, not the details.

### Reactive nature — Pedantic
Turned outward, it becomes a mind that cannot switch off. They question everything and enjoy the obscure fact that proves someone else wrong. Watching other people spares them from looking at themselves. Underneath is anger kept bottled up, and most days something lets it out.

### Gift — Precision
Precision starts on the day the questioning turns around on itself. If you were lost in details, you begin to ask why. If you used facts to win, you see that the misery was your own, from years of looking at anything but your own pain. Then it is as if someone cleaned the windows. You look at the tree and take in its life before you reach for its name.

You can tell which patterns are true and which only look true, and you put things and people where they belong. Your words get more exact, and people cannot help listening. It takes some nerve, because you risk being laughed at for speaking from the heart.

### Siddhi — Impeccability
Impeccability comes when you are no longer the one trying to get it right. Your words stop passing through your thinking. They come up out of your own silence, plain, and you need fewer of them the further this goes. What counts now is the moment of making, and not what gets made. A song that no one hears still counts, because the moment of singing it stays.

Nothing in you is out of place, the cracks included. A bowl mended with gold is worth more than one that never broke. The woodpecker finds its food and its home in a dead tree. You find what you need in the parts of your life you tried to leave behind.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 62, Preparation
Gate 62 drives you to get ready. It takes something vast and gives it a handle you can hold. You gather the facts and the small things, so nothing is missing when the time comes. It breaks a hard idea into plain steps that others can follow. This makes yours a voice of reason, grounded on what can be checked.

It goes wrong as over-preparing. The plan becomes more important than what it was for, and the lists grow to keep the fear of not being ready away. Meanwhile the day goes by unattended. Trust that you will know what you need to know when you need to know it.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center sits in your throat, and everything in you is trying to get there. It is where something inside becomes a word or an act. It has no power of its own to move anything. It speaks for whatever it is joined to. Here that is the mind, so what comes out is your view with the details in place. Timing decides whether that view is heard. Advice given before someone is ready works like medicine they cannot take, and it turns to poison. Wait until you are asked.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their voice is steady, with one tone, and they can nearly always find something to say. The other three have it open. Their voice changes with the people around them, and they can meet each person in the tone that reaches them. For them this gate gives the voice firm ground: the details, and what can be checked.

### What completes it — the Channel of Acceptance (17–62)
Gate 17 sits just above, in the part of your mind that forms a view of how things could go. Joined with this gate it makes the Channel of Acceptance, a way of explaining that other people can accept. Your view gives the shape and your details give it ground. What comes out is a hard thing broken into steps a stranger can follow. Gate 17 may be in you or in a person close by, and your details make sense only beside a view.

## BODY

### Physiology
The hum is the reason this energy is given to your throat. Every word you say begins as a small hum there. Two folds no longer than a fingernail flutter there a hundred times a second or more. Your tongue and lips cut that hum into words on the way out. As a baby you had only these sounds, a sigh or a groan. You learned to speak by shaping them. Low in the front of your throat sits the thyroid. Its whole day's work is a fleck, held within a narrow band. That small amount sets the pace of your whole body.

Lay a hand flat on your throat and hum. The buzz under your palm is your voice before it becomes a word. Now whisper the same word, and the buzz is gone. A whisper is only breath, and what you felt a moment ago is what every word of yours is made from.

### Amino acid
The sureness in your body has a substance, and one part of it is tyrosine. What your head is sure of can be argued away. What your body is sure of cannot, and the one thing it is sure of is love. Your body makes tyrosine for itself, so it is never missing. As your words become more exact, your heart shows through them.

A word, once spoken, does not come back. The substance it was spoken through was made in you before you chose the word. You share it with one other energy, the one that leads people with words. Break an old hard cheese and you find white grains that crunch. Those grains are tyrosine, the same substance your own body makes.

## RELATIONS

_UL 61 is this card's pair and its partner at once: the truth felt in silence, where this one finds its words._

### Pair — UL 61, Celestial Remembrance (Inner Truth)
This energy finds the exact word, and it runs out where words cannot reach. Celestial Remembrance is where it goes next: the sureness that needs no proof and moves people without an argument. Open it when every word you have is right and something is still missing. What is said exactly still needs something felt behind it.

### Inverse — UL 62, Voice of Nature (Small Preponderance)
Turn this energy end for end and it comes back the same. There is no other side to it to run to. Whichever way you come at it, it asks for the small thing done well.

### Programming partner — UL 61, Celestial Remembrance
This energy tries to reach the truth through the mind, one exact word at a time. Celestial Remembrance holds what it lacks: a way to the truth that goes around the mind altogether. Each on its own splits people in two, facts on one side and faith on the other. They come together only when the heart leads both. Open it when being right has stopped being enough.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of No Return
This energy chooses its words, and it cannot take them back once they are said. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of No Return, whose theme is the words you cannot unsay. Its one kin is UL 31, Theater of Truth, where your words move a whole room. This one finds the exact word for you.
