# UL 17 — Peral of Christos

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing It Coming · One Eye Closed · Ahead of Your Time · Waiting to Be Asked · Choosing Who to Follow · Certain Too Early · The Whole at Once

This energy is the one that sees ahead, the part of the mind that reads where a thing is going before it gets there. It watches one thing turn into the next, then the next, and from the shape of that it knows what is coming, the way you know rain from the colour of the sky an hour before it falls. It lives in the sorting mind, the part that compares things and finds the flaw. It is the instrument you were given for telling what is true.

Carried well, it is held lightly. What you see is a possibility until the thing has happened, so you go on looking after you have seen, and you let the view change when what you are looking at changes. Much of the time you will be early. What is plain to you now, others will see in twenty years, and the waiting is part of the work, so you learn to keep what you saw without needing anyone to agree with it yet.

Keep looking, and speak only when you are asked. The mind wants one fixed thing to hold so that fear stays still, and a view decided young then defended ever since is that thing. It is not sight. It is the one thing that stops sight. Let what you see be the answer to a question, never the interruption, because the seeing is the same either way and only the timing decides what it does.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Thunder: open water lying still, and under it the first stir of the year, the force that wakes what was buried. Thunder is the sun coming up in the east, and the lake is the sun going down in the west. Thunder inside the lake is evening, the stir gone in under the water, with morning sure to come after it. One thing follows another, in order.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is open water, flat, with a mist standing off it in the morning. It is the mouth, and it does what the mouth does: it speaks, it trades, it takes in gladly. In a family it is the youngest daughter, easy to please and easy with everyone, the one the others go to. In you it is the glad part, the part that meets people, says what it thinks and takes what is offered. On top, it is the one who follows because following is a pleasure and not a duty. Its risk is the mouth that runs on, cheer with nothing under it, water with no depth.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the force that comes up out of the ground in spring, the shock that wakes the seed and cracks the egg. It is the eldest son, the first to move, who goes ahead so the others can come after. In you it is the start of a motion, the thing you did before you had decided to. Underneath, it is what is followed: the strong one who has put himself below the softer ones, so that they take up his movement of their own accord. Its risk is starting again before the first motion has been followed anywhere.

### Reading
You are following, or you are followed, and the two are one situation seen from either end. One thing comes after another. The strong one has put himself under the rest, not over them, and because he is under them they move with him without being told. That is the whole art: whoever wants to lead learns first to go behind. Press people and they pull away. Serve them and they come, then stay. What is asked of you is to see what you are following. Following is neither weak nor strong in itself; it is only as good as what stands at the other end of it. Behind what is true, it goes right through. Behind what is nearest, or loudest, it turns on you. To get, first give; walk behind someone long enough and they will find they are walking behind you. The time itself is what to follow: rise when it rises, go in when it goes down. At nightfall the thunder goes under the water; the one who does this well goes indoors too, eats, rests, with nothing left to prove until morning.

In the body this energy is the foot. Notice where your weight is now: on the step ahead, or on the one you are standing on. Let the foot fall where the ground goes, one step after the last, and keep to what is true while it does.

### Judgement
- Following. It goes through from the source, and it grows.
- Hold to what is true and it goes well. There is no fault in it.

### Image

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the one in office changes what he serves, and goes out of his gate to meet others. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
The standard you served has changed, so what you follow changes with it, and holding to what is true through the change brings good fortune. It is not disloyalty to leave what was only familiar. What it asks is that you do not decide this behind your own door. Go out through the gate, mix with people who are not your usual people, and the change will be worth something.

**Line 2** · _image:_ tied to the small boy, the grown man let go. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
Held by the small boy, the one who needs you and is near, you have let the grown man walk off, the one who could have helped. You cannot keep both; the hands only hold so much. What has you is the lesser thing, and the comfort of being needed by it. Look up from it before the one worth following is out of sight.

**Line 3** · _image:_ tied to the grown man, the small boy let go. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
Held now by the grown one, you have let the small one go, and following gets what it was seeking. The cost is real: the easy tie, the one who leaned on you, is behind you now. Your nature may be softer than your resolve, so it asks you to hold to what is right and not waver, because a will that wavers loses what it chose.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a following that gathers a following of its own; sincere on the way, and clear. → becomes Hexagram 3, Difficulty at the Beginning.
People gather behind you now, and even with a clean aim this is dangerous ground, since a following of your own stands in the light of the one above you. Strength that follows strength goes too far. What it asks is sincerity made plain: stay on the way, know when to advance, when to step back, let your purpose show, and where is the fault in that?

**Line 5** · _image:_ sincere in what is good. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing Thunder.
In the middle of the figure, you trust what is excellent, and it goes well. There is no picture here, only the plain thing: keep faith with what is good. Cover the bad, lift the good, follow strength where strength is right, give way where giving way is, and spend nothing on what is small. Follow the heart's wish, and it will not step over the line.

**Line 6** · _image:_ held fast, tied, and bound; the king makes his offering on the western mountain. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
At the top the following is held so hard it has become a binding. There is devotion in it, the kind a king carries up the mountain where the sun goes down. There is also wanting to be followed without ever having followed, taking by force what only comes by giving. Let the tie loosen, or what you offer stays in the west and never comes home.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Opinion · _Gift:_ Far-Sightedness · _Siddhi:_ Omniscience

### Shadow — Opinion
Somewhere young you took a side, and you have been finding proof for it ever since. You have two eyes so that the world has depth. Close one and everything goes flat; an opinion is one eye closed. The seed was planted in your first seven years, before you had words. It came up in your teens, when the mind went looking for something solid and took whatever was nearest. The mind that does this is the sorting mind, the part that compares, ranks, finds the flaw, and there is nothing wrong with it. The trouble starts when a flaw it found becomes the ground a whole view is built on, and a molehill is a mountain by the time you are grown.

A view held this way has to be defended, and the moment you are defending it you have stopped looking. It was never about the subject, which is why it hardens when it is pushed. Take anything that troubles you and see whether there is a should inside it; that should is the first refusal of what is. Then notice that the view could change by tomorrow, which is how you know it was never you.

### Repressive nature — Self-Critical
Turned on itself, the sorting mind sides with everyone against this person. They do not rate their own view enough to hold one, let alone say it, so they have no ground. They rarely know they are doing it. Year by year they gather quiet proof that they are worth less than the rest.

### Reactive nature — Opinionated
Pointed outward, the same mind builds a doctrine and defends the walls. Logic is the weapon, and its work is making other people wrong. What heats it is anger they have not noticed, and under the anger, fear. Whatever has to be defended to stay standing was never standing on its own.

### Gift — Far-Sightedness
Both eyes open, the world has depth again, and this seeing can begin. You take in the small workings of a thing along with the whole of it, so you can watch one pattern give birth to the next, and from that you see a little way into what is coming. The seeing is not cold. It comes through the heart before it reaches the head. Nor is it idle; it has work, which is to call out what is being misrepresented, because you can see an argument built on one side with the other side hidden. You know opinion from the inside, so when you meet it in someone else you do not fight it, or try to turn them, since that cannot be done. Doubt your own doubt as freely as anyone else's, and you will have nothing left to defend.

At first the view comes and goes. It arrives like a contraction, a moment when the whole is clear, then ordinary sight again, and the plain time between is where what you saw gets taken in. You are given that time so what you saw can settle into an ordinary day.

### Siddhi — Omniscience
Nothing is left of the mind but one eye, and the seeing pours through it in every direction at once. The one who looked and the thing looked at have fallen into each other, so there are no longer two sides to stand between. This is the only part of the mind that survives the opening of the heart. The true seer is blind, because at this height you are not the one seeing: you are seen through.

It comes suddenly, like lightning, and it acts as it sees. A falcon sits hooded on the glove, dozing. The hood comes off, it is wholly awake, and from the eye finding its mark to the drop there is no gap. Seeing like that, turned not to hunting but to showing, ends the old fighting. Nothing needs doing. Whatever you decide is already in step with the whole, and it only comes once nobody is home.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 17, Anticipation
Here the pressure is to run the mind ahead. Gate 17 is the door it comes through: the drive to take what has happened before, find the pattern in it, and follow the pattern forward to what could be, further than most people think is possible. It is there to make order, and from inside it feels like a view on everything, arriving whether or not anyone asked.

That is where it goes wrong. The same pressure that produces the seeing produces the urge to say it now, and said before it was wanted, the view that could have opened a room closes it instead. Then it is read as the know-it-all. From outside the fault looks like arrogance. From inside it is a door that opens and cannot wait. The other habit that grows on it is doubt for its own sake, suspicion worn as intelligence, until the mind that was built to see what could be sees only what could go wrong. People who carry this all their lives learn that the seeing was never the hard part, and that the hard part is holding it until the room asks.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
Behind the brow this pressure works, in the Ajna centre, the part of you that takes what the head is inspired with and makes sense of it. It sees the whole with all its parts at once; it keeps what you believe and what you remember. It cannot act. Nothing joins it to any engine in the body, so its clearest idea sits still until something else moves it, and it must be asked before what it knows is any use; given to someone who did not ask, the same knowledge does harm. Grip a thought and the body tightens with it, in the hands first.

In about half of people this centre is switched on, and the mind never stops. For them, thinking is solid ground: they can rest on being certain, and they are not knocked about by other people's thoughts. What wears them down is believing every thought is a fact, and deciding from the head. In the other half it is not, so the mind has no one way of working. For someone like that a page read is gone, and the temptation is to take on whoever's thoughts are nearest so there is something in the head, then hold on after they have gone. They can be surer than anyone, and that is the sign. What they have instead is many ways of seeing, and the wisdom of choosing what is worth thinking about.

### What completes it — the Channel of Acceptance (17–62)
What is seen here has no way of being said. Its other half is Gate 62, in the throat, the drive for detail, for the small facts and their names put in order. The two make the Channel of Acceptance, through which a view of what could be is laid out in steps a stranger can walk, and it is built for everybody rather than for one life. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the seeing stays a hunch until it meets the facts that let it be used.

## BODY

### Physiology
The pituitary carries this energy, and the eyes are the reason. The gland hangs under your brain, in the middle of your head. The nerves from your two eyes cross directly above it, so everything you look at passes over it on its way to becoming sight. It does none of your seeing itself. Nearly everything it sends out is a message, to the gland in your neck that sets your pace or the ones over your kidneys that rouse you, and the rest of you does the doing. That is what seeing ahead is in you: a small thing that reads and sends word, then waits for you to move.

It is about the size of a pea, and how tall you grew was decided by it, year by year, in what it released while you slept. Close your eyes, put a fingertip to the bridge of your nose: the crossing is straight back from there, deep in the head, and the gland hangs just beneath it, under everything you have ever looked at.

### Amino acid
The seeing you carry has a substance in you, and the substance is arginine. It is one of the twenty small pieces everything in you is built from, and it is written into you six ways, more ways than almost any other piece. Your body makes some of it, and the rest comes in from meat or a handful of nuts. What people are still to become is folded into you there, with what you already are.

What sees ahead in you comes from the heart before the head, and in the body the two share one chemistry. There is nothing here for you to put a hand on. It is only that the eye that reads what is coming was written into you before you first opened the other two.

- **Ring Arcana — VII · The Chariot** (Control, Steer): Through the Ring of Humanity, this code carries the Chariot, the one who holds two opposed forces in harness and drives them as a single line. The seeing that takes both sides at once is exactly this, two halves reined into one forward motion that neither could make alone.
- **Lake (upper) — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lake above this hexagram wears the face of the Lovers, the moment of choosing, the union of two into a covenant. The line readings turn on this exact thing, the small son or the grown man, and which you take is the whole of it.
- **Lake (upper) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): Lake also carries the Star, the quiet hope that pours after the breaking, the vision held open. The far-sightedness of this code is its star, the eye that sees a little way into what is coming and trusts it.
- **Thunder (lower) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Thunder at the root wears the Tower, the lightning that breaks the fixed structure open. The arousing force underneath is the same shock that shatters a hardened opinion and frees what it was walling in.
- **Thunder (lower) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): Thunder also carries Judgement, the trumpet that calls the new life up out of the ground. The stir at the depth is a waking, the buried thing answering a sound it cannot refuse.

### Immortals
Zhong Li Quan stands above, the elder who carries the fan that revives the dead and turns stones to gold, and Cao Guo Jiu below, who walked away from the inherited court. A fan that wakes the dead is worth following and a title handed down is not, and the difference between the two is the whole art of choosing who deserves your steps and who only inherited them.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Cancer:** the cardinal water that holds and shelters, the sign of the protective shell, of holding a course by feeling rather than force. The Chariot's own sign, the will that moves while staying soft inside.
- **Hebrew letter — Cheth, the Fence:** the enclosure, the boundary that holds a thing safe so it can grow, the path of the Chariot on the Tree. The fence is what lets the seeing be held without being gripped.
