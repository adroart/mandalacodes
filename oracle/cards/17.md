# UL 17 — Pearl of Christos

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing Where It Leads · Ahead of Your Time · Speaking When Asked · Both Sides at Once · Choosing Whom to Follow · Holding a View Lightly · A Mind Made Up Young

When something is only starting, you can often already see where it is heading. You watch one step lead to the next, and the pattern shows you what comes after. You feel it in your chest before your head can explain it. What you see can lift a conversation and help people imagine more than they thought possible.

The people around you may not be ready to hear it for years. Said too early, it only makes them push back, and you may start to doubt what you saw. Hold on to it, and the world catches up in its own time.

So hold what you see as a possibility and keep looking as things change. Share it when someone asks, and people will take it as help. Then you can see both sides of a question at once, and what you say points everyone in a better direction.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Thunder is the hexagram of following and being followed. Thunder is the drive in you to get up and move. Lake is the pleasure you take in the people around you. The movement sits below the pleasure because people come along gladly only when nobody is pushing them. This hexagram has long been read as thunder gone down into a lake at nightfall. The storm rests under the water until morning, the way you stop and go home when the day is done. It is also read as the strong one walking behind the weaker ones. You are followed once you have learned to follow.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the part of you that talks with people, trades with them and enjoys their company. It sits on the outside because this is how people meet you first, openly and with pleasure. Lake has long been read as an open marsh with mist rising off it, and as a mouth. A kind word over a shared meal is how people come to walk beside you. On its own, it pleases everyone and goes nowhere.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the part of you that starts to move before you have decided to. It lies underneath because what moves a group comes from inside, where nobody feels pushed. Thunder has long been read as the force that rises out of the ground in spring and cracks a seed open. It is the same jolt that gets you out of your chair when something needs doing. On its own, it starts off and never looks back to see who is coming.

### Reading
This is a time of following. Someone goes ahead and others come after, and you may be on either side of it. People follow gladly or not at all. Push them and they pull away. Serve them and they come, and they stay. So whoever wants others behind them learns first to walk behind someone worth following. To get, you give first.

What it asks is care over what you follow. Following is only as good as what lies ahead of it. Behind what is right, it takes you a long way. Behind whatever is nearest or loudest, it turns on you. Follow the time as well: move while the day moves, and stop when it ends.

Your feet do this now, each step going where the last one led. Walk behind the one you trust, at their pace. When evening comes, go in and rest until morning.

### Judgement
- Following. Great success, and it goes well for the one who stays true.
- There is no fault in it.

### Image
- Thunder in the middle of the lake: that is following.
- So at nightfall the wise go indoors and rest.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the standard changes, and you go out of your door to meet others. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
What you have been following has changed, and you change with it. Staying true to what is right through the change brings good fortune. Do not work this out alone at home. Go out and meet people who are not your usual company, and good work comes of it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ tied to the small boy, the grown man let go. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
You hold on to the one who needs you and lose the one who could help you. You cannot keep both. Being needed is a comfort, and it is keeping you small. Look up and see who is worth following before they are out of sight.

**Line 3** · _image:_ tied to the grown man, the small boy let go. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
This time you go with the one who can help you and let go of the one who leaned on you. Following like this gets you what you are after. The cost is real, and your heart is softer than your choice. Stay with what is right and do not go back on it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a following that gathers followers of its own. → becomes Hexagram 3, Difficulty at the Beginning.
People have started to follow you, and it is working. Even so there is danger in it, because the one you follow may take you for a rival. Two strong people pulling the same way is too much strength. Be sincere, and let everyone see what you are aiming at.

**Line 5** · _image:_ sincere in what is good. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing Thunder.
You trust what is good, and it goes well. Speak up for the good in people and let the bad pass without comment. Spend nothing on small quarrels. Follow what your heart wants, and it will not lead you over the line.

**Line 6** · _image:_ held fast, tied and bound; the king makes his offering on the western mountain. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
The following here is held so tight it has become a binding. There is devotion in it, the kind a king carries up a mountain as an offering. There is also a wish to be followed without ever having followed. Loosen your hold, and let people come of their own accord.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Opinion · _Gift:_ Far-Sightedness · _Siddhi:_ Omniscience

### Shadow — Opinion
Somewhere young you took a side, and you have been finding proof for it ever since. You have two eyes so that the world has depth, and a fixed view closes one of them. The seed goes in during your first seven years, before you have words for it. It comes up in your teens, when the mind looks for something solid to hold on to. It finds a flaw in people or in you, and builds a whole story around it. By the time you are grown, a small thing has become a mountain.

A view held that way has to be defended, and while you defend it you stop looking. Take something that bothers you and see whether a should is hidden in it, the feeling that things should not be like this. That feeling is the first refusal of how things are. Notice, too, that the view could change by tomorrow. That is how you know the view is not who you are.

### Repressive nature — Self-Critical
Turned inward, the sorting mind compares this person with everyone and sides with the others. They do not value their own view enough to hold one, let alone say it. They rarely notice they are doing it. Year after year they gather proof that their life counts for little.

### Reactive nature — Opinionated
Pointed outward, the same mind builds a set of rules and uses logic to make other people wrong. Whatever needs defending to stay standing is only a view. What drives this person is anger they have not noticed, and under the anger, fear.

### Gift — Far-Sightedness
Far-sightedness is seeing both sides of a thing at once. With both eyes open the world has depth again. You take in the small workings of a thing along with the whole of it. This gift has work to do. It calls out an argument that shows one side and hides the other. You know a fixed view from the inside, so you do not fight it in anyone else or try to win them round. You doubt your own doubt as freely as anyone else's, so there is nothing to defend.

At first the view comes and goes. There is a moment when the whole is clear, then ordinary sight again. You need the plain days between, to bring what you saw into your life.

### Siddhi — Omniscience
Omniscience is one eye that sees in every direction at once. There is no longer you on one side and the world on the other, so there is nothing to take sides about. It feels less like seeing and more like being seen through, with nobody in the way.

A falcon wears a hood on the falconer's glove and dozes. When the hood comes off it is awake at once, and its body moves the moment its eye finds a mark. At this height you are the same: you see, and you act in that moment without second-guessing. Nothing needs doing, and whatever you decide is already right.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 17, Anticipation
Gate 17 presses your mind to run ahead. It takes what has already happened, finds the pattern in it and follows the pattern forward to what could be. It often reaches further than other people think is possible. It wants order in a world that keeps surprising you.

The same pressure brings the urge to say it now. A view offered before anyone asked shuts a room that it could have opened. The other habit is doubt for its own sake, until a mind built to see what could go right sees only what could go wrong. So hold the view until someone asks, and keep it open to change. People change, and not everything in a life follows a pattern.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
The Ajna Center sits behind your brow. It takes what your head is inspired by and makes sense of it, seeing the whole and all its parts. It keeps what you believe and what you remember. It cannot act, because no path runs from it to any engine in the body. A clear idea waits there until something else moves it. Offered to someone who is not ready, what it knows turns from medicine into poison. Grip a thought and your hands tighten with it.

About half of people have this centre defined. Their mind never stops, and they can rest on being sure of what they think. Their risk is making every decision from the head. In everyone else it is open. A page read is soon gone, and the pull is to borrow the nearest person's thoughts so there is something to hold. They can end up surer than anyone, which is the warning sign. Held lightly, their mind sees many ways at once and can choose what is worth thinking about.

### What completes it — the Channel of Acceptance (17–62)
Gate 62 sits in the throat and looks after details, the small facts and their names put in order. With Gate 17 it makes the Channel of Acceptance. This channel takes a view of what could be and lays it out in steps other people can follow. Gate 62 may be in you or in a person close by. A hunch becomes useful only when it meets the facts.

## BODY

### Physiology
Your pituitary gland carries this energy, and the reason is your eyes. The nerves from your two eyes cross just above it, deep in the middle of your head. Everything you look at passes over it on its way to becoming sight. It does none of the seeing itself. Nearly everything it releases is word sent to another gland, to your thyroid or to the glands above your kidneys, and they do the work. A pea-sized gland sends word, and the whole of you answers.

How tall you grew was set by this gland, year by year, in what it released while you slept. Close your eyes and put a fingertip to the bridge of your nose. The crossing is straight back from there, deep in your head, and the gland hangs just beneath it.

### Amino acid
Your far sight has a chemistry, and one substance in it is arginine. It is one of the twenty pieces every protein in you is built from. Your body makes most of what it needs, and the rest comes from meat, eggs or a handful of nuts. It is written into you in six different ways, more than almost any other piece.

You share this chemistry with the other energies that make a person human. What people are still to become is folded into it, along with what you already are.

## RELATIONS

_UL 18 is this card's pair, its inverse and its partner at once: the hands that mend what this energy sees coming._

### Pair — UL 18, Liberation of the Greater (Work on the Decayed)
This energy sees where a thing is heading, and it stops at seeing. Liberation of the Greater is where it goes next: the work of setting right what has gone wrong. Open it when you can see trouble coming and nobody has moved to fix it. Seeing a flaw is half the job, and mending it is the other part.

### Inverse — UL 18, Liberation of the Greater (Work on the Decayed)
Turn this energy end for end and looking ahead becomes looking back. Liberation of the Greater deals with what the past has left behind. Open it when the road ahead is clear but something behind you still needs care.

### Programming partner — UL 18, Liberation of the Greater
This energy sees the whole pattern and holds no tool to change it. Liberation of the Greater holds what it lacks: the need to find what is wrong and put it right. The two are born together in everyone, as opinion and judgement, and at their best they become far sight and integrity. Open it when you have seen enough and it is time to act.

With UL 21, Beyond Binary, you give up control. With UL 10, Internal Treasure, you find yourself. With UL 51, Unshakable Arrival, you wake up.
