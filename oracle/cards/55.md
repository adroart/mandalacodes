# UL 55 — Untouched Perfection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Free From Within · Life at Its Fullest · Every Feeling Your Own · Making From Plenty · Growing Lighter · Chasing the Next High · Blaming the World

Freedom is a fullness you feel from the inside. On a good day your whole life seems to be at its height, and your feelings run as high as they go. You know the lift does not come from the money or the person who walks in, so you have nothing to chase. There is a perfection in you that nothing happening around you can touch. What you make from there comes out of plenty.

Some days the feeling drops, and you want to blame something outside you for it. Let the low be yours as much as the high was. A low you do not pin on anyone passes on its own and leaves you clear.

You cannot chase freedom head on. It comes sideways, while you live an ordinary life with everything in it. When a time is at its fullest, spend it and share it rather than guard it against the end. Let yourself be as you are, and if that changes, let it. Each time you do, you grow lighter and freer.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Fire is the hexagram of a full life at its height. Fire is the clarity in you that sees the whole of what you have. Thunder is the drive in you that acts on it. The action rests on the clarity because what you do well grows out of what you see clearly. This hexagram has long been read as thunder and lightning arriving together. In one flash you see everything you have, and the thunder moves you to act on it. A storm like that is over fast, and so is a time this full. It is also read as the sun at noon, which has only one way left to go. Your fullest times are real, and they are yours to live while they last.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the stir in you that gets you up and moving when something must be done. It sits on top because a full time shows outside you as action, in what you do with what you have. Thunder has long been read as a sound rising from deep in the earth, when what was shut breaks open. Whatever you had kept shut breaks open at it, the way a seed splits. On its own, it startles you, and the trembling goes on after the moment has passed.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the part of you that sees clearly and tells one thing from another. It lies underneath because everything you do well grows out of what you understand. Fire has long been read as a light that spreads in every direction at once. People come to someone who sees clearly, the way they gather at a fire. On its own, it shines and does nothing with what it sees.

### Reading
Everything in your life has come to its height at the same time. The work is done, the people are near and there is plenty. A time this full cannot last, and the counsel here is plain: do not grieve. The sun at noon begins to set, and the full moon begins to wane. Spend the bright hours giving your light to everyone around you, the way the sun shines on all. Know when you have enough, and stop there.

The danger is a screen. Pride or doubt draws a curtain around the fullness until the stars show at noon. When someone doubts you, say plainly what you mean, and the shade lifts. Meet your equals as partners, and draw able people in. Whoever shuts the door on a full house ends up alone in it.

Your eyes and your feet do the work now. Your eyes take in the whole of what you have in one look, and your feet move while the light lasts. Stand the way someone stands at the top of a good day, with open hands and the doors open.

### Judgement
- Abundance goes through. The king reaches it. Do not grieve.
- Be like the sun at noon.

### Image
- Thunder and lightning come together: the image of abundance.
- So the wise decide a case with the clarity of the flash, and carry out the sentence with the force of the thunder.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ meeting your equal, who becomes your partner. → becomes Hexagram 62, Small Preponderance.
You meet someone of your own strength, and there is no fault in being alike. For a while, ten days or ten years, you sharpen each other. Pushed too far, being equals turns to disaster. Go forward together while it is fresh, and you will be honoured.

**Line 2** · _image:_ curtains so thick the stars show at noon. → becomes Hexagram 34, Great Power.
The curtains are so thick that the stars show at midday, and the one above you cannot see. Go to them now and you meet suspicion, which spreads like a sickness. The shade is made of doubt. Hold to what you truly mean, say it plainly, and the doubt lifts.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a darkness at noon, and the right arm broken. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing Thunder.
The dark is so deep that even the small stars show at noon, and your right arm is broken. Nothing great can be done now, and you are not to blame. You pushed too fast and fell back as fast. Rest the arm, and wait for the light to return.

**Line 4** · _image:_ stars at noon, and the hidden one met. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
The curtain is still thick, but the dark is thinning. In it you meet someone of your own kind, plain to look at and carrying more than shows. Be strong without forcing anything. Go toward the one you found, and it goes well.

**Line 5** · _image:_ brilliance coming, and the able gathered round. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
Light returns, and able people gather round you. There is praise, and there is good fortune. The striving nobody saw ends in an ease everybody sees. Empty yourself of the need to do it all, and the fullness arrives by itself.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the house made grand, the door shut, and no one seen for three years. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
The house is made grand and screened off, and whoever looks through the door finds it still and empty. For three years nobody comes. This is plenty hidden away for fear the light would show what is inside. Open the door while someone is still outside it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Victimisation · _Gift:_ Freedom · _Siddhi:_ Freedom

### Shadow — Victimisation
Every mood seems to come from outside you, and you can always name the cause. The high was the money, the good weather or the right person arriving. The low was the food, your partner or the news. Looking outside for the reason you feel the way you do is the commonest habit on earth. It is so common that it looks like plain noticing.

What holds you there is longing. When you are low, you long to be high. When you are high, you long to hold on to it. The hunger is for the search and never for the finding, because what you love is the hope. So you rise and fall on every swell, in love and then out of it. Your breath goes thin at the bottom and fills your chest at the top, and you call the swell freedom.

### Repressive nature — Complaining
Held in, this shadow becomes complaining, most of it silent. Every complaint puts you at the centre of a sad story and keeps the story going. Each one hands a little of your power away. Over time it wears the body down, and it makes life as hard as it says it is.

### Reactive nature — Blaming
Let out, the same shadow fires blame at a target. The arrow lifts the weight of your situation off you and onto someone else, so the drama goes on. You cannot blame another for your fate while you see you are playing a part. Catch the arrow in mid-flight, and you are free.

### Gift — Freedom
Freedom begins with understanding, never with a method. It dawns in the whole of you that your own beliefs have held you. To leave a prison, you first have to know you are in one. Then you start to take the suffering back inside, yours first and then other people's. The drama outside runs out of fuel. You stop pushing your life where you wanted it to go, and you drift, watch and let it be. The search that was desperate becomes almost funny.

A dragonfly lives for years underwater before it climbs into the air. The water from its old life is pumped into its new wings to spread them. Your feelings are that water. When you feel them to the end, they are what lifts you.

### Siddhi — Freedom
At its height freedom keeps the same name and stops being yours. You stop feeling shut inside your own body, and what you feel moves freely between you and other people. The idea that a feeling belongs to someone starts to seem absurd. There is one wave of feeling, and some people make it while others receive it. In love, neither of you pulls while the other pushes away, and what you share runs over like a full cup. You no longer fall in love; you rise in it. People can see the peace in your eyes.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 55, Spirit
Gate 55 presses you to feel life at full depth and to be changed by what you feel. Every feeling that passes through it grows larger on the way. It wants your passion to be what supplies you, and it fears not knowing which passion to follow. At its best it makes things from faith that there is enough. Pressed by fear, it hoards, holds back or takes more than its share. Then it forces things by will until the will runs dry. From outside, all of this can look like plain moodiness.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The Emotional Solar Plexus Center sits low in the front of you and makes feeling. It is both a motor and a way of knowing, and it moves in a wave. For this gate the wave is passionate and moody, mostly even, with short bursts up or down. Its low end can be melancholy, and there is wisdom in the low if you can stay with it. Nobody can tell when the melancholy will come, and time alone is the right use of it.

A little over half of people have this centre defined. Their feelings run all the time, so the truth of a feeling shows over days. A choice made on the crest tends to go wrong. The rest have it open. They take in the feelings of the people around them and feel them louder, and when they are alone the feelings leave. At a funeral they may feel nothing and wonder what is wrong with them. Nothing is wrong with them at all. When they are alone they know a steady peace.

### What completes it — the Channel of Emoting (55–39)
Gate 39 sits at the base of the trunk and provokes, poking at whatever has settled. Joined to this gate it makes the Channel of Emoting, which brings feeling out into the open to stir feeling in the people nearby. It often ignores what anyone thinks is possible and wants it anyway. Gate 39 may be in you or in a person close to you, and the stirring happens only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The sun is the reason this energy is given to the solar plexus, which is named for it. Two knots of nerves, each the size of a fingertip, sit on the front of your spine behind your stomach. Fibres go out from them like rays to everything in your belly. They run your organs and vessels on their own, without a word from your brain. A strong feeling passes through them with more force than any thought you could set against it. A full day or an empty one is felt there first.

Lay a hand in the soft hollow just below the tip of your breastbone and take a sharp breath in. The hollow pushes out against your hand. The sun in the middle of you lies about a hand's depth behind it.

### Amino acid
Your body is getting ready for a change in its own cells as you read this, and so is every other body. One substance in that change is histidine. Your body cannot make it and has to take it in from milk, fish or lentils. In your blood, the iron that carries each breath is held in place by it. Every breath you take while reading is carried on it.

This energy has a chemistry in you that rises and lets go, the way the body does at the height of love. You share that chemistry with its kin, so what rises in you rises in them too.

## RELATIONS

_UL 56 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the fullness carried on the road, where this one has it at home._

### Pair — UL 56, Infinite Journey (The Wanderer)
This energy fills a life to its height, and it runs out when the fullness is kept in one place. Infinite Journey is where it goes next: a taste for life that takes the world in and hands it back as a story. Open it when the good time at home has peaked and you feel the pull to move. At home many people gather round you, and on the road there are few, so each one counts.

### Inverse — UL 56, Infinite Journey (The Wanderer)
Turn this energy upside down and the full house becomes a traveller's room. Infinite Journey is the same fullness with no home to keep it in. Open it when staying put has started to feel heavy.

### Programming partner — UL 59, Mystics Treasures
This energy frees you from making anyone else the cause of how you feel. Mystics Treasures holds what it lacks: the longing to be known, which gets through whatever two people keep between them. Freedom asks you to hide nothing, and closeness is where that starts. Open it when you feel free on your own and guarded with the people you love.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of the Whirlwind
This energy works a quiet change inside your own life. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of the Whirlwind, bound by one chemistry. UL 49, Union in the Ashes, turns the same change outward into the upheaval you see when a whole society falls apart. Here it turns inward, and your moods stop running you.

### Tarot
One of the tarot's major cards meets this energy twice, through the ring and the upper trigram.

- **Ring Arcana — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Tower is the lightning that breaks open a life shut tight around its plenty, so you can walk out free.
- **Thunder (upper) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): Here the Tower is the jolt that shakes you when a good time starts to harden into a fortress.

### Immortals
Thunder above carries Cao Guo Jiu, born to royal blood, who gave away his wealth and left the court. Fire below carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar whose sword cuts through illusion. You can do what each of them did: let go of what you own before it owns you, and cut through what is false. Then no amount of plenty can hold you.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Mars:** the planet of force and heat, the drive in you that will not let a full time sit still.
- **Hebrew letter — Peh, the Mouth:** the mouth that opens and says what was shut inside, so the doubt that screens a full house lifts.
