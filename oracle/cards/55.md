# UL 55 — Untouched Perfection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Full to the Brim · The Top of the Wave · Nothing Outside Made the Mood · Longing to Be High When Low · The Doors Kept Open · Lighter Than Air · Rising in Love

This is the energy of abundance, a life come to its height with everything in it full at once. In you it is a feeling so full that it begins to shape what comes to you, so that you make from having rather than from lack. It arrives as a wave, and a wave has a top. Standing in the brightest of it you can feel that it is already on its way down, and that feeling is not a fault in the fullness; it belongs to it.

Carried well, this energy never asks what outside you made the mood. Nothing did. The high was not handed to you by the money or the person, nor the low done to you by the weather, so there is nothing to chase and no one to blame. What happens to you is the smallest part of a life; how you hold it is the rest. Held that way the fullness stays open, and every feeling that runs through you is taken back inside as yours, from wherever you had pinned it.

The way is not to chase the freedom you are after, because it cannot be caught head on. It comes sideways, while you live an ordinary life with everything in it, and it comes on its own. There will be days when the wave has gone out, with the breath itself thin, and the pull then is to find the reason so it can be fixed. Let the low be yours as much as the high was, and take the feeling all the way in; what you have felt through is what you are no longer carrying. You grow lighter, which is why freedom has always been drawn with wings. Let yourself be as you are, and if that changes, let it.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Fire: lightning with its thunder, the flash that shows everything at once, the sound that shakes it. Above is movement, below is light, and where the two arrive in one moment a thing is at its height. The traditional character is a vessel with plants heaped and flourishing on top of it, full past the brim, which is the word for abundance.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the shock that moves. It rises out of the depth of the earth in spring, the first sound of the year. What was shut all winter breaks open at it: the seed splits, the egg cracks. In a family it is the eldest son, the one who goes first and makes the noise. In you it is the stir that will not let a full moment sit still, the sudden move, the burst that comes up from below before you have decided anything. Its risk is the shaking that goes on after the thing has moved, a fullness that cannot stop striking.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Under the thunder is fire, and here it is lightning's own light, the flash that shows a whole field at once. Fire is what lets one thing be told from another. It clings to what it burns, gathers people round it, and at its height it is the sun with nothing left to climb. In a family it is the middle daughter, who sees everything and is seen. In you it is the clear sight at the top of a good time, the brightness others gather to. Its risk is the curtain: a light this bright draws one round itself, and what was full goes dark from inside.

### Reading
Everything in your life has come to its height at once, and the height is real. The work is in, the people have gathered, the sun stands in the middle of the sky. What it asks first is that you do not grieve. A height cannot be held. The sun at noon has one direction left; the full moon is already thinning; the sky itself fills and empties with the seasons, so you will too. That is not the sorrow in this. The sorrow would be to spend the brightest hour of your life watching for the dark. Give the light while it is yours, as the sun does, on everyone below. Know when you have enough, because whoever does not stop there turns fullness into excess and falls with it.

Then keep the light open. Abundance does not fail by running out; it fails by being screened, a curtain drawn round it until the stars show at noon. Meet your equal without rivalry. Draw the able people in. When the sky darkens, say plainly what you mean, because the shade is made of doubt and lifts when the doubt does. The one who shuts the doors on a full house sits alone in it for years.

In the body this energy is the eye with the foot: the eye that sees a whole field lit in one flash, the foot already moving under it. Notice which is ahead in you now. Let the eye have the whole of the light before the foot moves, then move while it lasts.

### Judgement
- Abundance goes through. The one who reaches it has no cause to grieve.
- Be as the sun at noon.

### Moving lines

Meeting your match, someone of your own kind and strength: there is no fault in it, though the two of you are alike. For a while, ten days or ten years, you sharpen each other; going forward together brings honour. Equality has a season. Go on while it is fresh, because a match held past its time turns into calamity.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the screens so thick the stars show at noon. → becomes Hexagram 34, Great Power.
The curtains have grown so thick that the stars show at midday, and the one above you cannot see. Go to them now and you are met with suspicion, a sickness of doubt that nothing you do can argue away. The shade is made of doubt, so it is not real. Hold to what you truly mean until it moves them; say it plainly, and the sun comes back.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a total eclipse, and the right arm broken. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing Thunder.
Day has gone over into night, the small stars out at noon, and the arm you act with is broken. Nothing great can be done from here, and no blame attaches to you for it. A strength that could not yield went forward too fast and has fallen back as fast. Let the arm rest, do the small things, and wait for the light on its own clock.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the same stars at noon, and the hidden lord met. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
The screen again, the stars still out at noon, but the dark is thinning now. In it you meet the hidden one: plain to look at, of your own kind, carrying more than shows. This meeting is the turn. Be strong without using your strength, nurse the light rather than spending it, and go toward the one you found in the dark.

**Line 5** · _image:_ brilliance coming, and the able gathered round. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
Light is coming back, and with it the able people gather round you. What fills the room is not yours alone, yet it serves the whole. There is reward in it, there is praise; it goes well. The striving nobody saw has ended in an openness everybody sees. Empty yourself, and the fullness comes of its own.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the house made grand, the door shut, and no one seen for three years. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
The house is made grand and the family screened off inside it. Whoever peers through the door finds it still, with nobody there, and for three years no face appears. This is fullness hidden for fear the light would show what is in it, and it is the one ruin abundance has. Open the door while there is still someone outside it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Victimisation · _Gift:_ Freedom · _Siddhi:_ Freedom

### Shadow — Victimisation
Every mood you have seems to come from outside you, and you can name what did it. The low was the food or the partner; the high was the money, or the right person arriving; a whole life goes to chasing what makes the good feeling and blaming what makes the bad. Hunting the outside cause of a mood is the commonest habit on earth, so common it does not look like a belief. It looks like noticing.

What holds you is the longing. Low, you long to be high. High, you hold on so it will not go, and the holding is its own low. The hunger is for the search and never for the finding: if you ever reached the thing you were chasing you would not know what to do with it, because what you loved was the hope. Under the surface of a life lived this way you rise on every swell then fall, in love then out of it. The breath is thin at the bottom, the chest full to bursting at the top, and you call the swell freedom.

### Repressive nature — Complaining
This person complains, mostly inside. Every complaint puts them at the centre of a sad story and keeps the story alive; each one hands a little more power away. The view is dim and looks like honesty. It wears the body down, and it makes life exactly as hard as it says life is.

### Reactive nature — Blaming
This person fires outward. The arrow of blame lifts the weight of a situation off their hands onto someone else, so the drama goes on. Blame is anger with a target; anger let out whole has none. Nobody can blame their fate on someone while seeing they are an actor in a play.

### Gift — Freedom
Understanding is what changes this, not a method. It arrives in the whole of you: you have been the prisoner of your own beliefs, and to leave a prison you first have to know you are in one. From there the walls thin. You begin to take the suffering back inside, yours and then other people's, so the drama outside runs out of fuel. You stop pushing your life toward what you wanted from it. You drift, you watch, you let it be what it is. The search that was desperate becomes almost amusing.

A dragonfly spends years underwater as a hunter with no idea what it is becoming. One day something in it fires, it climbs a stem into air it has never tasted, and its skin splits. The water left over from its old life is pumped into the new wings and spreads them. What drowned it is what lets it fly. Your feelings are that water. Felt all the way through instead of fled, they are what carries you up, and each time you cry all the way through, something old leaves with the salt.

### Siddhi — Freedom
Freedom at its height has the same name as freedom on the way, and this is the only place that happens. It does not become something else at the top; it stops being yours. The awareness that was shut in one body slips the net and runs between people, so that a feeling belonging to someone becomes a strange idea. There is one wave; some of you make it while others receive it. Behind the longing for the perfect other was this all along: a net across the whole world with a jewel at every knot, each one holding all the rest. The cup one side pulled at while the other pushed away is full now and running over, because only one is drinking from it. You no longer fall in love. You rise in it, and there is a stillness in your eyes.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 55, Spirit
Gate 55 is the pressure in you to feel life at full depth and to be changed by what you feel. Watch it over a week: the tide comes in, then goes out. One day the mood lifts you into something near certainty, and a few days on the same life looks empty. That is not a fault to be fixed; the wave is the engine, and every feeling that passes through here is made larger by the passing. What it wants is to live from your passion as the thing that supplies you, and its fear is not knowing which passion to follow.

The work is faith, in the plain sense: to make from enough rather than from fear. Pressed by scarcity this pressure hoards or withholds, takes more than its share, or forces an outcome by will alone until the will runs dry. From outside it can look like someone who is simply moody. What is going on is a feeling too large for the moment it arrives in, and people who carry this all their lives learn to let the wave finish before they call any of it true.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
This pressure rises from the centre of feeling, low in the front of you, the engine that is also a way of knowing. For this pressure the wave runs a particular course: passionate and moody, even for long stretches with short bursts up or down, its low end melancholy. The melancholy is not to be argued with. There is more wisdom in the bottom of this wave than at the top, if you can stay there long enough to hear it, and nobody can say in advance when it will come. Time alone at the low is the honest use of it. A feeling here lasts a few minutes if it is felt, a lifetime if it is stored.

In a little over half of people this centre is switched on, and the tide is always running. For someone like that the feeling of the moment is never the whole truth; what they know they know over days, so the pull to settle a matter on the spot is exactly the thing that wrecks it. In the rest it is switched off, and the tide they feel belongs to whoever is in the room. For someone like that there may be nothing at all at a funeral, where feeling is expected, and they wonder what is wrong with them. Nothing is; what they have instead is a steady peace when alone that the others never get.

### What completes it — the Channel of Emoting (55–39)
A feeling that floods inward with nothing to press against goes stale and stays. Its other half is Gate 39, at the base of the trunk, the provoking: the part of you that pokes at whatever has settled and will not let it sit. Together they make the Channel of Emoting, where feeling is brought out into the open and stirs feeling in everyone near it, which is how one person's wave becomes a shared one. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the stirring only happens where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
In the body this energy is given to the solar plexus, and the reason is the sun. The solar plexus is named for it: two knots of nerve the size of fingertips on the front of your spine, behind the stomach. Fibres go out from them in every direction like rays, to everything in your belly. It runs the vessels and the organs on its own, with no word from the brain; the voltage of a feeling passing through it is greater than any thought you could set against it. In you it is the sun in the middle of you, and a full day or an empty one is felt there first, under the breastbone.

Check it on a day the mood has turned: lay a hand in the hollow under your breastbone and notice whether it is full or empty. That is the sun in you, at its noon or its setting.

### Amino acid
A change is coming in the cells of your body, and this energy sets it going. Your body is getting ready for it as you read, in its own chemistry, and so is every other body. The substance of it in you is histidine. You cannot make it; your body takes it in, from milk, fish or lentils, or goes without. In your blood the iron that carries the breath is held in place by this one piece, so every breath you took while reading was carried on it.

What runs through all of humanity as a change runs through you as one person: a rising and a letting go, a force that spirals up through you, as the body's own does at the height of love. The same substance runs in the ones who share this energy, so what rises in you is one chemistry with theirs.
