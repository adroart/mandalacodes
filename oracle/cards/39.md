# UL 39 — Nobel Spark

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Pressure · The Button Pushed · Feeling Cornered · The Way Shut · A Child's Energy · Unblocking · Nothing Left to Provoke

This energy is pressure, the charge that builds in you and has to go somewhere. It is the urge to act that is in you before any thought, and it presses on whatever has gone still, in you or near you, to find out whether it is alive. Life uses it on you all the time: something disturbs you, and the disturbance is life asking you to grow rather than wilt. Whoever winds you up is carrying it to you. It lives wherever a way has closed, at the block in the road, in the person you cannot get past, in the part of your own body that will not move.

Pressure with somewhere to go lets the provocation in rather than turning away from it. The thing that gets to you is pointing at a pattern, usually one you had not been honest with yourself about. The block you keep hitting is not a wall; it is where your own force gathered and stopped, with something worth having behind it. Every block is a breakthrough not yet made. The people who live this way become the unlockers, first of themselves and then of anyone near them, because they know from inside how a stuck thing moves. Pried open by will, it does not move; the pressure forced out that way tightens again, harder than before.

Let the pressure find its work. Let out by steady work it comes back as the energy you had as a child, which in an adult is called making things, and the more you make the more of it there is. The force that fights when it is cornered is the same force that makes when it is free. The only difference is whether it has somewhere to go.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Mountain: a stream running down off a peak onto rock that will not move, danger above and a full stop below, with no path between them. The old character shows a foot and cold, blood not reaching the feet, a leg that will not carry you: a limp.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water here is the danger on top, the gorge, the pit, the place where the ground gives way under you. It runs downhill because it must, and it hides its force inside a hollow, one firm line held between two soft ones. In a family it is the middle son, the one who takes the risks. In you it is what keeps moving when moving is unsafe, the part that faces the drop and goes anyway. Its risk is the fall: force with no ground under it goes into the pit it was rushing toward, and takes whatever it carried down with it.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain underneath is the halt. It is the ground that rose until it could rise no more and has stood there since, the foot that stops walking. In a family it is the youngest son, who keeps still and watches. In you it is the part that knows when to stop: not fear, and not failure, but the plain knowledge that the next step goes nowhere. Under danger that knowledge is the whole of your safety, because seeing the danger and being able to stop is what knowing is. Its risk is going hard, when stopping becomes refusing, and stillness no longer waits for anything.

### Reading
You set out and the way closes, so you try another, which closes too. This is the blocked road, and the trouble in it is not your fault; it is in the lay of the land, water above you, rock beneath, a leg that will not do what you ask of it. Forcing it is how you fall. What the situation asks is that you see the danger and stop, which is not weakness but knowing. Then go where the ground is soft, not up onto the hard rock. There is someone who has crossed this before; find them, and follow what they hold to be worth having. Stay true through it and it comes out well.

While you wait, the work is on yourself. Where the will cannot push outward it can turn round; the one who would grow uses the stopped time to look at their own conduct and mend it. Water does get through in the end. It goes down, finds the way through the depth of what stopped it, and does that by being water, not by striking the rock.

In the body this energy is the feet. Notice them now, cold or warm, set flat or ready to go. A leg the blood has not reached will not carry you, whatever you decide. Stand where you are, feet on the ground, and let the stop be the stop.

### Judgement
- The blocked way. The southwest serves; the northeast does not.
- It serves to see the great one. Keep straight, and it ends well.

Keep to yourself, work on what is yours, and wait for the time to come round.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Provocation · _Gift:_ Dynamism · _Siddhi:_ Liberation

### Shadow — Provocation
Someone knows the note. Your partner, or a child at the one pitch that goes through you, says the small thing in the exact tone, and something in you has gone off before a thought could reach it; the tone was aimed at the place already raw. That is provocation, and it lives in sound: it is almost never what was said. Under the heat is a colder thing, the fear of being trapped, of losing the room to act, and anything cornered will strike. You are trapped the moment you react. The one who pushes and the one who is pushed are playing one old game; most people play both parts, on different days.

Look at what happened. Someone made sounds with their throat, and a handful of tones sent you into a state you did not choose; the only reason it worked is that you took it personally. Held down instead, the same force stops the breath short, and you run your days on the top of the lungs, slowly worn down. Move house or leave the job and the trapped feeling comes with you, because it was never in the room.

### Repressive nature — Trapped
This person's fear never turned into anything. It stays under the anger, the anger stays under the surface, and the whole store of life in them sits frozen, asleep, locked in a few moods. From outside it passes for a quiet nature. The trap holds because they do not know they are in it.

### Reactive nature — Provocative
This person throws the fear outward. They push the sore spot, say the word meant to sting, and call it honesty; it is fear made loud, the first crack in a long sleep. Each jab lands back on them. Underneath they are asking to be loved, in the one way that cannot get it.

### Gift — Dynamism
Let loose, this energy is the one that runs a small child: the crackle in the air around them, never spent, back the moment it was used up. Most of it was traded away by seven for the tightness you now call your character, and none of that trade is final. What comes back, when it comes, is not play any more but the same force grown up, which is creativity, and the plainest way to call it back is to do what you love; the more you make, the more energy there is to make with.

It is catching. In the company of someone living this way you breathe all the way down again, your horizons widen, and they never seem to run out, as if driven from somewhere past themselves. They do not wait; they begin things, spark them, push. There are grades of this fire: the one who fights because he cannot help it, the one who directs his heat where it is needed, and the rare one nothing can provoke. The release, when it comes, is closer to laughing until the body shakes loose, after long steady work.

### Siddhi — Liberation
Nothing can provoke this. The word still enters the ear, the mind still turns it into meaning, and the awareness it lands in does not move; you hear without following the sound. To get here the one thing that seemed to fuel you, your own separate freedom, is given up. It feels like a physical death, every plan and piece of work surrendered into a single vanishing point, the tunnel of agony that turns, without fail, into ecstasy. What comes back is larger than freedom.

The ones it has happened to are dangerous to be near: they aim their love straight at the weakest place in your armour, using any means to test how far you have surrendered, and if anything still lands personally there is further to go. Under the water a dragonfly nymph, a perfect hunter, one day climbs toward the light for no reason its life could give it. Something inside provoked the leap.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 39, Provocation
Gate 39 is the pressure to provoke, and what it drives you to do is disturb what has gone still, in a person or in a room, to see whether it is alive. Provocation means something more exact than trouble-making: you are here to needle people toward spirit, toward the knowledge that what they need is not going to run out, and you do it by teasing, playfully or not, until something in them moves. Timing is everything in it. Done when the person is ready it is an infectious spark that wakes the whole room; done only to let off your own pressure it is a nuisance nobody enjoys. From outside it can look like restlessness, a person who cannot leave well enough alone. What is happening is a search: the pressure is hunting for the place where the energy has stopped, because pressing on a stuck place is the only way to find it. People who carry this all their lives learn to be hard to influence, to use every moment of felt scarcity as a place to push off from, and to say the thing nobody is saying only when it will land.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The pressure comes from the base of the body, in the Root centre, the square at the bottom of the figure, which is a motor and a pressure at once: the spark that drives you to act, pumping stress into the system so that something gets done. Its one law is timing. The motor pulses on and off, like the spark in an engine; when you cannot get yourself moving the time is simply wrong.

In about six people in ten it is switched on. For someone like that pressure is familiar ground: they can bear it, focus it, turn it into a steady stream of doing, and are not easily pushed by another person's urgency into starting what is not theirs. In the other four it is not switched on. They take in the pressure of everyone near them, make it louder, and live like a pot with the lid on, always something to be done. The usual answer is to do it, the kitchen or the laundry, which never works, because the next task is waiting and the pressure was never theirs: the other person only said what needed doing. Some go the other way, into overwhelm, and stop altogether. What they have is the wisdom about which pressures to answer, and the strange capacity, when the time is right, to get an enormous amount done at once.

### What completes it — the Channel of Emoting (39-55)
A provocation with nowhere to land is a jab. Its other half is Gate 55, in the feelings, which holds the wave of mood, deep water that cannot be hurried. The two make the Channel of Emoting: the pressure to provoke carried into feeling, so that what stirs a person is not a fight but a change of heart. It is a moody channel, half empty then half full, and the wave is to be watched, never flattened. Whether the other half is in you or in someone you love, the spark only becomes feeling where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
The adrenals are where this energy sits in your body, and the reason is the rush. Two small glands, one on top of each kidney, answer a threat before you decide anything: within a second the rush is out, the blood has left your skin for your muscles, your hands go cold, a little damp, and everything in you is ready to move. It is real, and it is over in a couple of minutes, because your body clears it that fast. That is the work of this energy too: a charge that arrives in you before thought, has to go somewhere, and cannot be kept. A body that keeps calling for it is under a pressure with no way out, and from inside you feel that as a tiredness no rest touches.

The last time something startled you, your hands went cold a moment after, because the blood had gone where it was needed; when the rush had passed a deep breath came by itself, without your asking. That breath, not the rush, is what lasts in you.

### Amino acid
The urge to act that is in you before any thought has a substance, and the substance is serine. It is rooted low in your body, where the rush comes from, and it is one chemistry shared with the kin of this energy, each of which presses on you in a different way; yours is the gutsy one, explosive, all about action. It makes an enormous pressure in you and in the room around you; that pressure is what makes things: it is what drives a person past what they were.

A block is where the pressure in you has gathered, which makes it a place to look rather than a wall. When you are cut, the blood that closes the wound is turned into a clot by something that cuts with a serine at its edge.
