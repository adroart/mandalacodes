# UL 39 — Nobel Spark

## CODE

_Keywords:_ A Child's Energy · Getting Things Moving · Waking People Up · Doing What You Love · Stopping to Look · Buttons Being Pushed · Nothing Left to Provoke

You still carry a small child's energy, and it always wants to move. It rises in your body before you have thought, ready to act. When something stands in your way, this energy gathers there, so a block shows you where to look. It starts things and keeps them moving.

Some days a person gets under your skin, and you react before you can stop. Forcing a block open only makes you tighter. Holding the energy down leaves your breath shallow. Let in what bothers you, and ask which habit of yours it is pointing at.

Put the energy into what you love, and the more you make, the more of it there is. Breathe all the way down into your belly, the way a child does. Near you, people breathe more deeply and feel able to do more. This is the spark in you that wakes everyone up.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Mountain is the hexagram of the road that closes in front of you. Water is the part of you that wants to keep moving, even toward danger. Mountain is the part of you that knows when to stop. The danger sits above the stopping because it lies ahead, and only the one who stops in time sees it. This hexagram has long been read as water on a mountain, with no way down. Water held still on a height settles and clears, and your mind does the same when you stop. The texts also say to find someone who has crossed this way before. When the road closes, you get clear by stopping.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the part of you that takes the risk and goes toward the drop. Here it sits on top, outside you, as the danger in the road ahead. Water has long been read as a stream running into a gorge, throwing everything it has at one point. That is how you move when you take a chance, with all of you at once. On its own, it takes you straight into the danger you were racing toward.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that stops. Here it sits underneath, inside you, as the knowledge that the next step leads nowhere. Mountain has long been read as a range of peaks, the limit past which you cannot see. Standing at its foot, you look before you go on. On its own, it makes you stubborn, and you refuse to move at all.

### Reading
You set out, the way is blocked, and the next way is blocked too. It is not your fault; the trouble is in the lay of the land. Pushing on only takes you deeper into it. What this time asks is that you see the danger and stop. Stopping here is knowing, and it is different from giving up. Go where the ground is easy, away from the hard country. Look for someone who has already come through this, and follow what they value. Hold to what is right through it, and it ends well.

While the road is closed, the work turns back on you. Look at your own conduct and mend what you find, instead of blaming anyone. Held back from outside, you grow from within.

The body here is the feet. Notice them now, cold or warm, ready to go or planted. Let them stand where they are until the way opens.

### Judgement
- The blocked way. The southwest brings benefit; the northeast does not.
- It helps to see the great person. Holding firm brings good fortune.

### Image
- Water on the mountain: the image of the blocked way.
- So the wise turn their attention to themselves and shape their character.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ going meets the block; coming back earns praise. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
The block is at the first step, before you are caught up in anything. Go on, and you walk into trouble. Turn back, and you are praised for seeing the danger early and stopping at once. That is good sense, and it has nothing to do with fear. Wait where you are, work on yourself, and let the time come round.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the king's servant, meeting block after block, not for his own sake. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
You serve something larger than yourself, and the troubles come one after another. They are not your fault, and they cannot be stepped around. This is the one place where stopping is not the counsel. Give it all you have for the sake of the whole, and there is no bitterness at the end.

**Line 3** · _image:_ going meets the block; coming back, and those at home rejoice. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
You are strong here, and strength wants to push on into the danger. The people who depend on you are behind you. They are glad when you come home. Stop, turn back, and look after them while the road is closed.

**Line 4** · _image:_ going meets the block; coming back brings company. → becomes Hexagram 31, Influence.
You are alone and not strong enough to force a way out. Forcing it would only make the trouble worse. Come back and ask for help without shame, from someone who has the means you lack. Stand together on firm ground, and then go on.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the great block, and friends come. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
This is the worst of it, and friends come. They come because you kept steady in the middle, and others took you as their measure. It may get harder before it gets easier. Hold steady and let them stand with you.

**Line 6** · _image:_ going meets the block; coming back brings great good fortune, and it helps to see the great person. → becomes Hexagram 53, Development.
There is nowhere further to go, so everything now is in coming back. Your aim is inside you. Seek out someone who knows the way out step by step, and follow what they value. Turning back now brings you more than going on ever could.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Provocation · _Gift:_ Dynamism · _Siddhi:_ Liberation

### Shadow — Provocation
Someone close says a small thing in a certain tone, and something in you goes off before you can think. It is almost never the words. The tone finds the exact place where you already feel guilty or sore. Under the anger sits an older fear, of being trapped and losing your freedom to act. Anything that feels cornered will strike. You are caught the moment you react. Most people push buttons on some days and have theirs pushed on others.

Held in, the same energy keeps your breath high and short. Leaving the job or moving house does not help, because the trapped feeling moves with you. What changes it is how you meet the next tone.

### Repressive nature — Trapped
This person's fear never shows. It stays frozen under an anger that never reaches the surface. Their life force sits locked in the same few moods, as if asleep. From outside they look calm. The trap holds because they do not know they are in it.

### Reactive nature — Provocative
This person lets the fear out as anger. They press the sore spot and say the thing meant to sting. Each jab comes back on them. Anger is how frozen fear starts to move, though there is no freedom in it yet. Underneath, they want to be loved.

### Gift — Dynamism
Dynamism is the energy you see in a small child, which never runs out and comes back as soon as it is spent. Most of it was set into habit by the age of seven, and none of that is final. When it comes back in a grown person, it comes as creativity. The quickest way to call it back is to do what you love. Your breath drops back into your belly, the way a child breathes.

Other people catch it. Near someone living this, people breathe more fully and feel able to do more. This energy does not wait. It starts things and keeps them going, and it will risk hurting someone's feelings to shake them awake. Its joy comes after long, steady work, and it shows in a laugh that shakes the whole body.

### Siddhi — Liberation
Liberation is the state where nothing can provoke you. A word enters your ear, your mind turns it into meaning, and your breath does not catch. You hear the sound without following it. To get here you give up the thing that seemed to drive you, your own separate freedom. It feels like a physical death, every plan and piece of work let go at once, and it passes through agony into joy.

People who live here are hard to be near. They aim their love at your weakest spot to test how far you have let go. If anything still lands as personal, there is further to go. A dragonfly larva hunts well under the water, then one day climbs up toward the light. The same push can rise in you, out of nowhere, and take you past a life that was working.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 39, Provocation
Gate 39 sits in the Root, low in your body, and it presses you to stir up what has gone still. It drives you to provoke people, sometimes by playful teasing and sometimes more fiercely, until something in them moves. What you push them toward is trust that life will give them what they need. Everything depends on timing. With the right person at the right moment, it is a spark that wakes up the whole room. If you do it only to ease your own pressure, nobody enjoys it. This gate is hard to talk out of anything. It treats every moment of feeling that there is not enough as a place to push off from.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center is the square at the bottom of the chart, and it is a motor and a pressure at once. It pumps stress into you so that something gets done. Its one law is timing. The motor pulses on and off. When you cannot get yourself moving, the time is not right.

About six people in ten have it defined. Pressure feels familiar to them, and they can focus it into steady doing. Other people's urgency does not easily push them into starting what is not theirs. The other four in ten have it open. They take in the pressure of everyone around them and feel it louder, like a pot with the lid on. The usual answer is to clean the kitchen or finish some other task. The next task is always waiting, and the pressure is not even theirs. Some people stop doing anything at all. Their gift is knowing which pressures to answer, and when the time is right they can get a huge amount done at once.

### What completes it — the Channel of Emoting (39-55)
Gate 55, in the Solar Plexus, holds the rise and fall of feeling. With Gate 39 it makes the Channel of Emoting, where pressure turns into mood, and the mood stirs other people. It swings between the glass half empty and the glass half full. Watch the swing and use it, instead of flattening it. Gate 55 may be in you or in someone close, and the pressure becomes feeling only when both are there.

## BODY

### Physiology
The rush is the reason this energy is given to the adrenals. Two small glands sit on top of your kidneys, and they fire before you have decided anything. Within a second the rush is through you. Your blood leaves your skin for your muscles, your hands go cold and a little damp, and you are ready to move. The rush is real, and it is gone in a couple of minutes, because your body clears it that fast. A body that keeps calling for it is under a pressure with nowhere to go. You feel that as a tiredness rest does not touch.

Think of the last time something startled you. Your hands went cold a moment later, because the blood had gone where it was needed. When the rush had passed, a deep breath came on its own. That breath is what lasts.

### Amino acid
The pressure in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is serine. You feel it low in your body, where the rush starts. It is gutsy and all about action, and it never waits for your head. You share this chemistry with five other energies, and each makes a different pressure in you.

Yours is enormous, in you and in the room around you, and it is what makes things. It drives a person past what they were. When you cut yourself, serine is part of what makes your blood clot and close the wound.

## RELATIONS

_UL 40 and UL 38 hold the two ends of this energy: the release that comes after a block, and the long effort that gives the push its purpose._

### Pair — UL 40, Eternal Wellspring (Deliverance)
This energy builds up behind a block, and it runs out once there is nothing left to push against. Eternal Wellspring is where it goes next: the release, when the knot comes loose and the effort can rest. Open it when the block has finally given way.

### Inverse — UL 40, Eternal Wellspring (Deliverance)
Turn this energy end for end and the block becomes the way out. Eternal Wellspring is the same road seen from the far side of the knot. Open it when you are tired of pressing and want to know what comes after.

### Opposite — UL 38, Inner Light Symphony (Opposition)
Change every line of this energy and the push becomes a stand. Inner Light Symphony holds its ground where this one stirs things up. Open it when stirring things has stopped helping and something needs to be held.

### Programming partner — UL 38, Inner Light Symphony
This energy stirs people up, and on its own it has no staying power. Inner Light Symphony holds what it lacks: the perseverance to see a hard thing through. Together they make the old story of the warrior, pushed into a fight and staying in it. Open it when you have started something and need the patience to keep it going.

### Codon ring — Ring of Seeking
This energy is the push that starts every search. It belongs to a family of six, the Ring of Seeking, and each member makes a different pressure in you. UL 52, Timeless Blossom, keeps the pressure in check. UL 53, Creation Oscillation, carries the urge to begin. UL 54, Everlasting Bounty, carries the drive to rise. UL 58, Rhythm of Life, carries joy. UL 15, Ordinary Valiance, is where the seeking comes to rest. This one wakes first.

One tells you the worst can be survived, and the other that there is no hurry to go forward. Between them they show you how to wait out a closed road.
