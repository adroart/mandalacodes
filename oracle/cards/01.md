# UL 1 — Earth's Breath

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Creative Power · Making Something New · In Your Own Way · The Dry Spell Between · Waiting for the Next Wave · Nothing Forced · Beauty in What You Make

This is the energy of creation itself, the force that begins everything and is always beginning again. In you it makes what was not there before, in a shape no one else could have made. When it is moving you are more alive than at any other time. The work has light in it, something in you has to come out, and bringing it out is a joy. It arrives in its own time and leaves the same way.

In balance, this power has no fear of mistakes. Nothing new has ever been made without hundreds of wrong turns, so you begin before you know how it will end. What you are making will move one way and then another as it comes into the world. Let it, because it has a life of its own. Your part is to be there when it happens, with a light hand, the way a midwife is there for a birth. By the time it is finished it has made you as much as you made it, and that is where the beauty is.

There are stretches when nothing comes, and they belong to this power as much as the making does. What comes next is being made in you where you cannot see it, and it needs you quiet, mostly on your own. Asking what is wrong with you is the one thing that makes the quiet last. Let it be, rest, and it comes back the way it left. It returns all at once, carrying what it made in the dark.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Heaven is the sky, and under it the sky again, with nothing that gives way. Its creature is the dragon, which sleeps the winter at the bottom of deep water. In spring it wakes, comes up into the open field and climbs into the sky. The traditional character shows vapour rising off wet ground under a strong sun, and the same word said another way means to parch.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the part of you that keeps going once a thing has begun. It sits on top because this is the side of you the world meets, in what you do each day without tiring. Heaven has long been read as the sky, and the sun comes back to it every morning. You come back to your work the same way. On its own it is sun with no rain, and nothing it shines on can grow.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is also the part of you where something new begins. It lies underneath because every beginning starts inside you before anyone can see it. Heaven has long been read as open sky, and the stars and the clouds come out of that emptiness. A drawing or a letter starts the same way, from a blank page. On its own it keeps beginning, and nothing under it takes the weight of what it has begun.

### Reading
Something is starting, and the whole of its life is still ahead of it. Everything that begins passes through four seasons. It springs up, it grows and it is gathered in, then it is tried for what it is worth. What you are asking about belongs to the whole cycle, not to this part, so look at all four from here. Nothing is yours until it has come through the last, and that takes longer than any beginning expects.

Keep going, but never by force. The sky moves through the day and the night without straining, which is why it never wears out. Make yourself strong the same way, a little every day rather than all of it in one blaze. Keep the pace of what is unfolding, slow when it is slow and fast when it is fast. Before you set out, be sure who you are and where you came from. A force this size carries you a long way in whichever direction you are facing. The one danger in it is going too far. A strength like this can burn what it has made before it has taken shape.

A beginning is felt first in the head, high behind the eyes, long before the hands get any of it. Hold yourself the way the sky moves, steady and unhurried, with nothing in it strained.

### Judgement
- Heaven begins things, and what it begins comes through whole: it springs, it grows, it is gathered, it is tried.
- It goes well for the one who holds to what is true.

### Image
- The movement of the sky is full of strength, and it does not let up.
- So the wise make themselves strong, day after day, and do not tire.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a dragon under the water, not yet to be used. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
With the strength still under the water, this is not its time. What is in you is real, and from the surface nothing shows while underneath a great deal is moving. Brought up now, before it is ripe, it would be spent on whatever met it first. Feed it where nobody is watching, so that when it does come up it has somewhere to go.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a dragon come up into the open field, and someone worth going to see. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
Out in the open field, you can be seen. You have ability, though the judgement to use it is another matter, not yet yours. Be careful who you stand with, because what is like you makes you stronger and what is not wears you thin. Find the one who has walked further than you, someone with the open mind of a child, and put yourself where they can see you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ working the whole day through, and still watchful when night falls. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
Past the beginning and short of the height, you are in the exposed place. You have the strength for the work and the whole weight of what it is for. Part of the danger is real, part of it is your own hurry to arrive, and neither is a fault. Work while there is light, then keep watch over what you have built when it goes, and nothing will go wrong.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a leap up out of the deep, or a while longer in it, and either is allowed. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
At a fork between the open and the deep, and for once nothing is wrong on either side. One road is seen, the other is yours alone, and both come out somewhere good. The hesitation is you checking, which is why no harm comes of this. Look at the weather before you move, because a step back here is how speed is gathered for the leap.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a dragon up in the open sky, and the great one there to be seen. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
Up in the open sky at last, this strength is the one that hid, kept watch and leaped. None of it was wasted. Like draws like, and the people gathering round you have something of the same in them. What you have reached is not for you alone, so spend it on whoever it can help. Even at the height there is such a thing as late.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a dragon climbed past the top of the sky, with nowhere further to go. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
One step past the top, you are still going, because this strength knows only one direction. It is late and the friends who climbed with you have gone home. The regret you feel is not the failure but the turn, the one thing that brings a proud strength back down. Learn to come down the way you learned to rise, while it is still yours to choose.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Entropy · _Gift:_ Freshness · _Siddhi:_ Beauty

### Shadow — Entropy
This energy goes flat some days, and the days go flat with it. You reach the end of one tired and cannot say what it was for. The word for it is numbness. Numbness is a feeling, though almost nobody knows they are in it, and knowing is most of the way out. You will do nearly anything rather than feel it, reach for the phone or call someone, and each works for about a minute.

Put a hand on your chest and notice what is there, even if it is nothing. Nothing is what it is meant to feel like at first, so let it stay, with no pressure on it to change. It is not comfortable, nor is it meant to be. It is a gap, and a life needs its gaps. A bored child is not asking for a list of things to do, only for the blank to be seen, and you are the same. A fire begins as kindling that looks like nothing, then a breath on it. The flat you feel is the kindling, and your noticing it is the breath.

### Repressive nature — Depressive
The low closes over this person. Fear enters it and freezes it there. From there it drains the surface of their days until the shine has gone from their eyes. Some days the bed is as far as they get. Only they can walk themselves out, by facing the fear that froze it.

### Reactive nature — Frenetic
This person cannot bear the flat for a minute. The moment it arrives they fill every hour and call everyone they know, anything rather than feel what is there. Everything in the body says go down and be still. They go the other way, and the body pays for it.

### Gift — Freshness
Met, this energy is a fire, and what you bring out is alive the way a flame is alive. One morning the colour is back in things, and the joy has come with no more warning than sadness ever gave. With it comes something that has to be said or made, in your voice or with your hands. You take the step nobody has taken. Even when it fails you are out of the place you were shut in, which was the point.

Where it does most is a small room: a family, a few people who work together. Give it its moment and the whole room wakes. Then leave sooner than seems polite, because fresh things do not keep. The one who lit the room can drain it by staying too long. The ones who carry this well are soft rather than hard, plain rather than shiny. A fire is delicate and burns whoever grabs at it. Your strength is being able to be alone, trusting your own timing over anyone's.

### Siddhi — Beauty
Everything you look at is beautiful. A face or a grey week in winter shines with being exactly what it is, and the light is the same in all of it. It is so delicate that one word cracks it. A student once sat a whole dawn on a mountain beside his teacher. When the sun rose he said one word, beautiful. The teacher afterwards called him a noisy fellow. The moment you say a thing is beautiful you have stepped outside it, so you say less and see more.

It is the fire when there is no longer anyone it is happening to. Even the flat days are beautiful from here, because they were part of it. All you can do is live it where people can see. Some will resent you for it, and some will come to stand in the light.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 1, Self-Expression
The pressure in you to make things your own way comes from Gate 1. It is the drive to show what is inside you, in your shape and no one else's. Nobody taught you the style. It is the one you had as a child, before anyone told you how things are done. When there is nowhere for it to go you are miserable, without always knowing why. When it does get out, the people near you feel more like themselves for having seen it.

From outside, doing it your way every time can look like stubbornness. What is going on is simpler. The making and the self are one movement, so asking you to do it the usual way is asking you to be someone else. The cost is that you are misunderstood more than most, and a hard word about the work lands like a hard word about you. In time you learn that being different was the strength all along. Trying to be original on purpose is the one thing that chokes it.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The centre of the chest is where this pressure rises, the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life faces. It has no engine of its own, so it works by drawing rather than driving. What belongs in your life, the people as much as the work, is pulled toward it. What goes out through Gate 1 is that self showing its face. When you trust it, it sets the direction. When you do not, you end up fighting what it was already bringing you.

About six people in ten have this centre defined, and their sense of self stays put whoever they are with. What they make carries one signature all their life. What starves them is being made to work in another person's manner. They tend to think everyone should be going their way. If they give up their own way to follow someone else's, something in the chest goes out. In the other four it is open, so the sense of self is borrowed from the people close by, then let go when they leave. For them this pressure is one of the few places they feel solid. In the act of making they are somebody, for as long as the making lasts. Even where they sit in a room can change what they make.

### What completes it — the Channel of Inspiration (1–8)
What rises from the chest has no voice of its own. Gate 8 in the throat gives it one: the drive to bring something to others and have it count. The two make the Channel of Inspiration, through which you can put a hard thing so plainly that a stranger takes it in. This channel waits, speaking best when someone has asked to hear it, worst when it has forced its way into a room. Whether Gate 8 sits in you or in a person close to you, what you made stays yours until it is asked for.


## BODY

### Physiology
Spring is the reason this energy is given to the liver. The liver is the organ of spring. It is the first to wake in the year, it starts the growing, it keeps the life in you moving through the whole of you. When the liver is at ease, everything in you moves. When it is tight, everything waits. It is also the part of you that plans ahead, the general of the body, choosing which way the season will go. That is the work of the creative force too. It begins the season and sets the direction. The liver is where the body does the same.

Of everything inside you, the liver alone can make itself again. Lose most of it to injury or surgery and within months it has grown back to full size, working the whole time. The energy that begins things was given to the one organ that can begin itself again. It sits under your ribs on the right side, about the size of a small loaf. Rest your hand there and you are resting it on the part of you that starts over.

### Amino acid
The spark you were born with has a substance in you, and the substance is lysine. You cannot make it. Your body takes it in, from meat or beans, or goes without. It is the same with the fire in you. On a morning when yours has dropped to a flicker, nobody notices you, and nothing you do by force brings it back. What brings it back in you is being fed, from outside and in its own time.

While it burns, everyone wants to be near you, and what you have then is a fire with something to feed. That is the same fire a season on, gathering a harvest round it, and in your body the two are made of one thing. Push it up by will and you pay, in what you give up to keep it lit. Fed, it comes back to you on its own.


## RELATIONS

_What starts in you goes somewhere, and these are the ways it goes._

### Pair — UL 2, Beyond the Shell (The Receptive)
You have begun something and it will not land. Every day it moves and arrives nowhere, and more force does not help. Beyond the Shell is where it goes next: the ground that carries a thing to its end once it is handed over. Nothing drives there, and what it is given goes the whole way through. Open it when the starting is done and the rest has to be carried. A beginning cannot carry itself.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Some days you look for the other side of a drive like this, the softer face it must be keeping back. There is no other side. What looks back is the same drive, whichever way you hold it. That matters when you are tired of it. Nothing is waiting underneath to take over. What you need that is not more of this sits beside it, never behind it.

### Programming partner — UL 2, Beyond the Shell
The quiet has turned into worry about the quiet. What you are missing sits directly opposite you. The Gene Keys put Beyond the Shell there, the sense of which way a life faces. Its low is the feeling of being lost. The two feed each other, since nothing moves and nothing seems to hold. At the height the two are one thing, being home and finding it beautiful. Open it when worry has taken the place of waiting. It knows the way when you cannot feel one.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
The making has gone well for once, and now there is more than you know what to do with. Ancestors Bloom holds that moment: a loaded cart, and plenty that keeps only while it is going somewhere. You belong with it, and what the family holds is one thing. What you were given cannot be kept, only spent. Here it is spent on the making, there on the handing out. Open it when something is in your hands and you want to know what it was for.

- **Ring Arcana — XIV · The Art (Temperance)** (Flow, Alignment): A figure pours between two cups and spills nothing. One foot is in water and one on land. It comes by way of the family, a picture beside the road rather than a road. Meet it when what is in you needs mixing before it can be poured.

### Immortals
Han Xiangzi is the one with the flute, from the old stories. He left a scholar's house for the mountains, and when he played, flowers opened before their season. He is set beside this strength for the sky in it, not for anything in you. Keep him as company, breath with a shape put on it.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the archer's sign, the arrow already off the string toward a mark not yet seen. It belongs to the Art, not to you. Its one line: strength is what has been let go.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Peg:** the peg driven in so a tent can stand in the wind. It stands two steps from you, behind the Art. What it says: whatever never stops needs one point that does not move.
