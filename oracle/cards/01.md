# UL 1 — Earth's Breath

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Creative Power · Making Something New · In Your Own Way · The Dry Spell Between · Waiting for the Next Wave · Nothing Forced · Beauty in What You Make

This is the energy of creation itself, the force that begins everything and is always beginning again. In you it makes what was not there before, in a shape no one else could have made. When it is moving you are more alive than at any other time: the work has light in it, something in you has to come out, and bringing it out is a joy. It arrives in its own time and leaves the same way.

In balance, this power has no fear of mistakes. Nothing new has ever been made without hundreds of wrong turns, so you begin before you know how it will end. What you are making will move one way and then another as it comes into the world; let it, because it has a life of its own. Your part is to be there when it happens, with a light hand, the way a midwife is there for a birth. By the time it is finished it has made you as much as you made it, and that is where the beauty is.

There are stretches when nothing comes, and they belong to this power as much as the making does. What comes next is being made in you where you cannot see it, and it needs you quiet, mostly on your own. Asking what is wrong with you is the one thing that makes the quiet last. Let it be, rest, and it comes back the way it left, all at once, carrying what it made in the dark.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Heaven: the sky, and under it the sky again, with nothing that gives way. Its creature is the dragon, which sleeps the winter at the bottom of deep water, wakes in spring, comes up into the open field and climbs into the sky. The traditional character shows vapour rising off wet ground under a strong sun, and the same word said another way means to parch.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the sky, and what the sky does is go on. The sun rises before anyone has decided they are ready, crosses, sets, and the next morning it is back. In all the years there have been it has not missed once. In a family it is the father, up and dressed before the house, expecting the same again tomorrow. In you it is drive: you begin things and you finish them. You are still moving when everyone else has sat down, and nobody has to tell you twice. Its risk is drought, because a strength that never rests dries out the ground it meant to warm.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Underneath is the sky again. In nearly every other pairing something lies below to take the weight, ground or water. Here the drive comes up from beneath as hard as it comes down from above, and nothing between them says when it has gone far enough. In the world it is the second wind, arriving before the first is spent. In a person it is a drive with no brake: you do not run out, and going on is the only speed you have. Its risk is the same drought, doubled, since nothing under this strength can tell it whether it is warming a life or scorching one.

### Reading
You are at the start of something, and the whole of its life is still ahead of it. Everything that begins passes through four seasons: it springs up, it grows, it is gathered in, then it is tried to see what it was worth. What you are asking about belongs to the whole cycle, not to this part, so look at all four from here. Nothing is yours until it has come through the last, and that takes longer than any beginning expects.

What it asks of you is to keep going, never to force. The sky moves through the day and the night without straining, which is why it never wears out. Make yourself strong the same way, a little every day rather than all of it in one blaze. Keep the pace of what is unfolding, slow when it is slow and fast when it is fast. Before you set out, be sure who you are and where you came from, because a force this size carries you a long way in whichever direction you are facing. The one danger in it is going too far. A strength like this can burn what it has made before it has taken shape.

In the body this energy is the head. Notice where you are holding yourself now: high, behind the eyes, already looking at what comes next. A beginning is felt there before the hands get any of it. Hold yourself the way the sky moves, steady and unhurried, with nothing in it strained.

### Judgement
- Heaven begins things, and what it begins comes through whole: it springs, it grows, it is gathered, it is tried.
- It goes well for the one who holds to what is true.

### Image
- The movement of the sky is full of strength, and it does not let up.
- So the wise make themselves strong, day after day, and do not tire.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a dragon under the water, not yet to be used. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
With the strength still under the water, this is not its time. What is in you is real, and from the surface nothing shows while underneath a great deal is moving. Brought up now, before it is ripe, it would be spent on whatever met it first. Feed it where nobody is watching, so that when it does come up it has somewhere to go.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a dragon come up into the open field, and someone worth going to see. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
Out in the open field, you can be seen. You have ability, though the judgement to use it is another matter, not yet yours. Be careful who you stand with, because what is like you makes you stronger and what is not wears you thin. Find the one who has walked further than you, someone with the open mind of a child, and put yourself where they can see you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ working the whole day through, and still watchful when night falls. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
Past the beginning, short of the height, you are in the exposed place, with the strength for the work and the whole weight of what it is for. Part of the danger is real, part of it is your own hurry to arrive, and neither is a fault. Work while there is light, then keep watch over what you have built when it goes, and nothing will go wrong.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a leap up out of the deep, or a while longer in it, and either is allowed. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
At a fork between the open and the deep, and for once nothing is wrong on either side. One road is seen, the other is yours alone, and both come out somewhere good. The hesitation is you checking, which is why no harm comes of this. Look at the weather before you move, because a step back here is how speed is gathered for the leap.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a dragon up in the open sky, and the great one there to be seen. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
Up in the open sky at last, this strength is the one that hid, kept watch and leaped, so none of it was wasted. Like draws like, and the people gathering round you have something of the same in them. What you have reached is not for you alone, so spend it on whoever it can help, because even at the height there is such a thing as late.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a dragon climbed past the top of the sky, with nowhere further to go. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
One step past the top, you are still going, because this strength knows only one direction. It is late and the friends who climbed with you have gone home. The regret you feel is not the failure but the turn, the one thing that brings a proud strength back down. Learn to come down the way you learned to rise, while it is still yours to choose.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Entropy · _Gift:_ Freshness · _Siddhi:_ Beauty

### Shadow — Entropy
At its lowest, this energy goes flat, and the days go flat with it. You reach the end of one tired and cannot say what it was for. The word for it is numbness. Numbness is a feeling, though almost nobody knows they are in it, and knowing is most of the way out. You will do nearly anything rather than feel it, reach for the phone or call someone, and each works for about a minute.

Put a hand on your chest and notice what is there, even if it is nothing. Nothing is what it is meant to feel like at the start, so let it stay, with no pressure on it to change. It is not comfortable, nor is it meant to be. It is a gap, and a life needs its gaps. A bored child is not asking for a list of things to do, only for the blank to be seen, and you are the same. A fire begins as kindling that looks like nothing, then a breath on it. The flat you feel is the kindling, and your noticing it is the breath.

### Repressive nature — Depressive
The low closes over this person. Fear enters it and freezes it there. From there it drains the surface of their days, until the shine has gone from their eyes and the bed is as far as some days get. Only they can walk themselves out, by facing the fear that froze it.

### Reactive nature — Frenetic
This person cannot bear the flat for a minute. The moment it arrives they fill every hour and call everyone they know, anything rather than feel what is there. Everything in the body says go down and be still. They go the other way, and the body pays for it.

### Gift — Freshness
At its best this energy is a fire, and what comes through you is alive the way a flame is alive. One morning the colour is back in things, and the joy has come with no more warning than sadness ever gave. With it comes something that has to be said or made, in your voice or with your hands. You take the step nobody has taken, and even when it fails you are out of the place you were shut in, which was the point.

Where it does most is a small room: a family, a few people who work together. Give it its moment and the whole room wakes. Then leave sooner than seems polite, because fresh things do not keep, and the one who lit the room can drain it by staying too long. The ones who carry this well are soft rather than hard, plain rather than shiny, because a fire is delicate and burns whoever grabs at it. Your strength is being able to be alone, trusting your own timing over anyone's.

### Siddhi — Beauty
At its height, everything you look at is beautiful. A face, or a grey week in winter, shines with being exactly what it is, and the light is the same in all of it. It is so delicate that one word cracks it. A student once sat a whole dawn on a mountain beside his teacher, and when the sun rose he said one word, beautiful; the teacher afterwards called him a noisy fellow. The moment you say a thing is beautiful you have stepped outside it, so you say less and see more.

This is the fire when there is no longer anyone it is happening to. Even the flat days are beautiful from here, because they were part of it. All you can do is live it where people can see. Some will resent you for it, and some will come to stand in the light.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 1, Self-Expression
In the body, this energy is the pressure to make things your own way. Gate 1 is the door it comes through: the drive to put what is inside you out where it can be seen, in your shape and nobody else's. Nobody taught you the style. It is the one you had as a child, before anyone told you how things are done. When there is nowhere for it to go you are miserable, without always knowing why. When it does get out, the people near you feel more like themselves for having seen it.

From outside, doing it your way every time can look like stubbornness. What is going on is simpler: the making and the self are one movement, so asking you to do it the usual way is asking you to be someone else. The cost is that you are misunderstood more than most, and a hard word about the work lands like a hard word about you. People who carry this all their lives learn that being different was the strength all along, and that trying to be original on purpose is the one thing that chokes it.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The centre of the chest is where this pressure rises, the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life faces. It has no engine of its own, so it works by drawing rather than driving. What belongs in your life, the people as much as the work, is pulled toward it. What goes out through Gate 1 is that self showing its face. When you trust it, it sets the direction. When you do not, you end up fighting what it was already bringing you.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on, and the sense of self stays put whoever they are with. For someone like that, what they make carries one signature all their life, and the thing that starves them is being made to work in another person's manner. They tend to think everyone should be going their way, and if they give up their own way to follow someone else's, something in the chest goes out. In the other four it is not, so the sense of self is borrowed from whoever is nearest, then let go when they leave. For someone like that, this pressure is one of the few places they feel solid. In the act of making they are somebody, for as long as the making lasts, and even where they sit in a room can change what they make.

### What completes it — the Channel of Inspiration (1–8)
What rises from the chest has no voice of its own. Its other half is Gate 8, in the throat, the drive to bring something to others and have it count. The two make the Channel of Inspiration, through which you can put a hard thing so plainly that a stranger takes it in. This channel waits, speaking best when someone has asked to hear it, worst when it has forced its way into a room. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, what you made stays yours until it is asked for.


## BODY

### Physiology
In the body this energy is given to the liver, and the reason is spring. The liver is the organ of spring: it is the first to wake in the year, it starts the growing, it keeps the life in you moving through the whole of you. When the liver is at ease, everything in you moves. When it is tight, everything waits. It is also the part of you that plans ahead, the general of the body, choosing which way the season will go. That is the work of the creative force too. It begins the season and sets the direction. The liver is where the body does the same.

Of everything inside you, the liver alone can make itself again. Lose most of it to injury or surgery and within months it has grown back to full size, working the whole time. The energy that begins things was given to the one organ that can begin itself again. It sits under your ribs on the right side, about the size of a small loaf. Rest your hand there and you are resting it on the part of you that starts over.

### Amino acid
The building block this energy is given is lysine, and the reason is that it cannot be made. Your body builds most of its building blocks itself. Lysine is one of the few it cannot, so it has to arrive from outside, in food, and no effort of the body can force it. Creation is the same: it cannot be manufactured, only received. Once lysine is in you, it is what lets you burn. With one other building block it makes the carrier that takes stored fat to the place in the cell where it becomes heat and movement, so what you laid down in a full season becomes fire in a lean one.

It also holds you together. The strongest rope in your body is collagen, running through your skin and the cords that join muscle to bone. Its strands are tied to one another at lysine. Press the thick cord above your heel and try to bend it: it hardly gives. That is lysine holding, with every step you take. Meat is full of it, so are beans.


## RELATIONS

_What begins here needs what it cannot give itself: ground to land on, something to feed, and a hand on the measure._

### Pair — UL 2, Beyond the Shell (The Receptive)
Beyond the Shell is the ground this strength comes down onto, the same first act seen from underneath. Here everything drives forward and nothing receives. There nothing drives, and whatever is handed over is carried the whole way to its end. One is the force, the other the direction, and between them it is the ground that decides where the force goes. Open it when you have begun something and it will not go anywhere, because what a beginning needs next is not more force. It needs somewhere to land.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Turn the six lines over and they come back the same, one of only eight that do. A strength that is all one kind has no other face to show, no underneath, nothing waiting behind the first look. What looks back at you is the same rising you started with, so there is nothing else to open here.

### Programming partner — UL 2, Beyond the Shell
Beyond the Shell stands opposite you a second time, and this time the lows are paired with the lows, the heights with the heights. At the bottom the two make each other worse: the flat days here want to be left alone, while the lost feeling there cannot leave anything alone and starts asking what is wrong. At the top they are one thing, the beauty of a single thing and the whole it belongs to. Between them one is the thrust and the other the direction, so neither gets anywhere alone. Open it when the low has turned into worry about the low, because it knows the way home when you cannot feel one.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
This is a family of two, the smallest kind there is. The other is Ancestors Bloom, the same fire a season on, with a harvest standing round it. Here it is the first catch, with nothing to burn yet and needing nothing. There the heat has something to feed, something to give away, and what it throws off keeps only while it is going somewhere. What both know is what a fire costs: while it burns everyone gathers, when it drops to a flicker you are nobody, and forcing it back up is how you end up making bargains with yourself. Open it when the beginning has caught and you want to see what the burning was for.

### Tarot
Up in the sky the dragon climbs into, one face waits for it.

- **Ring Arcana — XIV · The Art (Temperance)** (Flow, Alignment): Through its family this strength carries The Art: a figure pouring between two cups without spilling a drop, one foot in the water and one on the land. A fire this hot is held to its measure here, mixed with what it needs before it is any use. Meet this face when the strength has to be blended with something before it can be poured out.

### Immortals
The sky is doubled, so a single figure from the old stories stands for both halves. Han Xiangzi is the one with the flute. In the stories he left a scholar's house for the mountains, having no use for what could be learned at a desk, and when he played, flowers opened out of season. That is this strength in a small instrument: breath with a shape put on it, and things growing before they were due.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the archer, a fire sign, with the arrow already off the string toward a mark the archer has not seen yet. Strength here is what has been let go, not what is being held.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Peg:** the peg driven into the ground so that a whole tent can stand up and take the wind, the prop under a rising fire. Even a strength that never stops needs one point that does not move.
