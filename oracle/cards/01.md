# UL 1 — Earth's Breath

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Creative Power · Making Something New · In Your Own Way · The Dry Spell Between · Waiting for the Next Wave · Nothing Forced · Beauty in What You Make

This is the energy that makes things. It is the push in you to make something that was not there before, and to make it your own way, and it keeps no schedule. It comes when it comes. While it is in, you are all the way in: the days have colour, the work gets you out of bed, and stopping is not something you could do if you tried. When it goes out there is nothing, and nothing you do brings it back a day sooner. Then one morning it is there again, and what it carries in is the start of something new.

Carried well it is fresh, and fresh means you are not afraid of getting it wrong. Everything new is found by trying the wrong thing first, many times over, until the right one shows itself, so you begin before you know how it ends. The idea moves one way and then another as it comes into the world, and you let it, because an idea held too tightly goes out like a small fire under a closed hand. What comes out could only have come from you. And it works on you while you work on it. By the end you have been changed as much as the material has, and that change is the beautiful part, more than the thing on the table.

The stretch between waves is the part most people get wrong. It looks like nothing is happening, and something is: whatever comes next is being made where you cannot see it, and it needs you quiet and mostly alone. Do not go looking for the reason you feel flat, and do not try to fix it. Asking what is wrong with you is what turns a flat week into a flat year. Rest, keep your own company, and let it come back in its own time. It returns the way it left, all at once.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Heaven: the sky, and under it the sky again, with nothing anywhere in it that gives. This is strength with nothing in it that yields, moving the way the sky moves, with no pause anywhere in the day or the night. Its creature is the dragon, which sleeps the winter at the bottom of deep water, wakes in spring, and climbs, up through the open field and on into the sky, and keeps climbing. The old sign for it is vapour lifting off wet ground under a strong sun, and the same word, said another way, means to parch.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the sky, and the one thing the sky never does is stop. The sun comes up whether the house is awake or not, crosses, goes down, and is back the next morning, and it has never once missed. That is the whole of this force: it goes on. In the world it is the father, the one already dressed when the others come down, who expects the same again tomorrow. In a person it is drive. You begin things and you finish them, you are still going when everyone else has sat down, and nobody has to say it twice. Its risk is drought. A strength that never rests dries out the ground it meant to warm.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Underneath, the sky again. In nearly every other pairing there is something below to take the weight, ground or water or a soft place; here the push comes from beneath as hard as from above, and nothing between the two says when it has gone far enough. In the world this is the second wind that arrives before the first has been used up. In a person it is a drive with no brake on it: you do not run out, and going on is the only speed you have. Its risk is the same drought, doubled. There is nothing under this strength to answer it back, and from inside it cannot tell whether it is lifting a life or scorching one.

### Reading
You are at the start of something, and the whole of its life is still in front of it. Everything that starts passes through four seasons: it springs up, it grows, it is gathered in, and then it is tested to see what it was worth. You stand at the first, and the counsel is to look at all four from here, because the thing will not be yours until it has been through the last, and that takes longer than a beginning ever thinks it will.

What it asks of you is to keep going and not to push. The sky is the picture: it moves all day and all night and it never strains, and that is why it never wears out. Make yourself strong the same way, by using your strength a little every day rather than spending it all in one blaze. Before you set out, take a breath and be sure of who you are and where you came from, because a force this size will carry you a long way in whatever direction you happen to be facing. And know that the one danger in it is going too far: it can burn what it has made before the thing has taken shape, and the person who cannot stop ends up sorry.

This energy has a place in the body, and it is the head. Notice where you are holding yourself right now: up, at the top, in the eyes and the forehead, already looking at what comes next. The beginning of a thing is felt there before the hands get any of it. So stand the way the sky moves: steady, unhurried, and never once straining.

### Judgement

- It goes well for the one who holds to what is true.

### Image
- The movement of the sky has strength in it, and it does not let up.
- So the wise make themselves strong, day after day, and do not tire.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a dragon under the water, not to be used yet. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
There is something with real strength in it lying low in you, and it is not time. From the surface the water looks still; underneath, a great deal is moving. If you bring it up now, before it is ripe, it will be spent on the first thing it meets and there will be none left for the thing it was for. So do not act on the first push. Feed what is down there where nobody is watching, and let it grow in the dark for as long as it needs. When it does come up, it will have somewhere to go.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a dragon come up into the open field, and someone worth going to see. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
What was under the water is out in the open now, in a field where anyone can see it. The place has changed from deep water to open ground, and the strength in you is the same in both. You have ability and people have noticed it, and you do not yet have the judgement to use it well; those are two different things, and only one of them is yours so far. This is the time to find the one person who has walked further than you and put yourself where they can see you. Be careful who else you stand with, because what is like you makes you stronger and what is not wears you thin. Then show yourself.

**Line 3** · _image:_ working the whole day through, and still awake to the danger at nightfall. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
You are past the beginning and short of the height, and this is the exposed place: not down in the field where nobody expected much, not up in the sky where the thing is done. You have the strength for the work and the whole weight of what it is for, so you work all day, and when the light goes you are still awake, going over what you did and what you missed. Part of the danger is real, and part of it is your own hurry to arrive. Neither is a fault. Work while there is light. When it goes, keep watch over what you have built, go carefully, and nothing will go wrong.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a leap up out of the deep, or a while longer in it, and either is allowed. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You have come to a fork, and for once nothing is wrong on either side of it. You can rise now and live in the open, where the work is seen, or go back down for a while and live in the deep, where the work is yours alone, and both roads come out somewhere good. The hesitating you feel is not weakness. It is you checking, and the checking is exactly why no harm comes of this. So look at the weather before you move. Going back down is not a defeat here. Sometimes you step back to get the run-up for the leap.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a dragon up in the open sky, and the great one there to be seen. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
The strength is at its full height and out where everyone can see it. What is flying now is the same thing that hid, then showed itself, then kept watch, then leaped; none of that was wasted, and this is what it was for. At this height like draws like: clouds follow a dragon the way wind follows a tiger, and the people who gather round you now are the ones with something of the same in them. Go and find them. What you have reached is not for you alone, and the way to keep it is to spend it, on whoever it can help, whenever the chance comes. Do not leave that for later. Even at the height there is such a thing as late.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a dragon climbed past the top of the sky, with nowhere further to go. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
You have gone one step past the top, and the strength that carried you here knows only one direction, which is on. So you are still going, though it is late and the friends who came up with you have gone home. Up here you are high with nobody beside you and nobody under you, and a strength like that cannot give life to anything any more; it can only burn. The regret you feel is not the failure. It is the turn, the one thing that ever brought a proud strength back down. A bow is not kept drawn all day. Learn to come down the way you learned to rise, and come down while it is still yours to choose.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Entropy · _Gift:_ Freshness · _Siddhi:_ Beauty

### Shadow — Entropy
At its lowest, this energy goes flat, and the days go flat with it. You get to the end of one tired and cannot say what it was for. The work has no light in it and neither do the people, and the street you live on and the things you watch all seem to take a little more out of you than they give. This is numbness. It is a feeling, though almost nobody knows they are in it, and knowing is most of the way out. You will do nearly anything rather than feel it: reach for the phone, the fridge, someone to ring, the next thing to do, and each one works for about a minute. And if you set out to find the reason for the flat, or to fix it, it sets hard, and a low that would have passed in a week is with you for a year. The more you are someone who makes things, the deeper these days go.

The way through starts with a hand on your chest. Notice how little is there, and let that be allowed. Nothing has left you. The low is not empty; it is a gathering, and something you cannot see yet is being made in the dark, the way a fire is laid before anyone strikes a match. It came on by itself, and if you leave it alone it goes the same way, and until then it wants you quiet and mostly on your own. What you are waiting for begins here, under the flat, whether or not you can feel it stirring.

### Repressive nature — Depressive
Here is the person the flat closes over. Fear gets into it, and the fear freezes the low in place, and the low goes on drawing energy away from the surface of their life until the shine is gone from their eyes and some days the bed is as far as they get. It can last a season or it can last years. Nobody else can walk them out. Only they can, by turning round and facing the fear that froze it.

### Reactive nature — Frenetic
Here is the person who cannot bear the flat for one minute. The moment it arrives they fill every hour and call everyone they know, and the schemes get wilder or the days get more mechanical, anything to get away from what is going on inside. Everything in the body is saying go down and be still, and they go the other way, and the body pays for it in the end.

### Gift — Freshness
Met and let in, this energy is the light coming back. One morning the colour is in things again, the sadness has switched to joy with no more warning than it came, and with the joy comes something you have to say or make, in your voice or with your hands. What comes out is new: not new the way a thing from a shop is new, new the way a fire is new, alive and burning. You are not afraid of getting it wrong. There is no path and you make one by walking, and most of the paths are dead ends, and the dead ends are how the real one is found. You can promise a thing before you know what is in it, and by the time it is due it has arrived. When it is finished you let it go, because you were never more than the one who was there when it happened.

Where this is at its best is a small room: a family, a few people who work together, a table of friends. Give it the floor at the right moment and the whole room wakes up, and you never wanted to be followed, and people follow anyway. Then leave, sooner than seems polite. Fresh things do not keep, and the same you that lit the room can drain it if you stay past the release. All of this is made from the flat days. What was laid in the dark while nothing showed is what catches now, and you do not get the one without the other. You cannot make it come, and when it goes you cannot call it back; what you can do is be alone well, and trust your own timing over anyone's.

### Siddhi — Beauty
At its height, everything you look at is beautiful. A face, a field at first light, a grey week in the middle of winter, each one shines with being exactly what it is, and it is all one light. This is what happens when you have given yourself to what you make so completely that it has remade you, and there is nothing left of you standing between you and what you see. It is delicate. There is an old story of a student who sat a whole dawn on a mountain beside his teacher, and at the sun's rising said one word, beautiful, and was spoken of afterwards as rather a noisy fellow. One word is enough to crack it. So you say less, and see more.

This is what the fire becomes when there is no longer anyone it is happening to. The one who was afraid to get it wrong, and then unafraid, has been burned away with the rest, and what is made now comes through you and goes out with nobody's name on it. Even the flat days are beautiful from here, because they were part of the making. It cannot be taught. All you can do is live it where people can see, and some will resent it, and some will come and stand in the light.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 1, Self-Expression
As a pressure in the body, the energy that makes things is the push to make them your own way. Gate 1 is the door it comes through: the push to put what is inside you out where it can be seen, in your shape and nobody else's. Nobody taught you the style; it is the one you had as a child, before anyone told you how things are done. In you it runs as an itch to make the thing and to say it the way you would say it, and when there is nowhere for that to go you are miserable and cannot always say why. When it does get out, the people near you feel more like themselves for having seen it.

From outside, doing it your way every time can look like stubbornness. What is going on is simpler: the making and the self are one movement, and asking a person like this to do it the usual way is asking them to be someone else. The cost is that you are misunderstood more than most, and a bad word about the work lands like a bad word about you. The people who carry this all their lives learn two things: that being different is the strength and not the fault, and that trying to be original on purpose is the one thing that chokes it.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
Seen through this lens, this pressure rises from the centre of the chest, the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life faces. It has no engine of its own. It works by drawing: the people and the work and the places that belong in your life are pulled toward it, and what goes out through Gate 1 is that self showing its face. When you trust it, it sets the direction. When you do not, you end up fighting what it was already bringing you.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on, and the self stays put whoever they are with. For someone like that, what they make carries one signature all their life, and the thing that starves them is being made to work in another person's manner; they tend to think everyone should be going their way, and if they give up their own way to follow someone else's, something in the chest goes out. In the other four it is open, and the sense of self is borrowed from whoever is nearest and let go when they leave. For someone like that, this pressure is one of the few places they feel solid. In the act of making they are somebody, for as long as the making lasts, and even where they sit in a room can change what they make.

### What completes it — the Channel of Inspiration (1–8)
What rises from the chest has no voice of its own. The half it lacks is Gate 8, up in the throat, the pressure to bring something to the group and have it count. The Channel of Inspiration runs between the two, and when it is whole, what was only yours becomes something other people can pick up and carry. This is a channel that waits. It speaks best when someone has asked to hear it and worst when it has pushed its way into the room, which is why a person who carries it whole can spend years feeling overlooked before they learn to wait for the asking. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, nothing crosses until both ends are lit.


## BODY

### Physiology
The liver is the largest organ inside you, tucked up under the right side of your ribs, and of the organs inside you it is the one that grows back. Take most of it away and within months it has rebuilt itself to size. Nearly everything the gut takes from a meal goes there first. It takes the sugar and stores it, and between meals lets it out again a little at a time, so that your blood holds one level whether you ate an hour ago or ten hours ago. It breaks down what is finished with and builds much of what the blood carries. All of this goes on while you sleep, and none of it asks you. Of everything in you, it is the part built to start again.

You can find it. Lay your right hand flat under the lowest rib on that side and breathe in as deep as you can. The liver sits just under the sheet of muscle that does your breathing, so the breath in pushes it down toward your fingers, and in some people its edge can be felt arriving. On the breath out it rises back. It goes down and comes up like that with every breath you take, and the work never stops for any of them.

### Amino acid
Lysine is the knot in the rope that holds you together. The rope is collagen, and there is more of it in you than of any other protein: it runs through your skin and your tendons and the frame of your bones. Collagen is strong because its strands are tied to one another, and the ties are made at this one building block and nowhere else. Take it away and the strands slide apart.

Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with food: meat, fish, eggs, beans and lentils are full of it, and grain is short of it, which is why beans over rice make a whole protein where either alone falls short. Reach down and take hold of the thick cord above your heel, and try to bend it sideways. It will not go. That cord is collagen taking the pull, knotted at lysine, and it will take that pull ten thousand times tomorrow without a thought from you.


## RELATIONS

_The kin here are what a beginning needs and does not have: somewhere to land, something to feed, and a hand on the measure._

### Pair — UL 2, Beyond the Shell (The Receptive)
Beyond the Shell is the ground this strength comes down onto, the same first act seen from underneath. Here everything pushes and nothing takes; there nothing pushes, and what is handed over is carried the whole way to its end. One is the force and the other is the direction, and in that pairing it is the ground that decides where the force goes. Open it when you have begun something and it will not go anywhere, because what a beginning needs next is not more push. It needs somewhere to land.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Turn the six lines over and they come back the same, one of only eight that do. A strength that is all one kind has no other face to show, no underneath, nothing waiting behind the first look. What looks back at you is the same rising you started with, and there is nothing else to open here.

### Programming partner — UL 2, Beyond the Shell
Beyond the Shell stands opposite you a second time, and this time it is the lows and the heights that are paired. At the bottom the two make each other worse: the flat days here want to be left alone, and the lost feeling there cannot leave anything alone and starts asking what is wrong, and the asking is what sets the flat hard. At the top they are one thing, the beauty of a single thing and the whole it belongs to. Open it when the low has turned into worry about the low, because it knows the way home when you cannot feel one.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
A family of two, the smallest kind there is. The other is Ancestors Bloom, the same fire a season on, with a harvest standing round it. Here it is the first catch, with nothing to burn yet and needing nothing. There the heat has something to feed and something to give away. What both know is what a fire costs: while it burns everyone gathers, when it drops to a flicker you are nobody, and forcing it back up is how you end up making bargains with yourself. Open it when the beginning has caught and you want to see what the burning was for.

### Tarot
The dragon climbs into a sky that never stops, and one face waits for it up there.

- **Ring Arcana — XIV · The Art (Temperance)** (Flow, Alignment): Through its family this strength carries The Art: the figure pouring between two cups without spilling a drop, one foot in the water and one on the land. A fire this hot is held to its measure here, mixed with what it needs before it is any use. Meet this face when the strength has to be blended with something before it can be poured out.

### Immortals
The sky is doubled, so one immortal stands for both halves. Han Xiangzi is the one with the flute. In the stories he left a scholar's house for the mountains, having no use for what could be learned at a desk, and when he played, flowers came out in the wrong season. That is this strength in a small instrument: breath with a shape put on it, and things growing that were not due yet.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the archer, a fire sign, and the arrow already off the string toward a mark the archer has not seen yet. Strength here is what has been let go, not what is being held.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Peg:** the tent peg driven into the ground, the small fixed thing that lets a whole roof stand up and take the wind. Even a strength that never stops needs one point that does not move.
