# UL 1 — Earth's Breath

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The First Move · Fresh Every Time · Strength Without Strain · Quiet Between Surges · The Pull to Force It · Up All at Once · In Your Own Shape

A force that begins things is up in you. You want to make and to begin and the wanting has no strain in it. People feel the freshness when you come into a room and come nearer without being asked. It moves the way the sky moves, all day and without effort, because nothing in it is being pushed.

What pulls at you here is the urge to force it when it goes quiet. Some mornings nothing pulls. The temptation is to drag the strength back up by the collar or to fill the day so the flat cannot be felt. The quiet is the strength gathering out of sight and it will not be dragged.

Let the quiet be. Go off by yourself and let it stay as flat as it is for as long as it is. Do not ask it what is wrong. When the rising comes it comes all at once. Move with it while it is warm and make the thing. It will not be the same thing twice.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven above and Heaven below, the same moving sky twice over. Nothing in it gives way and nothing up there has ever stopped. In spring the dragon wakes in the deep water and climbs through the open field into that sky. No ground meets it on the way. There is one kind of force here and there is a great deal of it.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
The sky does not rest, and that is the whole of Heaven's nature. It is the strength that keeps going after everything else has sat down. The sun does not take a day off. In the world it is the father, the one who is up first and out first and expects the same tomorrow. In a person it is drive. You start things. You keep going when the others have stopped and you do not need telling twice. Its risk is drought. Strength that never rests dries out what it set out to warm. It burns through its own beginnings before they have a shape.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Underneath is the sky again, the driving force at the bottom as well as the top. There is no ground under the push. Nothing soft takes the weight or slows the rise or says when it has gone far enough. What comes up from below is the same strength already overhead, so the two feed each other and neither tires first. In a person that is a drive with no brake on it. You do not run out. Running on is the one speed you have. With nothing under it to answer back a strength like this lifts a life or scorches it, and it cannot tell which from inside.

### Reading
The force that begins things is up in you and it is not going to stop. Whatever you have been putting off wants doing now. The wanting is strong enough to carry you if you let it. This is the part of the cycle where everything begins. What you start here will have to be grown and gathered and tested before the year is out. All of that is in front of you at once.

What this asks of you is to keep going without pausing and without forcing. The strength that crosses the sky does not push. It moves, and by moving it renews itself, and strain never comes into it. Do the same. Make yourself strong every day by the plain act of using the strength. Do not spend it all in one blaze. Keep to what you know is right while this lasts. A force this size with nothing to hold it will run through everything it has in a day.

Heaven's place in the body is the head. With the same force above and below there is nowhere else it would go. Feel for the lift under the skull now, the readiness behind the eyes.

Walk the way the sun crosses the sky, without stopping and without hurry. What you begin from here has the whole year to go.

### Judgement
- The Creative. It begins everything, and what it begins goes through and grows.
- Gain comes to the one who holds firm through the test.

### Image
- Heaven moves, and its moving is strength.
- So the wise make themselves strong from the inside, and do not pause.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ dragon under the water, and no use to be made of it yet. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
Something with real strength in it lies low in you, under the surface where nobody looks. The season for using it has not come. The stillness on top has a great deal going on underneath. Do not draw on it yet. Feed it where no one is watching and let it grow in the dark. When it does come up it will have somewhere to go and someone to meet.

**Line 2** · _image:_ dragon come up into the open field, and someone worth going to see. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
What was under the surface has come up into the open and people can see it. You have ability now and not yet the judgement to use it well. Find the one who has walked further than you and put yourself where they can see you. Keep company with people who make you more of yourself. What is like you will strengthen you. What shows itself finds its people.

**Line 3** · _image:_ working the whole day through, and still on guard at nightfall. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
You are in the exposed place, past the beginning and short of the height. You have great strength and the whole weight of what it is for. So you work all day and at dusk you are still watching. The tiredness is real and so is the danger. Neither is a fault. Keep working while there is light and stay awake after it goes. Step with care and nothing goes wrong.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a leap out of the deep, or a while longer in it, and either is allowed. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You have come to a fork with nothing wrong on either side. Rise now and be seen, or stay down a while longer and gather more. Look at the weather before you go. A leap taken with care lands well. So does a wait that you chose. Do not be hurried by the fear of choosing badly. Test the ground and then move a little at a time.

**Line 5** · _image:_ dragon up in the sky, and the great one there to be seen. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
The strength is at its full height, out in the open sky where everyone can see it. What it asks of you is to be someone worth looking at and to meet the others who have risen this high. Like draws like and clouds follow dragons. What has reached this height is for other people. Spend it on them and there is more of it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ dragon climbed past the top of the sky, with nowhere further to go. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
You have gone one step past the height. There is no further up. The strength that carried you here only knows how to go on. High with nobody beside you and nobody under you. The regret that comes is the right answer, since regret is what turns you back. Know how to withdraw as well as you know how to advance. Come down while the coming down is still yours to choose.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Entropy · _Gift:_ Freshness · _Siddhi:_ Beauty

### Shadow — Entropy
The days go flat. At its lowest this energy has gone under, the way a river goes quiet when whatever lives in it has sunk to the bottom. You get to the end of a day tired and cannot say what the day was. The colour drains out of the work and out of the people and nothing in it feels alive. This is numbness. Numbness is a feeling, though almost nobody knows they are in it. The usual move is to get away from it, to the phone or the fridge or anything rather than sit in the flat. What is being run from is a gap. The gap comes on by itself and leaves by itself if it is left alone. Argue with it and it sets hard.

The way through is to feel the numbness as numbness. Put a hand on your chest and notice that not much is there. Let that be allowed. The energy has not gone. It has gone down to gather, and something you cannot see yet is forming in the dark. It wants aloneness for as long as it takes. Trust the flat and it turns on its own.

### Repressive nature — Depressive
One person lets the flat set hard. The fear underneath takes over and keeps draining energy from the surface of the life. The eyes lose their shine and some days the bed is as far as it goes. Only they can walk out of it, and only by facing the fear that froze it.

### Reactive nature — Frenetic
Another person cannot bear it. When the flat arrives they fill the diary and ring everyone and the schemes get wilder, all to get away from what is going on inside. It runs the body against its own chemistry, and a week of quiet becomes a season of motion that the body pays for.

### Gift — Freshness
In the open this energy is freshness, the first move that nobody has made before. It comes up all at once. With it comes the need to say it or make it. What comes out is new. The aliveness in it is what people feel when you walk into a room. You take the step with no path under it and you are not afraid of the mistake, because the mistake is how the path gets made. A small group with you in it wakes up. You never wanted to be followed and you find people following anyway. When the thing has been given you go quiet again before it can spoil.

The flat season is what this is made from. What gathered under the surface while nothing showed is exactly what comes up now. There is no having it without the weeks of nothing. You cannot make it come and once it goes there is no keeping it. What you can do is be there when it happens and stay out of its way while it takes shape. The idea moves one way and then another and you let it. Soft hands, and a little wrong, and alive.

### Siddhi — Beauty
Up in the sky with nothing under it this energy is beauty. Beauty is what a life becomes when the making has gone all the way through. Nothing stands between you and what is there. A face, a field at dawn, a low grey week: each one shines with being exactly itself and all of it comes from one place. Quiet holds it. It is so fine that one word said about it can crack it, so you say less and see more.

This is what the freshness becomes when even the one who was fresh is let go of. There is no maker now. Whatever is made comes through you and goes on out and it is the more beautiful for nobody claiming it. Even the flat season is beautiful from here. Nothing about you needs showing and the ordinary is the whole of it. It is morning, and the dew is still on everything.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 1, Self-Expression
What rises in you, looked at as wiring, is a drive to make something that could only have come from you. Gate 1 is the part of you that has to express itself and it does not wait to be asked. Whatever is inside comes out in its own shape and in a style nobody taught you. The gift is that it is yours. Nobody else could have made it. When it comes out other people feel more themselves for being near it. In you it runs as an itch to make and to say the thing your way. When there is no outlet for it you are miserable and cannot always say why.

From outside the insistence on doing it your own way gets called self-absorbed or difficult for the sake of it. What is really going on is that the expression and the self are the same movement. A person like this cannot be told to do it the standard way without being told to be someone else. What they make is how they find out who they are.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The place this drive comes from is the middle of the chest, the Identity Centre. It holds who you are and which way your life faces. It has no engine in it. It works by drawing. What belongs to your life is pulled in toward it. The making that comes out of Gate 1 is that self showing its face.

Six people in ten have this centre defined, a self that stays put whoever they are with. If that is you, what you make will carry the same signature all your life. What starves it is being made to work in someone else's manner. Do that for long and something in the chest goes dim. In the other four the centre is open and the self is borrowed from whoever is nearest. If that is you, this drive is one of the few places you feel solid. In the act of making you are somebody for as long as the making lasts.

This energy does not run steadily. It is all or nothing, full on for a stretch and then completely at rest. What it needs is a form to go into and people who will give you the floor when the thing is ready. Then it needs a door you can shut behind you. Push against the rest and the work goes stale. Let it come when it comes and it comes as itself.

### What completes it — the Channel of Inspiration (1–8)
What rises from the middle of the chest has no voice of its own. The half it lacks is Gate 8 in the throat, Contribution, which is the drive to bring something to the group and have it count. The Channel of Inspiration runs between the two. What was only yours becomes something other people can take up and use. It is a wiring that waits to be invited before it speaks and it speaks best when it has been. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, nothing crosses until both ends are lit.


## BODY

### Physiology
Looked at from the body this rising sits in the liver, the large organ tucked up under the right side of your ribs. It is the one organ inside you that grows back. Cut most of it away and it rebuilds itself within months. Nearly everything the gut takes in from a meal goes to the liver first. It takes the sugar from a meal and stores it and lets it back out between meals. Your blood holds one level that way whether or not you have eaten. It breaks down what is finished with and builds much of what the blood carries. It does all of that while you are asleep and none of it asks your permission.

Lay your right hand flat under the lowest rib and breathe in as far as you can. On the breath in the liver is pushed down toward your fingers. In some people its edge can be felt arriving. On the breath out it goes back up. It moves that way with every breath you take, and so does the work.

### Amino acid
The rope that holds you together is tied at lysine. Collagen is the rope. There is more of it in you than of any other protein and it runs through skin and tendon and the frame of bone. The cross-ties that stop it pulling apart are made at this one building block. You cannot make it and have to eat it. It is one of only two building blocks that the body cannot turn into sugar, so when it is burned for energy it is burned the way fat is. Meat and fish and eggs are full of it and so are beans and lentils. Grain is short of it. That is why a bowl of beans over rice makes a whole protein where either one alone falls short.


## RELATIONS

_Around you here stand the things a force this size needs before it is any use, the ground that takes it and the fuel that keeps it and the hand that pours it out._

### Pair — UL 2, Beyond the Shell (The Receptive)
Beyond the Shell is this same force a season on. The push has been taken in by the ground and is being carried rather than driven. Where there is a dragon here there is a mare there, running behind and never in front. Nothing there goes first and what it is handed it carries to the end. Open it when you have begun something and cannot see what is holding it. Something is. That is the something.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Flip it and it comes back as itself, one of eight that do. A force that is all one kind has no other side to show. There is no underneath to it and no second face waiting behind the first. What looks back at you is the same rising you started with. There is nothing to open here.

### Programming partner — UL 2, Beyond the Shell
Beyond the Shell is also the pole the Gene Keys set against you. At their lowest the two make each other worse. The flat season here wants to be left alone, and the lostness there cannot leave anything alone and starts asking what is wrong. The asking is what fixes the flat in place. Open it when the low has turned into worry about the low. It knows which way the quiet was going.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
A family of two and the smallest kind there is. The other is Ancestors Bloom, the plenty that comes once the first flame has been fed and has something to burn. What the two share is the burning. Here it is the first catch, with nothing to burn yet and needing nothing. Over there the same heat has a harvest around it. Open it when the beginning has caught and you want to see what the burning is for.

### Tarot
The dragon climbs into a sky that never stops, and one face waits for it there.

- **Ring Arcana — XIV · The Art (Temperance)** (Flow, Alignment): By way of its family this carries The Art, the figure who pours between two cups without spilling a drop, one foot in the water and one on the land. A force this hot is held to its measure here. Meet this face when the strength has to be mixed with something before it can be used.

### Immortals
The same immortal holds both ends here, since the sky is doubled. Han Xiangzi carried a flute. In the stories he could make flowers open out of season by playing it. He walked out of a scholar's house to go into the mountains and learn what could not be taught indoors. A flute is breath with a shape put on it, and what he played made things grow.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the archer's sign, fire that is let go toward a mark it cannot see yet. The arrow does not strain in the air. It goes.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Peg:** the tent peg, the one fixed thing that lets a whole roof stand up and take the wind. Even a force that never stops has to be pegged to something to be of use.
