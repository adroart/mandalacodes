# UL 1 — Earth's Breath

## CODE

_Keywords:_ What Begins Things · Rises on Its Own · No Strain in It · The Flat Morning · Under, Not Gone · While It Is Warm · The Oldest Thing in You

This is the energy that begins things, the first move before anything exists. It is alive in you, it comes up on its own, and while it rises there is no strain in it. You want to make something, and the wanting is enough: you forget to eat, you are not tired, and by evening there is a thing in the world that was not there in the morning. Nobody showed you how to do that. It is the oldest thing in you, and it is yours.

What pulls at you is the morning nothing rises. The day goes flat, and the flat feels like a fault in you, so you try to haul the force back up by hand, or you fill the hours so full that it cannot be felt. It will not be hauled. It goes under between one rising and the next, the way the sun does. It is not gone.

Let the quiet be quiet. Do not ask it what is wrong, and do not fill it. When the rising comes it comes all at once, and you will know it by the wanting. Move then, while it is warm.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven above and Heaven below, the same sky twice. Nothing in it gives way, and nothing up there has ever stopped moving. In spring the dragon wakes at the bottom of the deep water and climbs, up through the open field and on into that sky, and nothing on the way tells it to stop. There is one kind of strength here, and there is a great deal of it.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the sky, and the sky does not rest. The sun comes up whether or not anyone is ready for it, crosses, goes down, and comes up again, and that is the whole of Heaven's nature: strength that keeps going after everything else has sat down. In the world it is the father, up first and out first and expecting the same of you tomorrow. In a person it is drive. You start things, you keep going when the others have stopped, and you do not need telling twice. Its risk is drought. Strength that never rests dries out what it set out to warm, and burns through its own beginnings before they have a shape.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Underneath is the sky again. The push comes from below as well as above, and there is no ground under it: nothing soft to take the weight, nothing to slow the rise, nothing to say when it has gone far enough. What comes up from below is the same strength that is already overhead, so the two feed each other and neither tires first. In a person that is a drive with no brake. You do not run out, and running on is the one speed you have. A strength like this, with nothing under it to answer back, lifts a life or scorches it, and from inside it cannot tell which.

### Reading
The situation here is strength with nothing above it. There is more of it in you right now than the day has room for; it came up on its own, it is still coming, and nothing is standing in its way. That is the whole of the situation: strength, with nothing above it to stop it, and no one to tell you when it has gone far enough. What you begin now can last for years.

What it asks of you is to keep going without stopping and without forcing, and those are two different things. The sun crosses the sky every day and never strains. It moves, and by moving it is made new, and it is never in a hurry. Do the same. Use the strength a little every day and you will have it every day. Spend it all in one blaze and there is nothing for tomorrow. Hold to what you know is right while this lasts, because a force this size with nothing holding it will run through everything it has before dark.

Heaven's place in the body is the head, and with the same strength above and below there is nowhere else this could sit. Feel for it now: the lift under the skull, the readiness behind the eyes, the way your gaze keeps going to the door.

So stand the way the sun crosses the sky, without stopping and without hurry. What you begin from here has the whole year in front of it.

### Judgement
- Heaven begins it. What it begins goes all the way through: it grows, it is gathered, and it is tested.
- It goes well for the one who holds firm.

### Image
- The sky moves, and never stops, and that is its strength.
- So the wise make themselves strong from the inside, and do not tire.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a dragon under the water, and no use to be made of it yet. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
Something with real strength in it is lying low in you, under the surface where nobody looks. You can feel it there. The surface looks still, and a great deal is going on underneath it. The season for using it has not come, and if you bring it up now it will be spent before it is ready. Feed it where no one is watching. Let it grow in the dark for as long as it needs. When it does come up it will have somewhere to go, and someone to meet.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a dragon come up into the open field, and someone worth going to see. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
What was under the surface has come up into the open, and people can see it. You have real ability now, and not yet the judgement to use it well, and those are two different things. This is the moment to find the one person who has walked further than you and put yourself where they can see you. Keep the company of people who make you more of yourself: what is like you strengthens you, and what is not wears you thin. Show yourself. What shows itself finds its people.

**Line 3** · _image:_ working the whole day through, and still awake to the danger at nightfall. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
You are in the exposed place, past the beginning and short of the height. You have the strength and the whole weight of what it is for, so you work all day, and when the light goes you are still awake, listening. The tiredness is real and so is the danger, and neither one is a fault. Keep working while there is light. Keep watch after it goes. Step with care and nothing goes wrong.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a leap up out of the deep, or a while longer in it, and either is allowed. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You have come to a fork, and for once there is nothing wrong on either side of it. You can rise now and be seen, or stay down a while longer and gather more, and both lead somewhere good. What is asked is that you look at the weather before you go: a leap taken with care lands well, and so does a wait you chose. Do not let the fear of choosing badly choose for you. Test the ground, and then move a little at a time.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a dragon up in the open sky, and the great one there to be seen. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
The strength is at its full height, out where everyone can see it, and it is carrying you. Nothing is hidden now and nothing needs to be. What this asks is that you be worth looking at, and that you go and meet the others who have risen this high. Like draws like: clouds follow the dragon, and people follow the one who has become what they could be. What has reached this height is not for you alone. Spend it on other people, and there is more of it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a dragon climbed past the top of the sky, with nowhere further to go. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
You have gone one step past the height, and there is no further up. The strength that carried you here only knows how to go on, and going on is the one thing it cannot do now. You are high, with nobody beside you and nobody under you. The regret that comes is not a failure. It is the turn. Know how to come down as well as you know how to rise, and come down while the coming down is still yours to choose.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Entropy · _Gift:_ Freshness · _Siddhi:_ Beauty

### Shadow — Entropy
At its lowest, this energy goes flat. The days go flat with it: you get to the end of one tired and cannot say what it was for. The light has gone out of the work and out of the people, and nothing you do puts it back. This is numbness, and numbness is a feeling, though almost nobody knows they are in it. You will do nearly anything rather than feel it. You reach for the phone, or the fridge, or someone to ring, and none of it works for long. Try to fix the flat, or find the reason for it, and it sets hard. The more you make things, the deeper these days go.

Put a hand on your chest and notice how little is there, and let that be allowed. Nothing has left you. What rises in you has only gone down to gather, and something you cannot see yet is being made in the dark. It wants you alone, and it wants you quiet, for as long as it takes. It came on by itself, and left to itself, it goes.

### Repressive nature — Depressive
This is the person the flat closes over. Fear takes hold, and it keeps pulling the energy down and away from the surface of their life, until the eyes lose their shine and some days the bed is as far as they get. Nobody else can walk them out. Only they can, and only by turning to face the fear underneath it.

### Reactive nature — Frenetic
This is the person who cannot bear the flat for a minute. The moment it arrives they fill every hour and ring everyone they know, and the plans get wilder, all to get away from what is going on inside. Everything in the body is saying go down and be still, and they go the other way, and the body pays for it.

### Gift — Freshness
The light comes back all at once. One morning the colour is in things again, and with it comes something you have to say or make, and the joy of it is as sudden as the flat was. What comes out is new. Nobody has made it before and nobody else could have, because it came up out of your own dark. You take the step where there is no path, and you are not afraid of getting it wrong, because that is how the new thing gets found. A small room wakes up when you walk into it, and you never wanted to be followed, and people follow anyway.

The flat days are what this is made from. What gathered under the surface while nothing showed is what comes up now, and you do not get the one without the other. You cannot make it come, and when it goes there is no calling it back. What you can do is be there when it happens and stay soft while it takes its shape. An idea moves one way and then another. Let it. Then, when it has been given, go quiet again. Fresh things do not keep.

### Siddhi — Beauty
Everything you look at is beautiful. A face, a field at first light, a flat grey week: each one shines with being exactly what it is, and it is all one light. This is what happens when you give yourself to what you make so completely that it changes you. There is nothing left between you and what you see. It needs quiet, the same quiet you learned in the flat days, because a beauty this fine can be cracked by one word said about it. So you say less, and you see more.

This is what the light becomes when you stop being the one it happens to. There is no maker now. Whatever is made comes through you and goes out, and it is the more beautiful for nobody claiming it. Even the flat days are beautiful from here. It cannot be taught, only lived. You are lit the way a morning is, and anyone who wants to can come and stand in it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 1, Self-Expression
Looked at as wiring, what rises in you is a pressure to make something that could only have come from you. Gate 1 is the door it comes through: the pressure to express yourself, in your own shape, without waiting to be asked. Whatever is inside wants out, and it wants out the way you would do it, not the way it is usually done. Nobody taught you the style. When it does come out, the people near you feel more like themselves for having seen it. In you it runs as an itch to make the thing and to say it your way, and when there is nowhere for it to go you are miserable and cannot always say why.

From outside, doing it your own way every time can look like stubbornness, or like being difficult for the sake of it. What is really going on is that the making and the self are one movement. Ask a person like this to do it the standard way and you have asked them to be someone else. What they make is how they find out who they are.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
This pressure comes from the centre of the chest, the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life faces. It has no engine of its own. It works by drawing. What belongs in your life is pulled in toward it, the people and the work and the places, and what comes out through Gate 1 is that self showing its face.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on, and the self stays put whoever they are with. For a person like that, what they make carries the same signature all their life, and the thing that starves them is being made to work in someone else's manner: do that for long and something in the chest goes dim. In the other four the centre is open, and the sense of self is borrowed from whoever is nearest and let go when they leave. For a person like that, this pressure is one of the few places they feel solid. In the act of making they are somebody, for as long as the making lasts.

### What completes it — the Channel of Inspiration (1–8)
What rises from the centre of the chest has no voice of its own. The half it lacks is Gate 8, up in the throat, the pressure to bring something to the group and have it count. The Channel of Inspiration runs between the two, and when it is whole, what was only yours becomes something other people can pick up and use. It is a channel that waits. It speaks best when someone has asked to hear it, and worst when it has pushed its way into the room. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, nothing crosses until both ends are lit.


## BODY

### Physiology
The liver is the largest organ inside you, tucked up under the right side of your ribs, and it is the one that grows back. Cut most of it away and within months it has rebuilt itself to size. Nearly everything the gut takes from a meal goes to the liver first. It takes the sugar and stores it, and between meals it lets the sugar back out a little at a time, so your blood holds one level whether you have eaten or not. It breaks down what is finished with and builds much of what the blood carries. It does all of this while you sleep, and none of it asks your permission. Of everything in you, it is the part that can start again.

Lay your right hand flat under your lowest rib and breathe in as far as you can. The liver sits just under the breathing muscle, so on the breath in it is pushed down toward your fingers, and in some people its edge can be felt arriving. On the breath out it rises back. It moves that way with every breath you take, and the work goes on through all of them.

### Amino acid
Lysine is the tie in the rope that holds you together. The rope is collagen, and there is more of it in you than of any other protein: it runs through your skin and your tendons and the frame of your bones. Collagen is strong because its strands are tied to one another, and the ties are made at this one building block. Without it the strands would slide apart.

Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with food. Meat, fish, eggs, beans and lentils are full of it, and grain is short of it, which is why a bowl of beans over rice makes a whole protein where either one alone falls short. Pinch the skin on the back of your hand hard between two fingers. It hurts before it gives, and it does not tear. That is collagen taking the pull, tied at lysine.


## RELATIONS

_Around you here stand the things a strength like this needs and cannot make for itself: the ground that takes it, the fire it belongs to, and the hand that measures it out._

### Pair — UL 2, Beyond the Shell (The Receptive)
Beyond the Shell is the ground this strength comes down onto, the same beginning seen from underneath. Here everything pushes and nothing receives. There nothing pushes, and what is handed over is carried all the way to its end. Together they are the first whole act: a force, and something to take it. Open it when you have begun something and it will not go anywhere, because what a beginning needs next is not more push. It needs ground.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Turn the six lines over and they come back the same, one of only eight that do. A strength that is all one kind has no other face to show. There is no underneath to it and nothing waiting behind the first look. What looks back at you is the same rising you started with, and there is nothing to open here.

### Programming partner — UL 2, Beyond the Shell
Beyond the Shell stands opposite you a second time, and this time it is the lows and the heights that are paired. At their lowest the two make each other worse. The flat days here want to be left alone, and the lost feeling there cannot leave anything alone and starts asking what is wrong, and the asking is what sets the flat hard. Open it when the low has turned into worry about the low, because it knows the way when you cannot feel one.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
A family of two, the smallest kind there is. The other is Ancestors Bloom, the same fire a season on, with a harvest standing around it. What the two share is the burning. Here it is the first catch, with nothing to burn yet and needing nothing. There the heat has something to feed and something to give away. Open it when the beginning has caught and you want to see what the burning was for.

### Tarot
The dragon climbs into a sky that never stops, and one face waits for it there.

- **Ring Arcana — XIV · The Art (Temperance)** (Flow, Alignment): Through its family this strength carries The Art, the figure pouring between two cups without spilling a drop, one foot in the water and one on the land. Fire this hot is held to its measure here. Meet this face when the strength has to be mixed with something before it can be used.

### Immortals
One immortal holds both ends here, because the sky is doubled. Han Xiangzi carried a flute, and in the stories what he played made flowers open out of season. He walked out of a scholar's house to go into the mountains and learn what could not be learned indoors. A flute is breath with a shape put on it. What he played made things grow.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the archer's sign, fire let go toward a mark it cannot see yet. The arrow does not strain in the air. It goes.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Peg:** the tent peg, the one fixed thing that lets a whole roof stand up and take the wind. Even a strength that never stops has to be pegged to something before it is any use.
