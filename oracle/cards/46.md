# UL 46 — Fountain of Light

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Glad to Be in a Body · Rising Without Pushing · Luck That Finds You · The Grip on How It Should Go · Held Lightly · Play as the Work · Alive Right Now

Lightness is the energy you carry, the rising that happens in a life when nothing in it is gripping. In you it is the plain gladness of being alive in a body: food that tastes of something, sleep that goes all the way down, a day with a shine on it. It lives below thinking, in the body itself, and it is what people are feeling when they call you lucky.

Carried well, it looks like this. A village in a long drought sends for the rainmaker, an old man who asks only for a hut, to be left alone; after a few days it rains. His secret is that he has no power over the weather at all. He has found what he is for, so he goes where he feels like going, and where he goes it happens to rain. Luck is what happens when you stop interfering with life; it is the world telling you that you are in step with it. So you hold your aims loosely enough to drop them if life offers something better, and let yourself be taken where it was going to take you anyway. People near you feel life turned up a little, and drift toward it without knowing why.

Tread lightly and worry less than you do, because life will take you where it wants you to go. Mostly the work is undoing a grip. There is a game a parent plays with a small child: hold them tight, and the harder they struggle the tighter the hold. The moment they go still the arms loosen, until they learn that the way out is to do nothing. You are the child, and the arms are your own. When the heavy seasons come, in which you are braced against how things will turn out, that is the hold, and it loosens the same way.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Wind, and wind here is also wood: a tree growing inside the ground, the root at work before anything green has broken the surface. The ground gives way, because that is what ground does. The traditional character means to rise, and it also names a measure for strong drink drawn off a ferment: the rising is a distilling, the finer thing coming up out of the thick.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Above is the ground, open all the way up, with nothing hard in it. Ground gives way; that is its whole nature, and it is why anything can grow at all. In a family it is the mother, the one who makes room. In you it is the part that can yield without losing itself, that lets the new thing through instead of standing in its way. Its risk is heaviness of the wrong kind, when the ground that should let the shoot through packs down hard over it, and giving way becomes holding down.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Under the ground is the wood, the tree at its root, which is the same sign as the wind. What a root does is enter: it finds the gap, goes in without breaking anything, and keeps going. In a family it is the eldest daughter, the one who gets things done quietly and is not stopped. In you it is the part that works by entering gently, pressing upward a little every day, so that the rising is never a shove but a growing. Its risk is that mildness thins into drift, a root that wanders sideways and never turns up toward the light.

### Reading
You are rising, and the rising is the kind that does not show. Something in your life is growing the way a tree grows inside the ground, adding to itself where nobody can see. The situation is a good one: the old text promises great growth, tells you not to fear, and sends you toward the south, the warm, bright side of things. What it asks is that you keep going in that direction without straining, heaping up small things until something high stands there. Do not overlook the small gains; they are what does the lifting. It asks, too, that you go and see the great one, whoever has already been where you are going, because the rising is welcomed from above only when you approach it.

What tries to bury you does not know you are a seed. Pressed down is exactly how a seed begins, so the weight on you is not against the rising; it is what the rising pushes off. Give it time to come through the resistance of the ground. Honour your roots as you go, because the roots decide how long you stay up.

In the body this energy is the thighs, the strongest muscle you own, the one that lifts you up a stair. Notice how you are carried now, whether the legs are doing the work or the shoulders are trying to. Let the lift come from low down, as a tree's does, and the top of you can stay light.

- Set out toward the south, and it goes well.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a rising that is welcomed, a good seed in good ground. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
The rising begins with a welcome, and the ground is ready for it: the seed is good, the soil too. Those above you agree with where you are going, so nothing here resists. Come to it humble and willing, approaching the ones who know more than you. This is the kind of rising that goes all the way.

**Line 2** · _image:_ sincere, so that even a small offering at the spring rite is accepted. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
You have less to bring than the others, and a feeling of not belonging follows you in. It is a spring offering, made when the stores are low, and it is accepted for what is in it rather than what it cost. You feel wrong here, and you are not. Stay sincere, bring the small thing, and there is joy in it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ rising into an empty city. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
You go up and meet no resistance at all: the gates open, nobody on the walls. It may be that the way is truly clear, or that you have rushed ahead of everyone who could have told you what was there. The city does not say which. Go forward, since there is nothing to push against, but go as one who has not yet found out what the emptiness holds.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the king makes his offering on the twin-peaked mountain. → becomes Hexagram 32, Duration.
The mountain is the place the whole line came from, and the offering is made there, at the source. To rise this way is to rise by honouring what you grew out of, going from low to high in order, entering a fine place by degrees. Present yourself where you began, and the rising keeps its roots.

**Line 5** · _image:_ steady and upright, going up the steps. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
Near the height the rising has become a stair, one step after the next with nothing skipped. What carries you the rest of the way is steadiness, and the pull here is to take the steps two at a time. The increase of what is good in you is as easy as a stair, when you take it as a stair. Go up one step at a time, calmly.

**Line 6** · _image:_ rising in the dark, and no pause in being upright. → becomes Hexagram 18, Work on the Decayed.
At the top the rising has gone blind: you have what you wanted and are still climbing, into the dark, past where up means anything, at the cost of what you gathered. What helps is to stay true without a pause, as a tree grows in the night as in the day. Keep your breath steady, keep to what is right, and rise by constancy or not at all.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Seriousness · _Gift:_ Delight · _Siddhi:_ Ecstasy

### Shadow — Seriousness
A small dark cloud travels over some lives, and it rains on them whenever they least want it to. That is seriousness, the most common ailment there is: worrying, expecting, wishing life were other than it is right now, with the attention fixed on what is coming or on what is gone. It feels like maturity, like caring how things turn out. What it is, mostly, is a grip, one that takes you out of life and love into control. Being out of step with the whole is what makes it rain.

It was laid down early. Your body learned in its first years whether the world was safe, from the people round you rather than from anything said. A body that did not learn it braces. The mind can be told that all is well; it is the body that has to feel it, and the mind will not settle until the body has. The grown-ups who handed you the bracing had forgotten how to play, and meant no harm by it. Nobody is more serious than the people who are trying hardest to be good, and it shows in their faces.

### Repressive nature — Frigid
The heaviness freezes this person. Their capacity to feel goes cold; they hide from life out of fear of their own body, and disliking it sours the life in them. It sets in the face as a pinched look. Enjoying the body instead of judging it is what thaws them, from a warmth buried deep.

### Reactive nature — Frivolous
This seriousness hides under a show of taking nothing seriously. The person plays the light one, joking; a scratch on the surface finds anger, which comes out when somebody is honest with them. Underneath they take life more deadly seriously than anyone, so they run from the truth, in loves that are many and short.

### Gift — Delight
Delight is what is left when the grip lets go, and it is not a mood you make. It comes from owning what is yours, the acceptance of a thing seen; once a thing has been seen and let go, more of you is free for being glad. Nothing really matters except life and love. A person who knows that in the body moves through the world lightly, letting the past go, riding the present, with no worries anyone can find. Others call them lucky. What they are is in step.

You train for years for a medal, fall ill the day before, fall in love with the nurse, and only years on see that missing the medal was the point: success is understood in hindsight. The body comes back to life: you wake with the sun, and you keep the habits the body enjoys instead of the pleasures you pay for later. You taste things the way a child does, through the whole body. You love good food and laughter. People find you attractive on a plain physical level, as if there were more life in you than in most, which has nothing to do with how you look.

### Siddhi — Ecstasy
Ecstasy is what the body is for, and it comes through the heart, not from anywhere far away. It is your state whenever the mind comes all the way to rest: the moment the mind is needed it steps back, and the moment the task is done it returns. A person living here does the shopping, sits at the family table, and is felt across a room by people who know nothing about any of this. It comes in waves, swelling and dropping like wind, so you give yourself to it.

There is no work on earth more important than play. The heart makes none of the mind's distinctions, success from failure, past from future. It knows only that it is alive and beating right now, which floods you. If you have felt it once you can find it again. It waits in the chambers of your heart, and it begins with a breath let out slowly.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 46, Embodiment
The pressure here is to be all the way inside your body. Gate 46 is the door it comes through: the love of the body, the joy of being in one, meeting life through its senses. It is the determination of a child in the womb pushing its way out. In you it is felt as a pull toward touch and movement, toward treating the body with tenderness, as what carries who you are rather than a bag the real you is kept in.

From outside it can look like vanity, or like a lack of interest in higher things. It is the opposite: this is the pressure that knows nothing higher can land in you until the body is a place worth landing. Turned away from, it is a person at odds with their own body, neglecting it, hiding it, or trying to make it into something else. It draws some to sport or to yoga, and leaves others out of step with the body altogether. People who carry it all their lives learn to ask how much life the body can hold, and to live with that much.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
Who you are, and which way your life faces, are kept in the centre of the chest, the Identity Centre. This pressure lives there because the centre is the spark that called you into a body. It has no engine. It draws, only draws, pulling toward you the people and the places a life needs. In the old teaching it is love itself, drawing what is beautiful toward it. The same spark leaves the body at the end.

In a little over half of people this centre is switched on, and the self stays put whoever they are with. For someone like that there is a self to rely on, a steady road, a life chosen from the menu. The body they are glad in is the same body every day. The risk is taking the solidity for granted, never asking whether they are glad in it at all. In the other four it is not, so the self comes and goes with the company. For someone like that it is a relief to hear that the thing they are doing is not the thing they must do forever. They fit any room, play the part, and leave it behind. What they have instead of a fixed self is the moment: writing, they are a writer, and in a body that is glad they are that gladness for as long as it lasts.

### What completes it — the Channel of Discovery (29–46)
The love of the body alone has nowhere to go. Its other half is Gate 29, low in the belly, the part of you that says yes, means it, and stays. Together they make the Channel of Discovery: you commit your whole self to an experience without knowing where it leads, which is the discovery. Once the belly has said yes the stamina is there; you turn up in the right place at the right time, succeeding where others fail. Whether the other half sits in you or near you, the finding out happens only where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to the blood in the body, and the reason is warmth. The blood in you is made from what you eat, and comes out red; it is remade all the time, two or three million new cells a second. It goes out to your palms so they can hold, to your feet so they can walk; where it draws back, a part of you goes white and numb. The warmth in your skin is it arriving. When you are at ease in your body it comes to the surface, so you are warm to the touch, with colour in your face. When you are braced it withdraws, and your hands go cold. Your body decides this before you do.

Lay a cold hand flat against the warm skin of your belly. What you feel is blood, heat made deep inside you, carried out to the skin. The same hand held in cold water goes white because the blood has drawn back from it. That is the energy you can feel arriving.

### Amino acid
The gladness of being in a body has a substance in you, and the substance is alanine. It is one of the twenty small pieces every part of you is built from, in nearly everything you eat, though your body makes its own anyway. It took you seven years to arrive fully in your body; what you learned then about being safe is written in how you breathe and touch things.

Delight is feeling alive in the flesh, and in you it has a chemistry. On a long walk with the meal gone, your muscle sends alanine through your blood to your liver, which sends it back as sugar. The same piece goes out as one thing and comes back as another. A body is there to be filled all the way through by what lives in it, and what follows is not something you make; it grows.
