# UL 46 — Fountain of Light

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Glad to Be Alive · At Home in Your Body · Luck That Finds You · Right Place and Time · Holding Plans Lightly · Room to Play · Worrying How It Goes

Some days things work out without any pushing, and you turn up in the right place at the right time. Delight lives in those days, the plain gladness of being alive in a body. Food tastes of something, and the air feels good on your skin. You love life more than any plan you have for it.

At times you brace against how things will turn out, and worry takes the place of the gladness. The mind can be told that all is well, but it is the body that has to feel safe. Worry holds you the way a parent holds a struggling child, tighter while you fight and loose once you go still.

Hold your plans loosely enough to drop them when something better comes along. Stay open to surprises, and let life take you where it was already going. Make room for play, for good food and for the people you love. Good fortune finds you there, and you are glad to be alive when it does.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Wind is the hexagram of steady growth, the kind that rises a little every day. Wind, which here is also wood, is the part of you that grows into whatever room it finds. Earth is the part of you that gives way and lets that growth come through. The yielding sits above the growing because nothing rises far unless something makes room for it. This hexagram has long been read as a tree growing inside the ground. Your own progress grows the same way, out of sight a little each day, until one morning it shows. Small gains add up the same way, until something tall stands there. You rise the way anything living rises, a little at a time.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the give in you that lets a thing grow at its own pace. It sits on top because the world around you is making room, the way soil opens for a shoot. Earth has long been read as the mother, who feeds and carries whatever she is given. You meet what comes and let it grow, instead of standing in its way. On its own, it lets you give way to everything and start nothing.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the quiet effort in you that keeps going where it cannot force. It lies underneath because growth starts at the root, out of sight, long before anything shows. Wind has long been read as a root, which feels for the soft places in the soil and goes in. Your effort works the same way, going a little further each day without breaking anything. On its own, it lets you find your way into everything and settle nowhere.

### Reading
The way ahead is open, and what you have worked at slowly begins to rise. Little stands in its way now. Do not worry about how it will go. Keep adding one small gain to the next, and do not overlook them, because they are what lifts you. Go and see someone who has already been where you are heading, and listen to them. Move toward what is warm and bright.

Honour where you came from as you rise, because your roots decide how long you stay up. Stay humble while you climb. People who climb fast and stop looking down miss the first signs of trouble. Calm, steady progress keeps you where you have climbed to.

Your thighs carry this time, the strongest muscles you have, the ones that lift you up a stair. Let the lift come from low down, and keep your shoulders loose.

### Judgement
- Pushing upward: from the source it goes through and grows.
- It serves to see the great one. Have no worry.
- Set out toward the south, and it goes well.

### Image
- Within the earth, wood grows: pushing upward.
- So the wise, yielding, heap up the small until it stands high and great.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Seriousness · _Gift:_ Delight · _Siddhi:_ Ecstasy

### Shadow — Seriousness
Seriousness is the most common ailment there is. It is worrying, expecting, wishing life were other than it is right now. Your attention sits on what is coming or on what is gone, and the day in front of you goes by. It feels like being responsible. Mostly it is a grip, and it takes you out of life and love into control. Out of step with life, you find it rains whenever you least want it to.

It is handed down. Parents who are serious about life pass it into the air a child grows up in, however much they want the child to be happy. They have forgotten how to play. Even people on a spiritual path take it too seriously, and it shows in their faces.

### Repressive nature — Frigid
Held in, the seriousness freezes the senses. They hide from life out of fear of their own body, and disliking it turns their vitality sour. It shows in a face that stays pinched. When they start to enjoy being in their body instead of judging how it looks, a warmth buried deep in them comes back.

### Reactive nature — Frivolous
Let out, the seriousness wears a show of taking nothing seriously. They joke and stay laid back, and under it they carry a great deal of anger. It breaks out when someone is honest with them. They take life more seriously than anyone, so they run from the truth, and their loves are many and short.

### Gift — Delight
Delight is the freedom of enjoying how rich it is to be alive. It begins with owning what is yours. Once you have seen something about yourself and let it go, you have more energy for being glad. Nothing really matters except life and love, and you feel this in your body.

A dry village sends for a rainmaker, and after a few days it rains, though he has no power over the weather. He has found what he is for, so he turns up where he likes, and it happens to rain. Find what you are for, and you need only turn up. Luck is what happens when you stop interfering with life. It tells you that you are in step with everything around you. People like this let the past go and ride the present, soft and at ease in their bodies, whatever they look like. Others are drawn to them without knowing why.

### Siddhi — Ecstasy
Ecstasy lives in the heart. It is your natural state whenever your mind is fully at rest. When the mind is needed it comes forward, and when the task is done the ecstasy returns. It lives in the shopping, at the family table, in the ordinary world. Others can feel it from across a room.

There is no work on earth more important than play. The heart knows only that it is alive and beating right now, and that knowing floods you. If you have felt this once, you can find it again. It begins with a slow, easy breath.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 46, Embodiment
Gate 46 is the love of the body, the joy of being in one and meeting life through its senses. It is the determination of a child in the womb, pushing its way out into the world. It draws you toward touch and movement, and toward caring for your body tenderly. Your body is not just something you live in. It carries your whole life, and it wants to hold as much life as it can.

Turned against, the same pressure leaves you at odds with your body. You may neglect it, hide it, or try to make it into something else so that you cannot be fully seen. Some are drawn to yoga or sport, and others fall out of step with their body altogether.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The Identity Center sits in the middle of the chest and holds who you are and which way your life faces. It is the spark that calls you into a body, and it leaves the body when you die. It does not push you anywhere. It only draws, pulling toward you the people, places and work your life needs.

A little more than half of people have this centre defined, and their sense of self stays the same whoever they are with. They choose their life off the menu, and their path tends to be steady. The rest have it open, and who they are shifts with the company and the place. They piece a life together from the buffet. Where they live and where they sit matter to them more than to most. It is a relief for them to hear that what they are doing is not what they must do forever. They become what they are doing while they do it: writing, they are a writer.

### What completes it — the Channel of Discovery (29–46)
The love of the body needs something to give itself to. Gate 29, in the Sacral Center low in the belly, is the yes that means it and keeps going. Together they make the Channel of Discovery: you commit to an experience before you know where it leads. Once your belly has said yes, you have the stamina to see it through. You may succeed where others fail, and fail where others succeed. Gate 29 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
Warmth is the reason this energy is given to the blood. Your blood is made from what you eat, and it comes out red. It goes to your palms so they can hold, and to your feet so they can walk. The warmth in your skin is the blood arriving. When you are at ease in your body, it rises to the surface. Your hands are warm, and there is colour in your face. When you brace, it draws inward and your hands go cold. Your body decides this before you do.

Lay a cold hand flat on the warm skin of your belly. The heat you feel was made deep inside you and carried out to the skin by your blood. Your marrow makes two or three million new red cells every second, so you are in new blood three times a year.

### Amino acid
The gladness of being in a body has a chemistry, and one substance in it is alanine. It is one of the twenty pieces every protein in you is built from. Your body makes its own, though nearly every food with protein in it carries some too.

It took you seven years to arrive fully in your body. What you learned then is written in your posture, your breathing and the way you touch things. Hours into a walk with no food, your muscles send alanine through your blood to your liver. Your liver sends it back as sugar. A body is there to be filled completely by the life in it, and delight is what that feels like.

## RELATIONS

### Pair — UL 45, Tribal Tapestry (Gathering Together)
This energy rises on its own when nothing grips it, and it runs thin when it rises alone. Tribal Tapestry is where it goes next: the pull that gathers people around what there is and shares it out. Open it when good fortune has found you and you want other people in it. Nothing grows far without people to lean on.

### Inverse — UL 45, Tribal Tapestry (Gathering Together)
Turn this energy end for end and the rising becomes a gathering. Tribal Tapestry is the same growth seen from the crowd that makes it possible. Open it when you have been climbing alone for too long.

### Programming partner — UL 25, The Mysteries Play
This energy grows lighter the less it grips, and it cannot let go of what it has not first accepted. The Mysteries Play holds that acceptance, the love that takes life as it is. The two grow out of each other, because you have to own a thing before you can let it go. Open it when you know you are holding on and cannot stop.

### Codon ring — Ring of Matter
This energy lives in the body, where every life first learns whether it is safe. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Matter, which shapes how you settle into a body up to the age of twenty-one. UL 57, Flight of the Tao, carries the months you spent in the womb. UL 48, Doorways of the Unknown, carries your second seven years. UL 18, Liberation of the Greater, carries your third. This one carries the first seven years.

The tarot's major cards, its Arcana, bring five pictures to this one through its family and its two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): The Emperor builds an order that lasts, the solid ground your body gives a life to grow from.
- **Earth (upper) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil is the grip on things, the weight you feel when you cannot let go of how life should go.
- **Earth (upper) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World is the journey finished and whole, which is where rising step by step ends.
- **Wind (lower) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel of Fortune turns your luck, and it turns your way once you stop trying to hold it still.
- **Wind (lower) — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death is the change that makes something new of you, the way a seed gives itself up to become a tree.

### Immortals
Earth above carries Lan Cai He, the wandering singer with a basket of flowers, who shows you how to go through life lightly. Wind below carries He Xian Gu, the one woman among the Eight, who holds a lotus. The lotus grows clean out of the mud, the way delight can grow out of a heavy time.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Aries:** the first sign of spring, when a seed breaks through the soil and a new year begins in you.
- **Hebrew letter — He, the Window:** a window lets light and air into a room, the way this energy lets life into your body.
