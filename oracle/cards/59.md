# UL 59 — Mystics Treasures

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Reaching for Another · The Wariness in the Room · What You Keep Hidden · The Old Pain, Held · Honest With Your Own Heart · Nothing Left to Protect

The reach toward another person is this energy, the drive in you to get through whatever stands between you and be known on the far side of it. In you it is felt first as a wariness. Alone in a room you are at ease; someone walks in, and a faint guardedness starts up in the air between you, so old that you stopped noticing it. The reach and the wariness are one thing. What you keep hidden is what the other has to fear, and it is the fear that holds the two of you apart.

Carried well, it begins with honesty, and the honesty begins at home. Before you can be honest with another heart you have to be honest with your own, about what you are hiding and why; most of what you find there is old pain rather than anything shameful. Held instead of run from, the pain lets go, and when it does there is room around your heart that was not there before. That room is what closeness is. It is not something you do to another person; it is what shows once you no longer need them to be different, and someone who feels that from you is free to be honest in turn.

The way is not to run from it. Every time the guardedness is blamed on the other person, the same knot ties itself again somewhere else, because it was never out there. Turn back toward the wariness, into the body, where it lives. Whatever you meet there is yours, and the moment you own it there is nothing left in you for anyone to fear.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Water: a wind crossing a pool, the still surface going to ripples and spray. What had frozen is melting, what had set hard is coming apart, the way fog lifts off a field or a crowd breaks up and goes home. The old name means to scatter; it is said of clouds, of suspicions, of a fear that had settled in and now lifts.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is what gets in. One soft line lies under two firm ones, the way air finds the gap under a shut door, and it works on a thing from outside, without force, until the thing gives. In a family it is the eldest daughter, the one who goes on gently until the house comes round. In you it is the part that reaches a closed person by staying, softly, rather than by pushing. Above, it is the pressure working on the surface of things. Its risk is the wavering: advance, withdraw, advance again, until nothing is settled and everyone is tired.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Underneath is water, here the pool that has stopped moving: a feeling that went down and set. One firm line is held between two soft ones, a hard thing inside something that yields, and nobody sees it from the surface. In the world it is the pond under ice, the whole depth of it still there, unmoving, waiting for a wind that is warm enough. In you it is the fear that lives at the bottom, older than any reason you could give for it. Its risk is the pit: a feeling left frozen long enough that you forget there was ever water under it.

### Reading
Something between you and other people has set hard; this is its breaking up. A suspicion gone solid, a self held so tight it has shut everyone out: the wind is on it now, and what was rigid begins to move. It feels like loss while it happens. It is a thaw. What it asks of you is warmth first. A cold wind chills the pool to ice; a warm one melts it, and what you scatter carries your own temperature, so if you go out cold, cold is what you spread. Warm yourself first, since no warmth comes in from outside to do it for you.

The old picture is the king going to the temple. When his people have drifted apart, hard with suspicion, he does not drive them back together. He stands in the ancestral hall with his whole heart, and the scattered gather on their own, because sincerity draws what force scatters. Scatter what ought to be scattered, the faction, the held grudge, and what ought not to be scattered can gather again. Now is the time for the big crossing: a boat on the wide water with the wind behind it, pushed forward by the same thing that broke up the ice.

In the body this energy is the thighs, the part of you that carries you across a room toward someone, or out of it. Notice which way yours are turned now. Stand easy in them, ready to cross, and let the warmth go first.

**Line 2** · _image:_ in the scattering, running to what holds you up. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
Everything round you is coming apart, and you run, not away, but toward the thing that holds. The horse breaks out of the stall and makes for open ground; you make for the one support you can rest on in the middle of the stream. Leave what was harming you for what is reliable, and every reason for regret goes with the leaving.

**Line 3** · _image:_ scattering yourself, with the aim set outside you. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle Wind.
Here what scatters is you. You stop guarding your own person, throw the whole of yourself into the water the way someone dives in after the drowning, and your aim lands outside you, in the ones who need what you have. Regret comes from keeping a self safe. Give it to the work in front of you, and there is nothing to regret.

**Line 4** · _image:_ scattering your own flock, and a crowd gathering like a hill. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
You leave your own side, the group whose loyalty kept you small, and this is the great good fortune of the whole scene. Out of that leaving a gathering forms that nobody planned, people standing together in the open like a hill, more than any faction ever held. Scatter the small loyalty, and let the larger one gather round you.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a great cry that scatters like sweat, and the king in his place. → becomes Hexagram 4, Youthful Folly.
The word goes out of you the way sweat leaves the body, and like sweat it cannot be taken back. From the centre of things you give the whole of what you have, opening your own stores to the people, and there is no blame in it. Say the thing that cannot be unsaid, then stay where you are, in your place.

**Line 6** · _image:_ scattering the blood, going far away, and coming out. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
At the end of the scattering what goes is the wound itself. You put the hurt at a distance, walk away from the fear that went with it, and come out into the open without blame. Not every leaving is running away. Get the harm far from you, keep it there, and watch for the sweet words of anyone who wants you back inside it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dishonesty · _Gift:_ Intimacy · _Siddhi:_ Transparency

### Shadow — Dishonesty
Dishonesty here is not a lie you tell. It is the part of you kept back from everyone, so old that it came with you. The fear it guards is not in you but in the air between two people; alone, it is not there. That fear is also the pull: as long as something in you stays hidden there is something for the other to be wary of, and the wariness draws them, which is why the moment you stop being afraid of a person the pull goes slack. None of this was chosen. It is the source of every hidden agenda you have ever had.

What it costs is the world made lonely: rooms full of people, and almost nobody known. The dishonesty begins inward, in the story you tell yourself about why you did what you did, and the story always moves the blame outside you. Then you change the person, or the country, and the knot ties itself again, because it was never out there. Under it is old pain, stacked in the body, most of it handed down to you, and any part of you that does not own that pain is the dishonest part.

### Repressive nature — Excluded
This person leaves themselves out and feels left out. Being outside the door keeps their guard whole, so they stay there, blaming the room, drawing the very slight they dread. It becomes a state they can no longer put down, because to walk in would be to lose control.

### Reactive nature — Intrusive
Here is someone who pushes in. They come through the door uninvited, into another person's space, their feelings, and when they meet a wall they turn to rage, then exclude themselves. It is the same fear, loud. They cannot stay with anyone honest enough to refuse them.

### Gift — Intimacy
Intimacy begins by allowing the fear to exist. Admit that it is there between you, understand it, let it stay, and the allowing works like a valve: the whole force behind it comes through. It is not gentle. This is the raw power that makes new life, creative and destructive at once; what it destroys is the pattern that kept two people separate. Allowed all the way in, it opens you to a state above the one you were living in.

Two people met like this make a third thing between them, a field neither had alone, and the more unlike the two are, the further it can take them. What dawns in it is not in the belly, where the force started; it rises to the heart, and there two people meet inside one awareness. The nearest thing to it you may already know is falling in love, when you feel inside the other and feel them from far away. It is honesty balanced with kindness. You do not pour everything out; your heart decides how much to share, and it listens as much as it speaks.

### Siddhi — Transparency
Transparency is what remains when nothing in you is left to hide, because there is no separate one left to hide it. What was a self has gone back into the sea it came from. What stays is a clear mirror: life passes through it and it adds no comment, no judgement, none of the story you lay over what you see. Inside and outside were the last wall; when that goes, everything is within you.

From here everyone alive is your brother or your sister, and no one is an island. Two joined like this cannot be parted by distance, or by death. Fear turns out to have been a skin, and this is the far side of it. The one purpose this energy ever had was to break down every barrier to union, and when the last one is down it is no longer needed.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 59, Sexuality
The force that gets through to another person is this energy's pressure in the body. Gate 59 is where it enters: the drive to break the barrier between you and someone else, making closeness where there was none. At its root it is the drive to make new life, the force that carries a species on, and it is impersonal, choosing for you whom you are drawn to. Above that root it reaches for people, wanting to understand them and be understood, taking down the walls between them almost without trying.

The trouble built in is where and with whom. The same force that fills a house with children starts wars when nobody asked it first. From outside it looks like someone who cannot leave a boundary alone, or who uses closeness to get their way; what is going on is a reach that has not yet learned to wait to be met. People who carry this all their lives learn to be clear before they act, and that the same power that takes a wall down can be used to take a person, which is the one use of it that leaves you alone in the end.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The reach is made low in the body, under the navel, in the Sacral Centre, the engine that keeps a life going. This pressure is the engine's own fertility: the part of it that makes bonds and children, then raises what it made. It does not decide; it answers, before you have found a reason, and it answers only to the person actually in front of you. In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time. For them the drive toward another is steady, bountiful when it follows a true yes, wasted when spent on a no. What they are given is the nerve for a whole life of bonding: the partner, the house built together. What drains them is being taken, by someone who sees the energy and gets it out of them through guilt.

In the other three the engine is open. Someone like that takes in the drive of everyone near them, and what they do most easily is merge: they slip into another person's world, want what that person wants, lose themselves there without noticing. They do not know when enough is enough until the body says so. What they have instead is the pause, the ability to sit beside all that reaching and not be pulled in, which the seven cannot do for themselves.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mating (59–6)
A reach toward someone has nothing to work against on its own. Its other half is Gate 6, above the navel in the place feeling is made, the door of friction, which holds the boundary between what you feel and what another feels. Together they make the Channel of Mating: the drive to get through, and the wall of feeling it has to get through, so that closeness is made in fact rather than only wanted. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the wall becomes a door only where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
Your reach for another body is given to the sacral plexus with the organs it serves, and the reason is that this is where new life is made. Against the inner face of the bone at the base of your spine a weave of nerves gathers; out of it run the nerves to everything between your legs, carrying the feeling of the place and readying your body for another body without a thought from you. In the same low ground are the ovaries or the testes, the part of you that carries you on into someone who does not exist yet, and the old physicians stored in the same low ground what makes children.

Each person you have ever met is here because this place in two other people did its work, and so are you. This place is also the part of you least under your instruction: nothing in you here waits for your permission, and nothing here can be talked into readiness by the head. That is the part of you this energy is given to.

### Amino acid
The longing for another has a substance in you, and the substance is valine. Your body does not make it; it comes in with what you eat, more of it in a bowl of oats than in white rice, and when food is short your body can burn it as sugar. Three other things in you share it, and it is the same substance in all four. Between you and someone you love there is a chemistry that does its work in silence; this is part of what it is made of.

The substance runs the whole way through you: the forgiving of yourself, the goodness that shows once you have, the pouring of yourself out into others, then this, the last of the four, where the pouring has emptied you and there is no longer an inside to keep anything in.
