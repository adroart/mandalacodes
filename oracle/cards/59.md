# UL 59 — Mystics Treasures

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Reaching for Someone · Letting Someone In · Honest and Kind · Listening Closely · Holding Something Back · Letting Old Pain Go · Nothing Left to Hide

When someone comes close, a longing rises in you to be known by them. You feel it in your body first. You let them see you as you are. You say what is true, and your heart decides how much to share. You listen as closely as you speak.

Some days you pull back from someone and cannot say why. Turn toward the feeling instead of away from it. Most often it is old pain. Hold it the way you would hold a frightened child, and in time it lets go of you.

Be honest with your own heart, and you can be honest with anyone. Once you no longer need the other person to change, your heart stays open whatever they do. What you kept hidden, once opened, turns out to hold love. Near you, other people feel free to be honest too.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Water is the hexagram of a thaw between people, when what had set hard breaks up. Water is the feeling in you that has been held down with nowhere to go. Wind is the gentle pressure in you that finds its way into a closed place. The wind sits above because a feeling locked inside rarely frees itself, and something has to reach it. This hexagram has long been read as ice breaking up and melting. A warm wind over a frozen pond turns the ice back into water, while a cold one freezes it harder. What is warm in you frees what has gone cold between you and another person.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is how you reach a closed person without forcing anything. You keep coming back, mildly, until something gives. It sits on the outside because this is the part of you other people meet first. Wind has long been read as a breeze that goes into every gap. A breeze under a shut door fills the whole room without the door being opened. On its own, it can go further in than anyone asked it to.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is what you feel underneath and do not show. It runs down to the lowest place in you and gathers there. It sits below because it is the inside of the situation, the part nobody sees from the surface. Water has long been read as a gorge, a steep place where you can fall. A stream at the bottom of a gorge cannot climb out by itself. On its own, it sinks further in, and you sink with it.

### Reading
Something between you and other people has set hard, and now it is breaking up. It may be a suspicion, a grudge, or a group you have held on to out of habit. While it breaks up it can feel like a loss. It is a thaw.

What it asks first is warmth from inside you. Nobody else can warm you up. Whatever you send out carries your own warmth or your own cold.

This time has long been pictured as a king who goes to his temple when his people are scattered. When people have drifted away from you, do not try to drive them back. Be sincere where they can see it, and they come back on their own. Let go of what ought to go, and what matters can come together again. This is a good time for a large undertaking, like a boat on wide water with the wind behind it.

In the body, it is energy that was blocked inside you starting to move again. Stay warm, stay sincere, and be ready to cross.

### Judgement
- Dispersion. Success. The king comes to his temple.
- It is good to cross the great water. It is good to hold to what is right.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a strong horse, and a rescue while the trouble is small. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
The trouble has only just begun, and you cannot deal with it alone. Help is close by, strong as a horse. Let it carry you out of the water onto dry ground. Take the help now, while the trouble is still small.

**Line 2** · _image:_ running to what holds you up, in the middle of the stream. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
Things around you are coming apart, and you run toward what can hold you up. You leave what was harming you, the way a horse breaks out of its stall. In the middle of the stream you find something steady to lean on. Go to what is reliable, and your regrets fall away.

**Line 3** · _image:_ letting go of yourself, with your aim set outside you. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle Wind.
Here what you let go of is your own interest. You throw yourself in, the way someone dives in after a person who is drowning. Your aim is outside you, with the ones who need you. Put them first, and there is nothing to regret.

**Line 4** · _image:_ leaving your own group, and a crowd gathering like a hill. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
You step away from the group you took sides with. Out of that leaving comes a larger gathering, people standing together like a hill. Nobody would have expected it. Let go of the small loyalty, and a larger one forms around you.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a great cry that goes out like sweat, and the king in his place. → becomes Hexagram 4, Youthful Folly.
You speak from the heart of things, and like sweat your words cannot be taken back. You open your own stores to the people around you. There is no fault in giving like this. Say what cannot be unsaid, then stay in your place.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the wound put far away, and walking out. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
At the end, what you let go of is the hurt itself. You put the harm at a distance and walk away from the fear that came with it. There is no fault in leaving like this. Keep the harm far from you, and be wary of anyone who flatters you back toward it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dishonesty · _Gift:_ Intimacy · _Siddhi:_ Transparency

### Shadow — Dishonesty
A part of you stays hidden from other people, and it came with you. When someone walks into the room, a faint fear starts up in the air between you. Alone, you do not feel it. As long as you keep something back, the other person has something to fear. That fear is also what draws two people together, and once you stop fearing someone the pull goes. Most of this is not a lie you choose to tell.

It starts inside, in the story you tell yourself about why you did what you did. The story puts the blame on someone else. So you change your partner or move to another country, and the same knot ties itself again. Under it is old pain, stacked in the body, much of it handed down to you. This is what makes a room full of people feel lonely.

### Repressive nature — Excluded
This person leaves themselves out, then feels left out. Staying outside keeps their defences in place, and the feeling becomes a habit they cannot put down. It lets them feel in control. The moment they choose to include themselves, it falls apart.

### Reactive nature — Intrusive
Here the same fear comes out as anger. They push their way into another person's space and feelings. When they are turned away, they storm off. To avoid being rejected, they try to run the relationship, so they never stay with anyone honest enough to stand up to them.

### Gift — Intimacy
Intimacy starts when you let the fear between you be there. You admit it, understand it and leave it alone. That allowing works like a valve, and the full force of attraction pours out. It is not soft. It is the raw power that makes new life, and it breaks up whatever kept two people apart.

When two people meet like this, something forms between them that neither had alone. The more different the two of you are, the further it can take you. The force rises until it reaches the heart, where two people share one awareness. The nearest most people come to it is falling in love, when you feel the other person even from far away.

### Siddhi — Transparency
Transparency is what is left when there is nothing in you to hide. You see the world the way a clear mirror does, adding no comment and no judgement. The last wall to go is the one between you and everyone else, and then nobody feels like a stranger.

Every man and woman is your brother or sister, and no one is an island. Two people joined this way are not parted by distance, or even by death. Fear turns out to have been a thin skin you had to pass through.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 59, Sexuality
Gate 59 is the drive to break through a barrier and get close to someone. At its root it is the urge to make new life. It chooses whom you are drawn to without asking you. Beyond that root it reaches for people of every kind, wanting to understand them and be understood.

Its question is where, and with whom. The same force can make a partnership or a business thrive, or start a pregnancy nobody wanted. The strength that takes a wall down can also be used to push past someone's limits or to get your way. So be clear before you act on it.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The Sacral Center sits low in the body, under the navel, and it is the strongest engine you have. It is the energy for making things, keeping a house, raising a child and sex. It does not decide. It answers, with a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat, and only to what is actually in front of you.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time. For them the pull toward another person is steady, and it grows when they follow a real yes. It drains away when they give it where there was no yes, or when others get it out of them through guilt.

In the other three the engine is open. They take in the energy of the people around them, and they can merge into another person's world without noticing. They do not know when enough is enough until the body gives out. Their gift is the pause, knowing when to stop.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mating (59–6)
Gate 6 sits in the Solar Plexus Center, where feelings and desires rise, and it guards the edge of what you feel. With Gate 59 it makes the Channel of Mating: the drive to get close meets the feeling that decides whether to let it in. Tension often comes before closeness here, and it is part of the process. Gate 6 may be in you or in a person close to you, and a bond is made only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
New life is the reason this energy is given to the sacral plexus and the sexual organs. The sacral plexus is a weave of nerves low in your pelvis. Its nerves carry the feeling between your legs and ready your body for another body without a thought from you. Close by lie the ovaries or the testes, where the start of a new person is made.

Everyone you have ever met is here because this place did its work in two other people, and so are you. It is also the part of you least under your control. Nothing here waits for your permission, and nothing here can be argued into readiness.

### Amino acid
The longing for union with another has a substance in you, and the substance is valine. Your body cannot make it, so it comes from food, more from a bowl of oats than from white rice. When food runs short, your body can turn it into sugar.

You share this substance with three other energies, and together they have long shaped how people are with each other. In you it runs from forgiving yourself, to the goodness that follows, to pouring yourself out for others. Then it comes to this closeness, where there is no inside left to keep anything in.

## RELATIONS

_Each card here is about how you come together with other people: what gives your closeness a shape, what meets it, and the family it belongs to._

### Pair — UL 60, Woven Light (Limitation)
This energy opens you up and takes down what stands between you and another person, and on its own it keeps spreading. Woven Light is where it goes next: the limit that says this far and no further, and gives a thing its shape. Open it when everything has been opened and nothing yet holds. What has been opened needs an edge to last.

### Inverse — UL 60, Woven Light (Limitation)
Seen from the other side, the opening in you becomes a place to stop. Woven Light is that stop, the joint in a bamboo stalk that divides it and holds it together at once. Open it when you have given yourself away and need to find where you end.

### Programming partner — UL 55, Untouched Perfection
This energy reaches out toward another person, and reaching is half of what makes something new. Untouched Perfection holds what completes it. This one is the seed, that one the egg. Where this energy changes how people come together, that one frees each person from the inside. Open it when the closeness you want starts to feel like a trap.

### Codon ring — Ring of Union
This energy takes down the barriers between people, and its family turns on it. It belongs to a family of four called the Ring of Union, whose aim is to bring people together as one family. UL 4, Veils of Knowledge, carries the understanding that lets you stop blaming. UL 7, Essential Nexus, carries the guidance that comes when you are truly together. UL 29, All In, carries the commitment that holds nothing back. This one carries your closeness to one other person.

### Tarot
Four of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, arrive through the ring and the two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lovers is the choice two people make to join, which is what this energy reaches for.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel is the turning point, the moment something stuck between you starts to move.
- **Wind (upper) — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death is a change of form, the old way of keeping yourself apart ending so a new one can begin.
- **Water (lower) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess listens before she speaks, which is how closeness is kept kind.

### Immortals
Wind above brings He Xian Gu, who holds a lotus: the part of you that is open with nothing to hide. Water below brings Li Tie Guai and his gourd of medicine: the old pain in you that carries a cure. Closeness asks you for both.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Gemini:** the twins, the part of you that wants another person beside you.
- **Hebrew letter — Zayin, the Sword:** the blade that divides, so you can see what is real between you and another before you join.
