# UL 29 — All In

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The Whole Yes · A Hand Kept on the Door · Flying Blind · Warmth Low in the Body · The Hard Middle · Knowing When It Is Over · Nothing Wanted Back

Everything you have, given to one thing before you can see where it leads: that is this energy, the whole yes. In you it is the moment a decision stops being weighed and becomes a road you are already on. It rises low, below thinking, as a quiet warmth that moves through the whole of you without arguing. Once it has risen the giving is no longer an effort; it carries you. In a life this is the work or the person you have gone all the way into.

Carried well, it is honesty first. You cannot know where a decision goes, nobody can, and the only thing that travels with you is how whole you were when you began. A thing begun whole makes its own luck; a thing begun with one hand on the door makes the other kind. The yes has a length of its own, a night or a life, and when it is over you feel that where you felt it begin. Your part is to stay through the hard middle, where it is tested, then to leave cleanly when it is done, rather than a little before or long after.

The way is to hold nothing back. Keep one part of yourself out of the thing, for safety, and the yes is not a little smaller; it is gone. Most of what you keep back is fear that was never looked at. Look at it once, then give the rest.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Water: a river through a gorge, and under it the gorge again, one drop below another. The water falls into the first pit, fills it to the brim, spills into the next, and none of the falling changes what it is. The traditional character shows earth opened into a hollow; the word set beside it, to repeat, shows a young bird beating its wings until they hold.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the river, and what a river does is go down. It finds the lowest place, takes the shape of whatever holds it, fills it, moves on, and through all of that it is still water. In a family it is the middle son, who went down into the things the others walked round. In you it is the part that goes into a feeling instead of past it: you sink, you do not skim. Above, it is the drop you can see coming. Its risk is the heart that aches, because a thing that only ever goes down can go past where anyone can reach it.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Underneath is water again, the river already in the pit, the danger you carry with you. Its picture is one firm line held between two soft ones: a strength nobody sees, inside a giving way that everybody does. In the world it is the deep pool under a fall, dark and still while the surface churns. In you it is the firmness you keep in the middle of yourself when everything round it gives, the courage that lives inside fear rather than instead of it. Its risk is stagnation. Water that stops moving in a hollow goes bad, and a feeling sat in too long does the same.

### Reading
You are in danger, and it is not the kind that comes once. There is a drop below you, and under that drop another, so the comfort that hard times pass does not hold here. What holds is that the same danger comes back until you have learned it, the way a young bird falls until its wings carry. What it asks is the way of water. Water does not shrink from any fall. It goes down, fills the low place to the brim, and when the place is full it goes on, still water after every pit, having lost nothing of itself. Go all the way into the thing, and keep to what you are.

What carries you through is the firm line in the middle, the heart kept true while everything round it gives way. Held with a true heart, even this goes through, and what you do from it has worth. Danger is not only what you fall into; it is what keeps a city safe, the mountain no army crosses, the river at the wall, and there is a time when it is the thing to use. Keep to the ones who bring what they have plainly, and let the grand rescuers pass.

In the body this energy is the ear, the one part of you that cannot be shut: it hears in the dark and it hears in your sleep. Notice what you are listening for now. Sit low, as water sits, and listen for the way through rather than looking for it.

### Judgement

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the pit repeated, and a hole in the floor of the pit. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
Already low, you go lower, into a hole in the floor of the pit, and that is what habit does: a wrong step, seen to be wrong, taken again the next day. Losing the way at the start is innocent; keeping to it after you have seen where it goes is not. Find the line you keep crossing, and stop there.

**Line 2** · _image:_ danger in the pit, and small things sought and gained. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
Danger all round, and you in the middle of it, firm, not sunk far. There is no way out yet, and the grand attempt would only take you deeper. Ask for a small thing, one foothold, one honest gain, and you will have it. It is not freedom, only enough to stop the sinking, and the way out is made of steps that size.

**Line 3** · _image:_ coming or going, a pit either way, and a hole at the bottom. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
Forward there is a pit, back there is a pit, and every move you make digs the one you are in. The trouble is not the direction; it is the moving. Nothing you attempt here comes to anything, so attempt nothing. Wait where you are, still, while the water settles, because the time to act will come and this is not it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a jug of wine and a bowl of rice in plain clay, handed in through the window. → becomes Hexagram 47, Oppression.
One jug of wine and a bowl of rice, in plain clay, handed in through the window rather than carried in at the door. At the hardest pressure the offering is stripped to what is honest and small. It is enough. Bring what you have, simply, to the one who can help, and let it in by whatever opening there is; in the end there is no blame.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the pit not full, the water level with the rim. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
The water has risen to the rim of the pit, level, not spilling, and level is also the word for peace. You are on the edge of getting out, and the ground is being made even under you. Do not fill it higher, and do not reach yet for the great thing. Stay level, and what you were sinking in becomes what carries you out.

**Line 6** · _image:_ bound with cords, set among thorns, and three years without the way. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
Tied with cords, set down among the thorns, and for three years no way out is found. The cords are your own, wound round you by a habit you kept because you could not see it, and the thorns grow the longer you sit. Three years is what refusing costs. Ask the one who has found the way out to show it to you, and begin.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Half-Heartedness · _Gift:_ Commitment · _Siddhi:_ Devotion

### Shadow — Half-Heartedness
Half-heartedness is a yes with something kept back, and from inside it feels like good sense. You go into the work or the person, and a part of you stays outside it, in case. That is not caution. It is a dishonesty practised on yourself, and a life that is being lied to withdraws. The days go dull or go frantic, and you stand at the side of your own life, watching. It hides in postponing. You have a picture of how things should go, life fails to match it, and you take yourself out of the present to run ahead in your head, missing the only moment you were ever in. It hides in quitting at the first hard weather, and in not knowing when to stop a thing that hurts. Both come from a fear you never went into. Break off a cycle before its end and life builds the same one again, from different people, until you finish it. Look back and you can see them, the same trouble wearing different faces, each one left before it was done.

### Repressive nature — Over-Committing
This person keeps every promise long after the thing it was made for has ended. Unable to tell when a cycle is over, they take on more than they can carry and call the tiredness loyalty. Not daring to say a thing should end, they let it use them, and slowly they belong to it.

### Reactive nature — Unreliable
Here is someone who says yes to everything and delivers on little. The yes is loud, the plan talked up, and when the weight lands they are gone, with shame following after. What sets them off is being expected, and they answer it by promising more.

### Gift — Commitment
Commitment, when it is real, feels nothing like excitement. It is not nervous and it does not shout. A clear decision settles in you the way a river settles into its bed. From then on you do not have to think about the goal, because the decision has the goal inside it, and only time will show where the river goes. What matters is not arriving but following it to its end. Cycles run inside cycles, some five minutes long, some a lifetime, and a life is the shape of a million small ones, so the same wholeness goes into washing a dish as into a night of love. A commitment like this lasts as long as it lasts. When it is over, both of you feel it at the same time, and what began clean ends clean. The length has nothing to do with success.

Nobody can give you the yes, no teacher, no system; it comes up from the belly and does not argue. Held whole, it rides out any storm, because it was never about the other person. It was about leaping with your eyes open.

### Siddhi — Devotion
Devotion is commitment with no one left holding it. You were always devoted to something; everyone is, the mother to her child, even the sad to their sadness, and this is that same pouring with the object gone. First it fixes on one thing, a face or a cause, and worships it without caution; people call that blind. Then the love poured out begins pouring back, from everything, until the rocks and the trees seem to bleed it toward you. There is no worshipper left, only what was loved.

Whoever comes near this catches it. It is nearly impossible to say no to someone like this, and they care nothing for the rules, only for the heart, which knows none. To be devoted is to lie forever in the lap of what you love.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 29, Perseverance
The pull to say yes and stay is this energy's pressure in the body. Gate 29 is the door it comes through, the drive that gives the whole of you to a thing and keeps on when the first excitement is gone. It says yes easily. Every yes you give has weight, every no as well, because each one sets the shape of the years after it. When the yes was the body's, you are the one still there when the others have left, and people build on you.

The trouble built in is the same drive saying yes to too much, or to the wrong thing out of a fear of saying no, so that you are held to a promise you had no strength for, and devotion turns into obligation. From outside it looks like stubbornness, or like someone who cannot plan past tonight; what is going on is that the yes came before the seeing, and you trusted it. People who carry this all their lives learn that the yes they can keep is the one that came from below the neck, and that the way to keep it is to give fewer of them.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
Low in the belly, under the navel, is the engine this yes comes out of, the Sacral Centre, the strongest engine you have. It does not decide. It answers, a yes or a no, made before you have a reason, and only to what has arrived: it cannot answer a thing that has not come, nor a question asked in your head. The one marriage this place asks of you is to its own yes and no. Wait for it to arrive, know your answer, honour it.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time, and the answer is the most reliable thing they have. Acted on, a yes does not run out, because the energy is renewed by being spent. What starves them is a yes talked out of them, the head listing reasons until the truth is abandoned. In the other three the engine is open: it runs on whoever is near, takes in their energy, outworks any of them for a while, then cannot keep it up. Someone like that can learn yeses and noes that were never theirs, then wear out keeping them. Theirs is the pause, the rest between the notes. Every drive seated here passes through the first excitement, the jump in and the plateau; whether you are finished there or only paused is a question this place can answer, if it is asked as a yes or a no.

### What completes it — the Channel of Discovery (29–46)
A yes with nothing to land in is a leap toward no ground. Its other half is Gate 46, in the centre of the chest, the love of being in a body, which knows where you should be and when. Together they make the Channel of Discovery: the yes given whole, and the body's own sense of the right place at the right hour to give it, so that the leap comes down somewhere. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the finding out only happens where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
Your body's yes is kept in the sacral plexus, a weave of nerves on the inner face of the bone at the base of your spine, and the reason is that it answers before you do. The engine that does your work is seated here, and it does not decide. Something is put in front of you, a sound comes up before a thought, and that sound is the only yes your body keeps its word to. Say yes from the head to a thing this place had no yes for, and you pay for it here; it is your body that gives out, not your plan.

Stand up from your chair. The order to the great muscle at the back of your hip, and to the ones that kept you level as the weight moved, went out from this weave before you had finished deciding to rise. Try to catch the moment you chose. You cannot, and that is the part of you this energy is given to: the whole of you going, with the deciding left behind.

### Amino acid
The yes you give whole has a substance in you, and the substance is valine. Your body cannot make it; it arrives in meat or lentils, or not at all, and turned from the water, it is folded into the inside of every protein you build. The part of you kept back is made of the same substance. What you keep from someone you love is kept below words, in your chemistry, out of fear. Down there a yes that keeps back one part in a hundred is the same as one that keeps back all but one.

Three other things in you share this substance; it is the same in all four. In you it is the yes said to life itself, with no promise about where it goes, since the only promise your body can keep is to what it is being led to now.
