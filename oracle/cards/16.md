# UL 16 — Grand Rising

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Excited to Begin · Starting Before You Feel Ready · Learning as You Go · Plans Left on the Table · Years of Practice · Good at Many Things · Living Well

Some mornings you wake up wanting to get to the thing you love. That is enthusiasm, and it says yes before you have thought it through. You begin before you feel ready, and you learn as you go. When you talk about it your voice lifts, and the people around you catch it.

There are days when nothing seems worth getting up for, and your plans stay on the table. It feels like tiredness, and under it is a fear that caring will swamp you. Your heart cannot be overwhelmed; it only opens wider. Go and do one real thing, and the caring comes back with it.

Begin on the thing you most enjoy, and a surge of energy comes up in you. You stop bending your path to suit other people. Nobody else can take this first step for you. Once you are in it, your days fill with purpose and you are glad to get up.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Earth is the hexagram of getting ready and then moving. Thunder is the excitement in you that gets you on your feet. Earth is the quiet part of you that rests and makes ready. The excitement sits on top because it comes up out of what was stored below. This hexagram has long been read as thunder rising out of the ground in spring, waking a whole field. Your excitement wakes a room the same way. Its name has been read both as delight and as getting ready, and your delight lasts when you have prepared. When you are ready, your joy carries others with it.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the jolt that starts you moving. It sits on top because it acts on the outside, where other people can see and hear it. Thunder has long been read as a chick breaking out of its egg, or a bud out of the branch. Your first real step on something you love breaks out the same way. On its own, it is all start and no follow-through.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the part of you that rests and gets ready. It lies underneath because it is the inside of the situation, what you laid in before anyone saw you move. Earth has long been read as the ground, which carries everything and feeds everything. Sleep, food and what you have already learned are ground of this kind under your day. On its own, the rest runs past its time and becomes the reason you never start.

### Reading
You have made ready, and the time to move has come. What you prepared in quiet is ready to come out, and the people around you are ready to follow. It is a good time to bring helpers in and set out together.

It asks you to prepare, then go without hesitating. Study the target before you let the arrow go, and once you have seen it, do not wait out the day. Move with the time, the way the sun and the moon keep their course and the seasons come when they should. Enjoy what comes to you, and keep some of it back for what comes after. The danger is on the far side of the joy, when pleasure turns into laziness and you forget the people who came with you.

Your feet are where the body feels this. Notice whether your weight has already shifted forward. Stand as you do just before you step off, ready and at ease.

### Judgement
- Enthusiasm. It furthers you to set helpers in place and to set the armies marching.

### Image
- Thunder comes resounding out of the earth: the image of enthusiasm.
- So the kings of old made music to honour what was done well, offered it up with splendour, and invited the ancestors in.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ telling everyone how pleased you are. → becomes Hexagram 51, The Arousing.
You tell everyone how pleased you are with yourself. Joy told too soon is joy spent, and the more you tell it, the less of it you feel. Your will runs out soon after the telling. Keep your delight to yourself a while longer, and it stays yours.

**Line 2** · _image:_ firm as a rock, not waiting out the day. → becomes Hexagram 40, Deliverance.
You stay steady as a rock while everyone around you is carried away. You want nothing from the excitement, so you see what is coming before it arrives. When a pleasure turns wrong, you let it go the same day. Stay that steady, and it goes well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ looking up wide-eyed at someone else's joy. → becomes Hexagram 62, Small Preponderance.
You look up at someone else's joy and wait for some of it to reach you. What you hope to get from them, you lose in yourself while you wait. The longer you wait, the more you will regret it. Turn back to your own joy quickly.

**Line 4** · _image:_ friends gathering like hair into a clasp. → becomes Hexagram 2, The Receptive.
The joy comes from you, and great things come of it. People who were scattered gather around you, the way loose hair is gathered into one clasp. Only doubt could spoil this, yours about them or theirs about you. Do not doubt, and they stay.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a long illness that does not kill. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
Something has troubled you for a long time, and it will not kill you. You cannot enjoy what others enjoy, so you keep to yourself. You stay upright because you hold to the middle of your life. That is better than chasing joy outside yourself. Let the old trouble be what drives you on.

**Line 6** · _image:_ joy that has gone dark. → becomes Hexagram 35, Progress.
You have gone on into the pleasure past the point where you can see clearly. When joy goes on past its peak, sadness follows by itself. You still have time. Change your course now, even this late, and there is no blame.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Indifference · _Gift:_ Versatility · _Siddhi:_ Mastery

### Shadow — Indifference
Not caring feels like nothing much, and under it is fear. You live in the comfortable part of your life, the emails and the job, and your attention stays on what does not matter. You are full of plans, and they rarely get off the ground. The book or the business waits for a day with more time, though time is not the reason.

The instrument you were born to play sits in the corner. Picking it up would make you different from everyone who is not bothering either, and there is safety in numbers. You can build a clever life out of skills and methods while it stays there. Skill of that kind makes an expert. It never makes a master.

### Repressive nature — Gullible
Held down, not caring turns into believing what you are told. If governments cannot fix the world, they ask, how could one person? They take the fear on the news as the truth, and they hide their own not caring behind everyone else's. Their head goes into the sand, sure that nothing can be done.

### Reactive nature — Self-Deluded
Pushed out, the fear hides inside systems and methods. They follow their own structures so closely that they forget why they built them, and the structures become a wall. They believe their own account of things completely. Behind the wall is a great anger, and it will bring them down unless they see through it.

### Gift — Versatility
Versatility begins with caring about one thing enough to lose yourself in it. You pick up the instrument and give it years. It takes about seven for a skill to be written into your body, so that your hands know it without you. By then the skill has become a talent, and you no longer have to think about it.

Once you know one thing through and through, you find you can pick up almost anything. The same pattern runs under every art and every science, and that pattern is what you learned. The discipline that looked hard from outside is easy once your heart is warm. You move from painting to business with the same ease, and you waste nothing. You spot a child's talent early and put it where it can grow.

### Siddhi — Mastery
Mastery is living well, and it has stopped being about skill. For years your heart opened, closed and opened again, and now it no longer needs to close. The person it happens to is ordinary. They are cheerful without pretending, they give because they want to, and they pass almost unnoticed. They may not be rich, and you can still see that they are prospering.

Here all learning ends, and there are no more levels to climb. The knowledge of how to play the hardest music is already in every person alive. At this height your enthusiasm is what the word first meant, to be filled with the breath of God. Nobody near you can stay indifferent to it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 16, Zest
Gate 16 is the pull to leap at something you love and get good at it. It says yes before the details are in, dives to the bottom of a subject and wants to share what it can do. It masters by repetition, the way an instrument is learned, one pattern played until it is yours. Your passion for it tends to spread to whoever is listening.

When it is off, it presses in one of two ways. You may hold a skill back for years because it is not perfect yet. Or a leap that went wrong may have burned you, until your zest feels dangerous and you take no risks at all. Leaping with no map and no food makes you look unreliable. Your enthusiasm needs something to carry it, so pack what you need, then jump.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center is where what is inside you becomes a word or a deed. Nearly all the energy in you heads here, so it works like a town square. Whatever reaches it comes out of you as words or action. Gate 16 puts your skill where other people can see it.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their voice is steady and stays in one register. It goes out rather than taking in, and it can sound blunt. They say what they know the same way to everyone. The other three in ten have it open, and their voice changes with whoever is in the room. They can meet each person in that person's own tone. For many of them the trouble starts in childhood, when nobody listens, and they learn to go quiet or to get louder. They feel the pressure to speak with nothing to say, and their own words come when someone asks.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Wavelength (16–48)
Skill on its own stays on the surface. Gate 48 sits in the Spleen Center, the part of you that senses whether you are safe. It holds depth, and the fear of not being good enough. Together they make the Channel of the Wavelength, a talent built over years by repeating and improving one pattern. The fear sends you deeper instead of stopping you. Gate 48 may be in you or in someone close by, and the talent sounds when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The parathyroid holds this energy in the body, and the reason is the store. These are four glands the size of grains of rice, tucked behind the thyroid, and together they weigh less than a paperclip. Every muscle that closes and every beat of your heart runs on calcium, and these four keep its level in your blood steady. When a meal falls short, they draw on a store you laid down over years, your bones. When the meal is enough, the bones are built back. A move that comes easily to you is drawing on years.

You cannot feel the glands, only find the place. Put a fingertip in the notch at the top of your breastbone, then move a thumb's width up, either side of the windpipe. Now close your hand into a fist. Every muscle in that fist closes on calcium those four grains keep level.

### Amino acid
Enthusiasm has a chemistry in you, and one substance in it is cysteine. Your body makes it for itself, but only from a piece it has to be fed. That piece comes from meat, eggs or beans. It is one of two pieces in you that carry sulphur, the smell of burning hair.

This chemistry belongs to finding the one thing you do better than anything else, and to the risk of being different that finding it takes. When you stop spending yourself on what does not matter, your body has more for that one thing. The further you go into it, the more the world comes to meet you halfway. You share this chemistry with the energy that gathers people, so your talent does best among others.

## RELATIONS

_UL 15 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: strength kept low and quiet, where this one rises and gets things moving._

### Pair — UL 15, Ordinary Valiance (Modesty)
This energy rises and gets things moving, and it runs out when the excitement turns into showing off. Ordinary Valiance is where it goes next: the magnetism that grows as you slow down. Open it when your excitement has become the loudest thing in the room. Delight lasts when it stays humble.

### Inverse — UL 15, Ordinary Valiance (Modesty)
Turn this energy upside down and rising becomes bowing low. Ordinary Valiance keeps its strength quiet, where this energy lets it out. Open it when you have talked enough about what you love and it is time to simply do it.

### Programming partner — UL 9, Ease in This
This energy leaps at what it loves. Ease in This holds what it lacks: small steps taken one after another, day after day. Without the steps the leap gets nowhere, and without the leap the steps go cold. Open it when the excitement is high and nothing has been done yet.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Prosperity
This energy finds the one thing you do best, and it cannot prosper alone. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of Prosperity. UL 45, Tribal Tapestry, gathers people around what there is and shares it out. This one brings the talent. Real wealth comes when people follow their own gift and work together.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards come with this energy, and each is a stage of what it does in your life.

- **Ring Arcana — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World is a long piece of work brought to its finish, the mastery your years of practice grow into.
- **Thunder (upper) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Tower is the sudden jolt that breaks open a life that had grown too comfortable.
- **Thunder (upper) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): Judgement is the call that wakes you, and you get up to answer it.
- **Earth (lower) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil is the comfort that holds you in place, the pull toward what does not matter.
- **Earth (lower) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World comes again through the ground, the finish that everything you prepared makes possible.

### Immortals
Thunder above carries Cao Guo Jiu, a nobleman's son who gave up his rank to wander. Earth below carries Lan Cai He, a street singer with a basket of flowers who had no rank at all. Both chose their lives by what they loved. Your enthusiasm asks the same of you, to follow what you love over what you were handed.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Saturn:** the slow planet of time and form, the discipline that lets your enthusiasm last for years.
- **Hebrew letter — Tav, the Cross:** the last letter of the alphabet, the mark you put on a long piece of work when it is finished.
