# UL 60 — Woven Light

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Common Sense · Knowing What Things Cost · Limits That Help · A Plan That Bends · Keeping What Still Works · A Rule Kept Too Long · Knowing When to Step Back

You have an idea you want to make real, and it needs a shape before it can stand. Common sense is what finds that shape. You know what a thing will cost, how long it will take and how much one day can hold. You set the edges first and build inside them. People come to you when they want something to actually get done.

Some days you are pulled two ways, toward the next big reach and back toward what feels safe. Go too far toward the reach and you burn out. Go too far toward safety and you shrink. You need the structure, and you need to be free of it at the same time.

So build what the idea needs, and hold the frame lightly. Make it the kind that bends when the ground moves under it. Then what you imagined has a place in the world, and it lasts.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Lake is the hexagram of how much you can hold. Lake is the part of you that is content inside its own shore. Water is everything that keeps pouring in, the work and the money to spend. The flow sits above because more always arrives than any one place can keep. This hexagram has long been read as the joints of a bamboo stalk. Each joint ends one length and starts the next, which is how the cane grows so tall. The year has joints too, which is why it turns through four seasons. You grow the same way, one measured length at a time.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the demand that keeps coming at you from outside, more than you planned for. It sits on top because this pressure comes from the world around you, and the world is where the test is. Water has long been read as a stream running down into a gorge. A stream fills whatever low ground it finds, the way one request can fill a free evening. On its own, it floods your days until nothing in them keeps its shape.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the calm in you that stays pleased even while things press in. It lies underneath because being at ease inside is what lets you meet a hard time. Lake has long been read as still water gathered in a hollow of the ground. It takes in what reaches it and settles, the way you settle after a hard day. On its own, it takes in more than its shore can hold and spills over.

### Reading
More is coming in than you can hold, and it needs a limit before it can be of use. A lake must hold its water without spilling over or drying out. So its level is checked all the time. Set a good limit, and things go well. What you set now is how much you will spend, how far you will go and what you will not do.

Then weigh what you do against those measures. A good measure harms nothing you own and hurts no one near you. Keep your measures sweet, and they will feel like your own. A rule that has turned bitter cannot be kept for long, and holding on to it only leads you to a dead end.

The body knows this in its joints. A knee or a knuckle is where one bone stops and the next begins, and that is why a limb can move at all. Sit easy, with every joint loose.

### Judgement
- Limitation. Success.
- A limit that has turned bitter cannot be kept.

### Image

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ not going out of the door and the courtyard. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
You stay inside your own door, and there is no fault in it. You have the strength to go, but the way ahead is blocked for now. Knowing when a road is open and when it is closed is the whole skill here. Stay quiet for now, since care at the start leaves you no regret at the end.

**Line 2** · _image:_ not going out of the gate and the courtyard, when the gate stands open. → becomes Hexagram 3, Difficulty at the Beginning.
You stay inside the gate after it has opened. The moment to act has come, and you are letting it pass. Waiting is watching for the right time, and hesitating is letting it go by. Know your limits, but do not mistake them for doubt about yourself. Go out through the gate while it stands open.

**Line 3** · _image:_ no limit at all, and then the sigh. → becomes Hexagram 5, Waiting.
You did as you pleased with no limit, and now you sigh over it. Nobody else is to blame. The harm is small this time, and it is your own. Decide where your line is before you set out, and keep to it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ peaceful limitation, the measure kept without effort. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
You keep your limits at peace, without strain. You took them from someone steadier and made them your own. Now they come without effort. Work inside what is fixed, and inside it there is no end to what you can do.

**Line 5** · _image:_ sweet limitation, from the middle place. → becomes Hexagram 19, Approach.
You keep your measure so well that it tastes sweet. You take neither too much nor too little, and you stay content even when things are hard. Others cannot be forced to keep a limit, but they follow one they see kept. Go on, and people will respect you for it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ bitter limitation, held to past its use. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
You hold yourself to a rule that has gone bitter. You can hold back, but you can no longer reach out. Even if the rule is right, keeping it brings harm. This is still a smaller fault than having no rule at all, so it can be mended. Let the rule go, and the regret goes with it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Limitation · _Gift:_ Realism · _Siddhi:_ Justice

### Shadow — Limitation
Closed thinking is the low of this energy. Your mind goes round the same track as yesterday, about your partner or your money. Nothing new gets in, because you keep no door open for it. Out in the world it is red tape, rules so tangled they no longer protect anyone. It is a child kept at a desk when every part of them wants to run. It cannot bear anything new or young.

Under it is a lack of trust. Life puts itself right in its own time, and this part of you cannot believe that. So it holds everything still. It will not move for force. It waits for one thing, which is time.

### Repressive nature — Unstructured
Held down, this energy leaves a life with no frame at all. Nothing they start lasts, and they keep changing where they live and what they do. Commitment frightens them, because it is a structure. So their gifts miss the people and the place they need to grow.

### Reactive nature — Rigid
Let out in anger, it grips hard. Nobody may question what they say or do, and anyone who crosses them meets their temper. Every new idea feels like a threat to their safety. They hold the form so tight that its purpose is lost, and unless they let go, they shrivel.

### Gift — Realism
Realism is knowing how a new idea becomes a real thing. A good idea will not arrive in the world on its own. It needs a shell, the way a seed does, or it never takes root. So you graft the new shoot onto an old, strong root, and change grows out of what already stands. All a new thing needs is some structure and an open mind.

You know that every structure you build will end one day, and you can smile about it. Knowing this keeps you from being trapped inside what you built. So you set up the easel or open the shop, and you begin from the heart. Something breaks through in the gap between what you meant and what you made. The best of this gift is knowing when to step back and let the thing go.

### Siddhi — Justice
Justice here is a gift given, never a punishment. A man once stole the silver from the one house that had taken him in. Caught and brought back, he heard the owner say the silver had been a present. Being forgiven by someone you wronged cracks you open. Life evens the balance, and nobody has to punish anyone.

What drops away at this height is weight. Let go of your past, and you become light, in both senses of the word.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 60, Conservation
Gate 60 is the pressure to bring something new into form within set limits. Its name here is Conservation, which means keeping what still works. In a time of change or loss, it keeps your eyes on what is still standing rather than on what fell. That is what lets you rebuild. Its gift is a lotus that grows up out of the mud. When nothing shows, you can feel that something is wrong with you. Nothing is wrong, since the seed is growing under the ground where you cannot see it.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center sits at the base of the body and runs on adrenaline. It is one of four motors in the body, but it does not run steadily. It pulses on and off, so everything it does depends on timing. Of the paths it drives, this is the one that brings sudden change into your life.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They can bear pressure and aim it. When the pulse is off, it feels as if the rug has been pulled out from under them. They cannot make themselves do what they know they need to do, sometimes for a month. The other four in ten have it open. They take in the pressure of everyone around them and try to get rid of it by doing. Starting things is hard for them. Their gift is knowing which pressures are really theirs to act on.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mutation (3–60)
Gate 3, low in the belly, is the drive to bring order out of a mess. Joined to Gate 60, it makes the Channel of Mutation. Its energy comes in bursts you cannot predict, each one trying to bring something new to life. It is change by growth, never by overthrow. Gate 3 may be in you or in someone close to you, and the new thing takes form when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The banks are the reason this energy is given to the colon. A river without banks is a flood, and the colon is the bank inside you. About a litre and a half of liquid reaches it each day. It takes all but a cupful of the water back into your blood, so what arrived loose leaves with a shape. It holds what it has for a day or two. Then it moves in a few long pushes, most often after the first meal of the day. Only at the very end does one muscle wait for your decision.

Lie on your back and press in low on the left, below the navel. When it is full, you may feel a firm rope under your fingers, the last bend. That is the part of you that gives the day's leftovers a shape.

### Amino acid
The weight you carry has a chemistry, and one substance in it is isoleucine. Your body cannot make it, so it comes from meat, cheese or beans. This energy is the earth at its most solid, the plain weight of things. Your muscles take the substance apart themselves, in the part of you that works against the pull of the ground.

You share it with two other energies. One of them is the moment a thought breaks out of its old track. So what keeps you steady and what sets your mind free share one chemistry.

## RELATIONS

_UL 59 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the water let out of the lake, where this one holds it._

### Pair — UL 59, Mystics Treasures (Dispersion)
This energy holds things in a shape, and it runs out when the shape starts to shut people out. Mystics Treasures is where it goes next: the longing that gets through the walls between two people. Open it when your edges have done their work and something needs to loosen. Nothing stays gathered forever.

### Inverse — UL 59, Mystics Treasures (Dispersion)
Turn this energy end for end and the holding becomes a letting go. Mystics Treasures is the same water, let out of the lake. Open it when what you built has started to hold you in. Holding and releasing are one motion.

### Programming partner — UL 56, Infinite Journey
This energy builds structures, and it can get so busy with them that it forgets what they were for. Infinite Journey holds what it lacks: a taste for the world that keeps moving and tells what it finds. Open it when your plans have grown heavier than the reason you made them. The traveller reminds the builder why it began.

### Codon ring — Ring of Gaia
This energy gives the earth its weight. It belongs to a family of three, the Ring of Gaia, through which you can feel your kinship with every living thing. UL 19, Solection, is the part of you that can feel what it is like to be another creature. UL 61, Celestial Remembrance, is the part of you that knows the truth at the heart of things. This one is the solid ground under your feet, where both of them stand.

### Tarot
The tarot gives this energy one of its major cards, its Arcana, and it speaks to what you grow.

- **Ring Arcana — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nature): The Empress is the earth that gives, and in you she is the plenty that needs a shape before it can grow.

### Immortals

### Deeper correlation

- **Hebrew letter — Daleth, the Door:** a door is a limit you can walk through when the time is right.
