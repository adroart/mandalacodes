# UL 11 — Sol Star

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing the Whole at Once · A Picture Set High · Lit From Inside · Lost in the Fog · More Ideas Than One Life · Held Until It Is Wanted · The Light Was in the Dark

This energy is peace: the hour when what rises meets what settles, and everything between them grows. In you it lives as pictures. They come faster than one life could ever act on them, each whole with all its parts at once. Among them is a picture of how things could be, set so high that no realist would put their name to it. That picture is what lights you. When it is alive in you the day has a direction, it gets you out of bed with something to do, and every choice begins to arrange itself around it.

Carried well, the picture is held loosely. It is not a plan to be built as drawn; it is what draws you upward. You cannot know what it will become as it comes into the world, only the feeling it stirs in you. Believe in the dream and give up what it looks like. Give it something to land in, a shape, a table it can be worked at, because a picture with nothing to land in stays a picture. Not every one that comes through you is yours to make. Most are for other people, and your part is to hold them clear until the one they are for arrives.

There are stretches when a haze settles between you and the world, when the pull is to read it as needing more: another teacher, a better account of how it all works. The light is behind the cloud, and the cloud is made of the pictures you have been pushing out of sight because they would not line up into sense. Let them come as they are, without asking them to mean something first, so the haze thins on its own. What you were looking for was already in you, waiting to be let out.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Heaven: the ground on top, the sky underneath, the wrong way round, and that is the point. What always rises is placed below, so it rises into what always settles, and the two meet in the middle instead of drawing apart. The traditional character shows a person standing in water, joined to what holds them up.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Above is the ground, whose whole work is to carry. It lies open under everything, asking nothing to be different; what falls on it is taken in and comes up again in its own time. Placed on top here it does something the ground rarely does: it comes down. In a family it is the mother, bending toward a child who is reaching up. In you it is the part that can receive a thing, hold it, and let it become what it is going to be. Its risk is going slack, when giving way turns into lying down under whatever comes.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Below is the sky, the force that does not stop. The sun goes over, goes down, and is back before the house is awake; it has never once failed to. Set below the ground, it presses upward into what is waiting to receive it rather than climbing away. In a family it is the father, home and at the table. In you it is drive with somewhere to go: the strength to begin, and something above it that takes what it gives. Its risk is drought, since the same word also means to parch, and a strength that nothing meets dries out the ground it was warming.

### Reading
You are in a good season, and it is a season, not a place. What was small in you is leaving, what is large arriving: the habit of adapting to whatever came along has less hold each day, and an aim you chose for yourself has room to grow. It is the first month of spring, with half a year of growing ahead, and it came because you walked the last stretch carefully. Things that would not meet are meeting: what you hoped for with what is there, the people above you with the people below.

The good season asks that you work inside it rather than rest in it. There is no flat ground without a slope coming, no going without a return, so the hour will pass. It lasts longer for the one who braces it: who spends what they have to keep the meeting open, who bears with what is rough and unfinished, who does not forget the ones far off or close the circle round a few. There is a time to make peace by strength and a time to keep it by yielding; this is the second.

The belly settled down over the head is this energy in the body, the soft front of you placed above the thinking. Let the belly lead, the head under it, and the meeting holds a while longer.

### Judgement
- Peace. The small departs and the great arrives.
- Good fortune. It goes through, and it grows.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a reed pulled up, its roots bringing the others with it. → becomes Hexagram 46, Pushing Upward.
Pull one reed and its neighbours come up with it, root tangled in root. The first move in a good season brings its own company: what you do well now gathers those who are like you, without your asking. Do not set out alone, or for yourself alone. Set the aim past your own concerns, go forward, and the others lift with you.

**Line 2** · _image:_ bearing with the rough, and crossing the river with no boat. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
Nothing is finished, there is no boat, and the way is still to go. Bear with the rough and unskilled, wade the river on your own feet, keep the far-off in mind, take no side. You have ability with no aim yet worth it, and the middle road is where the aim shows. Hold the whole barren field, not your favourites, and walk down the centre of it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ no flat ground without a slope, no going without a return. → becomes Hexagram 19, Approach.
Here is the border where sky touches ground, and the tide is about to turn. Every flat has its slope waiting, and it is not yours to stop. Stay upright through the hard stretch and no fault is yours. Do not grieve that you were good and it was not repaid. Eat what is on the table while it is there.

**Line 4** · _image:_ fluttering down, not rich, calling the neighbours. → becomes Hexagram 34, The Power of the Great.
Past the middle of the season you come down light, without the store you had, and turn to the people near you. They come without being warned, because the wish is from the heart. What was solid has thinned, and force will not thicken it. Trust the ones who want what you want, with no pact written, and let that be what holds.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the sovereign gives his younger sister in marriage, below her rank. → becomes Hexagram 5, Waiting.
The one at the top bends down on purpose: a sister of the royal house married to a lesser lord, and she goes under his roof. From that willing descent come blessing and great good fortune. Power that stoops to meet what is under it lasts. Whatever you hold, let it come down to the one who needs it, gladly, and you get what you wished for.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the city wall slides back into the moat. → becomes Hexagram 26, The Taming Power of the Great.
The earth dug out to raise the wall has fallen back into the ditch it came from, and the season is over. Do not send the army; there is nothing left to defend it with. You may still give orders inside your own gate, and even right ones bring regret, because the order has already gone. Mourn it, and stop speaking as if the wall stood.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Obscurity · _Gift:_ Idealism · _Siddhi:_ Light

### Shadow — Obscurity
A haze can settle between you and what is in front of you, so that you look at your beliefs about a thing, calling it sight. That haze is obscurity. It loves a system: a teacher, a method, anything that promises to lift the fog without your having to walk into it. The relief is real but short, because the new belief sits where the old one sat, between you and the thing. A method is a raft, for crossing, then for leaving on the far bank. Most people read the dimness as needing more, so they pile it on, and the pile is the problem.

What is in the cloud is the stream of pictures that comes up from the side of you that does not think in words, pushed out of sight because it would not make sense. Left there it turns into private fantasy and hidden wishes. The pictures you avoid most come back to find you, often wearing the face of the person you love. The fog is not the wall. It is the door, and it asks only that you not understand it yet.

### Repressive nature — Fantasizing
This person has sealed the pictures indoors. The vision runs on as a private world, richer every year, quietly draining the body of the strength that would have built the real thing. The life they were meant for is hidden behind the fantasy, and the longer it is kept there the darker it twists.

### Reactive nature — Deluded
Someone like this throws the picture at the world, insisting it is already true. They announce a future they cannot yet make, unable to let go of who they will one day be. It runs on denial and buried anger, ending in disappointment, because a false dream stands in front of a real one.

### Gift — Idealism
Trust the stream of pictures that is already yours and the fog begins to thin, not because you understood it but because you stopped demanding that it make sense before it could move. What opens is idealism, and not the soft thing the word has come to mean. You set a picture of how things could be so high that no realist would sign it, heaven on earth, the world made whole, and you do not believe it is literal. You let it organise you. Every choice begins to arrange itself around it, and it warms you from within long before any of it shows.

The craft is in the holding. You commit to the dream and give up what it will look like, because a picture coming into form never arrives as it appeared in your head. A castle you were shown turns out to be a way of working you could not have named. Grip the appearance and the flow chokes. Play with it, as with a story you know is a story, so it keeps moving. What you have to give was never the picture. It is your heart, open enough to catch other people alight.

### Siddhi — Light
One point of light in a dark sky is a single moment of pure awareness, a pause where thought stops. Then there is another, then another, until the sky of a life is more light than dark and the dark patches crack open from inside. That is what the pictures become when the last of them fuse into one, and the one is faced: a clear light behind every form, showing through it, not the light the eye sees, with no one left standing outside it. You find it only by going down into the dark, which is why the ones who carry it seem to lead people away from the light, deeper into their own murk. From here nothing is hidden and no one can pretend. Good and evil stop being the way the world is sorted; it comes back the way a child first met it, with the light showing through everything.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 11, Ideas
Ideas arrive in you as a pressure, and Gate 11 is the door they come through: more of them than one life could ever act on, with the urge to sort the flood into order. It feels like standing under a waterfall with a cup. The cup is real. The waterfall was never yours to bottle. Not every idea that comes through you is yours to act on. Most are for other people, and the skill is knowing the few that are, then guarding those, because others will take them to sell.

From outside, a person carrying this can look dreamy, all talk. What is going on is the labour of being a place where an idea can land and be kept until the one it is for turns up. The cost is a particular panic: that each idea must be seized, forced into the world, or it is lost. That panic wears a person down until they no longer trust their own timing. With years of this you learn to ask one question of any idea, what if, and then to let it go until it is wanted.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
The forehead is where this pressure is felt, the Ajna Centre, the part of you that takes what inspires you and makes sense of it. It can see the whole of a thing and all its parts at once. What it cannot do is act. It has no path of its own to any engine in the body, so its clearest idea sits still until something else moves it. Grabbed at, it tightens the whole body, and you can see it in the wrists.

Roughly one person in two has this centre switched on, and their mind runs the same way every day. Such a person has ground under their thinking: they know what they think, and they are certain, which is a strength until it hardens into being right. What starves them is having something clear to say with no one ready to hear it, since the same word is medicine to one person and poison to the next. The other half have it switched off, so the mind borrows its way of thinking from whoever is nearest and lets it go when they leave. For someone like that, ideas pass through like water through a sieve, certainty is not theirs to have, and holding the wheel loosely is the whole skill. They can see a thing from every side, which is a brilliance of its own.

### What completes it — the Channel of Curiosity (11-56)
An idea kept clear has no voice of its own. Telling a thing so that it lands in someone is the other half, Gate 56, in the throat. Idea and voice together make the Channel of Curiosity, through which a picture becomes a story. The story is what carries the idea out of you into the room. It tells the truth for everyone better than its own, so it needs a lived day in it to be believed. The idea crosses only where the two meet, whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you.

## BODY

### Physiology
The pituitary is the gland in you that sends word, and the reason this energy lives there is that a picture in you has to reach the rest of you. It is the size of a pea, sitting deep behind the bridge of your nose. Nearly everything it releases is a message to another gland: the one that sets your pace, the ones that make a child. It does none of your work itself. It tells the parts of you that do, and the whole body answers. That is what a picture does in you. It is seen behind the eyes, and it changes the body from there.

The nerves from your two eyes meet and cross just above it, so what you look at passes over it before it becomes seeing. Put a finger to the bridge of your nose: the crossing is straight back from there, deep in the head, with the gland hanging under it. How tall you grew was decided there, while you slept.

### Amino acid
The seeing in you has a substance, and the substance is threonine. Nothing in you makes it; it arrives in milk or cheese, or not at all. Three kin in you share it, and what the four do together is set how your body takes in light to make it something it can use. Yours is the part that concerns light in the mind: the eye, and the way an image made of light becomes, further back in the head, a thing you imagine. When there is a haze over what you see, that is where it sits, between the light and how you read it. Much of what you eat of it goes to renew the slippery lining that runs the length of you inside, a coat no light ever reaches.
