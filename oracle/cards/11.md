# UL 11 — Sol Star

## CODE

_Keywords:_ A Dream to Live By · Aiming Higher Than Sensible · More Ideas Than Time · Letting the Picture Change · Setting Others Alight · Lost in a Haze · Light Found in the Dark

Life changes when you stop reacting to whatever the day brings and start living toward something you chose. Idealism lives there, as a picture of how things could be. You set it higher than any sensible person would. It gets you out of bed in the morning, and every choice you make leans toward it. You feel it as a warmth in your chest long before any of it shows.

Some days the dream and your ordinary life look far apart, and people tell you to be realistic. Keep it anyway, because it is there to lift you.

Believe in the dream, and let go of what it should look like. It never arrives the way it first appeared in your head. Give it some structure to work through, and it starts to take form. What you have to give is your heart more than any picture. Leave it open, and it sets the people around you alight.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Heaven is the hexagram of a good season in your life. Heaven is the drive in you that keeps going and does not tire. Earth is the part of you that is easy to meet and lets things grow. The drive sits inside and the ease outside, because you can be strong within and still gentle with people. This hexagram has long been read as heaven and earth coming together. When the people above and the people below want the same thing, the work moves without a fight. It is also the first month of spring, so what you start now has half a year to grow. Your life goes well when your strength meets the world softly.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is how you meet people in this season, open and ready to carry what they bring. It sits on top because it is the side of you that others meet first. Earth has long been read as the ground, which holds up everything that stands on it and feeds whatever is planted. You take in what people hand you and help it grow. On its own, this ease gives way to anyone and forgets where it is headed.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the strength you carry inside, steady and untiring. It lies underneath because in this season your drive works from within, out of sight, while what shows is your ease. Heaven has long been read as the sky, where the sun keeps its course without missing a day. Your effort runs the same way, day after day, without being pushed. On its own, this strength burns hot and dries out whatever it touches.

### Reading
Things are going well, and they are going well because the parts of your life are meeting. What you hope for and what is in front of you line up. The people above you and the people below you want the same thing. This time came after a stretch of careful steps, and it is a season you are passing through.

It asks you to use what you have to keep things moving. Spend your means to hold the meeting open, and make room for the people on either side of you. Every level road has a slope ahead, so the season will turn. Some good times are won with strength, and some are kept with gentleness. This one is kept with gentleness, and sharing what comes is how it lasts.

In the body this is the belly placed over the head. Let what you feel toward people lead, and let your thinking follow.

### Judgement
- Peace. The small departs and the great arrives.
- Good fortune. It goes through, and it grows.

### Image
- Heaven and earth come together: the image of peace.
- So the one in charge divides and completes the course of heaven and earth, furthers and orders what they give, and helps the people with it.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a reed pulled up, its roots bringing the others with it. → becomes Hexagram 46, Pushing Upward.
Pull up one reed and the others come with it, their roots tangled together. When you move now, the people who are like you move too. Set your aim beyond your own concerns and your family's. Go forward, and it goes well.

**Line 2** · _image:_ bearing with the rough, and crossing the river on foot. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
You have strength, and the time supports bold moves, even crossing a river with no boat. Be patient with people who are rough and unskilled. Do not forget the ones far away, and do not keep to a clique. Walk the middle road, and you will be honoured for it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ no level ground without a slope, no going without a return. → becomes Hexagram 19, Approach.
The good time is at its height, and every level road has a slope ahead. What leaves comes back. Stay upright through the hard work, and nothing is your fault. Do not grieve that your honesty was not repaid. Enjoy the good that is still on your table.

**Line 4** · _image:_ fluttering down, not rich, together with the neighbours. → becomes Hexagram 34, The Power of the Great.
Past the middle of the season you come down without your old wealth. Your neighbours join you without being asked, because the wish comes from their hearts. Trust the people who want what you want, with nothing written down. That trust keeps the peace when the riches have gone.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the sovereign gives his younger sister in marriage, below her rank. → becomes Hexagram 5, Waiting.
The one at the top gives a sister of the royal house to a lesser lord, and she goes to live under his roof. Power that bends down to meet the people it leads is blessed. If you hold a high place, listen to the ones below it. That brings great good fortune and what you wished for.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the city wall falls back into the moat. → becomes Hexagram 26, The Taming Power of the Great.
The earth dug out to build the wall has fallen back into the ditch. The good time is over, and force will not bring it back. Do not send out an army. Even right orders given inside your own town bring regret now. Accept that the season has turned.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Obscurity · _Gift:_ Idealism · _Siddhi:_ Light

### Shadow — Obscurity
Obscurity is a haze between you and what is in front of you. You look at your beliefs about a thing and call it seeing. Most of what people take for the world is a dim version of it, seen through a narrow band of light. Inside the haze, belief feels like knowing. It loves a system, a teacher or a method that promises to lift the haze for you. A system can carry you across, the way a raft carries you over a river, and then you have to step off.

The haze is made of pictures you pushed away. They rise from the side of you that thinks in images rather than words. They come faster than you can sort, and they do not seem to make sense. So you put them out of sight, where they turn into secret daydreams and hidden wishes. The ones you avoid hardest keep finding you, often in the face of the person you love.

### Repressive nature — Fantasizing
Kept inside, the pictures grow into a private world. It drains their body of the strength it would take to live a real life. Their true path waits behind the fantasy, and the longer it is held in, the darker it twists.

### Reactive nature — Deluded
Pushed outward, the pictures are thrown at the world before they are ready. They hold a great idea and never bring it about, because they cannot let go of who they think they will one day be. Denial and anger sit under it, and it ends in disappointment.

### Gift — Idealism
Idealism is a mind that plays with the pictures that come to it. Once you start to trust them, they arrive in a flood, and some of them are huge. For an afternoon you may feel you are someone great from history. None of it is you. Each picture is moving through you, and you can enjoy it without becoming it. Only the present is safe, because the present is the one thing you cannot turn into a character.

Held that way, the pictures keep moving. A castle you were shown can turn out to stand for a way of living in fellowship. These pictures are old memories, far older than you, on their way into the world. First you have to find out which dreams are truly yours.

### Siddhi — Light
Light begins as one point in a dark sky: a single moment when your thoughts stop and you are simply aware. Then another comes, and another, while you wash the dishes or wait at a red light. In time there is more light than dark, and the dark patches open from inside. At the end the pictures fuse into one image of everything you fear and love. You sit face to face with it until nothing of you stands apart. Then a clear light shows through every form, and no one can hide or pretend. It is found only by going down into the dark. The world looks the way it did to you as a child, before good and evil divided it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 11, Ideas
Gate 11 is the gate of ideas, and it fills you with more of them than you know what to do with. It presses you to sort them, as if each one is a job to do. Most are not yours to act on. You come up with ideas for other people, and your part is to hold each one until the right person or moment appears. The few that are yours, you guard, because others will take them and sell them. When the pressure wins, you try to force every idea into being and wear yourself out. The question this gate keeps asking is what if, and it is happy to let the answer wait.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
The Ajna Center sits at your forehead and makes sense of what inspires you. It can see the whole of a thing and all its parts at once. It cannot act on what it sees, because it has no path of its own to an engine in the body. So its clearest idea waits until something else moves it. When you grip a thought, your body tightens with it, often first in your hands and wrists.

About half of people have this centre defined. Their mind works the same way every day, and they can trust what they think. Their risk is holding an answer so hard that it stops moving. Advice from them, given to someone not ready for it, turns from medicine to poison. For everyone else it is open. Information runs through them like water through a sieve, and they are not built to be certain. For them, a thought held lightly works best, the way a loose hand on a steering wheel keeps a car steady. They can see a question from many sides.

### What completes it — the Channel of Curiosity (11-56)
Gate 56, in the throat, turns ideas into stories. Joined to Gate 11 it makes the Channel of Curiosity, which carries a picture out of your head and into a room of listeners. Its stories reach for truths that hold for everyone. They land best with a lived day inside them. Gate 56 may be in you or in someone close, and the idea is spoken only when both are there.

## BODY

### Physiology
The pituitary is given this energy because a picture in you has to reach the rest of you. The gland sits deep behind the bridge of your nose, about the size of a pea. It does almost none of the body's work itself. Nearly everything it releases is a message to another gland, and the whole body answers. That is what a picture does in you: it is seen behind your eyes, and it moves you from there.

The nerves from your two eyes cross just above it, so everything you look at passes over it on the way to becoming sight. Close your eyes and put a finger on the bridge of your nose. The crossing lies straight back from there, and the gland hangs just under it. How tall you grew was set by this small gland while you slept.

### Amino acid
The pictures in you have a chemistry, and one substance in it is threonine. Your body cannot make it, so it comes from food such as milk and cheese. Light reaches your eyes, and further back in your head it becomes the images you imagine. This substance belongs to that passage, from light to picture. When a haze sits between you and what you see, it sits there, between the light and how your mind reads it.

You share this chemistry with three other energies, each a different way you take in light. Much of what you eat of it goes to renew the slippery lining of your gut, where no light ever reaches.

## RELATIONS

_UL 12 is this card's pair, its inverse and its partner at once: the shut season, where this one is the open one._

### Pair — UL 12, Petals of Freedom (Standstill)
This energy dreams how things could be, and it runs out when the good season closes and nothing will meet. Petals of Freedom is where it goes next: the shut season, when a guarded heart tells real from fake. Open it when doors that stood open have closed. Every good season is followed by a quiet one.

### Inverse — UL 12, Petals of Freedom (Standstill)
Turn this energy upside down and meeting becomes standing apart. Petals of Freedom keeps its heart to itself, where this energy opens to everything that comes. Open it when you have let too much in and need to know what is truly yours.

### Programming partner — UL 12, Petals of Freedom
This energy fills you with more pictures than you can use. Petals of Freedom holds what it lacks: the heart's sense of which ones are real. With it, you can use a dream without taking it literally. Open it when you cannot tell your vision from a story you were sold.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Light
This energy sees how things could be, and it belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Light. UL 5, The Space Between Time, is the answer you wait for. UL 9, Ease in This, is the small step you keep taking. UL 26, Lighter Than a Feather, is your knack for getting a thing done unseen. This one is the picture that shows you where to walk.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, come through the ring and the two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star is the one clear light you keep your eyes on, the dream that lifts you.
- **Earth (upper) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil is a belief you have mistaken for sight, which you are free to put down.
- **Earth (upper) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World is a dream that has found its shape and stands whole.
- **Heaven (lower) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool steps toward what cannot yet be explained, the way you follow a picture before it makes sense.
- **Heaven (lower) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress is the growing season, when what you tend comes up full.

### Immortals
Earth above carries Lan Cai He, the wandering singer who gives away coins without looking back. In a good season you can give like that. Heaven below carries Han Xiangzi, the flute player whose music makes flowers open. Your strength can help what is near you bloom the same way.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Aquarius:** the fixed air of someone who pictures a better world for everyone at once.
- **Hebrew letter — Tsade, the Fishhook:** the hook that brings a hidden picture up from deep water, so a dream you kept to yourself can come into the light.
