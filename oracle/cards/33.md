# UL 33 — Echos of Time

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Looking Back · The Same Week Again · Leaving While You Can · Time Alone · The Day's Harvest · The Story Told at Its Hour · Nothing Left to Say

Looking back is the energy here, the part of you that turns to read the road it has already walked. A life is mostly read the other way, as what is coming, with the next thing always arriving, so the road behind drops away unread and the same week comes round again as if it were new. This energy stops and turns. Gathered and held until it makes sense, what happened to you stops being a wound. It becomes something you can hand to another person. It lives in the quiet you keep for yourself, and in the story you tell when the quiet is over.

Carried well, it gives part of every day to what has just gone by. Ask what was best about yesterday and bring it back, the hours you were awake, into the body; the hours you were not, let go down the stream, forgiven. A day with no pause in it has no harvest, however much was done, and that is why some lives are exhausting in a way nobody can name. The pause need not be a thing set apart. Tea drunk with attention is a pause, and so is work done as if it mattered. Done with a friend, it is the oldest thing people do: sit by a fire and talk about the good times.

The way runs in seasons, and it is not always the season for turning round. When it is, the people near you will call it by the wrong names, dwelling, living in the past, and often you will call it that yourself. There will be days when you have gone quiet, hard to reach, without deciding to, and it is not the same as being lost. Something is being read in there that has not finished. What it comes to will be worth the wait.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Mountain: the sky far above, and under it a mountain, which is where walking stops. The sky moves on while the mountain stays, and the distance that opens between them is the retreat. The traditional character is footprints that stop short, beside a small pig cut in two by a hand: an offering made on the way out, the wealth a person carries off by walking away.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
The sky is above, out of reach and going on. The sky is the force that never rests, the father in a family, and here it is the brightness that has drawn off too high to be touched, still moving while the ground beneath it has stopped. On the outside of the situation that is the world: it goes on, and it will not wait for you. In you it is the strength that has not gone anywhere; it has withdrawn to where it cannot be worn down. Its risk is exhaustion, the other meaning of its name, since a force that only ever goes forward uses itself up.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Under it is the mountain, where a foot stops walking. The mountain is the limit, the thing that keeps the eye from seeing further, the youngest son in a family, and its hour is sunset at the start of winter. On the inside of the situation that is what has happened: you have stopped. In you it is the part that can hold still, that has come to the end of something and knows it, keeping its own counsel there. Its risk is that stopping hardens into refusing, and what should be firm goes stubborn.

### Reading
You are asked to leave, and to leave well. Something small has been gaining ground for a while, and it will not be argued with; this is the sixth month, when the days begin to shorten, which nobody can stop. To stay and fight is to spend strength on ground that is already going. What the season asks is to withdraw while withdrawing is still yours to choose, and that is not flight. You leave with what you own, at the pace you set, keeping your strength for a day when it will count.

Keep the small at a distance, and do not hate it, since hating would tie you to it. Be firm, be stern, shut the door, and keep no bitterness behind it. Knowing when to withdraw is the hardest wisdom there is, and the whole of it is timing: a step back at the right hour turns out later to have been the way forward. In small things it still pays to hold to what is right, because that is where the ground is kept for the return.

In the body this energy is the head and the hand, the head above still going on, the hand below stopped. Notice where you are holding yourself now: the hand that has let go of the work, the head still up and looking. Let the hand rest, keep the head where it is, and go.

- In what is small, holding to what is true still serves.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the tail of the retreat, and danger. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
At the tail of the retreat, with the danger at your back: you have left it late, and you are the last one out. Any move now would only draw the danger on. The deer that outlives the tiger runs at the head of the herd; if you cannot reach the front, step aside and stand still while the rush goes past. Make no move in any direction.

**Line 2** · _image:_ held fast with a strap of yellow ox hide, which no one can loosen. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
Held fast by a strap of yellow ox hide, which nobody can loosen: your purpose to withdraw is tied off, and nothing outside can work it free. Yellow is the colour of the soil at the centre, and hide cured that way does not give. Stay quiet, stay empty, so that nothing from outside gets in, no thought slips out, and neither praise nor money can move you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a retreat that is tied, dragging its sickness after it. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
A retreat that is tied, dragging its sickness after it: you want to go and cannot, because you are held by what is near you, too familiar to let go of. Held like that, nothing large can be done, and the strain of it wears you down. What you can still do is feed the household that depends on you. Keep that much, and let the great work wait.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a willing retreat, made in spite of what you love. → becomes Hexagram 53, Development.
A willing retreat, made in spite of what you love: the thing you are leaving is a thing you wanted, and that is the whole of the test. The small cannot do it; they cling, and call the clinging loyalty. Store the strength away while the small thing is still small, guard your life the way you would guard a jewel, and go without a backward pull.

**Line 5** · _image:_ an admirable retreat, made at its proper hour. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
An admirable retreat, made at its proper hour: the work is done, and the one who did it steps down before anyone asks. He could have taken the throne and did not. With brilliance to spare he shows none of it, keeps to the good, and lets nothing false damage what is real. Hold to what is right, and withdraw while it can still be done well.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a rich retreat, and nothing left to doubt. → becomes Hexagram 31, Influence.
A rich retreat, with nothing left in doubt: you go with everything you came in carrying, and nothing can move you or bind you. Inside there is no damage, outside no loss, and there is nothing to hold down, since nothing in you is pulling the other way. Go, and go gladly, because everything serves.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Forgetting · _Gift:_ Mindfulness · _Siddhi:_ Revelation

### Shadow — Forgetting
You have lived this week before, and it arrives feeling new. The argument and the cold days after it, many times over, each time as if for the first. That is what forgetting is like from inside: not a gap, but the present. A life is read as a rush, with something always coming at you, the next thing on the screen, and the road already walked drops away behind you unread. What keeps you asleep is the memories you carry, and what keeps you making more of them is every wish you have, each one pulling the net a little tighter. One wish loosens it, the wish to remember yourself.

The people you cannot get free of do most of the loosening. Someone you feel you have known from somewhere, loved and fought with in turns, is where the forgetting is thinnest, if you stay. Unattended, the forgetting leaves you feeling separate, alone, and that feeling is the one thing everyone shares.

### Repressive nature — Reserved
This person has gone into hiding. They say little, cannot get out of their own silence, feel forgotten by the world and arrange to be, in work or in a numbness that passes for calm. When they do wake, the wisdom they took in surprises everyone, themselves first.

### Reactive nature — Censorious
Others prod. Out of an old anger they point at the faults of the people near them, dressed as help, until they get the reaction they were after, because a fight proves someone is there. It builds until it bursts. When the pattern breaks, they remember what it is to be loved.

### Gift — Mindfulness
Something changes the day you catch yourself in the middle of an argument and see that you have forgotten who you are. You are not free of the pattern yet; you can see it, which is new, and for a while seeing is worse than sleeping, because now every repeat is felt. The patterns seem to get stronger the nearer you come to the end of them. They are not; you are more awake. Nothing you do can hurry it, and the only thing to lean on is the watching.

The old picture for it is a clearing in a forest. You have been walking head down for months, then the trees open, there is light, a place to sit, and from there the path you have been on can be seen for the first time. Sit down in it. What you bring back from there goes into the body, and told to a friend it softens the edges between you. This is also where genius lives: what you can watch in yourself without drowning is what you can make something from, once you are out of its way.

### Siddhi — Revelation
Revelation is a flood, and it comes as silence. Everything you were is forgotten, everything you could be is remembered, all at once, the whole of the story rising out of the deep of time so that you see it entire, and there is no past left to be cut off from. After the flood in the old stories there is always a sign, a dove or a rainbow, and the flood itself is a memory of your own future. The ones who live here have nothing to say, and when they speak it is to say so.

It has the colour of autumn: the leaves come down, brighter just before they fall, and the light turns inward. The story is ending, and the ending is not a loss. Fall in love with your own story and follow it without holding anything back; it will reach its end, unlike anyone else's.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 33, Retelling
Turning what happened to you into a story that can be handed on is the pressure this energy puts on the body. Gate 33 is where it enters: the need to step back from the world, alone, until what you went through has given up its meaning, then to come back and tell it. It is not gossip and it is not a report. It is the meaning that was hidden inside the experience while it was happening, found afterwards, in private, and carried out to whoever needs it. You need more time alone than most, and the time is not against anyone.

From outside the stepping back looks like distance, the looking back like dwelling on what is over, and both get said to you. The cost is a story that has set hard around the pain, told and retold as what was done to you, which keeps you where you were, with whoever hears it. People who carry this all their lives learn that a story has finished teaching only when it can be told so that someone else stands up straighter, and they wait for that hour.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The throat holds this pressure, the Throat Centre, the one place in a person where what is inside becomes something outside, a word or a deed. Everything in you presses to get here, from every side, and it has no engine of its own: whichever part of you it is joined to is the one that talks through it. Here the join is to the centre of the chest, where who you are lives, so what you say comes from who you are, and you are what you talk about. That is also why a hard word about what you told can shut you for years: it landed on you, not on the story.

In about seven people in ten this centre is switched on, and the voice is the same in every room, one song, sent out rather than taken in. For someone like that the retelling is always there to be done, and the whole craft is when. In the other three it is not, so the voice takes its shape from whoever is near and hands it back. For someone like that there is a pull to talk before anything has been listened to; kept quiet until asked, they end up saying what the whole room remembers.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Prodigal (13–33)
Looking back has nothing to tell until something has been taken in. Its other half is Gate 13, in the centre of the chest, the listener, the part that hears other people's stories and keeps them; the two make the Channel of the Prodigal, what was witnessed, held, then brought back changed. It gathers, goes quiet, and speaks at the hour the story can land. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the story comes back only where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
Your throat is given this energy because you have only ever heard your own voice from inside. Everyone else hears your voice through the air. You hear it by a second road too, through the bones of your own head, which carry the low notes best. So the voice you have listened to all your life is the one from within. The voice everyone else knows reaches you only played back, thin and high, like a stranger's. That is looking back in your flesh: a life heard from the inside by the one it happened to. Below your voice sits the gland that keeps a store of what it makes, two to three months laid down ahead, so that what is running you today was made a while ago.

Block both ears with your fingers and say a few words. Your voice is louder and deeper at once, because the road through the air is shut, leaving only the one through the bone. What you hear then is the part of your own voice that was always yours alone.

### Amino acid
No substance in you answers to this energy. It is a mark in what you are written in, one of three that name nothing, and where your body reads it the reading stops. The message runs on past it, written out to its end; it is the reading that stops there, not the writing. When this mark comes alive in you, it begins to unplug you from the world your outer senses show. Further on, past every stop in you, another beginning is written, so that something starts where you come to an end.

Two others in you carry the same stop, and this is the first of the three, where all of it begins. What shakes a body out of its sleep is the strength of its own dissatisfaction, and this is the stop that dissatisfaction wakes.
