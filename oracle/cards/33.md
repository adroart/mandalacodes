# UL 33 — Echos of Time

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Time on Your Own · Looking Back · Savouring What Was Good · Letting the Bad Hours Go · Leaving at the Right Time · A Story That Helps · The Same Mistake Again

After anything big, you need time on your own to look back at it. Mindfulness is alive in you here: you go over it quietly until you can see what it was about. Even your mistakes have something to teach you once you see them from a distance.

The hard part is the pace of life, with the next thing always coming at you, so the days go by without a second look. The people near you may read your quiet as distance. Take the time anyway, let the bad hours go, and forgive yourself for them.

Give part of every day to what has just gone by, even if it is only a cup of tea drunk slowly. Ask what was best about yesterday and let yourself feel it again. Talk it over with a friend, and you will both feel closer. Wait to tell your story until it can lift the person who hears it. Then what you have lived becomes something you can give.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Mountain is the hexagram of knowing when to step back. Heaven is the strength in you that keeps moving forward. Mountain is the part of you that knows where to stop. The stopping sits below the strength because a strength that never stops wears itself out. This hexagram has long been read as a mountain under a sky that is drawing away. A person who leaves in good time takes all of their strength with them. It is a time when something smaller has begun to gain ground, the way the days shorten after midsummer. You keep what matters by stepping back before you are pushed.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven shows in you today as the drive that gets up and goes on working, whatever else is happening. It sits on top because it is the side of you the world sees. Heaven has long been read as the sky, which keeps turning whatever happens beneath it. So you can walk away from one place and lose none of your strength on the way. On its own, this drive dries you out, because a force that only goes forward uses itself up.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain shows in you as the moment you stop and stay still. It lies underneath because the stopping happens inside you, before anyone sees a change. Mountain has long been read as a range of peaks, the place where walking ends and you cannot see any further. When you reach the end of something, you feel it the same way, as a limit you do not need to climb. On its own, this stillness hardens, and what should be firm turns stubborn.

### Reading
Something smaller than you has been gaining ground for a while, and arguing will not stop it. Staying to fight spends your strength on ground that is already going. So leave while leaving is still your choice. This is not running away. You decide how far and how fast, and you go with what is yours.

Keep your distance from what is small, and do not hate it, because hating would tie you to it. Be firm, and carry no bitterness out the door. In small things, keep doing what is right. Knowing when to step back is the hardest call there is. A step back at the right hour often turns out to be the way forward.

In the body this is the head and the hand. The hand below has put the work down, and the head above stays clear. Let the hand rest, keep your head up, and go.

### Judgement
- Retreat, and it goes well.
- In small things, it still pays to hold firm.

### Image
- A mountain under heaven: that is retreat.
- So the wise keep small-minded people at a distance, without anger, with reserve.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the tail of the retreat, and danger. → becomes Hexagram 13, Fellowship.
You are the last one out, with the danger close behind. Any sudden move now only draws it on. When a tiger comes, the deer at the back of the herd lives by stepping aside and standing still while the rush goes past. Make no move in any direction yet.

**Line 2** · _image:_ held fast with a strap of yellow ox hide, which no one can loosen. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
You have made up your mind to go, and it holds like a strap of ox hide that no hand can loosen. Keep quiet inside, so nothing from outside gets in and nothing of yours slips out. Praise and money cannot move you now. The outcome is not known yet, so hold to what you have decided.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a retreat that is tied, dragging its sickness after it. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
You want to go, and you are held by people too close and too familiar to leave. The strain wears you down, and nothing large can be done from here. You can still look after the household that depends on you. Do that much well, and let the great work wait.

**Line 4** · _image:_ a willing retreat, made in spite of what you love. → becomes Hexagram 53, Development.
You are leaving something you love, and you go of your own free will. That is the whole test, and small-minded people cannot pass it. Store your strength while what threatens it is still small. Guard your life as carefully as you would guard a jewel, and go.

**Line 5** · _image:_ an admirable retreat, made at its proper hour. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
The work is done, and you step down before anyone asks you to. You could stay and take more, and you choose not to. You have gifts to spare, and you keep them quiet. Hold to what is good, and do not let anything false spoil what is real. Leave while it can still be done well.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a rich retreat, and nothing left to doubt. → becomes Hexagram 31, Influence.
You leave with all you brought in. Nobody can hold you back or bend you now. No part of you is pulling the other way, so there is no doubt left. You have lost none of it, inside or out. Go gladly, because everything is working in your favour.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Forgetting · _Gift:_ Mindfulness · _Siddhi:_ Revelation

### Shadow — Forgetting
Forgetting is how a life goes when there is never a pause in it. Your eyes stay on what is coming, the next message or the next job. You rarely look at where you have just been. So you walk through the forest head down and cross each clearing without stopping. You miss what was waiting there, a sunrise or a stranger. The same argument comes round again, and each time it feels new. Life starts to feel hard in a way you cannot name.

The memories you carry keep you in the pattern, and every wish you have seems to add another. One wish loosens it: the wish to remember who you are. The people you cannot get free of help you most, the ones you feel you have known from somewhere, loved and fought with in turns. Stay with them, and they wake you up. Everyone forgets in this way, even though forgetting leaves each person feeling separate and alone.

### Repressive nature — Reserved
This person has gone into hiding. They find it hard to talk to anyone and cannot break out of their own silence. They hide in their work or a strict routine, far from other people or in a kind of numbness. When they finally wake, the wisdom they have taken in surprises everyone, themselves included.

### Reactive nature — Censorious
Anger drives this one, and so does a fear of feeling alone. They point out other people's faults, often calling it help, until they get the angry reaction they wanted. The resentment builds until it bursts. Once they stop putting their rage on other people, they remember what it is to love and be loved.

### Gift — Mindfulness
Mindfulness starts the day you catch yourself in the middle of a heated argument and see that you have forgotten who you are. You still hide or react, and now you can watch yourself doing it. For a while that is harder than sleeping, because now you feel every repeat. The patterns seem to grow stronger the nearer you come to the end of them. They are not stronger; you are more awake. Nothing you do can hurry this, and the watching is all you can lean on.

Then the trees open into a clearing. There is light and a place to sit, and from there you can see the path you have been walking. Sit down in it for a while. From here you can make something out of what you feel, because you can see it without drowning in it. Your best work happens when you are out of its way.

### Siddhi — Revelation
Revelation comes to you as silence, the way autumn comes to a forest. Your life turns brighter as it lets go, the way leaves do before they fall, and your attention turns inward. All that you were is forgotten, and all that you could be is remembered. The past no longer stands between you and this moment. Here you have no more to tell, and if you speak at all, it is to say that.

Your story is coming to its end. Part of you does not want it to end, and the greater part is ready to let go. Fall in love with your own story and follow it without holding anything back. It will reach its end, and no one else's will be like it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 33, Retelling
Gate 33 sits in the throat and pushes you to step back from the world after something has happened. You go off alone to go over it and gather your thoughts, and this is not hiding. Then you come back and share what you found, with a depth you could not have had before. The people near you may take the distance for coldness, so say plainly that you need the time. Its risk is going over the past too long, alone, until it turns to worry. When it runs well, your story lifts whoever hears it. When it runs badly, you tell a story of pain that keeps you and your listener stuck where you were.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center sits in your neck and is where what is inside you comes out, as words or as action. Energy from all over you rushes toward it. It is the meeting place through which you reach other people.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their voice is always there for them, whether or not they use it, with one steady tone that others know them by. The other three have it open. As children they were often not heard, so some went quiet and some got louder. They can feel pressure to speak without knowing what to say. Their gift is to meet each person in the tone that person needs, and to say what a whole room is feeling. They speak best when someone asks.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Prodigal (13–33)
Stepping back has nothing to go over until something has been taken in. Gate 13 sits in the centre of the chest and is the listener, the part of you that collects other people's stories. Together they make the Channel of the Prodigal, which takes life in and keeps it until the time is right to share it. It works in silence first and is not built for small talk. Gate 13 may be in you or in a person close by, and the channel runs only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to your throat because you hear your own voice from inside. Everyone else hears your voice through the air. You hear it by a second road as well, through the bones of your own head, which carry the low notes best. So the voice you have heard all your life is the one from within. A recording of it sounds thin and high, like a stranger's. Your life is the same: only you have heard it from inside. A gland below your voice stores two to three months of what it makes, so what runs you today was made a while ago.

Block both ears with your fingers and say a few words. Your voice sounds louder and deeper, because the road through the air is shut and only the one through the bone is left. What you hear then is the part of your voice that nobody else has ever heard.

### Amino acid
No substance in you answers to this energy. It is a mark in the code you are written in, one of three that name nothing. Where your body's reading reaches it, the reading stops. The written message runs on past it, and only the reading ends there. When these marks come alive in you, they begin to unplug you from the world your senses show. Past every stop in you, another beginning is written further on. Something starts where you come to an end.

This energy is the first of three such stops in you. Only the strength of your own dissatisfaction can shake you out of sleep, and it begins at this first stop.

## RELATIONS

_UL 34 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the strength that pushes forward, where this one steps back._

### Pair — UL 34, Sublime Power (The Power of the Great)
This energy steps back to look at what happened, and it runs out once the looking is done and something has to be acted on. Sublime Power is where it goes next: the full strength that pours in when something real calls for it. Open it when you have understood the past and it is time to move. What you gathered alone is spent in the world.

### Inverse — UL 34, Sublime Power (The Power of the Great)
Turn this energy end for end and stepping back becomes pressing forward. Sublime Power is the same strength seen from the side that advances. Open it when you have rested long enough and the time to act has come back.

### Programming partner — UL 19, Solection
This energy needs time alone, and alone it can forget the people who wake it up. Solection holds what it lacks: the pull toward the people you need and who need you. The two meet in your closest relationships, the hard ones most of all, where old patterns come up to be seen. Open it when you have been on your own long enough and someone is waiting for you.

### Codon ring — Ring of Trials
This energy looks back, and looking back is where waking up begins. It belongs to a family of three, the Ring of Trials, whose theme is the tests on the way to knowing who you are. UL 56, Infinite Journey, carries the test of being pulled away by your senses. UL 12, Petals of Freedom, carries the last test of meeting who you really are. This one carries the first: seeing that you have forgotten.

- **Ring Arcana — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs still and sees the world the other way up, the pause that lets you see where you have been.
- **Heaven (upper) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool steps off with nothing to carry, the fresh start waiting past a well-timed exit.
- **Mountain (lower) — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teach): The Hierophant hands on what has been learned, the way you tell your story once it has taught you.
- **Mountain (lower) — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): The Hermit climbs away from the crowd with one lamp, and finds the right path by being alone.

### Immortals
Heaven above carries Han Xiangzi, whose flute made flowers open out of season, and he tells you that leaving costs you none of your gifts. Mountain below carries Zhang Guo Lao, who rides his donkey backwards, and he tells you that looking where you came from shows you the way.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Water:** the element that finds the lowest place and rests there, as you do when you step back.
- **Hebrew letter — Mem, the Water:** the letter of deep water, where what happened settles until you can see it clearly.
