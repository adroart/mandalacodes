# UL 50 — Melt Into Perfection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Sensing What People Need · Knowing What Has Spoiled · Finding the Middle Again · Bringing a Room Together · Looking After Yourself First · Carrying Too Much · One Habit at a Time

A household or a team has fallen out of balance, and you can feel what is missing. Balance lives in you as a feel for what the people near you need, and for what has gone off. You sense it low in your body before you can put it into words. You add a little here and take a little away there, the way a cook tastes a pot and adjusts it. People settle around you without knowing why.

There are days when everyone near you is out of tune at once, and you feel it in your own body. Do not force them back into line, and do not walk away. Stay in the middle yourself, and let them find their own way back to it.

Start with your own balance, since a group finds its balance one person at a time. Change one habit at a time, slowly. Say out loud what is hidden, so nothing sits unspoken between you. In time the balance stops being work, and you melt into it.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Wind is the hexagram of turning what you were given into what feeds people. Wind, which here is also wood, is the patience in you that keeps feeding a task a little at a time. Fire is the clear sight in you that knows when a thing is ready. The seeing sits above the feeding because you can only judge what has been given time to cook. This hexagram has long been read as a bronze pot on three legs over a fire. Whatever raw thing you put in, you take out as food for a whole house. It is also read as the new order set up once an old one has fallen, which is harder than pulling the old one down. Whatever you inherit feeds people only once you have cooked it through.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire in you tells raw from ready, and what to keep from what to throw away. It sits on top because once the work is under way, clear sight decides when it is done. Fire has long been read as a flame, which clings to what it burns and lights up everything near it. Your attention works the same way, holding to the task in front of you until you can see every part of it. On its own, it burns on past ready, and what was cooking is spoiled.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind in you goes into a thing gently and keeps going, the way roots work down into the ground. It sits underneath because everything you make rests on the steady care you put in first. Wind has long been read as wood, the fuel under the pot. Your fire burns as long as you keep feeding it, and dies when you stop. On its own, it spends itself feeding everyone else's fire.

### Reading
An old way of doing things has ended, and something new has to be set up in its place. Setting it up is harder than pulling the old one down. What you have been handed is raw, and it has to be cooked before it can feed anyone. Feed what is best in the people around you first, since the new grows out of them.

Clean the pot before you cook, because whatever has gone stale from before will spoil what is new. Take your right place and stand in it, and your life sets firm the way bronze sets in a mould. Go gently, and your ears grow clear and your eyes sharp. Watch the heat to the end, since one slip near the finish can lose the whole meal.

Your ears and eyes do the work now. The ears hear what the people at your table need, and the eyes see when the food is ready. Stand the way the pot stands over the fire, steady on three legs while the heat does its work.

### Judgement
- The cauldron. Supreme good fortune. Success.
- Fire on wood is cooking: the wise cooked to make an offering to heaven, and cooked again to feed the worthy.
- Through gentleness the ears grow clear and the eyes sharp. The soft rises to the centre, and the firm answers it.

### Image
- Fire over wood: that is the picture of the cauldron.
- So the wise make their place right, and their fate sets firm.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the pot tipped over on its feet, and the stale poured out. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
The pot is tipped over, and what had gone stale falls out. That is the good in it. Before you add anything new, clear out the old, so the pot can stand level again. If the vessel you have will not hold the new thing, take another, even a humbler one. No one will blame you for it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ food in the pot, and one who will not come to eat. → becomes Hexagram 56, The Wanderer.
The pot is full, and the one who is set against you cannot come near to eat. Their objection cannot reach you now. It may be a part of you, your head refusing what your gut knows. Be careful where you go, because the tension is still there. For now, the meal is good.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the handles changed, the pot stuck fast, and the pheasant's fat uneaten. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
The pot's handles are gone, so it cannot be lifted, and the rich meal inside goes uneaten. You held firm and would not give an inch, so the work stopped. Regret comes like rain, and the rain clears it. Listen to what others are saying, because the way forward is in their words, and this ends well.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the legs broken, and the prince's meal on the floor. → becomes Hexagram 18, Work on the Decayed.
The legs break under a pot too full, and the prince's meal spills over his robes. You took on more than your strength could carry and leaned on your position instead. Stop, and put right what was spilled. Then look again at the people you relied on before you carry anything else.

**Line 5** · _image:_ yellow ears on the pot, and rings of gold to carry it. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
The pot has yellow ears and gold rings to lift it by. Yellow is the colour of the middle, so you are in the right place. The ears are hollow and the rings are solid, so the soft and the firm in you carry the weight together. Keep your attention on one thing. Stay humble, since everyone answers to someone above them.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a handle of jade. → becomes Hexagram 32, Duration.
The handle is jade, as hard as metal with a softness of its own, and no fire can melt it. The cooking is done, and the firm and the soft in you have become one. Everything you do now helps. Lift the pot and share what is in it.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Corruption · _Gift:_ Equilibrium · _Siddhi:_ Harmony

### Shadow — Corruption
Something in you was miscopied before you were born, and it sounds like a string that rasps instead of holding its note. It is not your fault, and it is yours anyway. You meet it first in the world, in crooked dealing and in people who answer to nobody. It shows up out there because it is wound around the inside of you. Nobody clears it from the world without first clearing it in themselves.

The fault stays alive because you give up on it. No one else bothers, you tell yourself, so why should you. You have tried and prayed, and it is still there. Or you go the other way: one bad day and you tighten up and try too hard, which only makes it worse. You are like a child learning an instrument, and the sound is painful at first. Then, one small tune at a time, your notes start to hold.

### Repressive nature — Overloaded
The whole weight of the world sits on this person. They feel they must protect everyone close to them, so they put their dreams away. Those dreams were where their creativity lived. Only when they face the fear underneath does it find a way back out.

### Reactive nature — Irresponsible
Here the fear comes out as anger, aimed at those above or those below. This person feels answerable to no one for what their actions do to others. They chase gain and standing. They are as trapped by the order they exploit as the people they exploit.

### Gift — Equilibrium
Tuned by ear, the same string in you holds its note. You find the middle by being thrown off it. Pour yourself in too hard and you tense up. Hold back and only watch, and you give up. Each extreme hurts, and the hurt sends you back to the centre.

Then you can walk into a room where everything has gone to pieces and feel what is missing. You put the parts back together, and harmony is possible again. A parent's unlived life shapes a child more than anything else, and what parents hide from each other comes out in their children. So balance cannot be imposed on anyone. Each person has to be trusted with their own.

### Siddhi — Harmony
Your note joins a music that was already playing. Balance can be lost, and this harmony cannot. Nobody makes it, and all you can do is enter it. First every sense of being separate falls away. Then your body becomes a chamber that rings, and in the end the chamber itself dissolves.

Every life is a note in one piece of music, and the music grows sweeter. It plays under your days whether you hear it or not. When one person arrives here, what was hidden comes to light around them, and the whole group shifts. You are the instrument, and the music plays through you.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 50, Values
Gate 50 presses you to look after your people. You feel responsible for whether they are well, fed in body and in spirit, and for the values that hold them together. It is a mother's drive, whoever carries it. Under it runs a fear of not caring enough, of letting them down. The fear makes you take on more than is yours, until you are carrying people who could walk.

From the outside this can look like meddling, or like rules so strict that no one may make their own mistakes. Care driven by guilt empties the one who gives it. Look after yourself first, since that is the only way there is anything left to give. Trusting the people you love to look after themselves is its own kind of care.

### Where it lives — the Spleen Center
The Spleen Center is the part of you that keeps you safe and well. It is the oldest kind of awareness you have. It speaks in the moment, through your senses, and it keeps time by meeting each moment fresh. It also holds the fears that once kept people alive, and this gate's fear is of failing the people in your care. Most of these fears are ropes taken for snakes. See the fear for what it is, thank it, and go on. When this centre is at ease, you have the lightness of a body that feels safe.

About fifty-five people in a hundred have this centre defined. Their bodies give off a glow that says all is well, and less settled people like to stand near it. Their fears go out into the room with the same strength, so they are worth clearing. In the other forty-five it is open. They pick up the state of whoever is nearby and can mistake another person's poor health for their own. Time slips away from them. At their best they have a fine sense of what would be good for another person.

### What completes it — the Channel of Preservation (27–50)
Knowing what would nourish people is not yet feeding them. Gate 27, in the Sacral Center low in the belly, brings the energy to do the caring. Together they make the Channel of Preservation, the drive to keep your people well and to pass on what holds them together. It knows what will nourish, in food and in teaching. Not everyone needs your care, and part of this is knowing who does. Gate 27 may be in your own chart or in a person close to you, and the care is complete only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The spleen holds this energy, and the reason is cooking. It has long been pictured as a pot on a stove, warmed from below, that turns what you eat into blood and strength. Nothing you eat is yours until it has been cooked there. From there the strength goes out to your arms and legs.

The organ itself sits under your left ribs, behind the stomach, and it does not cook. It skims the pot. All of your blood passes through it many times a day, and it takes out the worn cells and anything that should not be there. What goes round stays fit to feed you. For some months before you were born, it was one of the places your blood was made.

### Amino acid
Inside every cell of you, a messenger copies the instructions for life. It can misread them, the way fear misreads what people say in a room. This energy has a substance in you, and the substance is glutamic acid. Your body makes it itself. Nearly every other amino acid your body takes apart hands its nitrogen to this one. In your brain it is the messenger that says go, and the one that says stop is made from it in a single step.

Balance starts down at this level, in your cells. A real sense of belonging somewhere can bring it all the way there. The same substance runs through one other energy, the sense of who belongs together, and it ties that sense to your balance. The liquid in each of your cells tunes itself to whatever you line up with. If you live among chaos, chaos is what your body feels like.

## RELATIONS

_UL 49 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the fire that clears the old, where this one cooks the new._

### Pair — UL 49, Union in the Ashes (Revolution)
This energy cooks what it was given until it can feed people, and it runs out when the pot itself has gone bad. Union in the Ashes is where it goes next: the fire that clears an old way out so a new one can start. Open it when no amount of adjusting will set things right. Some things have to end before anything new can be cooked.

### Inverse — UL 49, Union in the Ashes (Revolution)
Turn this energy end for end and the cooking becomes a clearing. Union in the Ashes is the same fire seen from the side that throws the old away. Open it when you are keeping something alive that has already gone off.

### Programming partner — UL 3, Messengers of the Infinite
This energy keeps things in balance, and it can hold on so tightly that nothing new gets in. Messengers of the Infinite holds what it lacks: the fresh start that brings new ingredients, and the mess that comes with them. Balance with nothing new in it goes stale, and something new with no balance falls apart. Open it when everything is steady and nothing is growing.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Illuminati
This energy brings a group into balance. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of the Illuminati. Its theme is people working together as equals, with no one ruling over them. UL 44, Sophia's Orchestra, carries teamwork and the sense of who belongs together. This one carries the balance that lets them work as one.
