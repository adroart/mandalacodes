# UL 10 — Internal Treasure

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Being Yourself · In Your Own Skin · A Light Step · The Tiger's Tail · Watching Yourself · Easy Is Right · Nothing to Find

You are at ease in your own skin and it shows in how you move. Nobody is being performed for. You do the thing the way you do it. That is what keeps you safe near something that could hurt you, because being yourself has a lightness in the feet and the fierce thing lets the light step past.

The challenge here is the pull to watch yourself. It is walking into a room and checking how you are coming across before you have said a word, and the checking is what stiffens the step. A step that is watched is the one that catches.

Let the looking wear out. There is no second you to find, so the search can end without a result and lose nothing. Walk the way you walk and keep the step soft.

## ICHING

### Combination
The whole of heaven and under it an open lake. The highest and hardest force there is stands directly over the softest and most open, and the soft one does not run. It walks. A foot comes down on the tail of a tiger and the tiger turns and does not bite. Nothing was fought and nobody ran. What passed through was the lightness of the tread.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven does not stop. The sun comes up every morning whether or not anyone is ready for it. In the world it is the father, the one who makes and drives and goes first. In a person it is strength that wants to move and can keep moving. You get up and you go at it again. You do not tire easily and you do not like to wait behind anyone. Its risk is dryness. The same force that makes things also parches them. Driven with nothing to check it, it burns through what it was meant to build and calls the burning progress.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
A lake is water that has stopped running and lies open to the sky. Its surface takes in whatever stands over it and there is life in it. It is the marsh where water pools and the mouth in the family of things, talk and the pleasure of company. In the world it is the market and the meal and the joke that loosens a room. In a person it is gladness that shows. You say what you feel and people warm to you. Its risk is shallowness. The pleasing goes on past what is true, and the open water goes stale for lack of a current.

### Reading
You are walking close to something that could hurt you. A person with power over you, or a moment with teeth in it. You will come through, and how you come through is the whole of it. Nothing here is won by being stronger than the strong thing. It is won by how you carry yourself while you are near it.

What this asks is a light step. Do not provoke what is bigger than you and do not shrink from it either. Know which is high and which is low. One of you is the sky and one the water, and they are not level. Say what is yours to say and no more. Underneath the care there should be gladness, because a step taken in fear is heavy and a heavy step is what wakes the tiger.

Each of these two forces has a place in the body. Heaven is the head, held up and clear. Lake is the mouth. The tiger has a mouth too. Yours is the one that decides whether the tiger's stays shut, so mind what comes out of it near the tail.

The posture is a foot already on the tail and the weight not yet on the foot. Keep that posture. The way you behave when nobody is making you is the ground you are walking on, and it either holds or it does not. Walk as yourself and walk lightly.

### Judgement

- It goes through.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ plain shoes on the accustomed road, walking alone. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
You set out in plain shoes on the road you already know. Nothing is put on for anyone and nothing above your station is wanted. Walk it alone if that is how it is and want only what is yours. Going forward like this there is no fault in it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a level road, and someone quiet walking it apart from the crowd. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
The road is flat and wide and you have it to yourself. You have stepped out of the crowd and its wants and you are not lonely in it. Nothing outside pulls you off the line. Keep straight and keep quiet and it goes well. Do not call out for company. The road is the company.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a squint called sight, a limp called a stride, and a bold foot on the tail. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
You can see a little and walk after a fashion. You step on the tail as if you could see, and the tiger turns. This is what happens when you take on more than your strength and will not know it. Only a soldier fighting for his lord gets to be that reckless. Know the size of what you have and step to it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the tail underfoot, and a wary tread. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
Your foot is on the tail and you know it. You are afraid in the right way. Watchful and light, firm where you need to be and soft everywhere else. Go on like that. Caution is what carries you through here, and in the end it goes well.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a fork in the road and a firm step taken at it. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
You come to the fork and you decide and you walk your choice with a whole heart. Your footing is right and so is your place. Even so there is danger in it. What is won by a clean decision can be lost in the moment after by carelessness. Stay upright and stay watchful now more than ever.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the whole road walked, looked back on from the top. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
At the end you stop and look back at the whole way you came. Every step is there to be read. Weigh what it tells you. If the treading was sound from start to finish, what comes round to you now is the best of fortune. It is worth celebrating.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Self-Obsession · _Gift:_ Naturalness · _Siddhi:_ Being

### Shadow — Self-Obsession
Attention turned in on yourself and stuck there is this energy at its lowest reach. You walk into a room and the room is about you. How you come across and whether you are getting it right and who you even are. It is old. Some part of you once had to put its own body first to stay alive, and the habit stayed after the danger went. Under it is a fear of losing yourself and the fear sends you looking. Who am I is the question. The search for the answer becomes a maze you can walk for years with plenty of drama and never any arrival. The more expert you become on yourself, the less you can see anyone else. Everyone you look at turns into someone you would like to change.

The way out is inside the looking. Watch the pattern without adding to it. Nothing needs to be judged or fixed. Seen plainly the search wears out on its own, because what it was hunting was never a thing that could be found. What is left when it stops is you, unwatched.

### Repressive nature — Self-Denying
This is someone who has turned the attention off themselves so completely that there is no one at the centre. Their life is lived for others and through others. It looks like kindness from outside. Whatever authority they need they find somewhere else, because looking in has been made unbearable.

### Reactive nature — Narcissistic
This one has room in their life for nobody else. The fear of being lost lives in them as anger and the anger goes at anyone who might take their freedom. Nothing of themselves can be yielded. They are in love with a self, and it is not the true one.

### Gift — Naturalness
Where this energy is met it becomes the ease of being what you already are. Nothing is performed. You do the thing the way you do it and the doing has play in it, the way a child draws. Your edges go soft. There is nothing to defend because you have stopped holding a position on yourself. Easy is right, and the easy path has friends along it and places to sit. This cannot be practised or copied. The moment being natural becomes a method it is the watching again in a new coat.

The obsession with yourself is what this gift is made from. You had to go looking for who you are in order to find out there is no one there to find, and the looking had to be real. When it wears out the tension around the question goes with it. What was tied up in the search comes back as energy. You lose the sense of being someone and feel at home in something wider. What you make from there is new because it is copied from no one. It is what comes out when you stop editing yourself.

### Siddhi — Being
At the far end there is nothing to find and no one looking. The question that started everything is not answered here. It is gone, the way a thing you were carrying is gone when you set it down. Life has no hidden meaning left in it and it is beautiful, one moment turning into the next. There is laughter in this because from here the whole search looks like a joke everyone is in on. It is ordinary. You would not spot it in a room.

This is what naturalness becomes when even the one being natural lets go. The road goes on under the feet. Being is what is left, and it is here.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 10, The Behaviour of the Self
Being yourself, through this lens, is a pressure on behaviour. Gate 10 is the part of you that wants what you do to match who you are, down to the small acts nobody is watching. Its gift is not performing. People relax around it. Someone at home in their own skin gives everyone near them leave to be at home in theirs. In you it runs as a refusal to act a part. You would rather be disliked as yourself than liked as someone else. When you catch yourself putting something on it feels like a lie in the body.

From outside this gets called self-absorbed or difficult. What is happening is that a self is being lived out loud instead of edited for the room. Living it takes attention. People who carry this all their lives learn to tell the attention that keeps behaviour honest from the attention that only watches. They tell them apart by how the body feels while it is doing each.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The self being lived out loud has its seat in the centre of the chest. That is the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life is pointed. Through this pressure it faces outward. The question here is how who you are shows in what you do.

In about six people in ten this centre is fixed. If it is fixed in you there is a self to be true to that does not move with the room. You may not like it every day and it is still there. What it needs is to be allowed to behave as itself. What starves it is a borrowed manner kept up to pass in a room. Keep it up for years and the heart goes out of you. In the other four the centre is open. If it is open in you there is no fixed self to be true to. Being yourself then means something different. It means meeting what is in front of you as whoever you are that day. It means letting the place you are in do half the work, because an open centre takes its sense of self from the room. What starves it is the effort to be somebody.

How it moves is by attraction. There is no engine in the chest. What a self lived plainly draws toward it is other people who can be plain near it.

### What completes it — the Channel of Being Yourself (10–20) and its kin
Being yourself has to come out somewhere or it stays a private truth. One of its wirings runs to Gate 20, The Now. That is the part of the throat that speaks and acts in the present moment. The Channel of Being Yourself is that self said out loud as it happens, with no rehearsal. The same drive also wires to Gate 34 and to Gate 57, and each pairing makes a different life. The other half of this one may be in you or in someone whose company makes you say true things.


## BODY

### Physiology
Looked at through the body, being yourself is a heart that sets its own beat. The heart is a muscle the size of your fist behind the breastbone and a little to the left. It has four rooms and doors between them. It has its own timekeeper. A patch of cells in its upper right wall fires on its own and sets the beat. Nothing tells it to. Cut every nerve to it and it goes on beating. The brain can only lean on the rate, faster when you climb stairs and slower when you sleep. Put two fingers on the side of your neck now and wait for it. That is a thing in you behaving as itself with no instruction at all.

Walk into a room where you feel looked at and the beat picks up. That is the nerves leaning on it and the leaning is all self-consciousness ever gets to do here. The beat was going before you noticed it and it goes on after. It never once needed you to watch it.

### Amino acid
The first push of any step you take draws on something the body built partly from arginine. Arginine is a building block the body can make for itself, though not always enough. It is in nuts and seeds and meat and beans.

With two others it makes creatine and creatine sits in your muscle holding a charge. When you push off, the charge is spent before your breath has changed and before any food you ate is reached for. It carries the muscle through the first seconds, a step or a start, while the slower fuels come in behind it. Arginine is also where the body gets the gas that lets its vessels widen. The step itself was paid for before you decided to take it.

Ease in This is where this walk ends up a season on. The strength that was beside you is now under you, held by something small. There the force waits under a thin check and you get on with the small steps. Here you walk past the force lightly. Open it when the danger has passed and the question is what to do with power that is now yours to hold.

### Inverse — UL 9, Ease in This
Upside down this is Ease in This again, so the bond is one and it is written above.

### Programming partner — UL 15, Ordinary Valiance
The opposite pole is Ordinary Valiance, the self that goes grey and hides in the crowd, afraid of being different. Yours is afraid of being nobody and cannot stop looking at itself. At their lowest the two feed each other. The one who follows the crowd and the one who flees it are running from the same fear. Open it when being yourself has turned into an effort to be unlike anyone.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Humanity
The family here is six, and between them they tell the one story every human life tells. The Mysteries Play is the wound you start with. Inner Light Symphony is the fight with your own shadows. Peral of Christos and Beyond Binary are the mind's limits and the need to control, both given up. Internal Treasure is the finding of your own nature. The Thunder of Your Giving is the waking that comes after. Open any to see where in the story you stand.

### Tarot

- **Ring Arcana — VII · The Chariot** (Control, Steer): Through its family comes the Chariot, a figure in a stone car drawn by two sphinxes and holding no reins. The two would pull apart and the car goes straight anyway. Meet this face when the steering has to come from who you are.

- **Lake (lower) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star kneels at the pool and pours one jug onto the land and one back into the water. Nobody is watching her do it. Meet her when the ease you are after turns out to be the absence of an audience.

Zhong Li Quan lost an army and came back from the mountains with a fan that wakes the dead. Ask either what it took to leave the part they were given. One answers with a tune, the other with a grin.
