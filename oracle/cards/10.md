# UL 10 — Internal Treasure

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Being Yourself · At Ease in Your Own Skin · A Light Step Near Power · Watching Your Every Step · Nothing to Defend · The Search Wearing Out · Ordinary, and Content

This energy is being yourself: at ease in your own skin, walking your own way, and it shows in how you move. Nobody is being performed for. You do the thing the way you do it, and nothing about it is heavy: a person walking as themselves has a lightness in the step that a performed walk never has.

Real ease has nothing to defend, because you have stopped holding a position on yourself. Easy is right, and those three words are the whole of it. Most people do not do easy: the harder you struggle the more you feel you exist, so the easy way is the one you pass every morning. What you do with ease has play in it, and people relax near it, because someone at home in themselves gives everyone else leave to be at home too.

Let the looking run down. There will be days when you cannot stop watching yourself, checking how you are coming across before you have said a word, and the checking is what stiffens the step. The easy road is there, seats along it, friends along it, and most people turn back from it because it asks so little. Walk the way you walk.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Lake: the whole of the sky, with a stretch of open water lying still and glad beneath it. Its creature is the tiger, whose tail you have stepped on, and the tiger does not bite. The old character shows a body with its repeating steps, one word for the path and for the way a person walks it.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
The sky is the one who holds the power here. It goes on without stopping, rising before you are ready, setting whether or not you have finished, and it does not bend down to ask how you are. In a family it is the father, and in the world it is whoever stands above you: the one with the say over your work or your standing. In you it is the drive to go straight at things and keep going. Its risk is drought, because a force that never gives way dries out whatever lies under it, and what it meant to warm it scorches.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Under it lies the lake, water that has stopped running and lies open to the sky. Everything that stands over it is taken in on its surface, and the surface stays glad. In a family it is the youngest daughter, and in the world it is the mouth: the meal, the joke that loosens a room. In you it is pleasure that shows, the part of you that says what it feels and warms people by it. Its risk is shallowness, pleasing that goes on past what is true, until the open water goes stale for want of a current.

### Reading
You are walking close to something that could hurt you: someone with power over you, or a moment with teeth in it. You will come through, and how you carry yourself while you are near it is the whole of the matter. Nothing here is won by being stronger than the strong thing. The tiger does not bite because there is no fear in the step, only a plain gladness at walking, and gladness provokes nothing.

What it asks is that you tell high from low and keep to your own place in it. Do not provoke what is bigger than you, and do not shrink from it either, because both are ways of watching it instead of walking. Say what is yours to say and no more. Keep your steps light, and want only what is already yours. This is the ground a life is built on, the way you behave when nobody is making you, and it either holds or it does not.

In the body the sky is the head and the lake is the mouth. Notice where you are holding yourself now: the head up and clear, the mouth easy, the weight not yet on the foot that is down. Walk as yourself, walk lightly, and the way that is dangerous becomes the way through.

In plain shoes on the road you already know, you are not calling anyone's attention. Nothing is put on for the watchers and nothing above your place is wanted. This is not the hour to be noticed, or to ask for company. Walk it alone, wanting only what is yours, and there is no fault in going on.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a level road, and someone quiet walking it apart from the crowd. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
The road is level and wide, you have it to yourself, out past the crowd with all it wants. Whoever walks here has left the loud life for a quieter one, and is not lonely in it, because time spent with yourself is how you learn who to be. Keep straight, keep quiet, and do not call out for company. The road is the company.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a squint called sight, a limp called a stride, and a bold foot on the tail. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
Seeing with one eye, walking with a limp, you step on the tail as if you could see, and this time the tiger turns. You have taken on more than your strength, pushed by what is behind you into what is in front. Only a soldier fighting for his lord gets to be that reckless. Know the size of what you have, and step to it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the tail underfoot, and a wary tread. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
Your foot is on the tail and you know it, which is the right kind of fear. Not the fright that freezes, which gets bitten, but a watchfulness that goes on walking, firm where it must be and soft everywhere else. A step checked before it lands is not a step lost. Go carefully all the way through, and it ends well.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a fork in the road and a firm step taken at it. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
At the fork you decide, and you walk your choice with your whole weight. Your footing is right, so is your place, and even so there is danger, because the higher you stand the further there is to fall. What was won by a clean choice is lost in the moment after by carelessness. Stay humble, stay watchful, and keep what you have gained.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the whole road walked, looked back on from the top. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
From the top you can see the whole road you came by, and what it tells is your fortune. Look at where you were glad, where you were not, and turn toward the glad, because a walk that matches its road is what luck is made of. If the treading was sound from first step to last, what comes round to you now is the best there is.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Self-Obsession · _Gift:_ Naturalness · _Siddhi:_ Being

### Shadow — Self-Obsession
Attention turned in on yourself, and stuck there, is where this energy starts. You walk into a room and the room is about you: how you are coming across, who you even are. Most of the time it hides in busyness: the hours filled with work or errands, the resting put off until Friday, until the holidays. At the end a life has gone by. Underneath is a fear of losing yourself, and the fear sends you looking. Who am I is the question. The search for an answer becomes a maze with plenty of drama in it and no way out, because the one thing the search makes sure of is that you never have to face yourself. As long as you are looking at yourself you cannot see anyone else, and everyone you look at becomes someone you would like to change.

Nothing here needs judging or fixing. See the pattern and add nothing to it. The search wears out on its own once it is seen, because what it was hunting was never a thing that could be found. What is left when it stops is you, unwatched.

### Repressive nature — Self-Denying
This person has turned the attention off themselves so completely that nobody is home at the centre. Their life is lived for others, through others, and from outside it passes for kindness. It is compromise all the way down. Whatever authority they need they find in someone else, because looking in has been made unbearable.

### Reactive nature — Narcissistic
Here is someone with no room in their life for anybody else. The fear of being lost lives in them as anger, and the anger goes at whoever might take their freedom. They cannot yield a part of themselves to anyone. They are in love with a self, and it is not the true one.

### Gift — Naturalness
Being what you already are, with nothing put on, is the ease this energy is made for. You do the thing the way you do it, and the doing has play in it, the way a child draws. You are not your name, or your feelings, or anything else a label could be put on. Once that is felt the tightness around the question goes out of the body, and what was tied up in the search comes back as energy. Your edges soften. There is nothing to defend, because you have stopped holding a position on yourself. What you make from here is new, because it is copied from nobody. If it is playful it is yours. If you have heard it somewhere before, it is not.

This cannot be practised or copied. The moment being natural becomes a method it is the watching again in a new coat. Nothing you feel is unnatural, the laziness or the pain, and seeing that is what softens you. Softer, more open, more yielding: that is the whole of the way, and it has no path.

### Siddhi — Being
There is nothing to find here and no one looking for it. The question that started everything is gone, the way a thing you were carrying is gone once you set it down. Life has no hidden meaning left in it, and it is beautiful, one moment turning into the next. There is laughter in this, because from here the whole search looks like a joke everyone is in on. It is so ordinary you would not spot it in a room. If you stayed near it long enough, the one thing you would notice is how unrested you are.

This is what ease becomes when even the one being easy lets go. There has never been anything to do, because there is no one to do it, and so everything gets done. It is the plainest state there is.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 10, The Behaviour of the Self
The pressure here is on behaviour: that what you do should match who you are, down to the small acts nobody sees. Gate 10 is the door it comes through, and its other name is self-love, because the two turn out to be one thing. When you are at home in yourself the people near you relax and are freed to be at home in theirs. When you are not, it is unbearable, and the pressure turns outward into blame, or into a hard word that cuts. In you it runs as a refusal to act a part: you would rather be disliked as yourself than liked as someone else. Putting something on feels like a lie in the body.

From outside this can look like self-absorption, and sometimes it is, because a self being lived out loud takes a great deal of attention. People who carry this all their lives learn to tell the attention that keeps behaviour honest from the attention that only watches. They tell them apart by how the body feels while it does each.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
This pressure sits in the centre of the chest, in the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life faces. It is the heart of the whole chart. It has no engine. It works like a magnet with a single pole, only ever drawing: the people a life needs are pulled toward it, the work too. The way you behave is that self showing its face. When you trust it, what you do and who you are line up without effort. When you do not, you go to war with what it was already bringing you.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on: the sense of self holds, whoever they are with. For someone like that there is a self to be true to, which does not move with the room, and what starves it is a borrowed manner kept up to pass. Keep that up for years and the heart goes out of you. In the other four it is not switched on, and the self is borrowed from whoever is nearest, then let go when they leave. For someone like that, being yourself means meeting what is in front of you as whoever you are that day. Place does half the work: the wrong seat in a room can put them out of themselves. What starves them is the effort to become somebody.

### What completes it — the Channel of Being Yourself (10–20) and its kin
Being yourself has to come out somewhere or it stays private. One wiring runs to Gate 20 in the throat, the part of you that speaks or acts in the present moment. The two make the Channel of Being Yourself: the self said out loud as it happens, which wakes the same thing in whoever hears it. The same gate reaches Gate 34, the power to act, and Gate 57, the knowing in the gut, one door onto three rooms. Whichever half sits in you, or in someone near you, behaviour becomes a life only where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
This energy lives in the heart, and the reason is the beat. Nothing tells your heart how to beat. A patch of cells in its own wall sets the pace, and a heart cut from every nerve, as a transplanted heart is, goes on beating. The nerves only lean on it, faster or slower. It is the one part of you that behaves entirely as itself, which is what being yourself is from inside, a thing that was already going. The heart is the sovereign of the body, ruling by being in place rather than by doing. Your presence lives there, the thing in a face that tells you someone is home.

It sits behind the breastbone, left of the middle. Lay a hand flat there, or two fingers at the side of your neck, and wait for it. When you feel watched the beat picks up, and that is all the watching ever gets to do here: hurry it. It was going before you noticed, and it goes on after you stop.

### Amino acid
Being yourself has a chemistry in you, and the substance is arginine. It is one of the twenty small pieces every part of you is built from, and one your body makes for itself. Once you are grown you do not have to eat it. When you were a child your body could not make enough of it, and took the rest in from meat or a handful of nuts. Everything this energy is kin to shares the one substance, a single chemistry for the whole family, most of which is about the heart, about what happens when it is trusted. So ease is not added to you from outside. It is there inside you already, the moment you stop arguing with your life. Its substance was made in your own body, on its own, without a thought from you.

### Immortals
Han Xiangzi and Zhong Li Quan walked off the road laid down for them. Han Xiangzi was raised for the examinations and took a flute into the hills instead. Zhong Li Quan lost an army and came back from the mountains with a fan that wakes the dead. Ask either what it took to leave the part they were given. One answers with a tune, the other with a grin.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Cancer:** the crab, soft inside a shell it carries everywhere, at home wherever it walks. The Chariot's own sign, and the plain fact that a self needs an edge to move from.
- **Hebrew letter — Cheth, the Fence:** the fence round a field, the line that says where you end so that you can walk right up to it and not past it.
