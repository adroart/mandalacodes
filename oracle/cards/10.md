# UL 10 — Internal Treasure

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Being Yourself · The Question Who Am I · Self-Watching · Naturalness · Walking Near Danger · A Soft Step · Letting Go of the Search

Somewhere early you started watching yourself, and the watching never quite stopped. You walk into a room and the room is about you, how you come across, whether you are getting this right, who you even are. It runs deep, this turning inward, older than you, the same pull that once made a body put itself first so it could survive at all. So you go looking. You want to find yourself, name yourself, finally know who you are, and the looking can become the whole of a life, a maze with you walking it, full of effort and drama, the one thing it spares you being any arrival.

The trap is that the self you are hunting slips the moment you reach for it, the way a reflection slips. The more of an expert you become in yourself, the further off you drift. There is a quieter version of the same grip, the one that erases itself instead and lives so entirely for everyone else that there is no one left at the center. Both are the chest closed around something it is afraid to lose. You can feel where it lives, high in the body, a tightness behind the breastbone that pulls everything in.

What ends it is not more looking. It is the looking wearing out. The question stops getting answered and starts getting dropped, and what is left is just you, behaving, the way water behaves, with no second copy of you standing behind it checking the work. You cannot practise this. The moment naturalness becomes a method it is the watching again. It arrives sideways, as your edges softening, the chest unclenching on its own, a step that no longer braces for the tiger. You were never going to find yourself. You were going to stop holding on, and walk.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven sits over Lake. The hardest, highest force in the world stands directly above the softest, most open one, and the soft does not flee. It walks. The name of this hexagram, Lü, is a single character whose old picture is a body and a repeating footstep, a person putting one foot in front of the other along a path. It also carries conduct, the way you actually move through a thing, and even your means of living. The whole hexagram is the open one treading near the strong one without provoking it.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven, Qian. Three firm lines, the force that never stops, dry and tireless and pressing forward. The old gloss is sprouts and vapours rising into sunlight, the same heat that ripens a field and the same heat that scorches it. Above, this trigram is the strength you are walking under, the power that could turn on you.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake, Dui. Two firm lines under one yielding line, an open surface of still water with vapour rising off it. The mouth, exchange, glad speech. The youngest daughter, pleased and responsive. Below, this trigram is the lightness in the step, the satisfaction that lets you walk where you should be afraid.

### Reading
The situation this hexagram names is walking your own path across ground that could hurt you. The picture the old text gives is treading on a tiger's tail. The tiger does not bite. That is the whole strange teaching. You are close enough to the danger to step on it, and you come through, not because you fought it and not because you fled, but because of how you carried yourself while you were near it.

What carries you is the lower trigram, the glad open water that does not snap. Above is sheer strength. Below is pleased responsiveness. The open one treads on the solid one and is not eaten, because it does not provoke. It keeps its footsteps light. It knows how high the sky is and how deep the lake is, and it does not pretend the two are equal. Know the high from the low. Pick which battles are yours.

The path here is also conduct, the way you behave when no one strong is forcing you to. Most of the line readings turn on this. The plain shoe of the commoner who does not pine above his rank. The recluse on the level road who walks alone and is content. And against them, the one-eyed man who insists he sees and the lame man who insists he walks well, who tread the tail too boldly and are bitten. The danger is never the tiger. It is overreaching, mistaking your own bravado for capacity. Walk what is actually yours to walk, lightly, and the fierce thing lets you pass.

### Judgement
- Treading on the tiger's tail. It does not bite.
- This is conduct, and conduct grows you.
- The supple walks on the solid, and is glad, and is not disheartened.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the one-eyed man sure he sees, the lame man sure he walks. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
He squints and calls it sight. He limps and calls it striding. He treads the tiger's tail like this, and it bites. A crude soldier declares himself a ruler. The misfortune is not bad luck. It is taking on what is past your strength and refusing to know it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the tail underfoot, and the held breath. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
The same tiger's tail, but now the one treading it is afraid in the right way. Watch out, watch out. Apprehensive, wary, firm where firmness is needed and soft where it is not. The fear is not weakness here. It is accuracy. Hold it through, and in the end there is good fortune.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the resolute tread, the parting of ways. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
Guai, to fork, to cut off, to decide. You walk decisively now, following your own heart without overstepping. The position is correct. And still there is danger. Even right action, taken from strength, has to be guarded, or you lose what you have just won. Be upright. Stay watchful in the very moment of deciding.

**Line 6** · _image:_ looking back over the whole walked road. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
At the top, you stop and turn around. Observe the treading. Examine the omen the whole course gives. Xuan, to return to the same point, to come round again. If every step has been sound, the return is auspicious, and there is much to celebrate. Not a new road. The old one, reviewed and found good.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Self-Obsession · _Gift:_ Naturalness · _Siddhi:_ Being

### Shadow — Self-Obsession
This is the part of you that can only see itself. It begins as something necessary, the force that once made an early human put its own body first so it would survive at all. It tightens everything inward. The trouble is that it never lets go of the lens. You walk into a room and the room is about you, how you look in it, what it thinks of you, whether you are doing this right.

Most people read the unsettled, self-watching feeling as a problem to solve by looking harder at the self. So the looking becomes the whole life. The search for who you are turns into a labyrinth you never leave, full of drama and effort, and the one thing it spares you is ever actually arriving anywhere.

The fear underneath is precise. It is the fear of losing your identity, of not being anyone in particular. That fear sends you chasing a definition that keeps slipping, the way a reflection slips when you reach for it. The more expert you become in yourself, the further you drift from yourself.

Here is the part worth sitting with. The labyrinth has a door, and it is the same door you came in by. The self-obsession is not a flaw to defeat. It is where the inward road has to start. You cannot transcend a thing you will not first admit you are doing.

### Repressive nature — Self-Denying
Some people meet the self-obsession by reversing it completely. They erase themselves, live entirely for others, become useful and absent at once. It looks like selflessness. It is a person with no centre, projecting all worth outward because facing the self felt worse.

### Reactive nature — Narcissistic
Others throw it outward as anger. They deny that anyone else quite exists, guard their freedom against an imagined thief, and cannot yield a single part of themselves. The relationships are the hardest thing of all. They are in love with themselves, but not the true one.

### Gift — Naturalness
The exhaustion is the turn. The self-watching wears itself out, the question of who you are stops getting answered and starts getting dropped, and what is left is just you, behaving. Not performing yourself. Behaving the way water behaves, or a child at play behaves, without a second copy of you standing behind it checking the work.

This is naturalness, and you cannot practise it. There is no method, no being-natural technique, because the moment it becomes a technique it is the self watching again. It arrives sideways, as a softening of your edges. A great allowing. The grip on the question loosens and a huge amount of held energy spills out into the life as plain creativity and a kind of play that was waiting the whole time.

The old Taoists called the nearest thing to it wu wei, the doing that is not forcing. You stop arguing with life and discover that life was handling most of it anyway. The hardest part is the easiness. We are a species that trusts struggle, that feels more real the harder we push. So the easy path, the one with seats placed in good spots and small tea-houses along it, stays empty. Most walk past it on the way to somewhere they think is harder and therefore truer.

### Siddhi — Being
The differentiated self, having finally flowered, dissolves. Not into nothing. Into being, which is too simple to point at. There is no longer an I looking out. There is just consciousness, here, expressed as whatever this moment is turning into.

From here the great human seriousness looks like a game, a play put on with enormous conviction, and a lightness comes that the unawakened often mistake for not caring. It is the opposite. Nothing has been excluded. Self and not-self, mind and form, all of it floats in something like love, with no one standing outside it to keep score.

The strange thing is how ordinary it is. You might pass such a person and feel only their plainness, or you might sit near them and feel, with a small shock, how tightly you have been holding on. The self-obsession at the start was never the enemy. It was the long way round to here, the only road there was, and it ran the whole time toward a stillness that was always underneath it, waiting to be noticed.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 10, The Behaviour of the Self
This code is how a person actually moves through the world when no one is making them. Not what they believe about themselves, not what they say. The behaviour. The way the body conducts itself, moment to moment, and whether that conduct comes from who they truly are or from who they think they are supposed to be. A person carrying this code feels it as a pull toward acting in line with their own nature, and a real discomfort whenever they betray it.

There is a particular weight here, the work of loving the self enough to live as the self. That sounds soft and is not. It means standing on your own values when the room wants you to fold, and it also means catching the moment when self-respect curdles into self-importance.

The misread is to call this self-centredness, or vanity, or making everything about you. It can collapse into those. But underneath, the genuine drive is the opposite of vanity. It is the slow, unglamorous business of becoming someone whose outsides and insides finally match.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
Where this drive lives in you is the body's seat of identity and direction, the still centre the whole rest of the system organises around. It is the part that holds who you are and which way your life is pointed. The placement is the entire teaching. Here, behaviour is not a surface manner you could swap out. It is wired straight into the sense of self.

This centre does not push or strive. It magnetises. It draws toward you the people, the work, the places that match what you actually are, and it does this by being, not by chasing. So the conduct this code governs is not something you perform outward to get a result. It is something you let be true, and the right things arrive because of it. Force it from the head, manage how you come across, and the magnet fouls. Live the behaviour honestly and the direction sets itself.

### What completes it — the Channel of Being Yourself (10–20) and its kin
This code is unusual. Most gates reach toward a single other to finish their circuit. This one sits at a crossroads and reaches three ways. Toward the part of you that speaks in the living present, so the self gets expressed out loud, behaviour becoming voice. Toward the part that holds raw power, so the self has the force to follow its own convictions into action. Toward the part that hears danger before the mind does, so the self moves by a quiet inner knowing rather than by thought.

Alone, this code is a strong, true sense of self with no way out. It knows who it is and cannot yet show it, act it, or trust its own instinct enough to live it. Joined down any of the three roads, the identity stops being private. It becomes expression, or conduct, or survival lived as the self. The code is built to need more than one completion, which is why a person carrying it can feel pulled in several directions at once before any of them lands.

## BODY

### Physiology
The body seats this code in the chest, around the heart, the warm room at the centre that the old traditions tied to self-love long before anyone measured it. When the self-obsession of the shadow grips, it grips here. The chest tightens, the bearing closes, a person braces around the heart the way you brace around something you are afraid to lose. The whole posture of self-protection is felt in this one region, the inward pull made flesh.

The gift has its bodily floor here too, and it is a loosening rather than an effort. The chest opens, not because you decided to open it, but because the gripping wore out and the warmth came back on its own. The bearing softens. A person who has stopped guarding the heart moves differently, easier in the shoulders, lighter on the feet, the way the hexagram's glad lower water treads near danger without snapping.

This is the centre the rest of the body orients toward, the seat of how you carry yourself. Naturalness is not finally a thought you have. It is a chest that has stopped defending itself, and a step that no longer braces for the tiger.

### Amino acid
The chemistry of this code answers to Arginine, an amino acid the body can sometimes make for itself and sometimes, under stress or in early growth, cannot, so it has to be supplied. It does one quiet, vital job. It is the raw material the body turns into nitric oxide, the small molecule that tells the blood vessels to relax and open, that lets the flow widen and the warmth reach the edges.

There is a teaching sitting at the bottom of that. The code of self-love runs on the chemistry of opening. Not of tightening, not of armouring, but of the vessel softening its walls so that what is inside can move freely and reach the whole body. The shadow grips and the flow narrows. The gift relaxes and the flow widens.

The code runs all the way down to this. Naturalness, in the end, is a kind of letting open, written even into the way the blood is allowed to move.
