# UL 10 — Internal Treasure

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Being Yourself · At Ease in Your Skin · A Light Step Near Power · Nothing to Defend · Content Being Ordinary · Watching Your Every Step · Searching for Yourself

Ease lives in you as being at home in your own skin, through an ordinary day. You do things the way you do them, and nobody is being performed for. Your step stays light, even around people who could make things hard for you. Those near you relax, because your ease leaves them free to be at home in themselves. It is a treasure most people pass every day without stopping.

Some days you cannot stop watching yourself, checking how you come across before you have said a word. That watching is what makes your step stiff. See it without judging it, and the looking wears itself out.

Take the easy way when it is there, even when the hard way feels more like you. The harder you struggle, the more you feel you exist, so ease can feel like doing nothing. Once you stop defining yourself, you feel part of something much wider. What you make from here has play in it, and it is new because you copied it from no one.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Lake is the hexagram of how you walk when something stronger is near. Heaven is the strength in you that keeps pressing forward and never tires. Lake is the part of you that is open and glad and says what it feels. The strength sits above the gladness because most days you walk under a power bigger than your own. This hexagram has long been read as stepping on a tiger's tail and not being bitten. A glad step provokes no one, so whoever holds the power lets you pass. You come through close danger by walking as yourself.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the drive that carries your day forward, from the moment you wake to the moment you stop. It sits on top because it is the outer side of your situation, the part other people see and meet. Heaven has long been read as the sky, which turns without resting and never bends down. A drive like that pushes on whether or not you are ready. On its own, it keeps going until it dries out whatever lies under it.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Lake is the ease in you that speaks: you say what you feel, and people warm to you for it. It lies underneath because this openness is felt inside, under the strength that shows. Lake has long been read as open water, whose surface takes in the whole sky. When you are open like that, you take in what is above you without fighting it. On its own, this openness gathers and rests, and it moves nowhere.

### Reading
You are walking close to a power bigger than you, someone with a say over your life, in a mood that could turn. Your task is to get through without being hurt. Nothing here is won by being stronger than the strong one. Keep your step light and your manner glad, because a glad step provokes nobody.

Know what is above you and what is below you, and pick your battles. Do not provoke the one who can bite, and do not freeze in fear either, because fright gets bitten too. Keep your kindness, and do not turn fierce yourself to feel safe. You are the one steering here, and others rise or fall with your choices. Go at a steady pace, neither rushing nor holding back.

Your feet do the work now. One goes, then the other, firm and soft by turns. Walk like that, and the dangerous stretch is how you come through.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a level road, and someone quiet walking it apart from the crowd. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
The road is level and wide, and you walk it apart from the crowd. You have left the loud life for a quieter one, and you are not lost in it. Time spent with yourself is how you learn to be yourself. You do not have to be who others think you are. Keep to this road with nothing to hide, and it goes well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a squint called sight, a limp called a stride, and a bold foot on the tail. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
You squint and call it seeing, you limp and call it a stride, and you step on the tail as if nothing could happen. This time the tiger bites. You have taken on more than your strength can carry, pushed from behind into a fight in front. A soldier set on a king's throne falls fast. Know the size of what you have, and step no further.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the tail underfoot, and a wary tread. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
Your foot is on the tail, and you know it. Caution is right here, but panic gets bitten. Stay watchful and keep walking, firm where you must be and flexible everywhere else. Take care with each step to the last one, and it ends well.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a fork in the road and a firm step taken at it. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
At a fork in the road you choose, and you walk your choice with your whole weight. You are right, and you stand in the right place, and there is still danger. The higher you stand, the further you can fall. Stay humble once the choice is made, and keep watching the road.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the whole road walked, looked back on from the end. → becomes Hexagram 58, The Joyous Lake.
From the end of the road you look back over the whole of it. Notice where you walked gladly and where you did not. Turn toward what made you glad, and your walking and your road agree. If every step has been sound, the best fortune there is comes round to you now.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Self-Obsession · _Gift:_ Naturalness · _Siddhi:_ Being

### Shadow — Self-Obsession
Your attention turns in on yourself and stays there. You worry about your clothes, your future and what people think of you. Even when you look calm, you are running from yourself into work and busyness, and rest is put off until Friday or the holidays. Underneath is a fear of losing who you are, so you set out to find yourself. The search becomes a maze full of drama, where you never have to face yourself. While you look at yourself you cannot see anyone else, and everyone you look at becomes someone you would like to change.

You need no judging and no fixing here. See the pattern in your life, and add nothing to it. The search wears itself out once you see that what it hunts cannot be found.

### Repressive nature — Self-Denying
This person has turned their attention away from themselves altogether. Their life is lived for others and through others, and from outside it can pass for kindness. It is compromise from end to end, with no centre of its own. Whatever authority they need, they look for in someone else.

### Reactive nature — Narcissistic
Nobody else has room in this person's life. Their fear of losing themselves comes out as anger at whoever might take their freedom. They cannot give up any part of themselves to someone else. They are in love with a self, and it is not the true one.

### Gift — Naturalness
Naturalness is being what you already are, and nothing more. You are not your name, your feelings or your beliefs, and no label fits you. Once you feel that, the tightness around who you are lets go, and what the search held comes back as energy. It shows as play, and what you make is new because you copied it from nobody. You have nothing to defend, because you hold no position on yourself. The easy way opens in front of you, with places to rest along it and friends walking it.

You cannot practise this or copy it, because it has no method. It dawns as you see that everything in you is natural, the laziness and the pain included. Your edges soften, and you grow more open and more yielding.

### Siddhi — Being
Being is what is left when even the question of who you are has gone. There is nothing to find and no one looking for it. Life has no hidden meaning, only the beauty of this moment turning into the next. You see the whole search as a joke, and you laugh. This state is so ordinary that you would pass it in a room. Stay near it a while, and you would notice how tense you are yourself. There has never been anything to do, because there is no one to do it, and everything still gets done.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 10, The Behaviour of the Self
Gate 10 sits in the middle of your chest and presses you to act as who you are. Its other name is self-love. When you love yourself, the people near you are freed to love themselves. When you do not, it is unbearable, and you push the feeling away by blaming others. At its best you live by your own values and stand up for them without fear of judgment. People follow the example you set more than anything you say. Left unchecked, it can turn into self-centredness.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The Identity Center sits in the middle of your chest and holds who you are and which way your life goes. It works like a magnet with one pole, only ever drawing. It draws the people, the work and the places your life needs. When you do not trust yourself, you fight against what it brings you.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They have a steady sense of who they are, whoever they are with, and they walk their own path. Following someone else's direction makes them lose heart. The other four have it open. Who they are shifts with the people around them, and they can fit in anywhere. Place matters to them, down to the house they live in and where they sit in a restaurant. They are not on the way to becoming somebody. They meet what is in front of them as whoever they are that day.

### What completes it — the Channel of Being Yourself (10–20) and its kin
On its own, this gate is a self that no one hears. Gate 20, in the throat, is the drive to speak and act in the present moment. Together they make the Channel of Being Yourself, which says who you are out loud as it happens. It also reaches Gate 34, where being yourself becomes action. A third wiring runs to Gate 57, where it becomes a gut sense of what to do next. Gate 20 may be in you or in a person close by, and the channel runs only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
Your heart beats as itself, and that is why this energy is given to the heart. Nothing tells it how to beat. A patch of cells in its own wall sets the pace, about once a second. A heart cut from every nerve goes on beating, as a transplanted heart does. The nerves only make it faster or slower. The heart rules the body by being in place, and the presence in your face comes from it.

It sits behind the breastbone, most of it left of the middle. Lay a flat hand there, or two fingers at the side of your neck. When you feel watched or afraid the beat quickens, and when that passes it settles. It was beating before you noticed, and it goes on when you stop.

### Amino acid
Being yourself has a chemistry in you, and one substance in it is arginine. It is one of the twenty pieces every protein in you is built from. Your body makes it for itself, so as an adult you do not need to eat it. As a growing child you could not make enough, and you took the rest from meat, milk or a handful of nuts.

You share this substance with a family of energies, and much of that family is about trusting your heart. Your body was making it before you knew your own name. It needs no effort from you, and it goes on whether or not you argue with your life.

## RELATIONS

_UL 9 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the small steps that store up, where this one walks on as itself._

### Pair — UL 9, Ease in This (Small Taming)
This energy walks on as itself, and it runs out when there is no strength left to walk on with. Ease in This is where it goes next: small steps that store up strength toward something far off. Open it when you have been moving for a long time and need to gather yourself before the next stretch. Walking on and storing up take turns.

### Inverse — UL 9, Ease in This (Small Taming)
Turn this energy upside down and walking on becomes storing up. Ease in This stays and gathers, where this energy keeps moving. Open it when you have walked far enough and it is time to pause.

### Programming partner — UL 15, Ordinary Valiance
This energy is at ease being different, and at its lowest it runs from the crowd at any cost. Ordinary Valiance holds the other side: the fear of being different, which makes people follow the crowd. The one who follows the crowd and the one who flees it are both kept from being themselves. Open it when being yourself has turned into trying to be unlike everyone else.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Humanity
This energy finds your true self, and that is one chapter of a longer story. It belongs to a family of six, the Ring of Humanity, whose theme is the story every human life tells. UL 25, The Mysteries Play, carries the wound you start with. UL 38, Inner Light Symphony, carries the fight with your own shadows. UL 17, Pearl of Christos, carries the limits of the mind. UL 21, Beyond Binary, carries letting go of control. UL 51, Unshakable Arrival, carries the waking. This one carries finding yourself.
