# UL 2 — Beyond the Shell

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Being Carried · The Ground Under You · Found by Following · Letting It Come · Trusted Before You Speak · The Lost Feeling · Already Home

Something has been handed to you and it is yours to bring through. The bringing does not need you out in front of it. You can feel how much is being held and how little you are doing to hold it, and the ease of that is what being carried feels like. The ground under you is doing what ground does.

The pull here is to take the wheel. Being behind a thing feels like being nowhere, and the feeling says that if you set the direction yourself then at least the road is yours. The road was never in doubt. It only needed you on it.

Stay behind it and stay soft. What you are carrying knows where it is going, and it takes you along if you let it. Let things come to you instead of going out to get them. The feeling of being lost is only the feeling of not leading. You were on the way the whole time.

## ICHING

### Combination
The ground, and under the ground more ground. Nothing in it rises. Nothing in it strikes. Whatever falls onto it is taken in. Whatever is set into it comes up in its own time. This is the open field with the whole sky for a ceiling and the same field underneath it, holding up everything that stands on it.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the ground that carries everything. It is the field under every foot. What falls on it stays and rots and comes up again as grass. It never moves first. It gives way, and by giving way it holds. In a person this is the part of you that can take a thing in and bear it. You hear the bad news and nothing in you drops. You let the other person speak first and carry the house and the work. Its risk is going inert. The giving way stops being strength and becomes lying down, taking whatever comes with no yes or no left in it.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
The same Earth again, underneath where nobody sees it. Down here it is the dark of the soil where a seed lies through the winter before anything shows. It is the mother in the world. Every living thing came out of her and lies back down on her at the end. In a person this is deep patience. You can wait without needing proof that the waiting is working. You hold what you were given and keep it warm and say nothing. Its risk is heaviness. The waiting goes on past its season and turns into sleep, and what was held gets held too long and spoils.

### Reading
You are being asked to carry something through rather than drive it. Carrying is work of its own. A thing has been set in your hands and it wants bringing to completion, and the bringing is quiet. You will want to get out in front of it and set the pace, so that at least the direction is yours. Go first here and you lose the way at once. Come behind, and what is worth following shows itself.

What this asks is a mare's kind of strength. She can run as far as any horse alive and still she runs behind, along the ground, going where it goes. Keep to your own kind while this lasts. Your kind are the ones who grow things and make things and wait for them. The ones who charge and climb you will lose for now and losing them is part of it. Stay where your footing is sure and the rest takes care of itself.

This force has one place in the body and here it holds it twice over. The belly. The soft front of you that takes in food and carries weight and was never once told how. Put a hand there now and feel how little it is doing and how much it is holding.

Step out ahead of the ground you stand on and you are out of place at once. Hold your place and everything you were given comes to its end in your hands.

### Judgement
- The Receptive. From the source it goes through and grows. Holding firm the way a mare does brings it through.
- Set out to lead and you go astray. Follow, and you find the one worth following.
- Friends in the south and west. Friends lost in the north and east. Rest in what is steady, and it goes well.

### Image
- The ground lies open under all things. That is the earth's power.
- So the wise make themselves broad, and carry the world.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ frost underfoot, and the hard ice still to come. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
You step out one morning and the ground is white. Nothing has frozen yet. The frost is the first small sign of a cold that will set hard later. Nothing here is dangerous yet. What it asks is that you notice the small thing while it is small, and take care from the first step, because what sets slowly cannot easily be broken.

**Line 2** · _image:_ straight, square and wide, with nothing rehearsed. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
When you act from the grain of what you are, the act comes out straight. It keeps its own honest shape and it leaves nobody out. Nothing has to be rehearsed for this and nothing has to be repeated. You do the plain thing once as yourself and it is right everywhere at once. Trying would spoil it. Do it, and others fall into step behind you without being called.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a gift kept under the coat, and another's work brought to its end. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
You are good at this and the time to show it has not come. Keep what you know inside a closed mouth. If the work belongs to someone above you, do it well and let the credit be theirs. See it through to the end without a word about your part. There will be no finished glory here with your name on it. There will be the thing brought to its end, and the quiet of having done it is yours to keep.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the sack tied shut at the neck. → becomes Hexagram 16, Enthusiasm.
A closed season. Nothing you think gets out and nothing from outside gets in, and the bag is tied at the neck with one emptiness on both sides of the knot. Nobody will blame you for it and nobody will praise you. For now that is exactly right. Keep your plans to yourself. The knot comes loose in its own season, and when it does there is a gladness in it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the yellow garment worn low, under the rest. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
Yellow is the colour of the ground and of the middle of things. The garment is worn at the waist, under everything and on show to nobody. You are trusted before you speak. What is good in you shows through of itself and needs no announcing. Having to say what you are is the sign you are not it yet. Wear the good thing low and the ones who belong near you gather on their own.

**Line 6** · _image:_ two dragons fighting in the open country, and blood on the ground in the colours of sky and earth. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
At the top the yielding has gone hard. It has turned to fight the very thing it was made to receive. Two dragons tear at each other out in the wild and the blood on the grass is dark and yellow. The way has run out, and both sides bleed. If you are the one who set out to rule, stop now. What is torn away here was never meant to be held by force.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dislocation · _Gift:_ Orientation · _Siddhi:_ Unity

### Shadow — Dislocation
The lowest form of this energy is a feeling of having stepped off the world. You stand on the same ground as everyone and it does not feel as if it holds you. Somewhere far back a wrong turn was taken and you cannot say where. Since then you have been out of place in every room. It comes out as busyness. The day fills and the load goes onto the shoulders and the hands stay moving, so the hollow underneath cannot be heard. Under the motion is a certainty that life is happening at you and you are not in it. The usual move is to fix the outside. A new plan or a new town or a new job, anything that might close the distance. The relief lasts a few weeks.

You have never left the ground. You cannot, and there was no wrong turn. The feeling is real as a feeling and false as a fact, and it changes the moment it is fully felt instead of outrun. Let it soak all the way in. What you were running from was the longing for home, and the longing is the first sign of the way back.

### Repressive nature — Lost
Here is someone who has let go of the thread. Nothing larger is expected any more. They follow what serves them this week and call the flatness being sensible. With no felt connection to anything beyond their own life the days have no point they can name, and they get through them.

### Reactive nature — Regimented
There is also the person who lays a grid over life. A system for everything, a doctrine that promises to hold the chaos still. It looks like order. It runs on old anger. The grid keeps the lost feeling at a distance without touching it, and the harder life pushes the tighter it is drawn.

### Gift — Orientation
Met and let in, this energy is the gift of finding your way by being carried. You stop trying to steer and the ground starts moving you. Things begin to line up on their own. The book falls open at the page, the person you needed turns up, and you are in the right town without having planned it. None of that can be forced. It comes from softening. You drop the agenda and let whatever arrives arrive. Your timing sharpens until being out of step registers in the body as plainly as hunger. Your own face starts to show. The less you push, the more of you there is.

The feeling of being off the world is what this gift is made from. Felt all the way through, the lostness turns out to be the longing that points home. Once you can feel which way it points you are no longer lost. What you do with it for other people is the strange part. To orient someone you give no advice. You sit with them wanting nothing from them and in your company they find out that being afraid is allowed.

### Siddhi — Unity
At the height of it you are home and you know it. The seeking is over. It ended by finding out there was never a distance to cross. There is the ground and there is you and no line where one stops and the other starts. Two people standing together are already a third thing, the pair itself, and there was never a two on its own. Being home is ordinary. It is the plainest thing you have ever felt, and it holds everything.

This is what the gift of being carried becomes when the one being carried lets go. Nothing is leading now and nothing is following. Life goes on moving and you are the ground it moves over, the way the seasons move over a field. What is left is soft.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 2, Direction of the Self
Through this lens, the energy of being carried is the drive that gives a life its direction by receiving it. Gate 2 is the part of you that knows which way your life faces. It knows the way a field knows which way is downhill, by taking in what falls and letting it run. In you it runs as a pull toward a direction you cannot always justify out loud. Someone asks why you are going that way and the honest answer is that it is the way. It does not make the road. It feels where the road already goes. What belongs on the road is drawn toward you.

From outside a person moved like this looks like someone waiting. Nobody can see a direction being received, so they see the not-deciding and call it drift. What is happening is that the deciding is done by something quieter than the head and it works by letting things in. The people who carry this learn to let the direction arrive, and to let help in when it comes instead of carrying the whole road alone.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
Seen through this lens, the energy of being carried runs through the centre of your chest, the Identity Centre. It holds who you are and where you are going, and those turn out to be one thing. It has no engine in it and works the way a magnet works. What a life needs is drawn toward it, and the direction of the life is the sum of what it drew.

In about six people in ten this centre is defined, fixed in place. If it is defined in you, there is a steady sense of self that does not move with the room and a direction that holds across years. What starves it is walking someone else's road. Do that for long and you lose heart. In the other four the centre is open. If it is open in you, you take on the identity of whoever is near and make it louder. You may have been many things in one life. Place matters more than anything. The wrong house or the wrong seat in a restaurant can put you out of yourself, and the right ground gives you back. What starves an open centre is trying to be somebody.

This energy does not pulse and it does not surge. It draws quietly all the time. What it needs from you is to hold still for long enough to feel which way you are already being pulled.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Beat (2–14)
What this drive receives has to be spent by something, or it stays a heading with nobody walking it. Its other half is Gate 14, Power Skills. That is the drive low in the body that works and makes more than one person needs. The Beat is what runs between them. The direction stops being a thing you carry and becomes a road you are on at a rhythm of stop and go that belongs to nobody else. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the beat only sounds where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
Seen through the body, the energy of being carried sits at the sternum, the flat bone down the middle of the front of your chest. It is three pieces of bone in a line and by adulthood they are joined so well that you feel one plate. The collarbones hook onto its top. The first seven pairs of ribs meet it on either side through short lengths of cartilage. Behind it lie the heart and the great vessels leaving it. The plate is their shield. Inside it red marrow goes on making blood all your life.

Put two fingers on it now and breathe in. The plate lifts a little and the ribs swing out on their cartilage, and on the breath out it settles. It did none of that itself. The sternum has no muscle of its own that swings it. It goes where the ribs and the breath take it, a dozen or more times a minute. It has gone that way since your first breath.

### Amino acid
You have read the name phenylalanine more often than you think. It is printed on the side of many cans of diet drink. The sweetener in them is made from it. The can has to say so. It is one of the nine the body cannot make and has to eat, and it is in cheese and eggs and in beans and nuts.

Part of it is built into protein as it came. The rest goes to the liver and is changed there into a second building block, and the body makes two things from that one. It makes the signals that wake you and move you. And it makes the pigment of your skin and your hair and your eyes.


## RELATIONS

_What you meet here is what the ground was made for, the force it receives and the self that grows once it is held._

### Pair — UL 1, Earth's Breath (The Creative)
Earth's Breath is the fire that begins things, the force that comes down before anything can be received. These two are the first pair there is and neither makes a thing alone. That one burns. You carry what it lights. When the beginning has come and gone and something is left in your hands to bring through, you are here. Open it when the fire is up and you want the other side of it where nothing is started and everything is brought to its end.

### Inverse — its own reflection
Turned over, nothing here changes. It is one of eight that meet their own reflection and here the reason is plain. The ground has no back. Look at it from above or from below and it is the same field, holding whatever stands on it, with nothing hidden on the underside. Nothing turns up here that was not already here. What you are looking for on the far side of receiving is receiving.

### Programming partner — UL 1, Earth's Breath
Earth's Breath sits opposite you a second time because the Gene Keys pair these two as well. At their lowest the two feed each other. Its low season is a spark that will not catch and yours is the feeling of being nowhere, and each makes the other worse. Nothing starts because nothing seems to hold and nothing holds because nothing starts. Open it when the lost feeling has stopped everything from beginning. Something has to catch first.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Water
This is a family of two. The other is Odyssey of Freedom, the self that stops imitating and shows its own face. What the two share is water. There is a sadness in water, a longing for the sea it came from. Both of you know it. Here it is the ground that lets the water soak in, and there it is the water that will not be told what shape to take. Open it when you have been carried far enough to wonder who is doing the travelling.

He wore one shoe. Thick clothes in summer and a thin shirt in the snow, at home on any road because nothing was owned.
