# UL 2 — Beyond the Shell

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Being Carried · The Ground Under You · Found by Following · Letting It Come · Trusted Before You Speak · The Lost Feeling · Already Home

This is the energy of the ground: the part of you that takes in whatever is given and turns it into growth. It refuses nothing. A seed, a stone, rain or a dead leaf, all of it is received, and what can grow from it grows. In a life this is the stretch when your work is to hold what has come to you and let it become what it is going to be.

The ground gives, when you let it, a quiet kind of strength, the strength of being able to bear a thing without needing to change it first. Things come to you now when you stop going out to get them, so the work is to make room. What you do from trust gives everyone more, and what you do from fear takes from everyone, yourself first. The moves that matter are small, made by feel, and most of them look from outside like doing nothing.

The way is to let it come. There will be days when it feels as if you have been left behind and ought to be making something happen, and the ground has an answer to that: everything that has ever grown has grown out of it, and none of it was hurried. Stay soft, stay where you are, and receive.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Earth: the ground, and under it the ground again. Whatever falls on it is taken in, and whatever is set into it comes up in its own time. Its creature is the mare, who runs as far as any horse and runs behind. The traditional character sets the sign for earth beside a sign for stretching out: ground as far as you can walk, giving under every step.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the ground, and what the ground does is carry. It lies open under everything, the house as much as the road, and asks nothing to be different. What falls on it stays, breaks down, and comes up again as grass. In a family it is the mother, the one everything comes back to. In you it is the part that can take a thing in and bear it: you hear hard news, you are still standing, you carry what is put on you without a word. Its risk is going inert, when giving way stops being a strength and turns into lying down under whatever comes.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Underneath is the ground again, the dark of the soil where nobody looks. Here it is the seed lying through the winter with nothing showing above it. In the world it is the field at the start of winter, bare, with everything it holds gone under. In you it is deep patience, the part that can wait without proof that the waiting is working, hold what it was given, keep it warm and say nothing. Its risk is heaviness: the waiting runs past its season, sleep comes in place of rest, and what was held too long spoils where it lies.

### Reading
You have been given something to carry through, and the carrying is the whole of the work now. It is not yours to start, because it was started before it reached you, and it is not yours to steer. What it asks is the strength of the mare: she can run as far as any horse and still she runs behind, along the ground, going where it goes. Set out in front and the way is lost at once. Come behind, and the one worth following shows.

What you are carrying has a whole life ahead of it, from its first showing to its last test, and your part belongs to all of that, not to this stretch alone. Keep to your own kind while it lasts, the ones who grow things and wait for them. The ones who charge ahead you will lose for a while, and losing them is part of it. Soft at rest, firm the moment it moves: that is how the ground is, and how you can be. Rest in what is steady and it goes well.

In the body this energy is the belly, the soft front of you that takes in what it is given and carries the weight without ever being told how. Notice where you are holding yourself now. Let the belly go, sit as the ground sits, and hold to what was handed to you until it is done.

### Judgement
- The Receptive. From the source it goes through and grows. Holding firm the way a mare does brings it through.
- Set out to lead and you go astray. Follow, and you find the one worth following.
- Friends in the south and west. Friends lost in the north and east. Rest in what is steady, and it goes well.

### Image
- The ground lies open under all things. That is the earth's power.
- So the wise make themselves broad, and carry the world.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ frost underfoot, and the hard ice still to come. → becomes Hexagram 24, Return.
Frost underfoot is the first sign of the ice, and it is only frost. Nothing is hard yet, nothing is dangerous, and what will set later is still soft enough to turn. Small things gather here the way cold does, a little each night until one morning they will not break. Notice the small thing while it is small, and take care from the first step.

**Line 2** · _image:_ straight, square and wide, with nothing rehearsed. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
Straight, square and wide: this is what the ground does when it simply acts as itself. Nothing here was rehearsed and nothing needs repeating. When you act from what you are, the act comes out straight, keeps its own shape, leaves nobody out, and is right everywhere at once. Trying to improve on it would spoil it. Do the plain thing once, as yourself, and let others fall in behind.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a gift kept under the coat, and another's work brought to its end. → becomes Hexagram 15, Modesty.
A gift kept under the coat, and someone else's work brought to its end. You are good at this and the time to show it has not come. If the work belongs to someone above you, do it well and let the credit be theirs. Keep what you know behind a closed mouth. See it through without a word about your part, and keep the quiet of having done it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the sack tied shut at the neck. → becomes Hexagram 16, Enthusiasm.
The sack tied at the neck, with one emptiness on both sides of the knot. Nothing you think gets out and nothing from outside gets in. Nobody will blame you for it and nobody will praise you, which for now is exactly right. This is a closed season, when the wise keep to themselves. Keep your plans where they are, and the knot will come loose in its own time.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the yellow garment worn low, under the rest. → becomes Hexagram 8, Holding Together.
A yellow garment worn low, under the rest. Yellow is the colour of the ground and of the middle of things. You are trusted before you speak, and what is good in you shows through of itself. Having to say what you are is the sign that you are not it yet. Wear the good thing low, and let the ones who belong near you gather on their own.

**Line 6** · _image:_ two dragons fighting in the open country, and blood on the ground in the colours of sky and earth. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
Two dragons fighting in the field, their blood on the grass in two colours, the dark of the sky and the yellow of the earth. What gave way has gone hard, wanting to be obeyed instead of obeying, and both sides bleed for it. The way runs out here. If you have set out to rule what you were made to receive, stop now, before more is torn away.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dislocation · _Gift:_ Orientation · _Siddhi:_ Unity

### Shadow — Dislocation
This energy has a low, and the low is the feeling of having stepped off the world. You stand on the same ground as everyone else and it does not seem to hold you. Somewhere a wrong turn was taken, you cannot say where, and since then you have been out of place in every room. Most often it comes out as busyness: the days fill, the load goes onto your shoulders, and the hands stay moving so the hollow underneath cannot be heard. The usual move is to change the outside, a new plan or a new town, and the relief lasts about as long as the unpacking.

You have never left the ground. You cannot, and no turn you took was wrong. The feeling is real as a feeling and false as a fact. It changes the moment it is felt all the way through instead of outrun. Let it soak in, the way rain soaks into a field. What you were running from was the longing for home, and the longing is the first sign of the way back.

### Repressive nature — Lost
This person has let go of the thread. Nothing larger is expected any more, so they follow whatever serves them this week and call the flatness being sensible. With no felt tie to anything beyond their own life, the days have no point they could name, and they get through them.

### Reactive nature — Regimented
Here is someone who lays a grid over life. A system for everything, a rule for every hour, whatever promises to hold chaos still. It looks like order and runs on old anger. The grid keeps the lost feeling at a distance without touching it, and the harder life pushes, the tighter it is drawn.

### Gift — Orientation
Met, this energy becomes finding your way by being carried. You stop steering and the ground begins to move you. Things start to line up on their own: the person you needed turns up, or you find you are in the right town without having planned it. None of that can be forced, because it happens when you are not looking, and it happens in spite of you. Your timing sharpens until being out of step registers in your body as plainly as hunger does, and your own face begins to show.

What you do with it for other people is the strange part. To set someone on their way you give no advice, because advice they can get anywhere. You sit with them wanting nothing from them, letting them see your weakness as much as your strength, and in your company they find out that being afraid is safe. Nothing had to be done. Harmony is catching, and a person who has stopped fighting their own life steadies the room they are in.

### Siddhi — Unity
You are home, and you know it. The seeking is over, and it ended by finding that there was never a distance to cross. There is the ground, there is you, with no line where one stops and the other starts. Two people standing together are already a third thing, the pair itself, so there was never a two on its own. Being home is ordinary, the plainest thing you have ever felt, and it holds everything.

This is what being carried becomes when the one being carried lets go. Nothing leads now and nothing follows. Life goes on moving and you are the ground it moves over, the way the seasons move over a field. It leaves the world exactly as it is, with every mystery in place, and puts you in the middle of your life, where you had been all along.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 2, Direction of the Self
In the body, this energy is the pull that gives a life its direction by receiving it. Gate 2 is where it enters: the part of you that knows which way your life faces, the way a field knows which way is downhill, by taking in what falls on it and letting it run. It does not make the road. It feels where the road already goes, and what belongs on it is drawn toward you. Asked why you are going that way, the honest answer is that it is the way, and you cannot always say more than that.

From outside, a person moved like this can look like someone waiting. Nobody can see a direction being received, so they see the not-deciding and call it drift. The cost runs the other way: the pull is to take everything on alone and settle for less than you wanted, because you did not trust that support would come. People who carry this all their lives learn to let the direction arrive, and to let the help in with it.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The centre of the chest is where this pull is felt, the Identity Centre, which holds who you are and which way your life faces. Those turn out to be one thing. It has no engine. It works like a magnet with a single pole, only ever drawing: what a life needs is pulled toward it, the people as much as the work, and the direction of the life is the sum of what it drew. When you trust it, you go where you were already being taken. When you do not, you end up at war with what it was bringing you.

In about six people in ten this centre is switched on, and the sense of self stays put whoever they are with. For someone like that there is a direction that holds across years. What starves them is walking someone else's road, and if they do it long enough they lose heart. In the other four it is not, so the self is borrowed from whoever is nearest and let go when they leave. For someone like that there may have been many lives in the one life. Place matters more than anything: the wrong house, or the wrong seat in a room, can put them out of themselves, and the right ground gives them back. For them the road is the destination, in the moment, and the only wrong turn is trying to become somebody.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Beat (2–14)
What this centre receives has to be worked for by something, or it stays a heading with nobody walking it. Its other half is Gate 14, low in the belly, the drive that works and makes more than one person needs. Together they make the Channel of the Beat: a direction with the power to follow it, at a beat that belongs to nobody else, and what the work brings in is usually for others as well. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the beat only sounds where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
There is one bone in you that everything comes to and nothing leaves from: the breastbone, the flat bone down the middle of your chest, about the length of your hand. The collarbones come to its top, the ribs come to its edges from both sides, and it goes out to nothing. It holds the whole front of you together by being where things arrive, which is this energy's whole work. That is the work of the ground too: it starts nothing, everything is set into it, and it carries what it is given. The breastbone is where the body does the same.

Of everything in you it is the bone that is moved by everything and moves nothing itself. Rest two fingers in the notch at its top, between the collarbones, and breathe in. It lifts, then settles. Nothing in you can move it on purpose. The ribs carry it, a dozen or so times a minute, and have since your first breath. You are resting your fingers on the part of you that is carried.

### Amino acid
The trust this energy runs on has a substance in you, and the substance is phenylalanine. You cannot make it. Your body takes it in, from meat, eggs or beans, or goes without. Part of it is built into you as it came. The rest becomes what wakes you, what moves you, then the colour of your skin and hair. Trust, too, is a chemistry: it is made in you slowly, let out while you relax, never decided.

What you let all the way in goes into the body as chemistry, not as a thought, which is why the body can feel carried before you have understood a thing. As you are carried into a wider life, your own face begins to show. In you the quiet carrying and the face that shows share one substance. The more you give way, the more comes through you.


## RELATIONS

_What you carry came from somewhere and goes somewhere, and these are the places._

### Pair — UL 1, Earth's Breath (The Creative)
This energy carries what it is given until the carrying is done, and it cannot start the next thing itself. Nothing else has been handed to you, and the waiting has begun to feel like nothing. Earth's Breath is what comes next: the force that begins things, arriving in its own time and never to order. There nothing waits to be given. Something starts. Open it when the carrying is finished and you are standing ready for the next thing. The next thing is not handed over. It begins.

### Inverse — its own reflection
This energy yields through you the whole way down, and pushed far enough it looks as if it must turn into something else. It does not turn. The ground has no back. Dig under it and there is ground, and under that more ground. Whatever you are holding out for on the far side of receiving is receiving. What you are looking for is beside this, never under it.

### Programming partner — UL 1, Earth's Breath
This energy holds what is set down on it, and with nothing set down it starts rearranging what is there to have something to hold. The Gene Keys put Earth's Breath across from you: the force that makes something from nothing. Its low is the stretch when nothing comes, and yours feeds it. Nothing holds because nothing has started. At the height the two are one thing, a single made thing and the whole it belongs to. Open it when being lost has stopped everything from beginning. Something has to catch before anything can be carried.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Water
This energy finds its direction by following, and followed for long enough it stops knowing which part of the going is you. Odyssey of Freedom holds that: the self that stops copying the lives around it and shows its own face. You and it are one family, and the family knows one thing. Water finds its own way to what it belongs with. Here it goes where the ground goes; there it runs along the seams to its own people. Open it when following has left you wondering who is doing the travelling.

### Tarot
Three faces stand near this energy: one from its family, two from the ground.

- **Ring Arcana — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, the Right Path): An old figure alone on a peak, with a lamp and a staff. He went up to find the way, and the lamp shows one step at a time. The family brings him in, and no further. Meet him when the way home has to be walked without a map.
- **Earth (both trigrams) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): A pair stand chained to a block, and the chains are loose enough to lift off. What holds everything can hold too tightly, and safety turns into staying. The ground brings him, and only that far. Meet him when staying has become a habit.
- **Earth (both trigrams) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): A dancer inside a green wreath, with the whole thing finished around her. There is nothing more to reach for and nothing left out. She comes through the ground, company and not a promise. Meet her when what you carried has arrived and needs no one to claim it.

### Immortals
Lan Cai He is the wanderer with the basket of flowers. The old stories tie him to the ground, and the tie is a loose one. He sang in the street about how fast life goes and gave his coins away. He wore one shoe, thick clothes in summer and a thin shirt in the snow. Let him stay near: at home on any road, because nothing was owned.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Virgo:** the harvest sign, the one who sorts grain from straw and serves unthanked. It came in with the Hermit and goes no deeper. It says one thing: what is served is sorted first.
- **Hebrew letter — Yod, the Hand:** the smallest letter, a single stroke, the point the others start from. It arrived with the Hermit, two steps out. What it holds: all you carry began as one small thing put into a hand.
