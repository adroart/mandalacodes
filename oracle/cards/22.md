# UL 22 — Treasure of the Way

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Feeling Everything · Owning Your Mood · Nothing Put on Anyone · Kindness Under Pressure · Waiting Out the Wave · The Plain Thing Shown · Lifted After a Fall

Here the energy is grace, the warmth in a person who has felt everything and blames no one for it. In you it begins as feeling at full strength: joy, grief, wanting, anger, each one arriving whole and before you have a word for it. What you do with it in the minute after is where grace lives. It shows in how you take bad news, how you speak to a stranger, and whether the person across the table leaves lighter than they came.

You trust a feeling first and understand it later. It came for a reason you may never learn, so it is not fought, not fixed, not made sweeter than it is. Trusting it is not the same as acting on it. You can feel the whole of it and put none of it on the person in front of you, which is rarer than it sounds. Felt like that, a feeling lasts a few minutes, then moves on. The one who was with you comes away feeling heard, though you may have said nothing. That is what you have to give, and people are more grateful for it than they know how to say.

There is a low in this, and it comes in its own time, the way rain does. Nobody sent the rain, and nobody sent this, though the first thing you will want is to find who did. Looking for the sender is what keeps it. Stay in it instead, uncomfortable as that is, because there is no way up in you that does not go through what you feel. Take it in small moves that show nobody how hard it was.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Fire: a mountain, and a fire lit at its foot as the day goes. The light reaches up the slope, showing every fold the daylight had flattened, and the mountain is not changed by it. The traditional character sets cowry shells, the old money, beside flowers: what is worn, tied to what it is worth.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the place where the ground stops going up. What a mountain does is hold still: it is the limit the eye reaches, the wall at the end of the valley, the same tomorrow as today. In a family it is the youngest son, the one who watches and keeps his own counsel. In you it is the part that stays put, that does not rush to answer, that can be looked at from every side and is still the same shape. Its risk is going hard, when holding still turns into refusing to move at all.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Underneath is fire, which is light before it is heat. Its work is to show: near a fire everything can be told apart, this from that, edge from edge. A flame has nothing of its own to stand on, so it clings to whatever it is burning, and where it burns people gather. In a family it is the middle daughter, the one who sees. In you it is clear sight, warmth, the wish to have things plain. Its risk is that it burns what it lit, or that the showing becomes a show.

### Reading
You are seen, and what is seen is the outside of a thing whose inside is already set. The light is on the slope now, and the light is real: the way something is said, a face made ready. It changes nothing underneath, nor needs to. Its work is to let what is there be seen for what it is.

It asks you to keep the light for small things. Use it for the day's business, the greeting, the room made pleasant, and it goes well. Do not use it to settle anything that weighs. A fire at the foot of a mountain shows the shape of the mountain; it cannot tell you what is inside the rock, and a matter decided by how it looked will not hold. Let what is solid lead and what is fine follow, because the moment the ornament goes first the way is lost. If you want more from this than small gains, the road to it is not a finer surface but a longer walk: what you know becomes your beauty, what you have lived through becomes your grace, a little of each every day.

The eye under the hand is where this energy sits in the body. The eye is lit, taking in every edge of what is in front of it; the hand above holds still. Feel where you are holding yourself right now: looking, not yet reaching. Keep the hand still a while longer, and let the eye do its work.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the feet adorned; the carriage left standing, and the walk begun. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still.
Your feet are dressed for it, the carriage stands ready, and you get down to walk. Nothing is wrong with the ride; it is not yours to take today. What you have at the start is yourself, and the plain road on your own feet is what keeps that whole. Leave the easy thing standing where it is, and walk.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the beard adorned, and it moves only with the chin. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
A beard is groomed, and a beard goes where the chin goes. What you are adding is ornament, and it has no life of its own; it rises with what it is fixed to. The patience here is the kind that waits for hair to grow. Keep close to the one above you who has the substance, and let what you do move with them.

**Line 3** · _image:_ adorned and wet with favour; firmness kept through it. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.
Everything is going your way and it shows, the way wet stone shines. The favour is real, and it came from where you happen to stand rather than from anything you did, so it can leave the same way. The shine is safe only while what is under it stays firm. Keep to what is true through the whole of the good time, and no one can slight you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ adorned or plain; a white horse coming as if on wings, not a robber but a suitor. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging.
You cannot tell whether to dress this or leave it plain, and something is coming at you fast, white, as if it had wings. For a breath it looks like a raid. It is not. What is coming wants to join you, and the plainness is the sign of it. Let the doubt pass on its own, and take what arrives in plain colours as what it is.

**Line 5** · _image:_ grace among hills and gardens; a roll of silk, small, slight. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
You have gone up to the quiet places with a small roll of plain silk, less than the occasion seemed to ask. The shame of it is real and short. An offering that is sparing and meant is worth more than a lavish one that is not; in the quiet places this is known. Give what you have, tie the knot in the cord, and it ends in joy.

**Line 6** · _image:_ white grace, with nothing added to it. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
At the top the colour has gone out of it, and what is left is white, with nothing on it. The whole climb was toward this. The plain is not less than the fine; it is what the fine was for, and there is no fault in it. Wear nothing you do not need, say what is so in the fewest words, and what you set out for is yours.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dishonour · _Gift:_ Graciousness · _Siddhi:_ Grace

### Shadow — Dishonour
A feeling comes that you do not want, and the first thing you do is look for who sent it. The weather, or the one who said the thing. The reason is always out there, so the feeling is never yours, and a feeling that is never yours cannot be felt through, only pushed about. That is dishonour, which starts at home. You dishonour yourself every time you refuse the way you feel, whether you swallow it or fling it. Then you do the same to other people, handing them the mood to carry, and a mood put on someone dishonours both of you at once.

Every feeling you have was put there to be trusted. Trusting it is not doing what it says; it is letting it be in you without arguing. Left alone, it passes. Fought, fixed or hidden, it stays. It comes back too, because nothing you feel or do goes unheard, and what goes out from you returns to where it started. Nobody is at ease being uncomfortable. Being uncomfortable plainly, with no story about whose fault it is, is the whole of the way through; no higher place in you is reached by going round.

### Repressive nature — Proper
This person keeps it in. Outwardly they are calm, correct and hard to fault. Under the manners the feeling seethes, checked and checked again, because the one thing they fear is losing hold of it. It reads as composure. It is a lid, and a lid held long enough comes off all at once.

### Reactive nature — Inappropriate
Here is someone who puts it all out. Whatever they feel, the room feels next, before they have heard it themselves. There is fire in it, often real art, and a private life in pieces behind them. What they cannot do is listen, so even what they mean well arrives at the wrong time.

### Gift — Graciousness
Graciousness is what you have once your feelings are your own. You stop looking for who sent the mood and carry it yourself, so nobody near you is asked to hold it. It is slow work, sorting your own weather from other people's until you can sit beside someone in a low without going into it with them. Out of it comes a way of treating people that is rarer than it should be: everyone gets their dignity, and everyone feels listened to before they have finished speaking.

It is not polish. Think of an old floor of elm, weathered, marked by the saw, given a light sanding and laid with the marks still in it: honest about every year it has had, soft under the hand. You live fully, keeping no feeling back, and you keep every feeling off the people who did not ask for it. Careful without being fearful. Warm without being heavy. Candid without being cruel. It is tested all your life, which is what refines it, and in time you come to be grateful for whatever comes, the hard with the sweet, wanting less from either.

### Siddhi — Grace
Grace is the moment that lifts you when you have fallen, and you did not make it. A small bird on the hedge at dawn with a flame of gold on its head stops you where you stand, then it is gone, and the day is changed. Everyone has had these. They cannot be arranged. What can be done is to meet every fall cleanly, because grace is buried in every low you have ever had, waiting for you to act in a new way.

From here nothing you feel becomes anything but love. You are no longer the one deciding; you are an instrument, played. Fear has gone out of you for good, and with it the need to hide, since everything was always heard anyway. When you look back at the years of blame, the lids and the spills, there is nothing in them to be ashamed of. It was love, still on its way to you.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 22, Openness
This energy presses, in the body, to be heard. It comes in through Gate 22: a feeling that has arrived in you wants somewhere to go, and the somewhere is another person taking it in. Its fear is the plain opposite, that you will speak and nothing will land. The nervousness it makes is silence: you go quiet in a room, or a whole day passes in you unsaid. The pressure runs on the mood. When the mood is in, the depth of what you feel finds its words; when it is out, you do not want to speak to anyone, which is not a fault. It is the same door, closed for a while.

Seen from outside, this can look like charm, or moodiness, or too much feeling, and it is none of those. It is a wide instrument that takes in whatever comes near it, with a knack for timing: when to say the thing, when to listen. The cost is that when the feeling is held back for fear it will not be met, you settle for less than you wanted and call it being realistic.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The pit of the stomach is where this pressure is felt, the Emotional Solar Plexus Centre. It is the only place in you that both makes energy and knows something, which is why nothing has more power over a person than what they feel. It moves in a wave, from hope down to pain and back, so the same fact looks like everything one day, like nothing the next. There is no truth in the now here. What is true shows once the wave has passed and you are standing on the far side of it.

About half of people have this centre switched on, and they carry the wave all their lives. With this door it is the passionate, moody kind, mostly level, with quick rises and falls, its low end holding a melancholy that, if it can be borne, has much wisdom in it. For them the work is to wait the wave out before deciding anything, taking the low alone rather than forcing company. For the rest, it is not, so they have no wave of their own. They soak up the feeling of whoever is nearest, make it louder, and for a while it feels like theirs. For them the low goes when they leave the room, in a quarter of an hour, and the risk is being agreeable to keep the peace, until they no longer say what is true.

### What completes it — the Channel of Openness (12–22)
A feeling with nowhere to go stays in the pit of the stomach as a weight. In the throat sits the other half, Gate 12, the caution that knows there is a right moment to speak and holds back until it comes. The two together make the Channel of Openness, through which what you feel can be said so that a stranger feels it too, in the mood and never on demand. It makes no difference if that other half sits in you or in someone near you: the depth only gets out where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
The solar plexus is where this energy lives in you, and the reason is the catch. A feeling lands there before you have said anything: bad news comes, and in the first second there is a catch under the breastbone, the breath held a beat, the word for it arriving after. The solar plexus is a knot of nerves the size of two fingertips on the front of your spine, behind the stomach, a hand's depth in from the hollow under the breastbone. Whatever reaches you reaches there first. The moment after is this energy from inside: the feeling goes up to the throat and out, or it stays where it landed. Held long enough, it becomes fear kept as tension in every part of you; let through, it leaves the body at rest.

Lay a hand flat in that hollow and take a sharp breath in. The hollow pushes out against your palm, then falls back as the breath goes. Under the hand is the part of you that knows before you do.

### Amino acid
The grace that comes into you has a substance in you, and the substance is proline. One of the twenty pieces you are built from, it is made in you out of another of the twenty, so nothing you eat has to bring it. This energy does not rise in you from below; it comes down into you and saturates all of you. A woman early in pregnancy is flooded with a chemistry that readies her for what has not yet arrived, and it is like that: you are made ready, low down, for what you cannot yet see.

What floods you was never taken in from outside; your body makes it, in its own time. This same substance runs in the ones who share this energy, so what fills you is one chemistry with theirs. You cannot feel it being made, only that you have been made ready.
