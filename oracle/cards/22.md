# UL 22 — Treasure of the Way

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Owning Your Feelings · Kind Under Pressure · Knowing When to Speak · Dignity for Everyone · Blaming Someone Else · Swallowing What You Feel · Lifted After a Fall

A strong feeling comes up in you while someone else is in the room. Graciousness lives in what you do next. You feel it whole in your body, the joy or the grief or the anger. The people with you are treated kindly all the same, and near you they feel listened to.

Some days a low mood arrives for no reason you can find, and you want someone to blame. Let it pass through you without putting it on anyone, and it is gone within minutes. Decide nothing big until it has passed.

Say what you feel when the mood is with you, and wait when it is not. Say a hard thing kindly, at a time it can be heard. What you have lived through becomes a treasure, and it shows in the way you treat people. You grow grateful for whatever each day brings, and people turn to you when their own feelings run high.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Fire is the hexagram of how what is inside you shows on the outside. Fire is the clarity in you that sees each thing plainly. Mountain is the steadiness in you that stays as it is, whatever light falls on it. The clarity sits inside and the steadiness outside, because you see a great deal and show only a little of it. This hexagram has long been read as a fire at the foot of a mountain. Its light makes the hillside beautiful at dusk, and the rock underneath does not change. So the light is enough for the small matters of a day, never for settling a serious dispute. What you show is beautiful when it comes from what you are.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that knows where to stop. It sits on the outside, because the face other people meet does best when it holds still. Mountain has long been read as a limit, the place where the eye can see no further. A calm face works the same way: people see its quiet, and not everything behind it. On its own, it turns hard and stubborn, and nothing moves you at all.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is how clearly you see what is in front of you, telling one thing from another and putting each in its place. It sits inside, because this seeing happens in you long before anything shows. Fire has long been read as a light that spreads in every direction. People are drawn to you for it, the way they gather round a fire at night. On its own, it burns up whatever it clings to.

### Reading
Things are going well enough, and how they look matters now. A kind word or a tidy room helps, and each is a small thing. Take care over the outside of things, and let it show what is truly there. The beauty is real, and it changes nothing underneath.

Use it for the small matters of the day. Do not settle anything serious by how it looks, because a decision made on the surface will not hold. Let what is solid lead and the beauty follow. If you want more than small gains, learn and live more, a little each day. What you know becomes your beauty, and what you live through becomes your grace.

Your eyes do the work now, and your hands stay still. Take in every detail of what is in front of you, and hold off from reaching for it. Sit the way someone sits looking at a hillside at dusk.

### Judgement
- Grace has success. In small matters it is good to set out.
- Let what shows take the lead, and little comes of it.

### Image
- Fire at the foot of the mountain: the image of grace.
- So the wise bring clarity to the day's affairs, and dare not decide a serious dispute by it.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ the feet adorned; the carriage left standing, and the walk begun. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still.
Your feet are dressed well, and you leave the carriage and walk. You could ride, and today riding is not yours to do. Start low, on your own two feet, with nothing borrowed. Let the outside of you wait while the inside grows. Walk, and be content to walk.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the beard adorned, and it moves only with the chin. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
You tend your beard, and a beard moves only when the chin moves. What you add has no life of its own; it goes where the thing under it goes. Your own understanding has a limit. Stay close to someone wiser than you and rise with them. Be patient, the way hair takes its time to grow.

**Line 3** · _image:_ adorned and wet with dew; firmness kept through it. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.
Everything around you shines, as if wet with dew, and favour comes your way. It came from where you happen to stand as much as from anything you did. The shine that lasts comes from what is true in you, the way water sparkles by itself. Stay true through the whole good time, and no one can slight you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ adorned or plain; a white horse coming as if on wings, not a robber but a suitor. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging.
You cannot tell whether to dress this up or leave it plain. Something comes at you fast, a white horse that seems to have wings. For a moment it looks like an attack. It is someone who wants to join you. Ask plainly what they want, and welcome what comes in good faith.

**Line 5** · _image:_ grace among hills and gardens; a roll of silk, small, slight. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
You go up to a garden on the hill with a small roll of plain silk. It is less than the day seems to ask, and you feel the shame of it. A small gift that is meant is worth more than a lavish one that is not. Give what you truly have, and it ends in joy.

**Line 6** · _image:_ white grace, with nothing added to it. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
At the end the colour is gone, and only plain white is left. This plainness is what all the beauty was for, and it grows clearer as you get older. Wear only what is true of you, and what you set out for is yours.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dishonour · _Gift:_ Graciousness · _Siddhi:_ Grace

### Shadow — Dishonour
Discomfort comes, and the first thing you do is look for its cause outside you. You blame the weather, or more often another person. That is where dishonour starts, and it starts with you. Every time you refuse the way you feel, you dishonour yourself. Put your mood on someone else, and you dishonour both of you at once.

Think of your feelings as the strings of an instrument that hears everything. At this height the strings are out of tune, so whatever comes near sounds wrong. You try to fix your mood or change it, when it only needs to pass through. Every feeling is there to be trusted, and trusting it is not the same as acting on it. Nothing you do goes unheard, and what you send out comes back to you.

### Repressive nature — Proper
Held in, it looks like good manners. These people are calm, correct and at ease in company. Underneath, the feeling seethes, and they may carry lust or resentment nobody would guess. What they fear most is losing control. Left to stew, what they hold back can one day burst out.

### Reactive nature — Inappropriate
Let out, it spills onto everyone. These people cannot hold back their reactions and wear their hearts on their sleeves. Many make fine art or music, while their private lives lie in pieces. Above all they cannot listen, to others or to themselves, so even their good intentions come at the wrong time.

### Gift — Graciousness
Graciousness grows once your feelings are your own. You live fully and hold nothing back, and you still consider how the people around you feel. You express what you feel safely and disrespect no one, yourself included. Nobody else's mood makes a victim of you either. When someone close to you is low and puts it on you, you feel for them and stay clear of it.

Now the strings are in tune, and people feel listened to around you. You know that an unkindness done to someone comes back, so you treat everyone with dignity, however they behave. This is tested all your life, and each test refines it. You learn to be careful without being fearful, warm without being overbearing, and candid without being cruel. In time you grow grateful for whatever comes.

### Siddhi — Grace
Grace comes down on you, and you cannot make it happen. It can be a small moment. A tiny bird with a gold stripe on its head stops you on a morning walk, and you carry it all day. Moments like this lift you when you have fallen. You prepare for them by meeting each fall openly, because grace is hidden inside every low.

When it stays, your life is no longer yours to steer. You are the instrument, and something greater plays you. Fear ends for good, and every feeling turns into love. Whoever meets you feels it and never forgets it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 22, Openness
Gate 22 sits in the pit of your stomach and wants to be heard. What you feel presses to be taken in by another person. Its fear is speaking and not being heard, and when the fear wins you go silent. It follows your mood. When the mood is right, you say what you feel with depth, and people are moved. When it is not, you want no company, and nothing is wrong with that. It has a sense of timing, knowing when to speak and when to listen. Held back out of fear, it settles for less than it wants.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The Emotional Solar Plexus Center is where your feelings are made. It is the only centre that both drives you and lets you know things. What you feel moves in a wave, from hope down to pain and back. So there is no truth in the moment here, and a decision waits until the wave has passed.

A little more than half of people have this centre defined. With this gate their wave is passionate and moody. It runs mostly level, with short bursts up and down. Its low end holds a sadness with great wisdom in it, if it can be borne. They do well to spend the low alone, writing or playing music. The rest have it open. They take in the feelings of everyone near them and feel them louder, as if they were their own. Alone again, the feelings fade. They can be nice to avoid upsetting anyone, until they stop saying what is true.

### What completes it — the Channel of Openness (12–22)
A feeling on its own stays in the stomach. In the throat sits Gate 12, the caution about when and how a feeling is said. Together they make the Channel of Openness, which turns what you feel into words that move other people. Gate 12 may be in you or in someone close, and the feeling finds its words only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
A catch under the breastbone is the reason this energy is given to the solar plexus. Bad news comes, and in the first second something catches there, before you have a word for it. Your breath stops for a beat. Then the feeling either rises to your throat and is said, or it stays where it landed. Held there, fear settles in as tension through your whole body. Let through, it leaves your body at rest.

The solar plexus is a knot of nerves on the front of your spine, behind the stomach. It is two knots, each the size of a fingertip, with fibres going out like the rays of a sun. Lay a hand flat in the soft hollow below your breastbone and take a sharp breath in. The hollow pushes out against your hand. About a hand's depth behind it is where you feel things before you know them.

### Amino acid
The feeling that comes over you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is proline. Every protein in you is built from twenty pieces, and your body makes this one itself, from another of the twenty. You never need to eat it.

At its fullest this energy comes down into you and fills the whole of you. It floods you the way a woman early in pregnancy is flooded and made ready. Your body is readied, low in the belly, for something that has not yet arrived. What fills you then was made inside you and never taken in from outside. Three other energies in you share this chemistry.

## RELATIONS

_UL 21 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the bite that clears what is in the way, where this one shows what is there with care._

### Pair — UL 21, Beyond Binary (Biting Through)
This energy keeps the peace and lets each feeling pass, and it runs out where something has to be stopped. Beyond Binary is where it goes next: the will that takes charge and bites through what is in the way. Open it when kindness is not enough and a situation needs a firm hand. A kind word lasts when there is strength behind it.

### Inverse — UL 21, Beyond Binary (Biting Through)
Turn this energy upside down and the soft touch becomes the bite. Beyond Binary cuts down to what matters, where this energy shows it beautifully. Open it when what you are showing needs to be cleared away first.

### Programming partner — UL 47, Garden of Alchemy
This energy owns each feeling as it comes. Garden of Alchemy holds what it lacks: the old weight you were born carrying, which changes into something new when you face it. Graciousness is how you face it, one honest feeling at a time. Open it when a sadness feels older than your own life.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Divinity
This energy meets pain with kindness, and its family is built around suffering that opens you. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Divinity. UL 36, Crystal Creation, carries the dark stretch that tests you. UL 37, Journey Home, carries the warmth of the family you belong to. UL 63, Adornments of Time, carries the question that will not let you go. This one carries grace.
