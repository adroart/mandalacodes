# UL 4 — Veils of Knowledge

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Not Knowing Yet · The Young Mind · The Hunt for a Reason · Sure and Still Uneasy · Being Taught · Set Free to Play · Nothing Was Personal

Not knowing is where this energy begins: the young mind standing in front of something it cannot yet make sense of, wanting to. It is the state every person is in before they understand anything, and it is honest, because a mind that knows it does not know can still be taught. It comes up whenever something new stands in front of you, a person you cannot read, or a turn in your life you did not see coming. It stays until you understand, which is never as soon as you want.

The mind knows what it is for while it keeps to its own work. It is for understanding, for saying what it understands, never for deciding. Logic can argue either side of anything, and a mind handed the deciding argues itself into whatever it was afraid of. Being sure in the head has never once made a body feel safe. What this mind is really after is not knowledge. Knowledge is what it thinks it needs to take the unease away, and all knowledge can give is the hope of peace. Understanding is another thing, and it does not live in the head.

The pull, when something will not make sense, is to ask why, hunting the reason that will finally let you rest. No reason ever has. One answer is replaced by the next question, and that restlessness is the whole of the folly. The way through is not a better answer. Let the asking wear itself out, and keep the mind at what it is good at. Understanding comes up on its own, from lower down than the question, once you are tired enough of answers to live inside the question for a while. You will not stop thinking. You will stop asking.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Water: a spring coming out of the ground at the foot of a mountain. The water is clear at its source and has no idea yet where it is going. The mountain above stops the view, stops the water spreading, so it gathers there. The traditional character shows a plant under a cover: something growing where it cannot yet be seen.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is what stays put. Weather crosses it, rivers go round it, and it does not move. It is the end of the road, the closed door, the limit you cannot see past. In a family it is the youngest son, the one who stays home. In you it is the power to stop: to hold still, keep a limit, wait at the edge of something without going over. Its risk is turning to stone, when the stop that was wisdom becomes a wall and stubbornness starts calling itself patience.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Underneath is water, running downhill through whatever is in the way. It finds the low place, fills it, then moves on. In a gorge it cannot see out, and it goes on anyway. In a family it is the middle son, the one who takes the risks. In you it is depth: the part that can stay inside a question with no floor to it yet, without climbing out early. Its risk is the pit, when the river you meant to cross becomes a hole you have sunk into, and the feeling you went down into will not let you back up.

### Reading
Something has come up that you do not understand, and you are the young one here, at the foot of a mountain with the water just out of the ground. Everything that has ever grown was covered like this once, before it knew what it was. You are ready to learn without yet knowing what you need. The one thing you can feel clearly is the wanting to know.

What this asks of you is to go and ask. Find the one who knows the ground, ask them once, and mean it. The first answer is the clear one. Ask the same thing a second time, then a third, and you are no longer asking. You are stirring, and every stir clouds the water until nothing can be read in it. Take what you were given and carry it off without another word. Then do the plain things well while it works in you, each one through to its end, because a character is built out of small things finished.

In the body these two forces are the hand, which holds a thing steady, and the ear, which takes in without needing to see. Hold still and listen. Notice where you are holding yourself now. There is real danger under you, the danger of not knowing, so stop at the edge of it and do not go down alone.

### Judgement
- Youthful Folly. It goes through. It is not I who seek the young one. The young one seeks me.
- At the first asking I answer. Asked a second time, then a third, the water is muddied, and the muddied get no answer.
- Holding firm brings it through.

### Image
- A spring comes out at the foot of the mountain.
- So the wise carry each act through to its end, and a character grows from that.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a rule at the very beginning, and the shackles taken off again. → becomes Hexagram 41, Decrease.
At the very start of not knowing, a rule does more good than kindness. Someone sets a limit on you, or you set one on yourself, and the limit is what opens the eyes. Take it, then let it come off. A restraint kept on past its use stops teaching and starts to bind. Whoever goes on being punished learns only shame.

**Line 2** · _image:_ bearing kindly with those who do not know, a wife brought into the house, and the son running it. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
Here you are the one who knows a little, and the not-knowing around you is yours to bear with. Do it kindly. Take in what is soft and unfinished, the way a wife is brought into a house, because strength that bends toward the weak is what keeps a household standing. The young one you were patient with can run the place now. Let the next one take the head.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a girl who loses herself at the sight of a man with money, and no good in taking her. → becomes Hexagram 18, Work on the Decayed.
Somebody is latching on. It may be a person attaching to you because you seem to have something, or it may be you, running toward whoever looks strong and losing yourself on the way. There is no gain in either. What is given away that fast was never held. Stand on your own feet, and let people come to you with what they have.

**Line 4** · _image:_ shut in with the not-knowing, and nobody near who knows. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
This is being in the dark without knowing it is dark. You have kept company with people who know no more than you do, and the one who could have shown you is nowhere near, so the not-knowing has closed round you like a room with no window. The shame is real, and it is the way out. Real teachers do not hide from you. Go and find one.

**Line 5** · _image:_ not knowing the way a child does not know, open and ready to be shown. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
There is a not-knowing that is innocence. A child does not know, does not mind not knowing, asks, and takes the answer whole. That is you here, and it goes well. Stay open, let someone who knows more take you by the hand, and follow. Being unable and following the able is the plain road through. What was covered comes clear without being forced.

**Line 6** · _image:_ striking at the not-knowing, and the line between guarding and robbing. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
Here the not-knowing has to be struck: it has gone on too long, and harm is coming from it. Strike it, then watch which side of the line you stand on. Beating it out of someone makes you the one doing harm. A blindness can be taken away. Nobody's sight can be handed over. Keep the robber off them without becoming one, and you end up on the same side.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Intolerance · _Gift:_ Understanding · _Siddhi:_ Forgiveness

### Shadow — Intolerance
Intolerance begins as a slant on the mind's own logic. The mind has a bad day, decides it does not like someone, and by evening it has a list of good reasons. Under the reasons is a feeling, with logic wrapped round it, and the logic measures only what it was sent to find. That is how an opinion turns into a certainty, a resentment into a conviction, until you are blind with your own reasoning and cannot see that you are. Out of reasons come blame, justification and guilt, the whole furniture of a world split into sides.

Sit alone in a room for an hour with nothing to do and it shows itself closer to home: most people cannot bear their own minds. Under all of it is fear, thrown onto other people and defended from behind an argument. Look at the other side of your own opinion, honestly, and you will find the fear it was standing on. The mind was given a job it cannot do, which is to make you safe, and no amount of being sure in the head has ever made a body feel safe.

### Repressive nature — Apathetic
One person gives up on understanding. The mind goes slack and calls itself open, holds no opinion, because an opinion would have to be answered for. They think they are slower than others. They are frightened, and the fright has emptied the thinking out. The way back is to think again, without letting it rule.

### Reactive nature — Nit-Picking
Another makes the mind the judge and waits for it to hand down peace. It never does, so the anger goes onto whoever was supposed to have the answers. It comes out on the smallest thing, a word out of place. Each complaint lets off a little, and none is the point.

### Gift — Understanding
Understanding is what the water does when it is finally left alone: it runs clear on its own. It has nothing to do with knowledge. Knowledge is what the mind thinks it needs to take away its unease, and all it can give is the hope of peace, which is what keeps you asking. Understanding comes after the mind has looked at a thing from every side and found that every side can be argued. It is the way a riddle with no answer is given to a mind: at the moment the mind sees its logic will never solve it, something gives. Then the answer arrives as a feeling in the whole of you, up through the body from the belly, and the question is finished with.

The mind is let off the job of settling your life, which it was never fit for, and set free for what it loves: looking at patterns, turning them over, playing. You see both halves of any argument and take neither. You understand people the same way. The restlessness is still in you. Now it points outward, toward what could be put right in the world.

### Siddhi — Forgiveness
Forgiveness is a warmth that goes through the borders between people and finds there was never a debt on either side. Nothing was personal. The part of the mind that needed a reason for what happened has died. In its place is a plainness that watches the old story and does not pick it up. There is nothing restless left in it. You cannot do it. It comes when you are tired enough of the drama, lands like a surprise, and something in the chest that had been choked comes open. Then it runs backward, down the line of the people you came from, untying what they left tied, so that debts which stood for generations are cancelled at the source. The water has gone on past the mountain now, and anyone can drink.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 4, Formulization
Gate 4 is the pressure to make sense of things: the part of you that takes a pile of loose facts and finds the shape they make. It does not rest until something fits. In you it runs as a mind that puzzles at everything, that has to know how a thing works before it can leave the thing alone, and is happiest when a pattern finally closes. What it makes is an answer, and the answer is how this mind tries to make life safe. Every answer is a guess that fitted. Held as a possibility it can be dropped the moment a better one comes, and a mind that can drop its answers stays light.

From outside, a person built like this gets called a know-it-all, the one with an answer for everything, and is often wrong while sounding sure. The pull is to act on an idea the moment it arrives, before it has taken root, then to doubt yourself when it does not take. The idea was not wrong. It was early. Watch which of your answers the world goes on to prove, and let the rest go.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
The Ajna Centre is where this pressure runs: the place behind the brow where what comes in gets sorted into sense. It takes the questions that press down from the crown and turns them into something usable. It stores what it has learned as beliefs and memories. It has no engine. It can see and it cannot act. What it works out has to be carried somewhere else before anything happens, and it speaks best when someone has asked. The same answer is help at one moment and an intrusion at the next.

In about half of people this centre is switched on. For someone like that the mind runs all the time, cannot be turned off, and that is the design. They are also built to feel sure. The sureness is both a ground and a trap, because they can be sure while being wrong. In the other half it is not, so there is no fixed way of making sense of things: they take in the thinking of whoever is near and make it louder, then let it go when that person leaves. Someone like that is not built to be certain. Gripping a thought to feel steady is like gripping a steering wheel too hard. The harder the grip, the less of the road can be felt. Held lightly, a mind like that sees a thing from more sides than a fixed one ever will.

### What completes it — the Channel of Logic (4–63)
An answer with nothing to test it stays a guess. Its other half is Gate 63 at the crown, the pressure of a doubt that will not accept a thing as finished, and the two make the Channel of Logic. The question presses down, the answer forms, the question checks it, and so on until something can be relied on. It is slow wiring, and it waits to be asked before it speaks. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the checking only happens where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
The neocortex is where this energy lives in you, and the reason is youth. It is the thin folded sheet over the outside of your brain, the newest part of it in the history of animals, and the last part of you to finish. The front of it, where your judgement is made, is still being wired into your middle twenties. Everything it knows it took in from outside you. It is where your words are made, and every reason you have ever given for anything. That is the young mind's work in you too. It asks, takes in, holds what it was given.

What this layer of you has learned it cannot put down. Look at any word on this page and you cannot see it as marks. The meaning arrives before you can stop it, in a fraction of a second, doing what took you years to learn. Now try to un-read it. That is the first answer, gone into you, and there is no asking it out again.

### Amino acid
The mind that cannot leave a question alone has a substance in you, and the substance is valine. You cannot make it. Your body takes it in, from milk or meat, or goes without. When it builds a protein it folds valine into the middle, away from the water. The change this energy is moving toward is written in you there too: a child born with it has a mind that does not run riot, from the first day.

The reasons you give for not being able to bear someone are made of the same substance in you as the closeness that gets through a guard and the yes that goes all the way in. It is one chemistry in you for how people manage to be together. Without this energy there would be no reasons between you and anyone, no expectations, no blame.

It is the part of a bond that wants reasons and has to learn to do without them. What the four share is union. Here it is the reason nobody had to give.
