# UL 4 — Veils of Knowledge

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The Wish to Understand · Asking Once and Listening · Willing to Be Taught · Seeing Both Sides · Playing With Ideas · Needing a Reason · Nothing Was Personal

Understanding comes to you when you are new to something and its meaning is still under a veil. You understand people, even the ones who see a thing the opposite way to you. Loose pieces of what you learn fit together in you, and you can say the whole simply. You feel it in your whole body, as a knowing in your belly before your mind can explain it.

Some days your mind will not stop asking, and every answer it finds only opens another question. It may try to run your life, though it cannot tell you where to live or whom to love. Then say plainly that you are new to this. Stop, take a breath, and come back to one thing.

Let your mind look into things, talk them over and play with how they fit. Make your mistakes freely, since being new is what they are for. Live your questions fully, and understanding rises in you on its own, clear enough to share with anyone who asks.

## ICHING

### Combination
Mountain over Water is the hexagram of being new to something. Water is the danger in you of not knowing what you are doing. Mountain is the stillness that stops you there. The stillness sits above the danger because when you cannot see the way, stopping is what keeps you safe. That is why this hexagram has long been read as a spring at the foot of a mountain. A spring runs clearest where it leaves the ground and picks up dirt the further it goes. So what you learn right at the start of something matters most. Stop where you cannot see ahead, and you can be taught.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that knows where your understanding ends. It holds you still at that edge instead of guessing past it. It sits on top because that edge lies outside you, where the road ahead drops out of sight. Mountain has long been read as a foot that stops walking. On a dark stair your foot stops in the air and waits until it finds the next step. On its own, it turns stubborn, and you stay where you are long after you could have gone on.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the part of you that takes the risk of trying what you do not yet know how to do. Everything rides on one moment, the first attempt. It lies underneath because the risk is inside you, in the not knowing you carry into anything new. Water has long been read as a stream, which finds its way down by trying every gap in the rocks. You find your way into something new the same way, one try at a time. On its own, it falls, and one bad try becomes a pit you stay in.

### Reading
Something is in front of you that you do not understand, and you are the one who has to learn it. What you will become is still hidden, the way a seedling is hidden under the soil. You are ready to learn and do not yet know what you need.

This asks one thing of you: go and ask. Find the one who knows this ground, ask them once, and mean it. The first answer is the clear one. Ask the same thing a second time, then a third, and you have stopped asking. You are stirring the water, and every stir clouds it until nothing can be read there. Take the answer you were given and carry it off without another word. Then do the plain work well while the answer settles in you. A character grows out of small things finished.

Your hand and your ear are the two parts to use now. A hand holds a thing steady so you can look at it. An ear takes in what you cannot see. Hold still and listen. There is real danger under you here, the danger of not knowing, so stop at its edge and do not go down there alone.

### Judgement
- Youthful Folly. It goes through. It is not I who seek the young one. The young one seeks me.
- At the first asking I answer. Asked a second time, then a third, the water is muddied, and the muddied get no answer.
- Holding firm brings it through.

### Image
- A spring comes out at the foot of the mountain.
- So the wise carry each act through to its end, and a character grows from that.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a rule at the very beginning, and the shackles taken off again. → becomes Hexagram 41, Decrease.
At the start of not knowing, a firm rule does more good than kindness. Someone sets a limit on you, or you set one yourself, and that is what opens your eyes. Take the rule, then let it come off. A rule kept on past its use has stopped teaching you anything. Punish someone long enough and all they learn is shame.

**Line 2** · _image:_ bearing kindly with those who do not know, a wife brought into the house, and the son running it. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
Here you are the one who knows a little, and the not-knowing around you is yours to bear with kindly. Take in what is soft and unfinished, the way a household takes in someone new. The strong bend toward the weak, and that is what keeps a household standing. The young one you were patient with can run the place now, so let them.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a girl who loses herself at the sight of a man with money, and no good in taking her. → becomes Hexagram 18, Work on the Decayed.
Somebody here is latching on. It may be a person fastening to you because you look like you have something. It may be you, running at whoever looks strong and losing yourself on the way. Neither way gains anything. A person who hands themselves over that fast is not really giving anything. Stand on your own feet and let people come to you with what they have.

**Line 4** · _image:_ shut in with the not-knowing, and nobody near who knows. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
The people near you know no more than you do, and the one who could show you is far off. The not-knowing has closed round you like a room with no window. You cannot tell that you are in the dark. The shame you feel is real, and it is the door out. Real teachers do not hide from you. Go and find one.

**Line 5** · _image:_ not knowing the way a child does not know, open and ready to be shown. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
Here the not-knowing is only innocence. A child does not know and does not mind not knowing. The child asks, and takes the answer whole. That is you now, and it goes well. Stay open and let someone who knows more take you by the hand. Following someone abler than you is the plain road here. What was covered comes clear on its own.

**Line 6** · _image:_ striking at the not-knowing, and the line between guarding and robbing. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
The not-knowing here has gone on too long, and it has to be struck. Strike it, then watch yourself closely. Beat it out of someone and you become the one doing the harm. You can take a blindness away from a person. You cannot hand them your sight. Guard another mind from whoever would take it over, and do not take it over yourself.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Intolerance · _Gift:_ Understanding · _Siddhi:_ Forgiveness

### Shadow — Intolerance
Intolerance is a slant in your own logic. You have a bad day, decide you do not like someone, and by evening you have a list of good reasons. Underneath the reasons is a feeling, and the logic only measures what you sent it to find. That is how an opinion hardens into a certainty and a resentment into a conviction. You go blind with your own reasoning and cannot see that you have. Blame comes out of the reasons, then guilt, then a world split into sides.

Most people cannot sit alone in a room for an hour with nothing to do. They cannot bear their own minds. Under all of that is fear, thrown onto other people and held in place by an argument. Look honestly at the other side of your own opinion and you will find the fear it stands on. You handed your mind a job it cannot do, which is to make you safe.

### Repressive nature — Apathetic
One person gives up on understanding altogether. Their mind goes slack and calls it openness, holding no opinion because an opinion must be defended. They think they are slow. They are frightened, and the fright has emptied out the thinking. They come back by thinking again without letting the thinking rule them.

### Reactive nature — Nit-Picking
Another makes the mind the judge and waits for it to hand down peace. It never does, so the anger lands on whoever seemed to have the answer. It comes out on the smallest thing, a word out of place. Each complaint lets a little off, and none of them is the point.

### Gift — Understanding
Understanding is what the water does once you stop stirring it: it clears by itself. It has nothing to do with knowledge. Knowledge is what your mind reaches for to take the unease away. The most it can hand you is the hope of peace, and that hope is what keeps you asking. Understanding arrives after you have looked at a thing from every side and found that each side can be argued. A riddle with no answer works the same way on a mind: the moment it sees its logic will never solve the thing, something gives. The answer comes up as a feeling in the whole of you, from the belly, and the question is done with.

Freed from the question, your mind goes back to what it loves, which is looking at patterns and playing with them. You see both sides of an argument and take neither. You understand people the same way. The restlessness is still in you, pointed outward now, at what could be put right in the world.

### Siddhi — Forgiveness
Forgiveness is a warmth that goes straight through the border between two people and finds no debt on either side. None of what happened was personal. The part of your mind that needed a reason for it has died. What takes its place watches the old story and does not pick it up. No restlessness is left in you. You cannot make forgiveness happen. It comes when you are tired enough of the drama, and it lands like a surprise. Something choked in your chest comes open. Then the forgiveness runs backward down the line of the people you came from, untying what they left tied. Debts that stood for generations are cancelled at their source. Water that nobody stirs runs clear, and anyone downstream can drink.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 4, Formulization
You are under a pressure to make sense of things, and Gate 4 is the name for it. It takes a pile of loose facts and finds the shape they make. It will not rest until something fits. You puzzle at everything and have to know how a thing works before you can leave it alone. You are happiest when a pattern closes. What it makes is an answer, and that is how the mind tries to make your life safe. Every answer is a guess that fitted. Hold it as a possibility and you can drop it the moment a better one comes. A mind that can drop its answers stays light.

A mind like this gets called a know-it-all, the one with an answer for everything. You can be wrong and sound completely sure at the same time. The pull is to act on an idea the moment it arrives, before it has taken root. Then you doubt yourself when it does not take. The idea was not wrong; it was early. Watch which of your answers the world goes on to prove, and let the rest go.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
This pressure runs in the Ajna centre, the place behind your brow where what comes in gets sorted into sense. It takes the questions pressing down from the crown and turns them into something you can use. It stores what it has learned as your beliefs and your memories. It cannot make anything happen by itself. It can see and it cannot act. Whatever it works out has to be carried somewhere else before anything happens. It speaks best when somebody has asked, because the same answer is help one moment and an intrusion the next.

About half of all people have this centre running by itself. Their minds run all day and cannot be turned off, which is how they are made. They are built to feel sure. That sureness gives them somewhere to stand, and it keeps them standing there too long. The rest have no settled way of making sense of things. They take in the thinking of whoever is nearby and make it louder, then let it go when that person leaves. This kind of mind is not built to be certain. Gripping a thought to feel steady is like holding a steering wheel too tight. The tighter the grip, the less of the road you can feel. Held lightly, a mind like that sees more sides of a thing than a fixed one ever will.

### What completes it — the Channel of Logic (4–63)
An answer with nothing to test it stays a guess. What completes this drive is Gate 63 at the crown, the pressure of a doubt that will not call a thing finished. Together they make the Channel of Logic, one line of energy running between the crown and the brow. The question presses down, the answer forms, the doubt checks it. It is slow wiring, and it waits to be asked. Gate 63 may sit in you or in someone close to you, and the channel runs only when both gates are present.


## BODY

### Physiology
The neocortex is where this energy sits in you, and the reason is youth. It is the thin folded sheet over the outside of your brain, the newest part of it in the history of animals. It is also the last part of you to finish. The front of it, where you weigh things up, is still being wired into your middle twenties. It takes in everything it knows from outside you. Your words come from there, and so does every reason you have ever given for anything. That is the young mind's work in you. It asks, it takes in, it holds what it was handed.

What this layer has learned, it cannot put down. Look at any word on this page and you cannot see it as marks. The meaning arrives before you can stop it, in a fraction of a second, doing what took you years to learn. Now try to un-read it. That is a first answer gone into you, and there is no asking it back out.

### Amino acid
The mind that cannot leave a question alone has a substance in you, and its name is valine. You cannot make it. Your body takes it in, from milk or meat, or goes without. When your body builds a protein it folds valine into the middle, away from water. The same substance carries a change this energy is making, from asking to understanding. A child born with that change already has a mind that does not run riot, from the first day.

The reasons you give for not being able to bear someone are made of that same substance. So is the closeness that gets past a guard. So is the yes you give with the whole of you. It is one chemistry for how people manage to be together. Without this energy there would be no reasons between you and anyone, no expectations and no blame.


## RELATIONS

_Each card near this one is a place where your understanding gets tested: the start of something, a feeling that runs hot, people learning to live together._

### Pair — UL 3, Messengers of the Infinite (Difficulty at the Beginning)
This not-knowing asks about everything and starts nothing. Messengers of the Infinite is where it came from, the strain below the ground before there was anyone to ask. Open it when the question has gone quiet and something nameless in you has started pushing again.

### Programming partner — UL 49, Union in the Ashes
This mind hands you a reason for everything you feel, and it cannot tell which feelings begin in your body. Union in the Ashes is the pole set against it, the heat that rises before a thought arrives. At their lowest the two feed each other. The heat gives your mind something to be intolerant about, and your mind hands the heat its reasons. Open it when your reasons have started to feel hot. The whole thing may have begun below the neck.

### Codon ring — Ring of Union
Wanting a reason is what this energy does inside a bond, and it has to learn to live without one. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Union, and all four are about how you manage to be with other people. UL 7, Essential Nexus, is the guidance you follow in a group. UL 29, All In, is the yes you give with the whole of you. UL 59, Mystics Treasures, is the closeness that gets through your guard. This one carries the reasons people give each other.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards stand around this energy, and most of them show you a way to learn.

- **Ring Arcana — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lovers stand under an angel with a choice between them, which is the bond you have to choose rather than fall into.
- **Mountain (upper) — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teach): The Hierophant answers once from a raised seat, the teacher you go to when you are new.
- **Mountain (upper) — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): The Hermit holds out a lamp that shows one step at a time, which is as much as you need when you are new.
- **Water (lower) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess sits between the pillars with the book half hidden, and says nothing to anyone who keeps asking.
- **Water (lower) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs by one foot and sees everything the other way up, which is what a pause does to a question you keep pushing at.

### Immortals
Above stands Zhang Guo Lao, who rode his donkey facing backwards. You learn the way he travelled, by looking back at what happened. Below stands Li Tie Guai, who lost his own body and went on in a beggar's. You learn the way he did, from what a mistake costs you.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Gemini:** the twins, the sign of a mind in two halves that talks to itself and cannot stop asking. Two answers held at once until the truer one shows itself.
- **Hebrew letter — Zain, the Sword:** the blade that divides one thing from another. A question cuts the same way, and what it cuts apart it makes clear.
