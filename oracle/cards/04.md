# UL 4 — Veils of Knowledge

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Not Knowing Yet · The Young Mind · One Good Teacher · The First Answer · The Pull to Ask Again · Held Lightly · Nothing Was Personal

There is a not-knowing in you that is the start of learning. You have come up against something you cannot make sense of and you want to. That wanting is young and it is honest. It is the state every person is in before they understand anything. It is worth more than a shelf of answers because it is the one thing that can still be taught.

The pull this cycle brings is to keep asking why. You ask a friend and then you ask them again and then you ask someone else. Every asking muddies what the first one gave you. What you are looking for was already in it.

Ask someone who knows more than you, and ask once. Then stop and let what you were told do its work. Do the small things well while it does. Understanding does not come back by the road the question went out on. It comes up through you later, once you have let it be.

## ICHING

### Combination
Under a great mass of rock a spring is coming out of the ground. That is Mountain over Water. The water at the source is clear and has no idea where it is going. Above it the mountain stops the view and stops the water spreading, so it gathers at the foot. Danger below and stillness above. Something young is growing under a cover.

### Upper trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Stillness is the whole of Mountain, the mass that stays put while everything else comes and goes. It ends the road. Weather goes over it and rivers go round it and it does not move. In the world it is the boundary and the closed door. In a person it is the power to stop. You can hold still and keep a limit and wait at the edge of a thing without going over. It is the youngest son in the family, the one who stays home. Its risk is turning to stone. The stop that was wisdom becomes a wall, and stubbornness calls itself patience.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water runs downhill through whatever is in the way. It finds the low place and fills it and moves on. In a gorge it cannot see out and it keeps going anyway. In the world it is the river and the rain and every hidden pool. In a person it is depth. You can stay inside a question that has no floor yet and not climb out early. It is the middle son, the one who takes the risks. Its risk is the pit. What was a river to cross becomes a hole to sink in, and the feeling you went down into does not let you back up.

### Reading
Something has come up in you that you do not understand. You are the young one in this. Everything that ever grew was covered like this once before it knew what it was. You are ready to learn and you do not yet know what you need. The one thing you can feel clearly is the wanting to know.

What this asks of you is to go and ask. Find the one who knows the ground and ask them once and mean it. Then take what they say and carry it off without a word. The first answer is the clear one. Ask the same thing a second and a third time and you are no longer asking. You are stirring, and the water goes cloudy with every stir. A clouded answer is worse than none. Stay firm in the meantime and keep doing the plain things right. Plain things done all the way through are what a character is made of.

These two forces each have a place in the body. Mountain is the hand, which can hold still and keep a thing steady. Water is the ear, the part of you that takes something in without needing to see it. Hold still and listen. That is the whole posture here, and from outside it looks like nothing.

There is real danger underneath you, the danger of not knowing. Stop at the edge of it. Do not go down there alone.

### Judgement
- Youthful Folly. It goes through. I do not go looking for the young one. The young one comes looking for me.
- The first time you ask, I answer. Ask twice and three times and the water is muddied, and a muddied question gets no answer.
- Hold firm, and it goes well.

### Image
- A spring comes out at the foot of the mountain.
- So the wise do each thing all the way through, and a character grows from that.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ discipline at the start, and the shackles taken off again. → becomes Hexagram 41, Decrease.
At the very beginning of not knowing, a rule does more good than kindness. Someone sets a limit on you or you set one on yourself, and the limit is what opens the eyes. Take it. Then let it come off. A restraint kept on past its use stops teaching and starts binding, and the one who goes on being punished learns only shame. Use the rule once and lose it.

**Line 2** · _image:_ patience with the ones who do not know, a wife brought into the house, and the son running it. → becomes Hexagram 23, Splitting Apart.
Here you are the one who knows a little, and the not-knowing around you is yours to bear with. Do it kindly. Take in what is soft and unfinished and let it into the house, the way a wife is brought in. The young one you were patient with can run the place now. Strength that bends toward the weak is what keeps a household going.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the one who throws herself at whoever looks rich, and no good in taking her. → becomes Hexagram 18, Work on the Decayed.
Somebody is latching on. It may be a person attaching themselves to you because you seem to have something. It may be you, running toward whoever looks strong and losing yourself on the way. Either way there is no gain in it. What is given away that fast was never held. Stand on your own feet and let people come to you with what they have.

**Line 4** · _image:_ shut in with the not-knowing, and nobody near who knows. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
This is being in the dark without knowing it is dark. You have kept company with people who know no more than you do, and the one who could have shown you the way is nowhere near. So the not-knowing has closed round you like a windowless room. The shame of it is real and it is also the way out. Real teachers do not hide from you. Go and find one.

**Line 5** · _image:_ not knowing the way a child does not know, open and ready to be shown. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
There is a way of not knowing that is a gift. A child does not know and does not mind that it does not know. It asks and takes the answer whole. That is you here, and it goes well. Stay open and let someone who knows more take you by the hand. Being unable and following the able is the plain path through. What was covered comes clear without forcing.

**Line 6** · _image:_ striking at the not-knowing, and the line between guarding and robbing. → becomes Hexagram 7, The Army.
At the top the not-knowing has to be struck, because it has gone on too long and harm is coming from it. Strike it. Only be careful which side of the line you are on. Beating the ignorance out of someone makes you the one doing harm. Keep harm off them instead. Guard against the robber without becoming one, and the two of you end up on the same side.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Intolerance · _Gift:_ Understanding · _Siddhi:_ Forgiveness

### Shadow — Intolerance
The mind is stirring the water. At its lowest this energy is a mind that cannot leave a question alone, and every stir makes the water cloudier. It has a bad day and decides it does not like someone. By evening it has a list of good reasons. Underneath the reasons is a feeling with logic wrapped round it, and the logic only measures what it set out to find. Intolerance is a slant put on the mind by a fear underneath. Sit alone in a room for an hour with nothing to do and it shows itself as a mind you cannot stand to be in. Under all of it is a hunger for an answer that will finally make you feel safe. No answer ever has, so the next question is already forming. Sureness in the head does not make the body feel safe.

The way through is to look at the other side of your own opinion until you find the fear it was standing on. The questions are the seed. Kept up long enough they wear the mind out, and a worn-out mind is one that can finally stop stirring.

### Repressive nature — Apathetic
One person gives up on understanding. The mind goes slack and calls itself open. No opinion is held about anything, because holding one would mean being answerable for it. They think they are slower than other people. They are frightened, and the fright has emptied the thinking out.

### Reactive nature — Nit-Picking
Another person makes the mind the judge and waits for it to deliver peace. It never does, so they get angry with whoever was supposed to have the answers. The anger comes out on the smallest thing, the misplaced word. Every complaint lets off a little of it and none of them is the point.

### Gift — Understanding
Left still the water runs clear on its own. Understanding is what this energy becomes when it is met. It is a knowing that arrives in the whole of you and floods up through the body from the belly. It comes after the mind has looked at a thing from every side and found that every side can be argued. At that moment something gives. You stop needing to be right, because you have seen that logic can prove anything. The mind is let off the job of settling your life. Then it is free to do what it is good at. It looks at patterns and turns them over and plays. You see both halves of any argument and take neither, and you understand people the same way. The restlessness is still there and now it points outward toward what could be put right in the world.

The questions were the fuel for this. It was the hunger for an answer, pushed all the way to exhaustion, that broke the mind open. Nothing has to be defended now. The answer comes as a feeling in the body that the question is finished with.

### Siddhi — Forgiveness
The spring has left the mountain and runs out to everyone. This energy at its height is forgiveness, a warmth that goes through the borders between people and finds there was never a debt on either side. Nothing was personal. The mind that needed a reason for what happened has died, and in its place is a plainness that watches the old story and does not pick it up. It is quiet here, with nothing restless left in it.

This is what understanding becomes when even the one who understood lets go. You cannot do it. It arrives when you are tired enough of the drama. It lands like a surprise and something in the chest that had been choked comes open. It goes back down the line of the people you came from and unties what they left tied. The old debts are cancelled at the source. The water has gone on past the mountain and anyone can drink.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 4, Formulization
Wired into the body, the not-knowing is a pressure to make sense of things. It does not rest until something fits. Gate 4 is the part of you that takes a pile of loose facts and finds the shape they make. It is where an answer is formed. The gift is that the world stops being a blur. You can take what is confused and turn it into something a person can use and explain it in words they can follow. In you it runs as a mind that puzzles at everything. It has to know how a thing works before it can leave the thing alone. It is happiest when a pattern finally closes.

From outside a person like this gets called a know-it-all or the one with an answer for everything. What is really going on is that the answers are how the mind tries to make life safe. Every one of them is a guess that fitted. An answer held as a possibility can be dropped the moment a better one comes. A mind that can drop its answers stays light.

### Where it lives — the Ajna Center
This pressure runs in the head at the Ajna Centre, the place behind the brow where what comes in gets sorted into sense. It takes the questions that press down from the crown and turns them into something usable. It has no engine. It can see and it cannot act. What it works out has to be carried somewhere else before anything happens.

In about half of people this centre is defined. If it is defined in you, your mind runs all the time and you cannot switch it off. You are also built to feel sure. The sureness is a ground and a trap in one because you can be certain and wrong. In the other half the centre is open. If it is open in you, there is no fixed way you make sense of things. You take in the thinking of whoever is near and make it louder. You are not built to be certain. The harder you grip a thought the less you can see with it. Held lightly, an open mind of this kind sees a thing from more sides than a fixed one ever will.

What this energy needs is a real question to work on and someone who wants the answer. It starves on being ignored and on being made to decide. The same answer is help at one moment and an intrusion at the next.

### What completes it — the Channel of Logic (4–63)
An answer with nothing to test it stays a guess. The doubt it needs is Gate 63 at the crown. It is called Curiosity, the pressure of a question that will not accept a thing as finished. The Channel of Logic runs between the two. The question presses down and the answer forms and the question checks it again until something can be relied on. It is a slow wiring and it waits to be asked before it speaks. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the checking only happens where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
In the body itself the not-knowing sits in the neocortex, the thin folded layer over the outside of the brain. It is a sheet of cells a few millimetres thick and six layers deep. It wraps the older brain the way a rind wraps fruit. It is folded so tightly that about two thirds of it is hidden in the creases. Words and plans and sums are made here and so are the reasons you give for things. It is the newest part of you and the last to finish. The front of it is where judgement is made and it is still being wired into your middle twenties.

Look at any word on this page. You cannot see it as marks. The meaning arrives before you can stop it. The arriving is this layer at work, doing in a fraction of a second what it took years to learn. Now look at the same word and try to un-read it. What this layer has learned it cannot put down.

### Amino acid
Valine hides in the fold. When a protein folds up the parts of it that cannot stand water turn inward. Valine is one of those parts. It usually ends up buried in the middle, away from the water.

You cannot make it and have to eat it. Milk and cheese carry it and so do meat and eggs and soy. Whole grains hold more of it than of some of its kind, so a bowl of oats has it. It is one of three with a branch in its shape. Muscle takes those three apart for itself rather than sending them to the liver. The whole shape of a protein holds because of what is tucked away inside it.


## RELATIONS

_The company here is a schoolroom, the one who has just arrived and the one who cannot help reacting and the family that has to learn to live together, with the teachers on the wall._

### Pair — UL 3, Messengers of the Infinite (Difficulty at the Beginning)
Messengers of the Infinite is the season before this one, the push out of the ground that had no idea what it was pushing toward. Here the shoot is up and asking. Back there the whole strain was still below the surface and nobody could be asked anything. Turn either card over and you get the other. Open it when the question has gone quiet and something in you has started pushing again with no name on it.

### Programming partner — UL 49, Union in the Ashes
Union in the Ashes is the pole set against you, the heat that goes up before a thought arrives. At their lowest the two feed each other. The flash of reaction gives the mind something to be intolerant about and the mind hands the flash its reasons. Now the heat has an argument. Open it when your reasons have started to feel hot. The whole thing may have begun below the neck.

### Codon ring — Ring of Union
Four make up this family that works out how people manage to be together. One is the yes that goes all the way in. Another is the closeness that breaks through a guard, and the third is the gathering that gives a crowd its shape. This one is the mind in the middle of it. It is the part of a bond that wants reasons and has to learn to do without them. What the four share is union. Here it is the reason nobody had to give.

### Tarot
A spring under a mountain draws a crowd of faces, and most of them are teachers.

- **Ring Arcana — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The family hands this one the Lovers, two figures under an angel with a choice between them. It is the bond that has to be chosen rather than fallen into. Meet this face when understanding someone comes down to one decision.
- **Mountain (upper) — V · The Hierophant** (Tradition, Teach): The teacher on the raised seat with two pupils kneeling below. He answers once, and the answer was handed to him before it was handed on. Meet this face when you have found the one to ask.

- **Water (lower) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): Hung by one foot and at peace with it, seeing the whole thing upside down. Meet this face when the not-knowing has to be hung in until it turns over.

### Immortals

Li Tie Guai went out of his body and came back to find it burned, so he went on in a beggar's body on an iron crutch. One looks back at what he has learned and the other learned it the hard way.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Gemini:** the twins, the sign of the two-sided mind that talks to itself and asks and asks. Two answers held at once until the truer one shows.
- **Hebrew letter — Zain, the Sword:** the blade that divides one thing from another. A question cuts, and what it cuts apart it also makes clear.
