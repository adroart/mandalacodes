# UL 20 — Emerging as the Code

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The Pause Before the Act · Seeing Your Life Clearly · The Calm Others Feel · Choices That Come Clear · Letting Life Decide · Speaking From the Moment · Busy Instead of Present

Just before you act, you can stop and see your life as it is. Calm lives in that pause. You see the kind moments and the careless ones alike, without judging either. From there a choice emerges on its own, clear, from the whole of you. People near you feel this calm before you have said a word.

For long stretches you are elsewhere, reacting and rushing, without noticing. Then something brings you up for air, and you remember what being here is like. Meet those stretches gently. Forcing the calm back only makes you tight.

Make room for it in small things. Keep your space simple, and know where things go in your house. Before you drink a cup of tea, stop and look at it. Calm grows from a love of small things like these. Then it stays with you through the day, and you know that everything is going to be all right.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Earth is the hexagram of looking at your life and being seen. Wind is the attention in you that moves gently into every part of a day. Earth is your life as it is, the ground under everything you do. The attention sits above the ground because you see your life only when you rise a little above it. This hexagram has long been read as a tower on high ground, where you see far and are seen from far. Stand at a window over a street, and the people below can see you too. You are known by where you stand when you look.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the gentle way you reach people, a word or a look that gets where force would not. It sits on top because it is the side of you that others meet first. Wind has long been read as a breeze, which goes into every corner of a room without breaking anything. A kind remark can go that far into a person. On its own, this gentleness bends with every change of mood in the room.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the part of you that takes in whatever comes and carries it. It lies underneath because it is the inner side, what your life is before anyone looks at it. Earth has long been read as the ground, which carries everything that stands on it and turns nothing away. Your days rest on it the way a house rests on its field. On its own, it takes everything in and never stops to look at what it holds.

### Reading
You have stopped just before you act. The hands are washed, and the offering is not yet made. Nothing has happened yet, and this pause matters most. A sincerity gathers in you here, and it shows without a word being said. The people who look to you see it and are changed by it.

So look before you go on. Look at yourself first, at what you want from this and whether you are sure you are right. Sometimes it is not the question that needs looking at, but the one asking it. Then look out across your whole life, the way wind moves over a field and reaches every part of it. The seasons keep their order without a word from the sky, and what you teach by being seen works the same way.

Keep your eyes open and your hands still. Sit the way someone waits before an offering, and let what you see settle before you move.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Superficiality · _Gift:_ Self-Assurance · _Siddhi:_ Presence

### Shadow — Superficiality
Living on the surface of your own life is older than you are. An insect on a summer day is busy from the first warmth until dark, alive the whole time and aware of none of it. Much of a human day runs the same way. Your mind can only think in a line, from before to after, so each time you think you seem to leave the present. You still live in the present whether you like it or not. You only stop feeling it. Keeping busy then feels like being alive, though it is really a way of not being there.

It is painful to look back at the hurt you caused without noticing, or the dreams you left lying about. That is where the looking begins. At the end of a day, ask how much of it you were there for. Nothing is being judged. You are only seeing what is.

### Repressive nature — Absent
Their eyes go far away and seem to stare. A fear they cannot see freezes them, and for a while they have left their own body. Each time is a small death. It comes and goes with whatever the mind is doing.

### Reactive nature — Hectic
The same fear goes straight into doing. They cannot stop moving, wrapped up in themselves and in the next thing to do. The world around them grows busier because of it. Most of what they do would simply stop if the mind went still.

### Gift — Self-Assurance
Being looked after is the first feeling of this gift, as if something larger than you were keeping watch. That something is your own greater nature coming through. Life has its own plans, and you stop getting in their way. No method brings this; only life can show you how to let go. It rests on giving way rather than pushing, which makes it far more than confidence. Its seat is low in the belly. It grows through trust and patience. You let things come to you instead of chasing them down. This is a kind of holy laziness, and it is what lets you rest inside.

Criticism still comes, and you can take it in without falling over. Old habits come back and are let go again, slowly, achingly slowly. Life feels lighter, with a quiet humour inside it.

### Siddhi — Presence
A leaf falls from a tree in autumn, and the leaf does not decide it. The tree lets it go. Presence is that letting go, and one day it happens to you. You do not decide it either. Life lets you go a little more each day, until nothing in you is holding on. You could sit in one place for three days and feel no time pass. A leaf or a stone in front of you holds more life than any idea you have. There is nothing to say about it, so you are silent.

Anyone can feel it near you. The air around you eases, breathing grows deeper, and soon the whole room breathes as one. Your gaze turns soft.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 20, The Now
Gate 20 is the drive to act in the present, rather than planning ahead or going back over what is past. It carries a sense of timing, when to move and when to hold back, which is a different thing from haste. Waiting is part of it. While you wait, you sense what has to be set in motion behind the scenes, such as the right people or a skill to learn. When the moment is ripe, you move with all of your weight behind one act. In conversation your attention is undivided, and people feel it. The cost comes when you act too soon. You force what would have come on its own, then want to quit when it does not come.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center sits in your throat, where what drives you turns into a word or a deed. It is the town square of the body, with eleven doors opening onto it from six other centres. It has no power of its own. It works like a gearbox, passing on whatever power is wired into it. Through Gate 20 your voice is strongest when you speak from the moment you are in.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. They have steady use of their voice, in a tone that stays their own. The other three in ten have it open. As children they often spoke without being heard, so some went quiet and others got louder. Grown, they can meet each person in that person's own tone. They can say what a whole room feels and has not said.

### What completes it — the Channels of Integration (10–20, 20–34, 20–57)
Gate 20 has three partners. With Gate 10 it becomes the voice of loving yourself as you are, right now. With Gate 34, the strongest power in the body, a word and an act become one deed. With Gate 57 you say what your instinct knows the moment it knows it. The moment turns into something done only when one of them joins it. Each may be in you or in someone close by.

## BODY

### Physiology
Now is the reason this energy is given to the brain stem. The brain stem is the stalk your brain sits on, just inside the base of your skull. It sets the pace of your breathing and the beat of your heart. It has done this since before you were born, through every night's sleep and every hour your mind was elsewhere. It keeps no memory and makes no plan. Your mind can be in last year or next week, while this part of you is in this breath. When your mind comes back to your body, it comes back to where the brain stem already was.

Count the breaths you have taken since you began reading. You decided none of them. Put a finger in the hollow at the back of your neck, just under the skull. A finger's length inside, behind the bone, is the part of you that is always in the present.

### Amino acid
The calm in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is leucine. Your body cannot make it, so it has to come in from meat, milk or beans. Your body never turns it into sugar. It only burns it or builds you with it.

A child near you takes in what you are, more than anything you say. When you give without holding on, try new things and keep life simple, the child grows up sure of itself. Calm shares its chemistry in you with silence and with giving. It shares it too with a child's innocence and with celebration.

## RELATIONS

_UL 19 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the close look, where this one takes the wide view._

### Pair — UL 19, Solection (Approach)
This energy stands back and sees a life whole, and it runs out when seeing is all it does. Solection is where it goes next: coming close to the people who need warmth. Open it when you have looked long enough and it is time to go nearer. Watching from a height and coming near are two sides of one care.

### Inverse — UL 19, Solection (Approach)
Turn this energy end for end and looking up becomes looking down with care. Solection is the same watch kept from above. Open it when people look to you and you need to go to them.

### Programming partner — UL 34, Sublime Power
This energy sees clearly, and seeing alone moves nothing. Sublime Power holds what it lacks: the strength to act. Strength with no awareness in it becomes busyness that wrecks what it touches. Together they make right action, a deed that meets no resistance. Open it when you see what to do and still do not move.

### Codon ring — Ring of Life and Death
This energy watches, and a child learns by watching. It belongs to a family of six, the Ring of Life and Death. The other five are what a child needs to see you do. UL 27, Inner Majesty, carries the way you give. UL 42, Moving to Perfection, carries the way you let go. UL 24, Frequency Flutter, carries what you invent. UL 3, Messengers of the Infinite, carries the new things you try. UL 23, Beneath the Surface, carries how simply you live. This one carries your watching.

### Tarot
Its family and its two trigrams bring four of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, to this one.

- **Ring Arcana — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death is a change of form, the letting go a falling leaf shows you.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel keeps turning, and you can watch it turn without holding on to any part of it.
- **Earth (lower) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil is what you grip, and seeing the grip clearly is how it starts to loosen.
- **Earth (lower) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World is a thing brought to its end, your whole life seen at once with nothing left outside it.

### Immortals

- **Hebrew letter — Nun, the Fish:** a fish never closes its eyes, the way you can stay awake to your life as it moves.
