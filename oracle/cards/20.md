# UL 20 — Emerging as the Code

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing Your Whole Life at Once · Here for It · The Pause Before the Act · Busy Instead of Present · Letting Life Decide · Everything Is Going to Be All Right · The Calm Others Feel

Presence is the energy at work here, the power to see your own life from a height, whole, and to be in it as it happens. In you it is the part that watches without grabbing at anything, the part people feel near you without being able to name it. It lives in the quiet moments, a cup held before it is drunk, a person listened to with your whole attention.

When you carry it well it is calm, and calm stands out in a hectic world further than any charm could. The mind is no longer the one who decides. Decisions get made in the moment, through the whole of you, and come out clear. You stop chasing life down, you let it come to you, and what settles in you is the feeling that everything is going to be all right. You begin to feel what your presence or your absence does to the people near you, and the habits that did harm fall away on their own, because a thing seen clearly is hard to keep doing. A person living like this changes the air of a room, and the change is felt before it is understood.

Its way is to come and go on its own; forcing it only makes you tight. There will be stretches lived on the surface, with nothing in them felt, and that is normal. Most of a life is lived that way, pausing every so often to enjoy the sun. You live now, whether you like it or not, and everything that breathes in you has stayed there the whole time.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Earth: the wind moving across open ground, touching every blade of it and holding on to none. The traditional character is built on the sign for looking, with a wide-eyed waterbird set beside it. It means to look out from a high place, and to be looked at from below: a tower over a country, the country looking up.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the air moving, and what the wind does is enter. It comes under the door into every room without breaking anything, gentle and everywhere at once. It bends whatever it passes over without taking hold of it. In a family it is the eldest daughter, the one who gets her way quietly, by staying. In you it is awareness on the move: the look that reaches every corner of a day, the influence you have on a room without raising your voice. Its risk is that a wind settles nowhere, so the looking can go on for years without one thing being decided.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Underneath the wind lies the ground, the open field that carries everything and refuses nothing. Whatever stands on it can be seen from above, the houses and the people between them, because the ground hides nothing it holds. In a family it is the mother, the one everything comes back to. In you it is your life as it plainly is, the days as they are lived, there to be looked at without anything moved or tidied first. Its risk is going flat, when the ground that holds everything stops being alive, a surface that is looked at and never entered.

### Reading
You have come to the pause before a thing is done: the hands are washed for the act, and the offering has not yet been made. Everything is ready, nothing has happened, and the readiness is the point. It is the most charged moment, when sincerity gathers before it is spent. What is asked is to look before you act, and to let the looking be the work.

Look the way the wind moves over a field: across the whole of your life, into every corner, taking nothing. See how you are when no one is watching, how you are when they are, and the distance between the two. The ones who trust you are watching you as well, and what they see in you is what they will learn. The rulers of old went out among the people this way, saw, were seen, and made what they taught out of it. The seasons keep their order without a word being said, and what you teach by being seen wants that steadiness in it. Wash your motives clean before you go on, and if no answer comes, look at the judge rather than the judgement. Keep company with those who watch and wait with you.

In the body this energy is the thighs, the strength that stands you where you are. Notice how you are holding yourself now. Stand still, the weight settled, the way a person stands at a window, and let the whole of it be seen before one hand moves.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Superficiality · _Gift:_ Self-Assurance · _Siddhi:_ Presence

### Shadow — Superficiality
Living on the surface of your own life is the low of this energy, and it is older than you. Watch an insect on a summer afternoon: it never stops, busy from the first warmth to the dark, alive the whole time and present to none of it. A great deal of a human day runs on that current. The mind that came later can only think in a line, so the moment you think you seem to leave the present. A whole day can be done more or less right with a pane of glass between you and it. The only time you feel at one with life is when you are busy, and the busyness is the absence. None of this is your fault. Every so often you come up for air, in a moment of quiet or a piece of work, and remember what being here is like.

Looking back honestly is where it begins, at the hurt caused without noticing, the dreams left lying about, and it hurts to do. Turn the same look on today, gently, and see how much of it you were there for.

### Repressive nature — Absent
This person's eyes go far away. Something frightened them, once or often, and the life in them froze, then stepped out of the body, a small death each time. They are present one minute and gone the next, switched by whatever the mind is doing. From outside it passes for calm.

### Reactive nature — Hectic
The other person cannot stop moving. The same fear goes straight into the calendar, the pace, the next thing, so the quiet underneath is never heard. From outside it looks like drive. Most of what they do would simply cease if the mind went still, and somewhere they know it.

### Gift — Self-Assurance
Being held is what this energy becomes, and the first sign is that the mind stops being the one who decides. No method brings it; life shows you, by making its own plans while you agonised over yours. Decisions get made in the moment, through the whole of you, and come out clear. What settles in their place is a steady feeling that everything is going to be all right: you no longer have to run your life by hand, life moves you. This is nothing like confidence, which is built by pushing out at the world. It is built by giving way, and its seat is low in the belly, where the body's own knowing sits. You wait for things to come to you instead of chasing them down, and that waiting is a holy laziness, the ground all rest stands on.

Criticism passes through you the way wind passes through an arch, without knocking anything down. It comes slowly, achingly slowly, old habits falling back and being let go again. Knowing where things go in your house, a cup of tea watched before it is drunk: presence loves the subtle, and it grows in soil like that.

### Siddhi — Presence
A leaf lets go of a tree, and the leaf does not decide it. The tree lets it go; it falls, wholly surrendered, and that is presence. You do not let go; the tree lets you go, every day, every moment, until that is all you are. Whoever was watching is gone, and there is only the looking, with no one behind it. You can sit in one place for three days and no time passes, because time was something the mind was making. A stone or a leaf holds more life than every idea there has ever been, and there is nothing to say about it that is not shallow, so you say nothing.

It belongs to no one, which is why anyone can feel it near you: a silent ease in the air, the breath going deeper, until everyone in the room is breathing as one, and a softness in your eyes.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 20, The Now
Through this lens the energy of presence is the pressure to act at the moment itself, and Gate 20 is the door it comes through: the drive to do the thing now, in this instant, in place of planning it or going over what is already gone. Its name is The Now. What it carries is a sense of timing, when to move and when to hold, which is a different thing from haste. Waiting is part of it, and the waiting is not idle. It is watching what needs to be set in motion behind the scenes, the right people gathered, the skill learned, until the moment is ripe and you move with the whole of your weight behind one act. In talk your attention is undivided, and people feel it. From outside it can look like impatience, or like slowness, and it is neither. Its cost is that long plans bore you, that under pressure you act too soon, forcing what was going to come on its own, then want to quit when it does not. Carried for a lifetime, it teaches that the wait was the preparation, and that the moment always arrives.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Centre is where this pressure arrives, the town square of the body: eleven doors open into it from six directions, more than into any other place, and everything in you is trying to reach it. It has no engine of its own and cannot tell itself what to do. It is the gearbox, where whatever drives you is turned into a word or a deed, and the part of you wired to it is the part that speaks. Through Gate 20 what comes out is the present itself: your voice is at its most powerful when you speak from the moment you are standing in, and what you know here is when to bring power into the world.

In about seven people in ten this centre is switched on, and their voice is their own, held in one range, sometimes blunt. Their lesson is timing, since a voice always available is spent by speaking at every chance. In the other three it is not, so the voice tunes itself to whoever is near, and for them the trouble began as a child, when nobody listened: some went quiet, some got louder. Their strength is the other face of it. They can meet a person in that person's own tone, say what a whole room is thinking, and when they stop pushing to be heard, people come to them to be heard.

### What completes it — the Channels of Integration (10–20, 20–34, 20–57)
This pressure has three partners. With Gate 10, the drive to love yourself, it makes a voice that speaks the truth of who you are in the moment; with Gate 34, the strongest engine in the body, word and act become one deed; with Gate 57, the body's instinct, what you know in an instant is said in the same instant. The three together are the Channels of Integration, presence wired into the self, into power, into instinct. Whether the other ends sit in you or in someone near you, the now becomes a deed only where two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
Of everything in you, this energy belongs to the brain stem, and the reason is now. The brain stem is the stalk the brain sits on, at the base of your skull. It sets the rhythm of your breathing and the beat of your heart. It has done that since before you were born, through sleep, through every hour the mind spends somewhere else. It is the oldest part of your brain, the part a fish has, with no memory, no plan, nothing but this breath, this beat. Your mind can be in last year or next week; the stem is in this breath, and when the mind comes back to the body it comes back to where the stem already was.

Count the breaths you have taken since you began reading. You decided none of them. Put a finger in the hollow at the back of your neck, just under the skull, between the two cords of muscle: a finger's length in from there, behind the bone, is the part of you that has never once left the present.

### Amino acid
The calm in you has a substance, and the substance is leucine. You cannot make it; your body takes it in, from meat, milk or beans, or goes without. Your body never turns it into sugar: it burns it or builds with it, nothing else. Being at ease is a chemistry in you too, and it also came in from outside.

A child near you takes it in the same way. What a child grows up inside is not what you say but what you are, and one raised among people at ease with life comes out at ease with it, having taken in how they gave without keeping score, how they kept life simple. In your body the same substance is what silence is made of, giving, a child's innocence, the joy at a celebration. While you are at ease, that is what is in you.

### Programming partner — UL 34, Sublime Power
UL 34, Sublime Power, is the other half of this binary in the genetic code. Presence and raw force are wired to each other for a reason. Power without awareness is blind activity, motion for its own sake that wrecks what it touches. Awareness without power never reaches the world. Force that has finally learned to look before it moves is what these two make, and that is right action.

### Tarot
Every card this hexagram draws has turning or letting go inside it. The codon ring gives the great Arcana of Death, the passage. The two trigrams each lend their own faces: Wind above as the Wheel of Fortune, the turning that is always underway; Earth below as the Devil and the World, the grip that holds and the completion that lets go. The question underneath all four is whether you are watching the wheel turn or still holding on to one spoke of it.

- **Ring Arcana — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Through the Ring of Life and Death, this code carries the Arcana of Death. Not ending but the passage that runs under all life, the continual letting-go the leaf knows. Presence is what stands on the far side of every small death of the self.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wind above this hexagram wears the Wheel, the turning that never stops, rising and falling under everything. To contemplate is to watch the wheel turn and stay still inside the turning, neither grasping the high nor dreading the low.
- **Earth (lower) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Earth at the root wears the Devil, the pull of attachment, the chains that are loose enough to slip if you only look at them. The superficial life is bound this way, gripping the surface. Seeing the binding clearly is already half the loosening.
- **Earth (lower) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): Earth also carries the World, the closing of the circle, the dance at the centre of the completed thing. The long work of contemplation arrives here, the whole field seen at last as one, nothing left outside it.

### Immortals
He Xian Gu stands above, the one woman among the immortals, who took the contemplative path of purity and quiet and is shown holding her single lotus, and Lan Cai He below, the wandering minstrel who owns nothing and carries a basket of flowers through the world unbothered by it. What she sees by standing still, he proves by owning nothing, and the height only stays clear while the ground beneath it is held that loosely.
