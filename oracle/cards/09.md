# UL 9 — Ease in This

## CODE

_Keywords:_ The First Step · One Thing at a Time · The Groove · Yesterday's Footprints · Bored With Good Work · Held by a Breath of Wind · Halfway Up Without Noticing

This energy is the small step, with a great deal of power behind it. There is more strength in you than the day asks for, and the whole art of this is where you point it. Pointed at nothing it runs in a circle. Pointed at one thing you care about, it goes further than any great effort could, one plain step after another. In a life this is the long stretch made of small acts, the errands, the same tasks every day, which either add up or do not.

In a life that holds it well, it becomes a track worn by repetition. Do a thing with your heart in it, then do it again, and a groove forms that the rest of your life begins to run down on its own. Every act counts, however small, because each one makes more of whatever it was done from: an act done in resentment makes resentment, an act done in joy makes joy. When the small acts lead toward the one thing you want, even the chores stop being empty, since each is a step on the way. Without that one thing, the steps are decided by whoever is nearest, and you follow the crowd without meaning to.

Everything turns on where you look. Look up at the whole mountain and the knees go weak. Look down at your feet, and one step is small enough to take, with the next one easier. At every real beginning, a new year, a new house, the first few steps set the groove for all the rest, so take them with the thing you want held close.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Heaven: the sky's own strength moves below, and across the top of it passes a breath of wind, not stopping it, only shaping where it goes. Clouds have thickened over the western fields and no rain has fallen. The old character for the name puts a field under stored grass with a cover over it: a small store, kept.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the least visible thing there is, and it goes everywhere. It gets in under a door, through a whole wood, without anyone seeing it arrive. It is the wind that carries seed across a field, and the seed comes up where the wind left it. In a family it is the eldest daughter, who runs the house by a word here and there. In you it is the light touch: you get your way by getting in, not by pushing, so that people find they have agreed with you without remembering when. Its risk is thinness, a touch so light that nothing is held at all, only weather.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Beneath the wind is the sky's own strength, five strong lines of it, wanting to move. It is the force that gets things started and keeps them going, the one that does not tire. In a family it is the father, whose day starts first and ends last. In you it is the drive that would take on the whole mountain today if nothing held it. Here something does: a single thin line across the top, light as a breath, which the strength does not resent. Its risk is dryness, the force that goes on so long without rain that it parches the ground it meant to feed.

### Reading
A great force in you is being held by something far smaller than itself. The strength is real and wants to go, while the only thing shaping it is a breath of wind across the top, too light to stop it if it chose to break through. So the holding here is gentle, and it works by patience, by friendliness, by many small things kept, never by a show of power. The old king in this picture kept neighbours who wanted war calm by a steady stream of friendliness.

The clouds have thickened and it has not rained. Everything needed is present, the charge is high, and still it does not fall, because the moment has not come; forced, it would scatter. What this asks is to attend to the small. Tend the details that serve the larger thing, keep the outward things in order, your manner, the way the day is kept, and let the rest go. Be the shepherd who keeps a flock calm by the calm of their own mind, letting the sheep find their own grass, watching only that they are safe.

In the body this energy is the thighs under the head, the strength that walks, checked by what is thought above it. A warning is folded into the gentleness. Do not fall in love with the small advantage, because if small gains are all you take pleasure in, they are all you will have. Keep to the small with the great in view. When the rain comes, the holding is over.

To keep pressing after the work is done invites the trouble. Rest in what has been gathered, and let the fullness pass.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Inertia · _Gift:_ Determination · _Siddhi:_ Invincibility

### Shadow — Inertia
The small has got hold of you, and this is what it is like. A path of stepping-stones runs in a circle, and you look down at each stone as you take it, careful, attentive, never noticing that the footprints you are stepping into are yesterday's. The errands and the small things that do not matter drink your life a sip at a time. You stay busy, believing you are taking steps, when every step lands you back where you began. Enthusiasm goes first, then the energy, then each feeds the loss of the other, and what is left goes into complaining, out loud or under your breath. Looked at from inside the circle, the mountain is far too high, the knees go weak, and the question of what the point is arrives on time.

The mountain is not the trouble; the looking up is. A dull task is rarely the wrong task. It means the thread back to what you cared about has gone slack. The thread can be picked up again in one act, which resets the direction of every step after it.

### Repressive nature — Reluctant
This person sees the way out, understands it completely, and cannot make themselves walk it. Nothing in them chose this; the will has frozen along the old grooves, and from outside it looks like stubbornness. Leaving would mean walking straight into the fear, so they stay, and the decline is quiet.

### Reactive nature — Diverted
Here is someone who cannot sit still for a minute. Any noise or screen will do to pull the charge out of the body before it turns to rage. A fixed pattern would let the fury catch up, so nothing is kept to and nothing finishes. It looks like energy, and none of it rests.

### Gift — Determination
Determination is one step, taken. The mind shows you the whole mountain at once and the knees go weak just looking, so you stop looking up. You look down at your feet, and there is a stone to step onto. That first step is the hardest, and it is also the easiest, because it is only one step. Every step after it is easier, and if you fall off the path, the first step is what brings you back. The stones lead off over the horizon now; you cannot see where, and it does not matter, because they are leading somewhere.

The word sounds like force, gritted teeth, a long war of will, and it is closer to the opposite. The more of it you have, the less effort it asks, because what you are building is a groove: a thing done with heart, then done again, until the energy of your life follows the track on its own. By then the practice seems to hold itself up. Only the first few steps ask for real strength. Small acts done with heart gather a momentum that stops being stoppable, and one morning you are halfway up without having noticed the climb.

### Siddhi — Invincibility
Invincibility is in the weak, not the strong. The word sounds like an angel with a sword, and the thing is a child, soaked to the knees, running into a field of golden mist because she asked if she could. Nothing that delicate can be defeated, because there is nothing in it to defeat; what has let go of its edges is defenceless and unbeatable at once. The one force that works this way is love, which knows only giving, so it is never emptied.

The smaller a thing becomes, the more is packed inside it, until the very small opens into the boundless. All that ever was can sit in a palm, the size of a hazelnut, and the step you are taking is that small. Light brought to one point burns through anything, which is why a person living here can be hard to stand near: it lights up whatever you hoped not to see.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 9, Focus
Aiming is what this energy does in the body, and Gate 9 is the door it comes through: the drive to bring a great deal of power to bear on a very small point. The power underneath is real and wide; the gift is not in having it but in choosing where it lands. In you it is the pull to narrow down, to find the few details that serve the larger thing, letting the rest fall away. When it is on you can go for hours on one thing without noticing the room. It comes in waves, focus then scatter. It works best on one thing at a time; asked to hold three, it holds none.

From outside the scatter gets called restlessness, or a diagnosis, when what is going on is a focus with nowhere yet to land, spent on whatever is nearest. The trap on the other side is fixing on the detail until the whole thing disappears. People who carry this all their lives learn to hold the single step and the whole direction at once, the tree with the wood, letting neither erase the other.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The aim is taken low in the body, in the Sacral centre, the engine between the navel and the tops of the legs, the strongest you have. The choice of what deserves your energy is made here, not by thinking. Something is put in front of you, the engine answers before the mind has finished, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat, and the yes is the aim. What the engine has a yes for it can stay with for years; what it had no yes for drains it however hard the mind insists.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time. For someone like that the answer is the most reliable thing they have, and the danger is talking over it. Taken off its yes onto what the head thought important, the focus goes to pieces on trivia, then the engine burns out. An engine not used at all does the same, like a car battery that dies from sitting in the driveway. In the other three the engine is open. For someone like that the energy is borrowed from whoever is near, so they can concentrate like nobody else for a stretch, then not at all, without knowing when enough is enough until the body says. Their gift is the pause, the reading of when a thing is finished, which is the very thing a fixed engine cannot do for itself.

### What completes it — the Channel of Concentration (9–52)
An aim needs something to hold it still or the focus flickers and leaks. Its other half is Gate 52, at the base of the trunk, the stillness of a mountain: the pressure that stops the body and says wait. The two make the Channel of Concentration, in which the pressure to move is turned into hours of attention on one thing, so that stress becomes work. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone you work beside, where the two meet the attention finally has somewhere to settle, and stays.


## BODY

### Physiology
Every step you have ever taken began in the sacral plexus, which is why the body puts this energy there. From this weave of nerves at the base of your spine comes the largest nerve you have, thumb-wide where it starts, running down the back of each leg to the foot. Low in the same ground your body keeps its will, the part of you that goes on after the wanting has worn off. When your will is moving, the day has somewhere to go; when it stands, everything in you stands with it.

Walking is the groove already in you. Once you have taken the first step the rest go on without your thinking of them; the rhythm is made low in your spine and reaches your legs through this weave. Sit on something hard and feel the two bones you sit on. A little to the outside of each, the nerve that walks you passes on its way down, and you are sitting beside the part of you that takes the next step.

### Amino acid
The small step this energy is made of has a substance in you, and the substance is threonine. Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with what you eat, eggs or lentils, or not at all. Built into every protein you have, it carries the small hook where your body switches a protein on or off, one of the commonest switches in you. Beyond the building, it sets how your cells take light in and turn it into something you can use.

This substance is not this energy's alone. Three others in you share it; together the four unlock time and light. Here it is light brought to one point. A fine enough beam goes through anything, and that is what your focus is doing in you when it lands.

### Tarot
Five Arcana gather around this card, reached by two separate roads. The codon ring gives one Arcana, the Star, the quiet hope that keeps a person tending the small through a long darkness. The two trigrams each give their own faces: Wind above as the Wheel of Fortune and Death, the turning and the shedding; Heaven below as the Fool and the Empress, the first fearless step and the abundance it grows toward. The Star, the turning wheel, the shedding, the Fool and the Empress make one picture: a patient holding under a far hope, the strong force below learning to begin gently and to let what is finished fall away.

- **Ring Arcana — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): Through the Ring of Light, this code carries the Star, the small steady light that keeps faith through the long dark. The dense clouds have not broken into rain, and still the work continues, tended by hope that does not need proof yet.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wind above this hexagram wears the Wheel, the slow turn of cycles that no force hurries. The gentle restraint works with the turning, not against it, waiting for the moment to come round rather than seizing it.
- **Wind (upper) — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Wind also carries Death, the shedding of what is finished so the new can pass through. The soft penetrating breath is also the one that lets the old form go, clearing the way for what the accumulation is building.
- **Heaven (lower) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Heaven at the root wears the Fool, the fearless first step off the edge with no proof of the ground ahead. The great creative force begins exactly this way, by taking the one step the mind says cannot be taken.
- **Heaven (lower) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): Heaven also carries the Empress, the fertile fullness that small steady tending grows toward. The strong force below is generative, and what it accumulates, held gently, ripens into real abundance.

### Immortals
He Xian Gu stands above, the one woman among the Eight, who carries a lotus and walks a path of purity and quiet refinement, and Han Xiangzi below, the flute-player whose music makes flowers bloom and whose art works by gentleness rather than force. Neither of them pushes. She refines what she is already holding, he plays until the flower opens on its own, and the small accumulating happens in exactly that space.
