# UL 9 — Ease in This

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Determination · Small Steps Adding Up · One Thing at a Time · Doing It Again · A Strong First Step · Bored With Good Work · Going in Circles

There are times when only small things can be done, and they add up. Determination lives in that time, as the strength to keep taking small steps toward something you want. You have more strength in you than most days ask for. What matters is where you point it. Every act, the cooking and the washing included, either takes you toward what you want or keeps you where you are.

Its power is in doing a thing again. Do something with your heart in it, then do it again, and a groove forms that the rest of your life starts to follow. Each act makes more of whatever it was done from. Done in resentment it makes resentment, and done in joy it makes joy. At a real beginning, a new home or a new year, the first few steps set the groove for everything after.

Some days good work leaves you cold, and you start to wonder what the point is. That rarely means the work is wrong. It means you have lost touch with the dream behind it. One act done for that dream puts every step after it back on course.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Heaven is the hexagram of a great strength guided by something small. Heaven is the strength in you that wants to move. Wind is the gentle part of you that changes things a little at a time. The gentle sits above the strong because great strength is led best by kindness, never by force. This hexagram has long been read as thick clouds before the rain. The rain is gathering, and nothing you do will bring it sooner. Its name has also been read as raising animals and storing up food, and neither is done in a day. Your strength goes furthest under a gentle hand.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is how you move people without pushing them. It sits on top because it works on the outside of your life, in your manner and in how you treat the people around you. Wind has long been read as a breeze, which no one sees and which still carries seed across a whole field. A word said gently and often changes people the same way, without their seeing it. On its own, a touch this light gives way to anything that pushes hard enough.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the strength you wake up with, and it keeps going all day without tiring. It lies underneath because it is the inside of the situation, the power you carry whether or not anyone sees it. Heaven has long been read as the sky, where the sun and the stars keep to their course without rest. Your strength keeps going in the same way when it has the same road each day. On its own, it drives on until it has burned itself out.

### Reading
A great strength in you is being held back by something much smaller than itself. The strength is real and ready, and still its time has not come. The clouds are thick and the rain has not fallen. It is not yet the moment for the big plan.

So store up what you have, and do the small things well. Keep your manners and your days in order, since those are what you can change now. Be like a shepherd who keeps a flock calm by staying calm, and lets the sheep find their own grass. When something blocks you, take it in without bitterness, and it becomes part of how you grow. Do not grab for a quick result. Do not grow so fond of small gains that they are all you ever have.

Your legs do the carrying now, while your head keeps the far aim in view. Wait the way a farmer waits under a heavy sky, with everything ready for the rain.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ turning back onto your own road. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle Wind.
You turn back onto your own road, and there is no blame in it. You could have taken the main road with everyone else. On this one your strength grows a little each day, where no one sees it. Go back to the way your heart already knew was yours.

**Line 2** · _image:_ led back, one hand drawing the other. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
Someone takes you by the hand and leads you back. You go willingly, because you are not too proud to ask or to learn. Let yourself be drawn back to the middle of the road. You lose nothing of yourself by being led.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the spokes out of the wheel, a husband and wife looking away from each other. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
The spokes come out of the wheel, and the two at home will not look at each other. Strength that goes its own way, thinking only of itself, has stopped the cart. The problem in the way is small. Stop asking who is to blame, make peace at home, then go on.

**Line 4** · _image:_ blood gone, fear gone. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
Among many strong people you are the gentle one, with no force to hold them. Tell the truth and hide nothing, and the danger of a fight passes. The fear goes with it. Lean on the strength of the people near you. Choose to be a gentle wind rather than an anger.

**Line 5** · _image:_ trust binding, the wealth shared with a neighbour. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
Trust ties you to the people around you. They trust you because you keep faith with them, never because you ask it of them. What you gain here is shared with your neighbours. Give support and you will be given it, so keep no good fortune to yourself.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the rain has fallen and there is rest; the moon nearly full. → becomes Hexagram 5, Waiting.
The rain has fallen and there is rest. What you gathered a little at a time is complete. The moon is nearly full, and a full moon can only begin to wane. Pushing on now brings trouble, and so does pride in what you did. Rest, and let the fullness pass.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Inertia · _Gift:_ Determination · _Siddhi:_ Invincibility

### Shadow — Inertia
Inertia is what happens when the small things take hold of you. A path of stepping-stones runs in a circle, and you watch each stone as you step onto it. You never notice that the footprints are yesterday's. The errands and the things that do not matter take your life a little at a time. You feel busy and believe you are moving, while every step brings you back to the same place. Your enthusiasm goes, then your energy, and each loss feeds the other. What is left goes into complaining, out loud or under your breath.

Anything that is neither beautiful nor useful is trivia, and here it fills the days. Some days you make a great leap only to fall further, and it feels like you are back at the start.

### Repressive nature — Reluctant
Held in, the inertia freezes the will. These people see the way out and understand it completely, and still cannot take a step. They did not choose this, and the old patterns hold them fast. Leaving would mean walking straight into what they fear, so they stay, and the decline is slow.

### Reactive nature — Diverted
Let out, it turns into restlessness. Nothing in these people can sit still, and any noise or screen will do to draw the charge out of their bodies. A fixed routine would let their rage catch up with them, so they keep no commitment long. Their lives are full of movement and never at rest.

### Gift — Determination
Determination is one step, taken. The mind shows you the whole mountain at once, and your knees go weak just looking at it. So you look down at your feet instead, where there is one stone to step onto. That first step is the hardest, because the whole weight of staying put is against it. It is also the easiest, because it is only one step. Every step after it comes easier. If you fall off the path, that first step is what brings you back. Now the stones lead off over the horizon, and you do not need to see where.

The word sounds like force and gritted teeth, and it is nearly the opposite. The more determined you become, the less willpower it takes. Small acts done with heart build a momentum of their own. Break through once, and you know you can do it again.

### Siddhi — Invincibility
Invincibility lives in the weak, not the strong. It is a child running into a misty field at sunrise, soaked to the knees. Open like that, you cannot be beaten. There is no wall in you to break through. Love works this way: it only gives, so it is never emptied.

The smaller the thing you give yourself to, the more of your power goes into it. Held that way, one small thing in your hand can feel like all there is. People near someone who lives this can find them hard to bear. Their presence shows others what they most fear in themselves.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 9, Focus
Gate 9 is the drive to put all your energy into one thing. It picks out the few details that serve the larger aim and lets the rest fall away. When it is on, you can work for hours on one thing and forget the room. It comes in waves, focus and then scatter, and the scatter is part of it. From outside the scatter is sometimes given a diagnosis, when it is only how this gate works. Once it is set on something it is hard to turn. Its danger is getting so lost in a detail that you lose sight of the whole. At its best it holds the detail and the whole at once.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
Gate 9 sits in the Sacral Center, low in the body between the navel and the legs, where your energy for doing things comes from. This centre does not decide by thinking. It answers what is in front of you with a lift or a flat no, and focus follows the lift. Given something it has a yes for, it can stay with it for years. Pointed at what it has no yes for, the energy drains away on things that do not matter.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. For them the energy renews by being used. Left unused, it runs down like a car battery that dies in the driveway. The other three in ten have it open. They take in the energy of the people around them and can focus harder than anyone for a while. They often do not know when enough is enough. Their gift is the pause, knowing when a thing is finished.

### What completes it — the Channel of Concentration (9–52)
Focus alone is only a flicker. Gate 52, in the Root Center at the base of the spine, is the drive to keep still. Together they make the Channel of Concentration, which turns pressure into long hours of attention on one thing. With both present, you can stay with a task under stress and finish it. The risk is getting so absorbed that you forget to rest. Gate 52 may be in you or in someone you work beside, and the focus settles only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
Walking is the reason this energy is given to the sacral plexus. From this weave of nerves at the base of your spine comes the largest nerve you have. It is about as wide as your thumb where it starts. It runs down the back of each leg to the foot, so every step you take begins here. The same low ground is where your will is kept. It is the part of you that goes on after the wanting has worn off.

Once you have taken the first step, the rest go on without your thinking about them. The rhythm is made low in your spine and reaches your legs through this weave. Sit on something hard and feel the two bones you sit on. A little to the outside of each, the nerve that walks you passes on its way down.

### Amino acid
The focus in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is threonine. Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with food such as eggs, meat or lentils. It is where your body switches a protein on or off, one of the commonest switches you have.

You share it with three other energies in you, and what they do together is gather light. This one brings the light to a single point, the way a lens focuses sunlight until it burns.

## RELATIONS

_UL 10 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: steps taken with ease, where this one takes them with determination._

### Pair — UL 10, Internal Treasure (Treading)
This energy takes small steps toward something it wants, and it runs out when every step becomes an effort. Internal Treasure is where it goes next: the ease of being at home in your own skin, which lightens every step. Open it when the work is steady and the walking has grown heavy. The way is easier when you walk it as yourself.

### Inverse — UL 10, Internal Treasure (Treading)
Turn this energy upside down and gathering becomes walking on. Internal Treasure keeps moving, where this energy stores up. Open it when you have gathered enough and it is time to go.

### Programming partner — UL 16, Grand Rising
This energy keeps at one thing, step by step. Grand Rising holds what it lacks: the enthusiasm that gets a thing out of your head and into the world. When determination drains away, the enthusiasm goes first, so the two rise and fall together. Repetition is what turns that enthusiasm into skill. Open it when the steps are steady and the joy has gone out of them.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Light
This energy builds great things out of small ones, and it does not do it alone. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Light. UL 5, The Space Between Time, is the patience to wait for the right hour. UL 11, Sol Star, is the ideal that shows you where to walk. UL 26, Lighter Than a Feather, is the knack of getting a thing done unseen. This one is the step you keep taking.

### Tarot
Through the ring and the two trigrams come five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana.

- **Ring Arcana — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star is the quiet hope that keeps you at the small things while the rain holds off.
- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel turns in its own time, and a gentle touch works with the turning instead of forcing it.
- **Wind (upper) — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death lets a finished form fall away so that something new can grow.
- **Heaven (lower) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool takes the first step without seeing the ground ahead, the step this energy lives by.
- **Heaven (lower) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress is the full harvest that small and steady tending grows into.

### Immortals
Wind above carries He Xian Gu, the one woman among the Eight, who carries a lotus. Heaven below carries Han Xiangzi, the flute player whose music makes flowers open. Neither of them forces a thing to open. Your strength works best the same way, by tending until what you want opens by itself.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Aquarius:** the fixed air that keeps a far hope steady across a long stretch.
- **Hebrew letter — Tsade, the Fishhook:** the small hook and the patient line that bring up something far larger than themselves.
