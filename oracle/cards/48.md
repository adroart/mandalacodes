# UL 48 — Doorways of the Unknown

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Resourcefulness · Answers When Asked · Trusting Not Knowing · Sharing What You Find · A Little More Than Enough · Fear of Not Being Enough · Ordinary Wisdom

Resourcefulness wakes in you when someone needs more than you know how to give. You meet them open, without a plan, and the answer rises from somewhere below what you have learned. It is rarely what you expected, and it is just what the moment needs. What you do not know turns out to be a doorway.

Some days a quiet fear says you are not enough for what is coming, and you want to learn one more thing first. Sit with not knowing instead, and the fear has less to hold on to.

Give what comes up to the people who ask for it, and there will be more of it. You need only a little more than enough, and it arrives when it is needed. Again and again, you find you can do far more than you thought.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Wind is the hexagram of the source you draw from. Wind, read here as wood, is the part of you that goes down gently into what you cannot see. Water is the depth in you that holds what you need. Water sits above because what you find down there is lifted up into your day. This hexagram has long been read as a well. A town can move, but the well stays, and whoever comes can draw from it. It also asks you to keep people at their work, helping one another. What you need is under you already, and it is there for everyone who comes.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the part of you that will go into a hard place to get what is needed. Today it is the risk you take when you cannot tell how deep a thing goes. It sits on top because the danger is outside you, in the test the day sets. Water has long been read as a gorge with a stream running through it. You go down into the hard part of a day the way a stream goes down through rock, finding the way as you go. On your own, you can go in too far and fall.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the gentleness in you that gets into a place by going slowly. Today it is the patience you use to find a way into something hard. It sits underneath because everything you bring up rests on it. Wind has long been read as wood, a root that works its way between stones. You get through a hard conversation the same way, by easing in instead of pushing. On your own, you can reach into everything and hold on to nothing.

### Reading
You have been worn out, and now you come to the source. Everything around you may change, the work and the people, and the source does not. It gives neither more nor less than before. People come, draw from it and leave, and it is there for the next one. What you are facing goes deeper than it looks from the top.

Go all the way down. Water at the bottom helps nobody until it is brought up. The drawing fails in two ways only: the rope stops short of the water, or the jar breaks on the way up. So reach the whole way, and carry what you find in something whole. Then share it, and help the people beside you with their work.

The body's part is the ear and the thigh. The ear listens down into the dark, and the legs walk to the source and back. Stand still for a moment and listen before you reach.

### Judgement
- The well. The town may be changed, but the well cannot be changed.
- It neither decreases nor increases. They come and go and draw from the well.
- Nearly down to the water, and the rope does not reach; or the jar breaks: misfortune.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ mud in the well, and an old well that no birds come to. → becomes Hexagram 5, Waiting.
Mud lies at the bottom of an old well, and even the birds have stopped coming. What was once a source has been left too long, and nobody drinks from it now. Do not draw from what has gone stale. Find where the living water is now.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the depths water a minnow, and the jar leaks. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
The spring at the bottom waters only a small fish, and the jar is cracked and leaks. What you have is real, but it does not yet reach anyone above. Giving from here costs you more than it helps them. Mend the jar before you lower it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the well cleared, and nobody drinks. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
The well has been cleaned and the water is good, but no one drinks. That is a real sorrow, because the water is there to be drawn. Your worth is ready and overlooked, and it still reaches the ones who notice. Let it be seen by someone who can use it, and everyone shares the good fortune.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the well being lined with stone. → becomes Hexagram 28, Great Preponderance.
The well is being lined with stone. Nothing is drawn yet and nobody is served, and that is right for now. You are neither praised nor blamed while you repair. Tend your own walls before you are asked to give, and there is no mistake in it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a clear, cold spring, and it is drunk. → becomes Hexagram 46, Pushing Upward.
The well is clear, and people drink from its cold spring. This is a source doing what a source is for, empty on top and full below, giving without running down. Let it be drunk from, because what has a real source is not used up.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the well drawn from, and left uncovered. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle Wind.
The water comes all the way up, and the well stays open to anyone who is thirsty. When your good fortune is full, trust it and use it. Leave the cover off, and great good fortune comes.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Inadequacy · _Gift:_ Resourcefulness · _Siddhi:_ Wisdom

### Shadow — Inadequacy
The fear of not being enough sits low in the body, and it does not come and go. It has no reason. It was laid down between seven and fourteen, while your feelings were forming and the world sent them mixed messages. It says someone else knows what you do not. So you reach for more knowledge, or a system detailed enough to feel like solid ground. None of it reaches the fear, because nothing outside you can. Underneath, the fear is of the dark itself. It is like standing at night at the edge of deep, cold water with no bottom in sight.

That water is not empty. It is warm underneath, and full of light. Tension is something you learned, and as a child you were gentle. Go gently into the dark, and it is gentle with you.

### Repressive nature — Bland
Held down, the fear settles into whatever pattern the world hands over. There is no hard question and no look into the dark, only the head kept low. Life brings events that force a look, and once each one passes, the head goes further into the sand.

### Reactive nature — Unscrupulous
Pushed out, the fear becomes a hold over other people. Rage will not let this person admit they are afraid. So they use what they know to keep everyone else unsure, and they hide behind the system. Their own fear stays away while the room is more frightened than they are.

### Gift — Resourcefulness
Resourcefulness begins when you stop hiding from the fear and sit with it. You let a bucket down into the dark and pull it up to see what is there. What comes up surprises you, and so much of it comes out of the dark. It comes when you arrive open and unready, without the answer. Each time it comes up full, you trust it a little more, and the fear slowly loses its hold. The body takes about seven years to unlearn the old pattern.

It is meant for other people. They come and let the bucket down, and there is more each time. Used only for yourself, it dries up. Serving everyone is the surest way to serve yourself. It will not do the same thing twice, so keep moving. It needs the sky and the woods, and it fades behind a screen.

### Siddhi — Wisdom
Wisdom comes from not knowing, and it was in you before anything you learned. Knowing takes effort to gather, while not knowing is already there. Wisdom wells up gently from underneath, and if you grab it, it hardens into knowledge. It is like water, empty and full at once, taking the shape of whatever holds it. You are wise because you do not know you are wise.

It looks completely ordinary. Nothing inside you has changed, and seeing that is the whole change. Every feeling is allowed to rise and pass, the fear of not being enough among them. The body feels each one, and nothing in you is wiser than the body.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 48, Depth
Gate 48 is the pressure to go below the surface of a thing until you reach its heart. It does not work things out step by step. It gathers what it knows from experience, time spent and close watching, until the pattern underneath shows. Its fear is not knowing enough. The more you know, the more you see how much you do not, though you often know more than anyone in the room. Held too tightly, it waits for certainty before it acts, and the waiting can take over. It works best when you put what you found to use.

### Where it lives — the Spleen Center
The Spleen Center is the body's own alertness, the oldest awareness you have. It works in the moment, through your senses. It speaks once and softly. If you miss it, it does not say it again. It keeps the body's fears as well, and most of them are old or imagined, like a rope on the path taken for a snake. Only the present moment settles them.

About fifty-five people in a hundred have this centre defined. They can rest the way a cat rests on a porch in the sun, ears still turning, because the body will warn them. Their risk is pushing on through feeling unwell. The other forty-five have it open. They tend to have a more sensitive body, and they lose track of time or feel pressed by it. They hold on to things longer than is good for them. They also take in the fears of the people around them and feel them more strongly. Their gift, when they are well, is knowing which fear is theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Wavelength (48–16)
Depth alone has nothing to show for itself. Gate 16, in the throat, is the enthusiasm for a skill and the drive to perfect it. Together they make the Channel of the Wavelength, which turns what you have gathered into a skill, practised until it is yours. The fear of not being good enough keeps the practice going. Gate 16 may be your own or another person's, and depth becomes talent only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
Return is the reason this energy is given to the lymph. The lymph is water that has already been your blood. The finest vessels let water out into the spaces between your cells. Most of it is drawn straight back, and a second set of vessels gathers the rest, two or three litres a day. They carry it up to the blood at the base of your neck. There is no pump to move it. It rises from your feet with every step and every breath, so it moves when you move and pools when you sit. Beside it, the spleen under your left ribs holds a store of blood back until the body calls for it.

Sit for a few hours and your ankles grow fuller. Walk for a few minutes and they empty, because the water down there was waiting for a muscle to lift it. What went down comes back.

### Amino acid
The depth in you has a chemistry, and alanine is part of it. Your body makes it for itself, and you also take it in from almost any food with protein, meat or beans. About one in twelve of the pieces your body is built from is alanine.

This chemistry sends you out into the world of things to get away from the fear, into work and money. Nothing you buy reaches the fear, because it lives in your body itself. What this energy in you is for is bringing people what they need. Whatever you own will wear out one day, as all matter does. Small, ordinary things can teach you more than the cleverest thinker.
