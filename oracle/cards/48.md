# UL 48 — Doorways of the Unknown

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Out of Your Depth · Not Knowing Enough · Asked Before You Are Ready · Trusting the Dark · What Comes Up · Drawn for Others

This energy is depth, the well in you whose bottom cannot be seen. Look down it and you cannot tell how far it goes or what is waiting at the end. That is not a fault in it. The not knowing is the depth itself, and everything you have ever drawn from yourself came up out of it. It lives in the moments when you are asked for more than you know you have.

The well in you becomes trust each time you draw from it. You let something down into the dark, then pull it back up to see what it holds. What it holds is what the moment needed, which you could not have known from the top. Each time it comes up full, the fear loses a little of its grip, until one day you notice you have stopped bracing against the drop. What is drawn is for the people who come to you: drawn for them the well deepens, kept for yourself it dries. Your strength is in what you do not know, so keep moving, and do not try to do the same thing twice.

The way is gentle. There will be days when the depth feels like a lack, when you are sure you are not enough for what is coming, so you reach for one more thing to know. The reaching is what keeps the fear where it is. Nothing you learn reaches that far down. Sit at the edge with what you have, open and unready, then lower something into the dark.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Wind, the wind here being wood: a shaft sunk into the ground with water standing at the bottom of it, a wooden frame above to guide the bucket down and up. The old character is a field-sign, two lines crossing two, eight plots of land around one well at the centre, every family drawing from the same source.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the deep, the gorge, the pit you can fall into. Here it is the water itself, standing dark at the bottom of the shaft, the place the bucket must be let down into. In a family it is the middle son, the one who goes down where the others will not. In you it is the part that can enter what cannot be seen and come back holding something. Its risk is the fall: a depth gone into without a rope is not a well but a hole, and the one who goes in does not come back up.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Underneath is wind, which in the ground is wood: the root that goes down quietly and holds, the base that things rest on. It enters without forcing, a little at a time, the way a root finds its way between stones. Here it is the frame with its rope, the plain wooden support that reaches down to the water and guides what comes up. In a family it is the eldest daughter, who feeds the house unnoticed. In you it is gentleness, the part that goes into a hard place softly and gets further than force would. Its risk is going in without end, reaching everywhere and holding nothing.

### Reading
You have come to the source, and you have come to it after running dry. Everything around you has been moved, the work and the people at the table, the whole shape of a day. Under what was moved there is a place the nourishment comes from, and it has not moved. It gives neither more nor less than it always gave. People come to it, draw, then go, and it is there for the next one. What you are asking about is deeper than it looks from the top, and an answer that stops short of the depth will fall short of the answer.

What it asks of you is to draw all the way. Water at the bottom of a well is no use to anyone until it is brought up. The drawing fails in only two ways: a rope that does not reach, or a jar that breaks before the top. Go the whole way down, and carry what you bring up in something whole. Then give it. A well belongs to whoever comes to it, and the families that share one keep it by helping one another at the work. The well stays in its place, and the water in it never stops moving.

In the body this is the ear over the thigh: the ear that listens down into a hollow, the legs that walk to the well and back. Notice where you are holding yourself now. Let the head incline toward what cannot be seen yet, and keep the feet where they are.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ mud in the well, and an old well that no birds come to. → becomes Hexagram 5, Waiting.
Mud at the bottom, and a well so long left that even the birds have stopped coming. What is down there is too low to drink, its time long past; nothing feeds from it, nor should. This is what a source looks like when it has been presumed on and never tended. Do not draw here. Wait, and find where the living water is now.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the depths water a minnow, and the jar leaks. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
A real spring, down there, though all it reaches is a minnow in the weeds; the jar that should carry it up is cracked and leaks on the way. What you have is true, it is not yet enough for anyone else, and there is no one above to draw it. Giving from here costs you more than it gives. Mend the jar before you lower it.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the well cleared, and nobody drinks. → becomes Hexagram 29, The Abysmal Water.
The well has been cleared, the water is good, and nobody comes to drink. This is the one line that grieves out loud, saying my heart aches, because the water could be drawn and is not. Your worth is ready, overlooked, and still at work on the ones who notice. Let it be seen by someone with the eyes to use it, and the blessing is shared.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the well being lined with stone. → becomes Hexagram 28, Great Preponderance.
The walls are being lined with stone, so that what seeps in from the sides stays out and the spring stays clean. Nothing is drawn yet and no one is served yet, which for now is right; you are neither praised nor blamed for a season of repair. Tend the walls before you are asked to give, and there is no mistake in it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a clear, cold spring, and it is drunk. → becomes Hexagram 46, Pushing Upward.
Cold clear water from a spring with its source in it, and people drinking. This is the well doing what a well is for, empty to look at and full underneath, giving without running down. Nothing here needs fixing and nothing needs holding back. Let it be drunk from, because what has a source cannot be used up.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the well drawn from, and left uncovered. → becomes Hexagram 57, The Gentle Wind.
The water is brought all the way up and the mouth of the well is left open, so that anyone who comes can draw. It can be depended on now, because it no longer guards itself, and what is given freely from a source does not run out. Your own work on the well is done. Leave the cover off, and trust what is full.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Inadequacy · _Gift:_ Resourcefulness · _Siddhi:_ Wisdom

### Shadow — Inadequacy
Somewhere under the day there is a feeling that you are not enough for what is coming. It has no reason. It is not grief, which has a cause, but lower than that, a fixed hum in the body that does not come and go. It was laid down in you between seven and fourteen, as your feelings were forming, when the world sent you every message at once. It says you do not have what this asks, and that someone else knows what you do not. So you reach for what promises to fill it: more knowledge, a system with enough detail in it to feel like ground. None of it reaches the fear, because the fear was never about how much you know. It is about not knowing at all, standing at night at the edge of a dark, cold pool with no bottom you can see.

The pool is not empty. What looks from the top like nothing is warm underneath, and full. The fear was only ever of your own depth, and the way in is gentleness. Tension is learned; you were gentle as a child. Go gently into the dark, and it treats you gently.

### Repressive nature — Bland
This person has stopped looking down. They take whatever pattern the world hands them, settle into it, ask no hard question, keep the head low and the depth unvisited. Life arranges the events that would force a look, and after each one passes the head goes further into the sand.

### Reactive nature — Unscrupulous
Here the fear is put into other people. Rage will not let this person admit they are afraid, so they use what they know to keep everyone around them uncertain, hiding behind the system. Their own fear stays at bay for as long as the room is more frightened than they are.

### Gift — Resourcefulness
Something is let down into the dark and comes back up holding what the moment needed. That is the turn, and it works by surprise: what comes up is never what you planned, yet it is almost always right. You arrive at the hard moment open, unready, not knowing, and the answer surfaces from below thinking. People ask how you knew. You did not know; you are the field the knowing comes up through. Trust is built this way, by repetition, each time it comes up full the fear loses a little more of its grip, and the body takes about seven years to unlearn what it learned.

What you draw is for other people. They come, they throw the bucket down, and the well deepens by being drawn; kept for yourself alone, it dries. What you need arrives the same way, a little more than enough, at the moment it is needed, rather than stockpiled against a fear. It will not do the same thing twice, so keep moving, and bake the same cake a different way. It wants the sky and the woods; lost behind a screen it is the saddest thing there is.

### Siddhi — Wisdom
What is left when the reaching stops is wisdom, and it was already there. Knowing has to be gathered and costs effort; not knowing you had from the start. It rises gently from underneath, older than the mind. It cannot be kept, because held it turns into knowledge and goes hard. It is like water: empty and full at once, taking the shape of whatever holds it, still there when the vessel is gone. You are wise because you do not know you are wise. You are without fear because there is no longer anyone the fear could belong to; the body is not afraid to die, the mind is not either, and whatever in you is afraid cannot be found.

It looks like ordinariness. Nothing in you has changed, and seeing that is what changed you. Every feeling is allowed to arise and pass, inadequacy among them, since it was felt by the body, which is the wisest thing you own.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 48, Depth
Going under the surface of a thing is the pressure here, and Gate 48 is where it comes in: the need to go down into a subject or a craft until it gives up what it holds, satisfied with nothing less. The knowing it brings is not worked out; it is gathered, from time spent and things watched, until the pattern under the surface shows itself. There is a fear braided into it, that you will be asked for the answer before the descent is done, and that whatever you have brought up so far is not enough. The more you know, the more you can see you do not know, though in truth you know more than anyone.

From outside it looks slow, or hesitant, or never quite ready, and the cost is real: waiting for certainty before you act, until the waiting becomes the whole life. What the pressure is for is the moment you move without all the answers, trusting that what you need will arrive when it is needed. People who carry this all their lives learn that they already hold enough, and go down after that from curiosity rather than lack.

### Where it lives — the Spleen Center
Under the ribs on the left is where this pressure sits, in the Spleen centre, the oldest of the nine places, the one that keeps you alive. It works through the senses, in the moment; it speaks once, softly, and if you miss it, it does not repeat itself. It is the seat of the body's own alertness, so of its fears too, and this energy's fear is not knowing enough. Most of the fears kept here are not present dangers but past or imagined ones, a rope on the path taken for a snake, and only the present moment settles them.

In about fifty-five people in a hundred this centre is switched on, and the alertness is always there. Someone like that is the cat on the porch, asleep with one ear turning, resting because the body will warn them. Their trouble is pushing past feeling unwell because they always have, and letting the mind talk over what the body said once. In the other forty-five it is not, so they take in the fears of whoever is near and make them louder. Someone like that has a more sensitive body, loses track of time or is pressed by it, holds on to people or places longer than is good for them, from a feeling of lacking what it takes to survive. Their wisdom, when it comes, is knowing which fear is theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Wavelength (48–16)
Depth alone is a well with nobody to draw it. Its other half is Gate 16, in the throat, the enthusiasm for a skill with the drive to perfect it; together they make the Channel of the Wavelength, through which what was gathered in the dark comes up as something practised: a craft or an instrument, done again and again until it is yours. The fear of not being good enough is what drives the practice. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the depth only becomes talent where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
In your body this energy is the lymph, and the reason is return. The lymph is water that has already been your blood. The finest vessels let water out into the ground between your cells. Most of it is drawn straight back; the rest, two or three litres a day, a second set of vessels gathers and carries up to the blood at your neck. Nothing in you pumps it; it rises from your feet against gravity, lifted by every step, every breath, so it comes back only when you move and pools when you sit. It is clear, and its name, which you may have heard, is Latin for clear water. Beside it the spleen, under your left ribs, holds back a store of blood for when the body calls: the water there before it is drawn.

Sit for a few hours and your ankles are fuller. Walk to the corner and they are not, because the water there waited for a muscle to lift it. What went down was not lost; it comes back when you move.

### Amino acid
The depth in you has a chemistry, and the substance is alanine. Your body makes it for itself, though nearly everything with protein in it carries it, meat or beans. The silk you have touched was a third of it. There is more of it in you than of almost anything else you are built from. Others carry this same energy, and what is in you is the same substance in them.

The same chemistry sends you out into the world to get away from the fear, into work and money; nothing bought reaches it, because the fear is built into the body itself. What the energy in you is for is bringing resources to people, and none of what you bring lasts; nothing made of matter does. Wisdom comes to you from the smallest things, and a snail has more of it than the greatest logician.
