# UL 5 — The Space Between Time

## CODE

_Keywords:_ On Its Way · Full Sky Before Rain · Keeping Your Hours · Fed While Waiting · The Pull to Check · Time Enough · Ready Before the Hour

Something you need is on its way. You can feel that it is coming and there is nothing left to do to bring it. The strength in you is whole and rested. From the inside this feels like a full sky before rain, heavy and near and not yet falling.

The challenge here is the pull to check. It is opening the oven to see whether the bread has risen, and every opening lets the heat out. The bread does not rise faster for being watched. It rises in the dark with the door shut.

Let the hour come to you. Keep the shape of your days and eat properly and do the small things that were yours anyway. What is coming keeps its own time. Your part is to be rested and fed when it lands, and that is a good part to have.

## ICHING

### Combination
All the strength there is has gathered low, and over it the sky has filled with cloud and not let go. The rain is already made. It hangs whole over a dry field and nothing under it can pull it down. What waits below is the strongest force there is, and it has met the one thing strength cannot hurry.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
The deep water in a gorge does not know where the gorge ends, and it moves anyway. It finds the low places and fills them and goes on. Water is what keeps moving when it cannot see. In the world it is the river in the ravine and the rain held in a cloud. In a person it is depth. You feel a thing all the way down and you stay with it. You can live in a question a long while without needing it settled. Its risk is sinking. The depth that carried you stops moving, and you find you are living at the bottom of it.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven does not stop. The sun comes up every morning whether or not anyone is ready for it. In the world it is the father, the one who makes and drives and goes first. In a person it is strength that wants to move and can keep moving. You get up and you go at it again. You do not tire easily and you do not like to wait behind anyone. Its risk is dryness. The same force that makes things also parches them. Driven with nothing to check it, it burns through what it was meant to build and calls the burning progress.

### Reading
Everything you could do has been done, and the thing you are waiting on has not moved. That is the whole of the situation. Nothing about it is weakness. You are strong and the strength is ready, and in front of it is a stretch that cannot be pushed through by being strong. The timing belongs to the sky, and the sky does not take instructions.

What this asks is a particular kind of certainty. Be sure of the thing, and stop looking for the sign that it is coming. Waiting done well is a form of care. Tend what is near you. Feed the people in the house and feed yourself, and let the day have its usual shape. The danger ahead is real and you will cross it, and you cross it when the water says so and not a day before.

Both of these forces have a place in the body. Heaven is the head, the part of you that stays up and clear while the rest of you is made to sit. Water is the ear. You will hear the change before you see it, so stop watching the sky and listen for it.

Picture someone with the seed in and the tools cleaned, standing at the door under a sky that has not broken. Stand like that. Nothing about it is idle. The one move made to hurry the rain fails. Every small move you make from here comes good.

### Judgement
- Waiting. Hold to what is true in you, and there is light, and it comes through.
- Holding steady brings good fortune.
- The time is good for crossing the great water.

### Image
- Clouds rise up to the sky. Waiting.
- So the wise eat and drink, and take their ease with a glad heart.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ waiting far out in the open country, the danger nowhere in sight. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
You are a long way from the trouble and the trouble is not coming to you. Go on with the plain work of the day as if nothing were pending. Do not drift closer to see how bad it is. What keeps you safe here is the ordinary thing kept up without a break. Stay with it and no fault comes near you.

**Line 2** · _image:_ waiting on the sand by the water, with talk going round. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
You have come closer and the water is in sight. People have started to say things. A little of it reaches you and it stings. Do not answer it. The place you stand is firm and the talk cannot move you off it unless you go after it. Keep your footing and let the words run past you. This ends well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ waiting in the mud at the water's edge, and trouble coming to meet it. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
You went closer than the ground allowed and now it holds your feet. Each push to get free sinks you further, and the struggle is what draws the ones who take advantage. Nobody sent this. You walked into it, so stop pushing. Be careful and be courteous and give the trouble nothing to hold. Caution here is not defeat.

**Line 4** · _image:_ waiting in the place of blood, and a way up out of the pit. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
You are inside the danger now and it has cost something. There is no winning this by strength, because you have none to spare. Let go of the fight and listen. Someone steadier than you is near, and following them is the way up out of this. Give way, and you come out of the pit alive.

**Line 5** · _image:_ waiting with food and wine on the table, the trouble still outside. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
In the middle of the danger there is a quiet stretch, and this is it. The table is laid and you are at it. Nothing about the trouble has gone away, and it does not rule you. Eat. Take the rest that is offered. What holds you here is being right in yourself, and from that seat the crossing is made.

**Line 6** · _image:_ fallen into the pit, and three guests arriving unasked and unhurried. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
The waiting ran on past its end and you went down into the hole. Then three people come who were not sent for. They are in no hurry and they do not look like help. The place you are in is wrong for you, and still not much is lost. Treat them as honoured guests, and it comes right at the end.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Impatience · _Gift:_ Patience · _Siddhi:_ Timelessness

### Shadow — Impatience
A tightness in you says everything should already have happened, and this is the energy at its lowest. The reply is late and the light will not change and the thing on its way is somehow too slow. It feels like drive and it gets used as drive, and everything started from it lands a beat off. What sits under it is fear. The fear is that nothing is holding the pattern together, and that if you stop pushing the whole arrangement stalls. Breath goes high and shallow. The body braces like someone late. The afternoon under a sky that will not break gets longer the harder it is watched.

Impatience is not a flaw in you. It is your own rhythm gone missing, and what has gone missing can be found. The way through is to feel the restlessness instead of obeying it. Let it be there without acting on it and it sinks. Under it is the plain sense that life keeps its own time and that you are inside that time.

### Repressive nature — Pessimistic
Here is a person who decided some while ago that it was not going to get better. Nothing is expected any more, and the low mood calls itself realism. The rhythm did not break loudly. It ran down, and only something breaking in from outside will start it again.

### Reactive nature — Pushy
This one leans on the world to make it move. People get hurried and plans get forced and the voice goes sharp when life will not keep pace. Everything jams, and the answer to the jam is to push harder. It goes on until something or someone snaps.

### Gift — Patience
Stood with, this energy becomes the ability to let a thing take the time it takes. You notice the urge to force it, and you do not. The unease stays for a while and the loop that fed it goes quiet. Done often enough the waiting stops being a technique and sinks into the body, and the body cools. It is like the moment the cloud finally covers the sun on a hot afternoon. Nothing has changed and everything is easier. From there the rhythms you were too fast to hear come back. Winter comes in a life the way it comes in a year, and spring is built into the same turning. You stop needing the dark stretch to end before you can rest in it.

The fear that nothing holds the pattern is what this gift is made from. Felt through, the fear turns out to be the feeling of a pattern you are inside and cannot see the whole of. Trust is what patience is when it is not being practised. It opens the heart slowly, because a heart cannot be hurried open either. Patience is what love sends ahead of itself.

### Siddhi — Timelessness
No one is waiting at the height of it. The rain falls or it does not and nobody in you is keeping count. Patience is gone along with impatience, because both need time to happen in, and time has stopped being the thing you live inside. Moods still come and go. The patterns that make up a person keep running the way weather runs. Something has stepped out of the game and is not gripping any of it. This is very ordinary. It is quieter than the word suggests.

This is what patience becomes when even the one being patient lets go. Nothing is being fought any more. The strength that stood under the sky is still there, and the sky is still there, and they are one water. The rain is falling on the field now.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 5, Fixed Patterns
Inside a body that carries it, waiting is a need for the day to keep its shape. Gate 5 is the part of you that wants its meals at their hour and its sleep at its hour and the same small returns every day. Discipline has nothing to do with it. This is older. It comes from where the body keeps itself alive by doing the same things at the same times. In you it runs as a comfort in the repeated thing. The walk at the same time and the cup at the same hour and the bed made before anything else. When the day keeps its pattern you have more of yourself for everything in it. When the pattern goes you feel it before you can say why.

From outside this gets called rigid or boring or afraid of the new. What is happening is that a rhythm is being kept. The rhythm is what lets the rest of the life move freely. People who carry this all their lives learn to keep their hours without apology. They notice that the days they kept them are the days that went well.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The hours are kept low in the body, in the Sacral, the engine between the navel and the top of the legs. It gets up and works all day and runs down by night. It fills again while you sleep. Through this pressure the engine is tuned to the clock. It wants to run at the same hours and rest at the same hours. Allowed that, it gives the most.

In about seven people in ten the Sacral is fixed. If it is fixed in you it is the most reliable thing you have, and it answers before you have thought. What it needs here is a day with a shape. The same waking hour, and work that comes round like a tide. What starves it is a day with no shape, or being made to run on somebody else's hours. In the other three the Sacral is open. If it is open in you, you take in the pace of whoever is near and run at it faster than they do. You cannot keep it up. An open engine needs a rhythm more than a fixed one does. It will not make one on its own. Its gift is the pause, and the pause needs a place in the day.

How it moves here is by the clock. Beat and rest and beat at one interval, and the interval is yours.

### What completes it — the Channel of Rhythm (5–15)
A fixed beat needs something to swing against or it is only a clock. Its other half is Gate 15, Extremes, which loves the whole range of people and moods and weathers. It sits at the centre of the chest where a life takes its direction. The Channel of Rhythm is a steady tick with wild variation laid over it. A drummer holds one beat while the tune goes anywhere. The other half can sit in you or in someone you live beside. Where it does, the day stops being a timetable and becomes weather with a tide under it.


## BODY

### Physiology
Waiting has a nerve, and through the body this is where it sits. The sacral plexus is a weave of nerves on the back wall of the pelvis. It lies in front of the bone the spine ends in. Out of the weave run the nerves that serve the legs and the floor of the pelvis. With them run the nerves that carry the news that the bladder is full. Beside them is the nerve that lets you hold on. Right now something in you is filling on a schedule you did not set. The signal that it is time will rise through this weave. The same weave carries the order that keeps the door shut until the hour suits you.

The urge is not yours to command and the holding is, and both are wired here. Tighten the floor of the pelvis for a moment and let it go. The tightening is the sacral plexus doing what it does all day without being asked. It is waiting on purpose for a better time.

### Amino acid
The slippery coat that lines your gut from throat to end is built with threonine at every few places along its length. Threonine is one of the nine building blocks you have to eat. It is in eggs and cheese and meat and lentils. The gut takes an unusually large share of what comes in to keep the coat going. The coat is shed and remade all day. Food slides over it and the wall beneath is never touched. So the plain act of eating while you wait is paid for in the same building block. It is laid down fresh before the food arrives.


## RELATIONS

_These are the ones who hold a great deal and do not spend it early, and what you meet in each is a different way of being held._

### Pair — UL 6, Harmonious Mirage (Conflict)
Harmonious Mirage is the same strength a season on, walked out at last to meet the danger and finding the two want different things. The rain that would not fall has become the argument that will not settle. You waited here. There you contend, and neither side wins by force. Open it when the waiting ends in a disagreement.

### Programming partner — UL 35, Navigational Star
Navigational Star is set opposite. It is the hunger for the next thing, the appetite no meal ends. At their lowest the two feed each other. The hunger to get out of feeling unsafe makes you impatient. The impatience makes the hunger sharper, and under both is the fear that time is running out. Open it when you cannot tell whether you are waiting for something or only wanting it.

### Codon ring — Ring of Light
Four cards make this family, and each of them is the same strength held under something. Here it is held under a sky that has not broken. Ease in This holds it under a thin wind. Sol Star lets the ground come down to meet it. Lighter Than a Feather holds it under a mountain. What they share is a trust that what is held is not lost. Open any of them to see that strength held another way.

### Tarot
Under a sky that is full and has not broken, five faces keep their places.

- **Ring Arcana — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): Through its family the Star comes here, a woman kneeling at the water's edge and pouring from two jugs under one great star and seven small. The storm is over and nothing is rushed. Meet this face when what you are waiting for has already begun to pour.
- **Water (upper) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess keeps the door of the depth overhead, a scroll half hidden in her lap. She sits still and hears what has not been said yet. Meet her when the waiting needs an ear more than a plan.
- **Water (upper) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs by one ankle from a living tree, and his face is calm. He hangs there on purpose, and from upside down the crossing looks different. Meet him when the delay starts to feel like a punishment.
- **Heaven (lower) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool is at the cliff edge with a bag on a stick and one foot already lifted. He is the strength under the waiting, ready before anything is ready for him. Meet him when the readiness is the thing that needs holding.
- **Heaven (lower) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress sits in a wheat field beside running water, heavy with what is coming and in no hurry. The harvest is certain and it is not today. Meet her when the waiting needs feeding rather than watching.

### Immortals
Ask Li Tie Guai what a day's impatience costs. He left his body to travel in spirit and told a student to guard it for seven days. On the sixth the student burned it. The healer came back to nothing and took a dead beggar's body and an iron crutch. Han Xiangzi goes with him, the flute player whose tune made peonies open in the snow.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Aquarius:** the water bearer, who pours out what was gathered over a long while, in a fixed season and not before. The Star's own sky, cool and patient.
- **Hebrew letter — Tzaddi, the Hook:** a fish-hook let down into deep water and left there. The catch comes to the hook in its own time, and pulling early loses it.
