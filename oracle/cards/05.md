# UL 5 — The Space Between Time

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Everything Done, Nothing Moved · Rain Not Yet Down · The Pull to Check · Keeping Your Hours · Rested When It Lands · Time Enough · Ready Before the Hour

This energy is the wait for something already on its way. It is not the waiting of someone with nothing: the strength in you is whole, and what you are waiting on is real. From inside it feels like a full sky before rain, heavy, near, not yet falling. In a life it is the space between the asking and the answer, when nothing you do will bring the answer closer.

Certainty is what it becomes when it is carried well. What you are waiting for does not come when you call it; it comes when it calls you, and it will. Waiting and patience are not the same thing. You can wait either way, but only one of them rests. Act from resolve and the timing holds. Act from hurry and everything lands a beat off, however good the plan was. The pull is to check, to open the oven to see whether the bread has risen, when every opening lets the heat out. It rises in the dark with the door shut.

Let the hour come to you, and stay inside your days while it does. There will be a stretch when it feels as if nothing is holding anything together, as if the whole arrangement would stall the moment you stopped pushing. It does not stall, because you were never the one holding it. Rest where you are, stay with what is near you, and let what is coming find you.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Heaven: the rain has gathered in the sky over dry ground and has not come down. Under it the strongest force there is stands waiting, a force that could push through anything but this. The old character sets rain above someone stopped beneath it, made to wait for what only the sky can give.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is the deep, and what the deep does is go on without seeing. A river in a gorge does not know where the gorge ends and moves anyway, finding the low places, filling them, going on. In the world it is the rain held in a cloud, the pit in the road ahead. In a person it is depth: you feel a thing all the way down, you stay with it, and you can live inside a question for a long while without needing it settled. Its risk is sinking, when the depth that carried you stops moving and you find you are living at the bottom of it.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Below is the sky's own strength, the force that does not stop. It is up before the house is, and it is still going when the house has gone to bed. In a family it is the father, the one who makes, drives, goes first. In you it is strength that wants to move and can keep moving: you get up, you go at it again, you do not tire easily, you do not like to wait behind anyone. Its risk is dryness. The force that makes things also parches them, and driven with nothing to check it, it burns through what it meant to build.

### Reading
You have done everything that was yours to do, and the thing you are waiting on has not moved. That is the situation, with no weakness in it. You are strong, the strength is ready, and in front of it is a danger you cannot push through by being strong: a river too high to cross today, a person who has not decided. The timing belongs to the sky, which takes no instructions. Whoever crosses the great water crosses it after the long wait, when the crossing is sure.

What it asks of you is to be sure of the thing and to stop looking for the sign. Truth is what makes the waiting hold: if what you are waiting for is real, the light stays on while you wait. Keep to the ones who can wait beside you without talking about it. Eat, drink, sit at the laid table as the wise do while they wait; the rain finds a rested person ready.

In the body this energy is the head and the ear. The head stays up and clear while the rest of you is made to sit. The ear hears the change before the eye sees it, so stop watching the sky and listen. Stand the way a farmer stands at the door with the seed in, under a sky that has not broken. Nothing about it is idle. The one move made to hurry the rain fails; every small move made while it holds comes good.

### Judgement
- Waiting. With truth in you there is light, and it comes through.
- Steadfastness brings good fortune.
- The time is good for crossing the great water.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ waiting far out in the open country, the danger nowhere in sight. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
Waiting out in the open country, with the danger nowhere in sight. You are far from the trouble and it is not coming to meet you, so the day's plain work is still the whole of your work. The pull is to drift closer for a look, and drifting is how a wait is lost halfway. Keep to the ordinary without a break, and no fault comes near you.

**Line 2** · _image:_ waiting on the sand by the water, with talk going round. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
Waiting on the sand, with the water in sight and talk going round. You have come nearer, people have begun to say things, and a little of it stings. The ground you stand on is firm, and only going after the words could move you off it. Let them run past, keep your footing, and it ends well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ waiting in the mud at the water's edge, and trouble coming to meet it. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
Waiting in the mud at the water's edge, with trouble coming to meet you. You went closer than the ground allowed, before the time, and now each push to get free sinks you further, drawing the ones who take advantage. Nobody sent this; you walked into it. Stop pushing, be careful, be courteous, and give the trouble nothing to hold.

**Line 4** · _image:_ waiting in the place of blood, and a way up out of the pit. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
Waiting in the place of blood, with a way up out of the pit. You are inside the danger now; it has cost something, and there is no winning it by strength, because you have none to spare. Someone steadier than you is near. Give way, listen, and follow them up into the light.

**Line 5** · _image:_ waiting with food and wine on the table, the trouble still outside. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
Waiting with food and wine on the table, the trouble still outside. In the middle of the danger there is a quiet stretch, and this is it: nothing has gone away, none of it rules you. Resting is not putting off. Eat, take the rest that is offered, and stay right in yourself, because the crossing is made from this seat.

**Line 6** · _image:_ fallen into the pit, with three guests arriving unasked, unhurried. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
Fallen into the pit at the end of the waiting, with three guests arriving unasked, unhurried. The place is wrong for you, and still not much is lost. Help comes now from people you did not send for, who may not look like help; some of what they carry is your own strength, handed back. Honour them as guests, and it comes right in the end.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Impatience · _Gift:_ Patience · _Siddhi:_ Timelessness

### Shadow — Impatience
A tightness in you says that everything should already have happened, and the tightness is impatience. The reply is late, the light will not change, the thing on its way is somehow too slow. It feels like drive, it gets used as drive, and everything begun from it lands a beat off, because action out of agitation is never the same as action out of resolve. Under it is a fear older than anything that happened to you, the fear that nothing is holding the pattern together; a body tuned to that fear never feels safe, however much it arranges. Breath goes high and shallow. The whole of you braces like someone late. It is a dry afternoon spent watching a sky that will not break, and the afternoon gets longer the harder it is watched.

Impatience is not a flaw in you. It is your own rhythm gone missing, and a rhythm can be found again. Feel the restlessness instead of obeying it and it sinks on its own. Under it is the plain sense that life keeps its own time, and that you are inside that time, not outside it waiting to be let in.

### Repressive nature — Pessimistic
This person decided a while ago that it was not going to get better. Nothing is expected any more, and the low mood calls itself being realistic. The rhythm did not break loudly; it ran down, and nothing they do on their own will start it again. Only something breaking in from outside does.

### Reactive nature — Pushy
Here is someone who leans on the world to make it move. People get hurried, plans get forced, the voice goes sharp when life will not keep pace, and life answers by blocking every path. The answer to the block is to push harder, until something or someone snaps.

### Gift — Patience
Let a thing take the time it takes: that is the whole of patience, and it can be learned. It takes patience to learn it, and it is the one skill that proves itself, since every wait that pays makes the next one easier. At first it is a technique: you notice the urge to force, you do not act on it, the unease stays a while, then the loop that fed it goes quiet. Done often enough it sinks into the body, and one day the body cools on its own, the way a mist comes up off a river to calm a hot afternoon. Nothing has changed and everything is easier. From there you hear the rhythms you were too fast to hear. Winter comes in a life the way it comes in a year, and passes the same way, on its own, when it is let.

Patience is trust when it is not being practised. Life keeps a pattern you are inside and cannot see the whole of; hindsight keeps proving it was there. Kindness comes from the same slowing, and so does love, because patience is what love sends ahead of itself.

### Siddhi — Timelessness
No one is waiting any more. The rain falls or it does not, and nobody in you is keeping count. Patience is gone along with impatience, because both need time to happen in, and time has stopped being the thing you live inside. Moods still come and go. The patterns that make up a person keep running, the way weather runs, while something has stepped out of the game and is not gripping any of it. This is very ordinary, quieter than the word makes it sound.

It is what patience becomes when even the one being patient lets go: the end of all fighting. Everything alive is waiting, and a life can be seen as one long wait for its end; from here that is not sad, only true. The strength that stood under the sky is still there, and so is the sky. The rain is falling on the field.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 5, Fixed Patterns
A day with a shape is what this energy needs in the body, and Gate 5 is the door it comes through: the part of you that wants its meals at their hour, its sleep at its hour, the same small returns every day. Discipline has nothing to do with it. This is older, from where the body keeps itself alive by doing the same things at the same times, and in you it runs as comfort in the repeated thing: the walk at the same time, the bed made before anything else. When the day keeps its pattern you have more of yourself for everything in it. When the pattern goes you feel it before you can say why.

From outside this gets called rigid, or afraid of the new. What is happening is that a rhythm is being kept, and the rhythm is what lets the rest of the life move freely. It goes wrong the same way from both ends, forcing order on the day by will or giving up on shape altogether. People who carry this all their lives learn to keep their hours without apology, and to stop asking anyone else to keep them.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The hours are kept low in the body, in the Sacral centre, the engine between the navel and the tops of the legs, which works all day, spent by night. It decides nothing; it answers, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat, only to what is in front of it. Through this pressure the engine is tuned to the clock: it wants to run at the same hours, rest at the same hours, and given that, it gives the most.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time. For someone like that the answer comes before the thought. What feeds them is a day with a shape, work that comes round like a tide; what starves them is a day with none, or somebody else's hours. In the other three the engine is open. For someone like that it runs on whoever is near: they take in another person's pace, run at it faster than its owner, then cannot keep it up. An open engine needs a rhythm more than a fixed one does; it will not make one on its own. Its gift is the pause, which needs a place in the day. Every drive that sits here passes through the first excitement, the jump in, then the flat stretch where nothing moves: the place people quit, usually the pause before the next level.

### What completes it — the Channel of Rhythm (5–15)
A fixed beat needs something to swing against or it is only a clock. Its other half is Gate 15, in the centre of the chest, which loves the whole range of people and weathers, moving between extremes. The two make the Channel of Rhythm: a steady tick with wild variation laid over it, the way a drummer holds one beat while the tune goes anywhere. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone you live beside, where the two meet the day stops being a timetable and becomes weather with a tide under it.


## BODY

### Physiology
The body gives this energy to the sacral plexus, and the reason is that this is where you hold on. Against the inner face of the flat bone at the base of your spine a weave of nerves gathers, and out of it run the nerves of your legs, with the nerves of the doors below. What is behind those doors fills on a schedule you did not set. The weave carries the news that it is full, then keeps the door shut until the hour suits you. The engine that works you all day keeps itself here too, in the dark: it runs down by night, fills while you sleep, and is whole before you ask.

You are doing it now. Something below your navel has been filling since you last stood up, and this weave is holding it until the time comes. Put a hand flat on the base of your spine, on the bone between the hips. The weave lies just inside it, and you are resting your hand on the part of you that can wait.

### Amino acid
The stillness this energy asks of you has a substance in you, and the substance is threonine. It is one your body cannot make, so it arrives in what you eat, eggs or beans, or it does not arrive. Beyond what it builds, it sets in you how your cells take light in and turn it into something you can use. How much reaches you is set by how you live: fear closes it down, an open heart draws it in.

This substance is not this energy's alone; three others in you share it, and it does the same thing in all four. Anyone who has been in love knows what it does: the hours pass differently then, as if you had stepped out of time. Patience felt deep in your body does the same thing more slowly. It opens your heart, and the opened heart lets the light in.

## RELATIONS

_These are the ones who hold a great deal and do not spend it early, and what you meet in each is a different way of being held._

### Pair — UL 6, Harmonious Mirage (Conflict)
Harmonious Mirage is the same strength a season on, walked out at last to meet the danger and finding the two want different things. The rain that would not fall has become the argument that will not settle. You waited here. There you contend, and neither side wins by force. Open it when the waiting ends in a disagreement.

### Programming partner — UL 35, Navigational Star
Navigational Star is set opposite. It is the hunger for the next thing, the appetite no meal ends. At their lowest the two feed each other. The hunger to get out of feeling unsafe makes you impatient. The impatience makes the hunger sharper, and under both is the fear that time is running out. Open it when you cannot tell whether you are waiting for something or only wanting it.

### Codon ring — Ring of Light
Four cards make this family, and each of them is the same strength held under something. Here it is held under a sky that has not broken. Ease in This holds it under a thin wind. Sol Star lets the ground come down to meet it. Lighter Than a Feather holds it under a mountain. What they share is a trust that what is held is not lost. Open any of them to see that strength held another way.

### Tarot

- **Water (upper) — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess keeps the door of the depth overhead, a scroll half hidden in her lap. She sits still and hears what has not been said yet. Meet her when the waiting needs an ear more than a plan.

### Immortals

The healer came back to nothing and took a dead beggar's body and an iron crutch. Han Xiangzi goes with him, the flute player whose tune made peonies open in the snow.

- **Hebrew letter — Tzaddi, the Hook:** a fish-hook let down into deep water and left there. The catch comes to the hook in its own time, and pulling early loses it.
