# UL 5 — The Space Between Time

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Waiting Well · Your Part Is Done · Taking Your Rest · Keeping Your Hours · Life Keeps Its Time · Pushing Too Soon · Checking Too Often

This is the energy of waiting, and what you are waiting on is already real. This is not the waiting of someone with nothing coming. From inside it feels like a full sky before the rain comes down. In a life it is the space between the asking and the answer. Nothing you do in that space brings the answer nearer.

Wait without forcing it and you become sure of the thing. What you are waiting for does not come when you call it. It comes when it calls you. Waiting and patience are not the same thing, and only patience rests. Act from resolve and the thing comes at its right time. Act from hurry and the thing lands a beat off, however good the plan was. The pull is to check. You open the oven to see whether the bread has risen, and every opening lets the heat out. Bread rises in the dark with the door shut.

Let the hour come to you, and go on living your ordinary days while it does. There will be a stretch when it feels as though nothing is holding anything together. You will think the whole thing would stall the moment you stopped pushing. It does not stall. You are not the one holding it.

## ICHING

### Combination
Water over Heaven is the hexagram of waiting for what you cannot hurry. Heaven is the strength in you that never stops going at a thing. Water is the danger in front of you that no strength gets through. The danger sits above the strength because your strength pushes through everything but this. That is why this hexagram has long been read as clouds gathered over dry ground. The rain comes when the sky is full. The old character sets rain above someone stopped beneath it, made to wait for what only the sky can give. You are strong here, and the timing is not yours.

### Upper trigram — Water (Kan)
You can keep going into a thing without seeing where it ends. A river in a gorge does not know where the gorge ends and goes on anyway. You feel a thing all the way down, so you can live inside a question for a long while without needing it settled. The depth sits above your strength because what you cannot see through is what stops you, never weakness. Water has long been read as a pit in the road ahead. On its own it only goes down, and one day you find you are living at the bottom of it.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
You are up before the house and still going when it has gone to bed. You go at a thing again, and you do not like to wait behind anyone. In a family it is the father, the one who makes and goes first. Your strength sits under the danger because you are already moving before anything stands in your way. Heaven has long been read as the sky's own strength, the force that does not stop. On its own it parches what it grows, and it burns through what it meant to build.

### Reading
You have done your part, and the thing you are waiting on has not moved. There is no weakness in that. What stands in front of you will not be pushed: a river too high to cross today, or a person who has not decided. The timing belongs to the sky, which takes no instructions. You cross the great water at the end of the wait, when the crossing is sure.

What this asks is that you be sure of the thing and stop looking for a sign. You can only wait this long for something true. If the thing you are waiting for is real, it stays clear to you the whole time. Keep to the ones who can wait beside you without talking about it. Eat, drink and sit at the laid table the way the wise do. The rain finds a rested person ready.

Your head and your ear are the parts to use now. The head stays up and clear while the rest of you sits. The ear hears a change before the eye sees it, so stop watching the sky and listen. Stand the way a farmer stands at his door with the seed already in the ground, under a sky that has not broken. Nothing about that is idle. The one move you make to hurry the rain fails. Every small move you make inside the wait comes good.

### Judgement
- Waiting. With truth in you there is light, and it comes through.
- Steadfastness brings good fortune.
- The time is good for crossing the great water.

### Image

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ waiting far out in the open country, the danger nowhere in sight. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
Waiting far out in the open country, with the danger nowhere in sight. You are far from the trouble and it is not coming to meet you. The plain work of your day is still the whole of it. The pull is to drift closer for a look, and that is what spoils the wait. Keep to the ordinary without a break, and no fault comes near you.

**Line 2** · _image:_ waiting on the sand by the water, with talk going round. → becomes Hexagram 63, After Completion.
Waiting on the sand, with the water in sight and talk going round. You have come nearer, and people have begun to say things. A little of it stings. The ground you stand on is firm. The only thing that moves you off it is going after the words. Let them run past, keep your footing, and it ends well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ waiting in the mud at the water's edge, and trouble coming to meet it. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
Waiting in the mud at the water's edge, with trouble coming to meet you. You went closer than the ground allowed, and you went before the time. Now every push to get free sinks you further and draws the ones who take advantage. Nobody sent this trouble. You walked into it. Stop pushing, keep your manners, and give it nothing to hold.

**Line 4** · _image:_ waiting in the place of blood, and a way up out of the pit. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
Waiting in the place of blood, with a way up out of the pit. You are inside the danger now and it has already cost you something. There is no winning this by strength, and you have none to spare. Someone steadier than you is near. Give way, listen, and follow them up into the light.

**Line 5** · _image:_ waiting with food and wine on the table, the trouble still outside. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
Waiting with food and wine on the table, the trouble still outside. In the middle of the danger there is a quiet stretch, and this is it. Nothing has gone away and none of it rules you. Resting is not putting anything off. Eat, take the rest you are offered, and stay right in yourself. You make the crossing from this seat.

**Line 6** · _image:_ fallen into the pit, with three guests arriving unasked, unhurried. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
Fallen into the pit at the end of the waiting, with three guests arriving unasked and unhurried. The place is wrong for you, and not much is lost. Help comes now from people you did not send for, and they may not look like help. Some of what they carry is your own strength, handed back. Honour them as guests, and it comes right in the end.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Impatience · _Gift:_ Patience · _Siddhi:_ Timelessness

### Shadow — Impatience
Impatience is a tightness in you that says everything should already have happened. The reply is late. The thing on its way is somehow too slow. It feels like drive and gets used as drive. What you begin from it lands a beat off, because acting out of agitation is never the same as acting out of resolve. Under it is a fear older than anything that happened to you, the fear that nothing is holding the pattern together. A body tuned to that fear never feels safe, whatever you arrange around it. Your breath goes high and shallow. The whole of you braces like someone late. It is a dry afternoon spent watching a sky that will not break, and the watching makes it longer.

None of this is a flaw in you. It is your own rhythm gone missing, and you can find it again. Feel the restlessness instead of obeying it and it sinks on its own. Underneath is the plain sense that life keeps its own time. You are inside that time, not outside it waiting to be let in.

### Repressive nature — Pessimistic
This person decided a while ago that it was not going to get better. They expect nothing now, and the low mood calls itself being realistic. The rhythm did not break loudly. It wound down, and nothing they do alone will start it again. Only something breaking in from outside can.

### Reactive nature — Pushy
Here is someone who leans on the world to make it move. They hurry the people around them. The voice goes sharp when things will not keep pace, and life answers by blocking every path. Their answer to the block is to push harder, until something or someone snaps.

### Gift — Patience
Letting a thing take the time it takes is the whole of patience, and you can learn it. Learning it takes patience. It is the one skill that proves itself: every wait that pays makes the next easier. At first it is a technique. You notice the urge to force, you do not act on it, and the unease stays a while. Then the loop that fed it goes quiet. Done often enough it sinks in. One day your body cools on its own, the way a mist comes off a river to calm a hot afternoon. Nothing has changed and everything is easier. From there you hear the rhythms you were moving too fast to notice. Winter comes in a life the way it comes in a year. It passes the same way, on its own, when it is let.

Once you stop practising it, patience is only trust. Life keeps a pattern you are inside and cannot see the whole of. Looking back, you keep finding it was there. The same slowing makes you kinder. Patience goes first and love comes in behind it.

### Siddhi — Timelessness
No one is waiting any more. The rain falls or it does not, and nobody in you is keeping count. Patience has gone along with impatience, because both of them need time to happen in. The hours no longer have any hold on you. Moods still come and go. The patterns that make up a person keep running, the way weather runs. You are still in all of it and no longer gripping any of it. This is very ordinary, quieter than the word makes it sound.

It is what patience becomes when even the one being patient lets go: the end of all fighting. Everything alive is waiting, and a life can be read as one long wait for its end. From here that is not sad, only true. The strength that stood under the sky is still there, and so is the sky. The rain is falling on the field.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 5, Fixed Patterns
You need your day to have a shape, and Gate 5 is the name for that need. It wants its meals at their hour and its sleep at its hour. It wants the same small returns every day. Discipline has nothing to do with this. It is older, from where the body keeps itself alive by doing the same things at the same times. In you it runs as comfort in the repeated thing, the walk at its time, the bed made before anything else. When your day keeps its pattern you have more of yourself for everything in it. When the pattern goes you feel it before you can say why.

From outside this gets called rigid, or afraid of anything new. What is really happening is that you are keeping a rhythm, and the rhythm is what lets the rest of your life move freely. It goes wrong from both ends, by forcing order on the day or by giving up on shape altogether. Keep your hours without apologising for them, and stop asking anyone else to keep theirs.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
You keep your hours low in the body, in the Sacral centre. It is the engine between your navel and the tops of your legs. It works all day and is spent by night. This engine decides nothing. It answers, with a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat, only to what is in front of it. Your need for a shape tunes this engine to the clock. It wants to run at the same hours and rest at the same hours. Given that, it gives you the most.

About seven people in ten have this engine running all the time. Their answer arrives before the thought does. A day with a shape feeds them, work that comes round like a tide. A day with no shape starves them, and so do somebody else's hours. In the other three the engine is open. It runs on other people. They take in someone else's pace, run at it faster than its owner, and cannot keep it up. An open engine needs a rhythm more than a fixed one does, and it will not make one by itself. What it brings instead is the pause, and the pause needs a place in the day. Every drive that sits here goes through the first excitement, the jump in, then a flat stretch where nothing moves. That stretch is where people quit, and it is usually the pause before the next level.

### What completes it — the Channel of Rhythm (5–15)
A fixed beat needs something to swing against, or it is only a clock. What completes this drive is Gate 15, in the centre of the chest. It loves the whole range of people and weathers, moving between extremes. Together they make the Channel of Rhythm, a steady tick with wild variation over it. A drummer holds one beat that way while the tune goes anywhere. Gate 15 may sit in you or in someone you live beside. Wherever the two come together, your day stops being a timetable and becomes weather with a tide under it.


## BODY

### Physiology
The sacral plexus is where this energy sits in you. The reason is that this is the part of you that can hold something until it is time. It is a weave of nerves against the inner face of the flat bone at the base of your spine. Out of it run the nerves of your legs and of your bladder and bowel. Both of them fill on a schedule you did not set. This weave carries up the news that one is full, then holds it closed until the hour suits you. The engine that works you all day lives here too. It runs down by night, fills while you sleep, and is whole before you ask.

You are doing it now. Your bladder has been filling since you last stood up, and this weave is holding it until the hour comes. Put a hand flat on the base of your spine, on the bone between your hips. The weave lies just inside there, and your hand is resting on the part of you that can wait.

### Amino acid
The stillness this energy asks of you has a substance in you, and its name is threonine. Your body cannot make it. It arrives in what you eat, in eggs or beans, or it does not arrive at all. Beyond what it builds, it sets how your cells take light in and turn it into something you can use. How much light reaches you is set by how you live: fear closes it down, an open heart draws it in.

Three other energies in you run on the same substance, and it does the same work in all four. When you are in love the hours pass differently, as though you had stepped out of time. Patience felt deep in your body does the same thing more slowly. It opens your heart, and an open heart lets the light in.

## RELATIONS

_These are the ones who hold a great deal and do not spend it early, and what you meet in each is a different way of being held._

### Pair — UL 6, Harmonious Mirage (Conflict)
This waiting holds its ground until the thing you waited on arrives, and then someone disagrees with you. Harmonious Mirage is where it goes next: the same strength a season on, walked out at last to meet the danger. There the two of you want different things, and neither wins by force. The rain that would not fall has become the argument that will not settle. Open it when your waiting ends in a disagreement.

### Programming partner — UL 35, Navigational Star
This waiting can hold a thing so long that you stop knowing whether you want it. Navigational Star is the pole set against it, the hunger that no meal ends. At their lowest the two feed each other. Wanting out of an unsafe feeling makes you impatient, and the impatience sharpens the hunger. Under both is the fear that time is running out. Open it when you cannot tell whether you are waiting for something or only wanting it.

### Codon ring — Ring of Light
This waiting is strength held under a sky that has not broken. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Light. UL 9, Ease in This, holds the same strength under a thin wind. UL 11, Sol Star, lets the ground come down to meet it. UL 26, Lighter Than a Feather, holds it under a mountain. What the four share is a trust that what is held is not lost.

### Immortals
Li Tie Guai carries this energy above. The healer left his body and told a student to guard it for seven days. On the sixth day the student burned it, so he came back into a dead beggar's body, with an iron crutch. Han Xiangzi stands below, the flute player whose tune opened peonies in the snow. Between them they say what impatience costs and what a patient tune is worth.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Aquarius:** the water bearer, who pours out what was gathered over a long while, in its own season and not before. It is the Star's own sky.
- **Hebrew letter — Tzaddi, the Hook:** a fish-hook let down into deep water and left there. The catch comes to the hook in its own time, and pulling early loses it.
