# UL 42 — Moving to Perfection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Seeing Things Through · Knowing When It Is Done · Passing On the Good · Letting Things End · Feeling All of It · Waiting for Life to Start · Celebrating the Whole Story

Growth is alive in you as a steady pull to see things through. It carries a piece of work, or a bond with someone, from the first stir to its ripe end. You feel it low in your belly as a clear yes when a thing is done. When more comes to you than you planned, you pass some of it on, and more keeps coming.

Some days you set your heart on how a thing must turn out, and by when. Whatever arrives then falls short, and you catch yourself waiting for your real life to start. Keep your hope and let the picture go. Your life is already under way.

Finish what is ready, even when it was good, because each ending clears the way for the next. You are writing your life and reading it at the same time. You feel every part of it, even the letdowns. Your life moves toward perfection one finished thing at a time.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Thunder is the hexagram of a time when your life is gaining. Thunder is the stir in you that wakes something new and sets it moving. Wind is the part of you that carries what has woken out to the people around you. The carrying sits above the stirring because a gain kept inside you stops growing. This hexagram has long been read as wind and thunder, each making the other stronger. A good idea goes further when someone passes it on, and passing it on brings you the next one. The same two ask you to move toward the good you see in others, and to mend the faults you find in yourself. What stirs in you is meant to reach past you.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is the gentle way you reach other people, a word here and a kindness there. It sits on the outside because this is the side of your gain that others meet. Wind has long been read as a breeze, and as a tree with roots and branches. Your kindness gets into every corner the way a breeze does, without pushing. You are fed, as a tree is, through roots nobody sees. On its own, this gentleness goes everywhere and settles nowhere.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the jolt inside you when something heavy finally starts to move. It lies underneath because every gain begins inside you, before anyone can see it. Thunder has long been read as a rumble rising from deep in the earth. In you it is the force that breaks a new plan out of its shell, the way a shoot breaks out of its seed. On its own, it shakes you awake and goes no further.

### Reading
This is a time of gain. Opportunities come knocking, and more reaches you than you worked for. It comes from above you, from people or a life with more than enough, letting some of it go. The time is good for something large, even a crossing you would not risk in a leaner year. It will not stay open for long.

What it asks is that the gain keep moving. What you receive, give on, because giving is the only way the increase lasts. A full cup has to be poured before it can be filled again. Move toward the good in others as quickly as wind moves. Mend your own faults with the nerve of thunder. Kept for yourself alone, the gain turns against you.

Your feet and legs do the work now. The feet take the first step, and the thighs bring the rest of your body after them. Stand while the water is high, and cross.

**Line 3** · _image:_ gain out of misfortune, and a message sent under seal. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
Trouble comes, and it turns out to be how you gain. Let it polish you, and there is no blame in it. Stay honest and keep to the middle way. Tell the one above you plainly what happened, the way an officer shows the seal of their rank. Believe the trouble can serve you, and it stops being trouble.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the go-between in the middle of the road, trusted enough to move a city. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
You stand in the middle, carrying word between the one above and the people below. Walk that middle road, and your advice is followed. You could even be trusted to help move a whole city. You have no power of your own here, so lean on someone who has. Aim at everyone's good, and people will follow you.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a kind heart that has no need to ask. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Expectation · _Gift:_ Detachment · _Siddhi:_ Celebration

### Shadow — Expectation
Almost everyone is waiting for the day their life comes right. The money will be sorted, love will be in place, and there will be room to live at last. First there are a few things to clear up. So the living is put off, year after year, until you are old. It is never too late to be here now. It takes seeing through what you were waiting for, once and completely.

The trouble starts when the mind takes hold of hope and gives it a date and a shape. Whatever arrives is measured against that shape, and it falls short. Hopeful or gloomy, a fixed outcome narrows your eyes until you miss the good in front of you. Under every other ending sits the one nobody escapes. Your body is made to die, and your unease about time running out comes from there. Many disappointments turn out, later, to be blessings.

### Repressive nature — Grasping
Held in, the fear of change becomes a refusal to let anything end. They try to stay young and hold their loved ones back from their own roads. They cling to the past and call it love. What is never allowed to end cannot renew itself, and slowly they wither.

### Reactive nature — Flaky
Let out, the same fear keeps them from finishing anything. They avoid disappointment by never quite committing, and move on before a thing can end. A cycle broken early has to be lived again in a new form. So the same pattern keeps coming back, in love and in money.

### Gift — Detachment
Detachment means feeling more, not less. No fixed outcome squeezes what happens, so you feel all of it. You can even enjoy being disappointed. It cannot be forced. Hold a feeling down by will, and the grip only tightens. It comes when you give way to every feeling fully, without always acting on it.

From there your life reads like a book. You write it as you go and read it too, absorbed but never swept away by the words. You let go of each plan as soon as life changes it, and you can see where you still want to steer. You keep your hope and drop your demands. An obstacle starts to look like a chance to grow. You find this by giving in to being ordinary and human, breathing deeply and not flinching from what hurts.

### Siddhi — Celebration
Celebration comes when the story ends in a laugh. In detachment you still watched your life from a little way off. Now the one watching and what is watched are the same, so there is only one thing happening. Every cell in you starts dying the moment it is born. You are less a fixed thing than a passage life moves through. Somewhere deep in you, something gets the joke. The whole serious search was the set-up, and you laugh because you let yourself be tricked.

Sit by a river and follow its currents with your eyes. Then stop following and simply gaze. The river loses its direction and seems to flow at you and out of you from everywhere. No practice brings this on. Whoever it happens to wants only to share the laugh.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 42, Growth
Gate 42 is the drive to finish what has been started. It wants to see a cycle through its beginning, its middle and its end. It can stay with a piece of work or a person until it has ripened, and it knows real growth takes time. From outside, that can look like patience or stubbornness. Its gift is knowing how to wrap a thing up so there is room for what comes next.

Its trouble runs two ways. It can hold on to something long after it is over, because finishing was the whole point. Or it feels it should start everything itself, and a pile of half-done things grows too heavy to look at. Then it rushes to close them early, just to be rid of the pressure. It works best when it is led to what is ready to finish.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The Sacral Center sits low in the body, under the navel and above the legs. It is the strongest engine you have, and it answers rather than decides. Something is put in front of you and a sound rises, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat. Through gate 42 that engine turns toward the last stretch of a job. It has a knack for coming into the middle of something and carrying it home. Finished on a yes, the work gives back its energy. Finished to please or to fit in, it leaves a habit of resistance, and you blame yourself for it.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined, and its energy runs all the time. For them every drive here goes through the first excitement, the jump in, then a flat stretch where nothing seems to move. That flat stretch is where people quit. Whether you are finished or only resting there is a question your gut can answer with a yes or a no. The other three in ten have it open and run on the energy of the people around them. They are the pause between the notes, and they can tell when a thing is done.

### What completes it — the Channel of Maturation (42–53)
Finishing needs something begun. Gate 53, in the Root Center at the base of the spine, is the pressure to start new things. Together they make the Channel of Maturation, one whole cycle from the first step to the last. It carries a sense of timing, when to begin and when to close. Rushed, it leaves things half-done. Held too long, it keeps what is over. Gate 53 may be in you or in someone close, and nothing ripens without both.

## BODY

### Physiology
Finishing is the reason this energy is given to the sacral plexus. It is a weave of nerves low in your pelvis, part of the engine that keeps a body going without being watched. The low back around it has long been held as the body's store, where you keep what you carry until its season ends. In you this weave is where a movement is carried to its close.

Stand up from your chair. Your legs begin it. The last of the rise, straightening at the hip, is done by the largest muscle you have. That muscle takes its order from this weave. You have finished that movement thousands of times without knowing where the finish was made.

### Amino acid
The growth in you has a substance, and it is leucine. Your body cannot make it, so it comes in with fish, milk or lentils. The muscle you move with breaks it down, where most of what you eat is handled by the liver.

This energy shares that substance with five others in its family. Together they hold the whole run of every living cell, from its first innocence to what it has learned by the end. This energy is where that run closes. What was alive in the cell steps out of the shape it wore, the way you let go of someone who has died.

## RELATIONS

This energy grows by passing on what it gains, and it runs out when the good stretch ends. Beginning and the End is where it goes next: the time of less, when something is given up so that what matters can grow. Open it when the gain has stopped and you have to let something go. Less is where the next growth begins.

### Inverse — UL 41, Beginning and the End (Decrease)
Turn this energy end for end and gain becomes loss. Beginning and the End is the same flow seen from the hand that gives, where this one is the cup that fills. Open it when you are the one with more than enough.

This energy lets things end, and it loses its way when an ending feels like a failure. Art of Living holds what it lacks: a sense of what will last, and a fear of failing that the two share. Once you judge your life by success and failure, you are already caught. Open it when you are measuring a finished thing by whether it worked.

### Codon ring — Ring of Life and Death
This energy closes things, and every close leaves room for a start. It belongs to a family of six, the Ring of Life and Death, which holds one whole life. When you are starting over, open UL 3, Messengers of the Infinite. When you need to be here now, open UL 20, Emerging as the Code. For clarity, open UL 23, Beneath the Surface. To go back to where a thing began, open UL 24, Frequency Flutter. To look after someone, open UL 27, Inner Majesty.

- **Wind (upper) — X · Wheel of Fortune** (Turning Point): The Wheel turns up and down, and your good stretch is one turn of it.
- **Wind (upper) — XIII · Death** (Metamorphosis): Death stands here a second time, because what you pass on is also something you let go.
- **Thunder (lower) — XVI · The Tower** (Shock, Upheaval): The Tower breaks an old structure open, the jolt that starts your next gain.
- **Thunder (lower) — XX · Judgement** (Salvation, Renewal): Judgement wakes what was buried, the new life that stirs in you after an ending.

### Immortals
Wind above carries He Xian Gu, the one woman among the Eight Immortals, who holds a lotus. Her lotus grows clean out of mud, as your gain can grow out of trouble. Thunder below carries Cao Guo Jiu, who left the riches of the court he was born into. He gave away what he had, and you grow the same way when you pass your gain on.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Scorpio:** the water sign of endings and rebirth, where a thing has to end before it comes back new in your life.
- **Hebrew letter — Nun, the Fish:** a fish lives its whole life in moving water, as you can live through every change without losing yourself.
