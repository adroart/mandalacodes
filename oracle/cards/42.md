# UL 42 — Moving to Perfection

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Finishing Things · More Than You Were Holding · Passing It On · Waiting for the Better Day · Letting Things End · Inside Your Own Story · The Laugh of Relief

Increase is the energy here, more poured into you than you were holding, until the level rises and runs over toward whatever stands lower than you. It comes from outside, the way a good year comes to a field, and it has a second half that is easy to miss: it goes on coming only while it is passed on. It is also the part of you that knows how a thing wants to end, so that what is finished can be set down to make room.

Carried well, this energy trusts the shape of a life more than the plan for it. Hope is the growing force in you, the wish that things be better than they are; it turns into disappointment only when the mind takes hold of it and sets a date on what must arrive. Let the date go and keep the hope. Give as you gain, without waiting until you have enough, because the giving is what keeps the level rising. What you seek for yourself is on the right road when it turns out to be good for everyone near you as well.

The way through is to let things end. Every seven years or so you are standing in a body most of which has been replaced, cell by cell, without your noticing; something in you had to die each time for the new to have a place, and a life closes its cycles the same way. There will be a stretch when you catch yourself waiting for things to come right before the real living starts, and the real living is the waiting. See through that once, all the way down, and what you were saving for later is here, ready to be finished.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Thunder: the shock that starts things below, and above it the wind that carries what was started across the whole field. The upper sign is the tree as much as the wind, so wood grows here too. The old character is water poured into a vessel until it brims and runs over; the word means to add, to enrich, more than enough.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Wind is what enters everywhere and forces nothing. It goes into every crack, it carries seed from one field to the next, and where it has been things stay bent. The same sign is the tree, the root that goes down softly and holds; in a family it is the eldest daughter, who gets things done without being seen to. In you it is the part that spreads what it has to where it is needed. Set over the thunder it is the carrying: what is stirred below is taken outward, so the increase becomes everyone's. Its risk is thinning, the good spread so far that nothing lands anywhere.

### Lower trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Under the wind is Thunder, the first crack of spring coming up out of the earth. It is the jolt that starts a season, the seed splitting, the eldest son out of the door before the rest of the house is awake. In you it is the stir at the root, the surge that begins the increase and gives the wind something to carry. Set under the wind it is the start: nothing spreads that was not first shaken loose here. Its risk is a shock with nowhere to go, a start with nothing above it to carry it on, which burns out in the ground it began in.

### Reading
More is coming in than you were holding, and it is coming from above you, from the one with more than enough loosening their grip so that the level downstream rises. That is the shape of this time: what is full pours toward what is empty, and for once you are on the empty side. It was not asked for and it cannot be refused. It is a season, and a season does not stay; the time to undertake the large thing is now, while the water is high enough to cross.

What it asks is that the gain keep moving. Wind and thunder feed each other: the thunder shakes something loose, the wind carries it across the field; what one starts the other spreads. So the instruction is small and daily. When you see something good in another, move toward it, as quickly as wind moves. When you find a fault in yourself, mend it, with the nerve thunder has. Kept for yourself, the increase stops, and what stops is set upon from every side. Passed on, it goes on coming.

In the body this energy is the legs: the feet that take the first step, and the thighs that carry the rest of you after them. Notice where you are held now, whether you are sitting on what has come or already standing. Stand, let the weight settle into the thighs, and take the crossing while the water carries you.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a great work begun from the lowest place, with help pouring in from above. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
At the very start of the increase, more comes down to you than a low place would usually draw. It is the moment to throw the whole of your strength into something large, larger than a beginning has any right to attempt, because what came was not for you alone. Only if it comes off is there no blame. Begin big, and keep the aim out past your own gain.

**Line 2** · _image:_ a gift too large to refuse, and the king carrying it up to be offered. → becomes Hexagram 61, Inner Truth.
Something arrives that cannot be refused, worth ten pairs of tortoise shells, from outside and never asked for, which is why it came. Hold steady under it, because a windfall tests you harder than a loss does. A king given this much does not keep it; he carries it up to the highest and offers it. Take it with an open hand, and lift it higher than yourself.

**Line 3** · _image:_ gain out of misfortune, and a message sent under seal. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
The gain here comes through hard and unwanted events, the kind you would never have chosen. Let them polish you rather than sour you, and there is no blame in it. Stay honest, walk the middle of the road, and say plainly to the one above you what has happened, the way a messenger shows his seal. Believe that the trouble is feeding you, and it stops being trouble.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the go-between in the middle of the road, trusted enough to move a city. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
You stand in the middle, between the one who gives and the people it is for, carrying word both ways. Walk that middle without leaning and the one above you follows your counsel, far enough to lean on you for the largest move there is, moving a whole city. You have no power of your own here; borrow it. The trust holds because the aim is everyone's good.

**Line 5** · _image:_ a kind heart that has no need to ask. → becomes Hexagram 27, Nourishment.
You already have the answer, so this line answers you as a friend would: if the heart behind the question is kind, there is nothing to ask. Good done from a heart like that costs nothing, reaches everyone below, and comes back as good. It sees others and itself as one family; every word from it helps. Stop asking whether it is right, and do it.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the one at the top who adds to nobody, and the blow that comes from outside. → becomes Hexagram 3, Difficulty at the Beginning.
At the top, the one who should be pouring down keeps it for himself, and the flow turns round. Nobody adds to him now, and the blow, when it comes, comes from a direction he was not watching. A heart that keeps no steady rule cannot hold what it gathered. Put your own house in order before you set out to help anyone, or the help does harm.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Expectation · _Gift:_ Detachment · _Siddhi:_ Celebration

### Shadow — Expectation
Nearly everyone is waiting for the part of the story where it comes right. The money sorted, the love in place, room at last to breathe, but first a few things to clear up; so the living is put off, and put off, until the years have gone. Hope is not the trouble. Hope is the growing force in you, the wish that things be better than they are; it stays clean until the mind takes hold of it, giving it a date and a shape. Then whatever arrives is measured against the shape, falls short, and the disappointment becomes a way of seeing.

Underneath sits the one expectation nobody escapes, the ending of the body, written into every cell you have. The unease you feel about time running out comes from there. Bright or grim, an expectation narrows your eyes to the one outcome you are braced for, so that the good in front of you goes unseen and what this moment could hold is closed down. It is never too late to be in the now; it takes seeing through what you were waiting for, once, with nothing held back.

### Repressive nature — Grasping
This person will not let anything end. They keep everything the way it was, stay young by force, hold the ones they love back from their own roads, and call it devotion. What is not allowed to die cannot be renewed. Slowly they wither inside the grip.

### Reactive nature — Flaky
Here is someone who never finishes. They leave before the ending can disappoint them, move on, promise nothing, and take it for freedom. A cycle broken before its end has to be begun again in a new form, so the same pattern keeps returning, in love and in money, wearing another face.

### Gift — Detachment
Detachment means feeling more, not less. The word sounds like arms folded at a distance from your own life; the thing itself is the reverse, because you have stopped letting an expectation clamp down on what happens before it can land, and you can even enjoy being disappointed. It cannot be forced. Hold a feeling down by discipline and the attachment thickens, which is how the strictest fall hardest; the real thing comes by yielding, letting every feeling rise all the way, not always acted on, never refused.

From there your life reads like a story. You are inside it, writing it as you go, and you are its reader too, absorbed, turning the pages, never lost in the words. Let go of each expectation as quickly as life overturns it, or the waters of regret close over your head. You can see the places where you still want to steer, and you can let the plot change. What looks like distance is trust in life deep enough to stop bracing, and it is found by giving in to your own ordinary humanity, breathing the air, not flinching from what hurts.

### Siddhi — Celebration
The story ends here, and what is left is laughter. Being detached still kept a seam between the watcher and the watched; now the watcher has become the watched, so only one thing is happening. Everything is still felt as dying, every cell in you dying from the moment it was made, so that you were never a thing so much as a passage, matter turning to energy on its way through. Somewhere below thought, something gets the joke. The whole serious quest was the set-up, and you laugh because you let yourself be tricked.

Sit by a river following the currents with your eyes, then stop following and look through them: the river loses its direction, coming at you from everywhere at once. Birth and death stop being two events. Nothing brings this on, no practice earns it, so there is nothing left to do but celebrate, and whoever it has happened to wants only to share the laugh.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 42, Growth
Carrying a thing to its close is the pressure here. Gate 42 is where it enters: the drive to see a cycle through, to stay with a piece of work, or with someone, until it has ripened, and then to know the hour it is done. It is the pull that feels a loose end as a weight on the room, the appetite for the harvest rather than the planting. Time is on its side; a thing that needs years is given its years.

From outside it looks like patience, or stubbornness. The cost runs two ways. Held past the end, it clings to what is over because finishing was the whole point, and forgets that some growth is knowing when to let go. Turned into a demand to start everything as well, it fills a life with half-done things and a quiet judgement about them, or it rushes a close to be rid of the weight before the thing was ready. People who carry it all their lives learn to be led to what is ready to finish, and that the finishing makes room, which was what it was for.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The finishing is done low in the body, in the Sacral centre, between the navel and the tops of the legs, the engine that works all day. It does not decide when a thing is done; it answers, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat, when the question is put in front of it. Through this pressure the engine is turned toward the last stretch of a thing, with a knack for coming into the middle of another's work and carrying it home. Finished on a yes, the work gives back the energy it took. Finished to please, to fit in, to be good, it leaves a pattern of resistance that takes years to break, and you blame yourself for it.

In about seven people in ten this engine runs all the time. For someone like that every drive seated here passes through the first excitement, the jump in, then the flat stretch where nothing moves, where they quit. Whether a thing is finished or only paused is a question the engine can answer, asked as a yes or a no, and the answer is usually slower than the mind wants. In the other three it is open, running on whoever is near; for someone like that the pull to finish is borrowed, and what is their own is the pause between the notes, the knowing that a thing is complete, which a fixed engine cannot feel.

### What completes it — the Channel of Maturation (42–53)
Finishing has to be given something begun. Its other half is Gate 53, at the base of the trunk, the pressure to start things, to light the first fire without much thought for how it ends. The two make the Channel of Maturation, a whole cycle held in one circuit from the first push to the last stroke, with a sense of timing that knows both hours. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, nothing ripens that was not both begun and brought home.

## BODY

### Physiology
The sacral plexus is where the body keeps the finishing in you, and the reason is carrying through. The weave sits low, on the inner face of the bone at the base of your spine, and it belongs to the engine that keeps going: what you begin with your whole body is brought home from here. The same low ground is where your body stores what it must keep through a winter, holding a thing until its time is done. In you it is the part that stays with a thing when the interest has gone and the end is still a way off.

Stand up from your chair. Your legs begin it, and the last of the rise, the straightening at the hip that brings you all the way upright, is done by the largest muscle you have, on an order from this weave. You have finished that movement thousands of times without knowing where the finish was made. Sit down again and you are sitting on the part of you that completes the rise.

### Amino acid
The carrying through in you has a substance, and the substance is leucine. One of the nine your body cannot make, it comes in with fish, milk or lentils, or it does not come at all. The muscle you move with takes it apart, not the liver where nearly everything else you eat is dealt with; the muscle that builds with it unbuilds it. Everything alive in you runs one course, from a beginning that knows nothing to an end that has learned, and this substance is in you at both ends.

Six energies in you share it. Nothing in you is kept: each cell is taken apart, its substance built into the next, and what leaves at the end is what you were, stepping out of the shape it wore. Your body has practised that letting go in every cell since the day you were born.
