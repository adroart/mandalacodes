# UL 34 — Sublime Power

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Strength Without Strain · Answering What Is Real · Waiting to Be Called · Taking the Easy Way · Strong Without Knowing It · Pushing Too Hard · Busy but Powerless

You feel strong, and something real in front of you is asking for all of it. That is when strength moves through your whole body. You can work hard all day with nothing in you pulling against the work. The work flows out of you, and you become one with it.

Some days there is nothing real to meet, and the strength goes looking for something to push. You lean on things that will not move and want them to go your way. Listen for the word try in your own voice, because it comes just as the pushing starts. Once you hear it, stop, and the strain goes out of your body.

Let the strength rest until something real calls it. You will know the call, because the strength rises on its own, with no talking yourself into it. Take the way that feels easy, since the easy way is the right one. Then all of you goes in, and the whole of life is behind you.

## ICHING

### Combination
Thunder over Heaven is the hexagram of strength at its full height. Heaven is the steady strength inside you. Thunder is the urge in you to move, and to move now. The urge sits on top because once your strength is full, it wants to be used. This hexagram has long been read as thunder high in the sky. When your strength is this full, people feel it the way they feel thunder overhead. The same reading asks you to walk no road that is not right. You are strongest when your strength goes only where it should.

### Upper trigram — Thunder (Zhen)
Thunder is the jolt that gets you out of your chair when something stirs you. It sits on the outside because it is the part of your strength other people see. Thunder has long been read as a sound rising out of the ground, the one that breaks a bud open. The same jolt starts the first move in any heavy job. On its own, it only alarms the people near you.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is what carries you to the end of a long job. It lies underneath because it is your inner store, the part nobody sees until you act. Heaven has long been read as the sky, where the sun keeps the same path day after day. Your stamina keeps that same steady pace through a long piece of work. On its own, it burns on until it dries you out.

### Reading
Your strength has grown to its full height. The time of holding back is over, and you are ready to act on what is worth it. There is more power in you now than in anything around you. This is also where the danger lies. People with this much strength grow too sure of themselves and start to run wild. Great power draws envy from those who have less.

Use only as much strength as the moment needs. Never strike before you can strike at full strength, and never out of pride. Be fair with what you have, because only the weak are merciless. Strength spent on a cause nobody else supports is wasted. Put it behind what is right, and good people will stand with you.

Thunder belongs to the foot, the first part of you to move. Stand with your weight even over both feet. Let the strength settle there before you take a step.

### Judgement
- Great power.
- It goes well for the one who stays firm and upright.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ strength in the toes, and the toes already marching. → becomes Hexagram 32, Duration.
Strength has reached your toes, and they want to march. The power is real but not gathered yet, so setting out now would end badly. Find someone who knows more, and learn from them first. Stay where you are until the rest of you catches up.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the strong one at rest in the middle, with no fence in sight. → becomes Hexagram 55, Abundance.
You are strong and settled in the middle, with nothing to prove. You are firm inside and easy on the outside, with more in reserve than you show. Strong here means no passing feeling can throw you. Keep that balance, and it goes well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a ram charging the fence, its horns caught in the wood. → becomes Hexagram 54, The Marrying Maiden.
A ram charges the fence, and its horns get caught. Small people spend all their strength where it can be seen, and the wise spend none. Pushing on here would hurt you before it won you anything. Keep your strength out of sight, and stay away from the fence.

**Line 4** · _image:_ the fence opened, and strength in the axle of the great cart. → becomes Hexagram 11, Peace.
The fence opens on its own, and nothing catches. Your strength has moved from the horns to the axle, the hidden bar that turns the wheels while everyone looks at the cart. Nothing needs forcing now. Set yourself right first, then help others. Be the axle, and go.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the ram lost in easy country, and no regret. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
The ram wanders off in easy country, and you do not miss it. You have lost the butting kind of strength, and there is no fault in that. Open your mind to what other people know. Borrow what they have learned, and you grow strong in a new way.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the ram caught in the fence, unable to go back or forward. → becomes Hexagram 14, Great Possession.
The ram is caught in the fence and can go neither back nor forward. Nothing you push from here will help. See plainly how stuck you are, since seeing it is the way out. Admit the difficulty, and the fault will not last long.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Force · _Gift:_ Strength · _Siddhi:_ Majesty

### Shadow — Force
Force is the oldest power in you. It is older than any animal, and it has one aim, to keep one life going. It does not even know that anyone else is there. Caught in it, you keep going the way you set out, and help from outside falls on deaf ears. Other people cannot see how you could be so blind. Their anger takes you by surprise, because you had no idea what you were doing.

Your spine feels it first. Each time you push your life where it does not want to go, your back tightens somewhere and you begin to stoop. Go to the ache and stay with it, instead of fixing it from outside. It is there because of something you are doing or not doing. The longer you listen, the plainer it says where your life wants to go.

### Repressive nature — Self-Effacing
Here the power hides from itself. These people put themselves down and let others be their authority, often after a hard childhood. Everyone walks over them. All that strength backs up inside and comes out as tiredness. When they leave the situation that holds them, their strength comes back.

### Reactive nature — Bullish
Turned outward, the same power becomes bossy. These people push others around and have no idea they are doing it. They are furious when challenged and charge on when warned. It ends badly for them. They were taught to be this way and are deeply misunderstood. What they need is one healthy, hard thing to put their anger into.

### Gift — Strength
Strength has always wanted to be seen. A flower opens its colour to bring the bee, and there is no pride in it. Your strength shows in the same way, and people notice it without being told. Someone who moves and speaks from their centre has a strength you can feel across a room.

You may do a brave thing without deciding to, then feel uneasy when people praise you for it. It seems to have happened through you. Your movement is at its finest once you stop watching yourself. Often this strength means not moving at all. The easiest fight is the one you never start. Real strength is not aware of itself.

### Siddhi — Majesty
Majesty is life moving through a body with no one in there taking charge. On a crowded train, an old man calms a raging drunk by asking about his garden, while a trained fighter sits ready to strike. At this height you settle a fight the way that old man did, without lifting a hand.

Majesty is hidden and humble, and it always finds the easiest way. A god living in a human body would not know it is one. Then the whole of life moves through you, and you are joy in a body.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 34, Power
Gate 34 is the pressure to put your great store of energy to use. It can keep you busier than anyone around you. It gives you the stamina to keep going long after others stop. It is built to answer, not to start. When something real comes to you and your yes rises, the energy brings the right people together around one idea, and what you start lasts. When nothing has come, you stay busy to feel in control. All that work leaves you feeling powerless. The pressure asks you to carry the power rather than wield it.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The Sacral Center sits low in your body, under the navel and above the legs. It is the strongest engine you have. It drives your work, your sex and the raising of a child. It does not decide. It answers with a sound that rises before you think, a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat.

About seven people in ten have this centre defined. Their engine runs all the time and renews by being used on work they said yes to. Work without a yes burns it out, and so does doing nothing. The other three in ten have it open and take in the energy of the people around them. They can outwork everyone for a while but cannot keep it up. Their gift is knowing when enough is enough.

### What completes it — the Integration cluster (10, 20, 57)
Gate 34 reaches three ways at once. With Gate 20 in the throat it makes the Channel of Charisma, where your strength gets a voice and your words turn into action. With Gate 10 in the G Center it makes the Channel of Exploration, where it becomes behaviour that is yours alone. With Gate 57 in the Spleen it makes the Channel of Power, where it moves on instinct in the moment. The other gate may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
This strength is given to the sacral plexus, and the reason is that nothing in you is watched less. The plexus is a weave of nerves against the inside of the flat bone at the base of your spine. It sits low, below and around the level of your navel. Strength comes from here, from the belly, while pushing starts in the head. Its power is real because no thought sits on it.

Stand up from a chair. The big muscle of your buttock straightened your hip on an order from this weave, before you had finished deciding to stand. The muscles that kept your hips level took their orders from it too. You felt none of it, and that is how this strength works.

### Amino acid
The strength in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is asparagine. Your body makes it for itself from what it already has, so it asks nothing of your plate. The proteins on the outside of every cell you have wear their coats of sugar on it.

When the strength takes you over and you can hear no one, this chemistry is part of what is moving. Left alone, your life runs smoothly and costs you little. Each push against the way it wants to go uses more of your energy than the flow would have. You share this chemistry with one other energy.

## RELATIONS

_UL 33 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the strength drawn back, where this one moves it forward._

### Pair — UL 33, Echos of Time (Retreat)
This strength moves forward with everything it has, and it runs out when there is nothing left to move toward. Echos of Time is where it goes next: the step back, where you withdraw and let what happened make sense. Open it when your strength is spent and going on would only hurt you. Every great push is followed by a stop.

### Inverse — UL 33, Echos of Time (Retreat)
Turn this energy end for end and going forward becomes stepping back. Echos of Time is the same strength seen from the other side, gathered by withdrawing. Open it when you cannot tell whether to go or wait. The retreat is what built the strength in the first place.

### Programming partner — UL 20, Emerging as the Code
This strength works best when you are not watching yourself use it. Emerging as the Code holds what it is missing: being fully here, sure of yourself in the moment. Lose that sureness and you start to try. Open it when you catch yourself trying and your strength has turned to strain.

### Codon ring — Ring of Destiny
This strength is the power of the body, and it has already done its greatest work by standing the human spine upright. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of Destiny, which shapes where a life goes. UL 43, Cipher of Knowledge, carries the sudden knowing that breaks through your mind. This one carries the strength of the body. Together they are two halves of one destiny.
