# UL 6 — Harmonious Mirage

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Close Enough to Hurt · Guarding Yourself · Letting the Heat Drop · Saying the True Thing · Tactful Without Trying · Staying in the Room · Peace Underneath

Friction rises between two people the moment they come close. In you it is the pull toward someone and the flinch away from them, and both start in the same place. The flinch is no verdict on the person in front of you. It is the old fear that you can be hurt at all, and it wakes whenever someone comes near enough to matter. Every quarrel you have had began there, before anyone spoke.

You learn to say the true thing at the right hour, and that is what the heat is for. A true thing said too soon is a stone thrown. Let the heat drop first and the same words go all the way in. You are not fearless. You have stopped being afraid of the fear itself, so it no longer decides for you, and nobody can use it to move you. From outside it looks like calm. Inside you are frightened, and you stay in the room.

The fear is older than you, carried in the blood from people you never met. Whoever stands closest only wakes it. Feel the fear in yourself rather than in them, and it loosens. Then the wall you built against them comes down on its own, because nothing behind it is in danger. The people you have fought hardest are the ones who bring you nearest to peace.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Water is the hexagram of a quarrel. Heaven is the part of you that knows it is right and has stopped listening. Water is the part that feels wronged and keeps turning it over. The certainty sits above the hurt because being sure stops you hearing. That is why this hexagram has long been read as two natures moving apart. Two people can share a room for an hour and come out further apart. The old name sets speech beside the public square: a quarrel is what you kept in, said out loud. You are right, and being right is carrying you away from the person.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Your strength here goes upward, away from whoever stands under you. You have the case and the standing, and nothing in either will bend. It sits on top because whatever has risen no longer answers to what is below it. In a family this is the father whose word is final, who does not argue and leaves the room. Heaven has long been read as the sky going up and away. On its own it wins every point and stands there with nobody.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Your hurt goes downward and finds the lowest place in you to sit. The hurt is honest, and nobody can argue you out of it. It lies underneath because the hurt came first, and the being right was built on top of it. In a family this is the middle son, in the right and never heard. Water has long been read as the deep place, where the ground gives way. On its own it sinks into the pit, until the bottom is where you live.

### Reading
You are in a dispute, your case is honest, and the other side has stopped listening. Your truth is real and the door is closed. Nothing you say now will open it, because the two of you are moving in opposite directions. Every push sends you further apart. Nobody comes out of the far end of a quarrel still holding what they wanted. A victory won here is taken back before long. So stop halfway. The middle of a quarrel is the only place in it where anything settles well.

What it asks for is a fair third person, someone with no stake on either side. Let that person say plainly what is being fought over, and it stops being about pride. Leave the great crossings alone for now, the move, the new undertaking. Both of you are strong and pulling opposite ways, so nothing large will hold. Every dispute sits inside a relationship, and you will still live beside this person when it is over. Hold firm on the inside. Give way on the outside, the way water does. Giving here is how the peace is kept, and it is no surrender.

The body's part in this is the ear, hollow on the outside so that something can get in. Check yourself now: are you talking, or are you listening? Sit the way water lies, low and open, and let what is said come all the way in before you answer.

### Judgement
- Conflict. You are sincere, and you are blocked.
- Halt halfway, with care, and it goes well. Carry it to the end, and it goes badly.
- It helps to see the great person. It does not help to cross the great water.

### Image
- Heaven and water go opposite ways: that is conflict.
- So the wise, in everything they undertake, consider the beginning with care.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ not carrying the matter on; a little talk, then a good end. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
The quarrel has barely started and you are the weaker of the two. There will be a little talk about you, a few words behind your back, and that is the whole price. What is true and what is false will sort itself out without your help. Let it drop now, while dropping it costs you almost nothing.

**Line 2** · _image:_ not up to the contest; going home to a town of three hundred houses. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
The other side is stronger, and you cannot win this. Press it and the trouble comes down on everyone who stands with you. Go back to the small town you belong to, three hundred houses and nobody looking for you. Trouble passes over a house that does not rise to meet it. Make yourself small, and be safe.

**Line 3** · _image:_ living on what was earned before; serving a king and claiming no merit. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
You are living on what you earned earlier, and you are not strong enough to contend for more. Stay inside the portion that is already yours, and reach for no new ground while the dispute runs. If a greater person's work wants your hands, give them and let the credit be theirs. Hold to that, and it ends well.

**Line 4** · _image:_ not up to the contest; turning back, a change of heart, resting in what is right. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
You could fight this, because the strength is in you, and you would still not win. Turn back instead, toward the work that is yours, and let your mind change about the whole matter. A heart that has changed can rest. Rest in what you know is right, and peace comes of it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the dispute brought before the one in the centre. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
Here the matter comes before someone in the centre, fair, wanting nothing for themselves. That is the one place in the whole business where a case can be pressed. If that seat is yours, hear both sides and settle it. If it is not, this is the person to take the matter to. Contend here, and it goes very well.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a leather belt given in honour, taken back three times before the morning is out. → becomes Hexagram 47, Oppression.
You carried the quarrel all the way to the end, you won it, and the prize is in your hands. Watch how fast it leaves your hands, three times before the morning is out. What is taken by force goes back by force, and the respect you wanted is not in the prize. Come down now, while there is still something of yourself to bring.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Conflict · _Gift:_ Diplomacy · _Siddhi:_ Peace

### Shadow — Conflict
A feeling gets big enough and you stop having it, because it has you. From inside it the other person looks like the danger. That is where every quarrel starts: two people who have both stopped being anything but what they feel. Underneath sits one fear, older than any argument: that someone close can hurt you or take something from you. So you guard. Your chest hardens, and you keep a small distance going without deciding to. You learned that guard early, somewhere between seven and fourteen. Most of a life's strength goes into holding it up, and the cost never shows.

Think of the hardest relationship in your life now. What that person seems to hold over you is a part of yourself you would rather not meet. They are the only one who can bring it up. The fear is in you, not in them. Ask what the quarrel is really about, and the answer is nearly always the part of you they bring out. The people who will not leave you alone are carrying something you have not let yourself feel yet. That is why they stay.

### Repressive nature — Over-Attentive
This person keeps the peace whatever the price, and they pay it. They read a room and soften to fit it, agreeing sooner than they mean. Nobody near them trusts the agreement. What gets swept under lies there until it blows. Faced at last, a quarrel is smaller than they feared.

### Reactive nature — Tactless
The feeling goes out hot to whoever is standing closest, before it has been held for a minute. Then this person walks off before it lands. They cannot hear their own tactlessness, so the fault belongs to the other one. The only person who stays is the one who will bear anything.

### Gift — Diplomacy
You are tactful without trying, once the guard is down. This has nothing to do with the careful phrase, which anyone can learn and a shut heart can deliver perfectly. You feel in your body where the other person is, how open they are, how near their edge. A quarrel reaches you before it has taken a shape, and one word turned the right way lets it pass. When someone comes at you hot, you take the heat in and hand it back with nothing added, so the heat has nowhere to go. Refuse the game of blame and the other person is holding their own share, which either changes them or moves them out of your life.

Falling in love is the same motion: two natures rubbing until nothing stands between them. The people you are meant to open keep arriving, year after year, and each opening makes the next one easier. Being this open is the strength itself. Your heart is worn thin where anyone can get at it, and that is what lets you get at theirs.

### Siddhi — Peace
You have nothing to defend, because there is no one inside to be attacked. You used to work at keeping things even between you and everyone else. There is nothing to keep even now, and the work has stopped by itself. An old man rowing across a river was struck by another boat and turned to shout. The boat was empty and drifting, and the shout died in him. Live as that empty boat and nothing arrives as an attack. This peace lies under everything, all the time. Stop what you are doing and listen for it in the birds and in the wind dropping. Your own skin stops being a border, and you feel life moving in everything that lives. Nobody can take your life from you, because you are life.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 6, Friction
Something in you presses toward other people, and the rub is how you arrive. Gate 6 is the name for it. The wanting to be near a person and the fear of being seen arrive together, as one pressure. When two people warm to each other a heat comes up that does not feel like welcome. Most take that snag as proof the fit is wrong. It is the place where two feeling natures touch, and there is no closeness that skips it. The heat comes with the closeness itself, quick in some people and a slow simmer in others. Smooth it away and what remains is politeness with nothing under it.

From outside you look like someone who starts things. What happens is that you feel the edge before anyone else and cannot pretend it is not there. The cost is being called difficult by people who wanted the warmth without the rub. Live with this long enough and you find the rub was the way in. Where there is no friction there is no closeness either.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
You feel the rub low in the belly, in the Emotional Solar Plexus centre, where feeling lives. This centre gives you your energy and tells you what is going on, so a feeling here does two jobs at once. It moves in a wave that rises to hope and falls all the way to pain before it turns, over hours or days. There is no truth in the moment here, only in the whole wave. A verdict reached while the heat is up comes out the wrong shape. Wait it out and the same feeling tells you something you can use.

A little more than half of all people have this wave running of their own accord, with nobody else in the room. For them feeling is the water they swim in, and the lifetime's work is waiting for the wave to settle. Their upsets pile up quietly until one broken promise brings the whole lot down. The rest have no wave of their own. Other people's feelings come into them and get louder. They end up voicing a room's anger as if it were their own. Agreeableness is the wall they put up against it. On their own they settle into a steadiness that people with a wave of their own never get. Whichever you are, a feeling let pass lasts a few minutes, and a hand on your shoulder settles the whole of you.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mating (6–59)
On its own the rub has nowhere to go. What completes it is Gate 59, low in the belly, the drive to break through whatever stands between two bodies. Joined, they make the Channel of Mating. With both, the wall between two people comes down and closeness follows, in a bed or in a business built together. Gate 59 may sit in you or in someone close to you, and the channel runs only when both gates are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The belly knows first, and that is why this energy is given to the net of nerves over your gut. A feeling of yours is there before it is a thought. Your gut has a nerve system of its own, with more cells in it than your spine has. The great nerve running up to your brain carries news upward more than orders down, so the belly reports before the head decides. Under threat these nerves slow the gut and pull blood toward the muscles. That is the drop or the knot you feel in an argument before a word is said. It passes, and it is not a verdict.

Bring to mind someone you have been avoiding, and the belly answers before the thought is finished. Then breathe out slowly, a hand laid flat below your ribs, and feel the same place let go. The nerves that tightened it are the ones that release it when your breath lengthens. Your hand rests on the part of you that felt it first.

### Amino acid
When the last wall in you goes, the body follows, and every cell makes peace with the rest. That peace has a substance in you, and its name is glycine. It is the smallest of the twenty pieces every part of you is built from. Your body makes some and takes in more from food, from meat or from the bones a soup is boiled on. On the tongue it tastes sweet. It has been found in the dust of a comet, one of the few pieces of you ever picked up out there.

The friction in you shares this substance with three of its kin. Together they take what is raw in you and heat it until it changes. That happens in a body first, in the belly. Then it spreads, one opening making the next, out into the people around you.

## RELATIONS

_Held next to UL 5, this becomes the long art of waiting before a thing is forced, conflict softened by the patience that precedes it._

### Pair — UL 5, The Space Between Time (Waiting)
This friction meets whatever is in front of it now, and there is no patience in it. The Space Between Time is where it goes next, the wait that holds you ready for something already on its way. Turn either card over and you get the other. Open it when the heat came up because you could not let a thing take its time. A great deal of friction is only a wait that someone would not keep.

### Programming partner — UL 36, Crystal Creation
This friction happens between two people, and it can go a whole life without turning inward. Crystal Creation holds what this one lacks, the crisis that reaches the heart and goes on feeling past where the mind stops. At their lowest each one keeps the other going. The storm inside gives the quarrel its heat, and the quarrel gives the storm somewhere to point. Open it when the same fight keeps coming back. The trouble may have begun in your own heart, not between you.

### Codon ring — Ring of Alchemy
This friction is the raw material, the thing that has to be heated before it changes. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Alchemy, and the other three go by the numbers on their cards. UL 40 is the will that holds a life up and refills in quiet. UL 47 is the old weight that becomes something worth having. UL 64 is the light that is there before it shows. What the four share is turning something hard in you into something you can use.

### Immortals
Two figures carry this energy. Han Xiangzi is the flute player whose tune opened peonies in the snow. Li Tie Guai is the healer who came back from his own death on an iron crutch, carrying medicine in a gourd. Between them they say how a quarrel ends. Someone plays a note everyone can hear, and someone who has already lost comes back with the cure.

- **Hebrew letter — Beth, the House:** the house, four walls and a roof. A house holds two people who disagree without coming apart, which is the work here.
