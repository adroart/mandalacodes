# UL 6 — Harmonious Mirage

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Friction Between Two People · Feeling a Quarrel Coming · Honest at the Right Moment · Refusing to Trade Blame · Guards Coming Down · The Need to Be Right · Nothing Left to Defend

Someone close to you says something, and before you have answered you are already on guard. Friction is the heat of that guard, rising whenever two people come near each other and each holds to their own side. Under it sits one old fear, that another person can hurt you or take something from you. The friction lasts only as long as each of you keeps a guard up.

The same heat warns you of a quarrel before it has taken shape. It asks two things of you: say the true thing, and say it when it can be heard. When someone comes at you in anger, take the heat in and give it back with nothing added. Once you stop trading blame, the other person is left facing their own part. As the guards come down between two people, that same heat is what falling in love feels like.

Some days a feeling takes hold of you, and you forget that it is only a feeling. In that state, a little anger can cost you something that matters. The hurt is yours to feel, and nobody else is responsible for it. When you most want to be proven right, argue with yourself before you argue with them. Once you see that the fear lives in you and not in them, the fight loses its hold.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Water is the hexagram of a dispute. Heaven is the drive in you that keeps pressing forward and never tires. Water is the part of you that feels at risk and means every word. The strength sits above the risk because you push hardest when you feel least safe. This hexagram has long been read as the sky rising while water runs down. In an argument each of you keeps talking, and neither one moves toward the other. The harder you press your case, the further you get from the person you want to reach.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the stand you take and show to the world. It holds a position and keeps holding it, day after day. It sits on top because it is the outer side of a dispute, the part the other person meets. Heaven has long been read as the sky, which rises and never bends down to the ground. A stand like that stays high and never comes down to where the other person is. On its own, this strength keeps pushing until it has used itself up.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water is what a dispute is like from inside. Everything hangs on one point, and you know you could lose. It lies underneath because the danger is felt within, out of sight, while the stand is what shows. Water has long been read as a gorge, a steep place where the stream runs fast. In a dispute, one wrong word can be that step off the edge. On its own, this fear sinks and pulls you down with it.

### Reading
Your case in this dispute is honest, and the other side will not hear it. The two of you are pulling apart, and every push widens the gap. Nobody comes out of the far end of a quarrel with what they wanted. So stop halfway, while you still can. The middle of a dispute is the one place in it where things settle well.

This time asks for a fair third person, someone who wants nothing from either side. Let that person say what is really being fought over. Leave the big ventures alone until this is settled, the move or the new undertaking. Every dispute sits inside a relationship, and you will still live beside this person when it is over. Be firm inside and give way outside, the way water does. Giving way here keeps the peace, and it is no surrender.

Your ears do the work now. Listen until what is said has come all the way in, and only then answer.

### Judgement
- Conflict. You are sincere, and you are blocked.
- Halt halfway, with care, and it goes well. Carry it to the end, and it goes badly.
- It helps to see the great person. It does not help to cross the great water.

### Image
- Heaven and water go opposite ways: that is conflict.
- So the wise, in everything they undertake, consider the beginning with care.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ not carrying the matter on; a little talk, then a good end. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
The quarrel has barely started and you are the weaker of the two. There will be a little talk about you, a few words behind your back, and that is the whole price. What is true and what is false will sort itself out without your help. Let it drop now, while dropping it costs you almost nothing.

**Line 2** · _image:_ not up to the contest; going home to a town of three hundred houses. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
The other side is stronger, and you cannot win this. Press it and the trouble comes down on everyone who stands with you. Go back to the small town you belong to, three hundred houses and nobody looking for you. Trouble cannot reach a house that does not rise to meet it. Make yourself small, and be safe.

**Line 3** · _image:_ living on what was earned before; serving a king and claiming no merit. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
You are living on what you earned earlier, and you are not strong enough to contend for more. Stay inside the share that is already yours, and reach for no new ground while the dispute runs. If a greater person's work wants your hands, give them and let the credit be theirs. Hold to that, and it ends well.

**Line 4** · _image:_ not up to the contest; turning back, a change of heart, resting in what is right. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
You could fight this, because the strength is in you, and you would still not win. When two hard people meet, an argument always starts. Turn back instead, to the work that is yours, and let your mind change about the whole matter. Rest in what you know is right, and peace comes of it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the dispute brought before the one in the centre. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
Here the matter comes before someone in the centre, fair, wanting nothing for themselves. That is the one place in the whole business where a case can be pressed. If that seat is yours, hear both sides and settle it. If it is not, this is the person to take the matter to. Contend here, and it goes very well.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a leather belt given in honour, taken back three times before the morning is out. → becomes Hexagram 47, Oppression.
You carried the quarrel to the end, you won it, and the prize is in your hands. Three times before the morning is out, it is taken back. What you take by force goes back by force, and the respect you wanted is not in the prize. Stop before you lose more of yourself than you gained.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Conflict · _Gift:_ Diplomacy · _Siddhi:_ Peace

### Shadow — Conflict
The guard you keep against other people went up early, between the ages of seven and fourteen. You built it against the heated moments you lived through as a child. Most adults still carry it, and a great deal of a life's strength goes into keeping it up. Behind it your heart hardens, and you judge the other person or simply avoid them.

Think of the hardest relationship in your life now. That person seems able to ruin your peace whenever they like. What you dislike is the part of you they bring out. They are giving you a way into a fear far older than the two of you, carried down from those who came before. Relationships like this hold the start of your own peace, so do not shy away from them.

### Repressive nature — Over-Attentive
This person keeps the peace at any price, and pays it themselves. What they sweep under the carpet lies there until it blows up. People sense the falseness and never quite trust them. When they finally face a quarrel, it is never as bad as they feared.

### Reactive nature — Tactless
The feeling goes straight out at the closest person, before it has been held for a moment. This person lashes out, then storms away. They cannot hear their own lack of tact, so the fault always lies with the other person. The only one who stays is someone who keeps the peace at any price.

### Gift — Diplomacy
Diplomacy begins the moment your guard drops and your heart opens. It has nothing to do with strategy or the right words, which anyone can learn and use with a shut heart. You sense where another person's heart is and how far it is open. You can do this because you let yourself be hurt too, with your heart on your sleeve.

Fear still comes, and you are no longer afraid of it. So other people's fears stop moving you around. The people you are meant to open, and who open you, keep coming into your life. Each one opens you further, and that opens more in them.

### Siddhi — Peace
Peace is what is left when there is nothing in you to defend. An old man crossing a river bumped his boat into another, and the man in it cursed him. He did not react at all, since there was not a soul in his boat to shout at. Live like that and nothing reaches you as an attack. This peace lies under everything. It is there when you stop what you are doing and listen, to birds singing or the wind dropping. Even your skin stops being a wall, and you feel life moving in everything that lives. Nobody can take your life from you, because you are life.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 6, Friction
Gate 6 sits in the centre of feeling, low in the body, and presses you toward other people. It carries two things at once: the wish to be close, and the fear of being seen as you are. Closeness is the thing it fears most. When it runs well, your feeling brings people together in a way that is fair to each of them. When it runs badly, you feel that nobody will see you unless you force your way in. Its work is to trust that what you say will reach people at the right time, without pushing.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The Emotional Solar Plexus Center sits in the belly and is where feeling moves. It is the only centre that both drives you and tells you what is going on. Feeling here runs in a wave, from hope down to pain and back, over hours or days. There is no truth in the moment of it. What a feeling means becomes clear only once the wave has passed.

A little more than half of all people have this centre defined. They always have their feelings to hand, and they never know what they will feel next. Where this gate is part of a defined channel, upsets get pushed down and pile up. Then one broken promise brings the whole lot out at once. The rest have it open. They take in the feelings around them and feel them louder than the person who had them. To avoid the blast they may stay nice and leave the truth unsaid. Alone, they settle into a steady peace. For everyone, a feeling that is felt passes in a few minutes, and a hand on the shoulder calms the whole of you.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mating (6–59)
On its own, this pressure has nowhere to go. Gate 59, in the Sacral Center below, is the drive to break through the walls between people. Together they make the Channel of Mating, which turns the wish to be close into closeness itself. It builds intimacy through sex, through children and through a business built together. Gate 59 may be in you or in a person close by, and the channel runs only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The belly knows first, and that is why this energy is given to the net of nerves over your gut. Your gut has a nervous system of its own, with more nerve cells than your spinal cord. Most of the news on the great nerve between belly and brain runs upward, so the belly reports before the head decides. Under threat these nerves slow the gut and send blood to the muscles. That is the drop or the knot you feel in an argument before anyone speaks.

Bring to mind someone you have been avoiding, and your belly answers before the thought is finished. Then breathe out slowly with a hand laid flat below your ribs. The nerves that tightened it let it go as the breath lengthens. Your hand rests on the part of you that felt it first.

### Amino acid
When the last wall in you comes down, your body follows, and every cell in it makes peace with the rest. That peace has a substance in you, and its name is glycine. It is the smallest of the twenty pieces your proteins are built from, and it tastes sweet on your tongue. Your body makes some, and food adds more, from meat or the bones a soup is boiled on. The same substance that is in you has been found in the dust of a comet.

This energy shares that substance with three of its kin. Together they turn something dark in you into something lighter. It happens in the body first, in the belly, where the rough material is heated until it changes. Then it spreads from one opening to the next, out into the people around you.

## RELATIONS

_UL 5 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the need that waits, where this one presses its case._

### Pair — UL 5, The Space Between Time (Waiting)
This energy presses its case now, and it runs out when pressing only drives the other person away. The Space Between Time is where it goes next: the wait for what is already on its way, which no pushing brings sooner. Open it when you have said your piece and nothing has moved. A dispute often starts when people cannot wait for what they need.

### Inverse — UL 5, The Space Between Time (Waiting)
Turn this energy end for end and the push becomes a wait. The Space Between Time is the same need seen from the side that holds still. Open it when you are tired of pushing and nothing can be won by force.

### Programming partner — UL 36, Crystal Creation
This energy guards you against other people, and the guard grows out of a nervousness it cannot see. Crystal Creation holds that nervousness, the unease that rises whenever life feels uncertain. The two feed each other: your nerves make you defensive, and your defensiveness makes the people near you nervous. Open it when you find yourself on guard and cannot say why.

### Codon ring — Ring of Alchemy
This energy breaks down the walls between two people. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Alchemy, whose theme is turning something rough into something fine. UL 40, Eternal Wellspring, carries the new openness into a community. UL 47, Garden of Alchemy, carries the change of old ways. UL 64, Communion, carries the fresh possibilities that follow. This one carries the first opening, between two people.

They show you two ways a quarrel can end. You can say one thing so plainly that both sides hear it. Or you can lose, and come back with what heals the hurt.
