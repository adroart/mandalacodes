# UL 6 — Harmonious Mirage

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Close Enough to Hurt · The Heat Between Two · Guarding the Soft Place · Honest at the Right Moment · The Fight You Keep Having · Nothing to Defend · Peace Underneath

Friction is the energy here, the heat that rises between two people the moment they come close. In you it is the pull toward someone and the flinch away from them, arriving together, out of one place. The flinch is not a verdict on the person. It is the old fear that you can be hurt at all, and it wakes whenever someone is near enough to matter. Every quarrel you have ever had began there, before a word was said.

Carried well, this energy is honesty at the right moment. The true thing said too soon is a stone thrown; said when the heat has fallen, it goes in and does its work. You are not fearless, but you have stopped being afraid of the fear, so it no longer decides for you, and nobody near you can use it to move you. What looks like calm from outside is a person who can stand in the middle of their own alarm without leaving.

The way is to find the fear where it lives. It is in you, older than you, carried in the blood from people you never met, and whoever is standing closest only wakes it. Felt there, in you, it loosens, and the wall you built against them comes down on its own, because nothing behind it was ever in danger. The ones you have fought hardest are the ones who have brought you nearest to peace.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Water: the sky above, going up, and the deep water below, going down. Two things share one place while moving apart, and the gap between them is the quarrel. The traditional character sets the sign for speech beside the sign for public: words that were kept inside, said where everyone can hear them.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven here is the sky going up and away, sure of itself. It does not come down to meet what is under it, because it is already gone. In a family it is the father whose word is final, who does not argue, only leaves the room. In you it is the part that knows it is right and has finished listening. You have the strength, you have the case, and neither will bend. Its risk is winning every point alone, since a strength that rises away from what is beneath it has nothing left to hold.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Under it lies water, one whole line held between two broken ones: a true heart shut inside a hard place. Water goes down, seeking the lowest ground, and stops for nothing above it. In a family it is the middle son, caught between, who is right and cannot be heard. In you it is the feeling of having been wronged, kept low in the body where it goes on turning over. Its risk is the pit, because what keeps sinking finds the bottom, and the danger there is real.

### Reading
You are in a dispute, your case is honest, and the other side has stopped listening. That is the whole situation: your truth is real and the door is closed. Nothing you say now will open it, because the two of you are moving in opposite directions, and every push sends you further apart. Nobody has ever come out of the far end of a quarrel holding the thing they went in for, and even a victory won here is taken back before long. Stop halfway. The middle of a quarrel is the only place in it where anything is settled well.

What it asks of you is a fair third person, someone with no stake in either side, who can say plainly what is being fought over so that it stops being about pride. It asks you to leave the great crossings alone, the move, the new undertaking, because the strength in the room is contradicting itself. Every dispute sits inside a relationship, and the person you are arguing with is one you will still live beside when it is over. Be firm inside, as the sky is, and give on the outside, as water does; giving here keeps the peace, which is no surrender.

In the body this energy is the ear, hollow on the outside so that it can take a thing in. Notice where you are holding yourself now: talking, or listening. Hold yourself as the water lies, low and open, so that what is said comes all the way in before you answer it.

### Judgement
- Conflict. You are sincere, and you are blocked.
- Halt halfway, with care, and it goes well. Carry it to the end, and it goes badly.
- It helps to see the great person. It does not help to cross the great water.

### Image
- Heaven and water go opposite ways: that is conflict.
- So the wise, in everything they undertake, consider the beginning with care.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ not carrying the matter on; a little talk, then a good end. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
At the bottom of a quarrel that has barely begun, you are the weaker party and the thing is still small. There will be a little talk about you, a few words behind your back, and that is the whole price. What is true and what is false will sort itself out without your help. Let it drop now, while dropping it costs almost nothing.

**Line 2** · _image:_ not up to the contest; going home to a town of three hundred houses. → becomes Hexagram 12, Standstill.
Against someone stronger, you cannot win this, and pressing it would bring the trouble down on everyone who stands with you. Turn back to the small place you came from, where three hundred families live and nobody is looking for you. Trouble passes over a house that does not rise to meet it. Make yourself small, and be safe.

**Line 3** · _image:_ living on what was earned before; serving a king and claiming no merit. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
Living on what you earned earlier, in a spot that is exposed, you are not strong enough to contend and you know it. Stay inside the portion that is already yours, without reaching for new ground in the middle of a dispute. If a greater person's work asks for your hands, give them, and let the credit be theirs. Held firmly, this ends well.

**Line 4** · _image:_ not up to the contest; turning back, a change of heart, resting in what is right. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
You could fight this, since the strength is in you, and you would not win it. Turn back instead, toward what was given you to do, and let your mind change about the whole matter. This is not surrender. It is a change of heart, and a heart that has changed can rest. Rest in what you know to be right, and peace comes of it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the dispute brought before the one in the centre. → becomes Hexagram 64, Before Completion.
In the centre and fair, this is the one place in the whole matter where a case may be pressed, because the one pressing it wants nothing for themselves. If that seat is yours, hear both sides and settle it; if it is not, this is the person to bring the matter to. Truth lands clean here. Contend, and it goes very well.

**Line 6** · _image:_ a leather belt given in honour, taken back three times before the morning is out. → becomes Hexagram 47, Oppression.
You carried the quarrel all the way to the top, you won it, and the prize is in your hands. Watch how fast it leaves them, three times before the morning is over. What was taken by force is taken back by force, and the respect you wanted was never in the prize. Come down from it now, while there is still something of yourself to bring.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Conflict · _Gift:_ Diplomacy · _Siddhi:_ Peace

### Shadow — Conflict
A feeling gets big enough and you stop having it: it has you, so that from inside it the other person looks like the danger. That is where every quarrel starts, two people who have both agreed to be their feelings for as long as it lasts. Underneath is one fear, older than any argument, that someone standing close can hurt you or take something from you. So you guard. The chest hardens and a small distance is kept going. The guarding was learned early, between the ages of seven and fourteen, so most of a life's strength can go into keeping it up without a day of it being noticed.

Think of the hardest relationship you have now. What that person seems to hold over you is a part of yourself you would rather not meet, and they are the only one who can bring it up. The fear was never in them. Ask what the quarrel is really about, and the answer is nearly always the part of you they bring out. The relationships that will not leave you alone are the ones holding the thing you have not yet felt, which is why they stay.

### Repressive nature — Over-Attentive
This person keeps the peace whatever it costs, and it costs them. They read every room and soften to fit it, agreeing sooner than they mean, until nobody near them trusts the agreement. What was swept under lies there until it blows. Faced at last, a quarrel is never as bad as they feared.

### Reactive nature — Tactless
Whoever is nearest gets the feeling the moment it arrives, hot, before it has been held for a minute; then this person leaves before it lands. Deaf to their lack of tact, they find the fault in the other person, so the only one who stays is the one who will bear anything.

### Gift — Diplomacy
An open heart is tactful without trying, and that is what this energy becomes once the guard is down. It has nothing to do with the careful phrase, which anyone can learn and a shut heart can deliver perfectly. It is a sense, in the body, of where the other person is: how open, how braced, how near the edge. You feel a quarrel coming before it has a shape, and you can turn one word so that it passes. When someone comes at you hot, you take it in and hand it back with nothing added, so the heat has nowhere left to go. When you refuse the game of blame, the other person is left holding their own share, which changes them or moves them out of your life.

What this does between people is what falling in love is: the rub between two natures wearing through until nothing stands between them. The ones you are meant to open keep arriving, year after year, and each opening makes the next one easier. Being this vulnerable is the strength. Your heart is worn where anyone can reach it, and that is what lets you reach theirs.

### Siddhi — Peace
Nothing is left to defend, because there is no one inside to be attacked. Where there had been two sides to keep even there are no sides now, and the evening-out that took such effort stops on its own. An old man rowing across a river was struck by another boat, turned to shout, and found it empty, drifting, nobody in it. The shout died in him. Live as the empty boat and nothing that comes at you arrives as an attack, since there is no one in you for it to land on. This peace was under everything the whole time. Stop and listen: it is there, in the birds, in the wind dropping. Even the wall of your own skin stops being a border, and you feel life moving in everything that lives. Nobody can take your life from you, because you are life.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 6, Friction
Closeness is what this drive wants, and the rub is how it gets there. Gate 6 is the door it comes through: the wanting to be near someone with the fear of being seen too closely, arriving as one pressure. When two people warm to each other a heat comes up that does not feel like welcome, a snag in the warming, and most people take the snag as proof the fit is wrong. It is not proof, only the place where two feeling natures touch, the edge that real contact forms against. In someone who carries this, the heat comes with closeness itself, quick or a slow simmer, and smoothing it away leaves politeness with nothing under it.

From outside it looks like someone who starts things. What is going on is that they feel the edge before anyone else and cannot pretend it is not there. The cost is being called difficult by people who wanted the warmth without the rub. Those who carry this through life learn that the rub was the way in, and that a closeness with no friction in it never got close.

### Where it lives — the Emotional Solar Plexus Center
The rub is felt low in the belly, in the Emotional Solar Plexus centre, where feeling lives. This centre is an engine as well as a way of knowing, so a feeling here is fuel and news. It moves in a wave: hope rising, falling all the way to pain before it turns, over hours or days. There is no truth in the moment here, only in the whole wave, so a verdict reached while the heat is up comes out the wrong shape. Waited out, the same feeling says something you can use.

In a little more than half of all people this centre is switched on, and the wave runs with nobody else in the room. For someone like that, feeling is the water they swim in, and the work of a lifetime is waiting for the wave to settle before deciding, since in this kind upsets are stored quietly until one broken promise brings the whole store down. In the rest it is not, so there is no wave of their own. For someone like that, other people's feelings come in and get louder, until they are voicing a whole room's anger as their own, with agreeableness as the wall against it. Alone, they settle into a steadiness the others never get. Whichever you are, a feeling let pass lasts a few minutes, and a hand on the shoulder brings the whole system down.

### What completes it — the Channel of Mating (6–59)
Friction on its own has nowhere to go. Its other half is Gate 59, low in the belly, the drive to break down whatever stands between two bodies, and together they make the Channel of Mating. Through it the rub finds its purpose: the wall between two people comes down and closeness follows, in a bed or in a business built together. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the wall only comes down where the two meet.

## BODY

### Physiology
This energy is given to the nerves of your belly, the net over the gut, and the reason is that the belly knows first. Before a feeling of yours is a thought, it is there. Your gut has a nerve system of its own, with more nerve cells than your spine. The great nerve between your belly and your brain sends most of its news upward, so the belly has reported before the head has decided. When something threatens you, these nerves slow the gut and pull blood toward the muscles: that is the drop or the knot you feel in an argument before a word is said. The knot in you is a wave, not a verdict.

Bring to mind someone you have been avoiding, and the belly answers before the thought is finished. Then breathe out slowly, a hand laid flat below your ribs, and feel the same place let go. The nerves that tightened it are the ones that release it when your breath lengthens. Your hand rests on the part of you that felt it first.

### Amino acid
When the last wall in you goes, the body follows, and every cell makes peace with the others. That peace has a substance in you, and the substance is glycine, the smallest of the twenty pieces every part of you is built from. Your body makes it and takes in more from food, meat or the bones a soup is boiled on; it is sweet on the tongue. It has been found in the dust of a comet, one of the few pieces of you ever found out there.

The friction in you shares this substance with three of its kin, and together they turn a dark thing into a lighter one. That work begins in a body, in the belly, where what is raw in you is heated until it changes. Once started it spreads, one opening making the next, into the people near you.
