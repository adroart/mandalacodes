# UL 64 — Communion

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Almost There · Not Knowing Yet · The Fog Before the Idea · Nothing to Do But Wait · The Last Cold Stretch · Clear All at Once · Still Unfinished

Light is in you before it shows, and this is the energy of the stretch when it has not shown yet. It comes on when a change is under way, everything you knew about yourself in question, a whole picture arrived at once and out of order, bigger than you can lay out on a table. In a life it is the not-knowing that comes with any real change, when what is ahead is real and none of the steps to it can be seen.

Carried well, it is the nerve to not know for as long as it takes. The state itself is natural, the raw material before anything is made from it, with no order in it and a great deal of possibility. The mind will offer you a reason for it, then something to do, anything that makes the not-knowing stop, and the not-knowing is not the mind's to end. What ends it is time. The ones who carry this well have learned to give it that, staying open in the fog, because something arrives there that nothing else could have brought: an idea that was nowhere an hour ago, whole.

Wait without hardening. Left alone, the fog thins on its own. Now and then it does not thin but clears all at once, in a leap that lands you somewhere you could not have walked to. It asks one thing of you meanwhile, which is not to reach for the old order to feel safe, since that is what presses a change flat. The lit thing at the end of this was in you the whole time. It was only dark.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Water: the flame climbing off the top while the water drains out of the bottom, and nothing between them settles. Not one of the six lines is in its right place, yet each answers its partner across the figure. The old character is a tree grown to its full height with the root under the ground still alive. A small fox is a step from the far bank.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the flame, and what a flame does is cling: with no body of its own, it lives on what it burns. In the world it is brightness, the light that shows one thing from another. In the family it is the middle daughter, the one who notices. In you it is the clear picture, the eye that can tell what each thing is and where it belongs. On top here it is the seeing, climbing away from the water it would need to be of any use. Its risk is brightness with nothing under it, a picture so clear you take it for the crossing made.

### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Under the fire runs the water, the stream in its cut, cold and moving, which is where the fox is standing. One firm line is held between two soft ones: the strength is in the middle of it and cannot be seen from the bank. In the world it is the ford, where a crossing is made or lost. In you it is the last stretch of a danger you have nearly come through, the part that is not over because it is nearly over. Its risk is the tail going in, the moment your attention leaves the water because the shore is so close.

### Reading
You are a step short of the far bank, and the step is the whole of it. Most of what you set out to do is done. The other side is in plain sight, so close that stopping feels absurd, with one stretch of cold water still between you and it. A small fox has picked its way across the ford, quick, careful, then a step from the shore, sure the crossing is done, it stops watching the water. Its tail goes in. Nearly across is not across.

What it asks is care in telling one thing from another. Fire climbs, water sinks, and left alone the two pull apart until the work dissolves between them. Look hard at what each thing is, then set it where it goes, the way a table is cleared before the last piece is fitted. Do not force a direction on it. The push to be finished is not the same as being finished, and a move made out of that push is what wets the tail.

In the body this is the eye. It sees the bank, and the bank is not under your feet. The posture is the fox's before the last step: attention on the water rather than the shore, every step tested as if it were the first. The book of changes ends here, not on the finished figure, because nothing stays finished. The seasons turn, and what was completed begins again. You are standing in that turn.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Confusion · _Gift:_ Imagination · _Siddhi:_ Illumination

### Shadow — Confusion
Confusion is what light feels like before it shows. The word means poured together, everything muddled in with everything, nothing yet drawn out. In itself it is natural, the state before anything has been drawn out, and it turns bewildering only when the mind gets hold of it. The mind wants a reason for the state you are in, then a name for it, then something to do, anything that puts the furniture back and makes the not-knowing stop, so you tidy the desk at midnight, or explain yourself until the explanation sounds like an answer. Every move made from inside the muddle stirs it further, because a decision made out of confusion is confusion with a date on it.

Underneath is the wish for things to stay as they were. Confusion is the feeling of being unfinished, and most people will do almost anything rather than feel it, which is how a change that was trying to move you gets pressed flat under a false order. When you are confused you are alone. What the confusion needs is the one thing you least want to give it, which is time.

### Repressive nature — Imitating
Holding down that much unease takes more than one person has, so this one borrows the shape of a life. They do what their parents did, what the friends do, and the crowd is glad to help, busy with the same. Nothing has to be felt while everyone agrees. Some even look original doing it.

### Reactive nature — Confused
Some bodies cannot hold the unease down at all, and here it comes straight out into the life. The fights come from nowhere, the same closeness breaks in the same place each time, with an anger in it at everyone who looks settled. It is a war on life, and it passes for a personality.

### Gift — Imagination
Given room and time, the muddle turns into something that walks in unplanned. Imagination shares a root with magic, and the small syllable under both is about working on matter. A man once looked at the middle of his city, saw an iron tower there that did not exist, and inside ten years it did. Before that it was nowhere. It begins with the willingness to feel what the thinking was there to avoid: take away the mind's food, the belief that it can help, and the strength that fed it comes free, rises, starts to paint with you. What comes through first is mostly the ache, and giving the ache a shape, painted or told, is how it starts to show what it hid.

It is a way of doing, not only of seeing, because anyone can have the vision. The frightened version keeps it as a vision, since building it would change everything. Nor can it be hurried. Work it out in your head and it shuts down mid-sentence. Leave it alone and it hands you the picture whole. Nothing it makes is ever quite finished, and the unfinished edge is where the next thing enters.

### Siddhi — Illumination
Lit from inside, and anyone in the room can tell. The eyes shine, the brow gives off something, the air around you seems to shimmer as if the space had been warmed. All the long work was for this, the dark raw thing heated until it shone. It was always about light, the inner kind, which is not the kind the eyes see, and which shines the same in every part of you.

The imagination has lost its I. What comes through no longer belongs to you: it arrives as poems, as pictures that make no sense and perfect sense, for whoever happens to be listening. It travels in the pauses, the silence between one word and the next, because the mind cannot follow it there. You remain unfinished, even now, still a surface for whatever wants to come through next.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 64, Confusion
Something very large arrived in you all at once, out of order, and you have carried it since without knowing how to set it down. This is Gate 64, the pressure to make sense of the past. It came as pictures, dreams, the whole unfinished story, a download bigger than the years you have had to unpack it, and the one question it presses on you is how. How does what happened become a way forward. How does the picture get made real. Nothing in it is in sequence, so looked at through a straight line it reads as chaos, which is where the name comes from.

From outside you look scattered, someone who cannot finish a thought. Asked for the idea, you produce three halves of it. It is not a fault in the mind. It is a mind working at the size of the material. Pushed for an answer this week it goes dark, and the despair of that can shut the dreaming down altogether. Given its years, the fog thins on its own, and what was confusion turns out to have been the beginning of something you could not have seen any other way.

### Where it lives — the Head Center
At the crown, the very top of you, is the place this pressure comes in, one of two places in the body built to run on pressure, and all it does is ask. A question arrives and the push to answer it arrives in the same breath, so close that the two feel like one thing. This place decides nothing, moves nothing: there is no engine under it, so a thought that lands here is handed downward to be worked on somewhere else, and the mistake nearly everyone makes is to take the landing for an order. Rushed, a question gives up its answer badly. Waited on, it opens, and something arrives that pushing would never have found.

In about three people in ten this place is switched on, and the mind never stops: questions all day, whether or not anyone is listening. For someone like that the danger is the loop, a question with no answer in sight until they lose patience and act, or turn the pressure inward into dread. What they have is that they know what they think, even while confused. In the other seven it is open: the questions in the room are taken in, made louder, so the mind is tossed by whoever spoke last, and can go blank when asked. For them the way is to notice whose question it is, because most of the pressure was never theirs.

### What completes it — the Channel of Abstraction (64–47)
The pressure at the crown has one road out, straight down, to the part of you that takes a flood of pictures and turns it over until a pattern shows. Its other half is Gate 47, behind the forehead, and the two make the Channel of Abstraction, a mind that works by flashes: a long restless stretch with no through-line, then one morning it clicks. The lower half cannot make the click come sooner, the upper half cannot stop sending. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the two only meet in their own time.

## BODY

### Physiology
One small organ in you lives in the dark yet answers to light, and it is the pineal, in the middle of your head. That is why this energy is felt there: it is light not yet showing, and this is the part of you where the dark turns to light. The gland is the size of a grain of rice, and it never sees the day. It learns whether it is light or dark from your eyes, and when the light goes it lets out the substance of the night, which slows you toward sleep. Nearly everything you will ever understand is made first in the dark of the brain, where you have no way in, and this gland is the door out of that dark into the part of you that thinks.

Sit under a bright screen late at night and the gland reads it as day, so the night in you waits. Dim the room and within an hour or two you feel it begin, the slowing you cannot make happen on purpose.

### Amino acid
The light in you that has not yet shown has a substance, and the substance is glycine. Your body makes it, then takes in more with food, most of all the jelly a cooled stock sets into. It sits at the gate in you where memory is laid down, and the gate will not open until a glycine is in its seat, so nothing you ever learned was learned without it. What is hidden in you is the same substance in its kin, one family with one work.

The work is turning a dark thing into a light one, and it goes by colour: the black raw material, heated, turns white, then yellow, then the red of gold. Light is in every form, yours included, hidden until it is let show, and that showing is what alchemy is. The confusion in you is the black at the start.

### Inverse — UL 63, Adornments of Time
Turned end for end, the not-yet becomes the already. What reads from this bank as a difficult last step reads from the far one as a step completed, the same water seen from each shore, and the counsel is the same in both directions: keep tending. The one who has crossed and stops watching the river ends up back in it.

### Programming partner — UL 63, Adornments of Time
The Gene Keys set Doubt beside Confusion and Truth beside Illumination. The two run as one loop. A mind that doubts every settled answer feeds a mind that cannot tell one thing from another, and the muddle feeds the doubting back. At the top the same pair becomes two wings: the patient sequence that arrives at truth one step at a time, and the sudden leap that arrives lit. You need Adornments of Time's discipline to ground your leaps, and it needs your leaps to keep its logic alive.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Alchemy
The Ring of Alchemy holds four codes that each turn one substance into another, and this one is the last of them. UL 6 turns conflict into peace, UL 40 turns exhaustion into surrender, UL 47 turns the old inherited weight into fuel, and this one turns confusion into clear seeing. Four stages of one slow heat. The family works with what is already in the vessel and nothing else. Yours is the stage nearest the end, where the colour is about to turn and has not yet.

### Tarot
The fox is a step from the bank with its tail in the water, and three tarot faces stand over that step. The ring gives the Magician, the maker with one hand raised and the other pointing at the table, every tool gathered and the work not yet declared done. The two trigrams give their own: Fire above as the Emperor and the Sun, the order that sets each thing where it belongs and the clear day on the far side of the crossing; Water below as the Hanged Man, hung still on purpose because pushing forward will not work yet. Between them they hold the code's one gesture, a raised hand over finished tools, a pause in the water under a brightness about to be declared.

- **Ring Arcana — I · The Magician** (Manifestation): Through the Ring of Alchemy this code carries the Magician, who stands at the table with the cup, the coin, the blade and the staff laid out and one hand lifted to draw down what will move them. Everything the crossing gathered is on the table now. The Magician is the alertness of that lifted hand, the making held one beat before it is called complete.
- **Fire (upper) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion): The Fire above wears the Emperor, who rules by knowing what each thing is and where it belongs. The clarity climbing off the top of the figure is also his order, the sorting the whole hexagram counsels before the ford is tried again.
- **Fire (upper) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): Fire also carries the Sun, the open day in which nothing is hidden and nothing burns. It is the far bank seen in full, the brightness the code is crossing toward, and the reminder that clarity here arrives as warmth rather than as an answer forced.
- **Water (lower) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Water at the root wears the Hanged Man, hung by one foot, seeing the whole situation upside down because moving forward will not serve yet. The held moment in the stream is his: hang still, let the view turn over, and do not thrash.

### Immortals
Fire above carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar who was tested ten times before he was let through, who wears a sword at his back and commands the thunder that drives out whatever haunts a place; Water below carries Li Tie Guai, the healer who came back through his own death and keeps his medicine in a gourd. One came by his clarity through trials and the other by his cure through dying. Between the sword that clears and the gourd that mends, nothing in this crossing gets forced.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Mercury:** the quick messenger who moves between two shores with the word not yet delivered, the intelligence that can hold a message across a gap. The Magician's planet, and the right sky for a crossing held open.
- **Hebrew letter — Beth, the House:** the first enclosed space, the room that gives a work four walls and a roof. Even a step from the far bank the unfinished thing needs a house to be tended in, or it scatters; Beth is the letter of keeping it under one roof until it is done.
