# UL 64 — Communion

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Imagination · Pictures Before Answers · Giving It Time · Seeing Where It Goes · Making What You See · A Work in Progress · Lit From Inside

Imagination moves in you before anything makes sense. Pictures and ideas arrive whole and out of order, often in the middle of a change. You can see where something is going long before you know how to get there. The picture is real even while the road to it is dark.

Holding it well means giving the pictures time. Your mind will want to know how, and it will want to know now. Each answer forced out early only adds to the fog. Left alone, the picture clears on its own and shows you the next step. Then comes the second half, which is making the thing. Seeing it is not enough. It takes nerve to build what you have pictured, because building it will change your life.

So your life becomes a work in progress. Nothing in it is ever quite finished, and the unfinished edge is where the next picture comes in. There will be stretches when nothing is clear and you want the old order back. That is confusion, and it passes if you let it. Carried far enough, you stop holding the pictures as your own. You make them for whoever needs them.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Water is the hexagram of being almost across. Fire is the part of you that already sees where you are going. Water is the part of you still wading the cold stretch. The seeing sits above the wading because you can picture the far bank long before your feet reach it. That is why this hexagram has long been read as a young fox crossing a river. A step from the bank it stops watching the water, and its tail goes in. The book of changes ends here because nothing in it stays finished. You can see where you are going, and you are not there yet.


### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire in you shows you the whole shape before you can explain any of it. It sits on top because a picture always runs ahead of the life that makes it real. Fire has long been read as a flame. A flame has no body of its own and lives on what it burns. Your seeing is the same and needs something under it to feed it. On its own it hands you a picture so clear that you take it for the crossing already made.


### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
Water in you is the last cold stretch you still have to wade. It lies underneath because you were in the river before you could see the other side. Water has long been read as the ford, the place where a crossing is made or lost. A ford is shallow enough to walk and deep enough to take you. On its own it keeps your eyes so low on your footing that you forget the bank is near.


### Reading
You are almost across, and the last stretch is the whole of it. Most of what you set out to do is done. The far bank is so close that stopping now feels foolish. A young fox crosses a river quick and careful. A step from the shore it is sure it has made it. It stops watching the water. Its tail goes in.

What this time asks is care in telling one thing from another. Fire climbs and water sinks, so left alone the two pull apart. Look at what each thing is and set it where it belongs. Water can cool a fire or put it out. Fire can warm water or boil it away. Which one happens depends on where each one stands.

The eye is the body's part in this, because it reaches the far bank before your feet do. Keep it on the water under you until you are out. Test the last step as if it were the first. Wanting it done is not the same as being done.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Confusion · _Gift:_ Imagination · _Siddhi:_ Illumination

### Shadow — Confusion
Confusion is the natural state of a mind before anything in it has an order. It comes with every real change, when what you knew about yourself comes loose. It turns bewildering only when the mind tries to make sense of it too soon. The mind wants a reason, then a plan, then something to do. Each move made to end the confusion makes more of it. A decision made in the fog is the fog with a date on it.

Underneath is the wish for things to stay as they were. Most people will do almost anything rather than feel unfinished. So a change that was trying to move you gets pressed flat under the old order. What confusion needs is time, the one thing you least want to give it.

### Repressive nature — Imitating
Holding that much unease down takes more than one person has. So this one borrows a life. They do what their parents did and what their friends are doing. While everyone agrees, nobody has to feel anything. Copying is the opposite of imagining, though some manage to look original doing it.

### Reactive nature — Confused
Some bodies cannot hold the unease down at all. In them it comes straight out into the life. Fights start from nowhere, and the same closeness breaks in the same place. Underneath is anger at a world that seems to have it settled. The life becomes a quarrel with life itself.

### Gift — Imagination
Imagination is what the same fog becomes when you stop trying to change it. Pictures and ideas come too fast to stop and explain themselves. Imagination sees shape, colour and movement inside you. Then it carries them out where others can see them. Someone once pictured an iron tower in the middle of Paris. Ten years later it stood there. Often what comes first is the ache you were thinking to avoid. Give the ache a shape in paint or words and it starts to change.

This is a way of doing as much as seeing. Anyone can have the picture. It takes nerve to build it, because building it will change your life. You cannot hurry it either. Try to work it out in your head and it shuts down. Leave it alone and it hands you the whole thing. Your life becomes a work in progress that is never quite finished.

### Siddhi — Illumination
Illumination is imagination with no I left in it. The pictures still come, but nobody is making them. They arrive as poems and images that make no sense and perfect sense at once. They are for whoever is listening. You are lit from inside, and people in the room can tell. Your eyes shine and the air around you seems warmer. A poem can quiet the mind enough for this to come through, in the silence between its words. Even now you are unfinished and open to whatever comes next.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 64, Confusion
Gate 64 is a pressure at the top of your head full of pictures that do not make sense yet. They come as images, dreams and pieces of the past, all out of order. Along a straight line they read as a mess. That is why this gate is called confusion. Given time, the pictures give up what they meant. The one question this pressure keeps asking is how. How does the picture become real? Asked too soon, that question shuts the dreaming down. If an idea is yours to make, the way and the timing come clear on their own. From outside you can look scattered, with three halves of a thought. It is a mind working at the size of what it carries.

### Where it lives — the Head Center
The Head Center sits at the crown, the very top of you. It is one of two centres in the body that run on pressure. All it does is ask. A question arrives and the push to answer it arrives with it. The Head Center has no engine, so it cannot act on anything. It hands each question down to be worked on somewhere else. The common mistake is to take the question for an order. Rush it and it gives a poor answer. Wait on it and it opens.

About three people in ten have this centre defined. Their minds keep asking whether anyone is listening or not. They know what they think even while the pictures are still unclear. The other seven have it open. They take in the questions of the people around them and feel them louder. Asked for their own view, they can go blank. For them the useful thing is to notice whose question it is.

### What completes it — the Channel of Abstraction (64–47)
The Channel of Abstraction joins this gate to Gate 47, behind the forehead. Gate 47 takes the flood of pictures and turns them over until a pattern shows. This channel works in flashes rather than steps. A long restless stretch goes by with no line through it. Then one morning the whole thing clicks. The channel runs only when Gate 47 is there too, in you or in the person beside you.

## BODY

### Physiology
The pineal gland is given this energy, and the reason is light. It is the size of a grain of rice and sits in the dark middle of your head. It never sees the day. It learns whether the sun is up from your eyes alone. When the light goes, it lets out the substance of the night and you slow toward sleep. So a gland that lives in the dark keeps your time by light. In some lizards its relative is a third eye under the skin of the crown, with a lens of its own.

Sit under a bright screen late at night and the gland reads it as day. The night in you waits. Dim the room and within an hour or two you feel yourself slow. You cannot make that happen by wanting it.

### Amino acid
The light in you that has not shown yet has a substance, and it is glycine. It is the smallest of the pieces your body builds its proteins from. Your body makes it and takes in more from meat, fish and the jelly a cooled stock sets into. It sits at the gate in your brain where memory is laid down. That gate will not open until glycine is in its seat.

You share this substance with three kin. Together they are a family that turns dark things into light ones. The old alchemists heated a black raw material and watched it change. Its colour went from white to yellow to the red of gold. The confusion in you is that black at the start.

## RELATIONS

### Inverse — UL 63, Adornments of Time
Turn this energy end over end and what is not yet done becomes what is finished. Adornments of Time is this card three times over: pair, inverse and partner. From this bank the last step looks hard. From the far bank it is a step already taken.

### Programming partner — UL 63, Adornments of Time
This energy leaps, and a leap lands nowhere unless something carries it step by step. Adornments of Time holds what this one lacks. It is the patient sequence that reaches truth one step at a time. Open it when the picture is clear and you cannot get it into the world.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Alchemy
This energy turns one state into another, and the turning is slow. It belongs to a family of four called the Ring of Alchemy. UL 6 turns conflict into peace. UL 40 turns exhaustion into rest. UL 47 turns an old weight into change. This one turns confusion into fresh pictures.
