# UL 64 — Communion

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Imagination · Ideas Arrive Whole · Inner Light · Comfortable Not Knowing · Giving It Time · One Step Short · Confusion Before Clarity

Light is in you before it shows, and this energy is the stretch before it does. It arrives when a change is under way and everything you knew about yourself is in question. A whole picture comes at once, out of order, too big to lay out on a table. What is ahead is real. You cannot see one step of the way there.

This energy asks of you the nerve to not know for as long as it takes. The state is natural, the raw material before anyone has made anything from it. Nothing in it has an order yet, and everything is still possible. Your mind will hand you a reason for it, then something to do, anything that stops the not-knowing. The not-knowing is not the mind's to end. What ends it is time. Those who hold this well give it the time and stay open in the fog. An idea arrives there that was nowhere an hour before, and it comes whole.

Wait, and do not harden. Left alone, the fog thins on its own. Now and then it clears all at once instead, in a leap that lands you somewhere you could not have walked to. The one thing it asks meanwhile is that you do not reach back for the old order to feel safe. Reaching back is what presses a change flat. The lit thing at the end of this was in you the whole time. It was only dark.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Water is the hexagram of the step you have not taken yet. Fire is the seeing in you that knows where you are going. Water is the cold stretch you still have to cross. The seeing sits above the crossing because you see the far side long before your feet reach it. This hexagram has long been read as a small fox at a ford. The fox crosses well, then stops watching the water a step from the bank. Not one of the six lines sits in its right place. You can see where you are going, and you are not there yet.


### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
You see the shape of the whole thing before anyone has explained it to you. That clear picture sits on top because seeing a thing runs ahead of living it. Fire has long been read as the flame, which has no body of its own and lives on whatever it burns. Your clarity burns the same way, and it needs something underneath to feed it. On its own it hands you a picture so clear that you take it for the crossing made.


### Lower trigram — Water (Kan)
The cold part of this is still ahead of you, the last stretch of a danger you have nearly come through. It lies underneath because you were in the water before you could see the far side. Water has long been read as the ford, the place where a crossing is made or lost. On its own it takes your eyes off the water the moment the shore looks close.


### Reading
You are a step short of the far bank, and that step is the whole of it. Most of what you set out to do is done. The other side is in plain sight, close enough that stopping now feels absurd. One stretch of cold water still lies between you and it. A small fox picks its way across a ford, quick and careful. A step from the shore it is sure the crossing is done. It stops watching the water. Its tail goes in. Nearly across is not across.

Care in telling one thing from another is what matters now. Fire climbs while water sinks, so left alone the two pull apart and the work dissolves between them. Look hard at what each thing is, then set it where it goes. Do not force a direction on any of it. Wanting it done is not the same as being done. A move made out of that want is what wets the tail.

The eye is the body's part in this. It sees the far bank while your feet are still in the water. Stand the way the fox stands before its last step, with your attention on the water rather than the shore. Test every step as if it were the first. The book of changes ends on this hexagram, where the crossing is still under way. Nothing ever stays finished. The seasons turn, and what was completed begins again. You are standing in that turn.

### Judgement
- Not yet across. Even so, it grows.

- Nothing is gained by forcing a direction on it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ thunder loosed on the demon country, three years of it. → becomes Hexagram 4, Youthful Folly.

A head under the water at the end is worse than a wet tail at the start. The seasons do not overrun their bounds. Keep your measure now, when it seems least needed.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Confusion · _Gift:_ Imagination · _Siddhi:_ Illumination

### Shadow — Confusion
Confusion is what light feels like before it shows. The word means poured together, one thing muddled in with another, nothing yet drawn out. The state itself is natural, and it turns bewildering only when the mind gets hold of it. Your mind wants a reason for it, then a name, then something to do. It will put the furniture back any way it can. So you tidy the desk at midnight, or explain yourself until the explanation sounds like an answer. Each move you make from inside the muddle stirs it further. A decision made out of confusion is confusion with a date on it.

What sits underneath is the wish for things to stay as they were. Confusion is the feeling of being unfinished, and most people will do almost anything rather than feel it. That is how a change trying to move you gets pressed flat under a false order. When you are confused, you are alone. What the confusion needs is the one thing you least want to give it. That thing is time.

### Repressive nature — Imitating
Holding down that much unease takes more than one person has, so this one borrows the shape of a life. They do what their parents did, what their friends do, what the crowd is busy with. Nobody has to feel anything while everyone agrees. Some even look original doing it.

### Reactive nature — Confused
Some bodies cannot hold the unease down at all, and here it comes straight out into the life. The fights come from nowhere, one closeness breaking in the same place every time, with an anger in it at anyone who looks settled. It is a war on life, and it passes for a personality.

### Gift — Imagination
Given room and time, the muddle turns into something that walks in unplanned. Imagination and magic come from one old root, a word for working on matter. A man once looked at the middle of his city and saw an iron tower there that did not exist. Within ten years it did. It starts when you are willing to feel what the thinking was there to avoid. Take away the mind's food, the belief that thinking can help, and the strength that fed it comes free. That strength rises and starts to paint with you. What arrives first is mostly the ache. Give the ache a shape, painted or told, and it begins to show you what it hid.

This is a way of doing as much as a way of seeing, because anyone can have the vision. The frightened version keeps it as a vision, because building it would change everything. You cannot hurry it either. Work it out in your head and it shuts down mid-sentence. Leave it alone and it hands you the picture whole. Nothing it makes is ever quite finished, and the unfinished edge is where the next thing enters.

### Siddhi — Illumination
You are lit from inside, and anyone in the room can tell. The eyes shine, the brow gives off something, the air around you seems warmed. All the long work was for this, the dark raw thing heated until it shone. All of it was about an inner light, which is not the light the eyes see. It shines the same in every part of you.

Nobody is doing the imagining now. What arrives no longer belongs to you. It comes as poems, as pictures that make no sense and perfect sense, for whoever is listening. It travels in the pauses, in the silence between one word and the next, where the mind cannot follow. You are unfinished even now, still a surface for whatever wants to arrive next.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 64, Confusion
Something very large arrived in you all at once, out of order, and you have carried it since. The name for it is Gate 64, the pressure to make sense of the past. It came as pictures, as dreams, as the whole unfinished story. What arrived is bigger than the years you have had to unpack it. The one question it presses on you is how. How does what happened become a way forward. How does the picture get made real. Nothing in it is in sequence, so along a straight line it reads as chaos. That is where the word confusion comes from.

From outside you look scattered, someone who cannot finish a thought. Asked for the idea, you produce three halves of it. None of this is a fault in the mind. It is a mind working at the size of its material. Push it for an answer this week and it goes dark. The despair of that can shut the dreaming down altogether. Give it its years and the fog thins on its own. What looked like confusion was the start of something you could not have seen another way.

### Where it lives — the Head Center
At the crown, the very top of you, is the place this pressure comes in. It is one of two places in the body built to run on pressure, and all it does is ask. A question arrives and the push to answer it arrives in the same breath, so close that the two feel like one thing. This place decides nothing and moves nothing. There is no engine under it, so a thought that lands here is handed downward to be worked on somewhere else. The mistake nearly everyone makes is to take the landing for an order. Rush a question and it gives up its answer badly. Wait on it and it opens. Something arrives that no amount of pushing would reach.

About three people in ten have this place running on its own, and the mind never stops. Questions come all day, whether or not anyone is listening. Their danger is the loop, a question with no answer in sight until they act or turn anxious. What they do have is that they know what they think, even while confused. The other seven have it open. The questions in the room come in and grow louder, so the mind is tossed by whoever spoke last. Asked for their own view, they can go blank. For them the move is to notice whose question it is, because most of the pressure belongs to someone else.

### What completes it — the Channel of Abstraction (64–47)
The pressure at the crown has one road out, and it goes straight down. What completes it is Gate 47, behind the forehead, which takes a flood of pictures and turns them over until a pattern shows. The pair is called the Channel of Abstraction, a mind that works by flashes. A long restless stretch runs with no line through it, and then one morning it clicks. Gate 47 may be in you or in the person beside you, and the channel runs only where both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
One small organ in you lives in the dark and answers to light: the pineal, in the middle of your head. This energy is given to it, and the reason is light. The gland is the size of a grain of rice, and it never sees the day. It learns whether the sun is up from your eyes alone. When the light goes, it lets out the substance of the night, which slows you toward sleep. Nearly everything you will ever understand is made first in the dark of the brain, where you have no way in. This gland is the door out of that dark into the part of you that thinks.

Sit under a bright screen late at night and the gland reads it as day, so the dark in you waits. Dim the room and within an hour or two you feel the slowing begin. You cannot make that happen on purpose.

### Amino acid
The light in you that has not yet shown has a substance, and its name is glycine. Your body makes it, and food brings in more, most of all the jelly a cooled stock sets into. It sits at the gate in you where memory is laid down. That gate will not open until a glycine is in its seat, so no memory of yours was laid down without it. Its kin run on the same substance, one family doing one work.

Turning a dark thing into a light one is the work here, and it goes by colour. Heat the black raw material and it turns white, then yellow, then the red of gold. Light is in every form, yours too, hidden until something lets it show. That showing is what alchemy is. The confusion in you is the black at the start.

## RELATIONS

_Read with UL 63, this is the two banks of one river, the crossing not yet made and the crossing already behind you._

### Pair — UL 63, Adornments of Time (After Completion)
This energy holds you one step short of the thing you can already see, and there it runs out. Adornments of Time is where it goes next, the same river seen from the bank you have already climbed. Open it when the crossing is behind you and the keeping begins. What you reach still has to be tended, or you end up back in the water.

### Inverse — UL 63, Adornments of Time
Turn this energy end for end and what is not yet done becomes what is finished. Adornments of Time meets you three times here, as pair, as inverse and as partner. What reads from this bank as a hard last step reads from the far one as a step completed. The counsel is the same from either shore, so keep tending.

### Programming partner — UL 63, Adornments of Time
This energy leaps, and a leap lands nowhere unless something carries it step by step. Adornments of Time holds what this one lacks, the patient sequence that arrives at truth one step at a time. A mind that doubts every settled answer feeds a mind that cannot tell one thing from another. Open it when the picture is clear and you cannot get it into the world.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Alchemy
This energy turns one state into another, and the turning is slow. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Alchemy. UL 6 turns conflict into peace. UL 40 turns exhaustion into surrender, and UL 47 turns inherited weight into fuel. This one turns confusion into clear seeing, working only with what is already in the pot. Yours is the stage nearest the end, where the colour is about to change.

### Tarot
Four of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, arrive through the ring and the two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — I · The Magician** (Manifestation): The Magician stands at a table with every tool laid out and one hand lifted, the making held one beat before it is called done.
- **Fire (upper) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion): The Emperor rules by knowing what each thing is and where it belongs, which is the sorting asked for here.
- **Fire (upper) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): The Sun is the open day on the far side, where nothing is hidden.
- **Water (lower) — XII · The Hanged Man** (Pause for Perspective): The Hanged Man hangs by one foot and lets the view turn over, because going on will not serve yet.

### Immortals
Fire above carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar tested ten times before he was let through. He wears a sword and drives out whatever haunts a place. Water below carries Li Tie Guai, the healer who came back through his own death with his medicine in a gourd. One found clarity by trial, the other a cure by dying. Between the sword and the gourd, nothing gets forced.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Mercury:** the quick messenger moving between two shores with the word not yet delivered, the right sky for a crossing held open.
- **Hebrew letter — Beth, the House:** the room that gives a work four walls and a roof. Even a step from the bank an unfinished thing needs one, or it scatters.
