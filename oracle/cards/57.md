# UL 57 — Flight of the Tao

## CODE

_Keywords:_ A Hunch You Can Trust · Knowing in the Moment · The Soft Inner Voice · Telling Fear From Instinct · At Ease in Your Body · Your Own Path · The Gentlest Way In

Before you have thought a choice through, your body often leans one way. That lean is intuition, a quiet knowing that speaks in the moment and gives no reasons. When you act on it at once, you move before doubt has had time to speak.

It speaks once and softly, so the mind can talk over it. A worry and a hunch can feel alike in the body. The worry is about what might come, and the hunch is about now. When you cannot tell which is speaking, let the worry run its course before you decide.

Each time you trust it, it grows a little stronger. People soften toward you, and events move more smoothly than your worries predicted. It shows your own way, never anyone else's. When it leads you onto harder ground than you wanted, keep going, because the reason shows later. Followed gently, your hunches become a way through life that only you could have walked.

## ICHING

### Combination
Wind over Wind is the hexagram of getting through by gentleness. Wind is the part of you that goes in softly and keeps going. Here it meets more of itself, so each small effort you make is followed by another. A single gust passes and changes nothing. Wind that keeps blowing one way bends a whole field. This hexagram has long been read as wind following wind, and as a root going into the ground. A root finds the soft places between stones, and your patience works like that. You get where you are going by staying gentle and staying on course.

### Upper trigram — Wind (Xun)
Above, Wind is the effect you have on the people around you. It works by being mild, so nobody feels pushed. It sits on top because this is the side of you that others meet. Wind has long been read as a breeze, and grass bends wherever a breeze keeps blowing. A kind word said again over months changes a room more than one loud speech. On its own, this wind turns with every gust and moves nothing.

### Lower trigram — Wind (Xun)
Below, Wind is the part of you that gets underneath a thing before you have started to think about it. It works out of sight. It lies underneath because it is the ground your life stands on. The same trigram has long been read as wood, a tree whose roots go down between the stones. What you know works under the surface for years before anyone sees it. On its own, this root goes so deep that even you forget what it knows.

### Reading
You are in a situation that force will not move, and gentleness can. What works here is small, done again and again in one direction. You do not need to overturn anything here. Things are corrected a little, then a little more, the way wind shapes a field over a season. The strength of it comes from time. You come to this after a stretch with no place of your own, so going in gently is the only way open.

Have somewhere to go, then walk there step by step on the same path. Do not change course at every gust. Look below the surface, where the matter is really decided, and weigh each move by where you stand. Find someone who has done this before, and put yourself in front of them. The gains will be small, and they will hold.

Your legs do the work now, carrying you forward one steady step at a time. Walk softly, and keep walking.

### Judgement

- It helps to have somewhere to go. It helps to see the great person.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ advancing, then drawing back, then advancing again. → becomes Hexagram 9, Small Taming.
You go forward, then back, then forward again. Nothing out in the world is in doubt; the wavering is in your own mind. A soldier steadies before a march by deciding, and so can you. Decide once, and walk the way you decided.

**Line 2** · _image:_ something hidden under the bed, and many helpers called in. → becomes Hexagram 53, Development.
Something is hidden low, under the bed, and you cannot tell what it is. Go down after it. Call in the people who keep records and the people who read signs, more of them than looks sensible. Asking this openly is not confusion. Search low, ask for help, and it goes well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the same soft pressing, again and again, and shame. → becomes Hexagram 59, Dispersion.
You keep making the same gentle appeal to something that has not moved. Wind that never stops is no longer gentle, and it turns into a strain. The aim is blocked, and that is the shame of it. Stop repeating what failed, and find another way in.

**Line 4** · _image:_ regret gone, and three kinds of game taken in the hunt. → becomes Hexagram 44, Coming to Meet.
The regret is gone, and the hunt comes home with three kinds of game. There is enough for the offering, enough for the guests and enough for the kitchen. Steady, gentle effort has paid off. Take what it brings, and share it three ways.

**Line 5** · _image:_ no good beginning, but a good end; three days before the change, three days after. → becomes Hexagram 18, Work on the Decayed.
It did not begin well, and still it will end well. Hold steady in the middle, and stay willing to yield. Before a change, give three days' notice. After it, take three days to weigh how it landed, and good fortune follows.

**Line 6** · _image:_ still under the bed, and the axe lost. → becomes Hexagram 48, The Well.
You are still down under the bed when the time for staying low has passed. Somewhere down there you have lost the axe you made your decisions with. Softness kept past its moment costs you your means and your say. Come up, stand on your own feet, and be firm while you still can.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Unease · _Gift:_ Intuition · _Siddhi:_ Clarity

### Shadow — Unease
Sit still in a quiet room for a few minutes, and a discomfort starts up underneath with nothing it is about. It is a sense that all is not quite as it should be. It comes with living in a body that will not last, and it drives much of what you want and fear. Once it was a warning that went off only when danger was near. Now the mind picks it up and turns it into worry that never stops. You hum at whatever pitch is played beside you, the way a tuning fork does. Most of the world around you is playing fear. So you build safety out of money and plans, and the unease stays behind them. No amount of thinking ends it, because thinking is what keeps it going.

Turning to face it takes courage, since looking away is easy. It does not leave when you look. Once you can see it, it stops driving you.

### Repressive nature — Hesitant
This person's body knows what is right, down to every cell. A moment later the mind arrives with its doubt and a second opinion, and the knowing goes dark. They cannot bring themselves to commit. They live inside their worries, and the certainty they feel never gets to move them.

### Reactive nature — Impetuous
Here the unease is met with a fast decision, anything to make it stop. The choice comes out of the fear itself, so it brings more turmoil. It is not always wrong, since a life needs some upheaval. What ends the loop is feeling the fear fully before acting.

### Gift — Intuition
Trust is something you can live inside, and you move into it by listening. Intuition is the part of you that already knows the way out of the maze. It is a quiet voice, softer than thought, and nature speaks to you through it. Much of the time you hear it without following it, which is how you learn. The fear stays for a while. Then the knowing grows stronger than the old habits, and your body hums with life.

The mind explores and conquers, while this listens and receives. When the listening leads and the mind follows, you feel at ease in your body, perhaps for the first time. Fear turns out not to be inside you at all. It is a field you can live in, pass through or rise above.

### Siddhi — Clarity
The surprise, when it comes, is how gentle it is. The listening that guided you leads to an edge, and there it leaves you. You wait at that threshold, where the oldest fears live. Then something takes your hand, and you slip through. There is no path after that, because you have arrived. There is no fear, because there is no tomorrow.

You stop humming to whatever is played beside you, and everything goes quiet. What remains is softness at the heart of everything, like wind moving through the top of a tree. The more softly you touch a thing, the more it opens to you. Gentleness turns out to have no weakness in it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 57, Intuitive Clarity
Gate 57 presses you to know, right now, whether you are safe. It works through the senses and takes in the whole moment before thought can weigh it. It picks up a change in a room, or something coming before it has arrived. More than once it has moved you out of harm's way without your deciding anything. The knowing comes whole, with no reason attached, and it comes once.

Because no reason comes with it, a hunch is easy to argue away, especially when it goes against what everyone thinks. There is a second trap. You sense so much that you throw it forward and frighten yourself with a future you have made up. Nobody knows the future. What the pressure is for is timing: knowing when the moment is right, then moving in it instead of second-guessing what you already know.

### Where it lives — the Spleen Center
The Spleen Center sits low on the left side of the body and holds this pressure. It is the oldest awareness you have, the one plants and animals live by. Its job is to keep you alive, so it is quick and quiet. It speaks once, in the moment, and never repeats itself. It is also the centre of time, meeting each moment fresh. Its fears are the fears of survival, and the one that belongs to this gate is the fear of tomorrow.

About fifty-five people in a hundred have this centre defined. For them the hunch is always there. They can rest like a cat dozing in the sun, whose ears still turn at every sound. The body is a reliable guide if they act on it at once. Their trouble is the mind talking over it, or pushing past feeling ill because the body has always coped. The other forty-five have it open. Their bodies are more sensitive, and they often know they are getting ill before the first sign shows. They take in the fears of whoever is in the room and feel them louder. They lose track of time or feel pressed by it. Their work is to learn which fears are their own.

### What completes it — the Integration cluster (10, 20, 34)
This gate reaches three ways at once. Gate 10, in the Identity Center, turns the hunch into how you carry yourself and treat yourself. Gate 20, in the Throat Center, lets you say what you know the moment you know it. Gate 34, in the Sacral Center, gives the hunch the power to act. Wherever one of the three meets gate 57, whether in you or in a person close by, the hunch becomes something lived.

## BODY

### Physiology
Your spleen does its work without ever telling you, and that is why this energy is given to it. It is the one organ in your belly that you cannot feel. It lies under your left ribs, behind the stomach. All of your blood passes through it many times a day. It meets whatever should not be in the blood before anything in you hurts. It has no duct and no nerve that reports to you, so none of this reaches you as a feeling. Taste works in the same instant way, deciding before any thought whether a thing is safe to swallow.

Lay a hand flat on your left side, under the last ribs, and breathe in. You feel nothing there. A healthy spleen is about the size of a fist, and no hand can find it. Your hand is resting on the part of you that knows first and says nothing.

### Amino acid
This energy has a chemistry in your body, and one substance in it is alanine. Your body makes it for itself, though meat, eggs and beans all carry it too. Only one of the twenty pieces your proteins are built from is smaller. If you have had a blood test, you may have seen the letters ALT, which name the enzyme that makes it and takes it apart.

The unease in you came in at conception. Your body was built at the pitch of the world your mother lived in while she carried you. This is the oldest pattern written into you, the one that makes the world feel solid, and it was made to be unlocked. You share this chemistry with three other energies.

## RELATIONS

_UL 58 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: the joy that opens outward, where this one goes softly in._

### Pair — UL 58, Rhythm of Life (The Joyous, Lake)
This energy goes quietly inward and finds its way to the centre of a thing, and it runs out once the way is found. Rhythm of Life is where it goes next: joy that grows by being shared. Open it when a hunch has brought you somewhere good and you want to share what you found. What was found in quiet wants company.

### Inverse — UL 58, Rhythm of Life
Turn this energy end for end and the soft going-in becomes an open, glad meeting. Rhythm of Life is the same gentleness seen from the side that faces outward. Open it when you have been listening inward for a long time and want to be with people.

### Programming partner — UL 51, Unshakable Arrival
This energy knows the way in a soft voice, and it runs out when that voice has been ignored too long. Unshakable Arrival holds what it lacks: the shock that wakes you all at once. The unease of this energy and the agitation of that one feed each other, and at their height they wake together. Open it when the quiet voice has been missed and life has to shout.

### Codon ring — Ring of Matter
This energy lays down the first pattern of your body, in the nine months before birth. It belongs to a family of four, the Ring of Matter, whose theme is a body growing from conception to the age of twenty-one. UL 46, Fountain of Light, carries the first seven years. UL 48, Doorways of the Unknown, carries the second seven. UL 18, Liberation of the Greater, carries the third. This one carries the time in the womb.
