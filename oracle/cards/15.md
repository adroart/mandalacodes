# UL 15 — Ordinary Valiance

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Slowing Down · A Quiet Pull · Courage in Ordinary Days · Your Own Rhythm · Working Without Credit · The Grey Days · Flowering in Its Time

It takes a quiet kind of courage to meet an ordinary day in full, with the same street and the same people. That is where your magnetism lives. It grows as you slow down to the pace of growing things. Without any effort from you, it draws people and what you need. You do the plain work well and let the credit go, and people feel something near you that they cannot name.

Some days go grey and flat, with nothing wrong in them, and you fear that every day will be like this one. Running from it into something new does not help, and neither does sinking into it. Let the grey all the way in, and at some depth it fills with life again.

Trust your own rhythm, slow for a stretch and then fast. Welcome everyone who comes, the drab and the bright alike. Lived this way, an ordinary day turns out to be full.

## ICHING

### Combination
Earth over Mountain is the hexagram of a real strength carried low. Mountain is what is solid in you, the weight of what you can do. Earth is the plain, yielding side you show to people. The yielding side sits on top because people resent a strength that is shown off, and they help one that is kept low. This hexagram has long been read as a mountain inside the earth. Someone who looks ordinary can have a mountain of strength under the surface. You go furthest when you carry your strength without showing it off.

### Upper trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the part of you that gives way to people and makes room for them. It sits on the outside because the outside is where people first find you, and they find the part of you that yields. Earth has long been read as the ground, which holds up everything that stands on it and feeds what grows in it. You hold people up in the same way, and they rarely notice. On its own, it goes along with whatever comes.

### Lower trigram — Mountain (Gen)
Mountain is the part of you that knows where to stop. It is solid and steady, and once it has settled on something it does not move. It lies underneath because what you can really do stays inside, where nobody has to see it. Mountain has long been read as a boundary. A boundary tells you where your ground ends and someone else's begins. On its own, it turns stubborn and will not move when the time comes.

### Reading
You can do more than the people around you know, and this is a time to carry it low. Do the work in full and let the credit go. Treat the people around you as your equals, and share the plain work with them. People resent whoever is full of themselves, and they help whoever keeps low. Carried this way, every step turns out well, from the first to the last.

Weigh what is in front of you. Take some away where there is too much. Add where there is too little, and count your own share too. Then see the thing through, because starting is easy and finishing is the hard part. Keeping low is not the same as being weak. When someone threatens what is fair, stand up for it without taking it personally.

Your body does this through your hands, which work without anyone watching them. Stand with your weight low and your hands open, with nothing to prove.

### Judgement
- Modesty goes through, and grows.
- The one who carries it carries it to the end.

### Image
- A mountain inside the earth: that is modesty.
- So the wise take from what has too much and add to what has too little, weighing each thing and making it even.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ humble, and humble about that; the great river crossed. → becomes Hexagram 36, Darkening of the Light.
You keep low, and you keep quiet about keeping low. That is what gets you across a great river. Nobody sees you as a threat, so nothing rises to stop you. Keeping low is how you get ready for what lies ahead. Get ready, then cross.

**Line 2** · _image:_ humility that has made a sound, the call one bird knows another by. → becomes Hexagram 46, Pushing Upward.
People have noticed your modesty without your saying a word, the way birds know each other by their call. It rings true because it comes from your heart and not from a manner. Where you lack something, follow the one who has it, and say so. Keep it true, and it goes well.

**Line 3** · _image:_ the one who works hardest, and stays lowest. → becomes Hexagram 2, The Receptive.
You do the hard work, see it through, and do not stand on top of what you finished. Everyone turns to you for that. The good in you grows each day while your sense of it shrinks. Carry the work to its end, and the end is good.

**Line 4** · _image:_ humility stirred up and spread in every direction. → becomes Hexagram 62, Small Preponderance.
You have reached what you set out for, and now you stand close to real power. This is when confidence wants to loosen. Nothing here goes against you, as long as your modesty keeps spreading. Be as humble with those above you as with those below. Your place is still new, so keep it low.

**Line 5** · _image:_ not rich, and the neighbours come anyway. → becomes Hexagram 39, Obstruction.
You have no wealth to draw people with, and your neighbours come to you anyway. Your good and theirs are now one thing, and nobody gets on alone. Where someone will not play fair, use a firm hand, fairly and without pride. Stand with your neighbours, and stand against whoever takes more than their share.

**Line 6** · _image:_ humility trumpeted, and the army sent only against your own city. → becomes Hexagram 52, Keeping Still Mountain.
At the top, your modesty has turned into something you announce. The word is there without the thing, and few people follow a word. What you meant to do is not done yet. Use your strength on your own house, not on anyone else's. Set your own place in order first.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dullness · _Gift:_ Magnetism · _Siddhi:_ Florescence

### Shadow — Dullness
A grey day with nothing wrong in it is where this energy sinks lowest. The colour goes out of things. Underneath runs a fear that tomorrow will be the same, and every day after it. That is the fear of an ordinary life. Anger is not dull, and grief is not dull. Dullness is the dead middle, with no charge either way. On a cloudy morning your body changes a little, as everyone's does, and the dullness comes from how you take that change.

A dog can sit on a doorstep for a month and never be bored. Only a person can find a day dull, because only a person has an attitude toward it. Stuck in your head, you reach for anything to fill the day. Stuck in your feelings, you sink, and your energy sinks with you. No trick takes the feeling away. Let the grey in all the way, and at some depth it stops being dull.

### Repressive nature — Empty
Here the grey sinks into emptiness, close to depression. This person has quietly given up, and from outside it looks like acceptance. It is acceptance that stopped partway, stuck on a fear of fear itself. Once they find that fear, the emptiness goes deeper and turns into energy.

### Reactive nature — Extremist
This person rebels against the grey and keeps moving, from one place and one thrill to the next, with no rhythm or purpose. They cannot commit to anything or anyone for long, because a hidden rage drives them on. Once they see the pattern, they can stop and face what they are running from.

### Gift — Magnetism
Magnetism is a pull that grows as you slow down to the pace of the earth. The earth is never in a hurry: leave a city alone for a thousand years and it turns back into forest. The closer you come to that pace, the more you draw people and what you need. This is not charisma, which spills over the surface of a person. It comes from far below, and it may never show in your manner. People feel something around you that they cannot put a name to.

What you feared was draining your life turns out to feed it, the way an empty jar turns out to be full. Your love reaches people at every level of life, the grey ones included. Loving everyone does not mean liking them. It means you can feel what a person is and find something in them worth loving. The grey of the low days becomes colour here.

### Siddhi — Florescence
Florescence is a whole tree bursting into flower at once, in every direction. It comes when the seeking in you finally comes to rest. No effort brings it, and nothing can hurry it. People know it when they see it in someone.

Then the fruit falls back to the ground, and the flowering comes back into ordinary life. The ones it happens to come down off the mountain and become entirely ordinary, even a little strange. They may let you think they are weak or flawed, just to stay close to you. The holiest people are the gardeners, because they know that everything comes at its proper time. You learn the same, and everything in you flowers in the end.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 15, Extremes
Gate 15 is the pressure to live by your own rhythm, and that rhythm swings. You may work hard for weeks and then rest. You may spend time with people and then need to be alone. This is not balance. It is a love of the whole range of life, with room for every kind of person in it. From outside, the swings can look like you cannot follow through. Forced into fixed daily habits, the rhythm jams. Then you blame yourself for keeping hours that are not yours. You may also swing between giving everything away and keeping everything for yourself. Set limits around a stretch of work, go in fully while it lasts, and rest in between. Share from the heart without giving your heart away.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
Gate 15 sits in the Identity Center, in the middle of the chest, which holds who you are and the direction your life takes. It works like a magnet with one pole: it only draws. It pulls in what your life needs, people and work and places. When you trust yourself, you let that pull work. When you do not, you fight what it brings.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They know who they are and where they are going, and they lose heart when they follow someone else's direction. The other four in ten have it open. They take their sense of self from where they are and who they are with. Place matters to them, down to the city they live in and where they sit in a restaurant. They have usually tried many directions, and they grow wise about which ones are true.

### What completes it — the Channel of Rhythm (5–15)
A swing needs a steady beat under it. Gate 5 is your need for meals and sleep at the same hours each day. It sits low in your belly, in the Sacral Center. Joined with Gate 15, it makes the Channel of Rhythm, which lays your wide swings over a fixed beat. Through it the rhythms of nature guide your timing. Gate 5 may be in you or in a person close to you, and the rhythm holds only when both are present.

## BODY

### Physiology
The liver holds this energy in your body, and the reason is direction. It keeps your blood and your energy moving freely, and it sends blood wherever you need it. It also gives a life its plan and the way it faces. When it moves freely, you go through a day at your own pace and in your own direction.

After a meal, the liver stores the sugar your blood does not need yet. Between meals it lets it out a little at a time. So your blood stays at one level, whether you ate an hour ago or ten. Go half a day without food and you keep going, because the liver is paying out what it put by. Whatever pace your day takes, fast or slow, it keeps you level underneath.

### Amino acid
The pressure in you to seek has a substance, and the substance is serine. It is one of the twenty small pieces every protein in you is built from. Your body makes it from what it is already burning, so you never have to eat it. The same substance runs through this energy's kin, and each of them presses you to seek something different.

Here is where that seeking finally comes to an end. All your struggles and your highest moments are written into this chemistry. The seeking in you runs on something you make yourself, and in time it burns itself out.

This energy keeps what you can do low, and it runs out when something in you is ready to be heard. Grand Rising is where it goes next: the enthusiasm that gets a thing out of your head and into the world. Open it when you have kept quiet long enough and something wants to come out. What was held low can then be heard.

### Inverse — UL 16, Grand Rising (Enthusiasm)
Turn this energy end for end and what you kept low comes up out of you like thunder. Grand Rising is the same strength when you let it be heard. Open it when keeping low has started to feel like hiding.

This energy draws people to you only while nothing about you is an act. Internal Treasure holds what it needs: the ease of being at home in your own skin. When you are comfortable with who you are and accept other people's lives as they are, you are close to flowering. Open it when you catch yourself watching how you come across.
