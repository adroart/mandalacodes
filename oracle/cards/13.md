# UL 13 — Universal Crest

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Listening With All of You · Hearing What Goes Unsaid · Trusted With Secrets · Learning From Your Past · Heart Before Your Answer · Hearing Only Bad News · Strangers Becoming Your People

When someone starts telling you what they have kept to themselves for years, you go quiet and listen with all of you. Your whole body takes in what they mean and cannot say. You need time for this, and often a little distance. People sense it, and trust you before they know why.

Some days everything you hear sounds heavy, your own life included. Watch the heavy feeling without arguing with it, and it loses its hold.

Let your heart speak before your thoughts do, and give it quiet to speak in. Listen to your own past like that, and it starts to teach you. What kept repeating becomes something you learned from. The more you listen, the more strangers feel like your own people.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Fire is the hexagram of people joined by one aim. Fire is the clear sight in you that sees each person as they are. Heaven is the aim in you that keeps going, the same from every side. The aim sits above the sight because people who see each other clearly still need something to face together. This hexagram has long been read as fire climbing toward the sky. People who share an aim rise together, and each can still be seen. It also asks you to tell one kind of thing from another. You belong with the people you see clearly and walk the same way with.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the part of you that keeps walking toward something bigger than yourself. It gives the people with you somewhere to go. It sits on top because it is the outer side, what others see all of you doing together. Heaven has long been read as the sky, which moves without tiring and stands over everyone alike. A shared aim works the same way: it belongs to nobody, so it leaves nobody out. On its own, it dries out and burns, an aim chased so hard that nobody can warm themselves near it.

### Lower trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is what you feel toward people before anything is said: a warmth that draws them close, and a light to see them by. It lies underneath because it is the inner side, how you meet each person on your own. Fire has long been read as a flame, whose light lets you tell one thing from another. That is how you know, in a crowd, who is who and where you stand. On its own, it clings to whatever is nearest and burns for a small circle.

### Reading
You are among people who are not your family or your usual crowd, out in the open, and something has to hold you together. Blood will not do it here, and neither will habit. What holds you is an aim you all share, bigger than any one of you. Keep your own gain out of it. A group gathered for one person's profit breaks at the first strain. A group gathered for everyone's good can take on something hard together.

Be the first to give. Help the others toward what they want, and in time you find your own aim reached along the way. Do not leave a friend to walk home alone at night.

Your eyes do the work here. Look at each face as it is, without hurry, and keep your hands open. A company holds when every person in it has been seen.

### Judgement
- Fellowship in the open country, far from the walls. It goes through and grows.
- It is good to cross the great river.
- It goes well for the one who holds to what is true.

### Image
- Heaven, and fire rising under it: fellowship.
- So the wise sort the families by their kind, and see each thing for what it is.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ fellowship at the gate, just stepping out. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
You are at the gate, just stepping out to meet people. Nothing has been promised to anyone yet, and nobody can fault what you do here. Before you are through the door, you already know whom it is right to stand with. Go out openly, with nothing hidden behind you.

**Line 2** · _image:_ fellowship kept to one's own clan. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
You keep company only with your own family and the people who already agree with you. It is warm and it is small, and in time you regret it. Falling in with whoever happens to be near, you become one of them without choosing to. Open the circle to people who are not like you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ weapons hidden in the long grass, a watch kept from the high mound, three years without rising. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
You hide weapons in the long grass and watch from a hill, and for three years you never rise. You want someone who belongs with another, and the other side is too strong. Nobody is harmed, because nobody moves. Force cannot make this bond. Put the weapons away and be content with things as they are.

**Line 4** · _image:_ climbed onto the wall, and the attack not made. → becomes Hexagram 37, The Family.
You climb the wall with the attack ready, and the attack does not come. You are strong enough to force it, and something in you knows it would be wrong. Being stopped is your good fortune. Come down, and let the matter return to order.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the fellowship first weeps and cries out, then laughs; the great army wins, and they meet. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
First you cry out, then you laugh. Something stands between you and the people you belong with, and breaking through takes everything you have. Your heart is straight, so you weep openly and act without doubt. Give it all you have, and the meeting will come with laughter.

**Line 6** · _image:_ fellowship out in the meadow, beyond the town, the aim not yet reached. → becomes Hexagram 49, Revolution.
You are out in the meadow past the last houses, with nobody standing beside you. The great gathering you hoped for has not come, and you have nothing to regret. You can move among people now without being changed by them. Take the peace of this, and keep your aim.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Discord · _Gift:_ Discernment · _Siddhi:_ Empathy

### Shadow — Discord
Discord is hearing only one band of what is going on. An old part of you listens for danger, and on its days the world comes in as bad news, one crisis after another. It does not feel like a mood. It feels like being realistic. Every generation says it the same way: things were simpler once, and people cared more. Good news comes too, and you hear it and cannot believe it. How could so many people be wrong, and only you and a few others be right?

Under it is distrust, of people and then of life itself, as if the story were bound to end badly. Nobody chooses to hear this way. There is too much noise in the head and too much armour round the heart. Under the noise, what you have forgotten is love.

### Repressive nature — Permissive
Some agree with everyone and make a show of listening. People walk over them, and they learn nothing from it. It looks like kindness. Underneath, they refuse to take in either pain or pleasure, and settle for a safety that is not real.

### Reactive nature — Narrow-Minded
Others disagree with you whatever you say. They hold one hard view for life, bitter about what people are and angry at anyone who still hopes. Their fear has become a philosophy. Often their life's work is proving the hopeful wrong.

### Gift — Discernment
Discernment starts when you listen to yourself. The wants still run through your body, and you cannot stop them. Now something in you watches them go by and is not carried off. You see how much of your view of other people was your own mood. Your agenda comes into the light, where you can set it down. From there you can hear other people properly for the first time.

Then your past changes. What looked like a run of accidents shows a pattern, the same few stories every culture tells, and you are living one of them. That brings a hope you feel in your belly. The deeper you listen, the more of what you hear is love.

### Siddhi — Empathy
Empathy is being inside another person, not beside them. Sympathy needs two people. Empathy needs one, and the one is not you. It is a kind of death: the one who was listening goes. What is left is the listening itself, and it can take in any living thing from the inside. First there is a fear to cross, the cold of feeling cut off from everything alive.

To sit with someone who lives here is to be healed by being heard. Every fear you have comes up and is loved until it is gone. You stop being a point and become a place, with every creature in it.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 13, Narrative
Gate 13 is the drive to take in what has been lived, yours and other people's, and keep it until it means something. You give people direction just by listening to them with respect. What they confide is theirs, and keeping it private is part of this drive.

The cost is weight. Other people's stories stay with you, and so does your own past. You can end up living there, telling the old hurt until it is who you are. There can be so much room for others that none is left for you. The story you tell about yourself sets the direction of your life. You can tell it again, truer, on any day you choose.

### Where it lives — the Identity Center
The Identity Center sits in the middle of your chest and holds who you are, what you love and which way your life faces. It does not push you anywhere. It works like a magnet and draws to you the people, work and places your life needs. Stories are part of what it draws. So you never hold a story here at arm's length. One that is not yours can turn your whole life the wrong way, and a true one turns it back.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They know who they are and where they are going, whoever they are with. The stories they keep do not move them off their road. They can forget that others are still finding theirs. The other four in ten have it open. They take on the people and places around them and feel them more strongly. So where they live matters, and even where they sit in a room. For them the question is whether they can feel someone's story completely without becoming it.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Prodigal (13–33)
Gate 33, in the throat, is the drive to step back and go over what has happened before saying anything. With Gate 13 it makes the Channel of the Prodigal, the one who goes away and comes back with the story. It is built for keeping, not talking. It stores what it has witnessed and tells it when the time is right. Its need to be alone rejects no one. The stories you gather are told only when both gates are present, in you or in someone close.

## BODY

### Physiology
The flinch is the reason this energy is given to the amygdala. The amygdala is two clusters of nerve cells the size of almonds, one deep in each side of your head. It hears first: a rough copy of every sound and sight reaches it a moment before the thinking part of you has the whole. It keeps each memory with the feeling that came with it. A voice that once meant hurt is stored with the hurt still on it. A likeness alone can set your heart going before you know why. Someone speaks, and something in you has already answered. Real listening begins when you watch that answer instead of obeying it.

The next time a sudden sound makes you jump, notice that the jump comes before you know what it was. Put a fingertip just in front of the opening of your ear. A few centimetres straight in is the part of you that heard first.

### Amino acid
The heaviness you hear on a bad day has a chemistry in you, and it holds only while you believe it. One substance in that chemistry is glutamine. Your body makes it, mostly in the muscle, and bread is full of it. More of it floats loose in your blood than any other piece your proteins are built from.

It goes first to your gut. The cells lining your gut burn it before anything else, taking it from the blood as it passes. Your gut sorts what you eat: what feeds you is drawn out, and the rest is let go. Listening does the same with a day or a person, down in the belly, below thought. The desire that pulls against your listening runs on the same substance.

## RELATIONS

_Your listening leads on from here: to plenty, to guidance, and to the desire it is weighed against._

### Pair — UL 14, Ancestors Bloom (The Great Possession)
This energy gathers people and hears them, and it runs out once everyone is together with nothing yet to share. Ancestors Bloom is where it goes next: the plenty that work done from love brings in, more than anyone needs. Open it when the people are gathered and the question is what you will make together.

### Inverse — UL 14, Ancestors Bloom
Turn this energy over and people drawing close become a crowd with plenty. In Ancestors Bloom the people who drew close now share what they have. Open it when you have heard everyone and want to know what the gathering is for. Closeness comes first, and plenty follows it.

### Programming partner — UL 7, Essential Nexus
This energy listens to what has been lived, and on its own it can stay facing the past. Essential Nexus holds what it lacks: guidance that faces the future and gathers scattered people into one body. You only guide well when you have listened, and what you hear needs somewhere to lead. Open it when you have heard enough and people are waiting to know which way to go.

### Codon ring — Ring of Purification
This energy listens to the past, and desire is loud enough to drown it out. It belongs to a family of two, the Ring of Purification. The other is UL 30, Sparking the Blaze, the desire that heats a life from inside. Your listening and that desire are weighed against each other, and the ring is named for what the weighing does to both. Open it when wanting keeps winning over what you already know.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards show sides of this energy you may know in yourself.

- **Ring Arcana — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil shows chains nobody notices, here an old story held so long it feels like you.
- **Heaven (upper) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool steps out of the gate with nothing decided, which is how you meet strangers well.
- **Heaven (upper) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress feeds what grows around her, the plenty a gathering gives back.
- **Fire (lower) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): The Emperor sees who stands where, the clear sight you use to choose your company.
- **Fire (lower) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): The Sun shows everyone as they are, which is how it feels to be fully seen and heard.

### Immortals
Heaven above carries Han Xiangzi, the flute player whose music brings life back to things. Fire below carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar whose sword cuts through delusion. The music draws people to you, and the sword shows you which of them is true.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Capricorn:** the earth sign that climbs a long slope. It comes through the Devil, and means the steadiness a shared aim needs.
- **Hebrew letter — Ayin, the Eye:** it comes by the same thin thread, and means seeing what holds you clearly enough to let it go.
