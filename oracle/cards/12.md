# UL 12 — Petals of Freedom

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Telling Real from Fake · The Guarded Heart · Letting People In a Little · Speaking Only When Moved · The Shut Season · Falling in Love · Nothing to Defend

A pure heart is the energy here, with the sense that comes with one, which tells the real from the fake by what your heart does when each is put in front of you. A thing made from the heart lands one way in you, a thing faking it lands another, before you have a word for either. What you cannot fall in love with, you do not trust. It lives most of all in the voice, where what you meant is heard under the words you chose. You can pick the most beautiful words there are, and the tone under them will still tell what you meant. It comes with a guard, built early, which is what most people meet before they meet the heart.

Carried well, it is turned on yourself first. The same honesty that sees through everyone else is pointed at your own holding back, at where you keep people out, at why. What it finds there is a fear that someone could take what is clean in you. Nobody can. What is clean in you is out of everyone's reach, so the flood you have spent a life keeping out can be let in, the feelings, the wounds of other people, all of it, and it turns out not to drown you. Feelings rise, then they fall again, and what is left when they have fallen is that you remember what love is like. The aloneness you were looking for was never aloneness. It was this, your own heart clean, depending on nothing outside you.

The way is slow, a refining that goes on for the whole of a life. The heart opens the way a flower does, a petal at a time, each one let go as the fear behind it proves false. There will be stretches when you have let no one in for a while and cannot say why. The shut is not coldness. Behind it is someone who loves more than they will admit, waiting for it to be safe, and it was never unsafe. The last of the guard goes late, all at once.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Earth: the sky above and the ground beneath, each in the place that looks right for it. Each is moving away from the other, the sky rising, the ground sinking, so that nothing passes between them. The traditional character is a bird up in the sky that will not come down, with an open mouth between its wings, saying no.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Above is the sky, over everything, where it belongs, and it is leaving. The sky is the force that goes on without rest, the father in a family, and here it goes on upward, away from the ground it should have been warming. On the outside of the situation that is how things look: firm, strong, in order, nothing showing. In you it is the part that keeps going and keeps its face, whatever is happening underneath. Its risk is drought, since the same word said another way means to parch. A strength that has lifted clear of the ground dries out whatever it left behind.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Beneath it lies the ground, and the ground is going down. The ground is what carries, the mother in a family, the surface that takes whatever is set on it and asks nothing to be different. Here it settles away from the sky it should have been meeting. On the inside of the situation that is what is happening: everything has given way. In you it is the part that yields, that goes quiet, that takes what comes without a word, and here it has gone quiet somewhere nothing can reach. Its risk is that the waiting runs past its season, until yielding is the only thing it does.

### Reading
Nothing is getting through. What you say does not land, what you do goes nowhere, and the people who should be meeting you have turned away, each to their own side. This is a season, the seventh month, when the growing has finished and the decline has not shown yet. None of it is your doing. The small are coming forward while the worthy step back, and however unfair that is, it is what such a time is like.

What it asks of you is not to force the way open, because no force opens it. Draw in. Spend little, of money and of yourself. Refuse the pay and the honours a blocked time offers, since they go to the wrong people now. Keep to your own kind, the ones who go quiet with you, and let the rest go on without you for a while. Hold your own worth inside, where nobody can see it. Do not let the wrongness of what is happening rot the thing in you that is still clean. It is your turn to move and there is nowhere to move to; that is the whole of it. It is a season, so it ends.

In the body this energy is the head and the belly, the head above, the belly below, each keeping to itself with nothing passing between them. Notice where you are holding yourself now: the face in order, the belly gone quiet under it. Let the head come down a little, and wait the way the ground waits.

- The great departs. The small arrives.

The reed pulled up brings its neighbours with it, roots and all: this is the first hour of the closing, when nothing has set hard yet. You are not alone in it. The ones bound to you come the moment you move. Withdraw with them, hold to what is right, and the closing can still be turned before it is a closing at all.

**Line 2** · _image:_ bearing it and enduring; luck for the small, and the great held back. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
Going along with the blocked time is what the small do, and it pays them. For you it chokes. The world is not at peace, you are uneasy at heart, and the unease is the very thing that will get you through, because it keeps you from being carried off with the crowd. Bear the obstruction as it is, without bending to it, and it becomes your way through.

**Line 3** · _image:_ shame carried folded inside. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
Shame is being carried here, folded away where no one sees it, because the false has been taken for the real and called a plan. The position is wrong and some part of you knows. No one will warn you, since the shame is the warning. Stop hiding from what you can already see, and do the thing that helps, without a thought for how it looks.

**Line 4** · _image:_ acting on the order from above, and no blame; companions share the good. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
Something has begun to move inside the standstill, and not by your will. The order comes from above, from what was always right in you, as complete in the ordinary as in the wise, and it carries no blame. Turn back toward it and the good that was covered over appears. Move only as you are moved, and the ones like you will come to share it.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the standstill giving way, and a great one tied to a clump of mulberry. → becomes Hexagram 35, Progress.
The standstill is giving way, and the one who ends it does well. Still he says, it could fail, it could fail, then ties what he has to a clump of mulberry so it cannot be carried off. Trouble is beaten before it starts, in what the calm lets you overlook. End the block, keep ruin in mind, and fasten what you have recovered to something that holds.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the standstill overturned; first blocked, then joy. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
The standstill is over, and it goes as easily as turning over a hand, because nothing built to block can stand for long. What was shut collapses under its own weight. First the block, then the joy, in that order and no other. Empty out the old thinking with it, then go and find the ones who were scattered.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Vanity · _Gift:_ Discrimination · _Siddhi:_ Purity

### Shadow — Vanity
There is a part of you that believes it is on its own, and loves that. It is not pride, which needs an audience. This is quieter, hiding in what you feel or leave unsaid, and the finer you become the finer it becomes. Under it is one fear, that another person could take away your freedom, reach in and remove something. So you are careful with your heart. People are let in now and then, in small measured amounts, then they leave wanting more. You go on looking for more room and never find enough. The mind is sharp, always able to find the reason the other person falls short. On the surface you hold it together. Underneath, the feeling runs hotter than you let on, and being loved can be the most frightening thing of all.

You cannot think your way out of it, because even the thought that you are getting over it is more of the same. Know that it is there, and leave it. As long as you feel yourself to be separate it keeps pace with you. It lets go late, all at once, of its own accord.

### Repressive nature — Elitist
This person has gone refined. They agree with you out loud while thinking otherwise, or say nothing, staying apart, quietly clearer than everyone in the room, beyond any belief that could hold them. It looks like humility. It keeps them from the one leap they most want, until they see it for what it is.

### Reactive nature — Malicious
Others turn the sharp tongue outward. Feeling put upon, they find the exact word that wounds and say it, with a voice that knows how to reach a person like no one else can. None of it is planned. It is anger, under the anger fear, and it usually ends in their own wreckage.

### Gift — Discrimination
A fake reaches you as a discomfort before any reason for it: someone pretending, or a thing made with no soul in it. The sense is done by feeling, and it cannot be argued away. The same sense tells you what is good for you, what to eat, who to keep. It makes you a critic people are a little afraid of, one who cannot be taken in by anyone selling a dream. It sets you apart, because it will not stay near compromise. It turns into art, music or drama, the work that carries a feeling across to another person whole.

What it is for is love, the human kind, with all its drama and its danger. The people who carry it are moved by feeling all their lives, and they are here to pass it on. Enter the drama unafraid and let it run through you. In time you stop watching love from the side and become it. The voice that comes from that place stirs whoever hears it.

### Siddhi — Purity
Purity is a heart with nothing on it, and everyone has one under everything else. The self you were once fell in love with itself; here, what is highest in you falls in love with you, and the two turn out to be one love with the separation gone. Nothing can touch it. Lock such a person up and their love goes on. Even the worst of people has this at the centre, so there is no evil, only thicker and thinner layers over it.

To arrive here you give up everything you gained, the whole climb, and you come out like a child, still human, with a voice past words. Most who live it live the plainest of lives, unseen, passing quietly, reminding whoever meets them that this exists in a human form. Its colour is white beyond white. The moment you see anything as bad, it is gone.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 12, Caution
Saying what you feel, and not before its time, is the pressure this energy puts on the body. Gate 12 is where it enters: the drive to put a feeling into words, held back by a fine sense of when the words would land and when they would not. You speak when you are moved to, not because a silence needs filling. You take longer than most to answer, because the answer has to come up from what you feel before it is true. When the words come easily, the time is right. When they will not form, that is not failure; it is the signal to wait. Forced out early, they fall flat and leave you doubting them.

From outside the waiting looks like shyness, or like being hard to reach, and people who like speed will say so. The cost is that when you are overwhelmed you go silent, and the silence gets read as coldness. People who carry this all their lives learn that the shyness was never a weakness, only the timing. A word said at its hour weighs more than a hundred said before it.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
This pressure sits in the throat, the Throat Centre, the one place in a person where what is inside becomes something outside, a word or an act. Everything else in you is pushing to get here; it is the town square of the body. It has no drive of its own, so whichever part it is joined to speaks through it. Here the join is to the feelings, deep in the belly, so what comes out of you is how you feel. How you feel comes in a wave that rises and falls over days, which is why the words that were there on one day are not there the next.

In about seven people in ten this centre is switched on, and the voice is the same whoever is in the room, one song, sent out rather than taken in, sometimes bluntly. For someone like that the work is the hour: the voice is always there, and saying it at the wrong time is how it goes unheard. In the other three it is not, so the voice takes its colour from whoever is near and gives it back. For someone like that there is a pressure to talk without knowing what will come out; left quiet until asked, the words arrive on their own and say what the whole room was feeling. The pauses in a voice can carry as much as the sound.

### What completes it — the Channel of Openness (12–22)
The caution in the throat has nothing to say by itself. Its other half is Gate 22, low in the belly, where a feeling waits to be shared; the two make the Channel of Openness: the feeling given words, with the grace to know when a room can take them. The wave in the belly powers the voice, so in the mood the words reach the depths of despair or the heights of joy; out of it, none are forced. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the feeling speaks only where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
Swallowing is the reason this energy is given to the throat. Every time you swallow, the voice box lifts, a small flap folds down over it, the two folds inside it shut, and your breath stops, for about a second, while what you swallowed goes past behind. It happens several hundred times a day, and every time everything in you stands still so that one thing can pass. That is this energy in your flesh. A baby can breathe while it feeds; you lost that when your voice box came down to where words are made, which is the price of speech. When you laugh or cry, the pattern of your breath changes, and your chemistry with it.

Put a finger on the ridge at the front of your throat and swallow. It rises under your finger, and for that second no breath comes. The ridge under your finger is the Adam's apple, named for the man who swallowed what he should not have and never got it down, a mind caught on its own words.

### Amino acid
No substance in you answers to this energy. Nearly everything you are made of is written in small words, each naming one piece, read one after another. Three of the words you are written in name nothing, and this is one of them. Where your body reads it, nothing more is added, and what was being made is let go, whole. In you that is the passage from one state to a higher one, in which your tie to your own past is ended, so that you come out permanently changed. It ends your separate existence too, and the sense of being separate goes with it: crossing your own throat, you are swallowed by life.

Two others in you carry the same stop, and of the three this is the last, the innermost. Each of the three can bring you to a halt; this one comes at the end.

### Tarot
A secret sits at the middle of this card's Arcana. The ring gives one, the High Priestess, keeper of what is hidden. The two trigrams each give their own faces: Heaven above as the Fool and the Empress, the open beginning and the fullness that nourishes; Earth below as the Devil and the World, the binding attachment and the finished whole. A beginning that has not spoken, a silence that keeps its reason, and at the far end a whole that already contains it.

- **Ring Arcana — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): Through the Ring of Secrets, this code carries the High Priestess, who sits at the threshold and keeps what cannot be said aloud. The secret locked in this code is hers, carried in silence until the moment it can be known.
- **Heaven (upper) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Heaven above this hexagram wears the Fool, the one who steps off without knowing where he lands. The rising force that refuses the descent is also the innocence that has not yet spoken its first word.
- **Heaven (upper) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): Heaven also carries the Empress, the fullness that gives and feeds. The creative force overhead, even when it withdraws, holds the abundance that the open season pours out.
- **Earth (lower) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Earth at the root wears the Devil, the bind that holds you to what you cling to. The guarded heart of the standstill, the wall built of fear, is his teaching, the chain that looks like safety.
- **Earth (lower) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): Earth also carries the World, the finished whole, the dance at the end of the cycle. The sinking ground that contains everything is also the completion the long trial moves toward.

### Immortals
Han Xiangzi stands above, the flute player whose music made flowers open out of season, and Lan Cai He below, the wandering singer who carried a flower basket and sang verses about how fast a life goes by. A flute that opens flowers out of season and a wanderer's song about how briefly it all lasts are the two sounds that unseal a shut channel.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — the Moon:** the reflected light that waxes and wanes, that rules the tides and the hidden inward water. Like the standstill, it is the dark half of a cycle that always turns back toward the full.
- **Hebrew letter — Gimel, the Camel:** the long crossing of the desert, memory carrying water between worlds. Its path runs straight up the middle of the Tree, from the crown to the heart, the same abyss the Gene Key calls the throat that must be crossed for higher consciousness to dawn.
