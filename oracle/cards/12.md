# UL 12 — Petals of Freedom

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Telling Real from Fake · Trusting What You Love · Speaking When Moved · Letting People In · Keeping Your Heart Guarded · Falling in Love · A Heart Nothing Can Touch

Someone is talking to you, and under their words you can hear whether they mean them. That is your heart at work, telling the real from the fake before you can say why. What you can fall in love with, you trust. When you speak from your heart, people hear what you feel, and it stirs them.

There are stretches when you let people in a little at a time and keep the rest back. Turn the same honesty on yourself and you will find a fear under it, that someone could take something from you. Nobody can take it, because what is pure in your heart is out of anyone's reach. The feelings you kept out rise and fall again without drowning you.

So let people in, and the world with them. Your heart opens a petal at a time, and each one that opens leaves you freer. The aloneness you have been looking for turns out to be your own heart. From there you can love without holding anything back.

## ICHING

### Combination
Heaven over Earth is the hexagram of a time when nothing passes between you and the people around you. Heaven is the strength in you that stands firm. Earth is the softness in you that takes things in. Here the strength is on the outside and the softness is hidden within, so you look steady while nothing reaches you. This hexagram has long been read as heaven and earth drawing apart. A face kept firm over a heart kept shut is that drawing apart in a person. Around you, the small come forward and the worthy step back. You keep what you are worth inside until the time turns.

### Upper trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the part of you that carries on, day after day, whatever is happening inside. It sits on the outside of this time, because carrying on is what other people see. Heaven has long been read as the sky, which keeps turning whether anyone looks up or not. You keep your hours and your word the same way. On its own, this strength goes on rising and loses touch with the ground it came from.

### Lower trigram — Earth (Kun)
Earth is the part of you that bears what it is given without a word. It sits on the inside of this time, because inside is where you have given way. Earth has long been read as the ground, which carries whatever you lay on it and asks nothing back. You carry what this time sets on you in the same way. On its own, the giving way goes deeper, until you are carrying everything and saying nothing.

### Reading
Nothing is getting through. What you say is not heard, you reach out and no help comes, and the people who should meet you have turned away. The ones who flatter get ahead, and the ones with something real to give step back. This is a season, the end of summer, when the growing has stopped. No one can change it, and none of it is your fault.

Do not push against it. Draw in, and spend little, of your money and of yourself. Turn down the pay and the praise this time offers, because taking them ties you to it. Keep to the few who are like you. Keep what you are worth inside, where no one can make a show of it. Whatever others do, do not let it wear away your own honesty. Learn what you can while you wait, so the hard time is not wasted.

You may feel it as a tightness in the throat and an unease in the chest. Sit with it and keep your word. This time turns, as every season does.

### Judgement
- Standstill. The wrong people hold the way, and it does not favour the one who stays true.
- The great goes. The small comes.

- Heaven and earth do not meet: the image of standstill.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ a reed pulled up, and the roots of its neighbours come with it. → becomes Hexagram 25, Innocence.
Pull up one reed and the others come with it, because their roots are tied together. This is the first hour of the closing, and you do not move alone. The people bound to you move when you do. Hold to what is right now, while little has set, and it can still go well.

**Line 2** · _image:_ bearing it and enduring; luck for the small, and the great held back. → becomes Hexagram 6, Conflict.
Going along with this time pays the ones who flatter. For you it sits uneasy in the chest. That unease is what keeps you from being swept along with the crowd. Bear the block as it is, without bending to it, and you will come through.

**Line 3** · _image:_ shame carried folded inside. → becomes Hexagram 33, Retreat.
You carry shame folded away where no one sees it. The place you stand in is wrong, and part of you already knows. Looking away will not make it go. Face what you can already see, and do what helps, whatever it does to how you look.

**Line 4** · _image:_ acting on the order from above, and no blame; companions share the good. → becomes Hexagram 20, Contemplation.
Something has begun to move in the stillness, and not by your will. Act as your own sense of right directs, and no blame comes. That sense is as whole in ordinary people as in the wise. Follow it, and the ones who feel as you do will come and share in what it brings.

**Line 5** · _image:_ the standstill giving way, and a great one tied to a clump of mulberry. → becomes Hexagram 35, Progress.
The standstill is giving way, and it goes well for the one who ends it. Even now, say to yourself that it could still fail. Tie what you have won to something with deep roots. Keep trouble in mind while things are calm, and it will not catch you.

**Line 6** · _image:_ the standstill overturned; first blocked, then joy. → becomes Hexagram 45, Gathering Together.
The standstill is overturned, and it goes as easily as turning over a hand. Nothing built to block can last. First the block, then the joy. Let the old way of thinking go with it, and go to meet the people you were kept from.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Vanity · _Gift:_ Discrimination · _Siddhi:_ Purity

### Shadow — Vanity
Vanity is the quiet love of being separate, of being the one who is different. It needs no audience, which is what sets it apart from pride. It hides in what you leave unsaid as easily as in what you say. The finer you become, the finer it becomes, until you feel a little purer than the people around you. Your mind is sharp enough to find the reason each person falls short. On the surface you are calm and collected. Underneath, feeling runs hot, and being loved can frighten you more than being alone.

You cannot think your way out of it, because the thought that you are getting over it is more of the same. As long as you feel yourself to be separate, it keeps pace with you. It lets go late, and all at once.

### Repressive nature — Elitist
Held in, it goes underground. This person agrees with you out loud and thinks otherwise, or says nothing at all. They feel clearer than the people around them, above any belief. It passes for calm, and it keeps them from the one leap they most want to make.

### Reactive nature — Malicious
Turned outward, it becomes a sharp tongue. When this person feels wronged, they find the word that hurts and say it, with a voice that knows how to reach anyone. It is rarely planned. Under it is anger, under the anger fear, and it usually ends badly for them.

### Gift — Discrimination
Discrimination is the urge to be better, turned into art. A fake reaches you as a discomfort in the body, from a person with a hidden aim or a song with nothing behind it. Nobody selling a dream can take you in. You know which food and which people are good for you. It sets you apart, because it cannot sit near a compromise.

What it is for is falling in love, the human kind, with all its drama and its danger. You are moved by feeling all your life, and you are here to pass the feeling on. Enter the drama without fear and let it run through you. In time you stop watching love from the side and become it.

### Siddhi — Purity
Purity is a heart with nothing on it, and it lies under everything else in everyone. Lower down, you love yourself as someone set apart. Here, what is highest in you falls in love with you, and the two are one love. Lock such a person up and their love goes on. Even the worst person has this at the centre, so there is no evil, only thicker and thinner layers over it.

To arrive here you give up everything you gained. You come out like a child, still human, with a voice past words. Most who live it live plain lives, unseen, and remind whoever meets them that this can exist in a person. The moment you see anything as bad, it is gone.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 12, Caution
Gate 12 is the drive to put what you feel into words, held by a fine sense of when. You speak when you are moved to, not because a silence needs filling. You take longer than most to answer, because the answer has to come up from what you feel. When the words come easily, the time is right. When they will not come, wait. Forced out early, they fall flat and leave you doubting yourself.

People who like speed may read your pauses as shyness, and your silence when you are overwhelmed as coldness. The shyness is only the timing. You are shy and bold by turns, and when you do speak, your words carry weight.

### Where it lives — the Throat Center
The Throat Center is where what is inside you becomes something outside, a word or an act. Every other part of you is pushing to reach it, which is why it has been called the town square of the body. It has no power of its own, so whichever part it is joined to speaks through it. Gate 12 reaches toward your feelings, so what comes out of you is how you feel.

About seven in ten people have this centre defined. Their voice carries one steady theme, the same whoever is in the room. The other three in ten have it open. They tune their voice to whoever they are with, and can meet anyone in their own tone. Many of them were not listened to as children, and they learn that the right moment is what lets them be heard. For everyone, the pauses in a voice can carry as much as the sound.

### What completes it — the Channel of Openness (12–22)
Gate 12 on its own is the caution. Gate 22 completes it, from the Solar Plexus, where feelings rise and fall. It brings a grace with people and a feel for what they need. Joined, they make the Channel of Openness, your feelings given words with the grace to know the room. It runs on the mood. Some days your words reach from the depths of sorrow to the heights of joy, and some days you want only quiet. Gate 22 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
Swallowing is the reason this energy is given to the throat. Each time you swallow, your voice box lifts and a small flap folds down over it. Inside it, the two folds that make your voice close tight. For about a second your breath stops while what you swallowed passes behind. It happens several hundred times a day: everything in you goes still so that one thing can pass. When you laugh or cry, the rhythm of your breath changes, and your chemistry changes with it.

Put a finger on the ridge at the front of your throat and swallow. It rises under your finger, and for that second no breath comes. The old story calls it the apple caught in Adam's throat, the place where you get stuck on your own words.

### Amino acid
No substance in your body answers to this energy. Your body builds itself by reading a long written message, and three of the words in it name nothing. Where the reading reaches one, nothing more is added. The piece being made is let go whole. This energy carries that stop into your life. It is a passage from one way of being to another. Your hold on your own past ends, and you come out changed for good. At its far end the sense of being separate ends too, and you are taken into life itself.

Two other energies in you carry the same stop, and of the three this is the last.

## RELATIONS

_UL 11 is this card's pair, its inverse and its partner at once: the heart that meets people freely, where this one holds back._

### Pair — UL 11, Sol Star (Peace)
This energy keeps its heart shut until it can trust what is in front of it, and no heart stays shut forever. Sol Star is where it goes next: the time when everything flows and people meet freely. Open it when the guard has come down and you are ready to let the world in. Every standstill turns into its opposite in time.

### Inverse — UL 11, Sol Star (Peace)
Turn this energy upside down and the drawing apart becomes a meeting. Sol Star is the same two forces facing each other instead of turning away. Open it when you have kept to yourself so long that you have forgotten what meeting feels like.

### Programming partner — UL 11, Sol Star
This energy tells the real from the fake, and on its own it has nothing to reach for. Sol Star holds what it lacks, a dream of how things could be. The two need each other, because a dream with no feel for what is real stays a dream. Open it when your standards have left you nothing to hope for.

### Codon ring — Ring of Secrets, inside the Ring of Trials
This energy is the last thing in you to let go, and that is its test. It belongs to a family of three, the Ring of Trials, three tests on the way to knowing yourself. First comes forgetting who you are, in UL 33, Echos of Time. Then comes being pulled about by your senses, in UL 56, Infinite Journey. This one is the last test, the belief that you are separate. Inside the three it forms its own ring, the Ring of Secrets, and what you keep hidden there opens only when your heart does.

### Tarot
Five major tarot cards arrive through the ring and the two trigrams, each one a way you guard or open your heart.

- **Ring Arcana — II · The High Priestess** (Perceive, Listen): The High Priestess keeps what cannot yet be said, the way you keep your heart until you can trust.
- **Heaven (upper) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool steps out with nothing to guard, the freedom waiting on the far side of your caution.
- **Heaven (upper) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress gives freely, which is what your heart does once it opens.
- **Earth (lower) — XV · The Devil** (Attachments): The Devil is the chain that feels like safety, the guard you took on young.
- **Earth (lower) — XXI · The World** (Achievement): The World is the whole you join when you stop standing apart.

### Immortals
Heaven above carries Han Xiangzi, the flute player whose music made flowers open out of season. Earth below carries Lan Cai He, the wandering singer with a basket of flowers, who sang of how quickly a life goes by. One is your voice opening what was shut, and the other is the reminder that there is no time to keep your heart closed.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — the Moon:** the light that wanes and always comes back full, as a shut season in you gives way to an open one.
- **Hebrew letter — Gimel, the Camel:** the animal that carries water across the desert, as you carry what is in you through a dry time until it can be shared.
