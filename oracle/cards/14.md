# UL 14 — Ancestors Bloom

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Work You Love · Born With Plenty · Fire in the Belly · Doing It Your Way · Settling for Less · Sharing What Comes In · Help Comes to You

On the days you have more in you than you know what to do with, an appetite for the work you love takes hold. Your body is at the work before your mind has decided to start. You do it in your own manner, one nobody else could copy. What you bring in is more than you need, and it feeds the people around you.

This appetite fades when you settle, one small time after another, for work you do not want. Each time, something low in you tightens, and you feel a little worse. When you notice it, one choice a day that stays true to yourself is enough to turn it round.

Get clear on what you want your work to serve, even if that takes months. Then start, and let it build speed at its own pace. Work among people, and the right help finds its way to you. This is how the gifts you were born with, handed down through everyone before you, come into bloom.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Heaven is the hexagram of having a great deal and using it well. Heaven is the strength in you that keeps working, day after day, without being asked. Fire is the clear sight in you that tells what is worth keeping from what is not. The clear sight sits on top because so much strength needs a goal to give it shape. This hexagram has long been read as the sun high in the sky. Like that sun, what you have does the most good when it goes out to everyone around you. What you have stays good for as long as you can see what it is for.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is how you meet other people now: bright, easy to see and warm to be near. It sits on the outside because what you have is out in the open, and people gather round it. Fire has long been read as a flame whose light spreads in every direction. Your warmth reaches people the same way, and they come closer to it. On its own, your brightness keeps spreading until it has used up what feeds it.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven is the steady effort under everything you have. It lies underneath because what you hold was built by work done day after day. Heaven has long been read as the sky, which turns the same way every day and never stops. You work in that same way, up and at it again whether or not anyone is watching. On its own, it dries you out, the way sun with no rain parches a field.

### Reading
The harvest is in, and it is large. You have more than you need, and it came from years of steady work as much as from good fortune. The season is right, and for now everything you start goes well.

The danger now is not loss but pride. Plenty tempts you to believe you made it alone. Those who have a great deal should not let themselves feel full. Stay modest, and stay easy for people to reach. Stop what does harm, in yourself first, and let the good in you show. Offer the best of what you have to something larger than your own house.

The belly does the work now. Fill it and keep your mind empty. Take in all that has come without holding on to it in your thoughts. Stand the way a generous host stands, with plenty on the table and the door open.

### Judgement
- Great possession. Supreme success.
- The yielding line holds the high seat, and the strong lines above and below answer it.
- Firm and strong within, bright without, in step with heaven and the season.

- Fire high above heaven. Great possession.
- The one who has much stops what is harmful, lets the good show, and bends to the will of heaven.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ no mingling with harm, the hard ground kept in mind. → becomes Hexagram 50, The Cauldron.
Nothing much is yours yet, and nobody is looking. Keep away from what does harm, and count the thoughts you feed as company too. Remember that the ground is hard and the work repeats. Tend what you have out of sight, and nothing goes wrong.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the big wagon loaded, with somewhere to go. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
A big wagon stands loaded, with somewhere to go. What you have is real now, and you can carry it. Keep the load balanced in the middle, where it will not spoil. Choose where you are heading, and set off.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a prince laying his harvest before the son of heaven. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
A prince brings his harvest as an offering to the king. You have something worth giving, and it belongs to something larger than your own house. A small-minded person cannot do this, because their possessions hold them. Let the best of what you have serve something greater than you.

**Line 4** · _image:_ great means held in check, the drum unanswered. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
The drums beat, and you could drown everyone out. You have strength and you can see clearly, and it is better to lean on neither. Let others go their own way without pressing your views on them. Hold back, and what you have keeps growing.

**Line 5** · _image:_ trust going out and coming back, a dignity that needs no preparing. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
Your word is trusted, and the trust comes back to you. People can reach you, and you still carry yourself with dignity. Getting ready to impress would only show that you doubt yourself. Speak plainly and sincerely, and it stirs the will of others.

**Line 6** · _image:_ help coming down from heaven itself. → becomes Hexagram 34, Great Power.
Help comes down from heaven. Your strength and your clear sight have become one, and nothing you do fails to go well. Heaven helps the one who yields to it, and people help the one they trust. Take the help that comes.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Compromise · _Gift:_ Competence · _Siddhi:_ Bounteousness

### Shadow — Compromise
Settling for second best starts so young that most of us no longer notice we do it. The dream of being great that you had as a child was a real urge to excel. School taught most of us that work means boredom and effort, and by forty the dream has usually gone.

Each small surrender damps the fire in you a little more. Your spine droops, and your energy sinks with it. Not every give costs you. Staying home because someone you love is unwell costs nothing, since love made that choice. Work you dislike can cost nothing too, when it is a step toward your dream. What costs is doing what you do not want because others pressed you to. That becomes a habit, and what you were born with starts to leak away.

### Repressive nature — Impotent
Held in, the fear goes quiet. This person goes along with whatever happens, and it passes for being easygoing. The power meant for their work stays choked up inside them. Even desire fades with it.

### Reactive nature — Enslaved
Let out, the same fear turns into proving. This person works hard at a job that is not theirs, to show they are special. Recognition comes and still does not fill them. Provoke them and their rage shows how unsure they are.

### Gift — Competence
Competence is what someone who loves their work looks like while they do it. Nobody can teach it. It finds the quickest and simplest way past whatever is in the way. It has a style no one else could copy. When it meets a task it has never done, it learns it.

The fire sits in the belly, and it works by drawing rather than pushing. Your work goes like a saw pulled toward the belly: a finer cut for less effort than one shoved away. The heat of it spreads. It turns a small group into a team, all keen on the same thing. If you would rather work alone, look underneath the wish. Usually there is a fear of how large you could be.

### Siddhi — Bounteousness
Bounteousness begins as gratitude for a spark. The ember is tiny and easily lost, and you breathe on it in your hands until it holds. Fed, it grows into a flame and then a fire, until there is a sun inside you. A sun gives itself away all day and is filled again by the giving.

Nothing is taken anymore. Receiving is still here, and receiving only deepens the thanks. The more you give, the more you are given. What was plenty turns out to be fertility, moving through one life into many others.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 14, Power Skills
Gate 14 is a surge low in your body that wants to produce. It drives you to bring in resources, money and the things a life runs on, and much of what it brings is meant for others. It does not run at a steady level. It pulses, strong for a stretch and then gone, and on the strong days you can outwork anyone. Without a direction it turns into restlessness, and people may call you greedy or unable to stop. Pointed at something you want, it becomes stamina.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
The Sacral Center sits under the navel and above the legs, and it is the strongest engine you have. It powers work, sex, raising a child and anything you can keep at for years. It does not decide. It answers, with a yes that lifts or a no that goes flat.

About seven people in ten have it defined, running all the time. For them the task of a life is to find the right work and master it. They are built to fall asleep worn out and wake up charged. Work with no yes behind it drains them. So does sitting idle, the way a car battery dies in the driveway. When they leave the wrong work, their energy comes back within weeks. Others see this engine and borrow it through guilt, and a plain yes or no is its only guard. The other three in ten have it open. They take in the energy around them and can outwork everyone for a while, without knowing when enough is enough. Their gift is the pause.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Beat (2–14)
Gate 2 sits in the chest and knows which way your life is heading. Together with Gate 14 it makes the Channel of the Beat, which joins a direction to the power to follow it. It keeps a rhythm of its own, and it works best when you move to that beat rather than anyone else's. Gate 2 may be in you or in a person close by.

## BODY

### Physiology
The meal is the reason this energy is given to the small intestine. Several metres of it lie folded under your navel. It receives whatever you have eaten and turns it into what the rest of you can use. Its inside is folded, and the folds are covered in tiny fingers. So a tube you could close a hand around has a surface as large as a room's floor. Nearly everything that feeds you crosses that wall into your blood. It keeps nothing for itself, and it passes on what it cannot use.

After a meal more blood goes to it, which is part of why you feel warm and slow for an hour. Its lining is shed and grown again every few days, faster than any other part of you. Between meals a slow wave sweeps it clean, every hour and a half or so. The growl you call hunger is mostly that wave.

### Amino acid
The spark in what you are good at has a chemistry in you, and lysine is part of it. Your body cannot make it, so it has to come in from meat, eggs or beans. Grain alone leaves you short of it. A spark like yours is there from birth, and it has to be fanned before it becomes a fire. In your body, what fans it is what you eat.

On a long walk, hours after your last meal, you are running on stored fat. Lysine goes into the carrier that brings that fat out to be burned. That is the fire in you with something to feed.

## RELATIONS

_These are the cards your plenty moves toward: the people it is for, the style it lacks, and the spark it came from._

### Pair — UL 13, Universal Crest (Fellowship with Men)
This energy brings in more than one life needs, and it loses its point when nobody shares it. Universal Crest is where it goes next: people gathered who listen to one another. Open it when you have plenty and no one to share it with. A harvest is meant for a table of people.

### Inverse — UL 13, Universal Crest (Fellowship with Men)
Turn this energy end for end and plenty becomes company. Seeking harmony with people is what brings a harvest in. Open it when the work goes well and you are doing it alone. The harvest follows the fellowship.

### Programming partner — UL 8, Odyssey of Freedom
This energy works hard and well, and without a style of its own it turns out more of the same. Odyssey of Freedom holds what it lacks: the courage to do a thing exactly your own way. At their lowest the two feed each other, settling for less and a life that looks like everyone else's. Open it when your work is good and could be anyone's.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
This energy pours what it has into work, and the spark behind it was there first. Its one sibling is UL 1, Earth's Breath, which carries creation out of nothing. Your plenty grows out of that creative spark. The spark of genius needs fanning, and freshness is what keeps it alight. Open it when the work you love has gone stale.

### Tarot
Five of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, arrive through the ring and the two trigrams.

- **Ring Arcana — XIV · Temperance** (Flow, Alignment): Temperance pours from one cup into another and spills nothing, as your plenty is meant to flow on.
- **Fire (upper) — XIX · The Sun** (Glory, Clarity): The Sun lights everything without counting, the way your work warms the people near it.
- **Fire (upper) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): The Emperor runs what he has from a steady seat, as you run your own work.
- **Heaven (lower) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Fool steps out before the road is known, as you start before you are sure.
- **Heaven (lower) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): The Empress tends what grows, the plenty in you that feeds others.

### Immortals
Fire above carries Lu Dong Bin, the scholar whose sword cuts through what is false, for seeing clearly what your work is for. Heaven below carries Han Xiangzi, whose flute makes flowers open, for the strength in you that helps things grow.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the archer aiming at a far mark, as your plenty needs somewhere to go.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Peg:** the peg that holds a tent up, as your steady work holds up everything you have.
