# UL 14 — Ancestors Bloom

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Plenty in Your Hands · Work You Love · Giving In a Little at a Time · Fire in the Belly · Somewhere to Go · Giving It Away

Plenty is the energy here, and with it the drive to work at what you love. You were born with more than one life can use: a way of doing things that is yours alone, an appetite to work at it, and a store meant to be spent on that work. When the three are together the days have heat in them, your hands know the next move before you have thought it, and what you make feeds more people than you. It is the energy of a good season, and of the work that brings one in.

Carried well, this energy works from love and never from proof. You do the work in your own manner, you learn what you do not know, and you are not made smaller by help. It cannot be done in hiding, because the heat of it spreads. Give some of what you have before you are sure you can spare it. What is given makes room, and the room fills.

What thins this energy is never a blow. It is a small giving in, the kind nobody would call a choice, and after enough of them the plenty is gone without anyone having taken it. The way back is to spend what is left on something you would do for love, however small, and watch the level come back up. Work is the shape this energy takes in a life, and the work you love is the only kind that keeps it.

## ICHING

### Combination
Fire over Heaven: the sun at the top of the sky, and under it the whole strength of heaven. Everything is lit, everything is supplied, and none of it is fastened to you. In the name, great is a figure standing with arms and legs thrown wide; having is a hand reaching for the moon, held the way the moon is held, for a night then gone.

### Upper trigram — Fire (Li)
Fire is the light that lives on what it burns. It has no body of its own; give it wood and it stands up, take the wood away, it is gone. Up here it is the sun at the height of the day, showing the whole load, a warmth that draws living things to gather. In a family it is the middle daughter, the one everyone sits near. In you it is brightness: you see what is there, you tell one thing from another, and people come toward you while you are lit. Its risk is glare, showing everything until the warmth goes out of it.

### Lower trigram — Heaven (Qian)
Heaven under the fire is the strength that carries the light. It is the sky's own motion, going on without being asked, the same tomorrow as today. In a family it is the father who provides, up early and still at it when the house has gone quiet. In you it is the pull under the load: you can outwork anyone, you do not tire where others tire, and the work is where you are most yourself. Its risk is not knowing how to stop, because a strength that goes on past what was needed burns the one carrying it before it burns anything else.

### Reading
You have a great deal, and the season is good. The sun is high, nothing is hidden, and the strength under you has not tired. None of it was luck; it is the harvest of what you have been, and now you stand beside a full load wondering what a full load is for.

The danger here is not loss. It is fullness. A cup filled to the brim spills at the first step, and once you are full of what you have you begin to believe you made it alone, which is the short road to the one who thinks they have what they have not. What holds all this strength in place is one soft opening at the top, a place in you that is modest and can be reached, which the strong parts answer to without being made to. Keep that opening. Stop what is harmful in yourself before it gets on board, let the good in you show, and give the best of the harvest upward, to whatever is larger than your own house; the one who cannot do that is owned by the load.

In the body this energy is the eye, the part of you that sees in full light what belongs and what does not. Sort what you are holding. The posture for this hour is the one who has much and can still be reached: open at the top, firm underneath, in step with the season. Keep the load moving; it was loaded to be carried out, not kept.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ no mingling with harm, the hard ground kept in mind. → becomes Hexagram 50, The Cauldron.
Not much yet, nobody looking: at the bottom of great possession the danger is the company you keep, outside and in, because a thought you feed is company too. Keep what harms off the cart. Remember that the ground is hard, that the work repeats, and nothing will go wrong. Nurture what you have where nobody sees it, and it stays clean.

**Line 2** · _image:_ the big wagon loaded, with somewhere to go. → becomes Hexagram 30, The Clinging Fire.
The big wagon loaded, with somewhere to go. What you have is real now and the cart can bear it, so long as the load sits in the middle, where it will not spoil. It is time to carry it out where it can be seen. Choose the direction and go, through whatever the road throws up. A full wagon standing is the one thing this line does not allow.

**Line 3** · _image:_ a prince laying his harvest before the son of heaven. → becomes Hexagram 38, Opposition.
A prince laying his harvest before the king. You have standing now, something to give, and the move is to give it upward, into the service of something bigger than your own house. The prince does this and is not made poorer; the small man cannot, because what he owns owns him. Notice which of the two is speaking in you when the offering comes due, and give.

**Line 4** · _image:_ great means held in check, the drum unanswered. → becomes Hexagram 26, Great Taming.
Great means held in check, and a drum beating for you to show them. Strength is here, brightness is here, and the counsel is to lean on neither. Power that does not presume on itself looks, from outside, like less than it is, while inside it keeps growing. Let others go their own way without your principles pressed on them. Keep it in check, and see clearly.

**Line 5** · _image:_ trust going out and coming back, a dignity that needs no preparing. → becomes Hexagram 1, The Creative.
Trust going out, trust coming back, and a dignity that needs no preparing. In the high seat sits the one open place, and every strong part answers to it, since you can be reached. Sincere meets sincere, and what you say kindles the will of others. Let the dignity be the plain kind, since preparing to be impressive shows you doubt you are. Stay open at the top.

**Line 6** · _image:_ help coming down from heaven itself. → becomes Hexagram 34, Great Power.
Help coming down from heaven itself. At the top of great possession the firm has come to match the open, strength has become clear sight, so nothing here fails to benefit. This is the late fruit of keeping the load humble all the way up. Take the help. What the sky helps is the one who yields to it, and what people help is the one they trust.


## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Compromise · _Gift:_ Competence · _Siddhi:_ Bounteousness

### Shadow — Compromise
Everyone has a wagon, and yours was loaded before you were born. Almost nobody drives off with it. The load is let go one small surrender at a time, each too small to look like a decision. The kind that costs you is the inner kind. Staying home because someone you love is unwell costs nothing, since love made that choice; taking the job that pays over the one that pulls costs you every time, and so does letting the thing you wanted as a child slide a little further off each year. The tell is the same every time: you do not feel good, something low down contracts, and the wagon, still standing in the yard, begins to leak from underneath.

Compromise is catching, and it hides in sensible words, being realistic, being a team player. The contraction is a better guide than the words. It only happens because the load is still there to lose, and one act a day that does not sell it is enough to feel the leak stop.

### Repressive nature — Impotent
One answer to the leak is to go slack. This person stops reaching, goes along with whatever the day brings, calls it being easy. Nobody sees weakness; agreeing looks like peace. The power meant to be spent sits choked inside them, and even the body's appetite, in bed as at work, goes dim.

### Reactive nature — Enslaved
Others answer the leak by proving. This person works hard at something they did not choose, to show they count, and the recognition does not land, because what was in the wagon never got out. Push them and the rage is right there. Power that has to prove itself belongs to its audience.

### Gift — Competence
Competence is what working looks like on someone who loves the work. Stop the inner surrenders, one small act a day, and the wagon starts to roll, slowly, at its own pace, picking up speed every day it is not standing. The work goes low, into the belly, and from there it finds the simplest way through anything, the way a saw drawn toward you cuts finer for less effort than one shoved away. It has a manner that is only yours, and it is loose enough to take on a job it has never done, then learn it.

You cannot do it in hiding. The heat of it spreads the way the leak used to, only outward: it turns a few people into a team, gets an idea off the page, and draws the right help toward a wagon that is moving. If you would rather work alone, dig a little, and what you find is usually fear of your own size, dressed as modesty. Get clear on what you want to serve, take months over it if you must, then put your shoulder to the wheel and push.

### Siddhi — Bounteousness
Gratitude is where this ends, and it began as an ember. Out of nothing, a spark the size of a seed, cupped in two hands and breathed on until it caught. Fed, the ember becomes a steady flame, the flame a fire, the fire a sun, and the sun gives because giving is what it is. Whoever was driving the wagon is gone. There is only the unloading, with a strange arithmetic under it: the more goes out, the more arrives, and the more arrives, the more there is to be thankful for.

Nothing in this takes. Receiving is still here, and receiving is a different thing; it feeds the thanks. What you have in great measure turns out to mean fertility, plenty moving through one body into other lives with nobody managing it. Think of a person like that and something in your own week loosens. One life lived this way pulls everyone a little way toward plenty.


## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 14, Power Skills
Through this lens, plenty is a surge low in the body: get up, get working, make more than you need, turn it into something others can live on. Gate 14 is the door it comes through, the drive to generate resources in the plainest sense, money, material, the means to keep going, and what it generates them for is only partly you. It pulses. High for a stretch, then gone, and on the high days you can outwork anyone in the room while on the gone days you cannot say where it went.

From outside this gets called things: greedy, or someone who cannot switch off. From inside it is a pressure to produce that will not leave you alone, and it turns sour only when it has nothing worth producing for. Aimed at something you love it is stamina. Aimed at nothing it is restlessness, which is the same power with no road. People who carry this all their lives learn that the question is never how hard to work, only what for.

### Where it lives — the Sacral Center
This surge comes from the Sacral Centre, the engine under the navel that the body runs its work on: the energy that feeds a family, and any work you can stay with for years. It is the strongest engine you have, and its whole design is to be used. It falls asleep exhausted at night and wakes charged, so long as what it spent itself on was work it had a yes for. Spend it on a no and it drains without coming back, which is how someone with more stamina than anyone ends up burnt out. Leave it unspent and it goes the way of a car nobody drives.

Seven people in ten have this engine running all the time, and for them the job of a life is to find the right work, then master it. When they leave work that was wrong for them, the energy comes back within weeks. Other people see the engine and want to borrow it, by guilt or by asking; the only guard is a plain yes or a plain no, honoured every time. The other three run on borrowed energy: near people whose engine is fixed, an open one takes theirs in, can outwork them all for a while, and does not know when enough is enough until the body says so. Its strength is the pause. When you are producing nothing, look at what you last said yes to.

### What completes it — the Channel of the Beat (2–14)
On its own the surge has no idea where to go; producing for a lifetime is not the same as heading somewhere. Its other half is Gate 2, in the centre of the chest, the part of you that knows which way your life faces and cannot move a thing by itself. Together they make the Channel of the Beat: a road for the engine, legs for the knowing, and a tempo that is nobody else's. Whether the other half sits in you or in someone near you, the pace is only yours where the two meet.


## BODY

### Physiology
Every meal you have ever eaten became you in one place, the small intestine, and the reason this energy lives there is the meal itself. It is several metres of tube folded under your navel, made to receive what is plentiful and turn it into what the rest of you can use. Its wall is folded, the folds covered in fingers, so that inside a tube you could close a hand around is a surface the size of a room's floor, which nearly everything that feeds you crosses. It keeps nothing of yours: what crosses goes into your blood, what cannot cross is passed on down, and even its own lining is made new every few days.

Put an ear to a belly, your own if you lie quiet, and what you hear is liquid moving along this tube. Between meals, every ninety minutes, a slow wave sweeps it clean, and the growl you have called hunger is mostly that wave. You are listening to the part of you that is loaded every day and keeps none of it.

### Amino acid
The fire you work from has a chemistry in you, and the chemistry is lysine. It is built into every protein your body makes. You cannot make it; it comes in from meat, eggs, milk or beans, and grain alone leaves you short of it, which is why a bowl of rice wants beans beside it. What you are good at was in you as a spark at birth, and a spark has to be fanned into action, by a childhood if you were lucky, by you if not.

From lysine your body also makes the carrier that brings stored fat out to be burned, so on a long walk with the meal hours behind you, what you are running on was carried there by it. That is the fire with something to feed: what you took in weeks ago, spent now, on the road.


## RELATIONS

_Read alongside UL 13, these two are the harvest and the people it feeds, one wagon and the crowd it was loaded for._

### Pair — UL 13, Universal Crest (Fellowship with Men)
Turn the hexagram over and the sun that stood above heaven is now the flame under it, out in the open where people gather round. That is the pair, and the two are one picture read from both ends, what one person has in great measure and the company that having was always for. A full wagon with no one to share it is only storage. Fellowship with no one bringing anything is only a meeting. The old books put them side by side and say the harvest is many, and the gathering is close. Read together, one is the food and the other is the table.

### Inverse — UL 13, Universal Crest
The inverse here is the same card as the pair, so there is one bond and it is written above. What the turning itself shows is worth one look. The single open line that sat in the ruler's seat, the reachable softness that held all the strength, drops to the second place, down among the people in the field. The openness that kept your plenty humble is, seen from the other side, the openness that lets strangers in. Same line, other end of the wagon.

### Programming partner — UL 8, Odyssey of Freedom
Across the Gene Keys this code is wired to UL 8, Odyssey of Freedom, and the wiring runs through style. Both ends have a low season, compromise on this side and a settling for the ordinary on that, and each keeps the other stuck: plenty with no style of giving stays a hoard, and style with no plenty behind it stays a pose. Rise together and the wagon is full and carried in a way that could only be yours. Flip every line of this hexagram and you arrive at the same card, so the old books and the new agree on the partner.

### Codon ring — Ring of Fire
Here the spark from UL 1, Earth's Breath, has been fed and has something to burn, and what it throws off is a harvest. Two cards only in this ring, one fire at two moments. Over there is the wanting to make, the flame that starts from nothing and asks for nothing. Here is the making carried all the way out to a loaded wagon and handed round. That card starts things. This one is what starting was for.

- **Fire (upper) — IV · The Emperor** (Dominion, Lead): Fire also carries the Emperor, who has found the seat and governs from it without getting up. The lines keep asking for strength that is held in check rather than leaned on, and here it is, a ruler set over the plenty instead of a mob running off with it.
- **Heaven (lower) — 0 · The Fool** (New Beginning): The Heaven at the root wears the Fool, the step off the edge before the outcome is known. Under all this having is a willingness to begin without proof, which is where every load was first picked up.
- **Heaven (lower) — III · The Empress** (Abundance, Nurture): Heaven also carries the Empress, the field that turns seed into harvest. The force below becomes the plenty above by way of her, force made fruitful rather than stored, which is why the old name for this hexagram is also the word for harvest.

### Immortals
Two of the eight are here, one for each trigram. Over the Fire sits Lu Dong Bin, the scholar who carries a sword for cutting through what is false and who is said to have refused to ascend until he had helped the people below him. Under the Heaven is Han Xiangzi, whose flute was said to make flowers open out of season and things grow where they should not yet. A blade to prune the load, and a tune to bring it into bloom.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Sagittarius:** the mutable fire that aims past what it can see, the arrow loosed at a far mark. Plenty with a direction, which is the one thing a full wagon needs.
- **Hebrew letter — Samech, the Prop:** the peg that holds the tent up, the staff a climber leans on. On the Tree the path runs from the heart down to the foundation, Tiphareth to Yesod, and the letter says that what rises high needs something under it to lean on, or it comes down.
