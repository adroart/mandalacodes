# UL 58 — Rhythm of Life

## CODE

_Keywords:_ Glad for No Reason · Joy That Wants Company · Making Things Better · Kindness Nobody Asked For · Laughing at Yourself · Restless for More · Chasing Tomorrow

Some mornings you wake up glad to be alive, for no reason you could name. You want to tell someone, so you call a friend, and your good mood spreads to the people near you. You see how the things in front of you could be a little better, and making them better is a pleasure.

Joy has its own rhythm, and on some days it goes quiet. A restlessness takes its place, a feeling that life should be more than it is. Chasing the next plan or purchase will not bring the joy back any sooner. Let the restlessness be, and the gladness returns on its own.

Do something kind that nobody asked for, and notice how much more alive you feel. When you see what could be better, wait until someone asks, and your help will be welcome. Shared this way, joy comes back to you as more life, and the people around you feel it too.

## ICHING

### Combination
Lake over Lake is the hexagram of joy that is shared. Lake is the joy in you that you cannot hide. Here it meets the same joy in someone else. A lake on its own dries down in the sun. Two lakes keep each other full. That is why its image is friends gathered to talk a thing through. So the joy in you wants to be spoken. Your joy grows only when someone else has it too.

### Upper trigram — Lake (Dui)
Above, Lake is the joy you show. It is in your face and your voice, and it makes you easy to talk to. It sits on top because it is the side of you other people meet first. Lake has long been read as open water where people and animals come to drink. When you are glad, people come to you the same way, and the talk runs easily. On its own, the joy you show is only a pleasant surface.

### Lower trigram — Lake (Dui)
Below, Lake is the joy you feel before anyone sees it. It is the good mood you woke in, before anything had happened to be glad about. It sits underneath because a smile is only as real as what is felt behind it. Lake has long been read as the low ground where every stream runs. When you are at ease inside, what is good in the day finds its way to you. On its own, this joy is quiet and content, and it keeps to itself.

### Reading
Things are going well, and it is a time to enjoy them with other people. The work is done or on its way, and a celebration is in order. Gather your friends, talk over what each of you has learned, and practise it together. What you learn alone stays thin. Talked through with a friend, it deepens for both of you.

Stay firm inside while you are warm outside. Win people with friendliness, never by pressure. Honest joy shows in your face, and people trust it. Joy put on to draw people in turns sour. Be a little careful whom you open to, since some who come near your good mood want something from it.

When you lead people with joy, they forget how tired they are. When you face a hard thing beside them with joy, they forget their fear. Your mouth is where this joy shows first, in talk, laughter and food. Notice whether what you said today had any pleasure in it, and spend your talk and your meals with people who are good for you.

### Judgement
- Joy brings success.
- Staying firm and true is what makes it last.

### Image
- Two lakes, one resting on the other, each keeping the other full.
- So the wise meet with friends to talk things over and practise them together.

### Moving lines

**Line 1** · _image:_ joy at peace with itself, needing no one to agree. → becomes Hexagram 47, Oppression.
You are glad and at peace with yourself, and you need no one to agree. Joy like this comes without effort and copies nobody. You want nothing from anyone, so nothing you do is in doubt. Keep your joy this simple.

**Line 2** · _image:_ honest joy, and the regret goes out of it. → becomes Hexagram 17, Following.
Your joy is honest, and the regret you were carrying goes. What you mean inside shows on the outside, so people trust your aim. Enjoy what is real, never what only flatters you.

**Line 3** · _image:_ pleasure sent for, brought in from outside. → becomes Hexagram 43, Breakthrough.
You are looking outside yourself for something to cheer you up. Company kept for the lift it gives leaves you as empty as before. The people you used for it feel used. Joy that lasts comes from inside you, so stop reaching out for it.

**Line 4** · _image:_ two pleasures weighed, and no peace until one is let go. → becomes Hexagram 60, Limitation.
You are torn between two pleasures and cannot settle. Even at a party a sadness sits in you while the choice is put off. Look at what pulls you toward the lesser one. Then choose quickly and firmly, and the joy comes back.

**Line 5** · _image:_ trust given to someone who is wearing you down. → becomes Hexagram 54, The Marrying Maiden.
You have trusted someone who is wearing you down, and you do not see it. When you like being agreed with, you stop hearing honest words. Honest care can still help someone who has lost their way. Keep your eyes open to who is near you and what they take.

**Line 6** · _image:_ charm used to draw people after you. → becomes Hexagram 10, Treading.
You are drawing people after you with charm and clever words. If there is nothing true behind the charm, they leave no happier than before. Before you lead anyone toward joy, make sure you have some yourself.

## KEYS

_Shadow:_ Dissatisfaction · _Gift:_ Vitality · _Siddhi:_ Bliss

### Shadow — Dissatisfaction
Under most lives runs a restlessness with no cause. It is not sadness or worry, which have a reason. It is only the feeling that this is not quite it yet, always pointed at tomorrow. Picture a lake whose surface never settles, and you have what it is like inside. Your mind takes it as an order to fix something, so you improve the house, the job or yourself. Each change calms the water for a day, then the restlessness stirs it again. When the world runs out of things to fix, the search turns inward, to a practice or a teacher who promises peace later. That is the same search in different clothes.

The restlessness will not let you rest until every answer has been tried. The emptiness left then is the first honest thing it gives you. Everyone carries this same ache, the ache to come home, and there is something beautiful in that.

### Repressive nature — None
Nobody can hold this energy down. It is the life in your body, and a body cannot refuse its own life. Push the restlessness away and it comes back as the next thing to fix. So there is no quiet version of this shadow.

### Reactive nature — Interfering
This person meddles with life. They once felt joy arrive by itself, so they try to make it happen again, pushing at things that were already moving. Every push brings more discomfort, and that calls for another push.

### Gift — Vitality
Aliveness wells up once you stop chasing a better tomorrow. The force that poured out into the future turns back into your body, and the water starts to move freely. You are happy for no reason. Your thoughts slow, with more space between them, and what happens to you matters less. Even an old or tired body feels younger for it.

Loss is part of this joy. The people you have lost stay part of you, and your life is fuller for them. The wish to make things better is still here, but it no longer helps in order to ease your own ache. It feels where life is stuck in something and frees it without forcing. Walk into a room with this alive in you, and the room moves more easily.

### Siddhi — Bliss
Bliss is the lake gone still. Your seeking turns so far inward that it comes back to where it began. Something lets go at the very bottom, and you no longer feel a centre inside you. Nobody stirs the water any more, so it stays still. Awareness comes in like a flood and wipes you clean in less than a breath. Your gaze goes soft, your heart fills past what it can hold, and it shows in your face.

You cannot reach this by seeking, though seeking usually comes first. Look at your own restlessness until you can laugh at it. Then you see the joy was inside the ache all along, as the fruit is inside the seed.

## DESIGN

### The drive — Gate 58, Joy
Gate 58 is the drive to make life better because being alive is good. You feel it low in your body, a lively pressure that sees how whatever is in front of you could be more alive. For you, joy and improving things go together. Practice feeds it: you do the same thing again and again because the doing is a pleasure, and your skill grows.

It has two costs. Offered before anyone asks, your idea of what could be better is heard as criticism. When it is refused, it feels as if you were refused. The other cost runs inward. If you were taught that pleasure must be earned, you ration what you love and call it discipline. The joy was never a distraction from the work, since it is what drives it.

### Where it lives — the Root Center
The Root Center sits at the base of the body and holds the pressure to act. It does not decide. It presses, and it presses in pulses. When the pulse is on, you can start anything. When it is off, you cannot make yourself do even what you know you should. The key to this centre is timing: rest in the pause, and do not push when the push is not there.

About six people in ten have this centre defined. They have steady access to the pressure and can bear a great deal of it, but they risk burning out. The other four in ten have it open, and they take in the pressure of the people around them and feel it louder. Someone says the kitchen needs cleaning, and they are already at the sink. The pressure is never theirs, so each task done only brings the next. Felt without being obeyed, this pressure becomes the hum that draws a hummingbird to a flower.

### What completes it — the Channel of Judgment (18–58)
Joy knows something could be better without knowing exactly what. Gate 18, in the Spleen Center, sees the flaw. Together they make the Channel of Judgment, which improves whatever is in front of you so that people have more joy. Its corrections are welcomed when someone has asked for them. Gate 18 may be in you or in someone close to you.

## BODY

### Physiology
Fear takes hold first at the perineum, and that is why this energy is given to it. It is the small sling of muscle at the floor of your body, between the legs. When you are afraid, it tightens before you know it, and the grip climbs the spine into your jaw. Held tight, it puts you on automatic, doing the next thing without being there to choose it. The restless feeling that life should be more can live here for years, as a floor held tight and a breath kept high.

Laugh, and the floor tightens a moment before the laugh comes. It is the same muscle you use to stop the flow of urine. Squeeze it now and notice your breath rise. Let it go, and your breath drops on its own, with your shoulders after it.

### Amino acid
The restlessness in you has a chemistry, and one substance in it is serine. It is one of the twenty pieces every protein in you is built from, and there is plenty of it in meat, eggs and beans. When you eat, the juices in your gut cut the food into those twenty pieces, and serine is part of what does the cutting.

You share this substance with a family of pressures, each driving you to look for an end to your longing. That longing is fuel. As it falls away, the life in you runs clearer. Even bliss is chemistry: a release in the brain, felt as joy with no cause.

## RELATIONS

_UL 57 is this card's pair and its inverse at once: joy opens people in plain sight, and the wind reaches them unseen._

### Pair — UL 57, Flight of the Tao (The Gentle Wind)
This energy is joy spoken out loud, and it runs out where talking cannot reach someone. Flight of the Tao is where it goes next: a hunch that finds its way quietly into what words cannot open. Open it when your good mood has been shared and something still needs to go deeper. Joy opens the door, and gentleness goes the rest of the way.

### Inverse — UL 57, Flight of the Tao (The Gentle Wind)
Turn this energy over and what shows becomes what is hidden. Flight of the Tao works quietly, where this energy is out in the open. Open it when being seen has worn you out and you need to trust what you cannot say.

### Programming partner — UL 52, Timeless Blossom
This energy is restless to make things better, and it keeps moving. Timeless Blossom holds what it lacks: the stillness that comes at the end of a cycle. Joy settles only when it has somewhere still to rest. Open it when your good mood keeps running after the next thing.

### Codon ring — Codon Ring of Seeking
This energy is one of six pressures in the Ring of Seeking, each keeping you looking for more until it turns you inward. UL 15, Ordinary Valiance, is the pull people feel toward you when you slow down. UL 39, Nobel Spark, is the push in you that stirs what has gone still. UL 52, Timeless Blossom, is the restraint that lets you hold pressure without acting on it. UL 53, Creation Oscillation, is your urge to begin. UL 54, Everlasting Bounty, is your wish to rise. This one is your joy in being alive.

### Tarot
Three of the tarot's major cards, its Arcana, show your joy from three sides.

- **Ring Arcana — IX · The Hermit** (Wisdom, Right Path): The Hermit walks alone with a lamp, the seeker in you who finally looks inward for what the world could not give.
- **Lake (both) — VI · The Lovers** (Choice, Union): The Lovers are two people who choose each other, the way your joy grows once someone shares it.
- **Lake (both) — XVII · The Star** (Hope, Healing): The Star pours water freely under an open sky, the way your joy comes back when you give it away.

### Immortals
Zhong Li Quan, the one Immortal for Lake above and below, carries a fan said to bring the dead back to life. When you share your joy, it wakes up the people around you who had gone flat.

### Deeper correlation
- **Sky — Virgo:** the earth sign that sorts and refines, the part of you that wants whatever is in front of you a little better.
- **Hebrew letter — Yod, the Hand:** the smallest letter, a single point, the way one small kindness starts your joy moving outward.
