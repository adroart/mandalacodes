# The card sheet

Filled while reading and writing, per the six steps in
[`CARD-PASS.md`](CARD-PASS.md). Copy the block to `oracle/worksheets/NN.md`.
Every `-` line carries text, and the headings stay exactly as written:
`npm run card:gate -- NN` reads them.

It replaced a four-part worksheet on 2026-09-23. That worksheet wrote the card
down to one sentence first and then asked for 26 quoted reader checks. The
checks caught nothing, and the one sentence made every section say the same
thing (Adrian, reading card 64: "they're all saying the exact same thing").
