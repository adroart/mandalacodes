Against 21: 21 takes charge where others stall, a will over people; 34 is the body's strength answering a thing, with no one to command. Against 39: 39 is pressure pressing on what has gone still; 34's hard stretch is the opposite, pushing at what will not move. The old opening line ("Strength lies quiet in you until something real calls for it, then pours the whole of itself in") had the right energy but a picture for a verb (pours the whole of itself in) and shared "until ... then" with 33 and 49; it goes.
