## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 07 gathers scattered people into one body by listening; 08 is the freedom of being yourself, which finds the ones it belongs with; 13 is a quiet where strangers set down what they have told no one; 14 makes more than you need at the work you love, and the rest feeds other people; 21 takes charge where others stall and carries what follows; 31 turns people toward whoever says what they already felt; 37 gathers a few people around one table and keeps them there; 50 knows what will feed the people near you.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): Goodwill that lets a large crowd share what it has so that everyone is fed, and that spreads when whoever holds the power trusts others with it.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): goodwill ("I feel goodwill toward them"). The Gene Keys chapter says "it is this fractal energy of goodwill that has the real power, not profitability itself", that it "reaches outwards in ever-increasing spirals", and that trusting "the energy of goodwill above all else" changes how money moves through a life (gene-key-45.md). No other card's entrance names it.
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): people gathered around something they all need, and it has to go round. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24): human beings have to eat, and civilisation was born out of gathering together around food (gene-key-45.md); the gate's work is to share resources, material and energetic, for the greater good (qw-gate-45-distribution.md, gate-45.md); the thyroid gathers one thing out of what everyone eats and makes what the whole body runs on (body/organs/thyroid.md); and, as one thread among these, people assembling in thick clusters like grass (oracle-45-clustering.md), "when one accords with others, they are joyful; so it is called gathering" (cleary-taoist-45-gathering-together.md). Test: "goodwill lives here" draws no "why", because what there is goes round a crowd only where fear of not having enough has given way to goodwill. The title, Tribal Tapestry, is worked into CODE once, where it fits: each person adds their own thread and the crowd is woven into one people.

Checked against 37, the closest: 37 is warmth that keeps a few people around one table; 45 is a crowd far larger than a family, and its work is sharing out what there is. Checked against 07: 07 gathers by guidance and listening; 45 gathers by sharing and holds by handing power over. Checked against 21, its channel partner: 21 takes hold of the resources and manages them; 45 shares them out. Checked against 14: 14 makes the surplus; 45 sees that it goes round. The old entrance line ("A pull gathers people around what there is") used "pull", already on 19, 24 and 42, and put two ideas in one sentence; it goes.
