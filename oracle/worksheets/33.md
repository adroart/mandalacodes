## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 13 is the quiet where strangers set down what they have told no one (the listener, gate 13, this card's channel partner); 17 senses where something is heading long before it gets there; 24 takes you back to where a thing began so it can start again; 52 goes still at the end of a cycle and holds until the next is ready; 56 takes the world in and hands it back as a story; 12 tells real from fake by what the heart does; 42 carries each thing to its end so the next has room.

- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): the need to be on your own ("I need some time to myself"); the Gene Keys gift is Mindfulness, and the reading names it once as what is alive in you.
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): after anything big, you need time on your own to look back at it. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24): gate 33 steps back from the world to reflect and then returns to share (human-design/gates/gate-33.md); the Gene Keys talk says it is about pausing in the journey, having a cup of tea, drawing sustenance from the past and reflecting on where we have just come from (gk-64ways-33-retreat.md); the story shared when the time is right (qw-gate-33-retelling.md); the channel witnesses, remembers and withdraws for reflection (channel-13-33.md); and, as one thread among these, withdrawing at the end of an active involvement (oracle-33-retiring.md). The title, Echos of Time, is not forced in; the reading's looking back at what just happened is the echo, and the reader was asked whether the name fits.

Checked against 13: 13 hears other people's stories; 33 goes over its own and tells it later. Against 24: 24 goes back to begin again; 33 goes back to understand and keep the best. Against 56: 56 takes in the new and tells it as a story; 33 looks at what has already passed. Against 52: 52 is stillness waiting for the next cycle; 33 is active looking back. Against 17: 17 looks ahead. The old opening line ("Hindsight draws you off alone until what happened makes sense, then sends you back to tell it") had hindsight as its subject, which no one says they feel, and three verbs in one sentence; it goes.
