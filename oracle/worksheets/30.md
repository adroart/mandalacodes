- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 41 pictures a better life than is here and wants to feel it (Gate 41 is this card's channel partner); 35 looks past whatever you hold and meets an ordinary morning as new; 16 gets a thing out of your head and into the world before you feel ready; 28 asks of every day whether it mattered; 29 goes all the way in before you can see where it leads; 55 swells at a life's height so what you make comes out of plenty; 36 drops in the stomach and opens the heart past where the mind stops.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): A heat of wanting that rises in the stomach before any reason, lights a person up and warms the people near them, and burns well only when it is felt in full and followed only where it is worth the fuel.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): passion ("I feel passion for it"), Human Design's own name for Gate 30 (qw-gate-30-passion.md) and the Gene Keys' word for desire allowed to burn ("desire equates to fire and passion", gene-key-30.md); the Gift, Lightness, is how it is carried and closes CODE.
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): something catches you and you want it, and you feel the want in your stomach before you have a reason. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24: the card's name is his artwork title, the I Ching only feeds it): desire is genetic hunger with a deep connection to the need to eat, a leaning outward from the centre (gene-key-30.md; gk-64ways-30-the-clinging-fire.md: "we see something and we want it; we weren't looking for it"); Gate 30 is the desire to feel, the most intense gate, in the centre where passion is made (center-solar-plexus.md; qw-gate-30-passion.md); the pit of the stomach is where the body feels a want before it has decided anything (body/organs/solar-plexus.md); and, as one thread among these, a fire has no shape of its own and holds to what it burns (practical-30-the-spark.md; cleary-buddhist-30-the-clinging-fire.md). Test: "passion lives here" draws no "why": the want felt in the stomach before any reason is what passion is. The title, Sparking the Blaze, is worked into CODE once, where it fits: "A spark is enough to set you blazing."

Checked against 41, the channel partner: 41 pictures the experience before it is had; 30 is the heat felt in the body now, which 41's picture needs in order to catch fire. Against 35: 35 wants the next new thing and looks past what it holds; 30 may hold one thing for years if it is worth the fuel. Against 16: 16 pushes a thing into the world; 30 is a heat that can be felt without being acted on. Against 36: 36 is the drop in the stomach that opens the heart; 30 is the pang of wanting in the same place. Against 29: 29 commits the whole of you to one road; 30 burns toward many and has to choose. The old opening line ("Desire was in you before you had anything to want") named the energy by its shadow; it goes.
