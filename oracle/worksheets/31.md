- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 07 gathers scattered people and knows which way they should go, by listening; 13 is the quiet where strangers set down what they have told no one; 17 senses where something is heading and speaks when asked; 23 picks the one thing that matters out of the tangle and says it; 45 gathers people around what there is and says how it will be shared; 61 moves people by a sureness that needs no proof; 62 gets the small word exactly right and stops.

Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24): the gift becomes the voice of the collective and says what is coming before others have words for it (gene-key-31.md); the open throat takes in every voice in the room and speaks the unspoken, the voice of the room (center-throat.md, 28-activation-throat-center.md); Gate 31 is the voice of the people and must be called (gate-31.md, center-throat.md on 7-31); the throat is where the breath taken from the room goes back into it with your shape on it (body/organs/throat.md); and, as one thread among these, the feeling that passes between two before either moves (oracle-31-hexagram-31.md). Test: "influence lives here" draws no "why": whoever names what the room feels is the one the room turns to. The title, Theater of Truth, is not forced into the prose: the reader is asked why the energy belongs to it, and the card's whole ground (a true thing said in front of everyone, and the fall in front of everyone) answers it.

Checked against 17: 17 senses where a thing is heading and waits to be asked for its view; 31 says what the group already feels, not where it goes. Against 07: 7 knows the direction and gathers by listening; 31 is the voice that direction needs, the channel partner, and has no direction of its own. Against 23: 23 finds the one thing in a tangle; 31 finds the thing a room shares. Against 61: 61 moves people by sureness; 31 moves them by saying their own feeling back. Against 62: 62 is the exact word; 31 is the word that turns a room. The old opening line ("Influence passes between people and turns them toward whoever says what they already felt") had a verb nobody says ("influence passes") and "whoever"; it goes.
