## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 09 spends a strength as small steps toward one wanted thing; 11 is a dream of how things could be that arranges every choice; 14 is an appetite for the work you love that makes more than you need and feeds others; 21 is a will that takes charge where others stall; 26 is a drive to make things happen that grows stronger held in; 35 is a hunger for life that meets an ordinary morning as new; 41 is a hunger for a better life that pictures more than is here; 53 is the urge to start something new.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): The wish to rise from a low place where others already stand higher, which draws people when it is carried with trust and sends what it gains on to the ones below.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): ambition ("I'm ambitious"), Human Design's name for the gate (human-design/gates/gate-54.md), which the Gene Keys chapter places between greed and aspiration (gene-key-54.md).
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in the hexagram's plain words; CODE opens on it): starting low, in a place where others got there first, and wanting to rise. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram: the drive to elevate your status and move up in life (human-design/gates/gate-54.md); the pressure that courts recognition from those higher up in the hierarchy (gene-key-54.md); "at the moment you may find yourself in a position of low status" and "a change has come, beyond your control" (practical-54-the-marrying-maiden.md); the tailbone, the bottom rung where every climb up the back begins (body/organs/tailbone.md). Test: "ambition lives here" draws no "why", because starting at the bottom is exactly where the wish to rise is felt. The title, Everlasting Bounty, is worked into CODE once, where the source's flow of prosperity fits it: "Keep what you gain moving between people, and the bounty keeps coming back."

Checked against 14: 14 makes more than it needs from work it loves; 54 climbs from a low place and passes on what it gains. Against 41: 41 pictures a better life and wants to feel it; 54 wants a higher place and knows how the ladder is built. Against 53, its pair: 53 starts something new; 54 comes into something already arranged. The old opening line ("The drive to rise above the place you were given built the cities") carried the shadow's claim about civilisation and no person; it goes.
