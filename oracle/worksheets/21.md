## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 07 gathers scattered people into one body and leads by listening; 18 sees the flaw first and the hands go to mend it; 26 is a drive that grows stronger the longer it is held in; 31 turns people toward whoever says what they already felt; 34 lies quiet until something real calls, then pours the whole of itself in; 40 carries a life through and fills again only when you stop; 45 gathers people around what there is and says out loud how it will be shared; 51 sends you first, off the edge of what anyone has done.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): The will that steps in and decides when a thing is stuck, whose authority is handed to it by the people who trust it and grows as it lets go of the need to be in charge.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): authority ("I felt my authority", "she has natural authority"), the gift, which the chapter says is given by others and never asserted (gene-key-21.md); Human Design names the same drive the will (center-will.md).
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): something is stuck and everyone is waiting for someone to decide. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram: the chapter's pact that one person becomes the decision maker for the group (gene-key-21.md); the talk's taking action and being decisive, with the shadow as being indecisive, a follower (gk-64ways-21-biting-through.md); the channel's someone at the helm who makes the hard decisions (center-will.md, channel-21-45.md); and, as one thread among these, a tenacious obstacle that has to be dealt with decisively (oracle-21-gnawing-and-biting.md, legge-21-biting-through.md). Test: "authority lives here" draws no "why", because a stuck moment with no one deciding is exactly where authority is felt or missed. The title, Beyond Binary, is Adrian's; the sources give it a meaning (surrender makes authority submissive and submission authoritative; the one in charge and the one who serves work alongside each other) and CODE carries it without the word, in its last sentence: "no one is above you or below you".

Checked against 45, its channel partner: 45 gathers the group and shares out what there is; 21 decides and manages. Against 07: 07 leads by listening to the group; 21 takes the decision on itself. Against 34: 34 pours all its strength in at once; 21's strength comes from loosening its grip. Against 51, its ring sibling in the same centre: 51 goes first alone; 21 decides for others. The old opening line ("The will in you takes charge where others stall and carries the whole of what follows") named the will where people say they feel authority, and "takes charge" pulled the card toward the grip; it goes.
