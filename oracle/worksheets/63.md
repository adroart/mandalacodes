# Card 63 worksheet

## 1. The energy, whole

- Shadow, gift, siddhi (from frontmatter): Doubt, Inquiry, Truth; natures Self-Doubt and Suspicion; Human Design gate 63, Curiosity; I Ching After Completion.
- The energy in one sentence, lived well, covering all three heights (the shadow is one height, never the energy): the curiosity that keeps asking how a thing works and whether it holds, which keeps what you have finished sound, stays open without needing an end, and followed all the way finds the answer was there before it began.
- The word a person already says they feel, used as the entrance's subject: curiosity ("I'm curious"), the Human Design gate's own name for this energy. The first pass used doubt, which is the shadow.
- Why that word is not the shadow word, and not a virtue or an action in a noun's coat: curiosity is the asking itself, felt from inside; doubt is that asking turned into a pressure nobody can bear, which is only the lowest height. Curiosity is neither a virtue a person is praised for nor a verb dressed as a noun.
- The I Ching situation in one sentence: the crossing is done and everything is in its place, so the only way left to move is toward disorder, and the counsel is to keep watch in small things while all is well.
- The Human Design drive in one sentence: a pressure at the top of the head to know what comes next, which tests each step before it is taken and finds its answer through Gate 4 when someone asks for it.
- The body join in one sentence (the reason, from the organ entry): the pineal gland, and the reason is sleep, the part of you that says the day is finished, and the difference between the evening your head lets go and the evening it keeps asking.
