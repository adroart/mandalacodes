# Card 12 sheet

## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 44 picks out who belongs near you in the first seconds, before a reason; 50 knows what will feed the people near you and what has gone off; 59 is the longing to be known that gets through what two people keep between them; 18 sees the flaw first and mends it; 62 gets the small thing right, says the one word and stops; 33 draws you off alone until the past makes sense, then sends you back to tell it; 22 is warmth that leaves people lighter; 11 is a dream of how things could be.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): A heart that tells the real from the fake by feeling, trusts only what it can love, and opens to people a little at a time until nothing is held back.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): the heart ("my heart tells me"), the Gene Keys' own keyword for this card, A Pure Heart (gene-key-12-petals-of-freedom.md); its working is the gift, a feel for what is real (gene-key-12.md).
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): someone is talking to you, and under their words you can hear whether they mean them. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24): the gift feels anyone faking or hiding an aim as a deep discomfort and trusts only what it falls in love with (gene-key-12.md); the tone under the chosen words cannot be hidden (gene-key-12.md, allotted to card 12 by body/organs/throat.md); Gate 12 is choosy about who it opens up to and speaks only when moved (human-design/gates/gate-12.md); and, as one thread among these, the time when nothing passes between people and the worthy draw back (legge-12-standstill.md, deng-12-standstill.md). Test: "the heart lives here" draws no "why", since hearing whether someone means it is the heart's work, not the head's. The title, Petals of Freedom, is worked into CODE once, where it fits: the heart opens a petal at a time, and each one leaves you freer (gk-64ways-12-standstill.md: "we let the world into our heart and finally we taste that freedom that we have sought for so long").

Checked against 44: 44 recognises in seconds who belongs; 12 opens slowly, over a life, and its measure is real against fake, not belonging. Against 50: 50 knows what feeds others; 12 knows what is true for itself and whom to let in. Against 59: 59 breaks through the wall between two people; 12 is the one behind the wall, opening it a petal at a time. The old openings line ("A sense tells real from fake by what your heart does") named the energy "a sense", which 17, 32 and 50 also use, and put the heart second; the heart now leads.
