Checked against 37: 37 gathers people; 22 is how they are treated once they are there. Checked against 36: 36 is feeling that goes past the mind; 22 is feeling carried in company. The old line ("Warmth leaves whoever sits with you lighter than they came") named warmth, which is 37's word, and said the effect, not the energy; it goes.
