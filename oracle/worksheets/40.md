## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 21 is the will that takes charge where others stall and carries what follows; 26 is a drive that grows stronger the longer it is held in; 34 is strength lying quiet until something real calls, then pouring the whole of itself in; 52 is stillness at the end of a cycle, holding until the next is ready; 05 is an answer on its way, felt before it comes, met rested; 09 is a strength spent as small steps, one after another; 37 is warmth gathering a few people around one table; 39 is pressure that builds and presses on whatever has gone still.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): A will that works hard without wearing out because it gives itself only to what is yours, says no to the rest, and fills again in rest and time alone.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): the will ("I've lost the will to", "I've got the will back"), which the Gift names resolve (gene-key-40.md: "people with this Gene Key ... are born with great willpower"; "Resolve means that willpower is built into the activity from the outset"); Human Design names the same place the Will Center.
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): a long push is over and you can finally set things down. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24: the card's name is his artwork title, the I Ching only feeds it): the hard time is relieved and people can breathe freely again (huang-40-deliverance.md); the pull after work or after people to step away and restock (qw-gate-40-restoration.md, human-design/gates/gate-40.md); the Gift as deep physical relaxation, the art of doing nothing, and knowing when to stop (gene-key-40.md); the stomach that turns food into what you run on only while you are at rest (body/organs/stomach.md). Test: "the will lives here" draws no "why", because the will is what fills again once the push stops, and the whole chapter is the difference between will that is forced and will that refills. The title, Eternal Wellspring, is worked into CODE once, where it fits: the will "rises from deep inside you the way a spring does, and nothing in it has to be forced" (gk-64ways-40: "it'll keep filling us, replenishing us from within").

Checked against 21: 21 takes charge and carries others' work; 40 carries only its own and says no to the rest. Against 34: 34 lies quiet and then pours everything in at once; 40 works steadily and stops in time. Against 26: 26 grows by being held in; 40 fills by being let go. Against 52: 52 is stillness itself; 40 is the will, and rest is only where it refills. Against 39, its pair: 39 is the pressure building; 40 is the pressure let go. The old opening line ("Resolve carries a life through and fills again only when you stop") named the Gift with a verb nobody says of it; it goes.
