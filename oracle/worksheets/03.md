## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 01 is creation itself, making something from nothing, coming in waves; 04 is the wish to understand, asking until understanding comes up; 16 is enthusiasm, getting a thing out of your head before you feel ready; 24 is a pull back to where a thing began so it can start again; 42 carries each thing to its end so the next has room; 51 is a charge that sends you first off the edge; 53 is the urge to start something new, before any plan; 64 is imagination, seeing the shape of something new in what a change has scattered.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): The stretch just after something new has begun, when it has no settled shape yet and the shape is found by trying it many ways and letting what is around you change it.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): playfulness ("I'm feeling playful"), the Gift of Innovation, whose chapter says that to see innovation you only have to watch a young child at play (gene-key-03.md); its keyword is "Through the Eyes of a Child" (gene-key-03-messengers-of-the-infinite.md).
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in everyday words; CODE opens on it): something new has started in your life and nothing about it has settled yet. Read from all the sources together, never framed by the hexagram (Adrian, 2026-09-24): chaos is about beginnings and the greatest challenge is always at the beginning (gene-key-03.md); letting change in is uncomfortable, at least at the beginning (gk-64ways-03); gate 3 tries to bring order to something new arising, and a new thing takes time to take root (center-sacral.md); a matter merely in an undeveloped stage (practical-03). Test: "playfulness lives here" draws no "why", because a thing with no settled shape can only be found by trying it one way and another, which is play. The title, Messengers of the Infinite, is not worked in: no sentence carried it naturally, and the brief says leave it out then.

Checked against 53: 53 is the urge before anything starts; 3 is what happens once it has. Against 16: 16 pushes the thing out before you feel ready; 3 holds it unfinished and keeps trying. Against 64: 64 sees the whole shape at once; 3 finds it by hand, one try at a time. Against 01: 01 is the wave that makes something from nothing; 3 is the shaping of what the wave left. Against 04, its pair: 04 asks what the new thing means; 3 makes it. The old opening line ("The surge of something new finds its shape by trying") named a surge, which is 51's and 16's ground; it goes, and so do both of Adrian's released entrances.
