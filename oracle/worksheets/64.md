# Card 64 sheet

## 1. Place

- Neighbours (five to eight card numbers from entrance-openings.md, each with what it does): 04 asks and asks until understanding comes on its own; 11 lifts a life toward an ideal world not built yet, with pictures mostly for someone else; 17 follows a pattern out to where it ends; 23 sorts what matters and says only that; 41 longs for a larger life and pictures it; 43 knows an answer whole, out of quiet; 47 turns an old weight over until it changes state; 63 checks a finished thing for the flaw.
- What only this card is (one sentence, false for every neighbour): Imagination born out of the disorder of a change, which from inside that disorder pictures a new thing that has never existed and wants your hands to build it.
- The energy word (a word a person says they feel, never the shadow): imagination ("my imagination", "I can picture it"), the gift; it is where the I Ching's far bank seen from the water, the Head's pictures that ask how, and the chapter's own title (the Way of Imagination) meet.
- The card's moment (the situation in a life, in the hexagram's plain words; CODE opens on it): before completion is a time of change, one order ended and the next not yet formed (legge-64-before-completion.md: the reign of order has terminated and the struggle for its realisation recommenced; huang-64-before-completion.md: a transition from disorder to order; gk-64ways-64-before-completion.md: the name refers to the transformational state). Imagination lives in that time. Earlier drafts opened on "the moment just before something is finished" (the fox, the last stretch) and then on Adrian's own reading of the name; he asked for the sources only: "Stick with the information. I will put my own take on it later."

Tested against the outside read's hypothesis, "the finished shape seen from inside the unfinished": it is also true of 11 (a world not built yet, seen from this one) and of 17 (the end of a pattern seen from its start), so it fails step 1 as written. What 11 and 17 lack is the disorder: 11's pictures come from an ideal and 17's forecast from a pattern, while 64's pictures are made out of a change that has scattered everything. That is kept; the hypothesis's six facets held and are used below with one change (DESIGN never says "wait" or "time", so that "it needs time" has one home).

- Pictures the card leans on, and the meaning the card itself states for each: the young fox (Combination, Reading, Judgement): it takes itself to be across a step from the bank, its tail goes in, it cannot go on, and a crossing lost at the last step gains nothing; this meaning was missing until Adrian asked for it. Each moving line states its own counsel.
- The entrance beside the neighbours' lines, and how its shape differs: the neighbours open on a thing that arrives or comes ahead of something (04 wakes before, 17 follows out to its end while others are at the start, 23 knows before speech, 43 lands finished, 57 comes ahead of thought) or that lifts and runs out toward a future (11, 41). Sentence one here has no "before", no "arrives" and no future: imagination takes what a change has scattered and sees a new shape in it, so it works on what is already lying in pieces. Sentence two is not "It comes" or "It moves" (11, 17, 41, 43, 47 all use one); it says what it leaves to you. The old entrance's "brings pictures before it brings answers" is gone.
