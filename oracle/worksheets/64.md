# Card 64 worksheet

## 1. The energy, whole

- Shadow, gift, siddhi (from frontmatter): Confusion, Imagination, Illumination; natures Imitating and Confused; Human Design gate 64 in the Head centre, Channel of Abstraction with 47; I Ching Before Completion, Fire over Water; body pineal and glycine.
- The energy in one sentence, lived well, covering all three heights (the shadow is one height, never the energy): imagination, the pictures and ideas that arrive before they make sense, which clear on their own if you give them time and do not force an answer, and which, carried all the way, light you from inside with nothing of you in the way.
- The word a person already says they feel, used as the entrance's subject: imagination ("my imagination ran away with me", "I can picture it"). Every source meets on it: the Gene Keys gift, the gate's own reading (images that make sense later), and the I Ching's crossing, where you can see the far bank before you stand on it.
- Why that word is not the shadow word, and not a virtue or an action in a noun's coat: confusion is the same pictures with the mind demanding they make sense now; imagination is the pictures let be. It is not a virtue nobody praises you for it, and it is not a verb standing behind "the".
- The I Ching situation in one sentence: you are almost across the river and not yet on the other bank, and the one who hurries the last stretch wets its tail, so the counsel is to go carefully and put each thing in its place.
- The Human Design drive in one sentence: a pressure at the top of the head full of pictures and ideas that do not make sense yet, which clear in their own time and need a partner in the mind (gate 47) to turn them into something understood.
- The body join in one sentence (the reason, from the organ entry): the pineal gland, and the reason is light: the gland sits in the dark middle of the head and answers to light, given to the energy that is dark until it is lit.
