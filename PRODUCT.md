# Product

## Product direction

I want Mandala Codes to be a place to cast an oracle reading, study the 64 codes through the I Ching, Gene Keys, Human Design, and Tarot, and learn about mandala art. The Atlas brings the physical work into that experience. On the global map, people can explore where artworks have found homes, follow a piece back to its story or related code, and reach the Adrian Rasmussen site to buy an available work.

The oracle and the relationships among the 64 belong here. The Adrian Rasmussen site owns sales and the artwork registry across all series. The Atlas connects those experiences. The [cross-site plan](todo/plans/overarching-plan.md) records the art site's link to the globe on Mandala Codes. The [collector journey](../Adrian-Website/todo/plans/the-collector-journey.md) puts the globe and collector rituals within the long-term registry experience. Read the [Adrian Rasmussen outcome](../Adrian-Website/PRODUCT.md#current-outcome) for the commerce and registry side of the same journey.

## Current outcome

On a phone, a visitor can go from a reading to a related artwork, explore the Atlas, and open the exact artwork on the Adrian Rasmussen site. An available piece has a clear path to purchase. A collected piece can tell its story and appear on the map at city level when its keeper has opted in. The oracle reading remains a complete experience in its own right.

## Users

Seekers using an oracle to understand the 64 codes, their personal profile, and the relationships among the I Ching, Gene Keys, Human Design, and Tarot. The profile should support both immediate orientation and deeper study without requiring prior system knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Mandala Codes is an oracle that teaches. The profile maps eleven positions calculated from a birth moment across the 64 codes, helping a visitor understand how each position influences a different element of life. Guidance is the doorway; learning is the deepening.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, intimate, and contemplative. The interface speaks as an assured teacher, preserves the distinct character of each tradition, and lets the code itself remain the spine.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, dense control panels, decorative glass effects, spiritual spectacle, and interfaces that compress nuanced teachings into undifferentiated summaries. Do not let supporting controls compete with the chart.

## Design Principles

- Keep the central teaching object visually central.
- Reveal depth progressively while keeping each visible layer complete.
- Use restrained controls that disappear into the act of study.
- Preserve the identity of each system while showing their shared root in the 64.
- Prefer spatial coherence and calm hierarchy over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Core meaning must never depend on hover or color alone. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, touch interaction, and structural mobile reflow.
